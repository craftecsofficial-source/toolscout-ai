About ToolScout AI

ToolScout AI is a directory built to help people quickly find, compare and choose AI tools without wading through dozens of scattered blog posts. We organize tools into clear categories, give straightforward pricing summaries, and link directly to the official product so you can try it yourself.

How we pick tools

We look for tools that are actively maintained, have a clear use case, and are widely used or well reviewed within their category. Listings are researched independently; we do not accept payment in exchange for a favorable review.

How we make money

Some links on this site are affiliate links, meaning we may earn a small commission if you sign up through them, at no extra cost to you. This supports the site and never determines which tools we choose to list or how we describe them.

Get in touch

Want to suggest a tool, report outdated pricing, or ask about a listing? Visit our Submit a Tool page.
