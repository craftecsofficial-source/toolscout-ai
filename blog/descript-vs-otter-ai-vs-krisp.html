About Descript

Descript transcribes recordings into editable text; deleting or moving words edits the actual audio/video, and its Underlord AI co-editor and AI Speech features can clone a voice for corrections.

Pricing: Free tier available; Hobbyist from $16/mo (billed yearly), Creator (most popular) from $24/mo (billed yearly)

  • ✓Text-based editing dramatically speeds up rough cuts for talking-head content
  • ✓20+ built-in AI tools consolidate several point solutions into one app
  • ✓Strong Capterra scores for ease of use on podcasts and short-form video

About Otter.ai

Otter.ai automatically joins video calls to produce live transcripts, AI-generated summaries, and action items, letting teams search and chat across their entire meeting history instead of taking manual notes.

Pricing: Free tier (300 min/mo); Pro from $8.33/mo billed annually; Business from $19.99/user/mo annually

  • ✓Strong free tier for individuals and small teams
  • ✓Accurate transcription on clear, single-speaker audio
  • ✓Deep integration with Zoom, Teams, and Google Meet

About Krisp

Krisp removes background noise, echo and cross-talk from calls in real time using on-device AI, and layers on meeting transcription and AI-generated summaries. It works inside any conferencing app.

Pricing: Free plan (60 min/day); Pro from $8/mo; Business from $10/mo (billed annually)

  • ✓Noise cancellation is fast, effective and app-agnostic
  • ✓On-device processing means audio never leaves the user's machine
  • ✓Meeting summaries and action items save real note-taking time
