About Descript

Descript transcribes recordings so that deleting or moving words edits the actual audio and video. It adds Underlord, an AI editing agent, plus Studio Sound, eye-contact correction, filler-word removal, voice cloning, generated B-roll and translation.

Pricing: Free plan (1 hr media/month, 720p watermarked); Hobbyist $16/mo billed yearly ($24 monthly); Creator $24/mo yearly ($35 monthly); Business $50/mo yearly ($65 monthly); Enterprise custom

  • ✓Text-based editing makes rough cuts of talking-head and podcast content fast
  • ✓Many AI cleanup tools in one app instead of several separate ones
  • ✓Free plan with no credit card, though limited to 1 hour of media a month

About Otter.ai

Otter.ai records and transcribes meetings in real time, writes summaries and action items, and lets you ask questions across your past meetings, with bot or bot-free desktop recording.

Pricing: Free Basic (300 min/mo); Pro $8.33/mo billed annually ($16.99 monthly); Business $19.99/user/mo annually ($30 monthly)

  • ✓Usable free plan for light meeting loads
  • ✓Joins Zoom, Teams and Google Meet
  • ✓Searchable history and chat across meetings

About Krisp

Krisp started as real-time noise cancellation and now sells a bot-free AI meeting assistant (transcription, recordings, summaries, action items) that works with apps like Zoom, Google Meet and Teams. It also has separate call-center and developer products.

Pricing: 7-day free trial; Core $8/user/mo and Advanced $15/user/mo (shown on the annual toggle; $16 and $30 monthly); Enterprise custom

  • ✓Works across conferencing apps without a meeting bot
  • ✓Noise cancellation is unlimited on paid plans
