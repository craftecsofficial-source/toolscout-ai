About Profound

Profound tracks how brands appear in AI answer engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Google AI Overviews, and pairs that data with agents that help produce and update content. It now describes itself as an AI marketing platform.

Pricing: Free trial (50 prompts, daily for 7 days, 3 engines); Enterprise is custom-quoted. An Agency Growth self-serve plan exists but its price is not shown. Previously listed Starter ($99) and Growth ($399) plans no longer appear on the pricing page.

  • ✓Wide engine coverage on Enterprise (up to 9 engines)
  • ✓Connects monitoring to content agents in one product
  • ✓SSO/SAML and SOC 2 on Enterprise

About Scrunch AI

Scrunch is an AI search visibility platform that tracks how brands appear in AI answers across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode/Overviews and Meta, shows which sources AI cites, monitors AI bot crawls, and offers AXP, a machine-readable site layer for AI agents. Sitecore announced its acquisition of Scrunch in 2026.

Pricing: No free plan; 7-day free trial of Starter (no card). Starter $250/mo billed annually ($300 monthly); Growth $417/mo annually ($500 monthly); Enterprise custom. Extra seats $25/mo each.

  • ✓All core features on every plan; tiers differ mainly by volume
  • ✓Bot-crawl monitoring plus citation analysis in one tool
  • ✓Backing from a larger company after the Sitecore acquisition

Read the full Scrunch AI review →

About AthenaHQ

AthenaHQ is an answer engine optimization platform that tracks brand mentions, citations and share of voice across AI engines, then recommends on-page and off-page fixes. Usage is metered in credits, with a free Essential plan to start.

Pricing: Free Essential plan (300 credits, 5 engines). Starter $295/mo (3,600 credits, 11 models; annual billing listed as 17% off, annual price not shown). Enterprise custom. API access and extra credits cost extra.

  • ✓Real free plan, not a time-limited trial
  • ✓Broad model coverage on Starter (11)
  • ✓Unlimited seats on all plans
