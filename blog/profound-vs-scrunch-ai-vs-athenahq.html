About Profound

Profound is an AI answer engine optimization platform that tracks how brands appear across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and other AI engines, then uses AI agents to help create and optimize content in response. It is built primarily for enterprise marketing teams and Fortune 500 brands.

Pricing: No free tier; plans from $99/mo (Starter, ChatGPT-only) to $399/mo (Growth, 3 engines); custom Enterprise pricing

  • ✓Deepest data and widest engine coverage in the category
  • ✓Powerful AI Agents that turn insights directly into content
  • ✓Strong enterprise support, SSO/SOC2, and Fortune 500 track record

About Scrunch AI

Scrunch AI is an answer engine optimization platform that monitors brand visibility, citations, and AI-crawler traffic across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Mode/Overviews, and is distinguished by dedicated hallucination detection and an emerging Agent Experience Platform (AXP). Scrunch was acquired by digital-experience company Sitecore for roughly $225 million in June 2026.

Pricing: No free tier; 7-day free trial; plans from $250/mo (Starter) to $417/mo (Growth), billed annually; custom Enterprise pricing

  • ✓Only major AEO tool in this set with dedicated hallucination/false-claim detection
  • ✓Deep, GA4-connected AI crawler traffic analytics is consistently the most-praised feature
  • ✓Now backed by Sitecore's resources, distribution, and enterprise DXP customer base

About AthenaHQ

AthenaHQ is a Y Combinator-backed answer engine optimization platform, founded by ex-Google Search and DeepMind engineers, that tracks brand visibility across up to nine AI engines and turns findings into prioritized, executable content and technical fixes. It is notable for offering a genuinely free entry tier on top of a credit-based pricing model.

Pricing: Free Essential tier (credit-based); Starter from $295/mo (3,600 credits/mo); custom Enterprise pricing

  • ✓Only tool in this set with a genuinely free entry tier, not just a time-limited trial
  • ✓Broad engine coverage even on the entry paid tier, including Copilot and Grok
  • ✓Strong reported G2 rating (4.9/5) among early customers, plus G2 Spring 2026 High Performer badge

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper: Profound, Scrunch AI or AthenaHQ?

Based on published pricing: Profound (No free tier; plans from $99/mo (Starter, ChatGPT-only) to $399/mo (Growth, 3 engines); custom Enterprise pricing); Scrunch AI (No free tier; 7-day free trial; plans from $250/mo (Starter) to $417/mo (Growth), billed annually; custom Enterprise pricing); AthenaHQ (Free Essential tier (credit-based); Starter from $295/mo (3,600 credits/mo); custom Enterprise pricing). Exact value depends on your usage volume and which features you actually need, so check each tool's full pricing breakdown before deciding.

Can I use more than one of these aeo tools tools together?

Yes — many teams run more than one aeo tools tool for different parts of their workflow rather than picking a single winner. Start with whichever one matches your primary use case, and add a second only if you hit a real gap.
