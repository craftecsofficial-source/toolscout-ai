AthenaHQ vs PageLens.ai: Free Tier, Pricing and Content Engine Compared

AthenaHQ and PageLens.ai are the two most startup-friendly AEO tools on the market — here's how their free tiers, engine coverage and content capabilities actually differ.

Our pick: PageLens.ai

AthenaHQ's free Essential tier is genuinely generous for pure tracking, and it's the fairer fight of any tool on this list. But once you need real engine breadth or want content actually published to your site, PageLens's flat, predictable pricing and done-for-you publishing workflow are easier to plan around than AthenaHQ's credit-metered model.

The free tier: AthenaHQ genuinely wins this round

Credit where it's due: AthenaHQ's Essential plan is a real, ongoing free tier — not a time-limited trial — and it covers five engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews, Gemini, Claude) with 300 starter credits. PageLens doesn't have an equivalent ongoing free subscription; its free offering is a one-time, no-signup visibility audit rather than continuous tracking.

Once you pay, the pricing models diverge

AthenaHQ's paid Starter tier is $295/month for 3,600 credits and full 9-engine coverage — but credits are consumed per AI response, so monthly cost can vary with usage, and API access or extra credits cost more on top. PageLens's tiers are flat: $49, $199 or $699 a month, with no usage metering to track.

For teams that want to know exactly what they'll be billed each month without watching a credit balance, PageLens's model is simpler to budget against — even though AthenaHQ's entry paid price point ($295) sits between PageLens's Optimize ($199) and Growth ($699) tiers.

Content: research and drafting vs. research, drafting and publishing

AthenaHQ's Content Optimization Agent generates GEO-optimized articles and FAQs and improves them over time based on citation performance — a real capability. PageLens's Growth plan goes a step further in one specific way: it explicitly publishes the finished page to a CNAME'd /feed subdirectory on your own domain as part of the plan, rather than leaving publishing as a separate step.

Who should still pick AthenaHQ

If your priority is the widest possible engine coverage at the lowest possible cost — and you're comfortable managing a credit balance — AthenaHQ's free Essential tier and $295/mo Starter plan (covering all 9 tracked engines) are hard to beat on pure tracking value, especially backed by its strong early G2 rating (4.9/5).

Frequently asked questions

Does AthenaHQ or PageLens.ai have a better free plan?

AthenaHQ's free Essential plan offers ongoing tracking across 5 engines with 300 starter credits, which is more generous than PageLens's free offering — a one-time, no-signup visibility audit rather than a continuous free tier.

Which tool is cheaper once you need full engine coverage?

AthenaHQ's $295/mo Starter plan covers all 9 tracked engines. PageLens's equivalent full coverage (7 engines) requires the $699/mo Growth plan, though that price also includes done-for-you content publishing that AthenaHQ's Starter plan doesn't match.

Is AthenaHQ's credit-based pricing hard to predict?

It can be. Because AthenaHQ meters usage by AI response (1 credit = 1 response), monthly cost can vary, and API access or extra credits are paid add-ons on Starter. PageLens uses flat monthly pricing with no credit metering.

Which tool actually publishes content to my website?

Both generate content, but PageLens's Growth plan explicitly ships finished pages to a /feed subdirectory on your own domain as part of the plan. AthenaHQ's Content Optimization Agent generates and improves GEO content but doesn't describe an equivalent built-in publishing step.

How do their reviews compare?

AthenaHQ has a strong early G2 rating (4.9/5 from 34 reviews) and a Spring 2026 High Performer badge. PageLens.ai is too new to have an established G2 or Capterra listing yet, so neither company has a large, independently verified review base.

Is AthenaHQ or PageLens.ai better for a startup on a tight budget?

AthenaHQ's free tier makes it the cheaper way to start tracking. PageLens becomes more competitive once you want predictable flat pricing or done-for-you content published directly to your own domain.
