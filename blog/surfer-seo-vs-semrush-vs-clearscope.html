About Clearscope

Clearscope analyzes top-ranking content for a topic and scores drafts against real search intent and AI-answer patterns, helping writers and SEO teams create content that ranks in Google and gets cited by AI assistants like ChatGPT and Gemini.

Pricing: 14-day free trial; Essentials $129/mo, Business $399/mo, Enterprise custom

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper: Surfer SEO, Semrush or Clearscope?

Based on published pricing: Surfer SEO (From $49/mo (Discovery), billed annually — plans up to $299/mo, Enterprise custom); Semrush (No full free plan (limited free account only); paid plans from $139.95/mo, or $117.33/mo billed annually); Clearscope (14-day free trial; Essentials $129/mo, Business $399/mo, Enterprise custom). Exact value depends on your usage volume and which features you actually need, so check each tool's full pricing breakdown before deciding.

Which of these three has the best reviews?

Surfer SEO shows G2 4.8/5 (500+ reviews). Semrush shows G2 4.5/5 (3,434 reviews). Clearscope shows G2 4.9/5 (92 reviews). Weigh this alongside the feature and pricing comparison above, not as the only factor.

Which has the most features: Surfer SEO, Semrush or Clearscope?

Of the three, Clearscope lists the most features in our comparison above, though more features doesn't automatically mean it's the better fit — a simpler, more focused tool can win for a specific use case.

Can I use more than one of these marketing & seo tools together?

Yes — many teams run more than one marketing & seo tool for different parts of their workflow rather than picking a single winner. Start with whichever one matches your primary use case, and add a second only if you hit a real gap.
