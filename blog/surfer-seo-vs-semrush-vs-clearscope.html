About Surfer SEO

Surfer scores your articles against top-ranking pages as you write, audits existing content, plans topics, and tracks how your brand appears in AI answers such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode.

Pricing: From $49/mo (Discovery) billed yearly; Standard $99, Pro $182, Peace of Mind $299; AI Search Analytics $82; Enterprise custom

  • ✓Content Editor makes on-page optimization concrete
  • ✓Covers both classic SEO and AI visibility
  • ✓Free AI visibility audit tool to try first

About Semrush

Semrush bundles keyword research, backlinks, site audits, rank tracking, traffic estimates, content, ads and social tools, and tracks how brands show up in AI answers, under the 'Semrush One' banner.

Pricing: Free plan (limited: 1 demo project, 10 reports/day); SEO $139/mo ($117.33/mo annual); Starter $199/mo; Pro+ $299/mo; Advanced custom

  • ✓Very broad toolset and large data sets in one login
  • ✓Fast move into AI-search visibility tracking
  • ✓Free plan and a 7-day trial for paid tiers

About Clearscope

Clearscope helps teams find topics, write and optimize content against top-ranking pages, monitor published content, and track brand visibility in AI search, sold as per-month plans with unlimited users.

Pricing: 14-day free trial; Essentials $129/mo, Business $399/mo, Enterprise custom

  • ✓Unlimited users and projects on every plan, so adding writers does not raise the price
  • ✓Public prices and a 14-day free trial; the pricing page says no contracts
  • ✓Covers research, writing, monitoring and AI-search tracking in one product

Exact value depends on your usage volume and which features you actually need, so check each tool's full pricing breakdown before deciding.

Which of these three has the best reviews?

None of Surfer SEO, Semrush or Clearscope has a review count we could independently verify on G2, Capterra or Trustpilot as of writing, so lean on the feature and pricing comparison above along with your own trial.

Which has the most features: Surfer SEO, Semrush or Clearscope?

Of the three, Clearscope lists the most features in our comparison above, though more features doesn't automatically mean it's the better fit — a simpler, more focused tool can win for a specific use case.

Can I use more than one of these marketing & seo tools together?

Yes — many teams run more than one marketing & seo tool for different parts of their workflow rather than picking a single winner. Start with whichever one matches your primary use case, and add a second only if you hit a real gap.
