About Apollo.io

Apollo combines a B2B contact and company database (it claims over 240 million contacts and 30 million companies) with email sequences, a dialer, enrichment and AI agents, so sales teams can find prospects and contact them from one place.

Pricing: Free plan with no time limit; paid tiers Basic, Professional and Custom - per-user prices not shown on the pricing page today, check apollo.io/pricing

  • ✓Data and outreach tools in one product, so fewer separate subscriptions
  • ✓Free plan has no stated time limit
  • ✓Works from inside Gmail, CRMs and AI assistants as well as its own workspace

About Outreach

Outreach is a sales engagement and execution platform that helps revenue teams automate multi-channel outreach sequences across email, phone, and social. It layers in AI-driven deal insights, conversation intelligence, and forecasting to help reps prioritize the right accounts and managers coach more effectively.

Pricing: Custom quote-based pricing per seat, typically enterprise-tier; no public self-serve pricing, sales-assisted onboarding required

  • ✓Deep automation reduces manual prospecting busywork
  • ✓Strong reporting and pipeline visibility for managers
  • ✓Wide range of native integrations with CRMs and dialers

About Salesloft

Salesloft is a sales engagement platform for outbound cadences, conversation intelligence, deal tracking and forecasting. Its site now presents it as an AI revenue system, with Clari Forecast and Clari Conversations products alongside the Salesloft ones after the two companies agreed to merge.

Pricing: Not public. The pricing page lists no prices or plans; you have to talk to sales.

  • ✓Covers prospecting, deal tracking and forecasting in one vendor
  • ✓Rhythm gives reps a single prioritized to-do list
  • ✓Forecasting from Clari now sits in the same portfolio
