About Apollo.io

Apollo.io combines a large B2B contact and company database with sales engagement tools - sequences, dialer, and email - so teams can find, prioritize, and reach prospects in one platform. It's widely used by SMB and mid-market sales teams as an all-in-one alternative to stitching together separate data and outreach tools.

Pricing: Free plan available; paid plans start around $49/user/month (Basic), with Professional and Custom/Organization tiers adding advanced data, automation, and admin controls.

  • ✓Large, frequently updated contact database bundled with engagement tools at a relatively low price
  • ✓All-in-one platform reduces the need for separate data and sequencing tools
  • ✓Strong free and low-cost tiers make it accessible to small teams and individual reps

About Outreach

Outreach is a sales engagement and execution platform that helps revenue teams automate multi-channel outreach sequences across email, phone, and social. It layers in AI-driven deal insights, conversation intelligence, and forecasting to help reps prioritize the right accounts and managers coach more effectively.

Pricing: Custom quote-based pricing per seat, typically enterprise-tier; no public self-serve pricing, sales-assisted onboarding required

  • ✓Deep automation reduces manual prospecting busywork
  • ✓Strong reporting and pipeline visibility for managers
  • ✓Wide range of native integrations with CRMs and dialers

About Salesloft

Salesloft is a revenue orchestration and sales engagement platform that helps go-to-market teams run structured multi-channel cadences, track buyer engagement, and manage deals through AI-guided workflows. It combines prospecting automation with conversation intelligence and pipeline forecasting in one connected system.

Pricing: Custom quote-based pricing per seat, sold via annual contracts with sales-assisted onboarding

  • ✓Highly rated user experience compared to competitors
  • ✓Strong workflow automation via its Rhythm engine
  • ✓Good balance of prospecting tools and deal management in one suite

Exact value depends on your usage volume and which features you actually need, so check each tool's full pricing breakdown before deciding.
