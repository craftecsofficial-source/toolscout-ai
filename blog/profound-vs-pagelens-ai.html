Profound vs PageLens.ai: Which AI Visibility Platform Should You Choose in 2026?

Profound is built for enterprise teams with big budgets and dedicated analysts. PageLens.ai is built for teams who want to start tracking AI visibility today and ship citation-ready content this week.

Our pick: PageLens.ai

For most marketing teams outside the Fortune 500, PageLens.ai is the better starting point: transparent self-serve pricing from $49/mo, a free audit with no sales call, and a content engine that actually publishes to your own domain. Profound's deeper enterprise data and Agent tooling are real, but they come wrapped in a ChatGPT-only entry tier, a steep price ramp to real coverage, and a sales-led buying process that only makes sense once you're spending $2,000+ a month.

Pricing: transparent flat fee vs. enterprise ramp

PageLens.ai publishes every price on its site: $49/mo for daily ChatGPT tracking, $199/mo once you add Google AI Mode and Perplexity, and $699/mo for the done-for-you content engine that also covers Claude, Gemini, Grok and Copilot. There's no sales call required to see a number.

Profound's cheapest plan is $99/mo billed annually — but it only tracks ChatGPT. To get the three-engine coverage most teams actually need, you're looking at the $399/mo Growth plan, and independent reports put typical enterprise deployments between $2,000 and $5,000+ a month once seats, Agent credits and full engine coverage are added. Profound doesn't publish those higher-tier numbers; you find out in a sales conversation.

Engine coverage at the entry tier

Both tools gate full engine coverage behind a higher tier, but PageLens gets you to 3 engines (ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Perplexity) at $199/mo, while Profound's equivalent 3-engine tier is double the price at $399/mo. If your buyers are asking questions across more than one AI engine — and by 2026 they almost always are — PageLens reaches useful coverage at a meaningfully lower price point.

Content and publishing: PageLens closes the loop

This is where the two products diverge most. Profound's Agents can generate and optimize content, and Agent Analytics can track AI-crawler traffic back to your domain via CDN integrations (Cloudflare, Vercel, Akamai) — genuinely useful, but it's a configurable, credit-metered system aimed at teams with the technical setup to wire it in.

PageLens's Growth plan does one specific thing well: it reads six live sources (Reddit threads, G2/Capterra, competitor pages, news, search-demand data, existing AI answers), drafts a brief, and ships a finished page to a CNAME'd /feed subdirectory on your own domain — no CDN integration required, and no credits to manage. The SEO equity and URLs are yours from day one.

Who should actually pick Profound

Profound is the stronger choice if you're already running a large-scale AI-visibility program: an enterprise brand with a dedicated analytics team, a need for up to 9 tracked engines, SOC2/SSO paperwork already required by procurement, and budget north of $2,000/month. Its data depth and Agent orchestration (Aim) genuinely outclass most of the category at that scale.

In fairness to Profound

To be fair to Profound: if you're already an enterprise brand tracking AI visibility across nine engines with a dedicated team, Profound's data depth and Agent orchestration are more mature than PageLens's newer, leaner platform. The comparison above is written for the much larger group of teams who aren't there yet.

Frequently asked questions

Is PageLens.ai cheaper than Profound?

Yes. PageLens starts at $49/mo with published pricing at every tier, while Profound's cheapest plan is $99/mo for ChatGPT-only tracking, and real multi-engine usage typically runs $399/mo to $2,000+/mo once you factor in Agent credits and seats.

Does Profound have a free trial?

No, Profound has no free tier or trial. PageLens.ai offers a free, no-signup AI visibility audit that shows how ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity currently describe your brand.

Which tool tracks more AI engines?

Profound can track up to 9 engines on its custom Enterprise plan, more than PageLens's 7. But at comparable self-serve price points, PageLens's Optimize plan ($199/mo, 3 engines) covers more ground per dollar than Profound's equivalent Growth tier ($399/mo, 3 engines).

Can Profound publish content directly to my website like PageLens?

Profound's Agents can generate and optimize content and, via CDN integrations, help surface it, but it doesn't offer PageLens's specific done-for-you workflow of drafting and publishing a finished page to a /feed subdirectory on your own domain without extra technical setup.

Is Profound or PageLens.ai better for a small marketing team?

For a small or mid-size team without a dedicated analytics function, PageLens is the more practical starting point — transparent pricing, no sales call, and a content engine you can turn on without configuring CDN integrations or managing Agent credits.

Does Profound have real user reviews?

Profound has a G2 presence, though the reported review counts vary widely (roughly 300 to 1,000+) across different listing pages, and it has no visible Capterra presence as of August 2026. PageLens.ai is too new to have accumulated third-party reviews yet either, so neither company can be fully verified through review sites today.
