About Jasper

Jasper focuses on brand voice and campaign consistency, letting whole marketing teams generate on-brand blog posts, ads and social copy from shared templates.

Pricing: Pro from $59/mo per seat (billed yearly); Business custom-priced. No free plan.

  • ✓Best for teams needing consistent brand voice at scale
  • ✓Strong long-form and SEO content templates
  • ✓Deep workflow and collaboration features for marketing orgs

About Copy.ai

Copy.ai started as a short-form copywriting tool and, after its October 2025 acquisition by Fullcast, has repositioned around automating go-to-market workflows for sales and marketing teams, with a usable free tier for light writing needs.

Pricing: Free tier available; Chat plan from $24/mo (billed annually); Growth/Enterprise plans from $1,000/mo+

  • ✓Strong value for freelancers and solo creators
  • ✓Sales and GTM workflow templates save time for revenue teams
  • ✓Usable free tier to try before buying

Read the full Copy.ai review →

About Writesonic

Writesonic has repositioned from a budget AI writing tool into an AI Search Visibility platform, tracking how a brand is cited across ChatGPT, Gemini and other AI answer engines alongside legacy SEO article generation.

Pricing: 7-day free trial, no credit card required; plans from $79/mo billed annually

  • ✓Combines writing and AI-search visibility tracking in one platform
  • ✓Tracks visibility across up to 10 AI platforms on Enterprise
  • ✓Action Center surfaces prioritized fixes, not just diagnostics

Quick picks

  • ✓Jasper — Marketing teams producing high volumes of on-brand content across multiple writers.
  • ✓Copy.ai — Solo creators and sales teams who need fast short-form copy and outbound sequences.
  • ✓Writesonic — Marketing and SEO teams that need to monitor and improve brand visibility inside AI answer engines alongside traditional SEO content.

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper: Jasper, Copy.ai or Writesonic?

Based on published pricing: Jasper (Pro from $59/mo per seat (billed yearly); Business custom-priced. No free plan.); Copy.ai (Free tier available; Chat plan from $24/mo (billed annually); Growth/Enterprise plans from $1,000/mo+); Writesonic (7-day free trial, no credit card required; plans from $79/mo billed annually). Exact value depends on your usage volume and which features you actually need, so check each tool's full pricing breakdown before deciding.

Which of these three has the best reviews?

Jasper shows G2 4.7/5 (1,270+ reviews). Copy.ai shows G2 4.7/5 (182 reviews). Writesonic shows G2 4.7/5 (2,031 reviews). Weigh this alongside the feature and pricing comparison above, not as the only factor.

Which has the most features: Jasper, Copy.ai or Writesonic?

Of the three, Jasper lists the most features in our comparison above, though more features doesn't automatically mean it's the better fit — a simpler, more focused tool can win for a specific use case.

Can I use more than one of these writing & content tools together?

Yes — many teams run more than one writing & content tool for different parts of their workflow rather than picking a single winner. Start with whichever one matches your primary use case, and add a second only if you hit a real gap.
