About Jasper

Jasper is aimed at marketing teams that need consistent on-brand content across channels. Pro costs $59/seat/month billed yearly, Business is custom-priced, and there is no free plan.

Pricing: Pro $69/seat/mo monthly or $59/seat/mo billed yearly; 7-day free trial. Business is custom pricing (contact sales, 12-month commitment).

  • ✓Brand voice and knowledge applied automatically across a team
  • ✓Marketing-specific agents and workflows rather than a blank chat box
  • ✓Monthly Pro plan can be cancelled anytime

About Copy.ai

Copy.ai now sells itself as an AI platform for go-to-market teams, built around chained workflows, agents and a CRM-connected data layer, with a cheap Chat plan for ordinary writing.

Pricing: Free plan; Chat $29/mo ($24/mo annual); Growth $1,000/mo, Expansion $2,000/mo, Scale $3,000/mo (annual billing); Enterprise custom

About Writesonic

Writesonic is now an AI search visibility (GEO/AEO) platform: it tracks how brands appear in AI answers and recommends fixes, with a content agent and AI article writer included.

Pricing: Starter $79/mo, Basic $199/mo, Growth $399/mo (annual billing); Enterprise custom; 7-day free trial, no card

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper: Jasper, Copy.ai or Writesonic?

Based on published pricing: Jasper (Pro $69/seat/mo monthly or $59/seat/mo billed yearly; 7-day free trial. Business is custom pricing (contact sales, 12-month commitment).); Copy.ai (Free plan; Chat $29/mo ($24/mo annual); Growth $1,000/mo, Expansion $2,000/mo, Scale $3,000/mo (annual billing); Enterprise custom); Writesonic (Starter $79/mo, Basic $199/mo, Growth $399/mo (annual billing); Enterprise custom; 7-day free trial, no card). Exact value depends on your usage volume and which features you actually need, so check each tool's full pricing breakdown before deciding.

Which of these three has the best reviews?

None of Jasper, Copy.ai or Writesonic has a review count we could independently verify on G2, Capterra or Trustpilot as of writing, so lean on the feature and pricing comparison above along with your own trial.

Which has the most features: Jasper, Copy.ai or Writesonic?

Of the three, Jasper lists the most features in our comparison above, though more features doesn't automatically mean it's the better fit — a simpler, more focused tool can win for a specific use case.

Can I use more than one of these writing & content tools together?

Yes — many teams run more than one writing & content tool for different parts of their workflow rather than picking a single winner. Start with whichever one matches your primary use case, and add a second only if you hit a real gap.
