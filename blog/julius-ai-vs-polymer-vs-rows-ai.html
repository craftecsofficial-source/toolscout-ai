About Julius AI

Julius AI lets you upload spreadsheets and other data, ask questions in plain English, and get analysis, charts and reports back. It has since grown into a wider AI workspace that also makes presentations, websites, images and video, and includes browser agents for research.

Pricing: Free plan with a daily credit allowance (amount not stated). Plus $20/mo ($16/mo annual), Pro $45/mo ($37/mo annual), Business $450/mo ($375/mo annual), Enterprise custom.

  • ✓Easy entry: upload a spreadsheet and ask questions without writing SQL or Python
  • ✓Free plan to try it, with paid tiers from $16 to $20 a month
  • ✓One tool covers analysis, charts, slides and simple sites

About Polymer

Polymer (Polymer Search) is a no-code business intelligence tool that automatically transforms spreadsheets, CSVs, and connected data sources into interactive dashboards, charts, and visual reports. It uses AI to suggest the most useful visualizations and insights from raw data without requiring formulas, SQL, or a data analyst.

Pricing: Free plan for basic use; paid plans start around $25-$49/month per user, billed monthly or annually, with higher tiers for teams and more data sources.

  • ✓Very fast to get from raw spreadsheet to visual dashboard
  • ✓No coding, formulas, or BI training required to start
  • ✓Wide range of pre-built connectors for common business tools

About Rows AI

Rows is a spreadsheet-style tool with an AI data analyst on top. You ask for analysis or reports in plain language, it pulls data from 50+ sources (databases, analytics tools, bank accounts) or extracts it from PDFs, and keeps reports refreshed on a schedule. The site's banner states that Rows has joined Superhuman; the product and paid plans were still being sold as of 2026-10-29.

Pricing: Free $0; Plus $8/user/month ($6 billed annually); Pro $79/month + $8/user ($59 + $6/user billed annually); Enterprise via sales. Prices exclude tax.

  • ✓Plain-language analysis lowers the barrier for non-analysts
  • ✓Many ready-made data connectors cut copy-paste work
  • ✓Scheduled refresh keeps recurring reports current
