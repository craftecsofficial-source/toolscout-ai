About Julius AI

Julius AI is a conversational AI tool that lets users upload spreadsheets, CSVs, or databases and ask questions in plain English to get statistical analysis, visualizations, and charts without writing code. It acts like a personal data analyst, handling data cleaning, exploratory analysis, and chart generation through a chat interface powered by underlying large language models.

Pricing: Freemium model with a limited free tier and paid monthly/annual subscription plans that unlock higher usage limits, more advanced analysis, and additional integrations; exact tier pricing is subject to change on julius.ai.

  • ✓Very approachable for non-technical users who want quick data insights
  • ✓Fast iteration since follow-up questions refine analysis without re-uploading data
  • ✓Shows underlying code, giving technical users a way to verify or reuse logic

About Polymer

Polymer (Polymer Search) is a no-code business intelligence tool that automatically transforms spreadsheets, CSVs, and connected data sources into interactive dashboards, charts, and visual reports. It uses AI to suggest the most useful visualizations and insights from raw data without requiring formulas, SQL, or a data analyst.

Pricing: Free plan for basic use; paid plans start around $25-$49/month per user, billed monthly or annually, with higher tiers for teams and more data sources.

  • ✓Very fast to get from raw spreadsheet to visual dashboard
  • ✓No coding, formulas, or BI training required to start
  • ✓Wide range of pre-built connectors for common business tools

About Rows AI

Rows is a spreadsheet application that combines familiar spreadsheet mechanics with built-in AI functions and live integrations to APIs and business tools like Google Analytics, Stripe, and HubSpot. Its AI features let users generate formulas, summarize or classify text, and build reports directly inside the spreadsheet without switching to a separate BI tool.

Pricing: Free plan for individuals and small teams; paid Business/Team plans priced per user per month (billed monthly or annually), with custom enterprise pricing for larger organizations.

  • ✓Familiar spreadsheet interface lowers the learning curve for new users
  • ✓AI formula assistant speeds up building complex calculations
