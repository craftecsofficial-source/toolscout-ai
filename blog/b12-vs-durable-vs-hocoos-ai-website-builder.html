About B12

B12 uses AI to generate a complete, ready-to-launch website—including copy, images, and layout—from a short description of your business, then lets you refine it with an editor or professional design help. Beyond the site itself, B12 bundles business tools like online scheduling, payments, email marketing, and client intake forms into one platform.

Pricing: Free plan to build and preview a site; paid plans required to publish on a custom domain, starting at a monthly rate with higher tiers for more tools and features

About Durable

Durable is an all-in-one AI business builder that generates a complete website, logo, and copy in about 30 seconds, then bundles built-in SEO/GEO, a CRM, bookings, and AI marketing agents to help small businesses launch and grow.

Pricing: Free plan ($0/mo: subdomain, basic SEO, 5 AI images/mo, 10-contact CRM). Launch $25/mo ($22/mo billed yearly). Grow $49/mo ($41/mo billed yearly, unlimited team members and 10x AI usage). All plans include SEO, secure hosting, unlimited traffic, and CRM.

About Hocoos AI Website Builder

Hocoos is a no-code AI website builder that generates a complete website - layout, written content, images, and structure - directly from a short description of your business. It targets small businesses, freelancers, and solopreneurs who want a professional-looking site without hiring a designer or writing copy.

Pricing: Free plan available with Hocoos branding and limited features; paid plans start around $8-17/month (billed annually) for custom domain, no branding, and e-commerce features.
