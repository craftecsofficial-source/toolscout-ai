About Runway

Runway's Creative suite generates and edits video, images and audio using its Gen-4.5 model and other labs' models, with Workflows, Agent, a mobile app and a developer API.

Pricing: Free (125 one-time credits); Standard $15/mo ($12/mo annual), Pro $35/mo ($28/mo annual), Max $95/mo ($76/mo annual)

  • ✓Editing tools go beyond text-to-video
  • ✓Several models share one credit pool
  • ✓Free credits never expire

About Kling AI

Kling AI is Kuaishou's text-to-video and image-to-video generator, best known for natural-looking human and physical motion. Its site now lists a Kling 4.0 video model and a Kling Image 3.0 model.

Pricing: Consumer plan prices and free-credit amounts could not be verified on kling.ai today (pricing page is script-rendered). Separate API pricing exists at kling.ai/dev/pricing. Check the in-app membership page before paying.

  • ✓Widely praised for realistic human and physical motion
  • ✓Motion Brush and Motion Control give finer control than prompt-only tools
  • ✓API and agent (MCP/CLI) access suit high-volume pipelines

About Luma Dream Machine

Luma's Dream Machine has grown into Luma Agents, a canvas workspace where an agent plans and generates images, video, audio and text using Luma's Ray models plus third-party models.

Pricing: Plus $30/mo ($25/mo billed yearly), Pro $90/mo ($75 yearly), Ultra $300/mo ($250 yearly); Team and Enterprise are contact sales. The pricing page lists no free plan, though the site has 'Try for free' links with unstated terms.

  • ✓Photorealistic, HDR-grade output with strong camera motion
  • ✓Fast generation speed relative to render quality
  • ✓Agent-driven canvas unifies image, video, audio and text in one workspace
