About Grammarly

Grammarly checks grammar, spelling and tone as you type in browsers, desktop apps and mobile keyboards, with paid rewriting, plagiarism and AI-detection tools and a monthly AI prompt allowance. The company is now part of Superhuman.

Pricing: Free plan available (100 AI prompts/mo); Pro $12/member/mo billed annually or $30/mo billed monthly; Enterprise custom pricing

  • ✓Works in almost every place you already type
  • ✓Free plan covers basic grammar, spelling and tone view
  • ✓Edits your own writing instead of replacing it with generated text

About Wordtune

Wordtune is AI21 Labs' rewriting assistant: select text and get alternative phrasings, tone shifts, summaries and grammar fixes in a web editor and browser extensions.

Pricing: Free plan; Advanced $6.99/mo ($4.89/mo annual); Unlimited $9.99/mo ($6.99/mo annual)

  • ✓Several distinct rewrites per sentence
  • ✓Cheap paid tiers
  • ✓Free plan includes unlimited grammar checks

About Sapling

Sapling is a language toolkit with a browser extension, integrations for CRM and helpdesk tools, and a developer API and SDK. It offers grammar checking, autocomplete, rephrasing, snippets and an AI content detector.

Pricing: Free plan ($0, basic suggestions); Pro $25/mo or $12/mo billed annually; Enterprise from 10 seats at $15/seat/mo (quote on request); metered API with a one-time $5 minimum. Verified on sapling.ai/pricing on 2026-10-19.

  • ✓Free plan works in the browser extension, Google Docs, Outlook and Word
  • ✓Pro is $12/mo on annual billing, and registering gives a free trial without a card
  • ✓Developer API and MCP server are rare among writing assistants

Quick picks
