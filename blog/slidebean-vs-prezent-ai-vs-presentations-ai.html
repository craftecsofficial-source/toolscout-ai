About Slidebean

Slidebean is a pitch-deck-focused presentation tool with an AI deck builder, 100+ templates, view tracking, a cap table template and financial modeling resources. A higher tier adds advisory sessions and investor tools, and the company also sells deck design and fundraising services separately.

Pricing: Free plan (no card, not time-limited) with basic editing; sharing, downloading and brand customization need a paid plan. Starter is $7/month billed yearly; Accelerate is $99/month billed monthly. Done-for-you design and fundraising services have no listed prices.

  • ✓Starter at $7/month billed yearly is cheap for the focus it offers
  • ✓Built around pitch decks, with investor-oriented extras
  • ✓Free plan lets you build before paying

About Prezent.ai

Prezent.ai is an AI presentation platform built specifically for enterprise teams, helping them generate on-brand slides quickly while enforcing company templates, colors, and fonts. It layers in features like storyline structuring, a searchable slide library from past decks, and audience-aware content tailoring, aiming to cut the hours large organizations spend reformatting and rebuilding slides.

Pricing: Primarily enterprise/custom per-seat licensing with annual contracts for large teams; a free trial and limited individual plans are available for smaller-scale use. Exact pricing requires contacting sales for enterprise deployments.

  • ✓Strong brand and template enforcement for large organizations
  • ✓Storyline and audience-tailoring features go beyond basic slide generation
  • ✓Slide Library reduces duplicate work by surfacing already-approved content

About Presentations.AI

Presentations.AI is an AI presentation platform that converts prompts, documents, or URLs into branded, presentation-ready decks and exports natively to PowerPoint or Google Slides. Beyond one-off generation, it offers a "Refresh" agent that keeps master decks updated from connected data sources (Salesforce, HubSpot, Google Sheets, Snowflake, and more) and an API/white-label option for generating decks at scale inside other products.

Pricing: Free Starter plan with 100 credits; Pro at $20/month billed annually (5,000 credits); Gold at $100/month billed annually (50,000 credits); custom Enterprise pricing for larger teams and API/white-label use.

  • ✓Backed by known investors (Accel, Together Fund) and built by a team with enterprise software background
  • ✓SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant, which matters for enterprise/regulated buyers
  • ✓Refresh/data-sync feature genuinely differentiates it from one-shot AI deck generators
