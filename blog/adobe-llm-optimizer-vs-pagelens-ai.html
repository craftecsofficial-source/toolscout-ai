Adobe LLM Optimizer vs PageLens.ai: Enterprise Suite or Self-Serve Startup?

Adobe LLM Optimizer plugs AI-search visibility into Adobe Experience Cloud for six-figure enterprise budgets. PageLens.ai gets any team tracking and publishing in minutes.

Our pick: PageLens.ai

Unless you're already running Adobe Experience Manager and have a five-to-six-figure annual budget, PageLens.ai is the more practical choice: transparent pricing you can see without a sales call, no CMS lock-in, and a content engine that works with any domain.

Pricing you can actually see

PageLens.ai lists every plan and price on its pricing page: $49, $199, $699 a month, or custom Enterprise. Adobe LLM Optimizer publishes no pricing at all — it sells via annual contract on a prompt-based model with a 1,000-prompt minimum, and third-party estimates put typical annual spend around $115,000. You won't know your real number until you've been through a sales process.

Built for one ecosystem vs. built for any domain

Adobe LLM Optimizer's standout feature — pushing AI-optimized content live at the CDN edge in minutes — delivers its full value primarily to brands already running Adobe Experience Manager and the wider Experience Cloud. If you're on a different CMS or CDN, you lose a meaningful chunk of what makes the product distinctive.

PageLens works the same way regardless of your stack: content ships to a CNAME'd /feed subdirectory on your own domain, so it doesn't matter whether you run WordPress, Webflow, a custom stack, or Adobe's own tools.

A brand-new product either way

Adobe LLM Optimizer only launched in October 2025 and is mid-rebrand to Adobe Brand Visibility, so it currently has zero reviews on G2 or Capterra. PageLens.ai is newer still and in the same position. Neither company can point to a large, independently verified review base yet — the difference is that PageLens's pricing and setup are transparent enough to evaluate without waiting for reviews to catch up.

Who should still pick Adobe LLM Optimizer

If your organization already runs its content and analytics through Adobe Experience Manager, Adobe Analytics and Customer Journey Analytics, Adobe LLM Optimizer's closed-loop CDN deployment and native revenue attribution are a genuinely strong fit — assuming your budget supports a five-to-six-figure annual commitment.

Why teams choose PageLens.ai over Adobe LLM Optimizer

  • ✓Published pricing from $49/mo instead of a five-to-six-figure quote after a sales process
  • ✓Works with any CMS or CDN, not just Adobe Experience Manager
  • ✓A free, no-signup audit instead of a sales-led enterprise contract as the only entry point
  • ✓No 1,000-prompt annual minimum to commit to before you've seen results
  • ✓Self-serve setup in minutes rather than an enterprise onboarding process
In fairness to Adobe LLM Optimizer

If your marketing stack already runs on Adobe Experience Manager and Adobe Analytics, Adobe LLM Optimizer's native integration and CDN-edge deployment offer a level of closed-loop automation PageLens doesn't attempt to match.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Adobe LLM Optimizer cost compared to PageLens.ai?

PageLens.ai publishes pricing starting at $49/mo. Adobe LLM Optimizer publishes no pricing; third-party estimates suggest typical annual spend around $115,000, with a required annual contract and a 1,000-prompt minimum.

Do I need Adobe Experience Manager to use Adobe LLM Optimizer?

No, but its biggest differentiator — CDN-edge auto-optimization — delivers its full value mainly to brands already running Adobe's content and CDN infrastructure. PageLens works the same way on any domain.

Which tool is easier to try before committing?

PageLens.ai — it offers a free, no-signup AI visibility audit and self-serve paid plans. Adobe LLM Optimizer has no free trial and requires a sales-led annual contract.

Does Adobe LLM Optimizer have user reviews?

Not yet. It launched in October 2025 and shows 0 reviews on both G2 and Capterra as of August 2026, the same review-coverage stage PageLens.ai is currently at.

Which tool publishes content to my own domain?

PageLens.ai's Growth plan ships finished, citation-ready pages to a /feed subdirectory on your own domain regardless of CMS. Adobe LLM Optimizer deploys fixes at the CDN edge, which works best when you're already on Adobe's infrastructure.

Is Adobe LLM Optimizer being renamed?

Yes, Adobe has been transitioning the product to the name Adobe Brand Visibility through 2026, combining the original engine with Semrush market intelligence following Adobe's acquisition of Semrush.
