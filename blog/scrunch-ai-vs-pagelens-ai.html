Scrunch AI vs PageLens.ai: Comparing Price, Features and Fit in 2026

Scrunch AI brings dedicated hallucination detection and a Sitecore-backed roadmap. PageLens.ai brings a lower entry price, faster setup, and content that actually gets published.

Our pick: PageLens.ai

Scrunch AI's hallucination-detection feature is genuinely useful for regulated or reputation-sensitive brands, but it starts at $250-300/month with no free tier and now sits inside a much larger company's roadmap following Sitecore's 2026 acquisition. PageLens.ai starts at $49/month, publishes real content to your own domain, and isn't waiting on a beta feature (AXP) that most customers still can't use.

Pricing: PageLens is roughly 5x cheaper to get started

Scrunch AI's Starter plan is $250/month billed annually, with no free tier at all beyond a 7-day trial. PageLens.ai's Monitor plan is $49/month with no trial time limit on the paid features — you simply pay monthly. For a team wanting to dip a toe into AI-visibility tracking, that's a meaningfully lower bar.

Hallucination detection vs. content publishing

Scrunch's strongest differentiator is real: dedicated hallucination detection that flags when an AI model states something false or outdated about your brand, available on every tier including Starter. If that's your primary concern — say, in a regulated industry — Scrunch has an edge here.

PageLens doesn't market a dedicated hallucination-detection feature. Instead, its edge is closing the loop on the more common problem most brands actually have: being absent from AI answers entirely. Its content engine researches, drafts and publishes pages specifically built to earn citations, shipped straight to your own domain.

An acquisition changes the calculus

In June 2026, digital-experience platform vendor Sitecore acquired Scrunch AI for roughly $225 million. That's a strong outcome for Scrunch's investors, but it also means Scrunch's product roadmap, pricing and packaging are now subject to a much larger company's priorities — and its most distinctive upcoming feature, AXP, remains in limited beta with no public launch date. PageLens, as an independent company, is moving on its own roadmap.

Who should still pick Scrunch AI

If hallucination and misinformation monitoring is your primary requirement — for example, in healthcare, finance or another regulated category — Scrunch's dedicated detection feature and its new backing from Sitecore's enterprise distribution are worth the higher entry price.

Why teams choose PageLens.ai over Scrunch AI

  • ✓Roughly a fifth of Scrunch AI's starting price, with no annual commitment required
  • ✓An independent company on its own roadmap, not folded into a larger DXP's priorities
  • ✓A content engine that's live today, not a flagship feature still in limited beta
  • ✓Free, no-signup visibility audit vs. a time-limited 7-day trial
  • ✓Publishing to your own domain, not just page audits and recommendations
In fairness to Scrunch AI

Scrunch's hallucination-detection tooling is a real, differentiated capability that PageLens doesn't try to replicate. If false claims about your brand are your top concern, that feature alone may justify the higher price.

Frequently asked questions

Is PageLens.ai cheaper than Scrunch AI?

Yes, significantly. PageLens starts at $49/mo, while Scrunch AI's cheapest plan is $250/mo billed annually with no ongoing free tier.

Does Scrunch AI have a free plan?

No, Scrunch only offers a 7-day free trial. PageLens.ai offers a free, no-signup AI visibility audit plus paid plans starting at $49/mo.

What is Scrunch AI's biggest advantage over PageLens.ai?

Dedicated hallucination and misinformation detection, available on every Scrunch plan including Starter — a genuinely useful feature for regulated or reputation-sensitive brands that PageLens doesn't market as a standalone capability.

Was Scrunch AI acquired?

Yes. Sitecore acquired Scrunch AI for approximately $225 million in June 2026, folding its technology into Sitecore's broader digital experience platform.

Which tool actually publishes content to my website?

PageLens.ai's Growth plan drafts and publishes finished pages to a CNAME'd /feed subdirectory on your own domain. Scrunch AI's Page Audits recommend fixes to your existing pages but don't offer an equivalent done-for-you publishing workflow.

Is Scrunch AI's AXP available yet?

As of August 2026, AXP (Agent Experience Platform) — Scrunch's structured, machine-readable site layer for AI agents — is still in limited beta with select enterprise customers only.
