About Gong

Gong is a revenue intelligence platform that captures calls, emails, and meetings, then uses AI to surface insights on deal risk, rep performance, and market trends. It helps sales leaders coach reps, forecast more accurately, and replicate winning behaviors across the team.

Pricing: Custom, quote-based pricing tied to seats and deployment scope; Gong does not publish self-serve pricing and sells through annual contracts.

  • ✓Deep, well-established conversation analytics with strong transcription accuracy
  • ✓Extensive integration ecosystem across CRM, dialers, calendars, and email
  • ✓Widely adopted with mature coaching and forecasting workflows

About Chorus.ai

Chorus.ai, now operating as ZoomInfo Chorus, is a conversation intelligence platform that records, transcribes, and analyzes sales calls, meetings, and emails. It surfaces talk-track trends, competitor mentions, and deal risk signals, and feeds directly into ZoomInfo's broader go-to-market data platform for combined coaching and prospecting insights.

Pricing: Custom quote-based pricing, sold as part of ZoomInfo's platform bundles or as an add-on module; not available self-serve

  • ✓Strong transcription accuracy and searchable call library
  • ✓Deep integration with ZoomInfo's contact and intent data
  • ✓Useful coaching workflows for onboarding and rep development

Read the full Chorus.ai review →

About Clari

Clari is a revenue operations platform that gives sales leaders real-time visibility into pipeline health, forecast accuracy, and deal risk by connecting CRM, email, calendar, and call data. Enterprise revenue teams use it to replace spreadsheet-based forecasting with AI-driven insights and structured revenue cadences.

Pricing: Enterprise, custom quote-based pricing sold as annual per-seat contracts; no self-serve or free tier, and a live demo is required to get pricing.

  • ✓Highly regarded, accurate forecasting trusted by many enterprise sales organizations
  • ✓Strong executive-level pipeline visibility and reporting cadences
  • ✓Reduces manual CRM data entry through automatic activity capture
