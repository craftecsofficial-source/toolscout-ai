About Remove.bg

Remove.bg uses AI-powered image segmentation to automatically detect the subject in a photo and strip out the background in seconds, without any manual masking or selection. It offers a web app, downloadable desktop and mobile apps, browser extensions, and a developer API, making it one of the most widely integrated background-removal tools available.

Pricing: Free tier for low-res previews and limited credits; paid credit packs and subscriptions for HD/full-res downloads and API access

  • ✓Extremely fast and accurate for most common subject types
  • ✓No manual selection or masking skills required
  • ✓Deep ecosystem of integrations and an API for developers

About Slazzer

Slazzer is an AI-powered background removal tool that detects the subject in a photo and cuts it out from its background with a single click. It offers a web app, desktop apps, Photoshop plugin, and a developer API, making it usable both for one-off edits and for automating background removal at scale.

Pricing: Free plan with limited resolution and monthly image credits; paid subscription and pay-as-you-go API/credit plans for higher resolution and bulk processing.

  • ✓Very fast and accurate for common subjects like people and products
  • ✓Offers both a no-code app and a developer API for flexible integration
  • ✓Batch processing makes it efficient for e-commerce catalogs and bulk photo work

About Cleanup.pictures

Cleanup.pictures is a free AI-powered tool that lets users erase unwanted objects, people, watermarks, or text from a photo simply by brushing over them, with the AI filling in the background automatically. It's built for quick, no-signup photo cleanup and is popular for touching up travel photos, product shots, and social media images.

Pricing: Free to use for standard resolution with unlimited basic edits; a paid Pro tier (via the Clipdrop ecosystem) unlocks higher resolution exports and additional credits.

  • ✓Genuinely free and usable instantly without creating an account
  • ✓Simple, intuitive brush interface that anyone can use in seconds
  • ✓Produces convincing inpainting results for common cases like removing people or small objects
