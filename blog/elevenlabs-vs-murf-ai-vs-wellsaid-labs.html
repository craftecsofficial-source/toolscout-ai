About Murf AI

Murf is a browser-based voiceover studio with 200+ AI voices, pitch, speed and pronunciation controls, a voice changer and AI dubbing. Murf also sells text-to-speech APIs and conversational voice agents, and integrates with Canva, PowerPoint, Google Slides and Adobe Captivate.

Pricing: Free plan available; paid plans last listed from $19/mo billed yearly ($29 monthly) - could not re-verify on live pricing page

About WellSaid Labs

WellSaid (wellsaid.io, formerly WellSaid Labs) turns scripts into voiceover in a web studio using voices modeled on professional voice actors. It publishes self-serve plans for individuals and teams, an API, and Adobe integrations.

Pricing: Free trial (3 download minutes/mo, no commercial rights); Starter $10/mo billed yearly or $19 monthly; Pro $33/mo yearly or $49 monthly; Business $160/user/mo billed yearly; Enterprise custom.
