About Midjourney

Midjourney is widely regarded as the most visually striking image generator, popular with designers and artists for concept art, illustration and mood boards.

Pricing: From $10/mo (Basic); no free trial; annual billing saves 20%

About DALL-E 3

DALL-E 3 generated images directly from ChatGPT conversations, making it easy to iterate on an idea in plain language. OpenAI retired it on May 12, 2026 in favor of its successor, GPT Image 2.

Pricing: Retired May 12, 2026; was included with ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo) or ~$0.04-0.08/image via API — succeeded by GPT Image 2

  • ✓Was the most beginner-friendly image generator available
  • ✓Conversational refinement made iteration easy
  • ✓Bundled free with any ChatGPT Plus subscription

About Adobe Firefly

Firefly is trained on licensed and public-domain content, which makes it a preferred choice for teams that need commercially safe assets inside Photoshop and Illustrator, and now also provides access to partner models like Gemini and GPT Image.

Pricing: Free tier available; paid plans from $9.99/mo (Standard)

  • ✓The safe default for brand-safe, commercially licensed AI images
  • ✓Deep integration with the Adobe apps teams already use
  • ✓One subscription for Adobe's models plus major third-party partner models

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper: Midjourney, DALL-E 3 or Adobe Firefly?

Based on published pricing: Midjourney (From $10/mo (Basic); no free trial; annual billing saves 20%); DALL-E 3 (Retired May 12, 2026; was included with ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo) or ~$0.04-0.08/image via API — succeeded by GPT Image 2); Adobe Firefly (Free tier available; paid plans from $9.99/mo (Standard)). Exact value depends on your usage volume and which features you actually need, so check each tool's full pricing breakdown before deciding.

Which of these three has the best reviews?

Midjourney shows G2 4.4/5 (102 reviews). DALL-E 3 shows G2 4.1/5 (listed as "DALL·E", covering both DALL-E 2 and 3) (60 reviews). Adobe Firefly shows G2 4.4/5 (357 reviews). Weigh this alongside the feature and pricing comparison above, not as the only factor.

Which has the most features: Midjourney, DALL-E 3 or Adobe Firefly?

Of the three, Midjourney lists the most features in our comparison above, though more features doesn't automatically mean it's the better fit — a simpler, more focused tool can win for a specific use case.

Can I use more than one of these image generation tools together?

Yes — many teams run more than one image generation tool for different parts of their workflow rather than picking a single winner. Start with whichever one matches your primary use case, and add a second only if you hit a real gap.
