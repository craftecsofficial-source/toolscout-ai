About Napkin

Napkin (napkin.ai) generates diagrams, flowcharts, mind maps, infographics and data charts from pasted or imported text. You choose among generated layouts instead of writing prompts, then edit colors, fonts, icons and elements. It also has a beta Slides feature for building decks.

Pricing: Free ($0, 500 AI credits/week, PNG and PDF export, Napkin branding on visuals). Plus $9/user/month (10,000 credits/month, PPT and SVG export, branding removed, 3 Brand Styles). Pro $22/user/month (30,000 credits/month, custom branding, font uploads). Annual billing saves 25%. Enterprise: contact sales.

  • ✓Fast route from existing prose to a presentable visual
  • ✓Free plan allows unlimited editing and PNG/PDF export
  • ✓Option-picking instead of prompting keeps results predictable

About Plus AI

Plus AI (from Plus Docs, Inc.) is an add-on that creates, rewrites and remixes slides directly in Google Slides and PowerPoint, and can turn documents into presentations. It also offers a Presentation API and an MCP server for connecting AI assistants.

Pricing: No free plan; every plan starts with a 7-day trial (1,000 credits, card required, new customers only). Billed annually per user/month: Basic $10 (1,500 credits), Pro $20 (3,000), Team $30 (6,000), Max $200 (unlimited). Monthly billing is $15/$25/$40/$240. Enterprise: contact sales.

  • ✓Works inside the slide tools you already use
  • ✓Credit-based plans make cost predictable per task
  • ✓Document-to-deck and remix of existing slides, not just prompt generation

About Decktopus

Decktopus generates a full slide deck (structure, copy, layout) from a topic, notes or uploaded file, applies your brand colors, fonts and logo, and adds a rehearsal coach called Loop plus shareable links that show viewer activity. Exports to PDF, PPT and PNG.

Pricing: No permanent free plan: new accounts get a small amount of free credits (enough for about a 3-slide deck). Pro $9.99/mo billed annually ($119.88/yr), $19.99/mo billed every 3 months, or $24.99/mo monthly, with 750 AI credits/mo (about 150 slides). Business adds team features and 1,000 credits per seat (price not listed publicly). Enterprise is custom. Students get 50% off.

  • ✓Produces a complete first-draft deck, not just an empty template
  • ✓Loop rehearsal coaching is uncommon among slide generators
  • ✓Share links with viewer tracking and an email gate suit sales and consulting decks

Quick picks

  • ✓Napkin — Writers, consultants, marketers and PMs who already have text and need diagrams or infographics for docs, posts and slides.
  • ✓Plus AI — Business users and teams who build decks in Google Slides or PowerPoint and want AI drafting and rewriting without leaving them.
  • ✓Decktopus — Consultants, founders, coaches and educators who need a decent on-brand deck quickly, want to rehearse the talk, and like tracking who opens a shared deck.
