About Napkin

Napkin (napkin.ai) is an AI tool that reads text you already have — notes, blog posts, pitches, reports — and automatically converts it into diagrams, flowcharts, and infographics. Instead of starting from a blank canvas, users paste in prose and Napkin suggests multiple visual layouts to represent the ideas, which can then be styled, edited, and exported for use in decks or documents.

Pricing: Free plan with limited monthly visual generations; paid Pro/Plus plans (roughly $10-15/user/month billed annually) unlock unlimited visuals, more styles, and custom branding; Enterprise/Team pricing is custom with SSO and admin controls.

  • ✓Extremely fast way to visualize ideas you've already written down
  • ✓No design or diagramming skill required to get a polished result
  • ✓Generates several visual options per prompt so you can pick the best fit

About Plus AI

Plus AI is an AI presentation assistant delivered as a native add-on for Google Slides and PowerPoint, so users never have to leave the tool they already work in. It can generate a full deck from a text prompt or outline, and can also take an existing rough deck and redesign it using consultant-style layouts and frameworks (like SWOT, BCG matrix, and timeline templates).

Pricing: Free limited plan for basic generation; Pro/individual plans are roughly $10-20/user/month billed annually; Team/Business plans add more seats, shared brand templates, and admin controls; Enterprise pricing is custom.

  • ✓Works inside the presentation tools people already use, with no need to switch platforms
  • ✓Redesign feature is useful for cleaning up existing rough decks, not just generating new ones
  • ✓Consultant-style templates give a polished, professional look quickly

About Decktopus

Decktopus is an AI presentation platform that generates a complete slide deck - structure, copy, and design - from a single topic prompt or uploaded file, then applies your brand automatically from your website URL. It also includes Loop, a built-in AI rehearsal coach that lets presenters practice delivery and get feedback on pacing and clarity before they present live.

Pricing: Free trial to start; Pro from $9.99/mo billed annually ($24.99/mo month-to-month) with 750 AI credits; Business adds team features and 1,000 credits per seat; Enterprise is custom-priced.

  • ✓Generates a full deck (content plus design) in one pass, not just a template shell
  • ✓Loop's rehearsal/coaching feature is a genuinely different angle from most slide generators
  • ✓Brand Kit auto-detection saves manual re-theming for company decks
