About Notion AI

Notion AI drafts, summarizes and answers questions using the content already inside your workspace, and its Notion Agent can complete multi-step tasks grounded in your team's real notes, docs and connected apps.

Pricing: Free plan available; paid plans from $10/mo per seat (Plus); full Notion AI bundled into Business at $20/mo per seat

  • ✓AI is grounded in your team's real content, not a context-free chatbot
  • ✓Notion Agent executes tasks, not just drafts text

About ClickUp AI

ClickUp AI (ClickUp Brain) summarizes tasks, drafts updates and answers questions about project status directly inside ClickUp's task and docs system, with access to multiple frontier models under one subscription.

Pricing: Free tier available; ClickUp Brain AI add-on from $9/seat/mo (annual) on top of a paid plan; Everything AI tier at $28/seat/mo

About Motion

Motion automatically schedules tasks, meetings and deadlines into your calendar, re-planning your day whenever something changes.

Pricing: Individual plans from $19/seat/mo (Pro AI, billed annually) or $29/seat/mo (Business AI); no free plan

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper: Notion AI, ClickUp AI or Motion?

Based on published pricing: Notion AI (Free plan available; paid plans from $10/mo per seat (Plus); full Notion AI bundled into Business at $20/mo per seat); ClickUp AI (Free tier available; ClickUp Brain AI add-on from $9/seat/mo (annual) on top of a paid plan; Everything AI tier at $28/seat/mo); Motion (Individual plans from $19/seat/mo (Pro AI, billed annually) or $29/seat/mo (Business AI); no free plan). Exact value depends on your usage volume and which features you actually need, so check each tool's full pricing breakdown before deciding.

Notion AI shows G2 4.7/5 (11,962 reviews (Notion overall workspace)). ClickUp AI shows G2 4.7/5 (11,500+ reviews). Motion shows G2 4.1/5 (157 reviews). Weigh this alongside the feature and pricing comparison above, not as the only factor.

Which has the most features: Notion AI, ClickUp AI or Motion?

Of the three, Notion AI lists the most features in our comparison above, though more features doesn't automatically mean it's the better fit — a simpler, more focused tool can win for a specific use case.

Can I use more than one of these productivity & automation tools together?

Yes — many teams run more than one productivity & automation tool for different parts of their workflow rather than picking a single winner. Start with whichever one matches your primary use case, and add a second only if you hit a real gap.
