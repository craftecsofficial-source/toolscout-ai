About Notion AI

Notion AI adds writing help, workspace Q&A, meeting notes, search and a multi-step agent inside Notion, working on the pages and connected apps you already have.

Pricing: Free and Plus include only a limited AI trial; full Agent, AI Meeting Notes and Enterprise Search on Business at $20/member/mo; Custom Agents $10 per 1,000 credits

  • ✓Works directly on your existing Notion content
  • ✓No separate tool or bot to add
  • ✓Agents can do tasks, not just draft text

About ClickUp AI

ClickUp Brain is the AI layer inside the ClickUp work platform. It answers questions from your tasks, docs, chats and connected apps, drafts content, runs meeting notes, and lets you switch between models such as Claude, GPT and Gemini.

Pricing: Free plan to try; Brain AI add-on $9/user/mo; Everything AI $28/user/mo (billing period not stated on the page); requires a ClickUp workspace

  • ✓Model choice under one subscription instead of several separate AI accounts
  • ✓Works directly on your real tasks, docs and chats, so little manual briefing
  • ✓ClickUp states customer data is not used to train third-party models

About Motion

Motion automatically schedules tasks, meetings and deadlines into your calendar, re-planning your day whenever something changes.

Pricing: Individual plans from $19/seat/mo (Pro AI, billed annually) or $29/seat/mo (Business AI); no free plan
