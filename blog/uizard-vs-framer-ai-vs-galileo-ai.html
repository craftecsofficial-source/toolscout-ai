About Uizard

Uizard is an AI-driven UI/UX design platform that lets anyone create app and website mockups without traditional design skills. It can convert hand-drawn wireframes, screenshots of existing apps, or plain text prompts into editable, interactive prototypes in minutes.

Pricing: Free plan for a single project; paid Pro and Business plans starting around $19/month (billed monthly, cheaper annually), plus custom Enterprise pricing

  • ✓Extremely fast way to get from idea to a visual mockup, even for non-designers
  • ✓Multiple AI entry points (text, sketch, screenshot) fit different workflows
  • ✓Approachable interface with a shallow learning curve compared to full design tools

About Framer AI

Framer AI is a built-in feature of the Framer website builder that generates a complete, responsive website from a short text description of the business or project. The generated site is fully editable in Framer's visual, no-code canvas, letting users refine layout, content, and design before publishing with Framer's own hosting.

Pricing: Free to start building; paid plans required to publish on a custom domain, starting at a low monthly cost with higher tiers for more pages, CMS content, and traffic

  • ✓Goes from idea to a real, editable website in minutes instead of hours
  • ✓Generated designs are genuinely usable, not just rough placeholders
  • ✓Seamlessly hands off into Framer's powerful full design and animation toolkit

Read the full Framer AI review →

About Galileo AI

Galileo AI is a design tool that generates high-fidelity, editable user interface designs from simple text prompts, aiming to compress the early UI design process from hours to seconds. It gained attention for producing visually detailed, near-production-quality mockups rather than rough wireframes, but its standalone availability has been limited following its founding team's acquihire, with access largely gated behind a waitlist.

Pricing: Historically offered limited free access with a waitlist; broader public self-serve pricing has not been consistently available since the team's move to Google

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper: Uizard, Framer AI or Galileo AI?

Based on published pricing: Uizard (Free plan for a single project; paid Pro and Business plans starting around $19/month (billed monthly, cheaper annually), plus custom Enterprise pricing); Framer AI (Free to start building; paid plans required to publish on a custom domain, starting at a low monthly cost with higher tiers for more pages, CMS content, and traffic); Galileo AI (Historically offered limited free access with a waitlist; broader public self-serve pricing has not been consistently available since the team's move to Google). Exact value depends on your usage volume and which features you actually need, so check each tool's full pricing breakdown before deciding.

Which of these three has the best reviews?

Uizard shows G2 4.3/5 (About 100 reviews). Framer AI shows G2 4.7/5 (Over 150 reviews). Weigh this alongside the feature and pricing comparison above, not as the only factor.

Which has the most features: Uizard, Framer AI or Galileo AI?

Of the three, Uizard lists the most features in our comparison above, though more features doesn't automatically mean it's the better fit — a simpler, more focused tool can win for a specific use case.

Can I use more than one of these design tools together?

Yes — many teams run more than one design tool for different parts of their workflow rather than picking a single winner. Start with whichever one matches your primary use case, and add a second only if you hit a real gap.
