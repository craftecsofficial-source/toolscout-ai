About DataRobot

DataRobot is an enterprise AI platform covering predictive machine learning (AutoML), generative and agentic AI, governance and observability. Its homepage now leads with an agent platform, and it is sold mainly through sales, with a 30-day free trial advertised.

Pricing: No public pricing; sold through sales (custom quote). A 30-day free trial with full platform access is advertised; post-trial cost and trial limits are not published.

  • ✓Covers predictive, generative and agentic AI plus governance in one vendor
  • ✓Governance and observability suit regulated industries
  • ✓30-day free trial with full platform access advertised

About H2O.ai

H2O.ai sells enterprise AI software (Driverless AI for AutoML, h2oGPTe for private generative AI, Document AI, MLOps and more) on top of its open-source H2O-3 library. Pricing is by annual license and is not published.

Pricing: H2O-3 is open source and free to self-host. Commercial products (Driverless AI, h2oGPTe, etc.) are not priced publicly: the site mentions a single annual license based on compute and memory, and directs buyers to contact sales or request a demo.

  • ✓Free, mature open-source core (H2O-3) lets teams start without a contract
  • ✓Wide enterprise stack covering predictive AI, GenAI, evaluation and MLOps
  • ✓Deployment can be fully on-premises or air-gapped, which suits strict data rules

About Akkio

Akkio is an enterprise AI analytics platform now aimed at media agencies and data providers, covering campaign strategy, audience building, propensity and media-mix modeling, and performance measurement, with chat-with-data and no-code modeling tools.

Pricing: Enterprise only, custom pricing - contact sales. No public plans, dollar amounts or trial listed on the pricing page (checked 2026-10-26).
