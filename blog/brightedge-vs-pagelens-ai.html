BrightEdge vs PageLens.ai: Legacy SEO Data Depth or Modern AEO Simplicity?

BrightEdge extends nearly two decades of proprietary search data into AI visibility. PageLens.ai was built for the AI-answer era from day one.

Our pick: PageLens.ai

BrightEdge's Data Cube is a genuinely hard-to-replicate asset, but it comes at a high five-to-six-figure annual cost, a steep learning curve, and no self-serve trial. For teams that just need to track and act on AI visibility, PageLens.ai gets there in minutes at a fraction of the price.

Depth of data vs. speed to value

BrightEdge's Data Cube — a proprietary search data warehouse spanning 170 countries, built since 2007 — is widely regarded as difficult to replicate, and AI Hyper Cube (launched March 2026) extends that same depth into AI-citation tracking. That's a real asset for enterprise teams that need the deepest possible historical context.

PageLens doesn't have two decades of search data behind it, but it doesn't need to: its focus is running today's buyer prompts against today's AI engines and turning the gaps into published content, which you can start doing within minutes rather than after an enterprise onboarding process.

Pricing and access

BrightEdge sells through a custom, credit-based model — roughly one credit per keyword per SERP tracked — with no published pricing and no self-serve trial; mid-market quotes commonly run $40,000 to $80,000+ per year before add-ons. PageLens publishes every price and lets you start with a free audit and a $49/mo plan, no contract required.

Usability

BrightEdge reviewers consistently cite a steep learning curve and a complex UI with functionality spread across many tools (Data Cube, AI Catalyst, AI Hyper Cube, ContentIQ, Autopilot, Copilot, Instant). PageLens is intentionally narrow in scope, which keeps the learning curve short.

Who should still pick BrightEdge

If you're managing 2,000+ pages across multiple domains and international markets, and you have (or can hire) a dedicated SEO team of three or more to work with a BrightEdge customer success manager, its data depth and enterprise track record — including use by a majority of the Fortune 100 — deliver real ROI at that scale.

In fairness to BrightEdge

BrightEdge's Data Cube is a real, hard-to-replicate asset built over nearly two decades — for enterprises that need that scale and already have the SEO team to run it, that depth is worth taking seriously.

Frequently asked questions

Is PageLens.ai cheaper than BrightEdge?

Yes, substantially. PageLens starts at $49/mo with published pricing, while BrightEdge's custom, credit-based pricing is estimated at $40,000 to $80,000+ per year by industry sources.

Does BrightEdge offer a free trial?

No, BrightEdge does not offer a self-serve free trial; it sells through an annual, sales-assisted contract. PageLens.ai offers a free AI visibility audit with no signup.

What is BrightEdge's biggest advantage?

Data Cube, its proprietary search data warehouse built since 2007, and the newer AI Hyper Cube (launched March 2026) that extends that depth into AI-citation tracking — a data asset that's genuinely difficult for newer AEO tools to replicate.

Which tool is easier to use?

PageLens.ai. BrightEdge reviewers consistently describe a steep learning curve and a cluttered UI spread across many modules, while PageLens is built for a small team to start using within minutes.

Does BrightEdge have real user reviews?

Yes, a substantial base: roughly 4.4/5 on G2 across 800-830 reviews, and 4.1/5 on Capterra across about 45 reviews. PageLens.ai is too new to have an equivalent review history.

Which tool actually publishes content to my site?

PageLens.ai's Growth plan ships finished, citation-ready pages to a /feed subdirectory on your own domain. BrightEdge's Copilot generates content recommendations and ContentIQ audits technical issues, but neither offers an equivalent built-in publishing workflow.
