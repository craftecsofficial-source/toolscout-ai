About HubSpot AI Tools

HubSpot bakes AI into its marketing hub for drafting campaigns, summarizing customer data and, more recently, tracking visibility inside AI search answers, all operating on live CRM data rather than generic prompts.

Pricing: Free CRM tier; Starter from ~$15-20/mo per seat, Pro/Enterprise scale into hundreds-thousands/mo

  • ✓AI acts on live CRM data, not generic prompts
  • ✓Free CRM tier includes some Breeze AI features permanently
  • ✓Unifies marketing, sales and service AI in a single data layer

About Simplified

Simplified is an all-in-one AI marketing platform that unifies content creation, design, video, social scheduling, and campaign automation, letting marketers brief an AI agent once and get finished, on-brand campaigns back.

Pricing: Free plan available; Starter from $79/mo (campaign-based); Enterprise custom

  • ✓Combines design, writing, video, and social scheduling in one subscription instead of stitching together separate tools
  • ✓Riley agent produces full campaign assets (copy + design + scheduling) from a single brief, reducing tool switching
  • ✓Broad platform reach: web app, Chrome extension, WordPress plugin, iOS/Android, Shopify app

About Frase

Frase researches, drafts, optimizes, publishes, and monitors content in one workflow, tracking rankings on Google alongside citations in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini.

Pricing: 7-day free trial; Starter from $39/mo (billed yearly), Professional $103/mo, Scale $239/mo, custom Enterprise

Yes — many teams run more than one marketing & seo tool for different parts of their workflow rather than picking a single winner. Start with whichever one matches your primary use case, and add a second only if you hit a real gap.
