About Dify

Dify lets teams build AI agents and workflows on a visual canvas, ingest documents into a retrieval pipeline, plug in models and tools through a plugin marketplace, and publish apps as hosted pages, APIs, embeds or MCP-compatible tools. You can use Dify Cloud, self-host the Community Edition with Docker, or run Dify Enterprise in your own cloud.

Pricing: Community Edition is free to self-host. Dify Cloud: Sandbox free (200 message credits), Professional $590 per workspace per year, Team $1,590 per workspace per year (annual billing only listed). Enterprise is custom-priced.

  • ✓Self-hostable, so data and infrastructure can stay under your control
  • ✓Visual canvas covers agents, RAG and workflows in one product
  • ✓Free Community Edition and a free cloud Sandbox for evaluation

About Stack AI

StackAI is a drag-and-drop platform for building AI agents and workflows that connect to company data, with human-approval steps, audit logs and options to deploy in a VPC or on-premise. It targets IT teams and regulated industries; the public pricing page lists only a free plan and a custom Enterprise plan.

Pricing: Free plan at $0/month (500 runs/month, 2 projects, 1 seat); Enterprise is custom-quoted. No public mid-tier prices - see the official pricing page.

  • ✓Governance tooling (audit logs, approvals, versioning) built in
  • ✓Deployment choices include VPC and on-prem
  • ✓Model-agnostic with many integrations

About BuildShip

BuildShip is a visual, low-code platform for building backend logic, APIs, and automated workflows using a node-based canvas, with built-in support for AI integrations like OpenAI and other LLMs. It lets developers and makers assemble server-side functionality - endpoints, scheduled jobs, database operations, and AI pipelines - without managing infrastructure directly.

Pricing: Free plan available with generous limits for personal projects; paid Pro and Team plans start around $25/month with higher execution limits, and custom Enterprise pricing is available.

  • ✓Fast way to stand up working backend APIs without managing servers
  • ✓AI nodes make it easy to add LLM-powered steps into automation pipelines
  • ✓Supports dropping into custom JavaScript/TypeScript code when visual nodes aren't enough
