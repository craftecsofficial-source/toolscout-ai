About BGEraser

BgEraser is a free browser-based AI tool that strips backgrounds from product photos, portraits, and graphics in seconds, with batch processing for up to 20 images and no account required.

Pricing: 100% free web tool with unlimited use and batch processing up to 20 images; optional desktop app: Monthly $5, Yearly $19.99, Lifetime $29.99

  • ✓Completely free with no watermark, credit limit, or forced sign-up

About Pebblely

Pebblely is an AI product photography tool that removes backgrounds from uploaded product images and replaces them with AI-generated scenes, lighting, and shadows for ecommerce listings and ads.

Pricing: Free plan available (limited monthly images, no card required); paid plans from $9/mo (Lite) up to $39/mo (Pro), with 2+ months free on annual billing

  • ✓Fast, cheap alternative to a full product photo shoot
  • ✓Bulk generation suits large multi-SKU catalogs
  • ✓Direct Shopify integration for in-admin generation

About PhotoPacks.AI

PhotoPacks.AI generates 40+ professional headshots from about 10 uploaded everyday photos, delivering results in under an hour for a flat one-time fee, aimed at LinkedIn, resumes, and other professional profiles.

Exact value depends on your usage volume and which features you actually need, so check each tool's full pricing breakdown before deciding.

None of BGEraser, Pebblely or PhotoPacks.AI has a review count we could independently verify on G2, Capterra or Trustpilot as of writing, so lean on the feature and pricing comparison above along with your own trial.

Yes — many teams run more than one image generation tool for different parts of their workflow rather than picking a single winner. Start with whichever one matches your primary use case, and add a second only if you hit a real gap.
