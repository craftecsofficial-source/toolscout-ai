Which is cheaper: ChatGPT, Claude or Google Gemini?

Exact value depends on your usage volume and which features you actually need, so check each tool's full pricing breakdown before deciding.

Which of these three has the best reviews?

ChatGPT shows G2 4.7/5 (834 reviews). Claude shows G2 4.8/5 (~70 reviews). Google Gemini shows G2 4.4/5 (323 reviews). Weigh this alongside the feature and pricing comparison above, not as the only factor.

Which has the most features: ChatGPT, Claude or Google Gemini?

Of the three, ChatGPT lists the most features in our comparison above, though more features doesn't automatically mean it's the better fit — a simpler, more focused tool can win for a specific use case.

Can I use more than one of these chatbots & assistants tools together?

Yes — many teams run more than one chatbots & assistants tool for different parts of their workflow rather than picking a single winner. Start with whichever one matches your primary use case, and add a second only if you hit a real gap.
