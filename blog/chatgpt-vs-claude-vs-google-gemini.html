About Google Gemini

Gemini is Google's AI assistant, available free with a Google account and in paid Google AI Plus, Pro and Ultra plans that also bundle Google storage. It works across Google apps and has a 1-million-token context window.

Pricing: Free with a Google account; AI Plus $4.99/mo; AI Pro $19.99/mo; AI Ultra from $99.99/mo ($199.99/mo top tier)
