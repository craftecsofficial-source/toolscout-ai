About Gorgias

Gorgias is an ecommerce customer experience platform that unifies email, chat, social, SMS, and voice into one helpdesk inbox, with an AI Agent that autonomously resolves order and support questions using live store data.

Pricing: Starter $10/mo (50 tickets) up to Advanced $900/mo (5,000 tickets); AI Agent billed separately at $0.90-$1.00 per resolution; Enterprise custom

  • ✓Deep native Shopify integration lets agents edit orders and issue refunds without leaving the inbox
  • ✓Ticket-based pricing includes unlimited agent seats rather than charging per seat
  • ✓Strong reputation on G2 with a 4.6/5 rating across 547 reviews

About LivePerson

LivePerson's Conversational Cloud is an enterprise platform that unifies AI chatbots, live agents, and messaging across web, voice, and channels like WhatsApp and Apple Messages, automating and analyzing large-scale customer conversations.

Pricing: Custom quotes only across Bronze, Silver, and Gold tiers; enterprise contracts commonly range from roughly $40K to $110K+ per year

About Ultimate

Ultimate builds custom conversational AI agents that automate customer service across chat, messaging, and email in 100+ languages. Acquired by Zendesk in March 2024, it now ships as Zendesk AI Agents.

Pricing: No public self-serve pricing; custom enterprise quotes via Zendesk, billed per automated resolution ($1.50-$2.00) on top of a Zendesk Suite subscription

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper: Gorgias, LivePerson or Ultimate?

Based on published pricing: Gorgias (Starter $10/mo (50 tickets) up to Advanced $900/mo (5,000 tickets); AI Agent billed separately at $0.90-$1.00 per resolution; Enterprise custom); LivePerson (Custom quotes only across Bronze, Silver, and Gold tiers; enterprise contracts commonly range from roughly $40K to $110K+ per year); Ultimate (No public self-serve pricing; custom enterprise quotes via Zendesk, billed per automated resolution ($1.50-$2.00) on top of a Zendesk Suite subscription). Exact value depends on your usage volume and which features you actually need, so check each tool's full pricing breakdown before deciding.

Which of these three has the best reviews?

Gorgias shows G2 4.6/5 (547 reviews). LivePerson shows G2 4.2/5 (156 reviews). Weigh this alongside the feature and pricing comparison above, not as the only factor.

Which has the most features: Gorgias, LivePerson or Ultimate?

Of the three, Gorgias lists the most features in our comparison above, though more features doesn't automatically mean it's the better fit — a simpler, more focused tool can win for a specific use case.

Can I use more than one of these customer support tools together?

Yes — many teams run more than one customer support tool for different parts of their workflow rather than picking a single winner. Start with whichever one matches your primary use case, and add a second only if you hit a real gap.
