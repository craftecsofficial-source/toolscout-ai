Perplexity pairs an AI model with live web search and inline citations, positioning itself as a research-first alternative to traditional search engines, and now also ships its own AI-native browser, Comet.

About Poe

Poe is Quora's multi-model AI platform that gives users unified access to 100+ AI models — including GPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok — plus millions of user-built bots, all through a single chat interface and subscription.

Pricing: Free tier with daily limits; paid plans from $4.99/mo up to $249.99/mo based on monthly compute points

  • ✓Consolidates GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok and more into one subscription
  • ✓Custom bot builder lets anyone package a prompt into a shareable bot
  • ✓Fast, clean interface available on every major platform

About HARPA AI

HARPA AI is a Chrome extension that brings GPT, Claude, Gemini and other models directly into any webpage, letting users automate browsing tasks, monitor prices, extract data, and generate content without leaving their browser.

Pricing: Free demo tier; paid plans from $12/mo (billed annually) up to $19/mo, plus a $240 lifetime option

  • ✓Combines GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek and Perplexity in one extension for less than any single subscription
  • ✓Deep browser automation goes beyond simple chat — scraping, monitoring, form-filling
  • ✓Privacy-oriented: keeps data local, doesn't store logs

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper: Perplexity, Poe or HARPA AI?

Based on published pricing: Perplexity (Free tier available; Pro from $20/mo, Max at $200/mo); Poe (Free tier with daily limits; paid plans from $4.99/mo up to $249.99/mo based on monthly compute points); HARPA AI (Free demo tier; paid plans from $12/mo (billed annually) up to $19/mo, plus a $240 lifetime option). Exact value depends on your usage volume and which features you actually need, so check each tool's full pricing breakdown before deciding.

Which of these three has the best reviews?

Perplexity shows G2 4.4/5 (326 reviews). Poe shows G2 4.5/5 (18 reviews). HARPA AI shows Chrome Web Store 4.7/5 (3,200+ ratings). Weigh this alongside the feature and pricing comparison above, not as the only factor.

Which has the most features: Perplexity, Poe or HARPA AI?

Of the three, Poe lists the most features in our comparison above, though more features doesn't automatically mean it's the better fit — a simpler, more focused tool can win for a specific use case.

Can I use more than one of these chatbots & assistants tools together?

Yes — many teams run more than one chatbots & assistants tool for different parts of their workflow rather than picking a single winner. Start with whichever one matches your primary use case, and add a second only if you hit a real gap.
