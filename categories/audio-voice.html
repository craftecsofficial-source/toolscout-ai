CrystalSound

Audio & Voice

AI noise cancellation and meeting intelligence for cleaner calls

CrystalSound is a desktop audio utility that strips background noise from both sides of a call using on-device deep-learning processing, then layers on screen/audio recording and automatic meeting summaries. It installs as a virtual microphone so it can sit in front of Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Discord, Slack, Loom, OBS, and other apps without needing per-platform integration. A dedicated mode can isolate a single speaker's voice while muting everyone and everything else picked up by the mic.

Pricing Offers a free/starter tier plus a paid Premium plan (billed monthly or yearly per user, exact price not published in page content, with a 7-day free trial and a 30-day money-back guarantee), a custom-quote Enterprise tier, and an upcoming Elite tier listed at $228/user/year (pre-signup discount advertised).
