SurgeGraph

AEO Tools

Low-cost AI-citation tracking plus content fixes, aimed at agencies

SurgeGraph is an answer-engine-optimization platform that tracks whether ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Mode cite a brand, scores pages for both SEO and AI-citation readiness, and generates or rewrites content from the same dashboard. Pricing Spark $32.50/mo (billed $390/yr); Scale $65.83/mo (billed $790/yr); Signature $107.50/mo (billed $1,290/yr). No free plan; 14-day money-back guarantee. Monthly-billed prices are not stated on the page (implied roughly $39 / $79 / $129) - confirm at checkout.

Geordy

AEO Tools

Generates AI-readable copies of your pages on a separate subdomain, without touching what visitors see

Geordy scans your pages or sitemap and generates machine-readable versions of them (llms.txt, schema.json, Markdown and more) served from a subdomain, so AI crawlers can parse your content while the live site stays unchanged.

Pricing Free plan (40 files/month, no card). Paid: Pro $39/mo billed annually ($49 monthly), Business $319/mo annually ($399 monthly), Growth $1,999/mo annually ($2,499 monthly, sold via sales).

ContentOptima

AEO Tools

A deterministic pass/fail grader for whether one page is citation-ready for AI search.

ContentOptima scores a single URL or pasted copy against a fixed set of pass/fail checks (the vendor says 34, split between HTML-based and AI yes/no checks) for how readable and citable it is to AI search engines, and returns a 0-100 score with copy-paste fixes.

Pricing Free $0 (3 analyses/mo, no card); Starter $29/mo (50 analyses); Agency $79/mo (200 analyses). All plans get the same checks; quotas do not roll over.
