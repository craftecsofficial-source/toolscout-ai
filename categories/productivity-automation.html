Odin AI

Productivity & Automation

Enterprise AI agents that automate cross-system business workflows

Odin AI (by One Realm, Inc.) is an enterprise agentic platform for building AI agents, automating workflows and grounding them in company data. Its Autopilot builds agents from a plain-English prompt, and it offers 200+ integrations, a knowledge base, chatbot and notetaker features, and managed-cloud, self-hosted or on-prem deployment.

Pricing Pro is $20 per seat per month. Growth Accelerator is $15,000 one-time (30-day deployment) and Scale Accelerator is $30,000 one-time (60-day deployment). Larger custom enterprise deployments are not priced publicly — contact sales. The site's $150k–$500k+ figure is its comparison for a typical custom-built route, not an Odin price.
