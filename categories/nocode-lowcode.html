Forefront AI

No-code/Low-code

Fine-tune, evaluate, and run open-source LLMs on your own data without managing GPU infrastructure.

It provides a low-code layer over the fine-tuning workflow — dataset management, training, evaluation, and serverless inference endpoints — so teams can customize open-source models on proprietary data without building their own ML infrastructure.
