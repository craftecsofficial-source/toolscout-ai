Rewording.io

Writing & Content

AI paraphrasing and rewriting for essays, articles, and everyday text

Rewording.io is a browser-based AI paraphrasing tool that rewrites sentences, paragraphs, essays, and articles while preserving their original meaning. It lets users dial in how heavily text gets rephrased and pick a tone for the output, alongside companion utilities for checking AI-generated content, plagiarism, and grammar. The tool targets anyone who needs to quickly restructure existing writing rather than compose new text from scratch.

Pricing Freemium: a free tier handles basic paraphrasing with limited capacity, while paid access is sold as pay-as-you-go credits or a monthly/quarterly/yearly subscription for higher limits and extra tools — exact dollar amounts are not published in an easily accessible form on the site.
