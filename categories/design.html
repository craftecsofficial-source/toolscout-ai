NewTattoo.ai

Design

AI-generated tattoo concepts you can preview on your own skin before committing

NewTattoo.ai is a browser-based tool that generates tattoo artwork from text prompts and previews designs on a photo of your body, with a lettering generator and an idea gallery. It is aimed at people planning a tattoo and at artists mocking up concepts.

Pricing Free to start (the site does not list the free-tier limits). One paid plan, Pro Unlimited: $9.99 monthly, $19.99 quarterly or $47.99 annually, in USD, cancel anytime, with a money-back guarantee for trial use of up to 25 images. Checked on the live pricing page 2026-11-03.

Autodraw

Design

Google's free AI tool that turns rough sketches into polished icons instantly

Autodraw is a free browser-based drawing tool from Google that uses machine learning to recognize what you're trying to draw and suggests clean, professional icon-style replacements in real time. It pairs a simple sketch canvas with AI-powered shape matching and basic drawing/coloring tools, making it useful for anyone who needs quick clip art without any design skill.

Pricing Free, no account or sign-up required. The site lists no paid plans (we could only read the page's metadata, so this is not confirmed from on-page pricing text).

Cleanup.pictures

Design

Browser tool for erasing objects, people, text and watermarks from photos

Cleanup.pictures is a web-based AI inpainting tool: brush over something unwanted in a photo and it fills the area in. The free tier is limited to 720p exports; the Pro plan removes the resolution cap and adds a refiner, and the same company's Clipdrop Pro bundles it with other tools.

Pricing Free (unlimited images, exports limited to 720p); Pro from $3/month (the site's FAQ lists $5/month or $36/year; the page's headline figure and FAQ differ, so confirm at checkout); Clipdrop Pro from 11 (currency and period not stated); API usage-based.
