Fishbowl is a strong fit for small and midsize distributors, ecommerce sellers and manufacturers that want proper inventory and warehouse control at a published, per-user price. It is a poor fit for single-location shops with simple stock, or for large enterprises that need a full ERP. The software tracks stock across locations, handles purchasing and orders, and adds manufacturing (bills of materials, and on the Advanced edition MRP, routing and lot genealogy). Its AI assistant, Athena, answers questions from your live data and drafts purchase orders, transfers and sales orders that you approve before anything is sent.

Best for

Good for: distributors and ecommerce sellers managing multi-location stock, and small manufacturers needing BOMs and production tracking. Not for: tiny businesses with a handful of SKUs, companies wanting a free tier or self-serve trial, or large enterprises needing full ERP financials and deep customization.

Key features

01

Multi-Location Inventory Tracking

Tracks stock levels, transfers, and order status in real time across multiple warehouses or locations.

02

AI-Powered Forecasting & Reorder Points

Uses historical sales and purchasing data to recommend smart reorder points and forecast future demand automatically.

03

Fishbowl AI Insights (Athena)

An AI assistant and reporting layer that analyzes sales, inventory, costing, and manufacturing data to surface trends and answer business questions in plain language.

04

Manufacturing & BOM Management

Supports bills of materials, work orders, and production tracking for businesses that assemble or manufacture products.

05

QuickBooks & Xero Integration

Syncs inventory, sales, and purchasing data directly with QuickBooks or Xero, keeping accounting records aligned with warehouse activity.

06

Barcode Scanning & Automated Purchasing

Enables barcode-based receiving and picking, plus automated purchase order workflows tied to reorder thresholds.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Published per-user starting prices let you budget before talking to sales.
  • Tiered editions (Essentials to Advanced Manufacturing) mean small shops are not forced into warehouse or MRP complexity.
  • The AI assistant drafts orders and transfers from live data but every draft needs approval, which limits the risk of bad automated purchases.
  • An implementation package is included with every purchase, according to the pricing page.

Cons

  • The Advanced Warehouse and Advanced Manufacturing prices are only 'from' figures; final cost depends on users and deployment.
  • The pricing page lists a demo or quote, not a free trial, so you cannot test it hands-on first.
  • Add-ons such as the B2B portal, custom reports and advanced integrations have no listed prices, so total cost can exceed the base plan.
  • The vendor's own chat assistant warns its numbers should be verified; treat AI outputs as drafts, not facts.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Fishbowl cost?

Published starting prices, billed annually, are $229/mo (Essentials, 2 users), $429/mo (Growth, 5 users) and $729/mo (Scale, 10 users). Advanced Warehouse starts at $595/mo and Advanced Manufacturing at $675/mo, both quoted by users and deployment.

Is there a free trial?

The pricing page does not list one. It offers a demo or quote request instead.

What does the AI feature actually do?

Fishbowl AI Insights (Athena) answers questions about reorder points, committed stock and lead times from your data, and can draft purchase orders, transfers and sales orders. Drafts wait for your approval.

Which edition do I need for manufacturing?

Basic manufacturing is offered in the standard cloud plans; Advanced Manufacturing adds MRP, routing and lot genealogy for multilevel or regulated production.

Does it work with QuickBooks or Xero?

Fishbowl has historically integrated with both, but this was not confirmed on the pages checked today, so verify with the vendor.
