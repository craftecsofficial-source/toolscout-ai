Fishbowl is an inventory and manufacturing management platform built primarily for small and midsize businesses that have outgrown basic accounting-software inventory features. It is best known for its deep, longstanding integration with QuickBooks and Xero, letting finance teams keep their existing accounting system while gaining dedicated warehouse, order, and manufacturing functionality.

Fishbowl offers two main product editions: Fishbowl Drive, a cloud-hosted version, and Fishbowl Advanced, an on-premise deployment for businesses wanting more direct control over their data and infrastructure. On top of the core inventory and manufacturing engine, Fishbowl has introduced Fishbowl AI Insights, a reporting and analytics layer powered by an AI assistant called Athena, which surfaces trends, forecasts demand, and highlights risk across sales, purchasing, and manufacturing data.

The AI layer is aimed at reducing the manual reporting work business owners and operations managers previously had to do in spreadsheets, instead giving them plain-language insights and smarter reorder recommendations directly from their inventory data.

Best for

Fishbowl is built for small and midsize businesses, particularly those already using QuickBooks or Xero, that need dedicated inventory management and light manufacturing capabilities beyond what their accounting software offers natively. It fits operations managers and business owners who want AI-assisted forecasting and reporting without building custom BI tools. Companies doing product assembly or light manufacturing benefit from its BOM and work-order features alongside standard inventory tracking. It is less suited to very large enterprises needing full-scale, multi-entity ERP systems.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The QuickBooks and Xero integrations are mature and widely used, making Fishbowl a natural upgrade path for small businesses outgrowing accounting-software-only inventory tracking.
  • Fishbowl AI Insights gives business owners forecasting and trend analysis without needing to build separate BI dashboards or hire a data analyst.
  • Combining inventory, manufacturing (BOM/work orders), and purchasing in one system reduces the need for multiple disconnected tools.
  • Offering both cloud (Drive) and on-premise (Advanced) editions gives businesses flexibility based on their IT and data-control preferences.
  • Smart reorder points and automated purchasing workflows can meaningfully cut down on manual stock-monitoring work.

Cons

  • Lack of published pricing means prospective buyers must go through a sales process to understand total cost, which can slow down evaluation.
  • The on-premise Advanced edition requires more internal IT involvement for setup, hosting, and maintenance compared to fully cloud-native competitors.
  • Some of the most valuable AI forecasting and insight features are part of the newer AI Insights module, which may carry additional cost beyond the base platform.
  • As with many inventory platforms aimed at SMBs, very large enterprises with highly complex multi-entity operations may need a more heavyweight ERP instead.
  • Migrating existing inventory and historical data into Fishbowl can require a nontrivial implementation effort for businesses with messy legacy records.

Frequently asked questions

What is Fishbowl used for?

Fishbowl is inventory and manufacturing management software used by small and midsize businesses to track stock, manage purchasing, and handle light manufacturing, typically alongside QuickBooks or Xero.

What is Fishbowl AI Insights?

Fishbowl AI Insights is an AI-powered reporting and analytics module, featuring an assistant called Athena, that analyzes sales, inventory, and manufacturing data to surface trends and forecasts.

Does Fishbowl integrate with QuickBooks?

Yes, Fishbowl has a longstanding, deep integration with QuickBooks, as well as with Xero, syncing inventory and financial data between the systems.

What's the difference between Fishbowl Drive and Fishbowl Advanced?

Fishbowl Drive is the cloud-hosted edition, while Fishbowl Advanced is an on-premise edition offering more customization and direct infrastructure control.

Is Fishbowl good for manufacturers?

Yes, Fishbowl includes manufacturing tools such as bills of materials and work order tracking, making it suitable for businesses that assemble or produce goods, not just resellers.

How much does Fishbowl cost?

Fishbowl uses custom, quote-based pricing rather than public list prices, with cost depending on the edition, number of users, and add-on modules like AI Insights.
