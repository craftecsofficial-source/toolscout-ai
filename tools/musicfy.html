Musicfy is a good fit for hobbyists, independent musicians and content creators who want to try AI voice covers, clone their own voice, or sketch songs quickly, and a risky pick for anyone who needs clear commercial rights to covers of copyrighted songs or is wary of subscription billing complaints. It is a voice-first AI music tool: you can build a voice model from your own vocals, apply one of 100,000+ voice models to a song, generate music from text, and convert a hummed melody into an instrument. The site claims 5 million+ users. Its homepage lists stem splitting as 'Coming Soon', so do not buy for that feature alone. It runs as a web app with a documented API and an affiliate program, and competes with Kits.ai, VoiceDub and Suno's cover features.

Best for

Independent musicians, DJs, streamers and hobbyists who want to experiment with AI voice covers, clone their own voice or sketch original songs without a studio. Not for labels, agencies or brands needing airtight commercial licensing for covers of copyrighted songs, nor for anyone who wants a no-friction cancellation experience, given reported billing complaints.

Key features

01

AI Voice Cloning

Upload a small clean audio sample to build a custom AI model of your own voice, usable for singing your songs or sharing with collaborators.

02

100,000+ Voice Library

Browse community and Musicfy-made AI voice models spanning genres and character styles to apply to any track.

03

AI Voice Covers

Convert an existing song's vocals into a different AI voice, including parody and celebrity-style voice options.

04

Text-to-Music

Describe a style, mood, and instrumentation in text and generate a complete original song in seconds.

05

Voice-to-Instrument

Hum or vocalize a melody and have Musicfy render it as the sound of a chosen instrument, such as guitar.

06

Stem Splitting

Isolate vocals, drums and bass for remixing. The homepage marked this 'Coming Soon' on 2026-10-31, so availability is unconfirmed.

07

Commercial Licensing

Paid plans include a commercial license for distributing generated tracks and covers on streaming platforms.

08

API Access

Documented API lets developers integrate Musicfy's voice cloning and generation into their own apps.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • A 100,000+ voice model library gives far more stylistic variety than most single-voice cloning tools.
  • Voice cloning from your own vocals is accessible to non-technical musicians.
  • Free plan needs no credit card and lets you try the core workflow.
  • Professional and Studio plans list a commercial license.
  • Extras such as text-to-music and voice-to-instrument make it more than a voice swapper.

Cons

  • Third-party reviews and forum threads report unauthorized charges and difficulty cancelling; check the refund and cancellation terms first.
  • Commercial rights for AI covers of copyrighted songs are unclear; a commercial license does not clear the underlying song rights.
  • Even Studio caps custom voices at 30, limiting agency use.
  • Upload size and dataset limits scale with plan tier.
  • The pricing page was inconsistent (sale banners, unclear original prices), and stem splitting was marked 'Coming Soon' on the homepage.

Frequently asked questions

Is Musicfy free?

There is a free plan with no card required and limited generations. Custom voice cloning is listed on paid plans.

How much do paid plans cost?

On 2026-10-31 the pricing page showed $8, $20 and $56 per month for Starter, Professional and Studio under a limited-time sale. Regular prices could not be verified, so confirm at checkout.

Does it include a commercial license?

Professional and Studio list one; Starter does not. It does not clear rights to the original songs you cover.

Can I clone my own voice?

Yes, by uploading your vocals. Slots are 2 (Starter), 6 (Professional) and 30 (Studio).

Is stem splitting available?

The homepage marks it 'Coming Soon', so check before relying on it.
