Wisecut is a good fit for creators who record long talking-style videos and want short, captioned clips with little hands-on editing; skip it if you need frame-accurate control, colour work or multi-track timelines. It transcribes your footage, finds highlights, removes silences, adds captions and reframes to vertical, square or landscape. Wish lets you ask for edits in plain text, and Autopilot with Social Hub handles scheduling and posting to connected accounts. All tools draw from one credit balance, so your usable minutes depend on which tool you run.

Best for

Good for podcasters, coaches, interview-based YouTubers, churches and small teams repurposing long recordings and livestreams. Not for professional editors who need manual timelines, colour grading or compositing, or for anyone wanting AI-generated footage rather than editing of footage they already have.

Key features

01

AI Highlight Detection

Identifies the most engaging moments in raw footage to build a rough cut automatically.

02

Auto Captions & Translations

Generates styled, editable captions and can translate them into other languages for wider reach.

03

Autocut Silence Removal

Automatically trims awkward pauses and dead air based on voice-recognition analysis of the audio track.

04

Auto Reframe

Converts footage between vertical, square, and landscape aspect ratios to fit different social platforms.

05

Auto Punch-in/out

Adds automatic zoom cuts at natural points to simulate multi-camera editing rhythm.

06

Studio Voice & Noise Cancelling

Enhances vocal clarity and removes background noise without a separate audio tool.

07

WISH

A conversational, prompt-based AI editor that lets users describe an edit in natural language and have Wisecut execute it.

08

Autopilot + Social Hub

Extends automation into auto-publishing and scheduling across multiple connected social media accounts.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Covers the common repurposing chain in one place: highlights, silence removal, captions and reframing.
  • Free tier has no credit card requirement and no watermark listed, so you can test on a real video.
  • Wish lets you request edits in plain language instead of hunting through menus.
  • Top-up credit packs last 12 months, so a plan upgrade is not forced for a busy month.
  • Social Hub scheduling is built in, saving a separate scheduler for some workflows.

Cons

  • Free plan allows only 30 minutes a month at 480p with 3-minute exports, enough to test but not to publish regularly.
  • Autopilot uses roughly twice the credits of the Editor, so the 'hours' in each plan are halved if you rely on it.
  • Monthly prices ($33 and $118) are steep for solo creators; the annual discount exists but we could not verify the annual figures.
  • It is built around automation, not manual control, so precise edits, colour correction and layered timelines are not its strength.
  • We did not verify third-party review scores today, so treat claims of speed savings as vendor-level, not independently confirmed.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The pricing page lists a free plan with 30 credits a month, 480p output, exports up to 3 minutes and a 4 GB file limit. No credit card is required to try it.

How much does it cost?

On monthly billing Starter+ is $33 and Professional+ is $118. The page says annual billing saves up to 30%, but did not show annual prices when we checked, so confirm at wisecut.ai/pricing.

How do credits work?

One balance covers the Editor, Wish and Autopilot. Roughly 1 credit is 1 processed minute in the Editor and about 2 in Autopilot. Extra packs ($25 to $429) are valid 12 months.

Is it a replacement for a full video editor?

No. It automates cuts, captions and reframing. If you need manual multi-track timelines or colour grading, use a conventional editor alongside it.

Does it publish to social media?

Autopilot and Social Hub schedule and publish to connected accounts on paid plans; Starter+ allows 1 account per network and Professional+ up to 3.
