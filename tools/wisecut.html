Wisecut is an AI-powered video editing platform that automates the repetitive parts of turning raw footage into publish-ready content. Rather than presenting a traditional manual timeline, Wisecut ingests a video, transcribes the audio, and applies automated editing rules: it removes awkward silences and filler pauses, inserts 'punch-in' zoom cuts to add visual rhythm, generates styled captions, adds AI-selected background music with automatic volume ducking, cleans up background noise, and reframes horizontal footage into vertical, square, or landscape formats for different platforms. Its newer 'WISH' feature layers a conversational, prompt-based AI editor on top of this — users can describe an edit in natural language — while 'Autopilot' extends the pipeline further into automatic social scheduling and multi-account publishing through a companion 'Social Hub.'

Wisecut's pricing runs on a shared credit system across its Editor, Autopilot, and WISH tools, with credits consumed per processed minute (roughly 1 credit per minute in the Editor, 2 credits per minute in Autopilot). The company markets itself directly against competitors like Opus Clip, Submagic, Descript, VEED, Vizard, Klap, and CapCut, positioning its core differentiation around end-to-end automation depth (auto silence removal plus reframing plus auto-publishing in one subscription) rather than manual editing power. It serves a wide range of audiences per its own site — creators, podcasters, YouTubers, marketing teams, educators, agencies, churches, and broadcasters — and offers both a free tier and an Enterprise plan with API access and custom volume pricing for high-throughput teams.

Best for

Best fit: podcasters, interview-based YouTubers, coaches, and small marketing teams who want fast, largely automated rough cuts, captions, and reframing without learning a full NLE, and who value auto-publishing via Autopilot. Weak fit: professional editors or teams needing granular manual control (color grading, multi-track composites, frame-accurate edits) — Wisecut is explicitly not a full-purpose editor per its own users' feedback.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Capterra rates Wisecut 4.7/5 across 65 verified reviews, with an ease-of-use score of 4.7 and a customer service score of 4.8 — strong marks for a niche AI editing tool.
  • Multiple reviewers describe dramatic time savings — one Capterra review from a media production CEO says it 'cut typical editing workflows from 3-4 hours to 15-20 minutes' for a 60-minute source video.
  • The automatic silence-removal and 'punch-in' zoom-cut feature is repeatedly singled out by reviewers as feeling like a second camera angle, per multiple 5-star Capterra reviews.
  • Automatic subtitle generation and background-music/noise handling let solo creators skip separate captioning and audio tools, cited by reviewers as a major bonus for accessibility and engagement.
  • Wisecut's team is shown publicly responding to a critical 2-star Capterra review with a detailed apology and fix explanation, suggesting active follow-up on customer issues.

Cons

  • Multiple Capterra reviewers note it 'isn't an all-purpose video editor' — fine-grained manual controls like color correction, multi-track editing, and precise word-by-word caption editing are limited or missing.
  • A 2023 review flagged 'fatal video uploading flaws, poor UX and weak customer support' including unreliable uploads and lost files, though Wisecut's team responded that the underlying issue was later resolved.
  • Background noise handling, upload, and download speeds are cited as 'extremely slow' in at least one detailed review from a 1-2 year user.
  • The jump-cut/punch-in feature isn't fully tunable — one reviewer wanted the ability to manually force specific silences that the AI would otherwise auto-cut.
  • Professional+ pricing ($83.25/mo annual, or higher on monthly billing) is a meaningful jump from Starter+, which may be steep for solo creators who don't need 4K or Autopilot's social publishing layer.

Frequently asked questions

What does Wisecut actually automate?

It automatically removes silences and filler pauses, adds zoom 'punch-in' cuts, generates and burns in captions, adds background music with auto-ducking, cancels background noise, and reframes footage for different aspect ratios.

Is there a free plan?

Yes — the Free plan includes 60 minutes of processing per month at 360p resolution with a 4GB max file size, but without Autopilot or Social Hub.

How do Wisecut Credits work?

One shared credit balance covers the Editor, Autopilot, and WISH tools; roughly 1 credit equals 1 processed minute in the Editor and 2 credits equal 1 processed minute in Autopilot.

What's the difference between Starter+ and Professional+?

Professional+ adds 4K export (vs. 1080p), longer max export duration, a much larger monthly credit allotment, bigger file size limits, more connected social accounts, and priority support.

Can I buy extra credits without upgrading my plan?

Yes — one-time credit packs from 150 to 3,600 credits can be purchased and remain valid for 12 months without changing your subscription.

Does Wisecut publish videos automatically to social media?

Yes, on the Starter+ and Professional+ plans, the Autopilot and Social Hub features handle social scheduling and auto-posting across connected accounts.
