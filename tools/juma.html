Juma is a good fit for marketing teams and agencies that want shared AI chat plus ready-made workflows for reports, audits and briefs. Anyone who only needs a plain chatbot, or whose work has little to do with marketing data, should skip it and use a general assistant instead. It started in 2023 as Team-GPT and now pitches itself as a collaborative workspace with persistent knowledge and repeatable workflows. The core feature is Flows: prebuilt or custom tasks that connect to tools such as Google Ads, Meta Ads, HubSpot and Notion and produce finished marketing assets. You can also chat with several models (the site names GPT-5, Claude and Gemini) from one place, and keep shared Projects and a prompt library. Usage is metered in credits pooled across the workspace, and only successful Flow runs consume them. The company's older marketing cited compliance certifications and enterprise customers; we could not re-verify those on the pages fetched today.

Best for

Marketing teams, agencies and consultancies that want shared AI workspaces, reusable workflows and multi-model access without per-seat fees. Not for solo users who want a simple chatbot, for teams whose work does not touch marketing data sources, or for buyers who need predictable flat monthly costs, since credit use varies by task.

Dedicated Data Analysis, Strategy, Content and Research agents handle complex, multi-step tasks and deliver complete outputs.

Shared, persistent project knowledge keeps client or brand context available across every chat and Flow a team runs.

Save, organize and chain frequently used prompts into repeatable "standard operating procedures" for recurring work.

Integrations

Connects to marketing and productivity tools such as Google Drive, Notion, HubSpot and Meta Ads; check the live integrations list for your exact stack.

Enterprise options

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Unlimited seats on every plan removes per-user cost for agencies with many collaborators.
  • Free tier is not feature-gated, so you can test the real product before paying.
  • Flows aim to produce a finished deliverable, which saves manual export and copy-paste steps.
  • Multiple frontier models in one workspace can replace several separate chat subscriptions.
  • Only successful Flow runs use credits, so failed runs should not drain your balance.

Cons

  • Credits are the real limiter: the free plan gives 300 per user, and the pricing page does not say how much usage 5,000 credits covers.
  • The pricing FAQ contradicts itself on whether Pro credits roll over, so confirm before relying on it.
  • No annual discount is shown for Pro.
  • The Flow library is built around marketing use cases and loses value outside them.
  • Compliance certifications and customer logos from older descriptions were not verified today; ask for current documents.

Frequently asked questions

Is Juma the same as Team-GPT?

Juma began in 2023 as Team-GPT and was rebranded; some reviews and articles still use the old name.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Free starts at $0 with unlimited seats and 300 credits per user, and the site says no features are locked behind paid tiers.

How much does Pro cost?

Pro starts at $49 per month, billed monthly, with selectable credit packages from 5,000 to 100,000 per month. The page does not state which tier the $49 price corresponds to, so check at checkout.

Do I pay per seat?

No. The pricing page says seats are unlimited and there is no per-seat pricing; credits are pooled across the workspace.

How does it compare with Poe or ChatGPT?

Poe and ChatGPT are general chat tools; Juma adds shared team projects and marketing-specific Flows tied to ad and CRM data, at the cost of credit-based billing.
