Juma is a collaborative AI workspace that began life in 2023 as Team-GPT, a tool for giving teams shared, secure access to ChatGPT and other large language models. The company rebranded to Juma to reflect its evolution from a shared-chat interface into what it now calls a "marketing superagent" - a platform that plans, executes, and delivers finished marketing work rather than just generating chat responses. At its core sit Flows: more than 700 pre-built, AI-powered workflows covering analytics, content, social, paid ads, and strategy, each of which connects directly to a team's existing tools, runs the underlying process, and hands back a completed asset such as a PDF report, slide deck, spreadsheet, or landing page. Users can also chat directly with GPT-5, Claude, Gemini and other frontier models from a single interface, avoiding the need to manage separate subscriptions for each.

What differentiates Juma from a general-purpose chatbot is its direct integration with the data marketing teams already work in - Google Ads, Meta Ads, GA4, HubSpot, Ahrefs and more - so Flows pull live data automatically instead of requiring manual exports and copy-pasting. Persistent Projects store shared context, files, and brand or client knowledge that the whole team can draw on, while specialized Agents for data analysis, strategy, content, and research handle multi-step tasks end-to-end. Juma is used by teams at Salesforce, Maersk, Charles Schwab, EY, Yale, and Johns Hopkins University, and holds SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, and GDPR compliance, positioning it for regulated and enterprise buyers as well as the small agencies and freelancers who have used the platform since its Team-GPT days.

Best for

Juma is best suited to marketing teams, agencies, and consultancies that need a shared, secure AI workspace with automated workflows tied to real ad and analytics data, plus enterprise buyers who need compliance guarantees. It's a weaker fit for individuals who just want a simple single-model chatbot, or teams whose work doesn't touch marketing data sources like Google Ads or HubSpot, since much of the Flow library is built around those use cases.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • G2 reviewers rate Juma 4.8/5 across 38 reviews, with 21 specifically praising the user-friendly interface and onboarding.
  • Multiple reviewers highlight the ability to compare outputs from ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude side by side as a unique advantage over single-model tools.
  • Freelancers and agencies report using the Prompt Library and Project Knowledge to cut research and drafting time dramatically - one reviewer says it "saved hundreds of hours."
  • Enterprise customers cite SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, HIPAA and GDPR compliance as a deciding factor over consumer AI tools.
  • Customer support is repeatedly called out as fast and responsive, with several reviewers noting replies within 1-2 hours.
  • Users switching from paying for multiple individual AI subscriptions report meaningful cost savings by consolidating on Juma.

Cons

  • Image generation is limited - users can't easily refine or increase the resolution of generated images once created.
  • A handful of reviewers note a learning curve in finding the right workflow or Flow for their specific use case.
  • Native integrations with Slack and Microsoft Teams are still requested by users as of 2026 reviews.
  • No built-in deep web-search/browsing tool, which some reviewers say limits use for real-time research.
  • Marketing-agency-first branding and templates can make the tool feel less tailored to non-marketing, solo or operational use cases.

Frequently asked questions

Is Juma the same company as Team-GPT?

Yes. Juma launched in 2023 as Team-GPT and rebranded to Juma to reflect its shift from a shared-chat tool into a broader marketing workflow platform, though many G2 reviews still reference the Team-GPT name.

What AI models does Juma support?

Juma gives teams access to GPT-5, Claude, Gemini and other leading models from a single interface, letting users compare outputs or pick the best model for a task.

What are Flows?

Flows are Juma's 700+ pre-built, AI-powered workflows that connect to a team's existing tools and data sources to execute repeatable marketing tasks end-to-end, delivering a finished asset rather than a chat reply.

Does Juma integrate with my marketing stack?

Yes, Juma connects to Google Ads, Meta Ads, GA4, HubSpot, Ahrefs, Slack, Notion and over 100 other tools.

Is Juma secure enough for enterprise use?

Juma is SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, HIPAA and GDPR compliant, and offers private AWS cloud deployment for enterprise customers such as Salesforce and Charles Schwab.

Is there a free plan?

Yes, Juma offers a free plan with unlimited users and access to every model, Flow and integration, differing from paid plans only in monthly credit allowance.
