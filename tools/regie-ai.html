Regie.ai suits small outbound teams and solo sellers who want research, writing and dialing in one cheap workspace; large organizations needing deep Salesforce integration or heavy compliance review should look elsewhere or ask about Enterprise. RegieGO combines prospect sourcing, enrichment, AI-drafted messages, multichannel sequences across email, LinkedIn and phone, a power dialer and a built-in CRM, with agents that rank accounts and prep calls. Usage is metered by credits shared across the workspace, so cost depends on how much research and drafting you do.

Best for

Good for SDRs, founders and small teams starting outbound who want a self-serve tool with a free trial. Not for teams that need a Salesforce-native system of record, buyers who want unlimited usage at a flat fee, or anyone who needs a long list of integrations.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Pricing is public and starts at free, so you can test before buying.
  • Dialer, enrichment, drafting and CRM sit in one tool, reducing stitched-together subscriptions.
  • Pro does not charge credits when enrichment finds nothing.
  • Sends from your own mailbox, which helps deliverability and familiarity.

Cons

  • Credits are the constraint: they reset monthly without rollover and can't be bought alone.
  • Free credits are one-time, so the free plan is a trial.
  • Overage is optional and capped by the workspace owner, so heavy users may hit limits.
  • Integrations listed are limited; Salesforce was not mentioned on the pricing or home pages.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

Yes: $0 with 250 one-time credits per person, up to 10 people sharing one pool, no card needed.

What do paid plans cost?

Pro is $41/mo billed annually ($49 monthly). Team is $125/seat/mo annually ($150 monthly) with a two-seat minimum.

Do unused credits roll over?

No. Paid-plan credits reset each billing period, and extra credits can't be purchased on their own.

Does it have its own CRM?

Yes, a built-in CRM is free on every plan, or you can connect your own.
