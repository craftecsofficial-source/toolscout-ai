Vyond is a good fit for corporate training, HR and marketing teams that want polished animated or avatar videos with tight scene-by-scene control. Skip it if you are a solo creator on a budget or want a one-click script-to-video tool, since plans start at $58 per user per month billed annually and there is no permanent free plan. It is a scene-based editor where you assemble characters, backgrounds, props, camera moves and narration, now with AI avatars, text-to-speech, screen and webcam recording, and document or URL to video (Vyond Go). Vyond says 65% of the Fortune 500 use it; that is its own claim.

Best for

Great for L&D, HR, compliance and internal-comms teams, and agencies producing client videos, who need controlled, repeatable, on-brand output. Not for hobbyists, solo creators or anyone who needs a free plan, and not for people who want fully automated cinematic text-to-video clips; tools like Runway or Kling suit that better.

Key features

01

Scene-based animation editor

A drag-and-drop timeline editor where users build videos scene by scene with characters, props, backgrounds, camera moves and transitions for precise creative control.

02

Generative AI avatars

Professional plans and above let users generate custom AI avatars from a text prompt, in addition to Vyond's existing library of stock animated characters and photorealistic presenters.

03

650+ text-to-speech voices

A large library of natural-sounding AI voices across many languages and accents for narrating scenes without hiring voice talent.

04

Screen and webcam recording

Built-in recording tools let users capture screen demos or webcam footage and combine them with animated or AI-generated content in the same project.

05

Instant translation (95 languages)

Professional-tier feature that translates finished video scripts and narration into 95 languages, useful for global training and marketing rollouts.

06

4M+ Shutterstock asset library

Professional plans include access to millions of Shutterstock photos, video clips and graphics to supplement Vyond's native asset library.

07

Team collaboration and folder sharing

Higher tiers add shared team workspaces, folder-based collaboration and admin controls for larger content teams.

08

Commercial rights transfer

Agencies and freelancers producing client work can transfer commercial rights per video (fee-based on Professional) or unlimited (included on the Agency plan).

Frequently asked questions

Does Vyond have a free plan?

No permanent free plan. The site offers a free trial, but its length and limits are not stated on the pages I checked.

How much does Vyond cost?

Per user: Starter $99/mo monthly or $58/mo annually, Professional $199 monthly or $100 annually. Enterprise ($1,649/yr) and Agency ($1,999/yr) are annual only; multi-seat pricing needs sales.

Which plan do I need for AI avatars from a text prompt?

Professional or higher. Starter includes existing avatars and text-to-speech.

How long can videos be?

25 minutes on Starter, 40 on Professional, 90 on Enterprise and Agency.

Is it a good choice for client work?

The Agency plan is aimed at client-facing content, per Vyond's pricing page.
