SurgeGraph is a good fit for small agencies and solo SEOs who want AI-citation tracking and content rewriting under one cheap subscription. Skip it if you are an enterprise needing deep integrations and governance, or if you need all five AI engines tracked daily without paying for the top tier. It monitors which buyer prompts mention or omit a brand in AI answers, grades each page for search and citation readiness, and offers an AI writer and bulk generator to act on the gaps. A WordPress plugin shows AI-referred visits, and white-label reporting starts on the middle plan. Usage is metered in monthly credits, with tracking, articles and research limits rising by tier.

Best for

Good for agencies and freelancers managing a few to a few dozen client sites who want reports they can brand. Not for enterprises needing SSO-style governance, brands that want a free trial first, or teams that don't want AI-generated content in their workflow. Spark users wanting Perplexity, Gemini or AI Mode coverage must upgrade.

Key features

01

AI visibility tracking

Tracks whether a brand is cited for chosen prompts across ChatGPT, AI Overviews, Perplexity, Gemini and AI Mode, with refresh every 5 days, 3 days or daily depending on plan.

02

AEO + SEO page scoring

Each page gets a score for search ranking and AI citation readiness, with suggested fixes you can review before publishing.

03

Content generation

AI article writer, bulk generation from keyword lists, topic research and a WordPress-connected optimizer, all drawn from the monthly credit pool.

04

Traffic Impact Dashboard

A WordPress plugin attributes visits from AI platforms and updates every five minutes.

05

White-label reporting

Branded PDF exports and shareable links for clients, available from the Scale plan.

06

MCP and agentic workflows

Signature plan connects SurgeGraph to other AI tools for automation, plus custom project access controls.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Tracking, scoring and content creation share one login, so insights turn into edits without another tool.
  • Entry price is far below enterprise platforms, making client-level tracking affordable.
  • Branded reports and sharable links save agency reporting time.
  • Refund window of 14 days lets you test on a real client.

Cons

  • Spark misses Perplexity, Gemini and AI Mode, so cheap tier gives partial AI coverage.
  • Daily refresh is only on Signature; Spark updates every 5 days, which can miss fast changes.
  • Credits cap content and research volume, so output-heavy agencies may outgrow tiers.
  • Pricing page shows only annual-equivalent monthly figures; true monthly-billing cost is unclear.
  • Company's own performance claims are unverified by independent sources.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan or trial?

No free plan or trial is listed. There is a 14-day money-back guarantee.

Which AI engines are covered?

Spark: ChatGPT and Google AI Overview. Scale adds Perplexity. Signature covers ChatGPT, AI Overview, Perplexity, Gemini and AI Mode.

What do I pay if I bill monthly?

The page shows per-month figures tied to annual billing. Savings noted imply about $39, $79 and $129 monthly, but this is not stated directly - confirm at checkout.

Can I white-label reports for clients?

Yes, from the Scale plan upward, as branded PDFs or shareable links.

Do I need WordPress?

Tracking works without it, but the AI-referral traffic dashboard and the optimizer integration rely on the WordPress plugin.
