EchoNotes is an AI-powered note-taking and meeting assistant app designed to convert spoken audio and video into accurate, structured, searchable notes. Users can record directly inside the app during a meeting, lecture, interview, or brainstorming session, or upload existing audio and video files for transcription. Beyond raw transcription, EchoNotes layers on AI-generated summaries that surface key decisions, highlights, and action items, and it can also pull notes directly from online video links on platforms like YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and Twitter, which is useful for research or study workflows that don't start with a live recording. A defining feature is that transcription and summarization run on-device using local AI processing rather than sending audio to external servers, positioning privacy as a core differentiator versus cloud-transcription competitors.

The app is distributed primarily through the Apple App Store (and a related build on the Microsoft Store) under the developer name 杨胜 朱, operating via the small software studio Qiniusoft, which also publishes a portfolio of other niche utility apps (PDF tools, subtitle generators, transcription and voice-memo apps). EchoNotes is a young product, having launched in September 2025, and as of research it had not yet accumulated enough App Store ratings to display an aggregate score. It offers a free download gated by in-app purchases, with weekly and yearly subscription options unlocking full premium functionality. Note: the marketing domain echonotes.ai renders minimal content in automated fetches (likely a JavaScript-rendered landing page), and there are several similarly named but unrelated products in the market (e.g., EchoNote at echonote.xyz, myechonotes.com), so this profile is built primarily from the official App Store listing and consistent third-party AI-tool directory descriptions.

Best for

Best suited to students, journalists, and individual professionals who want a private, on-device AI note-taker for lectures, interviews, and meetings, and who are comfortable using an iOS-first app. It is a weaker fit for teams needing a mature, well-reviewed product with a full-featured web/desktop app, live collaborative notes, or deep calendar/video-conferencing integrations.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Local, on-device AI processing means sensitive meeting or interview audio is never uploaded to a third-party server, a meaningful differentiator for privacy-conscious professionals and journalists.
  • The ability to generate notes directly from online video links (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Twitter) extends its use beyond live recording into research and study workflows that competitors focused only on meetings don't cover.
  • Broad language and accent support (30+ transcription languages, 14 interface languages) makes it usable for international teams and multilingual students.
  • Automatic action-item and key-decision extraction saves users from manually re-reading full transcripts after a meeting.
  • The app explicitly states it does not collect user data, reinforcing its privacy-first positioning in its official App Store privacy disclosure.

Cons

  • As a recently launched app (initial release in September 2025), it has not yet accumulated enough App Store ratings to display a public score, so buyers have little independent validation of real-world reliability.
  • Distribution centers on iOS/iPad (with a related Microsoft Store build), which limits reach compared to browser-based or Android-first meeting assistants.
  • The marketing website at echonotes.ai renders very little content on standard fetches, meaning most reliable feature and pricing information comes from the App Store listing rather than a full product site.
  • Subscription pricing (especially the $3.99/week option) can become expensive over time compared to flat monthly SaaS transcription tools if a user forgets to switch to the annual plan.
  • The market has several similarly-branded note-taking apps (EchoNote, Echo, myEchoNotes), which creates real risk of user confusion when searching for or evaluating the correct product.

Frequently asked questions

Does EchoNotes send my audio to the cloud?

No, EchoNotes processes transcription and summarization on-device using local AI, so audio and video content is not sent to external servers.

What languages does it support?

It supports transcription in 30+ languages and accents, with the app interface itself available in English plus 13 additional languages.

Can I import existing recordings?

Yes, users can record directly in the app or upload existing audio and video files for instant transcription.

Can it transcribe online videos?

Yes, EchoNotes can generate notes directly from online video links on platforms like YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and Twitter.

What does it cost?

The app is free to download with in-app purchases; premium access is offered via a $3.99 weekly subscription or a $29.99 yearly subscription.

Is EchoNotes well-reviewed?

It is a newly launched app and had not yet accumulated enough App Store ratings to display an aggregate score at the time of research, so independent review data is limited.
