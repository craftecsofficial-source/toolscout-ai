Ideogram stands out for accurately rendering readable text inside generated images, useful for logos, posters and social graphics.

Best for

Anyone generating graphics that need clean, legible text baked into the image.

Key features

  • ✓Industry-leading rendering of legible text inside images
  • ✓Free tier with 10 images/day and commercial rights
  • ✓Style and color palette controls
  • ✓Fast generation speed

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The clear leader for posters, logos and social graphics needing readable text
  • Generous, genuinely usable free tier
  • No watermarks even on the free plan

Cons

  • Overall artistic quality slightly behind Midjourney for pure illustration
  • Smaller community and template ecosystem
