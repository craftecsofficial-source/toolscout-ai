Rio is a strong fit for sustainability and finance teams at companies over roughly GBP 10m revenue (or portfolio managers) that must report under frameworks like ISSB, ESRS or SFDR. Skip it if you want a general data-analysis or machine learning tool, or if you sit between GBP 1m and GBP 10m revenue, where no plan is published. Rio connects operational, sustainability and supply-chain data, then supports workflows to measure performance and risk, benchmark sites or portfolios, prioritise actions, and produce reporting evidence. The vendor also sells consulting and training, and says a 'Rio 3.0' with modelling and prediction is in development, which we treated as not yet available.

Best for

Sustainability, ESG, risk and finance functions at organisations over about GBP 10m revenue that face mandatory disclosure and would rather buy reporting logic than build it, plus asset managers needing financed-emissions reporting. It is not for teams that want general predictive modelling or ad hoc data analysis, for retail pricing or demand work, for mid-sized businesses between GBP 1m and GBP 10m revenue who need a published plan, or for buyers wanting transparent all-in pricing up front.

Key features

01

GHG accounting

Operational greenhouse gas accounting on all paid tiers, plus energy, water and waste accounting from Rio Enterprise upward.

02

Framework reporting

Customised reporting for ISSB, ESRS, SEC, SECR, CDP and CFD on Rio Enterprise; SFDR and SDR on the Financial Services tier.

03

Supply chain engagement

Tools to collect data from suppliers and assets, available from Rio Enterprise.

04

Financed emissions

Financed GHG accounting and portfolio engagement for portfolio managers across direct holdings, credit, alternatives, debt and equity.

05

Rio-AI and materiality

An AI sustainability assistant and automated materiality assessment, included from the free tier.

06

Climate-risk analysis

Physical and climate-risk analysis on Rio Enterprise and Financial Services.

07

Services and learning

Optional Rio Consultant Group access, support tickets (with agreed SLAs on paid tiers), and Rio Club learning content and events.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Ships reporting for many frameworks, so your team does not have to build the compliance layer from scratch.
  • Covers operational accounting, supply chain data and climate risk in one product.
  • The Financial Services tier addresses financed emissions and SFDR/SDR, which general carbon tools often lack.
  • A free single-user tier lets very small businesses begin GHG accounting without cost.

Cons

  • There is a gap in the published tiers: nothing is listed for revenue between GBP 1m and GBP 10m.
  • Enterprise and Financial Services prices are 'from' figures, and onboarding and consulting are extra, so total cost is unclear until you get a quote.
  • It does one thing; it will not double as a general analytics, forecasting or machine learning platform.
  • We could not verify audit acceptance, data accuracy or customer outcomes, or independent reviews of the product.
  • Check that the frameworks you must file under are covered; the list leans UK and EU (SECR, ESRS, SDR) alongside SEC and CDP.

Frequently asked questions

What does Rio do?

It structures operational, sustainability and supply-chain data for carbon accounting, benchmarking and framework-based reporting, with optional consulting and training from the same vendor.

What does Rio cost?

Micro Rio Enterprise is free for 1 user at businesses up to GBP 1m revenue. Rio Enterprise starts from GBP 12k per year (over GBP 10m revenue). Rio for Financial Services starts from GBP 50k. Onboarding and consulting are quoted separately.

Which reporting frameworks are covered?

Rio Enterprise lists ISSB, ESRS, SEC, SECR, CDP and CFD; the Financial Services tier lists SFDR and SDR.

Is there a plan for a company with GBP 1m to GBP 10m revenue?

None is published. Contact Rio to ask what applies.

Who should skip Rio?

Anyone who needs a general-purpose analytics or machine learning platform, or whose work is outside sustainability reporting.
