deckrobot is a strong fit for large enterprises and consulting or audit firms that live in PowerPoint and must enforce brand consistency, and a poor fit for individuals or small teams, since there is no public pricing or self-serve signup. It is more than a reformatter: the site now also lists prompt-based deck, slide, text and chart generation.

It installs as a Microsoft PowerPoint add-in (Windows and Mac) and applies corporate styles in one click, suggests alternative slide designs, arranges shapes, resizes decks, objects and fonts, and keeps titles, logos and page numbers consistent. It provides libraries of templates and icons and can analyze documents. The vendor claims 30,000+ users and 100+ hours saved per year; we could not verify these claims or test the product.

Best for

Built for enterprises and consulting or audit firms where many employees build PowerPoint decks that must follow brand rules, and for brand or IT teams rolling out design governance. Not for freelancers, students or small teams: there is no published price or self-serve plan, and it assumes you have brand guidelines to configure. Also not the best choice if you want a standalone web generator.

Key features

Existing slides are automatically restyled to match a company's defined fonts, colors, and layout standards.

02

Native PowerPoint add-in

Runs directly inside Microsoft PowerPoint rather than requiring a separate web application or file export/import step.

03

Corporate style and icon library

Provides access to a library of approved icons, templates, and design elements aligned to brand guidelines.

04

Bulk deck processing

Can reformat large, multi-slide decks in batches rather than slide-by-slide manual adjustment.

05

Cross-team consistency enforcement

Helps ensure decks produced by different departments or offices follow the same visual standards.

06

Enterprise-scale deployment

Built to support rollout across tens of thousands of employees within large organizations.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Works inside PowerPoint on Windows and Mac, so no new editor to learn

  • Also offers prompt-based generation and chart creation per the site

Cons

  • No public pricing; you must book a demo to learn cost
  • Not usable by individuals or small teams without a sales conversation

Frequently asked questions

No, it is software for Microsoft PowerPoint on Windows and Mac.

Mainly design and brand compliance, but the site also lists generating decks, slides, text and charts from prompts.

Can I buy it online?

Pricing is not public; contact sales.
