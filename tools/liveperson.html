LivePerson is a publicly traded (NASDAQ) enterprise software company, originally founded in 1995 as one of the pioneers of live web chat, that has since repositioned itself as a conversational AI platform for large-scale customer engagement. Its core product, Conversational Cloud, gives enterprise brands a single agent workspace to manage customer conversations across web chat, mobile apps, SMS, WhatsApp, Apple Messages for Business, Facebook Messenger, Instagram, Google Business Messaging, and several other messaging channels, blending AI-driven automation with human agent handoff. The platform was named a Niche Player in Gartner's 2025 Magic Quadrant for Conversational AI Platforms and has also been recognized as a Leader in several G2 Spring 2025 Grid reports covering AI agents, chatbots, and live chat.

LivePerson's generative AI layer, delivered through what it calls Conversation Copilot and a suite of AI Agents, includes conversation summarization, agent-assist suggestions, message rewriting for tone and clarity, intent-based routing, and hallucination detection that grounds AI responses in a company's own knowledge base, CRM, and CMS content rather than open-ended generation. The company also supports 'bring your own LLM,' letting enterprises plug in their preferred large language model for greater control over cost and compliance. Rather than self-serve signup, LivePerson sells through an enterprise sales motion with three packaged tiers -- Bronze, Silver, and Gold -- each unlocking progressively more automation, analytics, and generative AI capability, with all pricing available only via a custom quote.

Best for

LivePerson is built for large enterprises -- particularly in financial services, retail, travel and hospitality, telco, healthcare, and automotive -- that need to manage very high volumes of customer conversations across many messaging channels with a mix of AI automation and live agents, and that have the budget and technical resources for a custom enterprise deployment. It is a poor fit for small and mid-sized businesses or teams wanting transparent, self-serve pricing, since there is no published cost tier and typical contracts run well into five and six figures annually.

Key features

01

Conversational Cloud Agent Workspace

A unified, multi-channel agent and supervisor workspace covering web, app, SMS, WhatsApp, Apple Messages for Business, Messenger, Instagram, and more, letting agents manage all channels from one interface.

02

Intent Manager

Lets teams create, test, and activate intent domain models using LivePerson's NLU engine, with pre-built starter packs for common verticals and use cases.

03

Conversation Builder

A no-code bot builder that lets non-technical staff design, optimize, and visualize bots across messaging channels, including voice-enabled bots and post-conversation survey bots.

04

Conversation Orchestrator

Creates highly personalized conversational journeys using dynamic routing, a Conversation Context service, and a Next Actions API to decide what happens next in a conversation.

05

Generative AI Copilot

A suite covering Copilot Summary (conversation recaps), Copilot Assist (in-line AI-generated response suggestions), and Copilot Rewrite (tone and clarity editing) to speed up human agents.

06

Hallucination Detection

Grounds generative AI responses in verified business content from knowledge bases, CRM, and CMS systems to reduce inaccurate or fabricated answers in customer-facing conversations.

07

AI Agents (Knowledge and Routing)

Purpose-built AI agents that use existing knowledge bases and CRM/CMS content to increase self-service resolution and route consumers to the right bot or human agent based on intent.

08

Integration Hub and CRM Connectors

A self-serve integration hub powered by Workato plus native CRM connectors (including Salesforce) and access to 50+ data, connect, and customize APIs for enterprise system integration.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Channel breadth is a genuine differentiator: the platform natively supports WhatsApp, Apple Messages for Business, Google Business Messaging, KakaoTalk, LINE, Viber, and WeChat alongside standard web and app chat, which matters for global enterprises with regionally fragmented messaging preferences.
  • The generative AI stack goes beyond a basic chatbot layer, adding hallucination detection grounded in company knowledge bases and CRM/CMS content, plus the option to bring your own LLM for compliance and cost control.
  • Conversation Orchestrator's dynamic routing and Next Actions API allow enterprises to build genuinely personalized conversational journeys rather than static decision-tree bots.
  • Independent analyst recognition, including a place in Gartner's 2025 Magic Quadrant for Conversational AI Platforms and multiple G2 Leader badges (AI agents, chatbots, live chat), lends third-party credibility for enterprise procurement processes.
  • G2 reviewers specifically highlight strong live chat performance (a 9.2 sub-rating in some analyses) and effective automation of customer service tasks across channels.
  • The Integration Hub, powered by Workato, plus 50+ documented data and connect APIs give technical teams substantial flexibility to wire the platform into existing enterprise systems beyond the built-in Salesforce connector.

Cons

  • There is no published pricing anywhere on LivePerson's site; every tier (Bronze, Silver, Gold) requires a custom quote, and third-party estimates put typical enterprise contracts anywhere from roughly $40,000 to $110,000+ per year, making early-stage budget planning difficult.
  • G2 reviewers note that configuring the platform to match specific workflow needs can be genuinely challenging, with several describing a steeper learning curve than smaller, more focused conversational AI point solutions.
  • Support quality receives a comparatively modest sub-rating (around 8.3) in aggregated G2 analysis, suggesting enterprise support responsiveness is not uniformly considered a strength.
  • The most advanced generative AI capabilities (full Copilot suite, AI Agents, hallucination detection, bring-your-own-LLM) are reserved for the top Gold tier, so lower-tier customers get a materially less AI-forward product despite still paying enterprise-level custom pricing.
  • LivePerson's G2 review volume (156 reviews) is relatively modest for a company operating since 1995 at enterprise scale, which may reflect either a smaller active reviewer base or lower engagement with review platforms among its typically large enterprise customers.

Frequently asked questions

How much does LivePerson cost?

LivePerson does not publish pricing. It sells through Bronze, Silver, and Gold custom-quoted tiers, and third-party estimates suggest typical enterprise contracts range from roughly $40,000 to over $110,000 per year depending on scale and features.

What messaging channels does LivePerson support?

It supports web and app chat, SMS, WhatsApp, Apple Messages for Business, Facebook Messenger, Instagram, Google Business and RCS Messaging, KakaoTalk, LINE, Viber, and WeChat, with X (Twitter) available for an additional fee.

Can LivePerson use our own AI model instead of its built-in one?

Yes, the Gold tier supports 'bring your own LLM,' letting enterprises integrate their preferred large language model for enhanced control, compliance, and cost management.

Does LivePerson integrate with Salesforce?

Yes, it offers a native CRM connector for Salesforce that embeds the agent workspace within the CRM, along with access to 50+ additional data and integration APIs.

What's the difference between the Bronze, Silver, and Gold tiers?

Bronze covers the core agent workspace and channels; Silver adds the Conversation Builder bot tool, KnowledgeAI, and Conversation Assist; Gold adds the full generative AI suite (Copilot, AI Agents, hallucination detection, bring-your-own-LLM) plus Analytics Studio and a designated Customer Success Manager.

Is LivePerson suitable for small businesses?

Not typically. It's built and priced for enterprise-scale deployments with custom contracts, making it a poor fit for small businesses seeking self-serve, transparently priced conversational AI tools.
