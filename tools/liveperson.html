LivePerson is a good fit for large contact-center operations that want one vendor for AI agents, human agents and conversation analytics, and who are comfortable with a sales-led, custom-priced contract. Small and mid-sized teams, or anyone wanting transparent self-serve pricing, should skip it. The homepage now says SoundHound AI has acquired LivePerson, creating an omnichannel conversational AI company, though no price or closing date is given there. The platform, Conversational Cloud, identifies customer intent and routes conversations between bots and people. Around it sit analytics (Conversational Intelligence & Insights), Syntrix for testing AI agents and training live agents, a chatbot toolset, integrations, and a services arm including LP 360 contact center as a service.

Best for

Good for enterprises in banking, retail, travel, healthcare, telecom and automotive running high-volume messaging and voice support with a dedicated CX or contact-center team. Not for small businesses, startups, or teams that want to sign up and launch a bot the same day; not for buyers who need published pricing to compare; and anyone wary of roadmap uncertainty after the acquisition should ask pointed questions before committing.

Key features

01

Conversational Cloud

Analytics over conversation data intended to improve engagement and agent performance.

03

Evaluates AI agents and trains live agents before they face customers. The vendor claims 60% faster AI agent testing (vendor-reported, not independently verified).

04

AI chatbot toolset and integrations

Tools for building chatbots plus a catalog of connections to other business systems.

05

LP 360 and services

Contact center as a service, professional services, technical support and a partner marketplace for teams that want help running the deployment.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Scale: the vendor reports over a billion messages a month on the platform (vendor claim).
  • One vendor covers bot building, human agent workspace, analytics and AI testing, reducing tool sprawl.
  • Industry focus (finance, healthcare, telecom, retail) and stated GDPR, HIPAA and PCI DSS support help in regulated sectors.
  • Managed services mean you are not forced to staff the whole build internally.

Cons

  • Pricing is fully opaque, so budgeting and competitive comparison require a sales process.
  • The SoundHound acquisition is stated on the site without terms; product packaging, support and roadmap may change, and customers should ask about continuity.
  • Aimed at big deployments; expect a long implementation and real configuration effort.
  • Headline results (30% lower operating cost, 90% automation, 25% CSAT lift) are vendor-reported best cases, not guarantees.

Frequently asked questions

Was LivePerson acquired?

The LivePerson homepage states that SoundHound AI has acquired LivePerson. The site gives no price or closing date and links to SoundHound's newsroom for details.

How much does LivePerson cost?

Not public. The site has a pricing page but lists no plans or prices; you need to contact sales.

Can I try it self-serve?

This could not be verified from the public site; the positioning is enterprise and sales-led, so assume a demo and contract process.

Who should look elsewhere?

Small teams and anyone wanting a quick, fixed-price chatbot. Self-serve builders like Chatling or DocsBot are far lighter.
