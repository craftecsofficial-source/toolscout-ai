Firefly is a strong pick for designers, agencies and brand teams already paying for Adobe, who want generated images that can go straight into Photoshop or Express. Skip it if you only want the most striking artistic images or the cheapest video credits, since specialist generators and some partner models can do better per dollar.

It is a web and mobile studio for generating and editing images, video and audio. Adobe's own models are trained on licensed Adobe Stock and public-domain content, and the same interface also offers partner models from Google, OpenAI, FLUX, Runway, Kling, Luma and ElevenLabs. Paid plans give unlimited use of standard image features such as Generative Fill, while video, audio and premium models spend a monthly pool of generative credits.

Best for

Great for: agencies, in-house brand teams and Creative Cloud users who need image, video and audio generation tied to existing Adobe files, plus teams that care about training-data provenance. Not for: people wanting the best artistic look at the lowest price, anyone who dislikes credit metering, or users who need the licensing comfort on partner-model outputs (that comfort applies to Adobe's own models).

Key features

01

Image generation and editing

Text-to-image, image-to-image, style transfer, Generative Fill, object and person removal, background generation, upscaling and expansion, also available inside Photoshop.

02

Video generation and editing

Text-to-video, image-to-video, talking avatars, upscaling and video translation. Plan pages cap clips by tier, e.g. up to 20 five-second videos on Standard.

03

Audio tools

Text-to-speech, music, sound effects and audio/video dubbing, with monthly caps that scale by plan.

04

Partner models

Gemini (Nano Banana), GPT Image, FLUX, Runway, Kling, Luma, Topaz and ElevenLabs are selectable alongside Adobe's models.

05

Firefly Boards

A shared canvas for mood boards and storyboards that mixes generations with uploaded references.

06

Custom Models (public beta)

Train a model on your own images to match a style; enterprise tiers extend this.

07

Content Credentials

Outputs carry Content Credentials metadata marking them as AI-generated.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Native handoff into Photoshop, Premiere and Express keeps generated assets in a professional edit pipeline
  • Adobe's models are trained on licensed Adobe Stock and public-domain content, a clearer provenance story than many rivals
  • Partner models and Adobe's own sit under one login, so you can compare outputs without extra subscriptions
  • Standard image features stay unlimited on paid plans, so routine fills and removals do not drain credits

Cons

  • Generative credits reset each cycle and do not roll over; video and audio spend them fast
  • Commercial-safety messaging applies to Adobe's models, not the partner models
  • Several headline discounts are time-limited (the Pro Plus and Premium promo was due to end Oct 21), so the real ongoing price is higher
  • Plan perks differ in many small caps (video count, translation minutes), which makes comparing tiers fiddly
  • Free tier is only a limited daily allowance, not a full working plan

Frequently asked questions

Does Firefly have a free plan?

Yes. It gives limited free daily generations across image, video and audio that refresh each day; heavier use needs a paid plan.

What happens when my credits run out?

Credits reset each billing cycle and do not roll over. You can wait, upgrade or buy a credit add-on. Standard image features stay unlimited on paid plans.

Is everything generated in Firefly commercially safe?

Adobe describes its own Firefly models as commercially safe because of the licensed training data. That claim is tied to Adobe's models, not to partner models, so check the terms before relying on third-party model output.

How does it compare with Midjourney?

Firefly wins on Adobe integration, licensed data and video/audio breadth; Midjourney is usually preferred for stylised artistic images.
