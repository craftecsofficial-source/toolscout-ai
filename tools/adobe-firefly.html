Adobe Firefly is Adobe's generative AI platform for creating and editing images, video, audio and vector graphics, built into and around the Creative Cloud ecosystem. It's positioned as Adobe's answer to standalone generators like Midjourney, but with a specific emphasis on being "commercially safe" — Adobe trains its own Firefly models on licensed Adobe Stock content and public domain material, which it markets as a lower legal-risk option for brands and enterprises.

Its core differentiator is breadth and integration rather than being the single best model for any one task: Firefly gives users one login to access Adobe's own models alongside third-party partner models (Google Gemini/Nano Banana, OpenAI's GPT Image, Runway, Kling, ElevenLabs and others), plus direct integration into Photoshop, Express and other Creative Cloud apps.

Best for

Adobe Firefly is best suited for marketers, designers and content teams already inside the Adobe ecosystem who want AI generation integrated directly into Photoshop/Express workflows and who value commercially-safe licensing for brand work. It's a weaker choice for people chasing the single most artistic or photorealistic output (Midjourney typically wins there) or for anyone who wants simple, predictable flat-rate pricing rather than a credit system with shifting promotional terms.

Key features

01

Multi-model access

Firefly provides access to Adobe's own Firefly Image models plus partner models like Gemini (Nano Banana), GPT Image, FLUX.2, Runway and Kling, all through a single subscription and interface.

02

Generative Fill and image editing

Firefly's editing tools let users remove objects, expand backgrounds, upscale images and apply generative style transfer directly in the browser or inside Photoshop.

03

AI video generation and editing

Generates short video clips from text or images, with tools to upscale, translate or edit existing video using natural-language instructions.

04

Firefly Boards

A collaborative, infinite-canvas space for building mood boards, storyboards and brand concepts as a team, pulling in AI generations alongside uploaded reference images.

05

Custom Models (public beta)

Lets users upload their own images to train a custom AI model that captures a specific visual style, useful for maintaining brand or personal consistency.

06

Generative audio tools

Generates music, voiceovers and sound effects, and can translate or dub audio/video into other languages while preserving vocal tone and timing.

07

Commercially safe licensing

Firefly's own models are trained on licensed Adobe Stock and public domain content, which Adobe positions as reducing copyright risk for business use.

08

Creative Cloud app integration

Firefly features are built directly into Photoshop, Illustrator and Adobe Express, letting generated content move into a full editing pipeline without re-importing.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Single subscription provides access to Adobe's own models plus major third-party models (Gemini/Nano Banana, GPT Image, FLUX, Runway, Kling) without separate accounts
  • Deep, native integration into Photoshop and Adobe Express means generated assets can move directly into a professional editing pipeline
  • Commercially-safe licensing model reduces copyright uncertainty for brand and enterprise use compared to generators trained on unknown scraped data
  • Firefly Boards make it genuinely useful for team ideation and mood-boarding, not just single-image generation
  • Free tier and low $9.99/mo entry point make it more accessible to test than credit-heavy competitors
  • Broadest generation surface of any major tool: images, video, audio, vector graphics and voice in one product

Cons

  • Prompt adherence is a frequent complaint — users report Firefly ignoring parts of prompts or producing simplistic output that needs several regenerations
  • Failed or low-quality generations still consume credits with no refund
  • The credit system, combined with premium features drawing from the same pool as standard generations, makes cost unpredictable at scale
  • Firefly's own image models are generally seen as behind Midjourney and top partner models on raw artistic quality
  • Content moderation sometimes blocks prompts that aren't actually policy-violating
  • Pricing structure (regular vs. promotional pricing, credits vs. unlimited-model periods) changes frequently, making long-term cost hard to predict

What reviewers say

Adobe Firefly gets consistently solid, if not glowing, reviews across G2 and Capterra, both landing at 4.4/5. Praise centers heavily on ease of use and how seamlessly it fits into existing Adobe workflows. The most common complaint by a wide margin is inaccurate or low-quality output requiring multiple regeneration attempts, echoed in Adobe's own community forums. Reddit and community sentiment is mixed-to-positive: useful for simple, fast content needs, but serious creative professionals often find it less capable than dedicated image generators for demanding creative work.

Frequently praised

  • Seamless integration with Photoshop and the wider Adobe ecosystem
  • Commercially-safe licensing reduces legal risk for brand use
  • Access to multiple leading AI models (Adobe and partner) in one place

Frequently criticized

  • Prompt adherence issues requiring multiple regeneration attempts
  • Credits consumed even on failed or low-quality generations, with no refund
  • Confusing, frequently-changing credit and promotional pricing structure
  • Content moderation sometimes blocks non-violating prompts

Frequently asked questions

Is Adobe Firefly free to use?

Yes, there's a free tier with limited daily generations across image, video and audio, but higher-volume and premium features require a paid Standard, Pro, Pro Plus or Premium plan.

What are Firefly credits?

Credits are Firefly's usage currency — unlimited standard image generation is included on every paid plan, while premium features like video generation and non-Adobe partner models draw down a monthly credit allowance.

Can I use images generated by Firefly commercially?

Yes — Adobe positions Firefly's own models as commercially safe because they're trained on licensed Adobe Stock and public domain content, reducing copyright risk compared to models trained on unclear web-scraped data.

Does Firefly include access to other AI models like Gemini or GPT Image?

Yes, Firefly provides access to partner models including Google's Gemini (Nano Banana), OpenAI's GPT Image, FLUX.2, Runway and Kling AI, alongside Adobe's own Firefly models, through one login.

What's the difference between Firefly Pro and Pro Plus?

Pro includes 4,000 monthly credits at $19.99/mo, while Pro Plus includes 10,000 monthly credits at $49.99/mo (currently discounted); Pro Plus also unlocks unlimited generation on more models under Adobe's current promotional terms.

Is Adobe Firefly better than Midjourney?

It depends on the goal: Firefly is generally rated better for workflow integration, commercial licensing safety and multi-model access, while Midjourney is more consistently rated higher for raw artistic and stylistic image quality.
