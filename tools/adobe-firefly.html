Adobe Firefly

Commercially safe AI image generation inside Adobe apps

Free tier available; paid plans from ~$9.99/mo

Firefly is trained on licensed and public-domain content, which makes it a preferred choice for teams that need commercially safe assets inside Photoshop and Illustrator.

Best for

Agencies and brand teams that need commercially safe imagery inside an existing Adobe workflow.

Key features

  • ✓Generative Fill and Expand inside Photoshop
  • ✓Trained exclusively on licensed and public-domain content
  • ✓Full IP indemnification for commercial use
  • ✓Built into Illustrator, Express and Creative Cloud

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The safe default for brand-safe, commercially licensed AI images
  • Deep integration with the Adobe apps teams already use
  • Indemnification reduces legal risk for agencies and brands

Cons

  • Generative credits drain quickly on lower plans
  • Best experienced inside the Adobe ecosystem
