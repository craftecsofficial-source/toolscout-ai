Hive Inspect is a strong pick for working home inspectors who want AI-assisted comment writing and booking, agreements and payments under one flat subscription. It is a poor pick for anyone who is not a home inspector, or who just wants general voice notes or meeting transcripts. The core idea is Smart Add: you speak or type a few words about a defect, the app offers up to three matching comments from your own template library with match scores, and only drafts new wording, in your company's voice, when nothing fits. AI Comment Merge can fold up to three related defects into one comment, and a Context Layer box can add how common a defect is and how involved repairs usually are. Photos and video are attached to findings with markup tools, and the finished report goes out as a branded web or PDF report through client and agent portals. The vendor (Synapse Mobility) says the inspector stays responsible for every judgment on condition and safety; treat the AI as a writing accelerator. The time-saved figures on the site are the vendor's own claims. Pricing is flat at $99 per inspector per month with no per-report fees, there is a 5-report free trial with no card, and the AI tools need an internet connection even though the rest of the app works offline.

Best for

Solo and small-team home inspectors who write reports regularly and want writing, scheduling, payments and client delivery in one tool. Not for people outside home inspection, for very low-volume inspectors who would rather not carry a $99 monthly fee (the 10-report pack exists), or for anyone who only needs generic dictation or meeting notes.

Key features

01

Smart Add

You describe a finding in a few words by voice or text. The app shows up to three of your existing template comments with match scores and only drafts a new comment when nothing fits.

02

AI Comment Assist and Merge

Expands a brief description into a full comment in your company's voice, and can combine up to three related defects into one comment. Templates can also be rewritten with AI.

03

Photo, video and markup

Unlimited photos and video clips, a rapid-fire camera, arrows, circles, boxes, text and crop tools, location tags, and hands-free capture by volume button or voice keyword.

04

Context Layer

A labeled box in the report showing how common a defect is nationally and in your state and how involved the repair usually is. It can be turned off.

05

Templates and state forms

Import templates from Spectora, HIP or HomeGauge, use the Template Hub (for example commercial, mold, radon, pools), and use state forms such as Texas TREC, Florida wind mitigation and NPMA-33.

06

Offline and cross-device writing

iPhone, Android and web apps. You can write and edit without signal and sync later, then finish the same report at a desk.

07

Business management and delivery

Online booking, agreements, payments via Stripe, reminders, client and agent portals, a repair request builder, team permissions and pay splits, QuickBooks Online, Google Calendar and Mailchimp integrations, a Repair Estimator PDF emailed after publishing, and Spanish or French translation. An office AI agent and AI scheduling agent are listed as coming soon.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • One flat price covers every AI tool, the Repair Estimator and business features, with no per-report fees on the main plans.
  • It tries your own template comments first and drafts new text only when none match, which keeps wording consistent and limits invented text.
  • Writing and editing work offline in the field and sync later, which suits basements and crawlspaces.
  • Templates can be imported from Spectora, HIP and HomeGauge, and data migration is free, which lowers switching cost.
  • 5 free reports with no card and no time limit let you test on real inspections.

Cons

  • It is a vertical tool with no value outside home inspection.
  • The AI tools need an internet connection, so offline work is manual until you reconnect.
  • Time-saved figures are the vendor's own claims; results depend on how you write reports today.
  • Each extra inspector costs $69/month, and the new-inspector plan and 10-report pack are limited to single-inspector businesses.
  • The pricing page shows a year-end promo with no stated end date, so the price you see may change.
  • The pages checked do not say whether report data is used for AI training; the Data Privacy Pledge only says client data is not sold, so ask Hive directly.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Hive Inspect cost?

$99/month or $999/year per inspector with all AI tools included. Extra inspectors are $69/month or $599/year. There is also a new-inspector ramp ($39, $59, $79, then $99), a 10-report pack for $99, and a year-end promo of $799 for the first year.

Is there a free trial?

Yes: 5 free reports with the full software, no credit card and no time limit. Adding a second inspector requires a paid plan.

Does the AI write the findings for me?

No. The vendor says the inspector decides condition, severity and safety. The AI suggests or drafts wording from your short input and you approve it.

Does it work offline?

Writing and editing work offline and sync later, but the AI tools need an internet connection.

Can I bring my existing templates?

Yes, the site says you can import templates from Spectora, HIP or HomeGauge, and data migration is free.
