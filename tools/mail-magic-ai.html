Mail Magic AI is an AI email-drafting assistant built by Elysium Technology FZ-LLC (with the web extension published under 9361-9195 Quebec Inc), positioned as a productivity tool for Gmail and Outlook users who spend significant time composing replies. Its core mechanic is machine learning trained on a user's own outbox: the system analyzes past sent messages to learn writing patterns — vocabulary, sentence length, formality — and then automatically generates a draft reply as soon as a new email arrives, before the user even opens their inbox. Users review and edit the AI draft rather than starting from a blank compose window, with tone controls (professional, casual, friendly, formal, persuasive) to adjust the output for different situations.

The product spans multiple surfaces: a web application (mailmagic.ai) with monthly subscription tiers, a free Chrome extension usable inside Gmail/Outlook web interfaces for on-demand writing help (composing, grammar fixes, translation, tone adjustment, summarization), a Google Workspace Marketplace listing, and a separate native iOS app ("MailMagic – AI Email Assistant," published under the trymailmagic.com domain) with its own in-app purchase pricing. The company also ships a family of adjacent free browser extensions applying the same GPT-powered assistant pattern to other platforms — Shopify, Shoplazza, TikTok, WhatsApp, Instagram, YouTube, and Google Reviews — suggesting a broader "AI assistant everywhere you type" strategy rather than a single focused product. Security messaging emphasizes ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification and real-time processing without persistent data storage, an important claim given the tool requires deep inbox access to function.

Best for

Mail Magic AI suits individual professionals, customer support staff, and small teams who send a high volume of similar-style emails and want AI to pre-draft responses that already match their tone. It is a weaker fit for security-sensitive organizations uncomfortable granting broad inbox access to a small vendor, or for users who want a single consistent price across web and mobile rather than the current split web/iOS pricing.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The Chrome Web Store listing shows a 5.0/5 average from 41 ratings, with reviewers specifically citing the tone-matching and translation features as time-savers for international communication.
  • ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification is a concrete, verifiable security credential — meaningful reassurance for a product that requires reading a user's email history to function.
  • Native support for both Gmail and Outlook (rather than just one) covers the two dominant business email platforms out of the box.
  • The free Chrome extension acts as a genuine no-cost entry point (grammar, tone, translation, summarization) before any paid commitment is required.
  • Google Workspace Marketplace listing and "good record with no history of violations" badge from the Chrome Web Store add a layer of platform-level trust verification.

Cons

  • Review counts are very small across every surface (5 App Store ratings, 41 Chrome ratings), so the high average scores should be read cautiously rather than as proof of scale reliability.
  • The main web product is still labeled "BETA" on its own homepage as of the latest check, suggesting ongoing instability or incomplete features.
  • Pricing is fragmented: the web Personal plan is $14.99/month while the separate iOS app charges $5.99/month or $39.99/year for what appears to be a narrower feature set, which is confusing for prospective buyers.
  • The vendor is a small, lesser-known company (Elysium Technology FZ-LLC / 9361-9195 Quebec Inc), which is a meaningful trust consideration given the tool needs deep inbox read access.
  • Automatically drafting replies from inbox content requires granting broad email permissions, a bigger privacy/security commitment than typical writing-assistant tools that only see text you paste in.

Frequently asked questions

How does Mail Magic AI learn my writing style?

It uses machine learning to analyze your past sent-email responses and generates new drafts that mimic your vocabulary, tone, and phrasing patterns.

Which email platforms does it support?

Mail Magic AI integrates natively with both Gmail and Outlook, on desktop and mobile, plus a separate iOS app and Chrome extension.

Is there a free way to try it?

Yes — the Chrome extension offers free grammar, translation, tone, and summarization features, and the Personal web plan includes a 7-day free trial.

How much does it cost?

The Personal plan is $14.99/month with tiered draft limits (600, 1,200, or unlimited); Enterprise is custom-priced; the separate iOS app charges $5.99/month or $39.99/year.

Is my email data secure?

The company states it uses industry-standard encryption, has passed Google's security review, and holds ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification, with real-time processing and no stated persistent data storage.

Can it write emails in other languages?

Yes — Mail Magic AI supports multilingual drafting and can translate messages, which reviewers specifically highlight as useful for international communication.
