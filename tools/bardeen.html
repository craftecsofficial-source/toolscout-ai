Bardeen is a good fit for a salesperson, recruiter or founder who builds prospect lists in small to medium batches and wants scraping, contact enrichment and AI scoring in one tool. Skip it if you need very high-volume scraping on a budget, or general-purpose automation across hundreds of SaaS apps, because every row you process spends credits.

The product is built around four steps: a scraper (custom or premium, plus templates for sites such as GitHub, YouTube, Crunchbase and Zillow), web search that researches leads, an enrichment step that finds emails and phone numbers, and an AI step that ranks leads against a description of your ideal customer. Results export to CSV, Google Sheets, Airtable or Notion. Pricing is credit-based: scraping, web search and AI tools cost 1 credit per row, enrichment costs 3, and utilities are free. The pricing page gives an example where scraping 10 profiles, pulling contacts, verifying emails and qualifying them uses 64 credits, which shows how fast enrichment-heavy runs add up. Every account gets 100 free credits monthly.

Best for

Good for sales reps, recruiters, RevOps staff and solo founders building lead or candidate lists from the web without engineers. Not for teams doing very high-volume scraping on a tight budget, or those who want broad app-to-app workflow automation or full browser-agent control.

Key features

01

Web Scraper

A point-and-click scraper extracts structured data (search results, profile details, tables) from virtually any website without writing selectors or code.

02

Web Search

Automates searching across the web for leads or research targets instead of manually running and reviewing search queries.

03

Enrichment

Validates and enriches emails, phone numbers, and company data pulled from scraped records using third-party data providers.

04

AI Tools / Qualify with AI

Uses an LLM to score and qualify scraped leads against custom criteria automatically, flagging which records meet the target profile.

05

Bardeen Agents

Chains multiple actions — scrape, validate, qualify, export — into a single automated agent run rather than manual step-by-step execution.

06

Playbook Library

Hundreds of pre-built, editable automation templates ("playbooks"/"autobooks") cover common sales, recruiting, and research workflows.

07

Native Integrations

One-click export sends data to Google Sheets, Airtable, Notion, and major CRMs to keep records synced without manual copy-paste.

08

Enterprise Security

SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and CASA Tier 2/3 certification support handling sensitive prospect and customer data at scale.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • One workflow covers scraping, web search, enrichment and AI qualification, so you avoid paying for and wiring together separate tools.
  • The permanent 100 free credits per month is enough to validate a real workflow before paying.
  • Prebuilt scraper templates for popular sites cut setup time versus building selectors yourself.
  • Exports go to CSV, Google Sheets, Airtable and Notion, covering most small-team lead-list workflows.
  • The site claims SOC 2 Type II, GDPR and CASA Tier 2/3 certification, which helps with security reviews (we did not independently audit this).

Cons

  • Costs scale per row: a few thousand enriched leads can consume a whole Premium month, since enrichment is 3 credits per row.
  • Unused credits expire at the end of each billing period, so uneven usage wastes money.
  • The pricing page does not clearly say which features belong to which paid tier, so confirm before buying.
  • It is a specialised lead-gen tool; if you want generic triggers and actions between SaaS apps, a general automation platform fits better.

Frequently asked questions

Is Bardeen free?

Partly. Every account gets 100 credits per month at no cost, and unused credits expire. Beyond that you pay for a Basic, Premium or Enterprise plan.

How many credits does a task use?

Scraper, web search and AI tools cost 1 credit per row; enrichment costs 3 credits per row; utilities are free. Check the pricing page for the import/export wording, which is ambiguous.

What do the paid plans cost?

Basic starts at $10/month for 100 credits, Premium at $50/month for 1,000 credits or $480/year for 12,000, and Enterprise is custom. Larger credit amounts are selectable on the page.

Where can I send the data?

CSV, Google Sheets, Airtable and Notion are listed on the site.

How does it differ from a browser AI agent like HARPA AI?

Bardeen is organised around lead research, enrichment and qualification with metered credits; HARPA AI is a general browser automation assistant. Pick Bardeen for lead lists, HARPA for broader browser tasks.
