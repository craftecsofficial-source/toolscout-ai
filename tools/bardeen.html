Bardeen is a browser-based automation platform focused on go-to-market (GTM) teams: sales, recruiting, revenue operations, and customer success professionals who need to pull data off the web, clean it up, and act on it without writing code. Its core building blocks are a point-and-click Web Scraper that extracts structured data (search results, LinkedIn profile details, tables) from virtually any site, a Web Search tool that automates searching for leads or research targets, and an Enrichment module that validates and appends emails, phone numbers, and company data to scraped records. An AI Tools layer lets users "qualify with AI," scoring and filtering leads against custom criteria automatically, and results export in one click to Google Sheets, Airtable, Notion, or a CRM.

Bardeen positions itself less as a generic app-to-app automation tool (the Zapier/Make category) and more as a specialized GTM research and automation engine, chaining scrape → validate → enrich → qualify → export into what it calls a "Bardeen Agent." It ships with a large library of pre-built "playbooks" (also called autobooks) that users can run as-is or customize, covering use cases like account list building, job-post tracking, and competitor monitoring. The company reports customers including Deel, Kearney, and Casfari, with case studies citing tens of hours saved per week on manual research and prospecting. Bardeen is SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and CASA Tier 2/3 certified, and prices access through a credit system — 1 credit per scraped/searched row, 3 credits per enrichment row — layered under Basic, Premium, and custom Enterprise plans.

Best for

Bardeen is best suited to sales, recruiting, and revenue operations teams that need to repeatedly scrape, validate and qualify leads from the web (especially LinkedIn) and push clean data into a CRM or spreadsheet without engineering help. It's a weaker fit for teams that need generic app-to-app automation across hundreds of SaaS tools (a category where Zapier/Make have broader native integrations) or for budget-conscious users planning very high-volume scraping, since credit costs scale directly with usage.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Users consistently describe multi-hour manual data-collection tasks (e.g., scraping LinkedIn profiles, building account lists) reduced to minutes — one G2 reviewer called it a tool that "revolutionized our data collection process."
  • The playbook library removes most of the setup burden: instead of building an automation from scratch, users can start from a pre-built template and edit it to fit their workflow.
  • G2 reviewers repeatedly single out Bardeen's support team as fast and hands-on, including help wiring up bespoke automations for non-technical users.
  • Covers the full GTM research loop — scrape, validate, enrich, qualify with AI, export — in one tool rather than requiring a scraper plus separate enrichment and CRM-sync tools.
  • Holds a 4.8/5 rating on G2 across 36 reviews and 4.5/5 on Capterra, indicating consistently strong satisfaction despite a relatively small but vocal review base.
  • SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and CASA certifications give it credibility for handling sensitive prospect data at enterprise scale.

Cons

  • The credit-based system is a recurring complaint: heavy scraping or enrichment work can burn through a plan's monthly allowance quickly, and several reviewers specifically ask for a flat-rate "unlimited scraper" option.
  • More complex automations involving conditions, loops, or multi-step agent logic have a real learning curve, which some reviewers needed direct support to get past.
  • A minority of reviews report the Chrome extension being resource-intensive, with one G2 review describing high CPU/RAM usage; this appears to be an outlier rather than the norm but is worth testing on your own machine.
  • Enrichment actions cost 3x a standard credit, so lead-enrichment-heavy workflows are proportionally more expensive than pure scraping workflows.

What reviewers say

Bardeen is highly rated on G2 (4.8/5 across 36 reviews) and Capterra (4.5/5, though from a small sample), with reviewers consistently praising time saved on data collection and responsive support, while credit costs and the learning curve for advanced automations are the most common criticisms.

Frequently asked questions

What does Bardeen actually do?

Bardeen is a browser-based automation platform that scrapes data from websites (including LinkedIn), validates and enriches it (emails, phone numbers, company data), optionally qualifies leads with AI, and exports the results to tools like Google Sheets, Airtable, Notion or a CRM.

How does Bardeen's credit pricing work?

Each scraped or searched row costs 1 credit, each enrichment row costs 3 credits, and importing/exporting data is free; the Basic plan ($10/mo) includes 100 credits and Premium ($50/mo) includes 1,000 credits, with unused credits expiring at the end of the billing period.

Is there a free version of Bardeen?

Every account always gets 100 free credits per month, letting users try scraping, enrichment and AI qualification at a small scale before upgrading.

What is a Bardeen "playbook"?

A playbook (also called an autobook) is a pre-built, editable automation template for a common workflow — like scraping a Google Maps list or qualifying inbound leads — that users can run as-is or customize.

Does Bardeen work with my CRM?

Bardeen integrates natively with Google Sheets, Airtable and Notion, and can push data into major CRMs; it also supports Zapier/Make/n8n-style webhook triggers for broader connectivity.

How is Bardeen rated by users?

Bardeen holds a 4.8 out of 5 rating on G2 (36 reviews) and 4.5 out of 5 on Capterra, with reviewers most often praising its scraping speed/accuracy and support responsiveness, and most often flagging the credit-based pricing model as a downside.
