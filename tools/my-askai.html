My AskAI is a no-code platform for building AI-powered chatbots trained on a business's own content. Users point the tool at their website, upload PDFs or documents, or paste in help-center articles, and My AskAI automatically indexes that content so the resulting chatbot can answer visitor questions in natural language, sourced from real company information rather than generic AI knowledge.

The product is aimed at reducing repetitive customer support load: instead of a human agent answering the same FAQ-style questions repeatedly, the chatbot handles them automatically, captures leads when appropriate, and hands off to a human agent when it cannot confidently answer. It is commonly used both as a customer-facing support widget and, in some deployments, as an internal tool for employees to query company documentation.

My AskAI's pricing and setup are designed for small teams and solo operators who want to launch quickly without engineering resources, differentiating it from heavier enterprise support suites that require longer implementation timelines.

Best for

My AskAI suits small businesses, solopreneurs, and SaaS startups that already have a reasonably complete website or help center and want to convert that content into an automated support and lead-capture assistant quickly. It is a good fit for teams without dedicated engineering resources to build a custom RAG-based chatbot themselves. It is less suited to large enterprises needing deep CRM/ticketing integrations, complex multi-department routing, or omnichannel support beyond web chat.

Key features

01

Content ingestion

Automatically crawls a website or accepts uploaded documents and pasted text to build the chatbot's knowledge base without manual scripting.

02

Embeddable widget

A lightweight chat widget can be added to any website or app with a short snippet of embed code.

03

Lead capture

Configurable forms within the chat flow collect visitor contact details and route them to CRM or email tools.

04

Human escalation

When the AI is not confident in an answer, conversations can be escalated to a live agent or logged for follow-up.

05

Conversation analytics

Dashboards show what visitors are asking, where the bot fails to answer, and highlight content gaps to fix.

06

Third-party integrations

Connects to tools like Slack, HubSpot, and Zapier to route leads and notifications into existing workflows.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Setup is genuinely self-serve — most users can go from signup to a live embedded chatbot the same day without developer involvement
  • Published, transparent per-tier pricing (rather than 'contact sales only') makes it easy to budget for small businesses
  • A usable free tier lets teams validate the product before committing budget
  • Conversation analytics double as a content gap analysis tool, surfacing questions the help center doesn't currently answer

Cons

  • Conversation-based pricing means costs can rise quickly for higher-traffic sites, especially moving from Hobby to Pro to Business tiers
  • Customization and workflow logic are simpler than dedicated enterprise support platforms with complex routing rules
  • Answer quality is directly tied to how well-structured and current the source website/documents are, requiring some content upkeep
  • Lacks some of the deeper omnichannel (voice, SMS) capabilities found in larger customer service suites

Frequently asked questions

Does My AskAI require any coding to set up?

No, it is designed as a no-code tool — you add content sources (website URL, documents) and embed a widget snippet, with no custom development needed.

What happens if the chatbot can't answer a question?

Conversations can be configured to escalate to a human agent or be logged so the team can review and add missing content.

Is there a free plan?

Yes, My AskAI offers a free plan with one AI agent and up to 100 conversations per month, plus a 30-day trial on paid plans.

Can My AskAI capture leads, not just answer questions?

Yes, it supports configurable lead capture forms within chat conversations that can route to email, CRM, or other integrated tools.

How is pricing calculated?

Pricing is tiered based primarily on monthly conversation volume and number of AI agents, with higher tiers unlocking more integrations and analytics.

Can My AskAI be used internally, not just for customers?

Yes, some teams deploy it as an internal knowledge assistant so employees can query company documentation, in addition to customer-facing support.
