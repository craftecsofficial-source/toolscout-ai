My AskAI is a good fit for support teams that already run a helpdesk and have a decent help center, and want to automate repetitive tickets quickly. It is a poor fit for people wanting a cheap or free website chatbot, since the entry plan is now $199 a month, and for teams needing voice or SMS support.

It indexes your help articles, website pages, documents and past tickets, then answers customer questions from that material. It can sit inside your existing helpdesk or chat widget, take actions like refunds through connected systems, draft replies for human agents in copilot mode, and escalate when unsure. Usage is measured in conversation credits, where one credit equals roughly a ticket with two AI replies.

Best for

Support teams at SaaS, ecommerce and service businesses that already have a helpdesk and a reasonably complete knowledge base, and handle hundreds to thousands of tickets a month. It is NOT for hobbyists or tiny sites wanting a free or sub-$50 chatbot, for companies with thin documentation, or for teams that need voice, SMS or deep omnichannel routing.

Key features

01

Content ingestion

Automatically crawls a website or accepts uploaded documents and pasted text to build the chatbot's knowledge base without manual scripting.

02

Embeddable widget

A lightweight chat widget can be added to any website or app with a short snippet of embed code.

03

Lead capture

Configurable forms within the chat flow collect visitor contact details and route them to CRM or email tools.

04

Human escalation

When the AI is not confident in an answer, conversations can be escalated to a live agent or logged for follow-up.

05

Conversation analytics

Dashboards show what visitors are asking, where the bot fails to answer, and highlight content gaps to fix.

06

Third-party integrations

Connects to tools like Slack, HubSpot, and Zapier to route leads and notifications into existing workflows.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Works with the helpdesk you already have, so rollout does not mean replacing Zendesk, Intercom or similar
  • Training on historic tickets and learning from agent replies to escalated tickets helps it improve on your real questions, not just the docs
  • Copilot mode lets a team start with AI-drafted replies reviewed by humans before allowing automatic answers
  • Pricing is published per plan with stated overage rates and a 30-day trial that needs no credit card

Cons

  • The lowest paid plan is $199/month and no free plan is listed, so it is pricey for very small sites compared with simple docs chatbots
  • Cost depends on ticket volume: overage is about $0.10 to $0.12 per ticket, and email tickets use more credits because the first reply costs double a chat reply
  • Useful extras such as API access, Slack/Teams and branding removal cost $49/month each on Pro
  • Answer quality depends on how complete and current your help content is, so expect ongoing upkeep
  • Vendor stats such as tickets resolved and cost-per-ticket comparisons are self-reported and not independently verified

Frequently asked questions

How much does My AskAI cost?

Pro is $199/month ($130/month billed annually) with 1,000 tickets included. Scale is $499/month ($333 annually) with 2,000 tickets. Enterprise starts at $999/month. Extra tickets are about $0.12 on Pro and $0.10 on Scale.

Is there a free plan or trial?

The pricing page lists a 30-day free trial with no credit card for Pro and Scale (Enterprise also gets a trial). No permanent free plan was shown when checked.

What counts as a conversation credit?

One credit is an average ticket with two AI replies. A ticket resolved in one reply uses 0.5 credits; one needing three replies uses 1.5. Email tickets use more because the first reply costs twice a chat reply.

Do I have to replace my helpdesk?

No. It connects to existing helpdesks and chat widgets such as Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk, Gorgias and HubSpot, and can work as a copilot drafting replies for your agents.

Can it take actions like refunds?

Yes, on Pro and above it can connect to live user data and perform AI tasks such as refunds and cancellations, subject to how you configure it.
