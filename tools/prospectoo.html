Prospectoo bills itself as an outbound platform for B2B revenue teams that fuses three pieces of the top-of-funnel sales motion: a verified contact database, intent data that flags which accounts are actively in-market, and an AI sales development rep that can call, qualify, and route prospects on its own. Rather than treating data sourcing and outreach execution as separate problems solved by separate vendors, Prospectoo tries to close the loop end to end, from identifying a likely buyer to handing a human rep a qualified conversation.

The standout piece is the voice-led AI SDR, which is positioned as adaptable across use cases: screening job candidates for HR teams, qualifying prospective students for EdTech programs, working SaaS buying signals, and running general B2B outbound qualification. Where pure data providers stop at delivering a list and pure SDR-automation tools assume you already have clean data to work from, Prospectoo's pitch is that owning both halves lets the AI agent qualify against fresher, more relevant signals and hand off only prospects worth a human's time.

Best for

Prospectoo is aimed at B2B sales, marketing, and revenue operations teams that need a steady flow of qualified pipeline but don't want to keep expanding a junior SDR team to get it. It's a natural fit for SaaS, fintech, IT services, manufacturing, and similar B2B sellers, as well as adjacent use cases like HR screening and EdTech enrollment where voice-based qualification maps well onto phone screens or admissions calls. Compared with standalone contact-data vendors, it goes further by acting on the data; compared with pure conversational-AI or dialer tools, it differentiates by bundling in its own verified data and intent layer instead of requiring you to plug in a separate database.

Key features

01

Voice-led AI SDR

An AI agent places and adapts outbound qualification calls, using criteria like budget, urgency, ICP fit, and decision-maker involvement to decide who gets escalated to a human.

02

Industry-specific qualification frameworks

The same AI SDR engine can be configured for different qualification logic, from HR candidate screening and EdTech enrollment to standard B2B sales discovery.

03

High-intent B2B contact database

A large pool of verified business contacts with claimed high accuracy on email and mobile numbers, used to seed outbound campaigns and self-serve list building.

04

Intent signal detection

Real-time buying-signal tracking is layered onto the contact data so outreach can be prioritized toward accounts showing active purchase interest.

05

Chrome extension for LinkedIn extraction

A browser extension pulls verified contact details directly from individual LinkedIn profiles for quick, one-off prospecting.

06

Sales Navigator bulk extractor

A companion tool processes multiple LinkedIn Sales Navigator profiles at once to build larger prospect lists faster than manual extraction.

07

Lead management dashboard

A central dashboard organizes extracted and qualified leads, handles deduplication, and supports exporting lists for outreach.

08

Outreach tool integration

Qualified leads and lists can be pushed out via one-click export to outreach platforms such as Smartlead, and CRM integration is offered with the Custom AI SDR plan.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Bundling verified data, intent signals, and an AI SDR into one workflow reduces the number of vendors a sales team has to stitch together for outbound.
  • The AI SDR's ability to adapt its qualification script across HR, EdTech, SaaS, and general sales use cases makes the underlying engine reusable across departments, not just sales.
  • Offering three distinct engagement models (fully managed data, full AI SDR execution, and self-serve credits) lets teams pick how much control versus hand-holding they want.
  • Named compliance alignment with GDPR, CCPA, ISO 27001, and SOC 2, plus a stated 24-hour opt-out turnaround, signals a data-handling posture that larger, risk-conscious buyers will look for.
  • One-click export to outreach tools like Smartlead and CRM integration on the AI SDR plan reduce the manual work of moving qualified leads into a rep's existing sequence.
  • The self-serve tier gives smaller teams or individual reps a lower-commitment way to try the data and extraction tooling without signing up for the full managed offering.

Cons

  • None of the three plans list actual prices, so buyers can't gauge cost fit before talking to sales, which slows evaluation against competitors with transparent pricing.
  • Voice-based AI qualification is inherently harder to judge from a website alone; call quality, tone, and conversion rates likely need to be tested live before committing budget.
  • Claims like '500M+ contacts' and '95%+ accuracy' are vendor-stated figures on the marketing site and aren't independently verified, so buyers should validate data quality with a sample before rolling out broadly.
  • The managed Data as a Service and Custom AI SDR plans appear built for teams with dedicated outbound budget and setup time, which may put them out of reach for very small teams or solo founders.
  • Because the AI SDR needs to be trained on a company's specific ICP and use case, there's likely a ramp-up period before it performs at the level shown in demos or case studies.
  • Heavy reliance on scraped/extracted LinkedIn and Sales Navigator data means outcomes can be sensitive to changes in LinkedIn's own policies or access, which is outside Prospectoo's control.

Frequently asked questions

What does Prospectoo actually do?

It combines a verified B2B contact database, real-time buying-intent signals, and a voice-led AI SDR that qualifies prospects by phone and hands sales-ready conversations to a human rep.

How much does Prospectoo cost?

Pricing is not published; all three offerings (Data as a Service, Custom AI SDR, Self-Serve Data) are quoted after booking a demo, though the self-serve tier is credit-based with no long-term contract.

Is Prospectoo only for sales teams?

Sales and revenue teams are the core audience, but the same AI SDR qualification engine is also positioned for HR candidate screening and EdTech student enrollment calls.

Does Prospectoo replace human SDRs entirely?

It's designed to handle first-line outreach and qualification automatically and then hand off only interested, qualified prospects, so it's positioned to extend an SDR team's capacity rather than fully remove humans from the process.

What compliance standards does Prospectoo follow?

The company states alignment with GDPR, CCPA, ISO 27001, and SOC 2, along with encryption, role-based access controls, and honoring opt-out requests within 24 hours.
