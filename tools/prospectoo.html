Prospectoo suits B2B teams with an outbound budget that want an AI to make first-line qualification calls on top of purchased contact data, and are willing to run a demo-led evaluation. Skip it if you need transparent pricing, a solo-friendly tool, or email-only outreach, since cost and call quality cannot be judged without talking to sales.

It joins three pieces: a contact database, buying-intent signals and a voice-led AI SDR that is trained on your ideal customer profile and passes qualified prospects to your reps. The same qualification engine is pitched for HR screening and EdTech enrollment as well as B2B sales. Smaller users can buy credits for a Chrome extension and web list-builder.

Best for

Aimed at B2B sales, revenue-operations and marketing teams in SaaS, IT services, fintech or similar sectors that need steady qualified pipeline without growing SDR headcount, and at HR or EdTech teams with high-volume phone screening. Not for solo founders or small teams wanting a published-price tool, teams that only need a cheap contact lookup, or organizations unwilling to let an AI place voice calls to prospects.

Key features

01

Voice-led AI SDR

An AI agent runs outreach, follow-ups and qualification calls trained on your ICP, then hands off sales-ready conversations. Part of the Custom AI SDR offering.

02

Use-case qualification

The site positions the same engine for HR screening, EdTech enrollment, B2B SaaS and general outbound.

03

Contact database

Verified contacts with claimed 95%+ email and mobile accuracy. The site cites both 500M+ and 50M+ figures in different sections, so scale is unclear.

04

Intent data

Signal- and trigger-based targeting helps rank accounts showing buying interest; offered as the Data as a Service plan.

05

Chrome extension

Pulls contact details from LinkedIn profiles; included in Self-Serve Data.

06

Bulk list building

A web platform builds larger prospect lists, with verified mobile numbers and email data as an optional add-on.

07

Success manager and refreshes

Data as a Service includes a dedicated success manager and regular list refreshes.

08

CRM integration and analytics

Custom AI SDR lists CRM integration and performance reporting.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Having data, intent signals and calling in one product avoids stitching together several vendors for outbound.
  • The qualification engine is configurable for several use cases, including non-sales ones such as HR and EdTech.
  • Three engagement models (managed data, full AI SDR, self-serve credits) let you choose how hands-on to be.
  • The site names GDPR, CCPA, ISO 27001, SOC 2 alignment, encryption, role-based access and 24-hour opt-out handling, a posture risk-conscious buyers look for (claims not verified by us).
  • Self-serve credits with no long-term lock-in give a lower-commitment way to try the data.
  • Qualified leads can be exported to outreach tools, and CRM integration is listed on the AI SDR plan.

Cons

  • No prices are published for any offering, and self-serve credit rates are not shown, so you cannot compare costs before a sales call.
  • Voice AI quality, tone and conversion rates cannot be assessed from the website; insist on a live pilot.
  • Data claims (95%+ accuracy, 500M+ contacts, 100+ GTM teams) are vendor-stated, and the site gives two different database sizes; ask for a sample and test it.
  • Managed offerings look aimed at teams with real outbound budget and setup time rather than solo users.
  • The AI SDR must be trained on your ICP, so expect a ramp-up period before results match demos.
  • Calling prospects with an AI voice raises consent and telemarketing-rule questions that vary by country; check your own legal requirements.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Prospectoo cost?

Pricing is not public. Data as a Service and Custom AI SDR are quoted after a demo, and Self-Serve Data is credit-based with no published rates. Ask for credit pricing and contract length in writing.

Does the AI SDR replace human SDRs?

It is positioned to handle first-line outreach and qualification and pass on interested prospects, extending a team rather than removing humans entirely.

How accurate is the data?

The site claims 95%+ email and mobile accuracy, but this is unverified and database size figures are inconsistent on the site. Request a sample list and test it before committing.

What compliance does it claim?

The site states GDPR compliance, CCPA readiness, an ISO 27001 badge, SOC 2 alignment, encryption, role-based access and 24-hour opt-out handling. Ask for certificates and a data-processing agreement.

Is there a self-serve option?

Yes, a credit-based Chrome extension and web list builder with no long-term lock-in, though credit costs and expiry are not listed.
