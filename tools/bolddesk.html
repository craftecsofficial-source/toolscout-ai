BoldDesk is a good fit for support teams with many human agents that want to add AI without paying per seat, and a poor fit for tiny teams that only need a free or very cheap ticket inbox, since the current pricing page lists no free plan and AI plans start at $99/month. It is a help desk from Syncfusion covering ticketing, live chat, knowledge base, workflows and SLAs. On top sit AI agents: one answers customers and escalates with a summary, one triages and routes tickets in the background, and one helps staff write replies. Human seats are unlimited; you pay for AI through monthly credits.

Best for

Suits support teams of roughly 5+ human agents, or any team expecting AI to resolve a meaningful share of tickets, who want predictable seat cost. Not for solo founders or very small teams wanting a free or near-free inbox, nor for buyers who want fully fixed monthly costs with no usage metering. If you want no AI at all, the human-only plan from $20/agent/month applies.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Unlimited human agents and tickets on every AI plan, so adding staff does not raise the bill, which suits large or growing teams.
  • Entry AI plan is $99/month on yearly billing, with 15% off versus monthly billing.
  • Covers the whole help desk (ticketing, KB, SLAs, CSAT) so you do not need a separate chatbot plus ticketing tool.
  • Governance features such as approval steps, trace logs and PII/PHI redaction help with sensitive actions like refunds.
  • Page states there are no automatic overage charges; you get alerts at 80% and 100% of credits and can top up at $40 per 1,000 (top-ups do not expire).

Cons

  • The pricing page shows no free plan, only a 15-day trial, so tiny teams may find $99/month a jump.
  • AI cost is variable: a simple task uses 1-2 credits, medium 3-5, complex workflows 8-12, so 500 credits can run out faster than expected.
  • Key team features (chat/WhatsApp/SMS AI, multiple SLAs, CSAT surveys) start at $499/month; HIPAA and multi-brand at $999/month.
  • Monthly billing costs about 17% more than yearly, and yearly commitment is the headline price.
  • Beyond 2,500 credits a month you must contact sales, and the integration ecosystem is smaller than the biggest help desk vendors.

Frequently asked questions

Does BoldDesk charge per agent?

Not on the AI plans: human agents are unlimited and you pay for AI credits. The human-only help desk plan is billed per agent, from $20/agent/month billed yearly.

Is there a free plan?

The pricing page lists none as of 2026-11-02. Every plan has a free 15-day trial.

What happens when AI credits run out?

You get alerts at 80% and 100%; the page says there are no automatic overage charges. Extra credits cost $40 per 1,000 and top-ups do not expire.

How many credits does a task use?

Roughly 1-2 for simple tasks, 3-5 for medium, 8-12 for complex multi-step workflows. Handoffs with no AI answer use no credits.

Which plan do I need for chat, WhatsApp and SMS AI?

Momentum AI at $499/month billed yearly; Scale AI covers email AI only.
