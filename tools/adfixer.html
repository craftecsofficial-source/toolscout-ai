ADFIXER is a strong fit for established Amazon sellers and agencies already spending well above $10k a month on ads who want faster, explainable bid automation. Skip it if you spend little on ads, need one tool for SEO, content and email, or run mostly Meta or Kaufland campaigns, which are still listed as upcoming. It automates bids and budgets for Amazon Ads and DSP, with Google Ads also supported, and an AI assistant called Exo AI lets you build rules. Bidding weighs margin and product lifecycle rather than a flat ACoS target. Algorithm Studio lets you compose and backtest your own bidding logic, and a decision ledger records why each change was made. You can run it yourself or pair it with ADFIXER's PPC specialists. The main thing to model before signing up is the fee: base price plus 3% of spend, with the percentage applying to all spend once you cross $10k on Self-Service. The homepage claims $1.2B in optimized ad spend and a +31% average ROAS lift; these are the vendor's own figures and not independently verified.

Best for

Established Amazon sellers, brands and agencies spending roughly $10k+/mo on Amazon Ads (and optionally Google Ads) who want granular, explainable bid automation. Not for small sellers with low ad spend, teams wanting an all-in-one SEO/content/email platform, or brands whose main ad spend is on Meta or Kaufland, which are not yet supported.

Key features

01

Exo AI hourly bidding

Runs roughly a many bid adjustments per hour across a seller's live campaigns, reacting to conversion data far faster than manual or weekly-cadence management.

02

Goal-based, margin-aware bidding

Lets sellers set profit or ACoS targets per product, with the engine weighing conversion rate, margin, and lifecycle stage (launch vs. mature) instead of one flat target for everything.

03

Keyword funnel management

Organizes keywords by discovery and harvesting stage so winning search terms graduate into tightly bid exact-match campaigns while weak performers get pruned automatically.

04

Day-parting

Shifts bids up or down by hour and day of week based on when conversion rates are historically strongest, cutting spend during low-converting windows.

05

Automated negative-keyword detection

Continuously scans search-term reports and adds wasted-spend terms as negatives without requiring manual review.

06

Decision/audit ledger

Logs the stated reasoning behind every automated bid or budget change, directly addressing the 'black box' complaint sellers have about other AI bidding tools.

07

Algorithm Studio

Lets advanced users assemble custom bidding logic from modular blocks and backtest it against historical account data before it goes live.

08

Cross-channel budget allocation

Automatically reallocates spend between Amazon Ads and Google Ads based on marginal returns, with Meta and Kaufland integration listed as upcoming.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The decision ledger records the reasoning behind each bid or budget change, useful when a client or finance lead asks why spend moved.
  • Bidding accounts for margin and product lifecycle, so a new launch and a mature high-margin SKU are treated differently instead of sharing one ACoS target.
  • Algorithm Studio lets an in-house team compose and backtest custom bidding logic before it touches live spend.
  • Suggestion mode lets you review changes before switching to autopilot, which lowers the trust barrier.
  • No setup fees, seat fees or annual contracts; you can change or cancel from the dashboard.

Cons

  • On Self-Service, once monthly spend crosses $10k the 3% fee applies to the entire month's spend, not just the excess; the pricing page's own example is $15k spend = $750 total. Model this cliff before choosing a plan.
  • Managed PPC charges 3% from the first dollar on top of $999/mo, so costs scale with spend and there is no cap.
  • Meta and Kaufland support are only 'upcoming'; brands with large non-Amazon, non-Google spend need other tools.
  • Free trial length and card requirements are not stated on the pricing page, and Agency pricing requires a sales call.
  • Headline results (e.g. +31% average ROAS lift) are vendor-reported; ask for references in your category.

Frequently asked questions

What does ADFIXER cost in total?

Self-Service is $300/mo plus 3% of ad spend. Past $10k/mo the 3% applies to all spend, so $15k of spend costs $750 total. Managed PPC is $999/mo plus 3% of managed spend from the first dollar. Agency pricing is not public.

Is there a free trial?

The pricing page shows a 'Start free trial' button for Self-Service, but it does not state the trial length or whether a card is needed. Confirm both before signing up.

Does it work beyond Amazon?

It covers Amazon Ads/DSP and Google Ads. Meta and Kaufland are listed as upcoming, so it is not yet a single tool for all paid channels.

Can I see why it changed a bid?

Yes. Every change is logged in a decision ledger, and you can run in suggestion mode (you approve changes) or autopilot.

Is it worth it for a small Amazon seller?

Probably not. The $300 base plus the percentage fee needs to be outweighed by savings or sales lift, which is hard at low ad spend.
