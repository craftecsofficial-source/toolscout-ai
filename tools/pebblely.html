Pebblely suits small ecommerce sellers who need many clean, on-brand listing and ad images from simple product photos; it is a poor choice for brands that need exact color, label or texture fidelity, or for people-centric lifestyle photography. You upload a product photo and it swaps in an AI-generated scene, either from a template or a text prompt, and adds lighting and shadows so the product looks placed in the scene.

The homepage describes turning one product image into assets for marketplaces, social posts, banners and ads, with bulk generation and resizing. It lists channel logos (Shopify, Etsy, Amazon and others) as suitable destinations, which does not confirm direct integrations; the earlier claim of a dedicated Shopify app could not be verified today. The tool is run by Pebblely Pte Ltd, and its figures of 25M+ images generated are the company's own claims. The homepage cites 100+ templates while the pricing page cites 40+ themes, so the exact template count is uncertain.

Best for

Good for small online sellers, solo entrepreneurs and marketing teams producing product-on-surface imagery across a catalog on a budget. Not for brands that need pixel-accurate packaging, text, color or texture (cosmetic shades, fine jewelry detail), nor for anyone who mainly needs photography of people or complex lifestyle scenes, nor for very high-volume teams given the 500 images/month top tier.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Far cheaper and faster than booking a photographer for routine listing shots.
  • Bulk generation on Basic and Pro helps with multi-SKU catalogs.
  • Templates mean most users can avoid writing prompts.
  • Entry price of $9/mo keeps testing low-risk.

Cons

  • AI scenes may alter packaging proportions, printed text or colors, so each image needs a human check before publishing.
  • Close-up, texture-heavy shots are a reported weak spot per independent reviewers cited in the prior listing (not re-verified today).
  • Little control to fine-tune a scene after generation beyond regenerating.
  • Quotas are small: 30 images/month on Lite and 500 on the top plan.
  • Pricing page shows no free plan, and annual prices are not listed, so what a free trial includes could not be verified.

Frequently asked questions

Does Pebblely have a free plan?

The pricing page lists only Lite, Basic and Pro. It has a free-trial FAQ whose answer we could not read, so free access could not be verified; check pebblely.com/pricing.

How much does it cost?

Lite is $9/mo for 30 images, Basic $19/mo for 200, Pro $39/mo for 500. Yearly billing gives 2+ months free, with no annual price listed.

Can I write my own prompts?

Yes, custom prompts are listed on all three paid plans.

Does it work with Shopify and marketplaces?

The homepage shows Shopify, Etsy, Amazon and others as suitable channels, but a direct integration could not be verified.

Is it accurate for packaging and labels?

Not reliably. Always inspect outputs for distorted text, shapes and colors before using them in listings.
