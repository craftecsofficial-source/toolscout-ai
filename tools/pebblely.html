Pebblely (Pebblely Pte Ltd) is a Singapore-based AI product photography tool built specifically for ecommerce sellers who need professional-looking listing images without a physical photo shoot. Users upload a plain product photo, typically shot on a white or plain background, and Pebblely automatically removes the background and replaces it with an AI-generated scene, chosen either from a library of 40+ preset background themes (candle, jewelry, skincare, beverage, furniture, pet products, and more) or described via a custom text prompt. The company says its tool has generated more than 25 million images for creative companies globally, and it markets primarily to small businesses, solo entrepreneurs, and marketing teams managing product catalogs on marketplaces like Amazon, Shopify, and Etsy.

Beyond swapping backgrounds, Pebblely adds AI-rendered lighting, reflections, and shadows so the composited image looks like a single coherent studio shot rather than a cut-and-paste job, and it supports bulk generation so sellers can process a whole catalog rather than one image at a time. A dedicated Shopify app lets store owners generate and apply images directly from their Shopify admin. Compared to general-purpose image generators, Pebblely's narrower focus on product photography means less prompt engineering is required -- the interface is built around uploading a product and picking or describing a scene rather than open-ended text-to-image generation -- but that same specialization means it's less suited to lifestyle or model photography outside the product-on-a-surface format.

Best for

Best suited to small and mid-sized ecommerce sellers, especially Shopify merchants, who need fast, inexpensive listing photography for a growing product catalog and can tolerate occasional manual touch-ups. It's a weaker fit for brands that need pixel-accurate color or texture reproduction, such as cosmetics color-matching or fine jewelry detail, or for anyone needing lifestyle photography involving people or models, since Pebblely is built around product-on-background compositing rather than full scene photography.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • A single subscription replaces the cost and turnaround time of booking a photographer for routine product-listing shots, which matters most for sellers adding new SKUs frequently.
  • Bulk generation and the Shopify app integration let catalog-heavy stores process many products in one workflow instead of uploading images one at a time.
  • The 40+ preset theme library covers common ecommerce categories (skincare, candles, jewelry, beverages, furniture) well enough that most sellers can skip prompt-writing entirely.
  • Pricing scales from a $9/mo entry tier to a $39/mo high-volume tier, so small sellers aren't forced into an expensive plan just to try the tool.

Cons

  • Independent reviewers note that AI-generated scenes don't always preserve exact packaging proportions, textures, or brand colors, so outputs need a human quality check before publishing.
  • The tool performs noticeably worse on close-up, texture-focused product shots than on standard full-product listing images.
  • Editing controls after generation are minimal; there's little ability to fine-tune or adjust a generated scene beyond regenerating it.
  • Independent review coverage is thin: Pebblely shows zero verified reviews on Capterra and only a couple of mixed reviews on Trustpilot, making it harder to gauge consistency of quality at scale.

Frequently asked questions

Does Pebblely have a free plan?

Yes, Pebblely offers a free plan with a limited number of monthly image generations and no credit card required.

Can I use Pebblely images for commercial/business use?

Yes, generated images can be used for commercial purposes including product listings and advertising.

Does Pebblely integrate with Shopify?

Yes, there's a dedicated Shopify app that lets store owners generate product images directly from their Shopify admin.

What image sizes does Pebblely support?

Paid plans support custom output sizes up to 2048x2048 pixels.

Can I write my own prompts instead of using preset themes?

Yes, all plans, including the free plan, support custom text prompts in addition to the 40+ preset background themes.

What happens to unused image credits at the end of the month?

Unused monthly image credits do not roll over; each billing cycle resets the allotment.
