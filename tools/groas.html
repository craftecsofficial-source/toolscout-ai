groas is worth a look for established advertisers who want to hand paid search, Meta ads or SEO to a managed service at a flat fee, and it is a poor fit for small budgets or anyone who wants to run the software themselves. Skip it if you spend only a few hundred to a couple of thousand dollars a month on ads, since $1,499 per service per month would rival your ad spend. groas now describes itself as a done-for-you service rather than the self-serve ad-automation software this listing previously covered; the company says it retired the self-serve product in spring 2026. Businesses pick from three services, each $1,499 a month flat regardless of ad spend: Search Ads (Google, Bing and ChatGPT), Meta Ads, and SEO and AI Search. Each includes a dedicated strategist, a private Slack channel, monthly strategy calls and weekly reports. Agencies can resell it white-label at $299 per client account per service with a five-account minimum. Plans are month-to-month with a 7-day money-back guarantee, not a free trial. Claims like $1bn+ attributable revenue and 500+ clients are the company's own and unverified.

Best for

Good for established advertisers with meaningful ad budgets who want a managed team plus automation instead of a percentage-of-spend agency, and for agencies wanting a white-label back end. Not for small businesses with low ad spend, for people who want to operate the software or control bids themselves, or for anyone expecting a free trial (there is a 7-day refund window instead).

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Flat fee that does not rise with ad spend removes the percentage-of-spend incentive of many agencies.
  • Month-to-month with no setup fees or add-ons, plus a 7-day full-refund window.
  • You pay ad platforms directly and keep campaigns and content if you cancel.
  • Agency pricing is published ($299 per account per service), so margins can be calculated before a sales call.
  • A human strategist and weekly reporting are included, not extra.

Cons

  • Price floor is high: $1,499 per service per month, and covering search, Meta and SEO costs $4,497/mo.
  • It is no longer self-serve software; if you wanted to run an AI tool yourself, this is not that.
  • There is no free trial, only a refund if you ask within 7 days; older listings quoting $999 plans and a live-account trial are out of date.
  • Agency minimum is 5 service-accounts, which excludes freelancers with one or two clients.
  • Headline results and client counts are vendor claims that we could not verify independently.
  • Autonomous or outsourced bidding means less hands-on control over your account.

Frequently asked questions

How much does groas cost now?

Businesses pay $1,499 per month per service (Search Ads, Meta Ads, or SEO and AI Search). Agencies pay $299 per month per client account per service with a minimum of 5. Prices checked 2026-10-27.

Is there a free trial?

No. The site lists a 7-day money-back guarantee instead: request a refund within the first 7 days. After that billing is month-to-month and you can cancel anytime.

Is it software I log into?

The company says it retired its self-serve software in spring 2026 and now sells a done-for-you service with a dedicated strategist.

Does the fee include my ad spend?

No. You pay Google, Meta and other platforms directly; groas charges a flat fee and takes no cut of spend.

Is it worth it for a small business?

Usually not unless your ad budget is large enough that $1,499 per service is a small share of it. At a few thousand dollars of monthly spend the fee would dominate.
