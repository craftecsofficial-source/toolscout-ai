Kommunicate suits small and mid-sized support teams that want an AI agent live quickly with a human fallback, and it is easiest to justify if you plan to grow across WhatsApp and web. Skip it if you need Salesforce or HubSpot on a self-serve budget or very high volumes, since those push you to Enterprise or add-on fees. You build the agent in a no-code builder, connect your knowledge and an AI model (OpenAI, Gemini, Claude or Dialogflow), deploy it to chat, messaging and email channels, and route hard cases to people with the history attached.

Best for

Good for startups, SMBs and mid-market support teams wanting a controllable AI agent with human oversight across several chat channels. NOT for teams needing Salesforce/HubSpot integration without an Enterprise contract, high conversation volumes on a tight budget (overage and add-ons accumulate), or buyers who want a full helpdesk replacement; compare Zendesk AI agents or Chatling.

Key features

01

Kompose agent builder

No-code builder for agent behaviour and flows; the vendor positions it as launching in minutes.

02

Multi-channel deployment

Web, mobile SDKs (Android, iOS, Flutter, React Native), WhatsApp, Telegram and Instagram on Starter; the site also lists Messenger, LINE and Slack.

03

Human handoff

Escalates to a person with conversation context when the agent is unsure or out of scope.

04

Email and voice

AI email automation is included; Voice AI is billed at $0.06 per minute.

05

Model flexibility

Connects to OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, Dialogflow, Amazon Lex and IBM Watson.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Pricing for the two self-serve tiers is public and a 30-day no-card trial is offered, so costs can be tested before committing.
  • Human handoff and the option to decline out-of-scope questions are core to the product, which lowers the risk of an unsupervised bot.
  • Supports bringing your own model or Dialogflow backend, useful if you already built conversational AI elsewhere.
  • Channel coverage is broad for the price, including WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram and mobile SDKs.
  • The site lists ISO 27001, SOC 2, GDPR and HIPAA badges (vendor claims, not verified by me).

Cons

  • Starter includes only 250 conversations a month with one agent and one seat; extra conversations ($15 per 1,000), agents and seats ($20/mo each) add up fast.
  • Zendesk and Freshdesk need Professional ($200/mo) and Salesforce/HubSpot need Enterprise, so CRM-centric teams pay more than the headline.
  • The pricing page labels annual billing as 20% off but the listed annual prices are about 15-16% lower; confirm what you will actually pay.
  • Pricing page inclusions are inconsistent between monthly and annual views (voice and phone-call items), so confirm what your plan includes.
  • Third-party reviews I could not verify today have mentioned thin reporting and sync problems; test analytics in the trial.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes, 30 days on all plans, no credit card required, per the pricing page.

What happens if I exceed my conversations?

Extra conversations are billed per 1,000: $15 on Starter, $10 on Professional.

Which plan do I need for Zendesk or Salesforce?

Zendesk and Freshdesk are on Professional; Salesforce, HubSpot and Zapier are listed under Enterprise.

Is voice included?

Voice AI is billed separately at $0.06 per minute.

Can I use my own AI model?

Yes, it integrates with OpenAI, Gemini, Claude and Dialogflow, among others.
