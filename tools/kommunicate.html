Kommunicate is built by Intentive Technologies, founded by Devashish Mamgain and Adarsh Kumar, who previously co-founded the in-app chat SDK company Applozic in 2015. Kommunicate began in 2018 as a second product inside Applozic and became the founders' primary focus after Applozic's core chat-SDK business was wound down in 2022; the company is based in Bengaluru, India. The product centers on "Kompose," a drag-and-drop AI agent builder that lets non-technical teams launch a working bot in minutes, paired with a philosophy the company calls "Start Safe": automate a small, safe slice of conversations first, keep humans in the loop, and expand automation as confidence grows.

Beyond the core AI agent, Kommunicate bundles live chat, an AI email ticketing product, a voice AI agent, and omnichannel messaging across web, mobile apps, WhatsApp, Telegram, and Instagram, unified in a single dashboard. It integrates with major CRMs and helpdesks (Zendesk, Freshdesk, Salesforce, HubSpot) and with LLM providers (OpenAI, Gemini, Anthropic) and Dialogflow for teams that want to bring their own conversational AI backend. Case studies cited on its site include Conte.it (90% of insurance queries automated), Oklahoma City Community College (8.33 CSAT), TelOne (25% reduced agent workload), and Lula (40% CSAT increase), positioning it as a mid-market to SMB alternative to heavier enterprise platforms like Zendesk AI Agents or Intercom Fin.

Best for

Kommunicate is a strong fit for startups, SMBs, and mid-market support teams that want to launch an AI agent quickly, keep humans in the loop, and expand automation gradually across WhatsApp, web, and other channels without enterprise-level cost or complexity. It's a weaker fit for large enterprises needing deep, out-of-the-box analytics or teams that require Salesforce/HubSpot integration without paying for the Enterprise tier.

Key features

01

Kompose AI agent builder

A no-code, drag-and-drop builder for configuring AI agent conversation flows, letting teams launch a functioning bot in minutes rather than weeks.

02

Omnichannel messaging

Deploys the same AI agent across web, mobile apps (iOS/Android SDKs), WhatsApp, Telegram, and Instagram from one dashboard.

03

AI email ticketing

Automates responses to inbound support email, categorizing and drafting or resolving tickets without a human touching every message.

04

Voice AI agent

Handles phone-based support conversations with AI, billed separately at roughly $0.06/minute regardless of plan tier.

05

Agent Assist and auto-resolve

Available on Professional and above; surfaces AI-drafted responses and conversation summaries to human agents, and lets qualifying tickets auto-resolve without escalation.

06

CRM and helpdesk integrations

Native integrations with Zendesk, Freshdesk, Google Analytics on Professional, and Salesforce, HubSpot, and Zapier on Enterprise.

07

Campaign messaging

Lets teams send proactive, templated outbound messages (e.g., renewal reminders) through the same AI agent infrastructure used for support.

08

Governance and human handoff

Built-in escalation to human agents from day one, with tiered governance controls (Basic to Advanced) so automation scope can be tightly controlled.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • G2 and Capterra reviewers consistently cite the speed of getting a bot live — one reviewer described integrating Dialogflow, WhatsApp, and Facebook in a fraction of the time competing tools required.
  • At $40-$200/month for the two self-serve tiers, Kommunicate undercuts most enterprise AI customer service platforms while still including omnichannel deployment and CRM integrations.
  • Multiple long-time users (2+ years) report that support response times remain fast even years into the relationship, with the team actively implementing feature requests raised by customers.
  • The platform's Dialogflow integration is repeatedly singled out as best-in-class by reviewers who evaluated alternatives, calling it easier than building the connection manually.
  • The 'Start Safe' governance model with built-in human escalation from day one appeals to risk-conscious teams that are wary of fully autonomous AI agents.
  • Case studies show measurable outcomes at scale — 90% query automation for an insurance company (Conte.it) and a 25% reduction in agent workload for a telecom (TelOne).

Cons

  • A recurring Capterra complaint is that reporting is very poor and advanced analytics cannot be done without upgrading, which limits visibility for data-driven support teams.
  • One 1-star Capterra review from a 2+ year user described core functionality gaps: chat histories and assigned conversations not syncing correctly between the web dashboard and mobile app.
  • Higher-value integrations (Zendesk, Freshdesk, Salesforce, HubSpot) are locked behind the Professional or Enterprise tier, so Starter-plan users get comparatively basic connectivity.
  • Extra AI agents, extra seats, and additional conversation blocks all carry incremental add-on costs ($15-$30/mo each), which can make real-world bills higher than the advertised base price.
  • A handful of reviews mention the free trial period feeling short for thorough evaluation, and customer support occasionally needing multiple screenshots to diagnose an issue.

Frequently asked questions

Does Kommunicate offer a free trial?

Yes, all plans include a 30-day free trial with no credit card required, so teams can build and test an AI agent before committing to a paid plan.

What channels does Kommunicate support?

Web, mobile apps (iOS/Android), WhatsApp, Telegram, and Instagram are included from the Starter plan; phone-based Voice AI is billed separately at about $0.06/minute.

How is a 'conversation' counted for billing?

A conversation includes all messages exchanged in a single session; Starter includes 250/month and Professional includes 2,000-2,500/month, with overages billed per additional 1,000 conversations.

Can I use my own AI model with Kommunicate?

Yes, it integrates with OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic, and Dialogflow ES/CX, so teams can bring an existing conversational AI backend into the Kompose builder.

Does Kommunicate integrate with Zendesk or Salesforce?

Zendesk and Freshdesk integrations are available starting on the Professional plan; Salesforce, HubSpot, and Zapier integrations require the Enterprise plan.

What do reviewers say is Kommunicate's biggest weakness?

The most common criticism across G2 and Capterra reviews is limited built-in reporting and analytics compared to larger competitors.
