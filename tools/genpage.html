GenPage is worth a trial for B2B teams running paid ads, cold outbound or account-based campaigns who need many tailored landing pages without developer time. Skip it if you only need one or two pages, if you want deep hand-tuned personalization of a corporate site for a few named accounts, or if you dislike metered usage. It generates a page variant for each keyword, ad group, account or outreach list by learning your existing site's look and messaging, and it can pull lists in from CSV, Clay, Apollo, HeyReach, Google Sheets, Zapier or an API. Published variants are tested against each other; the pricing page lists Autopilot only under the custom-priced Ultimate tier. Cost is driven by credits: publishing one variant costs 1 credit, ten AI enrichments cost 1 credit, and page generation varies by model and complexity. Plan credits reset monthly, and top-ups run from $0.25 per credit (100 for $25) down to $0.16 (3,000 for $479), always dearer than plan credits. If credits run out, live pages keep serving traffic but new generation stops. Pro ($79/mo) adds unlimited seats and custom domains; heatmaps, session replays, webhooks and the HTML editor start at Scale ($249/mo). The Ads Module for Google and LinkedIn is extra, from about $399/mo, and the pricing table is ambiguous on how it is sold, so confirm with the vendor. Independent reviews are thin, so test on the free plan first. Note: ToolScout previously compared it with Mutiny, but Mutiny now appears in our directory as a different kind of product, so that comparison was removed.

Best for

Small and mid-size B2B marketing, growth and RevOps teams running paid ads, outbound or ABM who want many campaign-specific pages quickly and can manage a credit budget. Not for teams needing only a couple of pages, for those wanting enterprise-grade personalization of an existing corporate site, or for buyers who need fixed, predictable monthly costs.

Key features

01

AI page generation per audience

Builds page variants for a keyword, ad campaign, account or outreach list from your site, brand messaging and campaign data, or from blocks and templates.

02

Bulk import for outbound and ABM

Lead or account lists come in from CSV, Clay, Apollo, HeyReach, Google Sheets, Zapier or the API so many pages can be generated from one list.

03

Smart variables and enrichment

Custom and AI variables adapt copy and visuals per visitor; AI enrichments run at ten per credit.

04

Analytics and alerts

Page analytics and Slack/Gmail alerts are on every plan; heatmaps and session replays require Scale or above.

05

Custom domains and brand control

Paid plans host pages on your domain, remove the 'Made with GenPage' badge and match your site's design.

06

Integrations, API and MCP

GTM and CRM integrations plus MCP and API access start on Pro; webhooks, SDK and bring-your-own-key model selection start on Scale; a paid Ads Module syncs Google and LinkedIn Ads.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Free, Pro and Scale are self-serve, so you can test whether generated pages lift conversion before talking to sales.
  • Unlimited seats on every paid plan means adding marketers or SDRs does not raise the bill.
  • Bulk imports from Clay, Apollo, HeyReach and CSV make it practical for outbound and ABM lists.
  • Live pages keep serving when credits run out, and keeping them live costs nothing.
  • Analytics and Slack/Gmail alerts are built in, saving a separate tool.

Cons

  • Cost is metered: each published variant costs a credit, so heavy testing can exhaust the allowance, and top-ups are $0.16-$0.25 per credit versus cheaper plan credits.
  • Heatmaps, session replays, the HTML editor, webhooks and SDK are Scale-only ($249/mo), so Pro users do not get full optimization data.
  • The Ads Module is a separate add-on from about $399/mo, and the pricing table is unclear about which plans it attaches to.
  • Autopilot, SSO, RBAC and the brand knowledge base are Ultimate-only and sales-gated.
  • Plans are shown per month with a yearly-billing discount but no annual totals, so the exact bill is not clear from the page.
  • Independent third-party review volume is thin; we did not verify any ratings today.

Frequently asked questions

How much does GenPage cost?

Free (100 credits/mo), Pro $79/mo, Scale $249/mo, and custom Ultimate. Prices are listed monthly with a yearly-billing option showing 2 months free; extra credit options and the Ads Module (from about $399/mo) cost more.

Is there a free plan?

Yes, an ongoing free plan with 100 credits/month, 1 seat and 1 workspace, no card required. It includes the AI page builder, unlimited pages and analytics.

What does a credit buy?

Publishing a variant costs 1 credit and ten AI enrichments cost 1 credit; page generation varies by model and complexity. Plan credits reset monthly, and purchased top-ups last 12 months.

What happens when I run out of credits?

Published pages keep serving traffic, but generating new pages stops until you top up or upgrade.

Which plan do I need for custom domains and heatmaps?

Custom domains, badge removal and API/MCP access start on Pro. Heatmaps, session replays, the HTML editor and webhooks need Scale.
