Resleeve, built by Resleeve B.V. (headquartered in Eindhoven, the Netherlands), started as a narrower AI fashion design tool that turned sketches into photorealistic apparel and lookbook imagery, but has since expanded into a broad, prompt-driven AI business platform. Its current positioning is "Build. Launch. Grow.": rather than being a single-purpose image generator, Resleeve combines an AI app builder, brand-trained image and video generation, audio/music tools, marketing content creation, and automation agents into one workspace. The company explicitly frames itself as a replacement for stacks of separate tools, listing itself as an alternative to Midjourney, Adobe, and Leonardo for images; Runway, Sora, and Pika for video; ElevenLabs and Suno for audio; and Lovable, v0, and Cursor for app building, all accessible from a single natural-language prompt composer.

Within that broader platform, the image generation capability is brand-trained, meaning outputs are tuned to a user's established visual style, and covers product photography, headshots, avatars, logos, posters, and social assets, powered by current-generation models such as Nano Banana 2 and Seedance 2. The differentiation from single-purpose image generators is the surrounding workflow: a generated product photo can flow directly into a landing page, an ad campaign, or an automated follow-up sequence without leaving Resleeve or re-uploading assets elsewhere. Users retain full commercial rights and ownership over everything generated, and the company states user inputs are never used to train models accessible to other customers. Pricing is denominated in real dollars rather than abstract credits, with a monthly balance that can be spent across any combination of images, video, audio, apps, or automations, plus auto top-up options and dedicated Enterprise plans for teams needing custom volume, SLAs, and integrations.

Best for

Resleeve suits founders, creators, and small marketing teams who want AI image generation bundled with app building, landing pages, and automated marketing/sales follow-up in one prompt-driven workspace. It's a weaker fit for buyers who need a dedicated, review-validated, image-only generator, or enterprise teams that require established third-party satisfaction benchmarks before adopting a newer platform.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The unified prompt-to-outcome workflow means a generated brand image can immediately feed into an app, landing page, or ad campaign without exporting and re-importing across separate tools, a meaningful time saver for solo founders.
  • Dollar-based balances instead of ambiguous credit units make it straightforward to estimate real cost per image or video, addressing a common complaint about competitor credit systems.
  • Users retain 100% commercial rights and the company states inputs are never used to train shared models, addressing IP and privacy concerns common with AI generation tools.
  • Free trial includes $25 in starting credit with no credit card required, letting prospective users test image generation and other mediums before paying.
  • Broad third-party tool integration (Figma, Notion, Slack, Stripe, GitHub, Linear) lets teams plug Resleeve into an existing stack rather than migrating entirely.

Cons

  • No established presence yet on major review platforms (G2, Capterra, Trustpilot) with verifiable rating counts, so buyers can't benchmark real-world satisfaction against a large independent sample.
  • Because image generation is one of five pooled mediums sharing the same balance, teams with heavy image needs may find their monthly allotment (~250 images on Basic) consumed faster than on an image-dedicated plan.
  • As a fast-expanding, broad platform, the depth of any single capability, including image generation, may lag behind specialist competitors that focus exclusively on one medium.
  • The company's positioning has shifted significantly from its original fashion-design focus, which could create confusion for buyers expecting the earlier, narrower product.

Frequently asked questions

Is Resleeve just an image generator?

No, Resleeve is a broader AI platform that includes brand-trained image generation alongside AI app building, video, audio, marketing automation, and sales/support agents in one workspace.

What does Resleeve cost?

Plans start at $19/month (Basic) and $49/month (Plus, most popular), each providing a monthly dollar balance spendable across images, video, audio, apps, and automations; Enterprise pricing is custom.

Do I own the images I generate?

Yes, Resleeve states users retain 100% commercial rights and ownership over everything they create on the platform.

Does Resleeve use my data to train its models?

No, the company states user inputs stay private and are never used to train models accessible to other users.

Can I try Resleeve for free?

Yes, new users get $25 in starting credit with no credit card required to test image generation and other features.

What image models does Resleeve use?

Resleeve provides access to current top-tier models including Nano Banana 2 and Seedance 2 for image and video generation.
