Suno is great for people who want a finished, vocal-led song from a sentence or a set of lyrics without any musical training. Skip it if you need exact control over arrangement and mix, reproducible results, or a clean legal position on how the model was trained. It generates audio directly: you describe a style or paste lyrics, and it returns a full track with singing and instruments. The site promotes its v6 model as the newest, though its own FAQ still mentions v5.5, so the exact model naming is inconsistent. Beyond the prompt box, paid tiers add stem splitting, custom voices recorded by the user, custom models, and on Premier a browser editor (Suno Studio) with MIDI, effects and automation. Song downloads are metered separately from credits, which matters if you produce a lot of tracks.

Best for

Good for content creators, marketers, hobbyist songwriters and indie game or video makers who need original songs quickly and accept some randomness. Not for professional producers who need precise, repeatable arrangement control, artists who want fully human-authored masters, or anyone who must avoid any legal uncertainty around AI training data.

Key features

01

Text-to-song generation

Describe a genre and mood or supply your own lyrics and get a complete track with vocals and instrumentation.

02

Remix and rewrite

Change a song's style, rewrite its lyrics, or build a new track on top of your own recording.

03

Voices and custom models

Pro and Premier users can record or upload a voice to sing their lyrics, and train custom models on their own recordings.

04

Stem separation

Split a song into time-aligned stems for use in other audio software; Pro offers two split types, Premier three including an advanced split.

05

Suno Studio

A browser-based multitrack editor on Premier with MIDI, effects, automation and plugin design, plus adding new vocals or instruments to existing songs.

06

Creative controls

Sliders for weirdness and style influence, style exclusions and vocal gender help steer results, though outcomes still vary run to run.

07

Commercial rights

Pro and Premier songs may be used commercially per the pricing page; free-plan songs may not.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Speed and simplicity: a full vocal song from a prompt in about a minute makes it easy to test many ideas.
  • The free plan is a real trial: about 10 songs a day with no credit card, enough to judge quality before paying.
  • Pro is cheap for what it unlocks: commercial rights, priority queue, stems and custom voice for $8/mo.
  • Premier's Studio editor means output is not a locked file; you can arrange, edit and export MIDI.
  • Wide range of genres and vocal styles with controls like style exclusions and vocal gender.

Cons

  • Downloads are capped separately from credits (20/mo Pro, 60/mo Premier); extra downloads cost more, which is a gotcha for heavy producers.
  • Subscription credits do not roll over, and purchased top-ups require an active subscription.
  • Free plan gives no commercial rights, no stems and only free models.
  • Regenerating the same prompt gives different songs, so exact results take trial and error.
  • The pricing page is inconsistent about annual savings (20% vs 25%) and the site mixes v6 and v5.5 naming; check checkout before paying.
  • Labels sued Suno in 2024 over training data; I could not verify the current status of those cases today, so check recent news before relying on output commercially.

Frequently asked questions

Is Suno free?

Yes. The free plan gives 50 credits a day (up to about 10 songs) with no card, but it uses free models only and does not include commercial rights.

What does Suno cost?

Pro is $8/mo and Premier is $24/mo as shown on the pricing page today. Annual billing is advertised as a discount, but the page does not state the annual price clearly, so confirm at checkout.

Can I use songs commercially?

The pricing page lists commercial rights for Pro and Premier and none for Free. Read Suno's current terms for the details of what that covers.

What are the download limits?

Pro includes 20 song downloads a month and Premier 60; extra credits and downloads can be bought as add-ons.

How does it compare with other options in this category?

Suno is focused on whole songs with vocals from a prompt. Tools like Musicfy or Kits.AI are centered on voice conversion and vocal tools, and Landr on mastering and distribution of music you already made.
