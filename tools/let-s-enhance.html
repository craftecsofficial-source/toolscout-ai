Let's Enhance is a strong pick for photographers, sellers and marketers who regularly need to enlarge or clean up low-quality images, and overkill for someone resizing a photo once in a while. It uses AI to upscale, sharpen, denoise and restore images, and has expanded into background removal, image generation and video tools.

Processing modes include Prime (default), Gentle for small text and fine detail, and an Old Photo mode for damaged pictures. Paid plans remove watermarks, allow larger outputs and add priority processing. Developers can use its Enhancer and Upscaler APIs.

Best for

E-commerce sellers, photographers, print shops and content teams with many weak or small images, and developers who want an enhancement API. Not for occasional users who need one or two resizes (the free plan is watermarked and capped at 8 MP upscale), nor for anyone who needs exact, public volume pricing for business or API use without talking to sales.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • One credit per image makes costs easy to predict on personal plans.
  • Covers upscaling, denoising, restoration and background removal in a single tool.
  • Specialized modes like Gentle and Old Photo help with text-heavy or damaged images.
  • Large output ceiling (up to 512 MP on Max) suits print.
  • Free signup requires no credit card, so you can test on your own images.

Cons

  • 10 free credits, watermarks and the 8 MP upscale limit make the free tier a test only.
  • Cancelling forfeits remaining credits at period end.
  • Business plans do not accumulate unused credits.
  • Business, pay-as-you-go and API prices are not published on the pricing page.
  • Results still depend on the source image; we did not test output quality.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

Yes, 10 credits on signup with no credit card, but output is watermarked and upscaling is capped at 8 MP.

How much does it cost?

Personal plans are $9, $24 and $34 per month billed annually, or $12, $32 and $45 billed monthly.

Do unused credits roll over?

On personal plans they roll over while you stay subscribed; cancelling forfeits the rest. Business plans do not accumulate credits.

Is there an API?

Yes, Enhancer and Upscaler APIs exist, but API pricing is not shown on the pricing page.

Can I process many images at once?

The homepage mentions batch editing of up to 20 images at a time.
