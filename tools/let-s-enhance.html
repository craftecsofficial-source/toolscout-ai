Let's Enhance is an AI image enhancement platform built to solve a common problem: source images that are too small, blurry, noisy, or low quality for their intended use, whether that's a large product listing photo, a print, or a marketing banner. Its neural network-based upscaling can enlarge images multiple times their original size while intelligently reconstructing detail rather than simply stretching pixels.

Beyond upscaling, the platform offers automatic sharpening, noise and compression artifact removal, and color/light correction, along with specialized processing modes tuned for specific use cases like e-commerce product photography, real estate listings, and general photography or art. Bulk/batch upload lets teams process entire folders of images consistently in one pass.

For teams that need to embed enhancement into a pipeline, Let's Enhance also offers an API and integrations, making it usable both as a standalone web tool for individuals and as infrastructure for larger content or e-commerce operations.

Best for

Let's Enhance is well suited for e-commerce sellers who need consistent, high-resolution product images across large catalogs, as well as photographers and print businesses preparing images for large-format output. Marketing and content teams also use it to rescue low-quality or legacy images for reuse in campaigns. Developers benefit from the API to automate enhancement within existing content pipelines. It's less necessary for casual users only occasionally resizing a photo.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • AI upscaling produces noticeably better results than traditional interpolation-based resizing
  • Bulk/batch tools make it practical for e-commerce teams managing large product catalogs
  • Use-case specific presets reduce the need for manual tweaking across different photo types
  • API availability allows the tool to be embedded directly into existing content or listing workflows
  • Handles a wide range of quality issues (low-res, noisy, compressed) in a single pass

Cons

  • Credit-based subscription pricing can become expensive for very high image volumes
  • Output quality is still dependent on the source image; extremely degraded images have limits
  • Some advanced settings and preset choices require experimentation to get ideal results
  • Free trial credits are limited, so meaningful evaluation may require a paid plan
  • Processing time can increase for very large batch jobs or maximum upscale factors

Frequently asked questions

What does Let's Enhance actually do?

It uses AI models to upscale image resolution, sharpen detail, reduce noise, and generally improve photo quality automatically.

Is there a free plan?

There is a free trial with a limited number of credits; ongoing use requires a paid subscription or pay-as-you-go credits.

Can I process many images at once?

Yes, batch/bulk processing is supported for handling large sets of images in one pass.

Does it work for print-sized images?

Yes, upscaling is commonly used to prepare images for large-format printing where source resolution is too low.

Is there an API?

Yes, Let's Enhance offers an API for developers to integrate enhancement into automated workflows.

Will it fix a very blurry or low-quality photo perfectly?

It significantly improves quality, but results depend on the original image; extremely degraded photos have limits to how much detail can be recovered.
