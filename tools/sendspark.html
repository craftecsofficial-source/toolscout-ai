Sendspark is a good fit for B2B sales teams that already do video outreach and want to scale it without recording each clip. Skip it if you want general-purpose screen recording, a free plan, or videos for marketing, training or social content. You record one video with a first-name placeholder, import contacts by CSV, CRM or API, and it generates a personalized version per contact with the name, optional cloned voice, a recipient-specific website background and thumbnail. Videos are shared through landing pages that can include calls to action and an embedded calendar, with analytics on viewing. Usage is metered in Dynamic Video Minutes per plan.

Best for

Good for SDRs, account executives and customer-success teams doing repeatable outreach. Not for creators or marketers who want AI avatar or text-to-video generation, people who need a permanent free tier, or very high-volume senders who would blow through plan minutes.

Key features

01

Record once, personalize at scale

Record a base video with a first-name placeholder; the platform generates a version per contact and can clone your voice for the name.

02

Dynamic backgrounds and thumbnails

Each video can show the recipient's website behind the presenter, with a personalized thumbnail for the email.

03

Landing pages

Custom text, calls to action, embedded calendars and company branding for each video page.

04

Contact import and automation

Bring in contacts by CSV, CRM or API; Growth and above add Agentic Workflows, API/webhook access and advanced integrations such as Salesforce.

05

Viewer analytics

Shows who watched, what percentage they saw, and other actions taken.

06

Recording tools

Scripts and teleprompter, camera effects and backgrounds, combine-video intros, a Chrome extension and a desktop app.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Saves rep time: one recording becomes many personalized videos, which is the core value for outbound.
  • Landing pages with embedded calendars and viewer analytics support follow-up, not only creation.
  • Pricing is transparent, including seat, minute and workflow-task overage rates.
  • Trial needs no credit card, so you can test with real prospects.
  • Annual billing lowers the cost noticeably (Solo $468/year vs $588 monthly).

Cons

  • No free plan; after 7 days you must pay at least $49/month.
  • Minute caps are low on Solo (100 minutes) and overage adds up for high-volume outreach.
  • Agentic Workflows, API and advanced CRM integrations start at Growth, doubling the entry price.
  • The pricing page's feature list and comparison table disagree slightly on what each plan includes.
  • Vendor claims (50,000+ companies, G2 4.8 from 350+ reviews, customer results) are self-reported; we did not verify them on G2.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Sendspark cost?

As checked on 2026-10-21: Solo $49/mo, Growth $99/mo, Team $299/mo, Business $699/mo, with lower annual rates. Agency and Enterprise are custom. Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial.

Is there a free plan?

No free plan is listed, only a 7-day trial that the site says needs no credit card.

What is a Dynamic Video Minute?

It is the plan's usage unit for AI-personalized video output. Each plan includes a number (100 on Solo up to 3,000 on Business) and extra minutes are billed per minute.

Which plan do I need for CRM integrations and automation?

Solo has basic integrations. Growth and above add Agentic Workflows, API/webhooks and advanced integrations like Salesforce. Confirm specifics with Sendspark since the page is slightly inconsistent.

Is it secure?

Sendspark states it is GDPR and SOC 2 Type 2 compliant. That is the company's own claim, which we did not independently verify.
