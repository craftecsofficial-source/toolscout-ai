Sendspark is a sales-focused AI video personalization platform that distinguishes itself from general screen-recording tools like Loom or Vidyard by automating the personalization step itself. Instead of a rep recording a new video for every prospect, a user records one base video and Sendspark's AI voice cloning technology generates a unique version for each recipient — speaking the prospect's name, company, or other custom variables in the sender's own cloned voice — while a "dynamic background" feature can swap in each prospect's actual website behind the presenter. The company frames this explicitly as solving the scale problem in personalized video outreach: traditional personalized video requires linear time investment per prospect, while Sendspark's model decouples the number of personalized videos from the number of times a rep has to record.

The product is built specifically for B2B sales workflows — prospecting, deal progression, customer success and partnerships are called out as core use cases — and its pricing and integration depth reflect that focus. All plans include basic integrations (HubSpot, Gmail, Outlook, LinkedIn), while Growth tier and above unlock deeper CRM and sales-engagement integrations (Salesforce, Outreach, SalesLoft, Apollo, Smartlead) plus API/webhook access and "Agentic Workflows" that can automatically find a prospect's website and generate a personalized video without manual triggering. Usage is metered in "Dynamic Video Minutes," where each minute of AI-personalized video output (regardless of how many individual prospect versions are generated) consumes plan minutes, with overage priced per additional minute that decreases at higher tiers. Sendspark reports SOC 2 Type II and GDPR compliance, and offers dedicated Agency and Enterprise tiers for managing multiple client teams or advanced security requirements.

Best for

Sendspark is built for B2B sales development reps, account executives and revenue teams running high-volume, personalized video outreach who want to scale beyond what one-video-per-prospect recording allows, especially teams already using Salesforce, Outreach, SalesLoft or similar sales engagement platforms. It's a poor fit for anyone needing general-purpose video creation (marketing, training, social content) outside of sales outreach, or for very low-volume solo users who don't need automated personalization at scale.

Key features

01

AI voice cloning with variable personalization

Clones the sender's voice from one base recording and generates a unique version per prospect, inserting personalized variables (name, company, etc.) spoken in the sender's own voice.

02

Dynamic video backgrounds

Swaps in each prospect's actual website (or other dynamic imagery) as the on-screen background behind the presenter, making every video appear individually recorded.

03

Personalized thumbnails

Automatically generates a unique video thumbnail per recipient (often showing their name or company) to boost open/click rates in outbound email.

04

Agentic Workflows

Automates the end-to-end pipeline — finding a prospect's website, generating the personalized video, and sending it through a connected sales tool — without manual intervention, available on Growth plans and up.

05

CRM and sales-engagement integrations

Native integrations with HubSpot, Gmail, Outlook and LinkedIn on all plans, with Salesforce, Outreach, SalesLoft, Apollo and Smartlead added on Growth and above.

06

API and webhook access

Lets sales-ops teams build custom automation pipelines around video personalization, available from the Growth tier upward.

07

Scripts and teleprompter

Built-in scripting and teleprompter tools help reps record a clean, consistent base video before AI personalization is applied.

08

Custom domain and branding

Growth tier and above let teams host personalized video landing pages under their own branded domain rather than a generic Sendspark link.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Sendspark's core mechanic — one recording, AI-generated personalized versions per prospect — genuinely removes the linear time cost of personalized video outreach that tools like Loom and Vidyard still require.
  • Deep, tiered integrations with major sales stacks (Salesforce, Outreach, SalesLoft, Apollo, Smartlead, HubSpot) mean video personalization can be triggered directly from existing sequences rather than as a separate manual step.
  • Agentic Workflows automate the research step (finding a prospect's website for the dynamic background) as well as generation and sending, reducing manual work for high-volume SDR teams.
  • Rating consistency is notable: both G2 (372 reviews) and Capterra (84 reviews) independently converge on 4.7/5, suggesting reliable sentiment rather than a skewed small sample.
  • Transparent per-minute overage pricing that decreases at higher tiers ($0.39 down to $0.19 per additional Dynamic Video Minute) gives predictable cost scaling for teams that occasionally exceed their plan.

Cons

  • There's no ongoing free tier, only a 7-day free trial, so teams must commit to a paid plan to properly evaluate video quality and integration fit at scale.
  • The most valuable automation features — Agentic Workflows, Salesforce/Outreach integrations, API access — are locked behind the Growth tier ($99/mo) and above, so the entry Solo plan is fairly limited for serious outbound teams.
  • Dynamic Video Minutes caps (100/month on Solo) can be consumed quickly by active outbound teams, and overage costs, while transparent, add up for high-volume campaigns.
  • Live Slack support and a dedicated account manager are reserved for the top Business tier, meaning smaller teams get email-only support.
  • As a sales-specific tool, it lacks the general-purpose editing and template breadth of broader video generators, so it's not a good fit for teams needing marketing or educational video beyond outreach use cases.

Frequently asked questions

How is Sendspark different from Loom or Vidyard?

Sendspark automates personalization itself: you record one video and AI voice cloning generates a unique version per prospect, whereas Loom and Vidyard require recording a new video for each recipient.

What are Dynamic Video Minutes?

They're Sendspark's usage unit — each minute of AI-personalized video generated (with voice cloning, dynamic backgrounds, and thumbnails) consumes one Dynamic Video Minute from your plan's monthly allowance.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, every plan includes a 7-day free trial with no credit card required to start.

Which CRMs and sales tools does Sendspark integrate with?

All plans include HubSpot, Gmail, Outlook and LinkedIn; Growth plans and above add Salesforce, Outreach, SalesLoft, Apollo, Smartlead and API/webhook access.

What happens if I exceed my plan's Dynamic Video Minutes?

You're not cut off — additional minutes are billed per-minute at your plan's overage rate ($0.39 on Solo down to $0.19 on Business), or you can upgrade to a higher tier.

What are Agentic Workflows?

An automation feature (Growth plan and above) that can automatically find a prospect's website, generate a personalized video with that site as the dynamic background, and send it through a connected sales tool end-to-end.
