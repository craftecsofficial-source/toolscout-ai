Perplexity is a good fit if you mostly use AI to look things up and want to see where each claim came from; skip it if you mainly want a drafting, coding or brainstorming partner. It is a search-first assistant: each question triggers a live web search, and the answer is written with numbered links back to the pages it used.

Beyond the basic answer box it offers deeper research modes that run several searches and compile a longer report, file uploads, saved Spaces for ongoing projects, and a choice of third-party models on paid plans. It also makes Comet, an AI browser that can summarise the page you are on and act on simple web tasks. Citations lower the risk of unchecked answers but do not remove it, so important facts still deserve a click-through.

Best for

Best for students, analysts, journalists and anyone replacing a search engine with something that summarises and shows sources. Not for people who mainly want long-form creative writing, heavy coding help or a conversational companion, who will be better served by ChatGPT or Claude. Also not ideal if you need guaranteed unlimited usage on a low budget, since heavier research modes are metered.

Key features

01

Cited, real-time answers

Responses are built from a live web search and link the sources inline so you can verify claims.

02

Pro Search and Research modes

Longer multi-step searches that break a question into sub-queries and return a more thorough, sourced write-up. Allowances are higher on paid plans.

03

Comet browser

Perplexity's AI browser, available on Mac, Windows, iOS and Android, that can summarise pages, answer questions about them and handle simple web tasks.

04

Model choice

Paid plans give access to several third-party frontier models for a given query.

05

File uploads

Upload documents and images to ask questions alongside web results; limits rise on paid plans.

06

Spaces

Saved collections of searches and files for organising ongoing research, individually or with a team.

07

Enterprise controls

Enterprise plans add SSO, SCIM, audit logs, configurable file retention and a no-training-on-enterprise-data commitment.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Inline citations are the default, which makes verifying an answer a one-click job
  • Free plan is genuinely usable for everyday lookups
  • Comet is available on desktop and mobile, not just a browser demo
  • Paid plans let you switch between several frontier models without separate subscriptions
  • Annual billing brings Pro to roughly $17/mo

Cons

  • Citing a page does not guarantee the summary matches it; it can misread or overstate sources
  • Not built for creative writing, long drafting or open-ended conversation
  • Heavy research use hits metered allowances; the Free plan's exact daily caps are not stated clearly on public pages
  • Max at $200/mo is a large jump from Pro and only worth it for very heavy users
  • Enterprise seat prices are not published in a way we could verify; confirm before budgeting

Frequently asked questions

Is Perplexity free?

Yes, there is a free plan with basic cited search and limited use of the deeper modes. Exact daily caps change, so check the official pricing page.

How much is Perplexity Pro?

$20 a month, or $200 a year billed annually (about $17 a month).

Is Max worth $200 a month?

Only for people who hit Pro's limits constantly or want early features and the most advanced models. Most individuals will be fine on Pro.

How is it different from ChatGPT?

Perplexity is built around live search with source links on every answer. ChatGPT is a broader assistant for writing, coding and images where search is one feature.

What is Comet?

Perplexity's AI browser for Mac, Windows, iOS and Android. It can summarise pages and help with simple web tasks. Check the official Comet page for current pricing.
