Lacy.ai is a strong fit for US-style home-service and B2B service companies (HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, pest control) that lose jobs to missed calls and can commit to a 12-month contract. Skip it if you want a cheap self-serve tool, a multi-channel chat and email helpdesk, or month-to-month flexibility. It is an AI phone agent platform: the agent answers inbound, after-hours and overflow calls, asks qualifying questions, books appointments, routes urgent requests, updates your CRM and sends follow-ups such as SMS or email. It also runs outbound follow-up campaigns. A visual flow builder (from the Growth plan) lets you design conversation paths, call outcomes sync as structured data to CRMs and calendars through a library of 250+ integrations, and you get transcripts, recordings and call analytics. The company also sets up call types, booking rules and handoffs for you, which is why each plan carries a one-time setup fee on top of the monthly price.

Best for

Owners and office managers at home-service and field-service businesses, plus other B2B service firms, where phone calls turn directly into booked jobs and a one-time setup fee plus $499 or more per month is justified by recovered revenue. Not for: very small shops that want pay-as-you-go with no contract, ecommerce or SaaS teams needing chat, email and social support in one inbox, or buyers who want to try the product for a few weeks before committing.

Key features

01

Inbound & After-Hours Call Answering

AI agents answer business-hours, after-hours, weekend, and overflow calls so no inbound inquiry goes to voicemail or a hold queue.

02

Lead Qualification & Intake

Agents ask configurable qualification questions, capture caller identity, service address, and issue details, and classify urgency or category before handoff.

03

Appointment Booking

Qualified callers can be booked directly into a calendar with service-area and scheduling logic, moving a conversation straight into a confirmed appointment.

04

Outbound Call Campaigns

The platform can run outbound calling for follow-ups, reminders, and lead nurture sequences in addition to handling inbound traffic.

05

Flow Builder & Workflow Automation

A visual, no-code flow builder lets teams design conversation paths and connect call outcomes to downstream actions like SMS, email, or internal alerts.

06

CRM Sync & 250+ Integrations

Call outcomes and structured 'call variables' sync in real time to CRMs, calendars, help desks, and other business tools via a library of 250+ integrations plus APIs and webhooks.

07

Call Analytics & Transcripts

Dashboards track call volume, booking rates, and qualification outcomes, alongside full call recordings and transcripts with extracted customer context.

08

Multilingual Support & Security

Agents can operate in multiple languages, and the platform emphasizes SOC 2- and GDPR-oriented security controls including encryption, access controls, and audit logs.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Built around the phone call as a business event, so booking, urgency routing and CRM updates are core features rather than add-ons.
  • Pricing is published, including per-minute overage, which is rare among phone AI vendors.
  • Hands-on implementation (call mapping, booking rules, handoffs, testing) reduces the work for owners without a technical team.
  • Covers both inbound (after-hours, overflow) and outbound follow-up campaigns.
  • 250+ integrations plus APIs and webhooks, with call outcomes saved as structured data rather than just transcripts.

Cons

  • All plans require a 12-month agreement and a one-time setup fee of $2,500 to $5,000, so the real first-year cost is far above the headline $499/mo.
  • Minutes are billed separately (Ignite has no included minutes at $0.48/min), so costs grow with call volume.
  • The pricing page does not state free trial terms even though the site shows a 'Try for Free' button; confirm what the trial includes.
  • Phone-first: no native chat, email or social inbox, so omnichannel teams need another tool.
  • Cloned voice and Flow Builder start at Growth, and secondary languages on Ignite are a paid add-on.
  • Security claims (SOC 2, GDPR) are not backed by a public trust center we could verify; ask for documentation.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Lacy.ai really cost?

Ignite is $499/mo plus a $2,500 one-time setup, Growth $999/mo plus $3,500, Scale $1,499/mo plus $5,000, all on 12-month agreements. Minutes cost $0.48/min on Ignite, and $0.38 or $0.28/min after the 1,000 included minutes on Growth and Scale.

Is there a free trial?

The site has a 'Try for Free' button and a free 30-minute demo, but the pricing page does not state trial terms. Ask sales exactly what the trial includes.

Can I cancel monthly?

No. The pricing page states all plans require a 12-month agreement.

Is it only for home service companies?

That is the core focus, but the company also targets other B2B service businesses that depend on phone-based lead intake.

Does it work with my CRM?

It lists 250+ integrations plus APIs and webhooks, and Enterprise mentions ServiceTitan support. Check the integration library for your specific system.
