Paperpal is a good fit for researchers, grad students and journal authors who want language polishing and pre-submission checks in the tools they already write in; anyone writing non-academic content, or needing a heavyweight general writing assistant, should look elsewhere. It is an AI assistant that edits grammar and tone for scholarly writing, rewrites and paraphrases text, generates citations, lets you ask questions of uploaded PDFs, and runs plagiarism, AI-content, reference and journal-readiness checks.

It is made by Cactus Communications, the company behind the Editage editing service, and works as a web editor plus add-ins for MS Word, Google Docs, Chrome and Overleaf. Its site states that uploaded files are visible only to you and are not used to train AI. We could not verify current plan prices or usage quotas today, because the pricing page did not display them when fetched; check the live page before paying.

Best for

Researchers, graduate students and academic authors preparing manuscripts, theses or grant text, who want checks against journal requirements. Not for bloggers, marketers, fiction writers or students doing casual class essays, who would be paying for academic-specific features they will not use. Also not for anyone who needs published, comparable institutional pricing up front, as that is quoted via sales.

Key features

01

Language & grammar correction

Real-time grammar, spelling, and style suggestions tuned to academic writing conventions, going beyond generic grammar checking to flag scholarly tone and clarity issues.

02

Chat with PDFs

Upload research papers to ask questions, get summaries, and pull out key findings directly from the source PDF instead of reading it end to end.

03

Citation & reference generator

Generates and formats citations across 10,000+ citation styles and can surface relevant references from a large database of scholarly articles.

04

Paraphraser and AI rewrite tools

Rewrites sentences and paragraphs to vary phrasing, tighten wording to meet journal word limits, or adjust academic tone.

05

Plagiarism and AI-content detection

Scans manuscripts for text overlap against published sources and flags AI-generated content, with monthly word quotas that scale by plan.

06

Submission readiness checker

Reviews a manuscript against 30+ language, formatting, and technical checks commonly required before journal submission.

07

Copilot content generation

A generative assistant that drafts outlines, titles, abstracts, keywords, summaries, and email templates for academic communication, alongside research-backed answers as you write.

08

Multi-platform access

Works as a standalone web editor and as plugins for MS Word, Google Docs, Chrome, and Overleaf, so edits happen inside the researcher's existing workflow.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Suggestions are tuned to academic tone, so edits tend to suit papers and theses better than a general grammar checker.
  • One product spans editing, citations, PDF Q&A and integrity checks, which reduces tool switching during a manuscript.
  • Works inside Word, Google Docs and Overleaf, which matters for LaTeX users who are poorly served by many writing tools.
  • Backed by Editage's publication-support business, which informs the submission-readiness checks.
  • States that user files are private and not used for AI training, relevant for unpublished work.

Cons

  • We could not confirm current paid prices or free-tier limits from the live pricing page, so budget needs a manual check.
  • Narrow focus: weak choice for non-academic writing.
  • Plagiarism and AI-detection results are screening aids, not guarantees of what a journal or institution's own tools will report.
  • Paraphrase and rewrite tools can lead to over-editing or integrity problems if you accept output without review.
  • Institutional pricing is not public.

Frequently asked questions

What is Paperpal used for?

Editing academic writing, finding and formatting citations, chatting with PDFs, running plagiarism and AI-content checks, and checking a manuscript against journal submission requirements.

How much does Paperpal cost?

There is a free plan and paid plans, but we could not verify current prices from the pricing page on 2026-10-20. Check paperpal.com/pricing; institutions contact sales@paperpal.com.

Does it work in Word, Google Docs or Overleaf?

Yes, the site lists add-ins for MS Word, Google Docs, Chrome and Overleaf, as well as a web editor.

Is my manuscript used to train AI?

The site says your work is not used to train AI and that files are visible only to you and can be deleted anytime. Read the privacy policy if the material is sensitive.

Is it a good choice for non-academic writing?

Not really. Its features target research papers and journal submission; a general grammar or writing tool suits blogs and business writing better.
