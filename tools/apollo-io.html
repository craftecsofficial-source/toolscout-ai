A good fit for startups and small-to-mid sales teams that want contact data and outreach tools bundled together; skip it if you need fully published, predictable per-seat pricing or enterprise-grade governance across a complicated stack. Apollo is a go-to-market platform: you search its database, enrich records, and run email, call and LinkedIn-style outreach from the same system.

On top of that, Apollo now pushes AI agents that research accounts, rank leads, draft messages and aim to book meetings, plus a parallel dialer, deliverability tools and a Chrome extension. It can also be used from inside a CRM, Gmail, or AI assistants through Apollo Anywhere and MCP. Its size figures (240M+ contacts, 30M+ companies) are the company's own claims.

Best for

Founders, SDR teams and growing sales orgs that run outbound and want data plus outreach in one tool. Not for buyers who need transparent public pricing before talking to anyone, teams whose reps need different plan levels, or those who only want a cold-email sender without a data product (a dedicated sender may be cheaper).

Contact and company database

Email-based outreach sequences plus a parallel dialer for calling.

03

Deliverability tools

Domain warming, inbox rotation and reputation monitoring to protect sending.

04

AI agents and assistant

Agents that research accounts, prioritise leads, draft outreach and aim to book meetings, trained on your ICP and playbooks via an AI Context Center.

05

Enrichment and integrations

Waterfall enrichment to fill gaps in records, and 200+ claimed integrations.

06

Apollo Anywhere and Chrome extension

Use Apollo from a CRM, Gmail, the browser, or AI tools like ChatGPT and Claude.

07

Compliance support

Tools aimed at GDPR, CAN-SPAM and DNC rules; the company lists SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • One product covers data, sequencing, calling and AI research, which cuts tool sprawl.
  • Free plan stays free with no stated time limit, so you can test the data and workflow.
  • Credits are pooled across the team, which helps when usage is uneven between reps.
  • Can be used inside Gmail, CRMs and AI assistants, not just the Apollo app.
  • Includes deliverability tooling, which matters once outbound volume grows.

Cons

  • The pricing page shows no per-user prices or credit counts, so you cannot budget from it; earlier listings of about $49 and $79 per user could not be confirmed today.
  • Credits are granted each billing cycle and do not roll over.
  • All seats on an account share one plan level, so one rep needing Professional means upgrading everyone.
  • Free plan restricts email campaigns to Gmail and imposes a record selection limit.
  • Exports without a limit top out at 10,000 leads in one click.
  • Data accuracy and database size are the vendor's claims; test on your own target market before committing.

Frequently asked questions

Does Apollo have a free plan?

Yes. The pricing page says the free plan has no time limit, but limits email campaigns to Gmail connections and applies a record selection limit. The credit amount is not stated.

How much does Apollo cost?

The pricing page lists Free, Basic, Professional and Custom plans but shows no dollar prices, so check the live page or sales. Annual billing is discounted; the discount amount is not shown.

How do credits work?

Credits are pooled across the team, granted at the start of each billing cycle and not rolled over. Per-plan amounts are not published on the page.

Can different team members be on different plans?

No. All seats on an account share one plan level.

How does it compare with Clay or Seamless.ai?

Apollo bundles its own database with sequencing and dialing. Clay is more of an enrichment and workflow builder, and Seamless.ai centres on live contact search.
