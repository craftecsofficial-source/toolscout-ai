Craiyon is a fine free playground for memes, quick concepts and casual art, but skip it if you need private, watermark-free images without paying, top-tier realism, or API access. It turns text prompts into images in several styles and bundles editing tools: retouch by description, photo editor, upscaler, background remover, image-to-SVG and image-to-video. Free users get a limited number of watermarked, public images and may wait at busy times, while paid plans (Supporter, Professional, Ultra) add unlimited images, private output, no watermark and monthly credits for the editing tools. It began as DALL-E mini and is now its own product. The site does not name its underlying models.

Best for

Good for hobbyists, students, meme and social-post creators, and teachers wanting quick illustrations. Not for agencies or brand teams needing private, polished, high-fidelity output, developers needing an API (none public yet), or anyone who can't display the free-tier credit to Craiyon.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • You can try it free, and paid users can generate from text without spending credits.
  • Wide set of tools (upscale, SVG, background removal, retouch, video) in one site.
  • Commercial use is allowed on any plan; subscribers need no attribution.
  • Category pages make it easy to get logos, stickers or coloring pages without prompt skill.

Cons

  • Free images are watermarked, public and require credit to Craiyon.
  • Free limits are vague and peak-hour 'too much traffic' messages can block you.
  • Unused monthly credits do not roll over and sales are non-refundable.
  • No public API; the company says one is planned.
  • The pricing page showed blank dollar amounts when we checked, so confirm cost before subscribing.
  • Quality against premium generators was not tested by us.

Frequently asked questions

Is Craiyon free?

Yes with limits: a limited number of watermarked, public images and possible waits at busy times.

How much do paid plans cost?

We could not verify current dollar prices; the pricing page showed blanks. Check craiyon.com/pricing. Yearly billing is advertised as 2 months free.

Can I use images commercially?

The pricing page says any account can under its Terms of Use; free users must credit Craiyon, subscribers need not.

Are my images private?

Only on paid plans; free images are public.

Is there an API?

No public API currently; one is stated as planned.
