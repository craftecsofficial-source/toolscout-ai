Craiyon started life as DALL-E mini, the viral open-source project that made AI image generation accessible to the public before OpenAI's own DALL-E was widely available. It has since rebranded and evolved into its own standalone AI art platform, generating images in a signature grid layout from a text prompt and offering several style modes (Photo, Anime, Illustration, Vector, Artistic, and Raw) alongside built-in editing tools like retouching, upscaling, animation, and vectorization. Craiyon runs on two quality tiers internally: fast 'Lite' generations for quick iteration and higher-detail 'Pro' generations for final output, with free users getting unlimited Lite generations and a small daily allotment of Pro generations.

What differentiates Craiyon from most competitors is that its free tier has no hard cap on total images generated, an unusual stance in a market where most AI image tools gate free usage tightly behind daily or monthly limits. The tradeoff is that free-tier images carry a watermark, are made public in Craiyon's gallery, and require attribution under its terms of use. Paid plans (Supporter, Professional, and Ultra) remove watermarks and attribution requirements, unlock private generation, add monthly edit credits for the retouch/upscale/vectorize tools, and provide faster, priority processing. Craiyon LLC operates the product independently and lists recognizable brands (Google, Vodafone, Paramount) among the organizations that have used it, positioning itself as a low-cost, high-accessibility alternative to premium AI art generators rather than a photorealism leader.

Best for

Craiyon is a strong fit for casual users, students, meme creators, and budget-conscious hobbyists who want free or cheap AI image generation without worrying about hard usage caps. It's a weaker fit for professional designers, marketers, or agencies who need consistently photorealistic, high-fidelity output or API access, where premium tools like Midjourney or Adobe Firefly are better suited.

Key features

01

Grid-Based Text-to-Image Generation

Every prompt produces a grid of image variations at once, letting users compare options and pick a favorite rather than generating one image at a time.

02

Style Modes

Users can steer output toward Photo, Anime, Illustration, Vector, Artistic, or unfiltered Raw styles to match the intended use case.

03

Lite vs. Pro Quality

Lite generations are fast and unlimited for free users; Pro generations use a higher-quality model with more detail, limited to 2/day on the free plan and unlocked further on paid tiers.

04

Built-In Editing Tools

Retouch, upscale, animate, and vectorize tools let users refine or repurpose a generated image without leaving Craiyon, consuming monthly edit credits on paid plans.

05

Background Remover

A dedicated tool strips backgrounds from generated or uploaded images for use in design work or product mockups.

06

Negative Prompts & Aspect Ratios

Users can specify what to exclude from an image and choose square, landscape, or portrait framing for different platforms.

07

Craiyon Studio

A newer workspace mode for more extended, iterative creative sessions beyond single grid generations.

08

Image Search & Categories

A public gallery and search feature let users browse and get inspiration from previously generated community images.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The free tier's lack of a hard generation cap is a genuine differentiator; most competing AI image tools throttle free users to a handful of images per day, while Craiyon allows unlimited Lite-quality generations.
  • Entry paid pricing at $10/month (Supporter) undercuts many premium AI art subscriptions, making private, watermark-free generation accessible to hobbyists on a tight budget.
  • No signup is required to start generating images, reducing friction for first-time users who just want to try AI art immediately.
  • Built-in editing tools (upscale, animate, vectorize, background removal) mean users don't need to export to a separate app for basic post-processing.
  • Commercial use is allowed on any account tier, including free, as long as Craiyon attribution requirements are followed, which is more permissive than many competitors' free tiers.

Cons

  • Free-tier output is watermarked and made public in Craiyon's gallery, which is a dealbreaker for anyone needing private or client-ready images without paying.
  • Pro-quality (higher detail) generations are capped at just 2 per day on the free plan, so most free usage defaults to the lower Lite quality.
  • Independent reviews and comparisons consistently note that Craiyon's image fidelity and prompt adherence trail premium models like Midjourney, Flux, or DALL-E 3, positioning it as a budget/casual tool rather than a professional-grade generator.
  • Third-party review volume is thin (a Trustpilot score of 3.8/5 from only 9 reviews as of this writing), making it hard to gauge broader customer satisfaction trends.
  • No public API is currently offered, limiting its use for developers wanting to integrate generation into their own products.

What reviewers say

Trustpilot reviews are limited in volume but generally positive, with users praising Craiyon's friction-free, no-signup access and its ability to generate free AI art quickly.

Frequently asked questions

Is Craiyon really free?

Yes. Craiyon's free tier allows unlimited Lite-quality image generations with no hard cap, plus 2 higher-quality Pro generations per day, though free images are watermarked and public.

What was Craiyon previously called?

Craiyon was originally released as DALL-E mini, an open-source project that made AI image generation widely accessible before being rebranded as Craiyon.

Can I use Craiyon images commercially?

Yes, on any plan, as long as free-tier users credit Craiyon per its terms of use; paid subscribers can use images without attribution.

What's the difference between Lite and Pro generations?

Lite generations are faster and unlimited on the free plan, while Pro generations use a higher-quality model for more detailed results and are limited to 2/day for free users.

Does Craiyon offer an API?

Not currently. Craiyon has stated it plans to offer a public API in the future but does not have one as of this writing.

How do Craiyon's paid plans differ?

Supporter ($10/mo), Professional ($20/mo, most popular, adds private generation), and Ultra ($70/mo, fastest processing) all offer unlimited images and no watermarks, scaling mainly by monthly edit credits and processing priority.
