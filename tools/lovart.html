Lovart is a good fit for designers, marketers and small brands who want an AI agent to turn a written brief into a set of branded assets; skip it if you need flat predictable pricing or heavy multi-person collaboration without buying the Team plan. The site calls it an AI design agent that creates logos, packaging, social content and video ads from a conversation, on a canvas where outputs remain editable. It runs on credits, with fast generation drawing down credits and a slower queued Relax mode on some plans. I could not load plan prices today, so check the live pricing page before subscribing.

Best for

Good for freelancers, in-house marketers and small brand teams producing campaign visuals and short video. Not for people who want a single fixed price per image, enterprises needing shared seats on one account (that needs the Team plan), or anyone who needs pixel-exact product photography.

Key features

01

One-Prompt Campaign Kits

A single creative brief can generate a full, coordinated set of assets — logo, color system, typography, social templates, video, and brand guidelines — inside one infinite canvas instead of isolated single images.

02

Mind Chain of Thought (MCoT) Engine

Reasons through the brief — audience, brand context, intent — before generating, so outputs reflect a strategic plan rather than a one-shot interpretation of the prompt.

03

Skills Marketplace

Ready-to-use workflows for specific creative tasks (logo creation, poster design, brand identity, social campaigns) that can be reused or saved as custom repeatable Skills for consistent output.

04

Full Post-Generation Editing

Unlike one-shot AI image tools, every generated asset stays editable — adjust layers, swap elements, correct style, or restructure the design after it's created.

05

Multi-Model Access

Integrates leading image and video generation models (Nano Banana Pro, Seedance 2.0, Kling AI, Flux, Veo, Luma AI, and more) so users pick the right model per task without separate subscriptions.

06

Brand Kit Management

Lets agencies and multi-brand users store and apply distinct brand guidelines, letting one account manage consistent creative for dozens of separate brands or clients.

07

Batch Generation

Generates up to 40 images in one batch run, useful for producing multiple campaign variations quickly rather than one asset at a time.

08

Fast vs. Unlimited Relax Generation

Fast Generation draws from a monthly credit balance for immediate results; Unlimited Relax Generation offers unlimited slower, queued generations on eligible plans without consuming fast credits.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Works from a conversation rather than single prompts, which suits campaign-style jobs with several assets.
  • Bundles multiple outside image and video models, so you avoid separate subscriptions.
  • Outputs stay editable on the canvas instead of being flattened images.
  • Pricing page documents credit expiry and top-up rules clearly.

Cons

  • Plan prices and credit amounts could not be verified today; the page content I could load did not show them.
  • Monthly credits expire each cycle; annual plans issue credits monthly, so you cannot bank them.
  • Accounts are for individual use with session limits; teams must buy the Team plan.
  • Unlimited Relax runs on a fixed timeline, is a one-time benefit per account, and is queued by tier, so it can be slow.
  • Quality depends on the underlying third-party models and can vary.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Lovart cost?

I could not verify current prices today. The pricing page has a monthly/annual toggle and a Team plan; check https://www.lovart.ai/pricing for exact numbers.

Do credits roll over?

No for subscription credits: they reset each billing date, and annual plans issue credits monthly that expire at month end. Top-up credits last 366 days from purchase.

Can a team share one account?

No. Accounts are limited to 2 desktop web sessions and 1 mobile session; multi-user use requires the Team plan.

What is Relax generation?

A slower queued mode that does not use fast credits. Queue priority depends on tier, and the Unlimited Relax benefit runs on a fixed timeline per account.
