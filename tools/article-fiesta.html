Article Fiesta is a good match for small site owners and bloggers who want cheap, hands-off long-form SEO posts pushed straight to their blog. Skip it if you need expert-level or technical accuracy, strict editorial workflows, or a keyword research and content-scoring suite. You enter a keyword and it writes an article of roughly 2,000 words or more, adds meta data, headings, FAQ schema, images and internal links, then posts it as a draft, scheduled or live post.

Plans are priced by article count, from $19.99 for 5 a month up to $299.99 for 300. Image automation, bulk creation and the AI-detection rewrite option sit in higher tiers. The first two articles are free without a card, and the site says it will fix or replace unsatisfactory articles and refund if you remain unhappy. I found no independent ratings.

Best for

Article Fiesta suits small businesses, solo bloggers and lean marketing teams who want low-cost, automated SEO blog output and can proofread posts themselves. It is NOT for teams that need subject-matter depth, approval workflows, competitor-based content scoring, or independently verified vendor reviews before buying.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Keyword to published post in one flow, including draft, scheduled or live posting to your blog.
  • Low entry price: $19.99 for 5 articles, and the first 2 articles are free with no card.
  • Meta description, schema FAQ, internal links and alt-tagged images are generated automatically, reducing plugin and manual work.
  • Stated fix-or-refund promise for unsatisfactory articles.

Cons

  • Per-article caps: 5, 15, 30 or 300 a month, with a jump from $59.99 to $299.99 and nothing between.
  • Key features are gated: featured images start at Growth, schema, unlimited images, bulk creation and AI-detection rewrite at Pro.
  • Output still needs human fact-checking, especially on technical or niche topics, and the 'avoid AI detection' pitch is not a guarantee.
  • No keyword research or content-scoring tools, and no independent reviews (G2, Capterra, Trustpilot) found; testimonials are vendor-published.
  • Annual totals are not listed, and the pricing page does not say how many free articles the trial includes (the homepage says 2).

Frequently asked questions

How much does Article Fiesta cost?

Basic $19.99/mo (5 articles), Growth $39.99/mo (15), Pro $59.99/mo (30), Enterprise $299.99/mo (300), plus a contact-sales agency tier. Annual billing is pay 10 months, get 12.

Is there a free plan?

There is no free plan, but the first 2 articles are free with no credit card, per the homepage.

Which features need a higher plan?

AI featured images and scheduling start at Growth; unlimited images, schema, AI-detection rewrite and bulk creation start at Pro.

Can it publish automatically?

Yes, to a connected blog as a draft, scheduled post or live post. All plans include automatic publishing.

Is there a refund?

The site says it will fix or replace an article at no charge and refund you if you are still unhappy.
