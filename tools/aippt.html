AiPPT suits students, marketers and small teams who want a fast first-draft deck and a free tier to try; skip it if you need strict brand governance or can't verify pricing before committing. You can start from a topic, pasted text, or uploaded files (PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, images, URLs), then edit through conversation and refine the design.

The site presents several products or modes: AiPPT Agent, Classic, Flow and Visual. Decks export to PPTX, PDF or images, or can be shared by link, and the site says output stays editable in PowerPoint and Google Slides.

On pricing, the plans are credit-based: Free has 140 one-time credits, Plus 600 per month, Pro 1,200 per month. Higher tiers also unlock stronger AI models and longer Flow Mode decks (15 slides on Free, 25 on Plus, 50 on Pro). We could not see dollar prices, billing terms or which plan includes each export or feature, because those cells were blank on the page we loaded. As with any AI deck generator, short prompts give generic slides, so plan on editing and fact-checking.

Best for

Students preparing class decks, marketers wanting a quick pitch or report draft, and small business owners without design support, especially those who want a free trial first. Not for enterprise teams needing brand-compliance controls, anyone who needs a published price before signing up, or people whose input is mainly audio or video.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The 140-credit free allowance lets you generate and inspect a real deck before paying
  • Inputs cover documents, Office files, images and URLs, so existing material is easy to reuse
  • Agent, Classic, Flow and Visual modes give options from quick drafts to image-led decks
  • Conversational editing lowers the effort of tweaking slides

Cons

  • We couldn't confirm dollar prices, annual discounts or the Lifetime Membership cost, so budgeting requires a visit to the pricing page
  • Credit pools mean heavy use may run out before the month ends; credit cost per deck wasn't stated
  • The free tier uses free models and smaller slide caps, so quality may differ from paid output
  • Generated content is generic from short prompts and needs fact-checking
  • Feature availability per plan (exports, URL import, merge) wasn't visible in the table

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free version?

Yes. The Free plan gives 140 one-time AI credits, free models and one concurrent agent task. It does not say whether the credits have a time limit.

How much do paid plans cost?

We couldn't confirm. The pricing page shows Plus (600 credits/month) and Pro (1,200 credits/month) but no dollar amounts in the content we loaded; check aippt.com/price.

Can I upload an existing document?

Yes. PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, image and URL sources are supported.

What can I export to?

PPTX, PDF or images, or share by link. Which plans include each format wasn't clear.

Does AiPPT check facts for me?

No. Review and verify AI-generated content before presenting.
