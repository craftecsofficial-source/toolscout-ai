Interview Solver is great for candidates who want a paid practice and mock-interview aid for coding and system design rounds; skip it if you intend to use it covertly in a real interview, since that typically violates employer and platform rules and can cost you the offer. It is a desktop copilot that captures your coding window, transcribes interview audio, and returns code, complexity notes or system design diagrams through global hotkeys.

The pitch is a screen-share-hidden overlay for Zoom, Teams and Google Meet, plus a Companion mode and a phone-browser version, backed by a practice hub with company question banks and free tools such as an ATS resume checker. All stealth and 'undetectable' claims are the vendor's own and we could not verify them.

Best for

Good for engineers who want AI-assisted practice, mock interviews and prep for coding and system design rounds. Not for anyone who needs an employer-sanctioned tool: using it covertly in a live interview or proctored test likely violates the rules of most companies and assessment platforms. Also not for buyers who need refunds, since they are generally not offered.

Key features

01

Invisible interview overlay

A desktop overlay built to stay out of screen-share output on Zoom (up to a stated version), Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet, and to stay off macOS's Activity Monitor.

02

Screengrab-based solving

A capture tool sends the contents of a selected window to the AI, which returns solution code with syntax highlighting for LeetCode-style problems.

03

Real-time voice transcription

Listens to the interview audio and transcribes spoken questions from both interviewer and candidate, turning them into prompts for the AI without manual typing.

04

System design diagram generation

Produces flowcharts and system design structures for architecture-style interview rounds, not just algorithmic code answers.

05

Global hotkeys

Keyboard shortcuts trigger AI responses without switching windows or alt-tabbing out of the video call or coding environment.

06

Companion mode

Lets a second device, such as a phone, issue remote commands so fewer keystrokes are logged on the primary interview machine, aimed at proctored tools like HackerRank's downloadable app.

07

Document-grounded answers

Candidates can upload a resume, job description, or project docs so responses can reference their actual background instead of generic solutions.

08

Practice hub and free tools

Company-specific question banks, role-based tracks (frontend, backend, full-stack, mobile, data, DevOps), algorithm drills, plus free utilities like an ATS resume checker, STAR method builder, Big O cheat sheet, and salary lookup.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Covers both coding questions and system design diagrams, not only LeetCode-style answers.
  • Live transcription plus hotkeys keep a practice session flowing without typing or switching windows.
  • Resume and document upload lets answers refer to your own background.
  • Free trial needs no card, and the $149 three-month cycle is cheaper per month for a longer job hunt.
  • Practice hub and free utilities have value even if you never use the live overlay.

Cons

  • The core selling point, staying undetected, is exactly what employers and proctoring platforms prohibit; getting caught can mean disqualification.
  • Undetectability claims are unverified and tied to specific app versions (Zoom 'up to 6.16' stated), so they can break as apps update.
  • Refunds are generally not offered unless the product does not work on your system.
  • The free trial is only 10 messages, which is little for evaluating it properly.
  • Desktop stealth features exist only for macOS and Windows.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Interview Solver cost?

Monthly is $59/mo and Interview Cycle is $149 every 3 months (about $50/mo), per the site on 2026-10-29. A free trial gives 10 messages with no card.

Is there a refund?

Refunds are generally not offered. The site says one may be approved only if the product does not work on your system.

Is it safe to use in a real interview?

We cannot vouch for that. Most employers and assessment platforms forbid outside AI help, and the site's invisibility claims are unverified. Use it for practice unless the interviewer explicitly allows AI.

What platforms does it support?

A desktop app for macOS and Windows, a Companion mode that pairs a second device, and a phone-browser version (Mobile) with one free 10-minute session.
