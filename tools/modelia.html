Modelia is an AI platform focused specifically on fashion and e-commerce visual content, built around converting existing product photography (flatlays, mannequin shots, or even sketches) into photorealistic images of the product being worn by an AI-generated fashion model. It's backed by Madrid Innovation iLAB and distributed both as a standalone web app and as a Shopify app, positioning it for retailers who want to cut the cost and turnaround time of traditional photo shoots. Beyond the core flatlay-to-model conversion, it packages a fairly wide set of fashion-specific tools — virtual try-on, mannequin-to-model conversion, outfit generation, pose and expression changing, background swapping, and a 'Consistent Character' feature that lets a brand reuse the same AI model across multiple product images for a cohesive catalog look.

Its pricing model is credit-based across five tiers, from a free-forever Starter plan (watermarked, non-commercial) up to a $300/mo Business tier with unlimited consistent characters and 3,000 monthly credits. A distinguishing structural choice versus some competitors is credit rollover: on Basic, Pro, and Business plans, unused credits carry over indefinitely while the subscription remains active, rather than resetting each month. Modelia positions itself as an accessible, entry-level option in the AI-fashion-model category — several independent buyer's-guide sites (Uwear.ai, Nightjar, On-Model) list it among tools worth testing specifically for smaller, independent brands rather than enterprise retailers.

Best for

Modelia is a good fit for independent fashion designers, DTC apparel brands, and small-to-mid e-commerce sellers (particularly those on Shopify) who want to replace or supplement traditional model photo shoots with AI-generated images at a fraction of the cost, and who can work within Basic or Pro's consistent-character limits. It's less suited to large retailers needing dozens of consistent AI models simultaneously (that requires the $300/mo Business tier) or brands that need a no-commitment monthly trial, since Basic and Pro both lock in a 2-month minimum. Buyers should also budget for the fact that free-tier outputs are watermarked and non-commercial, so real production use starts at minimum the $12 one-time Project plan.

Key features

01

Flatlay to Model

Converts a flat-laid or ghost-mannequin product photo into an image of the garment worn by a photorealistic AI model, at 3 credits per image (6 in 4K).

02

Virtual Try-On & Mannequin to Model

Places existing garment photos onto AI models, including converting mannequin-shot photography into natural-looking on-model images.

03

Consistent Character

Lets a brand lock in one AI model's appearance and reuse it across an entire product catalog or campaign for visual consistency, at 3 credits per generation.

04

Outfit Generator & Smart Styler

Generates full outfit combinations or restyles a garment automatically, useful for building lookbooks without new photography.

05

Repose & Facial Expression Changer

Adjusts an AI model's pose or facial expression on an existing generated image without a full regeneration.

06

Background Changer & Upscaler

Swaps image backgrounds for different marketplace requirements and upscales exports up to 4K resolution (2 credits per upscale).

07

Public API Access

Available from the Basic plan upward, letting brands automate generation as part of a broader catalog or PIM workflow.

08

Shopify App Integration

A dedicated Shopify app connects Modelia directly to a store's product catalog for streamlined image generation and syncing.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The free Starter plan is genuinely free forever (not a time-limited trial), giving brands a real way to evaluate output quality before spending money, even if capped and watermarked.
  • Credit rollover on every paid tier (Basic, Pro, Business) means a slow month doesn't waste your subscription — credits accumulate rather than reset, which matters for brands with seasonal shoot schedules.
  • The toolset goes well beyond basic model generation to cover the full content-production loop for a fashion brand: styling, posing, backgrounds, upscaling, and even short AI video (25-40 credits per clip).
  • Public API access starting at the $35/mo Basic tier is relatively accessible pricing for programmatic/automated catalog workflows, compared to competitors that gate APIs behind higher enterprise tiers.
  • Trustpilot reviews for modelia.ai average 4/5 across 35 reviews, and its Shopify App Store listing shows 4.1/5 with 80% five-star ratings, giving reasonably corroborated independent signal for a tool this size.

Cons

  • Basic and Pro plans both carry a 2-month minimum subscription commitment, so brands can't simply try a paid tier for one month and cancel if it doesn't work out.
  • The free tier's images are watermarked and restricted to non-commercial use, meaning brands must upgrade to at least the $12 Project plan before they can legally use outputs in a live store.
  • Consistent-character limits scale steeply by price: only 1 character on the $12 Project plan, 3 on Basic ($35/mo), 6 on Pro ($85/mo), and unlimited only on Business ($300/mo) — brands with large, varied model rosters may find this restrictive until the top tier.
  • The Shopify App Store rating shows a notable bimodal pattern (80% five-star, 20% one-star out of a small sample), suggesting inconsistent experiences for at least a meaningful minority of users.
  • No G2 or Capterra numeric rating was independently verified during this research, so the review picture rests primarily on Trustpilot and the Shopify App Store rather than the fuller B2B review platforms.

Frequently asked questions

Is Modelia free to use?

Yes, the Starter plan is free forever with 20 credits/month (10/day cap), but outputs are watermarked and restricted to non-commercial use.

How much does a commercial plan cost?

Commercial usage starts at the $12 one-time Project plan (50 credits, watermark-free, 1 consistent character), with recurring plans from $35/mo (Basic) up to $300/mo (Business).

What is a 'consistent character' and why does it matter?

It's a feature that locks in one AI model's face/body so the same model appears across an entire product catalog or campaign; the number of consistent characters you can maintain simultaneously scales with your plan, from 1 on Project up to unlimited on Business.

Do unused credits expire?

On the Starter plan, credits reset monthly and don't roll over. On Project, credits never expire. On Basic, Pro, and Business, unused credits roll over and accumulate as long as the subscription stays active.

Is there a minimum commitment on paid plans?

Yes — Basic and Pro require a 2-month minimum commitment, while Business requires only 1 month. The Project plan is a one-time purchase with no recurring commitment.

Does Modelia integrate with Shopify?

Yes, Modelia has a dedicated Shopify app for connecting directly to a store's product catalog, and it's independently rated 4.1/5 on the Shopify App Store.
