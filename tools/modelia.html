Modelia is a good fit for small and mid-sized apparel brands that already have flat-lay or mannequin photos and want on-model images quickly; skip it if you need guaranteed unlimited output at a fixed price, or if your catalog requires exact garment fidelity that you cannot review by eye. It converts existing product photos into images of AI models wearing the garment, and bundles related tools such as outfit generation, pose changes, accessory try-on, background editing, upscaling and short video. It is sold as a web app with a Shopify app and an API, and its pages state that outputs carry C2PA Content Credentials. Pricing is credit-based on the entry plans and 'fair use' on the higher tiers, per its pricing page checked on 2026-10-20.

Best for

Good for independent designers, DTC apparel brands and Shopify sellers who want faster, cheaper catalog imagery and can review each result before publishing. Not for: buyers who need a fixed, explicit monthly generation count on higher tiers (Atelier and up use fair-use limits), teams who need Starter output for commercial use (free images carry a 'Created by Modelia' watermark), or anyone not ready to lose saved models and catalog data if they cancel. Pricing for the House tier is not public; it is quoted per contract.

Key features

01

On-model conversion

Flat-lay to model, mannequin to model and model to model. A standard image costs 3 credits at 2K or 4K; each request returns up to four options on paid plans (up to two on Starter).

02

Outfits and poses

An outfit generator and Repose, which produces several poses from one image.

03

Accessory try-on

Places hats, shoes, bags and glasses on models.

04

Editing tools

Background changer and remover, upscaler, inpainting and colour changer. Per-tool credit costs are on a separate table that could not be verified.

05

Video

Generates short clips from an image plus a text prompt for motion, camera or mood. Credit cost per clip could not be verified today.

06

Model casting and references

Saved model slots (3 on Creator, 10 on Atelier, 20 on Brand, unlimited on House) and reference limits (up to 100 on Atelier, 350 on Brand).

07

Shopify app, API and connectors

Shopify app and API are listed on the site; Creator includes API on the same credits, higher tiers use a separate API credit balance; Brand adds one PIM or DAM connector.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The free Starter plan gives a real test of quality, though it is watermarked and limited to 2K.
  • All self-serve plans are listed as monthly and cancel-anytime, so there is no minimum-term lock-in on the pricing page.
  • Credit packs (150 for $30, 400 for $60, 1,000 for $150) let Creator users top up without changing plan.
  • Paid-plan and API content is stated as not used to train models, which matters for unreleased collections.
  • C2PA Content Credentials are attached to outputs, helpful for AI-disclosure requirements.

Cons

  • Cancelling moves you to Starter and deletes saved models, backgrounds, poses and catalog, forfeits remaining credits and revokes API keys; images and downloads remain yours.
  • Plan upgrades are effective immediately at the full new price, with unused days on the old plan not refunded.
  • Atelier, Brand and House use 'fair use'; the page says it is not unlimited and heavy use can mean slower queues or blocked generations, with no numeric cap given.
  • Starter content may be used to improve models, unlike paid plans.
  • The jump from Creator ($30) to Atelier ($109) is steep, and API use is on separate credits above Creator.
  • Independent review ratings were not re-verified today, so no third-party score is quoted.

Frequently asked questions

Is Modelia free?

There is a free Starter plan with 20 credits a month, up to 2K output, up to two images per generation and a 'Created by Modelia' watermark. Paid plans remove the watermark.

How much does it cost?

As of 2026-10-20: Creator $30/mo, Atelier $109/mo, Brand $385/mo, House quoted on an annual contract. Prices are before tax and the number is the same in USD and EUR.

Is there a minimum commitment?

The pricing page lists monthly, cancel-anytime terms for Starter, Creator, Atelier and Brand; only House requires an annual contract.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You drop to Starter. Saved models, backgrounds, poses and catalog are deleted, remaining credits are forfeited, and API keys are revoked. Generated images and downloads stay yours.

Does it work with Shopify?

Its site lists a Shopify app and an API. Details of the Shopify app's behaviour were not verified today.
