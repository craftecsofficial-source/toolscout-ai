Writesonic is a good fit for marketing and SEO teams who want to see and improve how AI assistants mention their brand; it is a poor choice for bloggers or freelancers who just want cheap article drafts. It no longer works as a budget writing tool, and plans start at $79/mo on annual billing.

The platform tracks brand visibility, citations and sentiment for prompts you choose, then ranks fixes in an Action Center: content gaps, citation gaps, and technical issues from AI crawler visits. A content agent rewrites pages for AI answers, and the AI article writer remains. Self-serve plans track 3 platforms (ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews); Enterprise covers all 8 the site lists.

Best for

For: in-house SEO and marketing teams and agencies managing brand presence in AI search, with a $79-$399/mo budget. Not for: individuals or small sites wanting inexpensive article generation, or anyone needing a permanent free plan.

Key features

01

AI visibility tracking

Monitors brand mentions, citations and sentiment for chosen prompts; daily prompt and answer counts rise by plan.

02

Action Center

Ranks opportunities such as missing prompts, citation gaps and technical issues. Growth gets a limited trial (5 off-page and 5 on-page actions/mo); full access is Enterprise.

03

Content agent and article writer

Rewrites pages for AI answers with FAQ blocks and comparison tables; plans include 15 to 50 AI articles per month.

04

Crawler and site audits

Checks robots.txt, schema, indexing and errors from AI bot visits; audit counts and page limits scale by plan.

05

Integrations

Google Analytics, Search Console, Looker Studio, WordPress, APIs and MCP are listed on the site.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Closes the loop: measure visibility, get ranked fixes, then produce content
  • Site says fixes are checked at Day 14 and Day 28 for citation share and traffic
  • 7-day trial with no credit card
  • Writing tools are still included, so no second subscription for articles

Cons

  • Entry price $79/mo (annual) is far above its old budget-writer positioning
  • Self-serve plans track only 3 AI platforms; the rest need Enterprise
  • Action Center and agentic runs are trial-capped below Enterprise
  • Monthly-billed prices are not shown directly, so budget for annual commitment
  • Collaboration is thin: 1 user on Starter, 3 on Growth

Frequently asked questions

How much does Writesonic cost?

Starter $79/mo, Basic $199/mo and Growth $399/mo on annual billing; Enterprise is custom. Monthly billing is higher and not listed directly.

Is there a free plan?

No permanent free plan. A 7-day free trial with no credit card is offered.

Which AI platforms are tracked?

ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews on self-serve plans; Enterprise covers all 8 the site lists.

Is it still good for writing articles?

It includes an AI article writer (15-50 articles/mo), but the product is built around visibility tracking; cheaper dedicated writers exist.

Writesonic or Copy.ai?

Writesonic targets AI-search visibility; Copy.ai targets GTM workflow automation.
