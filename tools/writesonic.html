Writesonic began as an AI copywriting and article-generation tool but has substantially repositioned itself as an "AI Search Visibility" (Generative Engine Optimization / GEO) platform. It now focuses on tracking how brands are represented across AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity, and pairs that tracking with tools to fix visibility gaps through content and technical site changes. The company lists enterprise customers including Amazon, Unilever and Acer among 10,000+ marketing teams.

Its core differentiator versus traditional AI writing tools (Jasper, Copy.ai) is that Writesonic no longer competes primarily as a content generator — it combines AI-search-engine monitoring, an "Action Center" that prioritizes and can auto-execute fixes, and legacy content/SEO tools into a single dashboard aimed at marketing and SEO teams managing brand presence in an AI-search world.

Best for

Writesonic is now best suited to mid-market and enterprise marketing, SEO and agency teams that need to monitor and actively manage their brand's presence across AI answer engines alongside traditional SEO and content production, and who can justify a $79–$399+/month budget. It is a poor fit for individual bloggers, freelancers or small businesses looking for a cheap AI article generator — that market position has effectively been abandoned in favor of the higher-priced GEO/AI-visibility category.

Key features

01

AI Search Visibility Tracking

Monitors how a brand is mentioned, cited or recommended across AI platforms, tracking a set number of prompts and daily answers that scales by plan.

02

Action Center

Surfaces prioritized off-page, on-page and technical actions to improve AI search visibility, with trial actions on lower tiers and full execution access on Enterprise.

03

Sentiment Analysis

Analyzes how a brand is portrayed (positively, negatively or neutrally) within AI-generated answers, available starting on the Growth plan.

04

AI Article Writer & Content Strategy

Legacy content-generation tooling that produces AI-written articles aimed at closing content gaps identified by the visibility tracking.

05

Site Audits with automatic AI fixes

Crawls pages per audit and can automatically apply certain SEO/AI-readability fixes.

06

Agentic Workflows

AI agents that automate SEO, content and AI-search tasks, with limited trial runs on lower tiers and full access on Enterprise.

07

Ads monitoring

Tracks how a brand's ads appear within ChatGPT and Google AI surfaces, included on all self-serve tiers.

08

Integrations

Connects to Google Search Console, WordPress, Google Keyword Planner and Reddit data; Enterprise adds GA4 and Looker Studio.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Early and comprehensive move into GEO, tracking real visibility across up to 10 AI platforms rather than just traditional search rankings
  • Combines AI-search monitoring, content generation and technical site audits in a single dashboard
  • Strong, high-volume review scores across both G2 and Capterra, giving statistically solid confidence in ratings
  • Publishes concrete customer outcomes, which is unusual specificity for this category
  • 7-day free trial requires no credit card, lowering the barrier to evaluate the platform
  • Retains legacy AI Article Writer and content tools, so teams don't need a second subscription for bulk SEO content

Cons

  • The platform has repositioned dramatically away from being an affordable AI writing tool; entry price is now $79/mo, a large jump from its historical ~$19/mo positioning
  • Pricing tiers escalate steeply, with core features like Sentiment Analysis and full Action Center access gated behind the $399/mo Growth tier or higher
  • Reddit threads report a pattern of billing and cancellation complaints — unresolved charges and difficulty cancelling
  • Legacy content-generation quality complaints persist: users report getting shorter articles than needed, requiring heavy editing
  • A "fair usage policy" applies on top of stated plan limits, meaning quoted prompt/article/audit numbers may not be truly unlimited
  • Team collaboration is limited on lower tiers, expensive for small marketing teams trying to collaborate

What reviewers say

Writesonic scores well on both major review platforms, with reviewers praising speed, breadth of content formats and — more recently — the AI visibility tracking features. However, Reddit and independent commentary flag a real pattern of billing and cancellation complaints that is inconsistent with the polished review-platform scores, along with older, recurring criticism that generated articles can be shorter and less rank-ready than needed without human editing.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Writesonic cost now?

Self-serve plans run from $79/mo (Starter) to $399/mo (Growth) when billed annually, with a custom-priced Enterprise tier above that. This is a significant increase from Writesonic's earlier ~$19/mo AI-writing-tool pricing, reflecting its shift into AI search visibility (GEO) tracking.

Is Writesonic still an AI writing tool?

It still includes an AI Article Writer and content generation, but the product is now primarily marketed as an AI Search Visibility platform for tracking and improving brand presence across AI answer engines like ChatGPT and Gemini.

Does Writesonic have a free plan?

There is no permanent free plan; Writesonic offers a 7-day free trial with no credit card required, after which pricing starts at $79/mo billed annually.

Is Writesonic worth it in 2026?

For marketing/SEO teams that need to track and act on AI search visibility, it can be worth the cost given the breadth of platforms tracked. For someone just wanting cheap AI-written articles, the current pricing and feature gating make it a weaker value than dedicated writing tools.

How does Writesonic compare to Copy.ai or Jasper?

Writesonic has pivoted furthest into AI-search-visibility (GEO) tracking, whereas Copy.ai focuses on GTM workflow automation and Jasper remains closer to a brand-content generation tool.

Are there known issues with billing or cancellation?

Yes — Reddit and Trustpilot discussions describe a pattern of billing disputes, denied refunds and cancellation friction for some users. It's recommended to use monthly billing initially and retain cancellation confirmation records if signing up.
