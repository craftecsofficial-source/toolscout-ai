SummarizingTool is a good fit for students and casual readers who want fast, free summaries of articles, documents and videos. Skip it if you need to process large volumes daily, dislike ad-supported sites, or need confirmed Pro pricing before committing. You paste text, upload a document or give a video link, and pick a summary style: short, long, key points or a single best line, with adjustable length. The same site adds a paraphraser, rewriter, AI detector and plagiarism checker, plus a Chrome extension. Chat With Video, Flashcards and Mind Map are labelled Pro. Free use has a daily request limit that the site does not quantify, and signing in raises it. We could not load the pricing page, so paid prices are unverified. There is no independent accuracy testing here, so check summaries of anything important against the source.

Best for

Students, researchers and writers who need quick overviews of long text or videos and occasional rewording. It is not for heavy daily batch users, people who need guaranteed accuracy on legal, medical or academic material without checking the source, or anyone who wants an ad-free interface or a confirmed price list.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The summarizer is described as free for all users, so you can test it without paying.
  • Four summary styles cover skimming, studying and quoting.
  • One site covers summarizing, rewording and AI-detection checks.
  • A Chrome extension makes it quick to use while reading.

Cons

  • The free daily limit is not quantified, and once reached you must sign in or pay.
  • Paid prices could not be verified, so older figures for a monthly plan may be out of date.
  • The site is ad-supported and asks you to disable your ad blocker.
  • Language support is stated as both 'over 10' and '16+', so confirm your language works.
  • No independent accuracy testing; AI summaries can miss or distort details.
