Sapling is a good fit for business teams that write a lot of customer replies and want suggestions inside their CRM or helpdesk, and for developers who need a language or AI-detection API. Solo writers who just want a polished general grammar checker may be happier with a consumer-focused tool. Sapling is a language-model toolkit: a browser extension plus integrations that suggest corrections, completions and rewrites as you type, and an API and SDK for building the same features into your own product. The site also lists an MCP server so Claude and other MCP clients can call its tools, and enterprise options such as SSO, SCIM, PII redaction and self-hosting.

Best for

Good for support, sales and success teams working in CRM or helpdesk tools, developers who need a grammar or AI-detection API, and companies that need SSO or self-hosting. Not for people who only want a general-purpose personal writing coach with the least setup, and not for small teams needing Enterprise features without a 10-seat commitment.

Key features

01

Grammar and quality checks

Checks grammar and spelling and gives quality scores and error reports. The Free plan covers basic suggestions only.

02

Autocomplete Everywhere and Rephrase

Sentence completion and rewrites across sites. These are Pro features and are not in the Free plan.

03

Snippets

Shared text-expansion macros for repeated replies. Free is capped at 20; Pro is unlimited.

04

AI content detector

Gives an overall score and per-sentence predictions. Free allows 2,000 characters per check and 5 files per batch; Pro allows 100,000 characters and 10 files.

05

Integrations

Connects to CRM and messaging tools such as Zendesk, ServiceNow, Salesforce and Gmail, and to editors such as TinyMCE and Quill.

06

API, SDK and MCP server

HTTP endpoints plus Python and JavaScript libraries, billed by usage.

07

Enterprise controls

Chat assist, team analytics, conversational insights, SSO/SCIM, bulk user provisioning and self-hosted deployment are listed under Enterprise.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The Free plan is usable, not just a teaser: it runs in the browser extension, Google Docs, Outlook and Word.
  • Annual Pro at $12/mo is modest, and you can try it free for a month without a card.
  • The API, SDKs and MCP server let developers reuse the same language tools in their own apps.
  • Enterprise lists the admin and security controls larger companies ask for, including SSO, SCIM and self-hosting.
  • The pricing page mentions a discounted plan for students and nonprofits on request.

Cons

  • Free is limited: basic suggestions only, 20 snippets, and a 2,000-character AI detector cap.
  • Monthly Pro at $25 is more than double the annual per-month rate, so the real price depends on committing for a year.
  • Enterprise requires at least 10 seats and a sales quote, so small teams cannot buy it directly.
  • Chat assist, team analytics and custom integrations sit behind Enterprise only.
  • Premium domains are not available on Free, so some sites may not get suggestions.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Sapling cost?

Free is $0. Pro is $25/month, or $12/month billed annually. Enterprise starts at $15/seat/month with a 10-seat minimum and a custom quote. The API is usage-based with a one-time $5 minimum.

Does Sapling have a free plan?

Yes. It gives basic suggestions in the browser extension, Google Docs, Outlook and Word, with 20 snippets and a 2,000-character AI detector limit. New individual signups are also enrolled in a free trial.

What does Pro add over Free?

Advanced suggestions, Autocomplete Everywhere, Rephrase, unlimited snippets and premium-domain use, a 100,000-character AI detector, and email support.

How does Sapling compare with Grammarly?

Sapling leans toward business teams and developers, with CRM integrations, an API and enterprise deployment options. Grammarly is the more general consumer-oriented checker; compare the free tiers on your own text before paying.

Can I use Sapling through an API?

Yes. There is an API with Python and JavaScript SDKs, billed by usage with a one-time $5 minimum, plus an MCP server.
