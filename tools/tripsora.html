Tripsora is a reasonable pick for people planning one or two trips who like asking questions in chat and paying a small one-off fee; skip it if you plan trips constantly or want a neutral, commission-free comparison. It is an AI travel agent from Tripsora Capitals Ltd (registered in England and Wales). You describe your trip in conversation and it looks up flights, hotels and bookable tours, then assembles a day-by-day plan with weather, a map, calendar export and sharing. Booking is handed off to partners such as Trip.com, Booking.com, Expedia and GetYourGuide, which may pay Tripsora a commission. Earlier versions of this listing described a per-itinerary model; the live site now charges per question instead. We could not verify any independent user ratings.

Best for

Good for independent travelers planning a specific trip who want chat-style help and booking links and prefer a small one-time spend. Not for frequent planners who would burn through question packs, anyone under 18, or people wanting a neutral, commission-free comparison tool.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • One-off £4.99 packs with non-expiring questions suit people who travel once or twice a year.
  • One chat covers flights, hotels, tours and the itinerary, so less tab-switching.
  • Itineraries include weather, map, calendar export and sharing, which helps group planning.
  • Clarifying questions and translations don't use up your question allowance.

Cons

  • The free allowance is just 2 questions, so you will hit the paywall quickly; each message otherwise counts as a question.
  • A one-question-per-minute limit slows heavy planning sessions.
  • Booking partners may pay commission, so suggestions are not necessarily independent of affiliate revenue.
  • 18+ only, and we found no independent review-site rating to check quality at scale.
  • Pricing changed from per-itinerary to per-question; older third-party descriptions may be out of date.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Tripsora cost?

Free for 2 questions, then £4.99 for 10 more as a one-time payment with no subscription. A human travel advisor is offered but no price is listed. Checked on the live site on 2026-10-31.

Does Tripsora book trips for me?

It plans and surfaces options, but booking happens with partner sites such as Trip.com, Booking.com, Expedia and GetYourGuide, which may pay Tripsora a commission.

Do paid questions expire?

The site says paid questions don't expire.

Are there usage limits?

One question per minute, and users must be 18 or older. Clarifying questions and translations are free.

How is it different from ChatGPT or Gemini?

It is purpose-built for travel with live flight, hotel and tour data and booking links; general assistants are more flexible and have subscription pricing but no built-in travel booking.
