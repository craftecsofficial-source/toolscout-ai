Tripsora is an AI trip-planning tool from Tripsora Capitals Ltd, a company registered in England & Wales and headquartered in London, that positions itself explicitly against generic AI chatbots like ChatGPT for travel planning. Rather than generating itineraries purely from a language model's general world knowledge, Tripsora says it draws from a curated database of real traveler experiences, current prices, and local knowledge — covering 895+ cities across 148+ countries — to produce a day-by-day itinerary with insider tips, optimal visit timing, budget breakdowns, and local secrets, delivered in under two minutes. Example tips surfaced on its marketing pages include specific, non-obvious advice such as which Venice market locals actually shop at instead of the touristy Rialto, or the exact time to visit Kyoto's Fushimi Inari shrine to avoid crowds.

The product originated as a Kickstarter campaign ('Tripsora.com: The Smart AI Travel Planner' by Mohammed S Ali) and has since grown into a live web app and Google Play mobile app (listed under the developer name TripBuilder), reporting 75,000+ users across 148 countries. Unlike most AI trip planners that use a monthly subscription, Tripsora uses pay-per-itinerary pricing, with a free tier for short trial itineraries and paid tiers scaling by itinerary length. It also bundles a free global eSIM offer (500MB data plus a discount on top-ups) with paid plans, a practical inclusion aimed at travelers going abroad. The company markets an average 4.8-star rating from its user base, though this figure is self-reported on its own site rather than sourced from an independent third-party review platform.

Best for

Tripsora fits independent travelers and vacation planners who want a detailed, locally-informed itinerary for a specific upcoming trip and prefer paying once rather than subscribing. It's a weaker fit for frequent business travelers or trip planners managing many trips per year, where a flat monthly subscription tool would likely be more cost-effective.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Tripsora's core pitch — real traveler-vetted tips over generic AI output — is backed by concrete, specific examples on its marketing pages (exact times, exact markets, exact savings), which reads as more substantive than typical AI travel-app marketing.
  • Pay-per-itinerary pricing (from £7.99) suits people who only plan one or two trips a year and don't want a recurring travel-app subscription they'll forget to cancel.
  • Bundling a free global eSIM with data and a top-up discount adds tangible value beyond the itinerary itself for international travelers.
  • The company has real traction claims — 75,000+ travelers across 148 countries — and a public Kickstarter and Google Play presence supporting that it's an active, funded product rather than a landing-page shell.
  • Itineraries include budget breakdowns and current local prices, addressing a common complaint about generic AI travel advice going stale or hallucinating costs.

Cons

  • There is no subscription plan for frequent travelers; heavy users planning many trips per year will pay per itinerary each time, which can add up.
  • The free tier's 2-itinerary lifetime cap (not a recurring monthly allowance) is quite restrictive as a trial before requiring payment.
  • The prominently displayed 4.8★ rating is self-reported on Tripsora's own marketing site; no independent listing on G2, Capterra, or Trustpilot could be located to verify it against a broader review sample.
  • As a smaller, relatively young company (Kickstarter-funded), its 'curated real traveler database' claim isn't independently auditable the way a large established travel platform's review corpus would be.

Frequently asked questions

What is Tripsora?

Tripsora is an AI travel planner that generates personalized, day-by-day itineraries using a curated database of real traveler tips, current prices, and local knowledge rather than generic AI-generated lists.

How much does Tripsora cost?

It uses pay-per-itinerary pricing: free for short 3-day itineraries (2 lifetime), £7.99 for up to 7-day itineraries, and £9.99 for up to 14-day itineraries. There's no monthly subscription.

Is Tripsora available as an app?

Yes, alongside the web app at tripsora.com, it's available on Google Play (listed under developer name TripBuilder).

How is Tripsora different from asking ChatGPT to plan a trip?

Tripsora says it draws from a curated database of real traveler experiences, current prices, and local insider tips, rather than generic web-scraped or hallucinated travel advice.

Who runs Tripsora?

Tripsora is operated by Tripsora Capitals Ltd, registered in England & Wales and headquartered in London, and originated from a Kickstarter campaign.

Does Tripsora include anything beyond the itinerary?

Yes — paid plans include a free global eSIM offer (500MB data plus 15% off the first top-up) alongside the itinerary.
