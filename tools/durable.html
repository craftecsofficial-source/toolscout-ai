Durable is an AI-powered business builder that starts with generating a complete website — copy, images, layout, and branding — in roughly 30 seconds from a few prompts about the business. Beyond the website, Durable positions itself as a full small-business operating layer: every plan includes SEO, secure hosting, unlimited traffic, and a built-in CRM, and paid plans add AI agents that work in the background — a blog-writing agent that publishes posts, a lead agent that captures and replies to inquiries, an SEO/GEO agent that optimizes for both traditional search engines and AI answer engines like ChatGPT, and an always-on AI chat assistant that understands the business's context and can generate logos, social posts, and marketing copy on request. The company reports over 3 million businesses built and 11 million+ websites generated using the platform.

Durable differentiates itself from typical no-code website builders by framing itself as a replacement for multiple separate subscriptions — website builder, CRM, booking software, and marketing tools — bundled into one plan, with messaging that a typical stack of comparable tools would cost around $396/month versus $25/month for its Launch plan. Reviews on Trustpilot (4.8/5 across 275 reviews) skew overwhelmingly positive, with praise concentrated on the speed of site generation and, notably, on the quality of human customer support — many reviews name specific support agents by name for going 'above and beyond' resolving domain, billing, or editing issues. Because it's a no-code, AI-first platform, Durable intentionally limits HTML/CSS customization in favor of speed and simplicity, which the company states directly: users with deep custom-code needs are told to contact support rather than expect open code access. It's aimed squarely at non-technical small-business owners rather than developers or agencies building bespoke sites.

Best for

Durable is built for non-technical solo entrepreneurs and small local service businesses (coaches, contractors, cleaners, photographers, consultants) who want a professional website, CRM, and marketing automation live in minutes without hiring a developer or designer. It's a weaker fit for businesses that need deep custom code control, complex e-commerce functionality, or agencies building bespoke, highly differentiated sites for clients.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Speed of site generation is the single most repeated praise point — Trustpilot reviewers describe going from zero to a live, professional-looking website in minutes with no prior web-building experience.
  • Customer support quality stands out unusually strongly for a self-serve SaaS product: dozens of Trustpilot reviews name individual support agents (Stu, Rita, Aljohn, Anjie, Leodith) for resolving domain, billing, and editing issues quickly and patiently.
  • Bundling a website, CRM, bookings, SEO/GEO, and AI marketing agents into one subscription removes the need to stitch together multiple tools, which Durable markets as replacing roughly $396/month in separate subscriptions.
  • The AI business assistant is context-aware of the specific business and can generate logos, social posts, and copy on demand, extending value beyond the initial site build.
  • AI search/GEO optimization targets visibility in AI answer engines like ChatGPT in addition to traditional Google SEO, which is a differentiator versus many legacy website builders.
  • A G2 reviewer with no prior website-building experience said Durable 'built a website in seconds, making it look professional both with its appearance and its content,' echoing the broader review sentiment.

Cons

  • Durable is explicitly a no-code platform and does not offer extensive HTML customization, so users needing bespoke design control need to contact support rather than edit code directly.
  • An older G2 review noted that updating a site required starting an entirely new project rather than editing the existing one — a workflow friction some users flagged.
  • A minority of Trustpilot reviews are sharply negative, with one calling it 'the most scammy platform' due to perceived unsolicited outreach/spam tied to lead generation, and another citing lack of Russian-language support.
  • Durable is not purpose-built for e-commerce; the company itself says it isn't 'designed specifically' for online stores, even though some businesses have launched them successfully on the platform.
  • AI usage limits (chat messages, images, blog posts, lead replies) are metered by plan, so heavier users will need to move up to Grow to avoid hitting caps.

What reviewers say

Durable's strongest review signal is Trustpilot, where it holds a 4.8/5 'Excellent' TrustScore across 275 reviews, driven overwhelmingly by praise for fast site generation and standout live customer support; G2 has only 2 reviews (4.5/5 average) so is directionally consistent but too thin to be conclusive on its own.

Frequently asked questions

How fast can I get a website with Durable?

Durable generates a complete website — layout, copy, and images — from a short prompt in about 30 seconds, which users can then refine.

Is Durable free to use?

Yes, there's a Free plan with a subdomain, basic SEO, limited AI images/chat, and CRM for up to 10 customers; paid plans start at $25/month (Launch) for a custom domain and more AI usage.

Does Durable include a CRM?

Yes, every plan includes a built-in CRM, with paid plans adding an AI Lead agent that automatically captures and replies to leads.

Can I use my own domain name with Durable?

Yes, custom domains are included free with every Durable subscription (or you can connect an existing domain you already own) and setup takes a few minutes.

Can I edit the website's HTML directly?

No — Durable is a no-code platform and doesn't offer extensive HTML customization; users with specific code needs are directed to contact support.

What is GEO in Durable's SEO features?

GEO stands for generative engine optimization — Durable optimizes business listings and content so they're more likely to be surfaced by AI tools like ChatGPT, in addition to traditional Google SEO.
