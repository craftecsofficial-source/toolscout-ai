Durable is great for solo owners and small service businesses who want a working website plus lead tracking and bookings without assembling separate tools, and a weak choice for anyone who wants pixel-level design control or a large content site. The AI builds a site from a short description, then the same account includes a CRM, calendar-synced booking, payments, SEO and AI-search (GEO) tools, image and logo generators, and an assistant for marketing copy. The free plan is only a preview on a durable.site subdomain; Launch ($25/month) adds hosting, a domain and 1,000 AI credits a month, and Grow ($49/month) adds onboarding, unlimited team members, translation and 5,000 credits.

Best for

Good for new or established small businesses such as home services, wellness, pet and creative work that need a quick site with bookings and leads. Not for developers, agencies wanting full design control, or businesses needing a free custom domain on the free tier.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • One subscription replaces separate site, CRM and booking tools.
  • Setup is fast and needs no design skills.
  • Launch includes a domain credit and priority human support.
  • Free plan lets you see the generated site before paying.

Cons

  • The pricing page shows $25 vs $22 and $49 vs $41; the lower numbers are probably yearly billing but the page does not say.
  • Free plan caps CRM at 10 customers and offers custom domains only as a private preview.
  • AI credit allowances limit how much you can generate each month.
  • Previous third-party reviews noted limited customization and some complaints about spam-like outreach; not re-verified today.

Frequently asked questions

Paid plans include a free domain or your own; the free plan uses a durable.site subdomain.

Yes, bookings with calendar sync and built-in payment processing are listed.
