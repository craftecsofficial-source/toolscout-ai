Common Room is a good fit for B2B revenue teams that already have product, community or web signals and want them tied to CRM accounts; solo sellers and small startups should skip it because the entry plan costs $2,500 a month. It builds a profile of each buyer from CRM records, product usage, community activity and website visits, adds enrichment and a contact database called Prospector, and uses AI to score accounts, research them and trigger alerts in Slack or your CRM. Zoom said in July 2026 that it had acquired the company, so expect closer ties to Zoom's sales products, though the pricing page gives no Zoom plan details.

Best for

Good for B2B teams with several data sources (product, community, CRM) and a RevOps person to configure workflows. Not for individual reps or tiny teams who need a cheap lead list, not for companies with no product or community signal to feed it, and not for anyone who needs a free tier.

Key features

01

Person360 and buyer profiles

Merges identities across CRM, product, community and web data so a rep sees one timeline per person and account.

02

Prospector and enrichment

A contact database with credit-based lookups, plus premium phone-number enrichment credits on each plan.

03

RoomieAI research and scoring

AI research on accounts and contacts, drawn from a monthly credit allowance, with AI scoring to prioritize outreach.

04

Alerts, workflows and segments

Unlimited rules that push signals into Slack, CRM or sales-engagement tools.

05

Website activity and deanonymization

Tracks site visits and attempts to identify visiting companies; volume limits rise with the plan.

06

MCP server

Lets AI assistants query Common Room data; included on all plans per the pricing page.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Pulls product-usage and community signals alongside CRM data, which most sales-intel tools skip.
  • Alerts, workflows and segments are unlimited on every plan, so you are not metered on automation.
  • Pricing is published, letting you budget before talking to sales.
  • All plans include CRM, sales-engagement and Slack integrations.

Cons

  • Starting at $2,500/month, it costs far more than lead-database tools aimed at individual reps.
  • Seats are bundled (5/15/30), so growing teams may be pushed up a tier.
  • Credits cap AI research and prospecting; product signals, DataAgent actions and extra Prospector credits cost extra.
  • The Zoom acquisition may change packaging; the site does not say how.

Frequently asked questions

Does Common Room still have a free plan?

The current pricing page lists only Essential, Advanced and Enterprise. A free plan was not shown on 2026-10-29, so confirm with sales if you need one.

Was Common Room acquired?

Yes. Zoom announced on 2 July 2026 that it would acquire Common Room and stated the transaction closed in July 2026. The post did not say whether the brand will change.

What does the entry plan cost?

Essential is $2,500 per month billed annually, with 5 seats and up to 100k contacts. Advanced and Enterprise are quoted.

Which tools does it integrate with?

The site cites Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Outreach and 50+ others. Exact availability varies by plan (select vs comprehensive).
