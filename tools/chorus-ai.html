Chorus.ai was founded in 2015 as an independent conversation intelligence platform, helping sales teams record, transcribe, and analyze customer-facing calls and meetings. It built a reputation as one of the earliest and most credible players in the category, alongside Gong, by combining natural language processing with sales-specific insights like competitor mentions and objection handling.

In 2021, Chorus.ai was acquired by ZoomInfo, a leading B2B contact and intent data provider, and has since been rebranded and integrated as "ZoomInfo Chorus." This gave the product a direct pipeline into ZoomInfo's account and contact data, letting conversation insights feed back into prospecting workflows and account scoring.

Today Chorus functions best as part of the broader ZoomInfo revenue platform, appealing to sales and marketing teams that want conversation analytics unified with firmographic and intent data rather than operating as a purely standalone coaching tool.

Best for

Chorus.ai (ZoomInfo Chorus) is best suited for sales organizations that already use or are evaluating ZoomInfo's data platform and want conversation intelligence unified with prospecting and intent signals. It's a strong fit for sales enablement teams running structured coaching and onboarding programs that rely on call review. Revenue leaders wanting deal risk visibility derived directly from customer conversations will find it valuable. Teams looking for a lightweight, purely standalone call recording tool may find simpler alternatives more cost effective.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Transcription and search quality are strong, making it easy to find specific moments across large call libraries
  • Being part of ZoomInfo means conversation data can be combined with account intent signals for richer prioritization
  • Coaching scorecards and rep benchmarking are well suited for structured onboarding and ramp programs
  • Established, mature product with years of refinement in the conversation intelligence category

Cons

  • Since the ZoomInfo acquisition, buying Chorus as a fully standalone tool can be less straightforward and less cost-competitive
  • Total cost can escalate quickly when bundled with broader ZoomInfo data products
  • Some legacy Chorus users have noted a slower pace of independent feature releases post-acquisition
  • Advanced analytics and momentum scoring require ramp-up time for managers to use effectively

Frequently asked questions

Is Chorus.ai the same as ZoomInfo Chorus?

Yes, Chorus.ai was acquired by ZoomInfo in 2021 and now operates under the ZoomInfo Chorus brand as part of its platform.

Can Chorus be bought without ZoomInfo's other products?

It can be purchased as a standalone module in some cases, but it's most commonly sold bundled with ZoomInfo's broader data platform.

What does Chorus record?

It records and transcribes sales calls, video conferencing meetings, and can also analyze email communication.

How does Chorus compare to Gong?

Both are leading conversation intelligence platforms with similar core capabilities; Chorus's differentiator is its tight integration with ZoomInfo's contact and intent data.

Does Chorus integrate with Zoom and other video tools?

Yes, it integrates with major video conferencing platforms including Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet.

Is Chorus good for rep coaching?

Yes, its scorecards and searchable call library are commonly used by sales enablement teams for onboarding and coaching.
