Urbint is a New York-based company that builds an AI risk prediction platform specifically for the utility and infrastructure sector. Its core product, often referred to as Urbint Foresight, ingests data such as historical incidents, geospatial and asset information, weather conditions, and crew/job details to generate predictive risk scores for specific locations and work orders.

The platform is used in two primary contexts: damage prevention, where it helps utilities anticipate and reduce risk of third-party damage to buried infrastructure (such as gas or electric lines) during excavation, and employee safety, where it helps identify field conditions and job types most likely to lead to worker injuries. In both cases, the goal is to shift safety programs from reactive incident response toward proactive risk mitigation.

Urbint's customers are typically large gas, electric, and telecom utilities with significant field operations and existing safety and damage-prevention programs. The platform is designed to plug into an operator's existing data systems (GIS, work management, weather feeds) rather than replace them, adding a predictive risk layer on top.

Best for

Urbint is designed for gas, electric, telecom, and other infrastructure utilities with significant field operations and formal damage-prevention or safety programs. It's best suited to organizations with existing GIS, incident, and work-management data that Urbint's models can draw on to generate meaningful risk scores. Safety and operations leaders looking to move from reactive incident response to proactive, data-driven risk mitigation are the primary buyers. It is not intended for small contractors or businesses outside the utility/infrastructure field-operations space.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Urbint's models are purpose-built around utility and infrastructure risk scenarios, rather than being a generic enterprise risk-management tool retrofitted for the sector.
  • Combining GIS, weather, and historical incident data into one predictive score gives operations teams a more holistic risk view than siloed data sources alone.
  • Prioritizing high-risk jobs and locations helps utilities allocate limited safety and inspection resources more efficiently rather than spreading them evenly.
  • Shifting from reactive incident tracking to proactive, leading-indicator-based risk management aligns with how regulators increasingly expect utilities to manage safety.
  • Mobile risk context for field crews supports better in-the-moment decision-making before and during jobs.

Cons

  • As an enterprise platform sold on custom quotes, Urbint is generally out of reach for small contractors or operators without large field-safety budgets.
  • The accuracy and usefulness of risk predictions depend heavily on the quality and completeness of a utility's existing GIS, incident, and operational data.
  • Integration with legacy utility systems (GIS, work management platforms) can require meaningful IT involvement to fully realize the platform's value.
  • Its narrow focus on utility and infrastructure field safety means it isn't a general-purpose risk or safety tool applicable to other industries.
  • Like many predictive safety tools, demonstrating clear ROI requires sustained tracking of incident trends over time, which can take longer than a single budget cycle to show.

Frequently asked questions

What does Urbint do?

Urbint is an AI risk prediction platform that helps utilities and infrastructure operators anticipate safety and damage risks in field operations before incidents occur.

Who uses Urbint?

Urbint is primarily used by gas, electric, and telecom utilities and other infrastructure operators with large field workforces and damage-prevention or safety programs.

What is Urbint Foresight?

Urbint Foresight is the core product that generates predictive risk scores for locations and jobs using historical incident, GIS, weather, and operational data.

Does Urbint prevent excavation damage?

Yes, one of Urbint's main use cases is damage prevention, predicting the risk of third-party damage to underground infrastructure during excavation work.

Does Urbint help with employee safety, not just infrastructure?

Yes, Urbint also offers employee safety risk prediction, scoring jobs and field conditions for injury risk to help safety teams prioritize precautions.

How is Urbint priced?

Urbint is sold on custom, quote-based enterprise pricing, typically scoped to a utility's service territory size and the modules deployed.
