Synthesys is a good fit for marketers and small agencies who need a steady flow of short ad creatives and avatar-led sales videos without juggling separate model subscriptions. Skip it if you need a free tier to test first, long-form training video with deep avatar customization, or predictable cost on premium models, since credits drain fast. The platform is an AI video agent: you describe a job, and it picks a video model (the homepage names Sora 2, Google Veo 3.1, Wan and Kling) to produce UGC ads, talking-avatar videos, product ads, explainers and commercials. It adds stock avatars, a voiceover studio, a brand kit and, on higher plans, an ads library with competitor tracking and scheduled publishing. Everything runs off one monthly credit allowance, and commercial rights are included on every plan.

Best for

Good for marketers, e-commerce sellers and small agencies producing high volumes of short social ads and avatar-led promo clips. Not for teams wanting a free tier to evaluate, long-form corporate training video with tightly controlled branded avatars, or buyers who need guaranteed per-video pricing on premium models.

Key features

01

Multi-model video generation

Jobs are routed to third-party video models such as Sora 2 and Google Veo 3.1; resolution ranges from 480p to 4K depending on the model.

02

UGC ads and avatar videos

Produces UGC-style ads, talking-avatar sales videos, product ads, animated explainers and reel-format ads in 9:16, 16:9, 1:1 and 4:5.

03

Voiceover studio

Voiceover, music and script writing are listed as not consuming credits on any plan.

04

Brand Kit

Store brand assets for consistent output; available from the Pro plan upward.

05

Ads library and publishing

Agency and Max add a winning video-ads library, competitor tracking, and publishing and scheduling to social platforms.

06

MCP and API access

Connected apps and API keys can spend up to 5,000 credits per day, resetting at 00:00 UTC.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Commercial rights are included on every plan, so the $20/mo yearly Starter tier carries no extra licensing step for ad use.
  • One credit pool covers several third-party video models, so you can try different looks without separate subscriptions.
  • Voiceover, music, scripts and agent chat do not use credits, which stretches the allowance toward actual video generation.
  • Credit top-up packs ($10 per 1,000, $25 per 2,750, $50 per 6,000) do not expire, giving a way to cover a busy month without upgrading.
  • Agency tier adds ads-library research, competitor tracking and scheduled publishing, which suits teams producing for several clients.

Cons

  • The pricing page lists no free plan or trial, so you pay before testing output quality; refunds are limited to 3 days and 25% or less of credits used.
  • Credits are consumed per generation by model and length, and premium models cost far more than basic ones, so real output per month varies widely from the headline credit number.
  • Credit packs require an active plan, so they are a top-up, not a pay-as-you-go alternative.
  • Annual discount means the cheap headline prices ($20, $41, $83) require a yearly commitment; monthly is $29, $59, $119.
  • Max pricing above 30,000 credits is not published and needs a sales conversation.
  • Plan details changed from earlier tier names (Indie/Studio/Agency); the pricing page does not state avatar counts or voice-clone limits, so confirm those before buying if they matter.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Synthesys cost?

Yearly billing: Starter $20/mo, Pro $41/mo, Agency $83/mo, Max from $139/mo. Monthly billing: $29, $59, $119 and from $199. Credits per month are 3,500, 8,000, 17,000 and 30,000 to 250,000+.

Is there a free plan?

The pricing page does not mention a free plan or trial. Refunds are available within 3 days of purchase if you used no more than 25% of the plan's credits.

Do I get commercial rights?

Yes, the site states full commercial rights are included on every plan.

What happens if I run out of credits?

You can buy credit packs (1,000 for $10, 2,750 for $25, 6,000 for $50) with an active plan; packs do not expire.

Which video models does it use?

The homepage names Sora 2, Google Veo 3.1, Wan and Kling. Which model is used affects both quality and how many credits a clip costs.
