simpleshow is a good fit for training, HR and internal-comms teams that need to turn scripts or documents into short animated explainer videos without editing skills; skip it if you want cinematic, social-first or fully custom video, or need public per-plan prices. Its homepage says it is now part of the D-ID family. You supply a script and the platform adds animation, visuals and timing automatically, in its own illustration styles or your own, with AI avatar videos and premium formats using real footage, existing videos or interactive components. Enterprise buyers get brand customization, a creative-services team for custom projects, onboarding and a dedicated customer success manager, and the site states ISO 27001, TISAX and SOC 2 compliance. Pricing pages failed to load today, so plan names, limits and prices are unverified.

Best for

Best for corporate learning, HR, compliance and communications teams and educators who regularly need short, consistent explainer videos from existing text. Not for creators wanting cinematic or highly stylized video, social-media editors who want timeline control, or buyers who need transparent prices before signing up.

Key features

01

Content-to-script extraction

Users can paste long-form text or upload a document, and the platform automatically condenses it into a short, structured video script rather than requiring the user to write one from scratch.

02

Automated illustration matching

The system analyzes the script and selects matching illustrations, icons, and animated scenes automatically, replacing manual timeline and asset placement.

03

AI avatar presenters with voice cloning

Videos can include an AI avatar narrator, and users can record their voice once to have it reused (voice imitation) across future videos in 20+ languages.

04

Slides-to-video conversion

Existing PowerPoint decks can be converted into a narrated video with an AI avatar and layout that mirrors the original slides.

05

Corporate branding automation

Company colors, fonts, logos, and backgrounds can be applied automatically across generated videos to keep output on-brand without manual styling per video.

06

Engagement analytics and interactive quizzes

Published videos can include AI-generated quiz questions and provide analytics on views and quiz performance, aimed at training and internal-comms use cases.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Automates animation, visual selection and timing from a script, so non-designers can finish a video quickly.
  • Enterprise-oriented: brand customization, creative services, onboarding and stated ISO 27001, TISAX and SOC 2 compliance.
  • Offers AI avatar videos and interactive or real-footage formats alongside classic illustrated explainers.
  • Separate paths for SMBs, enterprise and education suit different buyers.

Cons

  • Plan prices and limits were not retrievable from the site today, so cost comparison is not possible from here.
  • Illustration-led output suits explainers and training; it is not built for cinematic or social-first video.
  • Free-tier terms, watermark rules and commercial-use rights could not be verified; confirm before publishing anything externally.
  • Newer avatar and interactive formats are tied to the D-ID relationship and may change; check which are live on your plan.
