MailMaestro is worth considering for people who live in Outlook or Gmail and send plenty of routine business email; it is a weaker buy if Gemini or Copilot already covers your needs, or if you want sales-reply coaching. It adds AI to your mailbox to draft new emails, write replies, tidy rough drafts and summarize long threads, with agents that can label, draft and follow up.

It is the continuation of Flowrite: Maestro Labs acquired Flowrite's AI email-writing business, and the Flowrite team now focuses on a separate LLM evaluation product, Flow AI. On Maestro Labs' site MailMaestro is listed for Outlook and Gmail; earlier claims on this page that it also runs in Teams and Word for email could not be verified, and the separate TeamsMaestro product covers meetings (notes and transcripts). Maestro Labs' own pricing page was readable, but mailmaestro.com blocked our fetch, so details there were not checked.

Best for

Individuals and teams handling steady general business email in Outlook or Gmail who want drafting and summaries without leaving the inbox. Not for sales teams wanting reply-rate or deliverability coaching, not for people with light email volume who are already covered by Gemini or Copilot, and not for anyone who needs a Teams or Word writing assistant, which we could not verify.

Write, reply and improve

Drafts emails from a short prompt, produces replies from the thread you are reading, and edits existing drafts.

02

Condenses long emails and threads into short summaries.

AI agents

Per the pricing page, agents can label mail, draft responses and follow up.

04

TeamsMaestro and MaestroDuo

A separate meeting-notes product ($17/seat/month annual) for Teams, Meet and Zoom calls, bundled with MailMaestro as MaestroDuo.

Enterprise controls

Enterprise adds SSO, custom data retention and dedicated support, for 100+ seats.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Works within the mailbox, removing the copy-paste step required with a general chatbot.
  • Drafting, replying and summarizing in one tool.
  • Trial includes all features, so you can test the agents before paying.
  • Annual per-seat price is lower than many team AI add-ons and the MaestroDuo bundle discounts adding meeting notes.
  • Enterprise tier lists recognised compliance credentials.

Cons

  • Free-plan limits and monthly pricing are not published clearly, making total cost hard to predict.
  • Annual plans are not refundable after purchase, per the pricing page.
  • Overlaps with Gmail's Gemini and Outlook's Copilot, so the extra value is mainly cross-client convenience and agents.
  • No outbound-sales coaching or deliverability scoring.
  • Supported apps verified only for Outlook and Gmail.

Frequently asked questions

Is MailMaestro the same as Flowrite?

Effectively yes for email writing: Maestro Labs acquired Flowrite's AI email-writing business and directs Flowrite users to MailMaestro. Flowrite's team now works on a separate product, Flow AI.

How much does MailMaestro cost?

$12/seat/month billed annually ($144/seat/year), after a 14-day trial. The monthly price is not stated directly; the page says annual saves 20%. Enterprise is quoted.

Is there a free plan?

Yes, the pricing page lists a $0 plan after the trial but gives no limits. Earlier figures like 3 requests a week could not be verified.

Which apps does it work in?

Outlook (via Microsoft Marketplace) and Gmail (via Chrome Web Store) are confirmed. Teams and Word support for email writing could not be verified.

Should I pay for it if I have Copilot or Gemini?

Only if you want its agents, summaries and consistency across both Outlook and Gmail. If the built-in AI already meets your needs, skip it.
