Verbatik suits creators and small agencies who want one workspace and one credit pool for voiceovers, short video, music and captions; skip it if your whole job is top-quality voice work or you need predictable, published pricing. It bundles text-to-speech, voice cloning, AI avatar and video generation, music and sound effects, image generation and auto-captions, plus apps and an API.

Pricing is the thing to read carefully. As of today the plans are Free (no generation credits), Standard at $20/month for 200 credits and Pro at $59/month with several credit tiers, with 30% off for the first 12 months under a Black Friday code until November 30, 2026. Credits are spent differently by each model and setting, so the pricing page cannot tell you how far a plan will go for your mix of work. Earlier listings of Verbatik (a $1 trial pass, $39 Pro, $330 Business) no longer match the live site. The homepage also states voice and language counts inconsistently, so verify the voices and languages you need by testing them.

Best for

Individual creators, YouTubers and small marketing or agency teams who want to replace several AI subscriptions with one. Not for buyers who need the single best voice-cloning fidelity, high-volume enterprise dubbing with published pricing, or anyone who wants a generous free plan (it has zero generation credits).

Key features

01

Text-to-speech

Converts scripts to speech in a workspace editor with MP3, WAV and FLAC export. Language and voice-count claims vary across the homepage (150+ to 161 languages).

02

Voice cloning

Builds a custom voice from an uploaded audio sample; included in Standard and Pro per the pricing page, and on the API at $3 per clone training plus $0.10 per 1,000 characters.

03

AI avatar and video

Generates avatar-led, UGC-style and cinematic video; Standard includes all image, poster and video models.

04

Music and sound effects

Generates music tracks and sound effects for use as background audio.

05

Image generation

Image and poster models; Pro adds 4K models, brand kits and custom styles.

06

Captions and transcription

Auto-generated captions and transcription, aimed at social video.

07

Apps, API and agency tools

Desktop and mobile apps, a voice API, MCP server, team seats and white-label exports.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Consolidates voice, video, music, image and captions, so one login and one bill can replace several tools.
  • Standard at $20/month list includes voice cloning, all video and image models and music, which is a low price for the breadth.
  • Stated 14-day money-back guarantee and cancel-anytime terms reduce risk of trying a paid plan.
  • API publishes concrete prices for voice cloning ($3 per clone, $0.10 per 1,000 characters), unlike many rivals.
  • Pro adds brand kits, 4K models and unlimited seats, which suits agencies.

Cons

  • Free plan has 0 generation credits, so it is an exploration tier, not a usable free tier.
  • The credit allowance on Standard (200/month) is modest, and generation cost varies by model and settings, so heavy video use will need credit packs.
  • Pro price does not map clearly to its four credit tiers on the page.
  • Black Friday discount (30% off for 12 months) ends November 30, 2026; budget on list prices of $20 and $59.
  • Voice and language counts differ across homepage sections (200+ vs 1,500+ voices; 150+ vs 161 languages), so confirm your language before committing.
  • Third-party review scores were not verified today, so no rating is shown.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Verbatik cost?

Free is $0 with no generation credits. Standard is $20/month list for 200 credits; Pro is $59/month list with 750 to 4,315 credits depending on tier. Until Nov 30, 2026, code BFCM26 takes 30% off the first 12 months. Annual prices are not shown.

Is there a free trial?

No timed trial is stated. There is a free plan with no generation credits and a 14-day money-back guarantee on paid plans.

Does it do voice cloning?

Yes, voice cloning is listed under Standard and Pro, and the API charges $3 per clone plus $0.10 per 1,000 characters for cloned-voice TTS.

Is there an API?

Yes, with TTS, voice training and voice listing endpoints. Only the cloning rates are public on the homepage; other API pricing is not listed.

How many voices and languages are supported?

The site is inconsistent: 161 or 150+ languages, and voice counts from 200+ to 1,500+. Test the language and voice you need before paying.
