Polymer is a good fit for non-technical marketers and small business teams who want a dashboard from a spreadsheet or ad account in minutes, and a poor fit for anyone needing governed, multi-table enterprise BI or a clear price list. It reads uploaded files or connected sources, builds starter dashboards with AI, lets you chat with the data, and increasingly pitches an embedded analytics API with white-label options for software companies.

Treat its pricing with caution: the live pricing page displays several overlapping plan sets with different numbers, and includes leftover placeholder text, so the exact current price needs to be confirmed with the vendor.

Best for

Marketers, e-commerce operators and small teams without an analyst who want visual answers from spreadsheets and ad or store data, and product teams wanting lightweight embedded charts. It is not for enterprises needing governance, complex multi-table modeling or very large datasets, nor for buyers who need a firm, easily confirmed price.

Key features

01

Instant dashboards

Upload a spreadsheet or connect a source and Polymer proposes charts and a starter dashboard.

02

AI-generated insights and chat

AI generates dashboards and insights, and a chat feature answers questions about your data; chat responses are capped by plan.

03

Connectors

Google Sheets, Shopify, Facebook Ads, Google Analytics 4, Google Ads, Salesforce and file uploads, plus ETL tools and warehouses via the API.

04

Embedding and white-label

Embed charts and dashboards in your own app with custom branding and API-based permissions.

05

Custom metrics and templates

Higher plans add custom metrics, branding and a set number of custom templates.

06

Sync frequency by plan

Data refresh ranges from manual to automatic daily or hourly depending on plan.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Upload-to-dashboard is quick, and the AI picks sensible starting charts so you edit instead of build from scratch.
  • Marketing and store connectors (Google Ads, Facebook Ads, GA4, Shopify) suit performance reporting.
  • Unlimited viewers are listed on at least one plan version, so sharing results is cheap.
  • Embedding and white-labelling are available if you want analytics inside your own product.

Cons

  • The pricing page shows conflicting numbers and placeholder text, so you cannot trust any one price without confirming.
  • Lower tiers cap editors and AI chat responses, which limits heavy exploratory use.
  • Subscriptions are stated as non-refundable per the page FAQ.
  • Less capable than Tableau or Power BI for complex modeling, governance and relational data.
  • A free plan could not be confirmed on the current site; only a 7-day trial for API access was seen.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Polymer cost?

The pricing page shows conflicting sets: entry plans from about $10 to $50 per month and Teams at $250 per month, with yearly billing at half price. The homepage says API access starts at $500/month. Confirm before buying.

Is there a free plan?

We could not confirm one. The homepage offers a 7-day free trial; the pricing page mentions a 14-day trial on one Pro version.

Which data sources work?

CSV and Excel uploads, Google Sheets, Shopify, Facebook Ads, Google Analytics 4, Google Ads and Salesforce, with more via ETL tools or the API.

Can I embed dashboards in my app?

Yes, the homepage promotes embeddable charts, white-label branding and an API; customizable embeds and API access sit on higher or API plans.

Can I get a refund?

The pricing page FAQ says subscriptions are non-refundable and cancellations take effect at the end of the billing cycle.
