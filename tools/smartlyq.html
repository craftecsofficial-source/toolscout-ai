SmartlyQ is an all-in-one AI marketing platform aimed at solo marketers, content creators, ecommerce brands, coaches, and small agencies, combining copywriting, social media scheduling, AI video/image generation, chatbot building, a CRM, funnel/landing-page tools, and an SEO article generator into one dashboard. Its core pitch is consolidation: the company markets itself directly against a typical stack of point tools (naming Jasper, Canva, Buffer, Chatbase, CapCut, Zapier, ChatGPT Plus, and freelance designers), claiming to replace roughly €843/month in separate subscriptions with a single €97-497/month plan. The platform centers on 'AI Captain,' billed as an autonomous marketing operator that can plan and execute tasks (content, scheduling, chatbot responses) across the connected tools, alongside custom AI assistants that can be trained on a business's own knowledge for specific roles like legal review, funnel copy, or video editing.

SmartlyQ is a newer entrant (a Product Hunt-featured launch) competing against both all-in-one platforms like GoHighLevel and HubSpot and single-purpose tools it explicitly compares itself to on its own site (vs. Jasper, Hootsuite, Mailchimp, Semrush, Kajabi, Opus Clip, Submagic, and others). Its differentiator is breadth at a mid-market price point — 40+ integrations (Meta, Google Ads, Shopify, WooCommerce, Stripe, WordPress, Slack, Zapier, TikTok, Discord) and whitelabel/SaaS-mode reselling on its top tier — rather than depth in any single category compared to specialists.

Best for

SmartlyQ is a reasonable fit for solo marketers, ecommerce brands, coaches/consultants, and small agencies currently paying for several separate tools (a copywriter, a scheduler, a chatbot builder, a video editor) who want to consolidate into one platform and one bill, and who are comfortable with a mid-market monthly price (€97+) rather than free or ultra-cheap point tools. It's a weaker fit for larger, established marketing teams that need deep, best-in-class functionality in any single category, or for buyers who require independently verified reviews and a long track record before committing — SmartlyQ does not yet have that on major review platforms.

Built-in CRM to capture and nurture leads, paired with a drag-and-drop funnel and landing-page builder for campaigns without a separate tool.

Website chatbots that answer visitor questions and capture leads, with a unified inbox for messages and comments and scheduled performance reports; higher tiers support multiple chatbots.

SEO Article Generator

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Genuinely broad feature consolidation — content, social scheduling, chatbots, CRM, funnels, and video editing are all in one dashboard rather than requiring 6-8 separate tools and logins
  • 40+ pre-built integrations (Meta, Google Ads, Shopify, WooCommerce, Stripe, PayPal, WordPress, Google Analytics, Slack, Zapier, Canva, TikTok, Discord) cover most of a small business's existing stack
  • The Enterprise tier's whitelabel/SaaS mode with client rebilling is a real differentiator for agencies wanting to resell a platform under their own brand
  • A 30-day money-back guarantee reduces the risk of switching from an existing tool stack to test it properly
  • AI Captain and role-based custom assistants (legal, funnel, video, chatbot) go beyond simple content generation toward actual task execution

Cons

  • No listings found on G2, Capterra, or Trustpilot — every testimonial and results claim (follower growth, revenue increases) currently comes only from SmartlyQ's own marketing pages, not an independent review platform
  • Entry price (€97/month for Pro) is a real commitment relative to many of the single-purpose tools it replaces, and the value proposition depends on actually using most of the bundled features
  • AI credit limits (1,000-2,500/month depending on tier) apply across all AI-generation activities (content, video, images), so heavy video or image users may burn through the allotment faster than expected
  • The company's own 'vs' comparison pages (e.g., vs. GoHighLevel, vs. HubSpot) invite direct scrutiny against much more established, deeply reviewed competitors
  • Similar naming to the unrelated, much larger enterprise ad-platform 'Smartly.io' can create confusion during independent research

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free trial?

SmartlyQ advertises 'Start free' access on its homepage and backs paid plans with a 30-day money-back guarantee rather than a traditional free trial; check the signup flow for current terms.

What's included in the entry-level Pro plan?

1,000 AI credits/month, the AI Captain autonomous operator, social scheduling for 10 accounts, a funnel/landing page builder, built-in CRM, AI video/presentation generation, and a website AI chatbot — starting at €97/month.

How do AI credits work?

Credits are consumed across AI-generation activities (content, images, video); allowances scale by plan from 1,000/month (Pro) to 2,500/month (Enterprise), with add-on credit packages available.

Can agencies resell SmartlyQ?

Yes, on the Enterprise plan via 'SaaS Mode,' which allows full whitelabeling under the agency's own domain/branding with client rebilling to set their own margins.

What integrations does SmartlyQ support?

40+ integrations including Meta, Google Ads, Shopify, WooCommerce, Stripe, PayPal, WordPress, Google Analytics, Slack, Zapier, Canva, Zoom, Discord, and TikTok.

Is SmartlyQ the same as Smartly.io?

No — SmartlyQ (smartlyq.com) is a separate, newer all-in-one marketing platform for SMBs and agencies; Smartly.io is an unrelated, larger enterprise advertising-creative and media-buying platform.
