SmartlyQ suits solo marketers, coaches and small agencies who want one dashboard for posts, chatbots, video clips, funnels and a basic CRM and are fine paying €97 or more a month for breadth. Skip it if you need best-in-class depth in one area, such as serious SEO research or advanced email automation, or if you want a cheap single-purpose tool. It bundles an AI agent called AI Captain that drafts posts, campaigns and landing pages for you to approve, plus social scheduling, website chatbots, short-form video tools, a funnel builder, a CRM and an article generator.

Higher tiers add whitelabel options for agencies, with SaaS mode and client rebilling at Enterprise. Everything draws on a shared monthly AI credit pool, so heavy generation can run into limits, and top-up packs are sold separately. The company is a newer entrant and I found no independent review listings to check its claims against.

Best for

SmartlyQ fits solo marketers, creators, ecommerce owners, coaches and small agencies who currently pay for several separate tools and want a single bill. It is NOT for large teams needing specialist depth in one category, buyers who want a low-cost tool for a single job, or anyone who requires an established track record and independent reviews before committing.

Key features

01

AI Captain

An autonomous marketing operator that plans and executes tasks across SmartlyQ's tools — content, scheduling, chatbot responses — rather than requiring the user to manually run each tool.

02

Custom AI Assistants

Users can build unlimited assistants trained on their own business knowledge for specific roles: legal/compliance advisor, funnel specialist, content creator, video editor, or chatbot specialist.

03

Social Scheduling & Autopilot

Schedules and auto-publishes posts across multiple social platforms with queues, bulk scheduling, and labels; higher tiers unlock more connected accounts and unlimited posts.

04

AI Video Generation & Editing

Generates and edits short-form video content, including viral-style clips optimized for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts, with a monthly AI-video quota by plan.

05

CRM & Funnel Builder

Built-in CRM to capture and nurture leads, paired with a drag-and-drop funnel and landing-page builder for campaigns without a separate tool.

06

AI Chatbots

Website chatbots that answer visitor questions and capture leads, with a unified inbox for messages and comments and scheduled performance reports; higher tiers support multiple chatbots.

07

SEO Article Generator

Generates SEO-oriented blog articles as part of the content suite, paired with ads analytics on the Business tier and above.

08

Whitelabel / SaaS Mode

The Enterprise tier lets agencies resell SmartlyQ under their own domain and branding, with client rebilling so agencies can set their own margins on usage.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Replaces several subscriptions (scheduler, chatbot, video clipper, funnel builder, CRM) with one login and one bill.
  • Entry plan already includes the AI Captain agent, CRM, funnels, chatbot and 10 social accounts.
  • Agency features (whitelabel, client workspaces, rebilling) are built in at the higher tiers.
  • Monthly billing with no contract, plus a stated 30-day refund on the homepage.

Cons

  • Starts at €97/month, well above many single-purpose tools; the €297 and €497 jumps are steep.
  • AI credits are pooled and capped (1,000 to 2,500 per month); top-up packs cost extra and their prices are not listed.
  • Pricing pages disagree on terms: the homepage mentions the 30-day refund and a single €97 plan, the pricing page shows a 14-day trial and does not mention the refund. Confirm before paying.
  • Annual prices are not listed, only a 20% saving.
  • Breadth over depth: each module is likely lighter than a specialist, and no independent reviews (G2, Capterra, Trustpilot) were found.

Frequently asked questions

How much does SmartlyQ cost?

Pro is €97/month, Business €297/month, Enterprise €497/month, plus a custom quote tier. Yearly billing saves 20%, though exact annual prices are not shown.

Is there a free trial?

The pricing page lists a 14-day trial on the three paid plans, and the homepage mentions a 30-day money-back guarantee. The two pages differ, so check the signup terms.

What limits should I watch?

Monthly AI credits (1,000, 1,500, 2,500), connected social accounts (10, 25, unlimited), chatbot count and AI video quotas by tier.

Is it a replacement for dedicated SEO tools?

No. It has an article generator from Business upward, but it is not a keyword research or rank-tracking suite.

Can agencies resell it?

Whitelabel starts at Business; SaaS Mode with client rebilling is on Enterprise.
