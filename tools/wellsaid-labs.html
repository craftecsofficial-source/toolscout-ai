WellSaid Labs is a Seattle-based AI voice-generation company, originally spun out of the Allen Institute for AI, focused on realistic, professional-grade English voiceovers for corporate training, internal communications, marketing and product video. Its flagship voice model, Caruso, is included on every paid tier at no extra cost, and the company leans heavily toward enterprise learning-and-development and marketing buyers.

Where Murf and ElevenLabs push breadth (many languages, many use cases including agents, dubbing and sound effects), WellSaid concentrates on a narrower set of highly polished English voices with fine-grained tone, pitch and emotional controls, billed on unlimited generation plus metered download minutes rather than per-character credits. It also emphasizes enterprise trust signals — SOC 2 Type II certification, a no-training-on-customer-data policy — more heavily than Murf or ElevenLabs' self-serve tiers, and has recently shifted from opaque 'contact us' pricing to transparent public list pricing.

Best for

WellSaid Labs is best suited for corporate L&D, internal communications and marketing teams that need polished, consistent English-language voiceovers integrated into existing Adobe-based video workflows, and that value enterprise security guarantees over raw feature breadth. Teams needing strong multilingual or dubbing support, custom voice cloning, or the lowest possible entry price should look at ElevenLabs or Murf AI instead.

Fine-tune delivery per clip, including AI-driven phonetic suggestions and pronunciation customization for accurate US/UK pronunciation.

Adobe integrations

Adobe Express integration on Pro and above, plus Adobe Premiere Pro integration on Business and Enterprise.

05

Team workspace & collaboration

Shared team drives, commenting, shared pronunciation libraries and access control features on Business and Enterprise plans.

06

Caption file export

Auto-generated .SRT and .VTT caption files bundled with audio clips on all paid plans.

07

API access

Programmatic access for embedding WellSaid voices into other products, with a free API trial key available.

08

Enterprise language & translation support

Additional non-English languages plus clip translation, available exclusively on the Enterprise plan.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Consistently rated among the most natural, broadcast-ready English voices in independent and Reddit comparisons, especially for corporate training
  • Unlimited generation and retakes on every paid plan mean users aren't penalized for iterating
  • Caruso, the company's top-tier voice model, is included on all plans at no upcharge
  • Strong enterprise trust posture: SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR-compliant, customer data never used to train models
  • Adobe Premiere Pro and Express integrations plug directly into existing video-production workflows
  • Now publishes transparent list pricing instead of requiring a sales call for every tier, a recent and notable change

Cons

  • Historically priced and marketed toward enterprise buyers, and the Business tier ($160/mo per user, capped at 5 seats) remains expensive relative to Murf or ElevenLabs
  • Language coverage is comparatively narrow — non-English voices, translation and dubbing are available only on the Enterprise plan
  • Some users report occasional mispronunciations and limited ability to adjust reading speed
  • Lower tiers (Starter, Pro) are capped at MP3 export only; higher-quality formats and sample rates require Business or Enterprise
  • Download-minute metering, rather than flat unlimited downloads, means heavy users on Starter or Pro can still hit caps
  • Capterra review volume is quite small, making that aggregate score less statistically reliable than G2's larger sample

What reviewers say

WellSaid Labs scores well on G2 with a reasonably sized review base, but its Capterra footprint is thin, where sub-scores skew lower on Value for Money than on Ease of Use. Reddit and independent reviews consistently rank its English voice quality among the best available, calling it 'worth every penny' for business-focused English voiceover work, but repeatedly flag that it's priced and built for larger organizations rather than individual creators, with weaker multilingual support than ElevenLabs or Murf.

Frequently asked questions

Is WellSaid Labs pricing public now, or do you have to contact sales?

As of its latest pricing update, WellSaid Labs publishes transparent list pricing for Starter, Pro and Business plans starting at $10/mo (billed annually); only the Enterprise tier requires contacting sales for a custom quote.

Does WellSaid Labs have a free plan?

Yes, a free trial plan with no credit card required, offering 3 download minutes per month, 3 projects and 10 minutes of generation, though it does not include commercial usage rights.

How much does WellSaid Labs cost?

Individual plans run from $10/mo (Starter, billed annually; $19/mo monthly) to $33/mo (Pro, billed annually; $49/mo monthly); Business is $160/mo per user (billed annually, up to 5 seats); Enterprise is custom-priced.

Does WellSaid Labs support languages other than English?

Non-English languages plus translation are available only on the Enterprise plan — Starter, Pro and Business are English-only.

How does WellSaid Labs compare to Murf AI and ElevenLabs?

WellSaid focuses on a smaller set of highly polished English voices with unlimited generation and strong enterprise security, while Murf and ElevenLabs offer far broader language coverage, lower entry prices, and (in ElevenLabs' case) self-serve voice cloning and API-first tooling.

Is WellSaid Labs worth it?

For teams needing consistent, professional English voiceovers for training or internal communications with Adobe workflow integration, most reviewers say yes; for multilingual needs, custom voice cloning, or tight budgets, ElevenLabs or Murf are generally considered better fits.
