WellSaid is a good choice for teams producing English narration for training, internal video and marketing who want to iterate freely and keep commercial rights. Skip it if you need many languages without an Enterprise contract, or the lowest possible per-minute cost for high volume. The product is a web studio where you paste a script, pick a voice, adjust tone and pitch, and download audio. The company has moved from wellsaidlabs.com to wellsaid.io; the homepage says its voices come from professional voice actors and lists 280+ voice actors. Generation is unlimited on paid plans, but downloads are metered by the minute.

Best for

Who it is for: L&D, internal communications and marketing teams making English voiceover who want licensed voices, unlimited retakes and, on higher tiers, shared workspaces. Who it is not for: multilingual publishers without an Enterprise budget, hobbyists who need a free tier with commercial rights, and high-volume producers who need unmetered downloads.

Key features

01

Studio

Script-to-voice editor. Paid plans allow unlimited generation; the trial caps generation at 10 minutes.

02

Voice controls

Tone, pitch and emotional controls are included from the trial tier up.

03

Caption files

Starter and above include .SRT and .VTT caption files.

04

Adobe integrations

Adobe Express on Pro and above; Premiere Pro on Business and Enterprise.

05

Collaboration

Team workspace, commenting, shared pronunciation library, favorite voices and access controls on Business and above.

06

API

Available for automation; the pricing page gives no API pricing, so ask before budgeting for it.

07

Enterprise controls

Languages and translation, up to 96 kHz export, SSO, SOC 2 reports, custom terms and a customer success manager (for more than 3 licenses).

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Unlimited generation and retakes on paid plans, so iterating does not eat your allowance
  • Commercial rights start at Starter ($10/mo annual)
  • Transparent pricing for all but Enterprise
  • Team features such as shared pronunciation libraries and Premiere Pro integration
  • Minutes bought in Studio can top up an overrun

Cons

  • Business at $160 per user per month and a 5-seat cap is expensive for small teams
  • Starter's 20 download minutes a month is tight for regular producers
  • Unused download minutes do not roll over
  • Languages beyond English and translation need Enterprise; the homepage advertises 50+ languages, but we could not verify which plan unlocks them
  • Pricing page is inconsistent on Pro export formats; we did not resolve it
  • Homepage names no certifications; SOC 2 reports are listed under Enterprise only

Frequently asked questions

Is WellSaid Labs the same as WellSaid?

Yes. wellsaidlabs.com now redirects to wellsaid.io and the company calls itself WellSaid.

How much does it cost?

Starter is $10/mo billed annually or $19 monthly; Pro is $33/mo annually or $49 monthly; Business is $160 per user per month billed annually; Enterprise is custom.

Is there a free plan?

A free trial with 3 download minutes a month and 10 minutes of generation, but no commercial rights.

Does it support languages other than English?

Starter, Pro and Business list English voices only. All languages and translation are on Enterprise.

What happens if I run out of download minutes?

You can upgrade or buy add-on minutes in Studio; unused minutes do not roll over.
