ChatGPT is OpenAI's consumer and business AI assistant, built on the GPT model family (GPT-5.5 and GPT-5.6 as of mid-2026). It is the AI chatbot with the largest install base by a wide margin, credited with popularizing the category in late 2022, and OpenAI has since expanded it from a text chatbot into a broader assistant with voice, image generation, coding and third-party app integrations.

Its core differentiator is breadth: ChatGPT bundles chat, image generation, voice conversation with live video, a coding agent (Codex) and a marketplace of Custom GPTs into one product, whereas Claude focuses on writing/coding quality, Perplexity focuses on cited search, and Gemini focuses on Google-ecosystem integration. That breadth comes with the most complex pricing ladder of the four tools.

Best for

ChatGPT is best for individuals and teams who want one general-purpose assistant covering chat, image generation, voice, light coding and research without switching apps, and for anyone already invested in the Custom GPT ecosystem. Teams whose top priority is the highest-quality long-form writing, the most reliable coding agent, or the most current citation-heavy factual answers should evaluate Claude or Perplexity instead.

Key features

01

GPT-5.6 model family with adaptive reasoning

Plus and Pro users can access multiple model variants that trade off speed against depth of reasoning depending on the task.

02

Deep Research

An autonomous agent that runs multi-step web research and returns a cited report; available in limited form on Free/Go and expanded on Plus/Pro.

03

Custom GPTs and Projects

Users can build and share configured chatbot personas and organize files and conversations into persistent Projects for ongoing work.

04

Advanced Voice with video

Real-time spoken conversation that can also see and discuss what's in front of a phone camera or shared screen.

05

Native image generation

Text-to-image built on OpenAI's image model, with an optional 'thinking' mode on paid tiers for more accurate compositions.

06

Data analysis and file tools

Users can upload spreadsheets, PDFs and other files and have ChatGPT run Python-backed analysis and build interactive tables and charts.

07

Codex coding agent

An integrated agent for software development tasks, with usage expanded on Plus and maximized on Pro, competing directly with Claude Code.

08

Scheduled tasks and connected apps

Recurring automated tasks plus integrations with apps like Canva, Spotify and PowerPoint.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Broadest single-app feature set: chat, image generation, live voice/video, a coding agent and a marketplace of custom GPTs
  • Largest install base of any AI assistant means most third-party integrations and browser extensions support ChatGPT/GPT models first
  • Highest G2 rating among these four tools with the largest review volume, reflecting broad familiarity and ease of use
  • Free tier is genuinely usable for casual, everyday queries rather than being a stripped-down demo
  • Codex and Advanced Voice with video differentiate it from chat-only competitors
  • Frequent, fast model updates keep the free and Plus tiers current

Cons

  • Pricing is the most convoluted of the four tools, with six overlapping tiers and model names that change often enough to confuse buyers
  • Reddit's 2026 consensus is that its long-form writing 'sounds like ChatGPT' and needs more editing than Claude's output
  • Message and usage caps on Plus have historically been a recurring complaint, especially during high-demand periods
  • Capterra reviewers note answers can be generic or miss small details compared to more research-focused tools like Perplexity
  • Free plan's image generation and deep research are heavily throttled compared to paid tiers
  • SSO and admin controls are gated behind the $200/mo Pro or Enterprise tiers, pricier than competitors' equivalents

What reviewers say

ChatGPT is the highest-rated of the four tools on G2, scoring especially well on ease of use but comparatively weaker on quality of support. Capterra reviewers are positive overall but repeatedly flag answers as occasionally too general, inconsistent memory across sessions, and factual errors that require verification. Reddit sentiment in 2026 consistently ranks ChatGPT ahead on image generation, voice and ecosystem breadth, but behind Claude on writing polish and instruction-following for long documents.

Frequently asked questions

Is ChatGPT free to use?

Yes, the Free plan gives everyone limited access to GPT-5.5 Instant, image generation and deep research with no credit card required. Paid plans (Go at $8/mo, Plus at $20/mo, Pro at $200/mo) remove most limits.

What's the difference between ChatGPT Plus and Pro?

Plus ($20/mo) gives expanded access to GPT-5.6 reasoning models, projects and custom GPTs. Pro ($200/mo) removes most usage caps entirely, adds a more capable model, and maximizes Codex and deep research usage.

Is ChatGPT better than Claude?

It depends on the task. Reddit and benchmark data in 2026 generally favor Claude for coding accuracy and writing that needs less editing, while ChatGPT leads on image generation, voice features and overall ecosystem breadth.

Can ChatGPT search the web like Perplexity?

Yes, ChatGPT has built-in search and a Deep Research mode that cites sources, but Perplexity is purpose-built around live search with inline citations as its primary interface, which reviewers describe as more transparent.

Is ChatGPT Plus worth it?

For anyone using it daily for work, coding or research, most reviewers say yes: it removes the Free plan's tightest limits and adds custom GPTs, projects and expanded Codex access. Casual users can generally stay on Free or the cheaper Go plan.

Does ChatGPT have a mobile app?

Yes, ChatGPT is available on iOS and Android with feature parity to the web version including voice mode.
