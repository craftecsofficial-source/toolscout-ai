ChatGPT is a good default for people who want a single assistant that does a bit of everything: chat, images, voice, research and coding help. If you mainly care about the most polished long-form writing or need heavy daily usage without hitting caps, look at the alternatives or budget for the expensive Pro tier.

OpenAI's app combines a chat assistant with image creation, voice mode, deep research, memory, custom GPTs and projects, and the Codex coding agent. Free users get unlimited text chats under abuse guardrails but only limited uploads, images, voice and research. Paid tiers raise those limits step by step, and the official pricing page keeps usage limits deliberately loose ('limits apply'), so exact message counts are not published there.

Best for

Good for individuals and small teams who want one general-purpose assistant for everyday work and are willing to pay $20/mo (Plus) for higher limits. Not for people who need guaranteed, published usage quotas, those who object to ads on the cheapest paid plan, or anyone unwilling to opt out of model training on personal plans (Free, Go, Plus and Pro content may be used for training unless you opt out).

Key features

01

Advanced reasoning models

Plus and above add reasoning models for harder problems; Pro gets larger context windows (128K instant, 400K reasoning) versus 27K on Free.

02

Deep research

Runs multi-step web research and returns a report; limited on Free and Go, expanded on Plus and maximal on Pro.

03

Custom GPTs, Projects and scheduled tasks

Build configured assistants, group files and chats into projects, and schedule recurring tasks (Plus and above).

04

Voice and image creation

Spoken conversation and image generation on every plan, with limits on Free and faster, unlimited image creation on Pro.

05

Memory

Remembers details across chats; longer on Go, expanded on Plus and maximal on Pro.

06

Codex coding agent

An agent for software tasks; limited on Free, expanded on Plus, with longer sessions on Pro.

07

ChatGPT Work

Agent-style work feature that is desktop-only on Free and extended to web and mobile on Plus.

08

Mobile and desktop apps

Apps are available for phones and desktop alongside the web version.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Broadest feature set in one app: chat, images, voice, deep research, memory, projects and a coding agent
  • Free plan allows unlimited text chats (under abuse guardrails), so you can test it properly before paying
  • Pro offers three usage tiers, so heavy users can scale up instead of jumping straight to one fixed price
  • Big library of custom GPTs and app integrations
  • Education discounts exist: nonprofits can get up to 75% off Business or Enterprise, and verified US K-12 teachers get a free plan through June 2027

Cons

  • Official pricing page gives no numeric message limits, only 'limits apply' and 'expanded', so you cannot predict when you will hit a cap
  • Go plan may show ads, and its context window (54K instant) is still modest
  • Free, Go, Plus and Pro content is used for model training unless you opt out
  • Pro starts at $200/mo, a large jump from Plus at $20/mo
  • Image creation, voice, research and Codex are all throttled on Free

Frequently asked questions

Is ChatGPT free?

Yes. The Free plan offers unlimited text chats subject to abuse guardrails, with limited uploads, image creation, voice, deep research, memory and Codex. No credit card is needed.

How much does ChatGPT Plus cost and what do you get?

Plus is $20/month. It adds advanced reasoning models, expanded uploads, deep research and memory, projects, scheduled tasks, custom GPTs and expanded Codex. OpenAI says 'limits apply'.

What is the difference between Plus and Pro?

Pro starts at $200/month and offers three usage tiers, longer Codex and ChatGPT Work sessions, unlimited faster image creation, maximum deep research and memory, and larger context windows (128K instant, 400K reasoning).

Does ChatGPT train on my data?

On Free, Go, Plus and Pro, your content is used to improve models unless you opt out in settings. Check OpenAI's current business terms for Business and Enterprise.

Is ChatGPT or Claude better?

ChatGPT has more built-in features (images, voice, custom GPTs). Claude is often preferred for writing and coding workflows. Try both free tiers on your own tasks.
