Julius AI is a good choice for individuals and small teams who have spreadsheets or data files and want answers and charts by asking questions in plain English. It is a poor choice if you need a governed, enterprise BI stack or live warehouse connections on a small budget, since the database connectors we saw are listed on the $450/mo Business plan. Julius started as a data-analysis chatbot and its site now presents it as a broader AI workspace: analysis and reports, but also presentations, websites, images, video and browser agents. Usage is measured in credits per plan, so how far a plan goes depends on how heavily you use it.

Best for

Who it is for: individuals and small teams who work in spreadsheets and want fast answers, charts and slides without coding. Who it is not for: organizations needing governed enterprise BI on a small budget, teams wanting predictable flat per-seat pricing, and anyone who needs live database connections but cannot afford the Business tier.

Key features

01

Plain-English data analysis

Upload spreadsheets and data files, ask questions in everyday language, and get analysis, tables and charts.

02

Charts and reports

Turns data into charts and reports without manual chart configuration.

03

Presentations

Builds slides with templates such as board updates and quarterly reviews; custom brand templates are listed.

04

Websites and media

Builds websites and generates images and video; the Free plan has limited access to these.

05

Browser and parallel agents

Agents that research and perform web tasks; unlimited parallel agents are listed on Pro.

06

Data connectors

Free lists Google Drive, OneDrive and SharePoint; Business adds Postgres, BigQuery and Snowflake.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • You can start with a spreadsheet and plain-English questions, with no SQL or Python needed.
  • A free plan lets you test it on your own data before paying.
  • Entry paid plans are inexpensive at $16-$20 a month on annual or monthly billing respectively.
  • One workspace produces charts, slides and sites from the same analysis.
  • Higher tiers add more memory (16 GB on Plus, 32 GB on Pro), which matters for larger files.

Cons

  • Credit-based usage makes cost harder to predict than a flat per-seat price.
  • Warehouse connectors (Postgres, BigQuery, Snowflake) appear only on Business at $450/mo.
  • The Free plan's daily credit amount is not stated, so we could not verify how much you get.
  • Collaboration with teammates is listed starting at Business; Plus and Pro read as single-user.
  • As with any AI analysis tool, results on messy or complex data should be spot-checked; we did not verify accuracy claims.

Frequently asked questions

Is Julius AI free?

There is a Free plan with a daily credit allowance and limited access to some tools. The exact daily amount was not stated on the pricing page, so check before relying on it.

How much does Julius AI cost?

Plus is $20/mo ($16/mo billed annually), Pro $45/mo ($37 annual), Business $450/mo ($375 annual). Enterprise is custom.

Can it connect to my database?

Postgres, BigQuery and Snowflake connectors are listed on the Business plan. Free lists Google Drive, OneDrive and SharePoint connectors.

Do I need to code?

No. Its site describes analyzing spreadsheets in plain English. We did not verify in detail whether generated code is shown in the current product.

Can my team share work in it?

Teammate invitations and collaboration are listed from the Business plan, with up to 50 team members.
