Napkin is a good fit if you write first and visualize second: memos, blog drafts and pitch text become diagrams and infographics in a couple of clicks. Skip it if you need pixel-level layout control, heavy brand governance on the free tier, or a complete slide-deck tool. It reads the text you select, proposes several visual layouts, and lets you edit the result element by element, then export to PNG, PDF, and (on paid plans) PPT or SVG. A Slides feature exists but is labeled beta.

Best for

Good for writers, consultants, founders and marketers who have prose and need visuals fast. Not for designers who need freeform canvas control, teams needing strict brand output without paying (free has Napkin branding), people who want a finished multi-slide presentation from a prompt, or anyone who needs to generate visuals on a phone.

Key features

01

Text to visuals

Select text and get generated diagrams, flowcharts, mind maps, infographics or data charts, with multiple layout options to choose from.

02

Editing

Change colors, fonts, layouts, icons and individual elements after generation; editing is unlimited on the free plan.

03

Import and export

Imports PPT, DOC, PDF, HTML and Markdown. Exports PNG and PDF free; PPT and SVG on paid plans.

04

Teamspace

Shared workspace with real-time editing, commenting and highlighting; paid plans bill per accepted member.

05

Brand styles

Plus includes 3 Brand Styles; Pro offers unlimited custom branding and font uploads.

06

Slides (beta) and API

A beta deck builder and an API for automating visual generation in your own workflow.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Pasting in an existing draft and picking a layout is quicker than building a diagram from a blank canvas.
  • Free tier is genuinely usable: unlimited editing, imports and PNG/PDF export, with a weekly credit allowance.
  • No prompt engineering is required, which lowers the chance of unusable output.
  • Paid plans start at $9/user/month, low for a tool that removes branding and unlocks PPT/SVG.

Cons

  • Credits are charged at about one per word selected, so long documents can exhaust the 500 weekly free credits fast; Napkin says this may change for complex outputs.
  • Free exports carry Napkin branding and PPT/SVG export is paywalled, which matters if you work in PowerPoint.
  • Visual generation is desktop-only for now.
  • Slides is beta; Napkin does not offer student or non-profit discounts per its pricing page.

Frequently asked questions

Can I remove the Napkin watermark?

Yes, on Plus ($9/user/month) and above. Free plan visuals carry Napkin branding.

Can I get visuals into PowerPoint?

PPT and SVG export are on paid plans; free exports PNG and PDF. The site also lists PowerPoint, Google Slides, Canva and Keynote as supported destinations.

How do credits work?

Roughly one credit per word selected for generation; Free gives 500 per week, Plus 10,000 and Pro 30,000 per month.

Does it work on mobile?

You can view and edit presentations on mobile, but generating visuals is desktop-only at present.
