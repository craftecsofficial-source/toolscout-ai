Text2Infographic is a good pick for bloggers and marketers who want single-image infographics fast and prefer paying once rather than subscribing; skip it if you need multi-slide decks, team collaboration, or tight pixel-level design control. You paste text, give it a blog URL, or upload a document or spreadsheet, and it researches the topic, places images, adds source citations and produces a designed infographic, which you can then adjust (colors, fonts, images, logos, content).

It can also produce structured layouts such as timelines, mind maps, Venn diagrams and SWOT/PESTLE analyses, and works in 20+ languages. The site does not list export formats, so check what you can download during the free trial.

Best for

Bloggers, social media managers and content marketers who regularly turn written content into shareable visuals without design skills, and business users who need a quick SWOT, PESTLE, timeline or mind map. It is not for people who need slide decks, teams that need shared workspaces and approval workflows, designers wanting full layout control, or anyone who needs a guaranteed long-term vendor.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • A pay-once pass at $70 is cheap for someone who needs infographics only occasionally, compared with monthly design subscriptions.
  • Built-in citations are rare among visual generators and make public-facing infographics easier to fact-check.
  • Beyond marketing graphics it covers SWOT, PESTLE, timeline and mind-map layouts useful for reports.
  • Starting from a blog URL or spreadsheet saves writing a prompt from scratch.

Cons

  • It is a small single-purpose product without Canva- or Visme-style template libraries, brand kits or team collaboration.
  • With only 2-3 free credits, and the site stating both numbers, it is hard to judge quality before paying.
  • Editing is limited to colors, fonts, images, logos and content; heavily branded work may need finishing elsewhere. Export formats are not listed on the site.
  • 'Unlimited' and 'lifetime' are not defined on the pricing page (no fair-use or service-life terms seen), and credits are not refunded.
  • Researched facts and images should be checked manually before client-facing use.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

You get a few free sign-up credits, then a one-time Lifetime Access pass listed at $70 (reduced from $120). Enterprise/API pricing is not public.

How many free credits do I get?

The site is inconsistent: the homepage says 3 and the FAQ says 2. The FAQ says credits do not expire.

Can I edit the infographic after it is generated?

Yes, per the FAQ you can change colors, fonts, images, logos and content. Depth of control compared with a full design tool is limited.

What can I turn into an infographic?

Pasted text, a blog URL, an uploaded document or a spreadsheet.

Is there an API or business plan?

An Enterprise option with API access is offered via 'contact us'; no price is published.
