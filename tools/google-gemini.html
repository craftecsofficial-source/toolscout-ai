Gemini is a strong pick if you already live in Google's apps or Android and want an assistant that sits inside them, and it is hard to beat on price for casual use because the Free plan is capable. Skip it if you want a tool independent of Google's ecosystem or if coding and writing quality are your top priority, where rivals are often preferred.

The assistant runs on Google's Gemini models and is reachable in its own app, in Google apps and on Android. The Free plan already includes Deep Research, image generation, Gemini Live voice, Canvas and Gems with lower limits. Paid Google AI plans raise usage limits (2x on Plus, 4x on Pro, then 5x and 20x Pro on Ultra) and bundle Google storage, so part of what you pay for is Google One. Limits are compute-based and reset every 5 hours up to a weekly cap.

Best for

Best for Gmail, Docs, Drive and Android users, students and families who like the storage and sharing bundled with Google AI plans, and anyone who wants a capable free assistant. Not for people who need predictable fixed usage quotas, who want a vendor-neutral tool outside Google, or whose main need is top-tier coding or long-form writing, where ChatGPT or Claude are often preferred.

Key features

01

Google app integration

Gemini works in Gmail, Docs, Drive and other Google apps, drawing on your own content when you allow it; some features depend on plan and region.

02

1-million-token context

Google lists a 1 million-token context window across its paid plans, enough for very long documents or codebases.

03

Deep Research

Multi-step web research that produces a longer report; included on Free with limits, and expanded on paid plans.

04

Image, video and music tools

Image generation and editing are on Free; paid plans add Flow credits for video and music (for example 200 on Plus, 1,000 on Pro, 10,000 or 25,000 on Ultra).

05

Gemini Live, Canvas and Gems

Voice conversations, a side workspace for documents and code, and custom assistants called Gems.

06

Bundled storage

AI Plus includes 400 GB, AI Pro 5 TB, and Ultra from 20 TB, shared across Gmail, Drive and Photos.

07

Developer tools

Pro and Ultra raise limits for Jules, AI Studio and Antigravity; Pro also includes $10/mo of Google Cloud credits.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The Free plan includes Deep Research, image generation, Live voice, Canvas and Gems
  • AI Plus at $4.99/mo is one of the cheapest paid assistant tiers and includes 400 GB of storage
  • Tight Gmail, Docs, Drive and Android integration is hard for rivals to match
  • 1-million-token context lets you load large documents in one prompt
  • Storage and family sharing make Pro good value for households already paying for Google One

Cons

  • Usage is compute-based, depends on prompt and chat length, and resets every 5 hours up to a weekly limit, so quotas are hard to predict
  • Plans, model names and bundles change frequently; confirm on the official page before buying
  • Part of the price is storage, which has no value if you do not need it
  • Reviewers have scored its accuracy and coding below some rivals, so verify facts and code
  • Ultra tiers are expensive and the page shows only 'from' prices for them; some perks are limited to eligible countries or adults

Frequently asked questions

Is Gemini free?

Yes. The Free plan needs only a Google account and includes Deep Research, image generation, Gemini Live, Canvas and Gems, with lower usage limits and 15 GB of storage.

What does Google AI Plus cost and include?

$4.99/mo for about twice Free usage, 400 GB of storage and 200 Flow credits. AI Pro is $19.99/mo with about four times Free usage and 5 TB.

What are the usage limits?

They are compute-based and vary with prompt complexity and chat length. They reset every 5 hours until a weekly limit, and you can buy AI credits to extend them.

Is Gemini better than ChatGPT?

It depends on your setup. Gemini wins on Google app integration, storage bundling and price; ChatGPT and Claude are often preferred for coding and writing quality.

Is there a free trial for paid plans?

Google's plans page links to a free trial offer but does not state the terms. Check the official page for current length and eligibility.
