Meshy is a generative AI platform focused specifically on 3D content -- models, textures, rigs, and animation -- built for creators who don't have traditional 3D modeling skills or who need to produce assets far faster than manual workflows allow. The company (Meshy LLC) positions itself for three main audiences: game developers who need a steady stream of style-consistent props and characters, 3D printing hobbyists who want a quick prompt-to-printable-model pipeline, and product, toy, or jewelry designers prototyping physical objects. Meshy holds SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and GDPR compliance badges on its site, signaling it targets studio and enterprise customers alongside individual hobbyists, and its own FAQ states it serves millions of creators and studios, including paid enterprise clients.

Functionally, Meshy covers the full 3D pipeline in one browser-based tool: Text-to-3D and Image-to-3D generate a textured mesh from a prompt or reference photo in under a minute; AI Texturing applies full PBR maps (albedo, normal, roughness, metallic, ambient occlusion) to any uploaded mesh from a text or image description; and Auto-Rigging & Animation rigs humanoid or quadruped models and applies any of 500+ built-in animation presets without manual bone placement. A newer 3D Agent mode lets users iterate conversationally, refining a model over a multi-turn chat rather than re-writing prompts from scratch. Meshy also offers remeshing/topology tools to hit a target polygon budget (roughly 3,000-100,000 polys), an API and CLI for pipeline automation, an MCP server for use inside AI coding agents, and plugins for Blender, Unity, Unreal, Godot, and several 3D-printing slicers.

Best for

Meshy is a strong fit for indie game developers, 3D printing hobbyists, and product or toy designers who need a high volume of draft-quality or game-ready 3D assets fast and don't have or want to use traditional 3D modeling skills. It's a weaker fit for mechanical/CAD engineers or studios that need dimensionally precise, cleanup-free hard-surface geometry, since generated meshes in that category typically require manual retopology or CAD work afterward.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • A single generation (text or image to a textured, downloadable mesh) typically completes in under a minute, letting artists iterate at a pace manual modeling can't match.
  • Every paid tier includes full API access and unlimited downloads, so Meshy fits into automated content pipelines, not just manual one-off use.
  • The free plan is unusually generous for a 3D generation tool -- 100 credits/month with no credit card required -- and even free-tier outputs carry a CC BY 4.0 license permitting commercial use with attribution.
  • Auto-rigging and 500+ animation presets remove one of the most time-consuming parts of game-asset prep, turning a static mesh into an animation-ready character without touching a rigging tool.
  • SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certification, plus a private-by-default asset policy, make it easier for studios to justify adopting a cloud AI tool for production work.

Cons

  • Independent reviews consistently flag weaker results on hard-surface, mechanical, or CAD-precision geometry, with one review scoring this dimension 3.5/5 and noting studios needing production-ready geometry without cleanup should look elsewhere.
  • At 20 credits per generation, iterating on shape before committing to a final texture pass burns through the Free and Pro tier's monthly allowance quickly.
  • Monthly credits don't roll over, so inconsistent usage months effectively waste unused allowance compared to purchasing on-demand credits.
  • Free-tier assets are CC BY 4.0 licensed rather than exclusively owned, which some commercial users may find limiting compared to paid-tier full ownership.
  • Refunds are rare by policy, citing high GPU operating costs, so users are expected to evaluate carefully on the free tier before subscribing.

Frequently asked questions

Can I sell 3D models I create with Meshy?

Yes. Paid-plan users own their generated assets outright and can sell or redistribute them; free-plan users get a CC BY 4.0 license and are asked to credit Meshy.

What file formats does Meshy export?

Models export as FBX, OBJ, USDZ, GLB, STL, or Blend files; texturing-only jobs accept FBX, OBJ, STL, GLTF, or GLB uploads.

Does Meshy offer an API?

Yes, Meshy provides a full API plus a CLI and an MCP server for use inside AI coding agents like Claude or Cursor.

How long does a generation take?

A complete text-to-3D or image-to-3D generation typically finishes in under a minute, though queue times vary by plan tier.

Is Meshy good for 3D printing?

Yes, Meshy includes dedicated 3D printing workflows and plugins for Bambu Studio, Creality Print, Cura, and Lychee Slicer.

How do credits work?

Credits are Meshy's internal currency; a standard text-to-3D or image-to-3D generation costs 20 credits, monthly plan credits reset each billing cycle and don't roll over, and permanent credits (purchased or earned) never expire.
