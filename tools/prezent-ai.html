Prezent.ai is a good fit for big companies, particularly life sciences firms, that want every employee's slides to stay on brand and are happy to pay enterprise rates; individuals, students and small teams should skip it, since the cheapest listed plan is $399 per user per month. It combines an AI deck builder, a converter that restyles existing decks into approved templates, and a library of ready-made slides.

The current site leads with life sciences communications. It describes a self-serve AI platform (with an agent called Astrid), agentic workflows with API access, overnight decks built by experts, and fixed-price expert projects. Integrations with sources such as PubMed, Veeva and ClinicalTrials.gov are advertised. Claims such as client counts and time savings are the vendor's own and were not independently checked.

Rollout is usually led by a central team that sets up templates and brand rules. I could not verify details such as exact slide download limits on higher tiers, since those plans are quote-only.

Best for

Best for large enterprises, particularly pharma and life sciences communications, marketing and sales enablement teams, that need many people to produce consistent decks and want optional expert help. Not for students, freelancers, startups or small teams who need an occasional deck: the entry price is high and the strongest features sit in quote-only tiers. Also not ideal if you want a free, no-setup tool.

Key features

01

AI deck generation

Builds presentations from prompts or documents using approved brand templates, with charts, frameworks and citations on higher tiers.

02

Template Converter

Applies a company template to existing decks; listed as unlimited on Lite.

03

Slide Library and storylines

Ready-made expert slides (10K+ on Lite, 35K+ on higher plans) and 100+ best-practice storylines on higher plans.

04

Overnight and expert services

Pro and above add decks built by experts overnight; the site states a 24-hour turnaround.

05

APIs and workflows

The Custom tier adds Auto Generator and Template Converter APIs, custom workflows and SCIM seat management.

06

Communication coaching

Premium and above include courses, workshops and avatar-based practice.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Template Converter solves the common problem of off-brand legacy decks.
  • The expert overnight service gives a human fallback for important decks.
  • Large slide library and storyline set speed up recurring decks like QBRs.
  • Life sciences integrations and compliance-oriented positioning suit regulated teams.
  • Higher tiers add API and SCIM support that IT teams expect.

Cons

  • At $399 per user per month, Lite is far above typical AI deck tools.
  • Lite caps downloads at 300 slides per year with no rollover.
  • Premium, Pro and Custom prices require a sales conversation.
  • Value depends on setup of templates and library, which takes admin effort.
  • Marketing now centres on life sciences, so fit for other industries is less clear from the site.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Prezent.ai cost?

Lite is listed at $399 per user per month, with 15% off for annual billing. Premium, Pro and Custom are contact-sales only.

Is there a free trial?

The site shows 'Try for free' buttons on every plan. Trial length and limits were not stated on the pages I could read.

Can it restyle my existing decks?

Yes, the Template Converter applies your brand template to existing decks. It is unlimited on Lite.

Does it offer human-built decks?

Yes. Pro and Custom include overnight presentations built by experts, and fixed-price expert projects are advertised.

Is it only for life sciences?

The current site focuses on life sciences, but the plans are described generally for enterprise teams. Check fit with sales for other industries.
