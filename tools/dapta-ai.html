Dapta is a good fit for small sales-driven businesses that want AI agents to call, text and book meetings from inbound leads; skip it if you need predictable per-minute voice pricing or deep contact-center features. It combines voice agents, SMS and chat agents, and WhatsApp messaging and calls, plus a notetaker, forms, booking pages and multi-step sequences. Everything runs on credits, and the pricing page does not say how many credits a call minute or message uses, so you must trial it to estimate real cost. A pricing calculator on the site recommends a plan from calls, contacts and messages.

Best for

SMB sales teams in services, real estate or education wanting automated lead follow-up without developers. Not for buyers who need transparent per-minute pricing up front, enterprise telephony or compliance guarantees we could not verify, or consumers wanting a general chatbot.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Free tier lets you build and test a first agent before paying.
  • One platform for voice, text and WhatsApp avoids stitching tools together.
  • Unlimited agents and flows on every paid plan.
  • Higher tiers add real human onboarding support.

Cons

  • Credit consumption per call minute or message is not published, so monthly cost is hard to predict.
  • Pro to Scale Up is a 5x price jump.
  • Free plan is capped at 1 agent, 2 flows and 1 user.
  • We found no independent review-site ratings to verify call quality; treat performance claims as vendor claims.
  • Integration count differs between sources (300+ earlier, 1,000+ now); confirm your CRM is supported.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

Yes: $0 with 2K credits, 1 agent, 2 flows and 1 user.

How much does it cost?

Pro $99, Scale Up $499, Prime $1,499 per month; Custom via sales.

How are credits used?

Calls, texts and chatbots consume credits, but the per-interaction rate is not published. Use the free tier or pricing calculator to estimate.

Does it support WhatsApp?

Yes, WhatsApp agents and WhatsApp AI calls are listed.

How does it compare to TalkSmart AI?

Dapta focuses on outbound-style sales follow-up and booking; TalkSmart positions as an AI employee answering every call and chat. Compare on your use case.
