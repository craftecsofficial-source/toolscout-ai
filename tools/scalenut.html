Scalenut is an AI content and SEO platform originally built around the classic long-form SEO workflow — keyword research, topic clustering, AI-assisted writing, and on-page optimization scored against top-ranking competitors — that has since expanded into Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), the practice of optimizing for visibility inside AI answer engines rather than just traditional search results. The platform's SEO core includes a Keyword Planner that groups semantically related terms into content clusters, a Content Optimizer that scores drafts in real time against NLP terms and top-30 competitor pages, and 'Cruise Mode,' Scalenut's flagship feature that can generate a near-complete, SEO-briefed blog draft from a single topic input in a few minutes. On top of this, Scalenut has built a GEO Action Center and AI Visibility product that tracks how often and how favorably a brand appears in ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and (on higher tiers) Perplexity responses, refreshed weekly, alongside a GEO content audit tool that scores existing web pages for AI-readability at scale.

Scalenut positions itself against both narrower SEO content tools (Surfer SEO, Clearscope, Frase) and general AI writers (Jasper), differentiating by bundling research, drafting, optimization, and now AI-visibility tracking into a single workspace rather than requiring several subscriptions. The company reports over 100,000 marketers and 1 million+ businesses have used the platform, with 5 million+ SEO blogs generated to date, and backs this scale with a real, verifiable review record: a 4.7 rating on G2 and 4.8 on Capterra, cited on Scalenut's own reviews page as '4.8/5 by 600+ users across G2, Capterra, Trustpilot & more.' Pricing is usage-based around monthly article/optimization/keyword-cluster quotas and AI-prompt tracking volume, with frequent limited-time promotional discounts (commonly 60% off on annual plans) layered on top of the standard monthly rates, plus a top-tier 'VIP Service' where Scalenut's own strategists and writers execute GEO content and backlink work directly for the client.

Best for

Best suited to content marketers, SEO specialists, and agencies that want one platform covering keyword research, AI-assisted drafting, on-page optimization, and now AI-answer-engine visibility tracking, especially teams already investing in both classic SEO and GEO. Less ideal for teams that only need a narrow point solution (e.g., just AI writing, or just rank tracking) and would find Scalenut's broader feature set unnecessary overhead.

Key features

01

Cruise Mode

Generates a full, SEO-briefed blog draft from a single topic input in minutes, intended to replace the manual outline-then-write process.

02

Content Optimizer

Scores drafts in real time against NLP terms, competitor word counts, headings, and readability pulled from the top-ranking pages for a target keyword.

03

Keyword Planner

Groups semantically related keywords into topic clusters to plan content calendars around search intent rather than one keyword at a time.

04

AI Visibility / GEO Action Center

Tracks how often and how favorably a brand's content is surfaced in ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity responses, refreshed weekly.

05

GEO content audit

Scans existing web pages at scale (200-2,000+ pages depending on plan) and flags which ones are and aren't well-optimized for AI-engine visibility.

06

AI content detector and humanizer

Detects AI-generated text and rewrites flagged content to reduce detectability, with a monthly word allowance on paid tiers.

07

Internal linking and topic gap analysis

Surfaces internal linking opportunities and content gaps versus competitors as add-on modules on Plus and above.

08

Publishing integrations

Auto-publish finished content directly to WordPress or Shopify, plus integrations with Semrush and Copyscape.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Scalenut bundles keyword research, AI drafting, on-page optimization, and AI-visibility (GEO) tracking into one workspace, reducing the number of separate SEO/content subscriptions a team needs to run.
  • Cruise Mode is a genuinely fast path from topic to a structured, SEO-briefed first draft, which users on G2 specifically call out as a time-saver over manually building an outline first.
  • The review record is both real and strong: Scalenut cites 4.7/5 on G2 and 4.8/5 on Capterra, backed by 600+ combined reviews across G2, Capterra, and Trustpilot, with G2-tracked sub-scores of roughly 9.3/10 for ease of use and 9.3/10 for ease of setup.
  • Being an early mover into GEO/AI-visibility tracking (alongside classic SEO) means existing SEO-focused customers get AI-answer-engine monitoring without switching platforms as search behavior shifts.
  • Multiple G2 badges (High Performer, Leader in Content Creation, Momentum Leader) across several quarterly cycles suggest sustained, not one-off, positive market reception.

Cons

  • Standard monthly sticker pricing climbs quickly — $199/month at the Professional tier — before factoring in any add-ons like the AI Link Manager, Social Upreach, or Backlinks Marketplace.
  • Scalenut runs frequent limited-time promotions (commonly citing 60% off annual plans), which makes the 'real' ongoing price a moving target that buyers need to verify at signup rather than trust the sticker rate.
  • As with all AI writing tools, Cruise Mode output is a strong first draft, not a finished, fact-checked, brand-voice-matched article — human editing is still required before publishing.
  • Lower tiers cap AI prompt tracking at just 10-25 prompts/month (Starter/Plus), which may be too thin for brands wanting to monitor AI visibility across a broad set of queries without upgrading to Professional.

What reviewers say

Scalenut's official reviews page states a combined 4.8/5 rating from 600+ users across G2, Capterra, and Trustpilot; G2 specifically lists Scalenut at 4.7/5, and G2 category benchmarks show Scalenut scoring around 9.3/10 for ease of use and ease of setup. Reviewers consistently praise the SEO Assistant's competitor/NLP analysis and Cruise Mode's drafting speed.

Frequently praised

  • Reviewers repeatedly call out the SEO Assistant for surfacing NLP terms, top-30 competitor analysis, and 'People Also Ask'-style questions in one report, replacing multiple standalone SEO tools
  • Cruise Mode is frequently cited as a major time-saver for producing a full SEO-briefed blog draft (some reviewers claim ~2,500-word drafts in under 5 minutes)
  • Multiple reviewers say Scalenut replaced 2-3 other tools they were previously paying for (e.g., separate keyword research, writing, and optimization subscriptions)

Frequently criticized

  • Scalenut's cited review counts are aggregated across G2, Capterra, and Trustpilot rather than broken out per platform, making it harder to assess volume on any single review site independently
  • Some third-party comparison sites note the promotional pricing structure (frequent large discounts) makes standard cost unclear without checking current offers
  • As with most AI content tools, a portion of reviews and comparison content note that output still needs human editing for voice and accuracy before publishing

Frequently asked questions

What is Scalenut used for?

Scalenut is used for SEO content research, AI-assisted blog writing (via Cruise Mode), on-page content optimization against competitor benchmarks, and tracking a brand's visibility in AI answer engines like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews (GEO).

How much does Scalenut cost?

Standard monthly pricing runs $59 (Starter), $89 (Plus), and $199 (Professional), each with a 7-day free trial; Scalenut also frequently runs promotional discounts (commonly around 60% off on annual billing) and offers a custom-priced VIP Service tier.

What is Cruise Mode?

Cruise Mode is Scalenut's feature for generating a full, SEO-briefed blog draft from a single topic input in a few minutes, intended to replace manually building an outline and writing from scratch.

Does Scalenut track AI search visibility, not just Google rankings?

Yes — its GEO Action Center and AI Visibility tools track how a brand appears in ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and (on the Professional plan) Perplexity, refreshed weekly.

What are Scalenut's real G2 and Capterra ratings?

Scalenut is rated 4.7/5 on G2 and 4.8/5 on Capterra, with a combined 600+ reviews cited across G2, Capterra, and Trustpilot on the company's own reviews page.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, Scalenut offers a 7-day free trial on its paid plans before requiring payment.
