Scalenut is a good fit for content and SEO teams that want keyword research, AI drafting and AI-search visibility tracking under one login, and who will pay annually. It is a poor fit if you need only one narrow job done, such as rank tracking or a plain AI writer, or if you want a predictable monthly price without reading the fine print on limits. It started as an SEO writing tool built around keyword clusters, a content optimizer and Cruise Mode, which drafts a blog post from a topic. It now also sells GEO features: tracking how a brand shows up in AI answers, auditing existing pages, and a done-for-you service run by Scalenut strategists.

Best for

Who it is for: in-house marketers, SEO freelancers and agencies producing regular long-form content who also want to watch AI-search visibility. Who it is not for: teams needing only a single point tool, buyers who want heavy AI-prompt tracking on a small budget (Starter tracks 10 prompts, Plus 25), or anyone expecting publish-ready articles with no editing.

Key features

01

Cruise Mode

Generates a structured blog draft from a topic. Treat the output as a first draft that needs editing and fact-checking.

02

Content optimizer

Scores your draft against terms and structure from pages that rank for the target keyword.

03

Keyword planner

Groups related keywords into clusters; the advanced planner with cannibalization analysis is on Professional.

04

AI visibility tracking

Tracks a set number of prompts (10, 25 or 100 by plan) across ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews, with Perplexity on Professional.

05

GEO content audit

Audits existing pages for AI-search readiness: 200 pages per month on Plus, 1,000 on Professional.

06

Internal linking and topic gaps

Advanced content audit, internal linking and topic gap tools are listed from Plus upward.

07

Publishing

Auto-publish to WordPress and Shopify is listed on Plus and Professional.

08

VIP Service

Custom-priced done-for-you GEO: a strategist, writers and editors, AI agents, backlinks and monthly technical audits.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Covers keyword research, drafting, optimization and AI-visibility tracking, so fewer separate subscriptions
  • Annual billing is far cheaper per month than monthly (Professional $70 vs $199 per the pricing page)
  • Higher tiers add real capacity: 100 tracked prompts, 1,000-page audits and unlimited seats
  • A managed VIP Service exists for teams that do not want to operate the tools themselves

Cons

  • Monthly pricing is steep, and the cheap annual price means a longer commitment
  • The pricing page we read contained conflicting figures (for example keyword cluster limits for Professional) and mixed promotional text, so confirm limits before paying
  • The page says annual plans carry 3X limits, so monthly buyers get lower quotas than the annual headline suggests
  • Starter and Plus prompt tracking (10 and 25) is thin for broad AI-visibility monitoring
  • AI drafts need human editing; we did not verify output quality independently

Frequently asked questions

How much does Scalenut cost?

Monthly billing is $59 (Starter), $89 (Plus) and $199 (Professional). Annual billing is shown at $21, $31 and $70 per month. VIP Service is custom. Promotions change, so check the live page.

Is there a free trial?

The pricing page shows a 7-day trial on the monthly view. We could not verify whether it requires a card.

Are annual plans limited differently from monthly?

Yes. The page states annual plans get 3X limits (for example Plus articles rise from 30 to 90 per month) and the discount applies to annual only.

Does it track visibility in ChatGPT and Perplexity?

It tracks ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews on all paid plans, and adds Perplexity on Professional.

Does Cruise Mode produce publish-ready articles?

No. It produces a structured first draft that you should edit and fact-check.
