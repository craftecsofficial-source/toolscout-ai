Slazzer is a good choice for e-commerce sellers, photographers and developers who need dependable background removal in bulk or through an API; skip it if you want a full photo editor or a flat monthly fee for unlimited images. You upload an image and get a transparent cutout back in seconds, with a built-in editor for touch-ups.

It also bundles upscaling, enhancement, shadows, sky replacement and colorizing old photos, and is available on web, mobile, desktop apps, a Photoshop extension and an API. We could not verify the file-size limits: the homepage cites 50 MP and WebP support, while the pricing page cites 25 MP and 12 MB for JPG/PNG.

Best for

E-commerce sellers, photographers and developers who process many images or want to automate cutouts. Not for people who want an all-in-one photo editor, anyone needing unlimited free HD output, or very high-volume users who would be better served by flat-rate or self-hosted options.

Key features

01

Automatic background removal

Detects the subject and returns a transparent cutout, including people, products, cars and animals.

02

Built-in editor

Swap backgrounds, erase or restore areas, and add text, logos or blur.

03

Desktop, mobile and plugin access

Apps for Windows, Mac, Linux, Android and iOS, plus a Photoshop extension.

04

API

Documented API for bulk processing inside your own product.

05

Extra AI tools

Upscale (2x/4x), enhance, shadow, sky replacement, relighting, de-oldify and catalog-style tools for grocery, food and furniture.

06

Enterprise options

On-premise deployment, custom training and dedicated account management on request.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Cutouts come back in seconds and handle hair and fur well on typical photos.
  • Web, mobile, desktop, Photoshop and API access fit both casual and automated use.
  • Published price tiers make cost per image easy to calculate.
  • Subscription credits roll over and top-ups last two years, so light users are not forced to burn credits.
  • Preview-quality results are free and unlimited on the website, so you can test accuracy first.

Cons

  • Free previews are capped at 0.25 MP; HD files cost credits.
  • Subscription prices exclude VAT while top-up prices include it, which makes comparison fiddly.
  • Low-contrast, transparent or motion-blurred subjects may still need manual cleanup.
  • Per-image pricing can exceed flat-rate or self-hosted alternatives at high volume.
  • Commercial use is restricted to top-up buyers or 300+ credit subscribers.

Frequently asked questions

Is Slazzer free?

Partly. You get 2 free credits and unlimited free previews up to 0.25 MP; HD downloads use credits.

How much does it cost?

Subscriptions start at $11/month for 100 credits (excl. VAT); one-time packs start at $6 for 10 credits. HD background removal is 1 credit.

Can I use it commercially?

Per the pricing page, commercial use requires a top-up plan or a subscription of at least 300 credits.

Is there an API and a Photoshop plugin?

Yes, both are listed, along with desktop apps for Windows, Mac and Linux.

What file sizes are supported?

Unclear. The pricing page says JPG/PNG up to 12 MB and 25 MP; the homepage says up to 50 MP including WebP. Check before bulk use.
