AICarousels is a good fit for solo creators and consultants who publish LinkedIn or Instagram carousels often and want a quick, narrow tool; skip it if you need scheduling, brand-kit governance or a general design suite. It drafts slide copy from a topic or text you supply, then places it in an editor that resizes elements automatically and offers preset color and font pairings. Output targets LinkedIn, Instagram and TikTok carousel formats. The site also lists side tools such as a LinkedIn post generator, post ideas generator, quote cards, infographics and a headshot generator, which it says come with the PRO plan. Pricing and free-plan limits were not visible on the pages we could load today, so confirm them on the site.

Best for

Solo creators, coaches and small-team marketers who post carousels regularly. Not for agencies needing approval workflows, scheduling or shared brand kits, nor for anyone wanting freeform custom layouts or video.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Trying it without an account lowers the cost of evaluating it.
  • Bonus generators add LinkedIn post and quote-card output without a second subscription, per the site.

Cons

  • No public price on the pages we could load, so total cost and free-plan caps (watermark, download limits) are unconfirmed.
  • Template-driven editing limits heavily custom brand designs.
  • No publishing or scheduling, so you export and post elsewhere.
  • Only carousels and a few side formats; not a replacement for a full design tool.
