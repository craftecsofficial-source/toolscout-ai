Surfer SEO is an on-page content optimization and AI search visibility platform made by Surfer, based in Wrocław, Poland. It's used by an estimated 150,000+ customers, including brands like FedEx, Shopify, ClickUp and Square, and competes most directly with Clearscope, MarketMuse and the content-optimization modules inside Semrush and Ahrefs.

Surfer's core differentiator is its Content Editor: a live, NLP-driven scoring system that reverse-engineers the top-ranking pages for a target keyword and gives writers real-time, numeric feedback as they type. Where general-purpose SEO suites like Semrush bolt content scoring onto a much broader toolset, Surfer built its entire product around that one workflow, then expanded outward into keyword research, audits and — as of 2026 — AI/LLM visibility tracking.

Best for

Surfer is best suited to content teams, in-house SEOs and agencies producing recurring long-form content who want data-backed, real-time guidance on individual articles plus site-wide content planning. It's a weaker fit for freelancers or very low-volume publishers, who are the group most likely to find the pricing hard to justify.

Key features

01

Content Editor

A live writing environment that scores content in real time against the top-ranking pages for a target keyword, recommending word count, keywords, headings and images.

02

SERP Analyzer

Analyzes the current top-ranking pages for any keyword across 500+ on-page and off-page signals.

03

Keyword Research & Keyword Surfer

Finds related keywords, search volume and topic clusters; Keyword Surfer is a free Chrome extension version of the same keyword data.

04

Content Audit

Scans existing published pages, flags ranking drops, and surfaces which articles have the best 'quick win' potential to refresh.

05

Topical Map

Plans content clusters around a niche to build topical authority and close content coverage gaps before writing starts.

06

AI Tracker (AI Visibility)

Tracks how often a brand is mentioned or cited in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode and Gemini, with mention-gap and brand sentiment reporting.

07

Surfy (AI writing assistant) + AI Detector/Humanizer

An in-editor AI assistant for drafting and one-click optimization, paired with tools to detect and rewrite AI-flavored text.

08

Integrations

Direct integrations with WordPress, Google Docs, Contentful and Zapier, plus API access on higher tiers.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The Content Editor's real-time, NLP-driven scoring is repeatedly singled out in reviews as removing guesswork from on-page optimization
  • Very high satisfaction on independent review sites: 4.8/5 on G2 and 4.9/5 on Capterra, among the strongest ratings in the content-optimization category
  • Topical Map and Content Audit extend the tool beyond single-article optimization into ongoing content planning and decay monitoring
  • Actively expanding into AI-search visibility tracking as search shifts away from pure blue links
  • Deep integrations with WordPress, Google Docs and Contentful fit into existing writer workflows
  • Customer support is consistently praised in Capterra reviews as fast and responsive

Cons

  • Plans were renamed and repriced in 2026, so third-party pricing content is frequently out of date and conflicting
  • Entry-level Discovery plan's document and page-tracking caps are tight for anyone publishing at real volume
  • Pricing is the single most common complaint on G2, with some reviewers calling it costly relative to competitors
  • The Content Editor doesn't support building tables directly; they must be created outside the editor and pasted in
  • Capterra reviewers note the tool performs less reliably for non-English content, specifically citing Spanish
  • Advertised low prices require annual billing paid upfront in full

What reviewers say

Surfer is rated unusually high across both major review platforms, with reviewers consistently praising the Content Editor's precision and ease of use. The dominant negative theme on both platforms, and echoed in Reddit discussion, is price: reviewers acknowledge it delivers strong results but describe the cost as steep, especially at the entry tier's document/credit limits.

Frequently asked questions

Does Surfer SEO have a free plan?

No ongoing free plan, but Surfer offers a free trial and a genuinely free Chrome extension, Keyword Surfer, for basic keyword data. Paid plans start at $49/mo (Discovery), billed annually.

How much does Surfer SEO cost in 2026?

Current tiers are Discovery $49/mo, Standard $99/mo, Pro $182/mo and Peace of Mind $299/mo, all billed annually; Enterprise is custom-priced starting around $999/mo.

Is Surfer SEO worth the price?

Most G2 and Capterra reviewers say yes for active content producers, citing measurable ranking improvements from the Content Editor. Budget-conscious or low-volume users are more likely to find the cost hard to justify.

How is Surfer different from Semrush for content?

Surfer is purpose-built around one workflow — real-time content scoring against top-ranking pages — while Semrush bundles a lighter content-optimization feature inside a much broader SEO/PPC/AI-visibility suite.

Can I track AI search visibility with Surfer?

Yes. Surfer's AI Tracker monitors brand mentions and citations across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode and Gemini, with tracked-prompt volume scaling by plan.

Does Surfer support languages other than English?

Surfer states it works with all languages, but Capterra reviewers report it performs less reliably outside English, specifically flagging Spanish content optimization as weaker.
