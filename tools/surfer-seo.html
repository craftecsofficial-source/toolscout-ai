Surfer SEO suits content teams, in-house SEOs and agencies that publish articles regularly and want concrete, data-led edits for each one. Freelancers or low-volume publishers will find the yearly price hard to justify, and those wanting backlink or competitor data need another tool alongside it.

Its core is the Content Editor, which analyzes pages ranking for your keyword and gives live guidelines while you write. Around it sit a content audit that ranks fixes for underperforming pages, topical maps and competitive gap analysis, and AI visibility tracking that watches how your brand appears in ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Mode and AI Overviews. Surfer also offers an MCP connection, and lists an agent called Surfy Agent as coming soon.

Best for

For: content marketers, in-house SEOs and agencies producing recurring articles who want writers to follow data-based guidelines. Not for: casual bloggers, people who need backlink analysis or broad competitor research, or anyone who wants a month-to-month low-cost tool, since the advertised prices assume annual billing.

Key features

01

Content Editor

Live guidelines against top-ranking pages for a keyword, including AI-search guidance, brand voice and templates.

02

AI visibility tracking

Monitors brand mentions across ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Mode and AI Overviews and flags when competitors gain mentions. Prompts included depend on plan (none on Discovery).

03

Content audit

Finds underperforming pages and ranks fixes by expected impact.

04

Topical map and gap analysis

Suggests topics and content plans based on competitor coverage.

05

Re-optimization alerts

Notifies you when a page slips or an AI answer changes which brands it names.

06

Surfer MCP

Connects Surfer to other tools and workflows.

07

Free tools

A free AI visibility audit and an AI volatility tracker, separate from paid plans.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Content Editor turns a vague 'optimize this' task into specific edits
  • Handles both classic SEO and AI-answer visibility in one product
  • Plans scale from one seat to ten, plus enterprise
  • Free AI visibility audit lets you test the AI side before buying
  • Bank transfer accepted on yearly plans

Cons

  • Prices shown are per month but billed yearly, charged in full upfront (Discovery is about $588)
  • Discovery has no AI prompt tracking, so AI visibility needs Standard or above
  • Document and page caps mean heavy publishers will reach Pro or Peace of Mind
  • The page does not state a free trial length; check checkout before assuming one
  • PayPal is not supported
  • Not a backlink or broad competitor-research tool; most teams still pair it with a larger SEO suite

Frequently asked questions

How much does Surfer SEO cost?

Per month, billed yearly: Discovery $49, Standard $99, Pro $182, Peace of Mind $299, and AI Search Analytics $82. Enterprise is custom. Annual billing is charged in full upfront.

Is there a free plan?

No free plan is listed. Free tools exist, such as an AI visibility audit and an AI volatility tracker. Trial length is not stated on the pricing page.

Do I need Surfer if I already have Semrush?

Semrush covers research and tracking more broadly; Surfer goes deeper on article-level optimization. Whether you need both depends on how much content you publish.

Can Surfer track AI search visibility?

Yes, across ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Mode and AI Overviews. Discovery includes no prompts; Standard has 25 weekly, Pro 50 daily, Peace of Mind 100 daily.

What does Unlimited mean on Peace of Mind?

Documents and brand workspaces are unlimited subject to a fair usage policy.
