Instantly is a strong value pick for agencies, founders and small SDR teams that send high volumes of cold email across many mailboxes and want warmup and deliverability tools included. Skip it if you need a CRM-centered, multichannel enterprise sales engagement suite, or if you cannot tolerate the risk and compliance burden of cold outreach. It connects unlimited email accounts, warms them up, runs sequences, and sells a separate B2B lead database and AI agents on top. Pricing is spread across three product lines (outreach, lead credits, and combined bundles), so the real monthly cost depends on which pieces you combine.

Best for

Who it is for: cold-email agencies, outbound-focused founders and small SDR teams who want many mailboxes without per-inbox fees. Who it is not for: enterprises needing deep Salesforce-native multichannel cadences, teams that cannot accept cold-email deliverability and compliance risk, and buyers who want one simple price.

Key features

01

Unlimited email accounts and warmup

Outreach plans (Growth, Hypergrowth, Light Speed) list unlimited email accounts and warmup; monthly send and contact caps vary by plan.

02

Lead database and credits

A 450M+ contact B2B database with filters, email enrichment and AI research, bought as monthly credits (1,500 to 200,000+).

03

AI agents

Bundles add an AI Sales Agent; Scale and above add an AI Reply Agent.

04

Unibox

Replies consolidated in one inbox; listed as a Scale-bundle feature on the pricing page.

05

Deliverability tooling

Warmup, inbox placement testing and, on higher tiers, a private deliverability network (Enterprise) and the SISR system (Light Speed/Agency).

06

VIP managed service

Done-for-you setup of domains, accounts and campaigns; custom pricing, contact sales.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Unlimited email accounts and warmup on the outreach plans remove per-mailbox fees, which matters for agencies running many domains
  • Entry outreach price ($47/mo, $37.60 yearly) is low for a cold-email sender
  • You can buy sending, lead data and AI reply handling from one vendor
  • Higher tiers raise caps to 500,000 emails/month with premium support

Cons

  • The pricing page showed conflicting numbers for some tiers (e.g. Supersonic credits at $97 and $197), so the final bill needs checking at checkout
  • Entry caps are small: Growth allows 1,000 contacts and 5,000 emails/month
  • Lead credits are an extra recurring cost on top of outreach, so total spend can exceed the headline price
  • Warmup does not protect a bad list; sending practices still decide whether you land in spam
  • I could not verify current third-party ratings or support quality today, so none are claimed

Frequently asked questions

How much does Instantly cost?

Outreach-only plans are Growth $47/mo, Hypergrowth $97/mo and Light Speed $358/mo (about 20% less billed yearly). Bundles start at $94/mo, and lead credits are priced separately.

Is there a limit on email accounts?

Outreach plans list unlimited email accounts and warmup. Limits apply to monthly emails and contacts instead.

Do I need to pay for leads separately?

Outreach plans do not include the lead database; credits plans or the Starter, Scale and Agency bundles do.

Is cold email safe for deliverability?

Warmup and placement tools help, but results depend on your domains, list quality and volume. No tool removes spam-folder risk.

Does it have a CRM or multichannel outreach?

The homepage mentions an AI-powered CRM; I could not verify depth. Its focus is email, so LinkedIn and calling are not highlighted.
