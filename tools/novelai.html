NovelAI is a good fit if you write fiction or roleplay and want AI help with both the story and anime-style art for it. Skip it if you want a free tool, business or marketing copy, or photorealistic images. It is a subscription service that runs in the browser or on mobile. The site lists NovelAI Diffusion V5 for images (more characters, sharper detail, full comic pages) and GLM 4.6 for story generation, with world and character memory so a story stays consistent. Image tools include natural-language and tag prompting, character positioning, separately defined consistent characters, inpainting, Director Tools for poses, clothing and lighting, and a built-in canvas. The FAQ says prompts are encrypted and generated images are not stored on servers unless you save them. Subscriptions start at $10 a month; you can also buy Anlas on demand for image generation.

Best for

Good for fiction writers, tabletop and text roleplayers, and fan-fiction authors who also want character artwork. Not for people who need a free tool, marketing, SEO or factual writing, photorealistic images, or fully verified pricing before they sign up (the full tier list was not visible to us).

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Story writing and image generation sit under one subscription, handy if you want art for your own characters.
  • Character consistency and inpainting help with illustrated stories or comics.
  • Memory for worlds and characters is aimed at long-running stories.
  • Runs in a browser or on mobile with nothing to install.
  • The site states prompts are encrypted and images are not stored unless you save them, though we could not audit that.

Cons

  • Not free: the FAQ says it is not a free image generator, and trial terms are unclear (the site shows 'Start for free' buttons without stating limits).
  • We could not load a pricing page, so tiers above the $10 entry level are unverified.
  • Images lean anime-style and the writing focus is fiction, so it suits business and factual writing poorly.
  • Image generation consumes Anlas, so heavy use may mean extra purchases.
  • Output quality and model behavior change as models are swapped, so past impressions of the product may be out of date.

Frequently asked questions

How much does NovelAI cost?

The site says subscriptions start at $10 a month and cover image and text generation. We could not verify higher tiers; check the pricing page.

Is there a free trial?

The site mentions a trial period but does not state its length or limits, and the FAQ says NovelAI is not a free image generator. Confirm the terms at sign-up.

What models does it use?

The homepage lists NovelAI Diffusion V5 for images and GLM 4.6 for stories. These may change over time.

What are Anlas?

Credits used for image generation. You can buy them on demand; prices were not shown on the pages we loaded.

Is it a good tool for marketing copy?

Probably not. It is built for stories and anime-style art; tools such as Jasper or Copy.ai target marketing work.
