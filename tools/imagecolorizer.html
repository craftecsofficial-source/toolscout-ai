ImageColorizer is a good, inexpensive choice for people digitizing family photos who want one-click colorization and basic repair. Skip it if you are a professional restorer who needs exact, historically accurate color control, or if you want unlimited free use.

You upload a photo and the AI colorizes it, or repairs damage, sharpens it and retouches faces. It works in the browser and in mobile and desktop apps. Usage is metered in credits: the free plan gives 50 a month with ads, and paid plans add more credits, priority support, legacy batch processing and no ads.

We did not test output quality. We also could not see yearly prices or the Free plan's resolution limits; the page only states that paid users should upload photos under 100MB and 4000px on one side.

Best for

ImageColorizer suits families, hobbyist genealogists and small memorial projects that want fast automatic colorization and cleanup of old photos. It is not for professional colorists or archivists who need manual color control and historical accuracy, or for people who want to process thousands of photos for free.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • A free plan with 50 credits a month is enough to test it on a handful of photos
  • Colorizing, restoring and enhancing are all under one account
  • Paid credits can roll over to the next month, and the entry paid tier is under $7
  • Web and mobile apps mean you can work from a phone or computer

Cons

  • The free plan includes ads and only 50 credits, which runs out fast on a full album
  • Repeat downloads of the same image cost an extra credit
  • Batch processing is labelled legacy, and Business credits are listed as unlimited V2 access without a number, so check what you actually get
  • We did not test color accuracy; automatic tools can misjudge clothing or scene colors

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

Yes: $0 with 50 credits a month, single processing and ads.

How much do paid plans cost?

Starter $6.9, Premium $9.9 and Business $19.9 per month. Yearly billing saves up to 20%, but yearly prices weren't shown.

Do unused credits roll over?

The pricing page says subscription credits can roll over to the next month.

Is there a refund?

The page lists a 7-day money-back guarantee that applies to the trial period.

What file size can I upload?

For paid users, under 100MB and 4000px on one side; larger photos are resized. Free plan limits weren't stated.
