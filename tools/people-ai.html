People.ai was founded on the idea that CRM data is chronically incomplete because reps don't have time to manually log every email, call, and meeting. Its core technology captures this activity automatically from connected email and calendar systems and writes it back into Salesforce or other CRMs, giving revenue leaders a far more complete picture of what's actually happening in accounts.

On top of this activity data, People.ai layers engagement analytics that map out who within a buying committee reps are actually talking to, flagging when deals are over-reliant on a single contact or when key stakeholders have gone quiet. This is paired with pipeline inspection tools that surface deal risk earlier than traditional CRM stage tracking would catch it.

The platform competes in the same broader revenue intelligence category as Clari and Gong, though it has historically differentiated itself with a strong focus on automatic data capture and account/contact-level relationship mapping rather than call recording as the primary product anchor.

Best for

People.ai is best suited for enterprise sales organizations with complex, multi-stakeholder deals where manual CRM logging by reps is unreliable or incomplete. Sales operations and enablement teams use it to get accurate rep activity data and to identify coaching gaps at scale. Revenue leaders managing large buying committees benefit from its relationship mapping and engagement scoring. It's less suited to small sales teams without the budget or CRM complexity to justify an enterprise deployment.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Automatic activity capture significantly reduces the administrative burden on reps and improves overall CRM data completeness.
  • Relationship mapping across buying committees helps managers spot single-threaded deals before they stall or get lost to a competitor.
  • Rep performance analytics give sales managers concrete, activity-based data to ground coaching conversations rather than relying on anecdotes.
  • Pipeline risk signals surface issues earlier than manual stage-based CRM reviews typically would.

Cons

  • Enterprise sales cycles and implementation timelines mean it's a slower, heavier lift than lighter revenue intelligence tools.
  • Requires meaningful data governance work to map fields and configure capture rules correctly across the CRM.
  • Brand recognition and market share are smaller than category leaders like Clari or Gong, which can affect ecosystem and integration breadth.
  • Pricing opacity means budgeting requires a sales conversation rather than transparent self-serve tiers.

Frequently asked questions

What is People.ai used for?

It automatically captures sales activity from email, calendar, and calls to populate CRM records and provide engagement and performance analytics.

How does People.ai differ from Clari or Gong?

It places more emphasis on automatic activity capture and buying-committee relationship mapping, while Clari focuses more on forecasting and Gong on call recording.

Does People.ai require manual data entry from reps?

No, its core value proposition is eliminating manual logging by automatically capturing activity from connected systems.

How much does People.ai cost?

Pricing is custom and quote-based, sold as annual enterprise contracts with no published self-serve pricing.

Who uses People.ai?

Primarily enterprise sales organizations with complex, multi-stakeholder deals and dedicated sales operations teams.

Does People.ai integrate with Salesforce?

Yes, it integrates with Salesforce and other major CRMs to write captured activity data directly into existing records.
