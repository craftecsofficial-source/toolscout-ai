Backstory is for revenue leaders and RevOps at mid-to-large B2B companies who need to know which deals are real; small teams and individual reps looking for outreach help should skip it, since the vendor itself says it is not a rep productivity tool. The product, formerly People.ai, captures emails, meetings, calls and CRM records, then turns them into pipeline health views, forecasts, risk flags, account plans, stakeholder maps and qualification scores. Its MCP server lets leaders ask questions from Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot or Slack. The vendor says the rename changes nothing about the platform or customer data.

Best for

Good for CROs, VPs of Sales and RevOps running complex, multi-stakeholder deals on Salesforce, Dynamics or Oracle. Not for startups with a handful of reps, teams wanting an outbound sequencing or dialing tool, or buyers who need transparent self-serve pricing.

Key features

01

Revenue Decisions

Analytics, engagement scoring and deal summaries, with answers tied to the meeting, stakeholder and dollar value behind them.

02

Pipeline Health

AI forecasting, waterfall views of what changed, and headlines flagging stalled or cooling deals.

03

Account Strategy

Auto-generated account plans, parent-account roll-ups and stakeholder mapping.

04

Opportunity Qualification

MEDDPICC scoring from observed activity, custom signals and an AI sales coach.

05

MCP server and assistant

Ask questions about deals from Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini or Slack.

06

Activity capture

Pulls email, calendar and meeting data from Gmail, Outlook, Zoom and Teams to reduce manual CRM entry.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Answers cite the underlying meeting, contact and amount, which helps leaders trust forecast calls.
  • Supports multiple CRMs at once, useful after acquisitions.
  • MCP integration brings deal data into the AI tools leaders already use.
  • Long history in activity capture, with two years of deal history analyzed at start per the vendor.

Cons

  • No published pricing, so budgeting requires a sales cycle.
  • Aimed at leadership; reps get less day-to-day workflow help than from engagement tools.
  • Rename from People.ai to Backstory can cause confusion in older reviews and integrations.
  • Time savings claims (75-85% less data entry) are vendor-reported and unverified.

Frequently asked questions

Is People.ai the same as Backstory?

Yes. The vendor states 'same platform, new name'; accounts and data are unchanged, and the legal entity is still People.ai, Inc.

How much does it cost?

No prices are published. The site says pricing is usage-based rather than per seat. Request a quote.

Which CRMs does it support?

Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics and Oracle, including more than one at once, per the site.

How long does setup take?

The vendor says most teams go live in 2-4 weeks; treat that as a claim, not a guarantee.
