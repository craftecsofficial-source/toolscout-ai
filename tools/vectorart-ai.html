Great for people who need quick, scalable SVG icons and illustrations and are happy to touch them up; skip it if you need polished, brand-critical logos with precise geometry, or if you only need a handful of images a year, where a pay-per-download pack may be cheaper than a subscription. VectorArt.ai generates SVG files from text prompts or rough sketches, offers style collections, and hosts a large browsable library the site says is free for personal and educational use. It also has an SVG editor, an SVG background remover, a PNG to SVG converter and an AI vectorizer, plus an API page with no public details. Paid plans grant commercial rights; the homepage claims over 100,000 users, which is self-reported.

Best for

Good for freelancers, small teams and marketers who need quick vector assets with commercial rights. Not for illustrators who need exact path control and brand-grade logos, for anyone who needs full-colour photoreal imagery, or for light users who might do better with a single paid download.

Key features

01

Text-to-SVG Generation

Converts a written prompt directly into an editable, scalable vector graphic rather than a flat raster image.

02

Sketch-to-Image Prompts

Lets users draw a rough sketch as input, which the AI interprets and refines into a polished vector illustration.

03

Illustration Style Collections

Offers curated visual styles and themed collections (e.g., Geometric Grace, Retro Reveries) to guide the aesthetic of generated art.

04

Free Vector Library

A searchable, browsable gallery of 100,000+ pre-generated vector images across categories like logo, icon, cartoon, and landscape.

05

Built-in SVG Editor

Allows basic post-generation editing of vector paths and elements directly in the browser.

06

SVG Background Remover

Strips backgrounds from vector or uploaded images to produce transparent-background assets.

07

API Access

Provides programmatic access to the vector generation engine for developers building it into other products.

08

Royalty-Free Commercial Licensing

Paid subscribers get full commercial usage rights for website, social, marketing, and advertising use.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Outputs real SVG files rather than raster images dressed up as vector, so they scale and can be edited in design tools.
  • Pay-per-download packs ($4.99 single, $14.99 for ten) suit occasional users who do not want a subscription.
  • Includes handy adjacent tools (background remover, PNG to SVG, vectorizer) so simple clean-up stays in one place.
  • Free library lets you judge style quality before paying.

Cons

  • Free usage is limited to personal and educational use, and the free-account download cap is not stated.
  • Pricing jumps from $29 (1,000 images) to $99 (unlimited) with nothing in between.
  • AI vector output often needs manual cleanup of paths, and the logo results should be treated as concepts rather than final brand marks.
  • API terms are not public, so developers must contact the vendor before planning around it.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

Yes: free downloads for personal and educational use, with a limited number of downloads per free account (exact limit not published).

How much do paid plans cost?

Premium is $29/month for up to 1,000 images, Unlimited is $99/month, and one-off SVG downloads are $4.99 each or $14.99 for ten, per the pricing page today.

Can I use the images commercially?

Commercial use requires a paid plan or a paid download; free downloads are personal and educational only.

Do I need a subscription for a one-off project?

Not necessarily. The single and 10-pack download options avoid a recurring charge.

Is the output good enough for logos?

Treat it as a starting point. Verify results yourself with trial generations and clean up in a vector editor.
