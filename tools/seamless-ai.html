Seamless.ai suits outbound sales teams that want a contact finder, intent data and basic outreach in one place, and are willing to negotiate. Skip it if you need published prices or month-to-month flexibility: paid tiers are not listed publicly, and third-party buyer reports raise renewal and cancellation terms as the main risk.

Its pitch is that searches run live each time instead of returning an old stored record. Around the search it offers a Prospector list builder, Buyer Intent, Pitch Intelligence for AI-written messages, an Engagement Hub with emailing and calling, and AI agents. The company's own figures (billions of emails, hundreds of millions of phone numbers) are unaudited marketing claims.

The 50-credit free plan is the sensible way in: run your real target list through it, check bounces and whether numbers connect, and only then talk to sales. Read the contract's renewal and notice clauses and get them in writing.

Best for

Outbound-heavy SDR teams and agencies who want phone-first prospecting plus light outreach tooling and can negotiate annual terms. Not for buyers who need transparent self-serve pricing, month-to-month flexibility, or guaranteed data accuracy; Apollo.io or Clay may suit them better.

Key features

01

Real-time search engine

Instead of only returning stored records, Seamless.ai re-searches and attempts to verify an email or phone number at the moment you request it, which is the platform's central differentiator versus purely static contact databases.

02

Prospector

Lets you build lead and account lists using filters such as job title, company size, and recent job changes, functioning as the main list-building workflow.

03

Buyer Intent

Surfaces accounts showing signals of active in-market buying behavior, intended to help reps prioritize outreach toward prospects more likely to convert soon.

04

Pitch Intelligence

An AI writing feature that generates personalized outreach messaging per contact, meant to reduce manual copywriting for cold outreach.

05

Engagement Hub (Connect)

Bundles email sending, an AI-assisted dialer for calling, and social-selling touchpoints so reps can prospect and reach out without switching tools.

06

Outbound and Inbound AI Agents

Newer automation layer intended to run parts of outbound campaign sequencing and to personalize responses to inbound leads with less manual setup.

07

Chrome extension

A browser add-on that surfaces contact data while browsing sites like LinkedIn, useful for reps who prospect manually alongside the main app.

08

CRM enrichment and integrations

Pushes cleaned/enriched contact data into a connected CRM and claims connections to 6,000+ applications, plus an API for custom workflow automation.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The free plan is enough to test bounce rates and phone connect rates on your own target accounts.
  • Prospecting, intent data and outreach live in one subscription, so a small team avoids stitching tools.
  • Live-lookup design aims to catch job changes sooner than a static list.
  • The vendor states invalid emails are credited back, which gives some recourse.

Cons

  • Paid prices are not visible without sales or signup, which blocks comparison shopping.
  • Third-party reviews and pricing writeups often cite auto-renewals and long cancellation notice on annual deals; verify your contract terms.
  • Reviewers also report stale numbers and bounces despite the real-time pitch, so run a pilot.
  • Free credits are one-time, so the free plan is a trial, not a lasting tier.
  • Headline database and revenue statistics are self-reported and unaudited.

Frequently asked questions

Is Seamless.ai free?

Yes, there is a free plan with up to 50 credits for one user. The credits are one-time, so treat it as a trial.

How much do paid plans cost?

Seamless does not list paid prices on the pages we could read. Third parties report about $147/month for Basic and custom quotes above that; these are unverified, so confirm with the vendor.

Does it refund bad emails?

The site says invalid emails are automatically refunded as credits. That covers emails, not phone numbers, so test both.

What should I check before signing?

Ask for renewal, auto-renew and cancellation notice terms in writing, and run your own list through the free plan first.

Seamless.ai or Apollo.io?

Apollo shows tiers openly and is easier to start; choose Seamless if its phone data tests better on your list.
