WordHero is a good fit for solo marketers and small teams who want campaign copy, SEO articles and images from one workspace at a low monthly price. Skip it if you need a large team seat count, strict per-seat admin controls, or a free plan with no card. The product has moved to aivolut.com/wordhero and has been reworked since its older template-only days. You write a brief, and a Canvas can produce an email, LinkedIn post, ad and article together. Brand Memory keeps your audience, offer, voice and topics so you do not repeat them. The SEO article flow covers keyword research, brief, outline, draft, featured image and a WordPress draft. Usage is capped by plan on projects, brand voices, keyword searches and image credits.

Best for

Good for freelancers, solo marketers, small agencies and small business owners who produce mixed content (ads, emails, social, blog) and want shared brand context. Not for large teams needing many seats (Business allows only 3 invited members), anyone wanting a no-card free plan, or people who expect guaranteed SEO rankings, which WordHero says it does not promise.

Key features

01

Canvases

Create several assets such as an email, LinkedIn post, ad and article from one brief, then refine and reorder them in one place.

02

Brand Memory

Saves your audience, offer, brand voice and content topics so each new task starts with that context.

03

SEO Articles

Walks from keyword research to brief, outline and draft, adds a featured image and prepares a WordPress draft.

04

WordHero Art

Generates square, landscape and portrait images, limited by monthly image credits per plan.

05

Custom Templates

Lets you save recurring prompts and workflows; available on Pro and Business.

06

108 writing languages

Write in 108 languages; the app lists 109 locale options because English has US and UK variants.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • One brief can generate a set of related assets, which suits campaign work better than single-prompt tools.
  • Brand Memory stores audience, offer and voice, so you do not re-brief each time.
  • The SEO article flow covers keyword research through a WordPress draft in one place.
  • Entry price of $15/mo is low, and every plan has a 7-day trial.
  • Writing is supported in 108 languages.

Cons

  • A card is required to start the 7-day trial, so there is no card-free free plan.
  • Caps on projects, brand voices, keyword searches and image credits can bind quickly for agencies.
  • Team use is limited: only Business includes invited members, and only up to 3.
  • The pricing page's yearly savings figures do not match its own monthly prices, so check the total at checkout.
  • WordHero says it does not guarantee SEO rankings, and AI drafts still need human editing.

Frequently asked questions

How much does WordHero cost?

Monthly pricing is Starter $15, Pro $29 and Business $59. Yearly billing is $180, $348 and $708 per year as listed on the aivolut.com page.

Is there a free plan or trial?

There is no free plan. Every plan starts with a 7-day trial, but a card is required and you must cancel before day 7 to avoid being charged.

What are the main usage limits?

Limits are on projects (1, 3 or 10), brand voices (1, 5 or 20), monthly keyword searches (20, 100 or 500) and image credits (20, 100 or 900). Standard writing is unlimited under fair use.

Can a team use WordHero?

Only Business lists team access, with up to 3 invited members. Larger teams may find that restrictive.

How does it compare with Jasper or Writesonic?

WordHero is cheaper to start and centered on brief-to-campaign assets and SEO articles. Jasper targets bigger marketing teams, while Writesonic focuses on AI search visibility.
