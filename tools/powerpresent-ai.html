PowerPresent AI is great for founders, students and marketers who want a quick AI first draft and care most about ending up with a real, editable PowerPoint file; skip it if you need enterprise compliance or live data connections. It also suits occasional users because you can pay per export instead of subscribing. You describe an idea or upload a document, the AI builds the deck, and you edit it in a browser editor before exporting. Your own PowerPoint template can be applied, and a finished deck can become an AI-narrated video. The free plan has no usage limits on creating, editing and presenting, but watermark-free export is paid.

Best for

Good for founders making pitch decks, students, and sales or marketing people who need an editable .pptx on their own template, especially if they only export a few decks a year. Not for large enterprises needing compliance certifications, live CRM data or automated deck generation at scale, nor for people who want final-quality exports without paying anything.

Key features

01

Prompt or document to deck

Describe an idea or upload a document and the AI generates narrative, layouts, text and charts.

02

Editable PowerPoint export

Paid exports include .pptx, PDF and Google Slides without watermark; the site says charts stay editable.

03

Custom template upload

Apply your own PowerPoint template's layouts, fonts and colors to new decks.

04

Browser editor

Edit slide elements, charts, icons and shapes, with AI-assisted polishing and browser presenting.

05

Document upload

Convert source files into slides; uploads are capped at 1 MB on free and pay-as-you-go, 10 MB on subscriptions and lifetime. The pricing page states no page limit.

06

AI-narrated video

Turn a deck into a video with script and voiceover for 3 credits.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The free plan lets you create, edit and present without limits, so you can judge the editor before paying.
  • Pay-per-credit export at $1 suits people who need only occasional clean files.
  • Custom PowerPoint template upload helps decks match existing brand files.
  • A lifetime option at $499 avoids recurring billing for heavy long-term users.
  • Exports to .pptx and Google Slides keep the result editable outside the app.

Cons

  • The free plan produces only watermarked PDFs, so it is a trial rather than a way to get final decks.
  • Free and pay-as-you-go uploads are limited to 1 MB, which can block long source documents.
  • Smaller than the category leaders; I did not verify independent Trustpilot or G2 data, and the 4.3 rating and 40,000+ presentations are the site's own claims.
  • No compliance certifications were visible on the pages I read.
  • Claims in its comparison against rivals are self-reported marketing.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

Yes: unlimited creating, editing and presenting, with watermarked PDF export and no credits included.

Can I export a real PowerPoint file?

Yes, watermark-free .pptx export costs 1 credit per presentation or is included with a paid subscription's credits.

Can I turn a PDF or Word file into slides?

Yes, document upload costs 1 credit. The pricing page lists file-size caps (1 MB or 10 MB) but no page limit.

Can I use my own template?

Yes, the site says you can upload a PowerPoint template and have its layouts, fonts and colors applied.

What is the cheapest way to get clean exports?

Pay as you go at $1 per credit, or Starter at $9 per month for 10 credits.
