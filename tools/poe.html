Poe is a good pick if you like trying many AI models and want one app to do it; skip it if you mostly use a single model heavily or need fixed, predictable monthly costs. Poe, made by Quora, does not build its own model. It is a front end that lets you chat with models from providers such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta and Mistral, along with bots built by other users.

You can start a chat with one model and move to another, and some bots generate images, video or audio. Usage is paid for with compute points shared across all models, so a heavy model or long conversation uses up your allowance faster than a light one. You can also create your own bot by pairing a model with a custom prompt and publishing it. Poe publishes its plan tiers and point allowances on its own pricing screens, which we could not load automatically today, so confirm current prices there.

Best for

Good for individuals and small groups who want to compare models, experiment with community bots, or build simple prompt-based bots without code. Not for people who live in one model all day (the provider's own plan is often simpler), teams that need a flat predictable bill, or organisations that need enterprise admin controls and strict data agreements.

Key features

01

Multi-Model Access

Chat with GPT-5.x, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Llama and more without maintaining separate subscriptions to each provider.

02

Custom Bot Creation

Build and publish your own bot backed by any supported model plus a custom prompt or knowledge base, entirely no-code.

03

Compute Points Billing

A usage-based currency shared across all models is spent per message, with cost varying by model and by the length of conversation context.

04

Multimodal Generation

Generate text, images, video, or audio depending on which underlying model or bot is selected.

05

Developer API

An OpenAI-compatible chat completion endpoint lets developers route calls through Poe to any supported model using their subscription's points.

06

Cross-Platform Apps

Native apps for web, iOS, Android, macOS, and Windows keep chat history synced across every device.

07

Bot Creator Payouts

Creators can charge for access to bots they publish and earn a share of the revenue those bots generate.

08

Multilingual Support

The interface and many models support French, German, Italian, Korean, Portuguese, Simplified/Traditional Chinese, Spanish, and more.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • One login and one point balance replace several separate chatbot subscriptions if you only use each occasionally.
  • Switching models inside the same conversation makes it easy to see how different models answer the same question.
  • The bot builder lets non-developers wrap a prompt and model into a reusable, shareable bot.
  • Native apps for web, mobile and desktop with synced history.
  • Covers more than text: depending on the bot, you can generate images, video and audio.

Cons

  • Compute points are an abstraction: the same plan buys very different numbers of messages depending on model and conversation length.
  • Point costs grow with context, so long chats on premium models drain your allowance faster than expected.
  • Allowances and plan structures have shifted over time, so a plan's value can change without warning; re-check before paying annually.
  • You get models through Poe's layer, so new provider features (specific tools, newest modes) may arrive late or not at all.
  • Quality of community bots varies widely and many are just a prompt on top of a standard model.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

Yes, Poe has a free tier with limited daily usage. The models included and the limits change over time, so check the app for what you currently get.

How does Poe pricing work?

Paid tiers grant a monthly pool of compute points that every message spends from. Cost per message depends on the model and context length. We could not verify current tier prices today, so see Poe's official pricing page.

Is Poe cheaper than subscribing to ChatGPT and Claude separately?

It can be if you use each lightly. If you use one model heavily, the points may run out sooner than a dedicated plan's limits would, so compare against your actual usage.

Can I build my own bot?

Yes. You can combine a supported model with a custom prompt and publish it as a bot, without coding.

Do unused points roll over?

Historically subscription points have not rolled over, but policies change; confirm on Poe's current pricing page.
