MovableType.ai is a good fit for experts and entrepreneurs who want a complete non-fiction book draft quickly, with marketing material included, and can edit the result. Skip it if you are on a tight budget, write fiction, or want to hand-craft every sentence. You give it one core idea and it builds a proposal, research, an outline for your approval and a first draft over 35,000 words, which the site says takes under 15 minutes. It also generates sales pages, ads and an SEO keyword list, plus audiobook, cover and translation options. Drafts open in Google Docs for editing. Plans run on credits, where one credit is one book generation.

Best for

Who it is for: subject-matter experts, coaches, consultants and entrepreneurs who want a non-fiction book draft and launch materials fast and will edit the output. Who it is not for: budget-conscious writers, fiction authors, or anyone who wants full manual control of the writing.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • A full-length first draft arrives in minutes, which is far faster than writing from scratch.
  • Marketing assets (sales pages, ads, SEO keywords) come in the same package.
  • A 14-day trial lets you test output before paying, though its limits are unclear.
  • Credits roll over, and you can cancel from the account portal.
  • Google Docs editing keeps revision in a familiar tool.
  • Author Persona training aims to keep the voice yours.

Cons

  • Entry pricing is about $199 a month, high for casual users; the yearly plan needs $1,999 upfront.
  • The trial page does not say how many credits you get, so you cannot tell if you can generate a whole book.
  • Each generation spends a credit, so redoing a weak draft uses your allowance.
  • Extra items such as audiobooks cost credits (5 for an audiobook).
  • We found no independent reviews; listings called Movable Type on review sites are for an unrelated CMS.
  • AI drafts still need human fact-checking and editing before publishing.

Frequently asked questions

How much does MovableType.ai cost?

Monthly is $199 for 10 credits, quarterly $549 for 30 credits plus 10 bonus, and yearly $1,999 for 120 credits plus 25 bonus, per the site on 2026-10-19.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, the site offers a 14-day free trial. It does not state how many credits or generations are included.

What is a credit?

One credit is one premium book generation. An audiobook costs 5 credits, and credits roll over.

Can I cancel?

The site says you can cancel any time with one click in the account portal.

Is it the same as the Movable Type CMS?

No. It is an unrelated AI book-writing product that shares a similar name with the older Six Apart CMS.
