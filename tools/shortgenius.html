ShortGenius is a good fit for people who run several faceless or UGC-style short-video channels and want videos made and posted on a schedule; skip it if you only need an occasional single video, because there is no free plan and the cheapest tier is $39 a month. It takes a topic, article or script, writes the copy, generates the video using third-party models, adds voice and captions, and then auto-posts to TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and X. Its credit allowance is small at the entry level, so heavy experimenting with different video models can use a month of credits quickly. A REST API and SDKs exist for teams that want to automate the pipeline.

Best for

Social media managers, agencies and solo creators who run recurring short-form channels and want generation plus scheduled posting in one place. Not for people who want one polished video now and then, anyone who needs a free tier, or teams that mainly want to cut clips from existing long recordings.

Key features

01

Script and topic-to-video generation

Feed it a topic, article, or your own script and it drafts copy and turns it into a short video, using large language models (Claude, GPT, Gemini) for the writing stage.

02

Multi-model video and image generation

Generations can be routed through different underlying engines (Sora, Veo, Luma for video; FLUX and Stability for images), so output style varies depending on which model you pick per project.

03

Voice cloning and UGC avatars

Clone a voice or use a UGC-style avatar to produce faceless or presenter-style short videos without filming anything yourself.

04

Automated multi-platform scheduling

Videos can be queued and auto-posted to TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, and X on a set cadence rather than exported and uploaded manually to each app.

05

In-app editing tools

Trim clips, add captions, swap scenes, replace voices, and apply a brand kit before a video goes out, so the auto-generated draft isn't a black box.

06

Large voice and music libraries

Over 3,000 voice options and 10,000+ music tracks are available to vary the sound of generated videos across a channel.

07

API and SDKs

A REST API with TypeScript/JavaScript and Python SDKs lets developers script bulk video generation and publishing outside the web UI, useful for agencies building their own pipelines on top of it.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Posting is built in: the scheduler cross-posts to five platforms, so you do not need a separate scheduling tool.
  • You can choose among many underlying video, image and voice models instead of one house engine.
  • An API and SDKs make it usable for agencies building their own pipelines.
  • Every plan includes voice cloning, avatars, no watermark and commercial use, so the cheapest tier is not feature-crippled.

Cons

  • No free plan on the pricing page, so you cannot try it on a real project without paying $39.
  • Credits are small on the entry tier (30), and cost per video depends on the model you pick, so spend is hard to predict.
  • Yearly prices are not published despite the '6+ months free' claim.
  • The pricing cards include unlabelled numbers, so the exact per-credit limits are unclear from the page.
  • Output quality depends on third-party models, so results vary by project.

Frequently asked questions

Does ShortGenius have a free plan?

The pricing page shows only four paid monthly plans starting at $39/month and no free tier. Paid plans say 'cancel anytime'. The page's meta text hints at a free start, but no details are given, so not verified.

What do the higher tiers add?

The page lists the same features on every plan (voices, voice cloning, UGC avatars, HD without watermark, cross-posting, commercial use, priority support). Higher tiers add credits (30/60/120/300) and, at the lower tiers, more posts per day.

What does yearly billing cost?

The page says yearly billing gives '6+ months free' but does not show a yearly price. Not verified; check at checkout.

Can I use it only for editing existing footage?

It is built to generate videos from scripts and topics. It has an editor for trimming, captions and scene swaps, but it is not mainly a clip-cutting tool for long recordings.

Is there an API?

Yes, the site documents a REST API with TypeScript and Python SDKs for automating generation and posting.
