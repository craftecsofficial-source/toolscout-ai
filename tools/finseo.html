Finseo suits mid-market brands and agencies that want AI-visibility tracking with visible euro pricing and some action tooling, as long as three engines are enough. Buyers who need every engine from day one should skip the self-serve tiers: they cap you at three models and 50 to 350 prompts, and full coverage means a custom quote.

It runs prompts through AI engines, records mentions, rankings and sentiment against competitors, flags factual errors in answers, and turns gaps into tasks you can export to project tools. Higher tiers add attribution, bot-traffic analytics, an agent for drafting articles and, at Growth, API access.

Best for

Fits SEO and marketing teams at growing companies, and agencies, that want a credit-card purchase and unlimited users. It is NOT for brands needing broad multi-engine coverage on a small budget, regional engines (extra add-ons) or high prompt volumes, nor for teams who only want a cheap tracker with no extras.

Key features

01

Daily LLM monitoring

Finseo runs a defined set of prompts against the major AI engines on a daily schedule and extracts which sources, products, and rankings show up in the responses, functioning like rank tracking for AI answers instead of search results.

02

Competitor and sentiment tracking

Alongside a brand's own visibility, Finseo tracks how named competitors are ranked and described in the same AI responses, and scores the sentiment of how a brand is characterized when it does appear.

03

Query fan-out analysis

Shows how an AI engine breaks a single user query into multiple underlying sub-queries before generating an answer, which is meant to help teams understand why certain content does or doesn't get cited.

04

AEO task checklists

A structured set of optimization tasks tied to AEO best practices, intended to give content and SEO teams a concrete to-do list rather than just a dashboard of scores.

05

AI traffic and attribution analytics

Tracks bot traffic from AI crawlers and attempts to connect AI-engine referrals to actual site traffic and downstream conversions.

06

White-label report builder

Lets agencies and internal teams generate branded visibility reports for stakeholders or clients without manually compiling the underlying data.

07

AI-assisted content generation

Generates content drafts informed by the platform's own prompt-research and visibility data, positioned as a supporting feature rather than a full publishing workflow.

08

API, MCP access, and integrations

Growth and Enterprise tiers include API access, and all tiers include MCP (Model Context Protocol) server access plus a claimed 200+ integrations with tools like Shopify, Salesforce, HubSpot, and Google Analytics.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Public prices for three tiers plus yearly discounts let you budget without a demo.
  • Unlimited users on all plans suits agencies and cross-team use.
  • Tasks, attribution and content generation come in at mid tiers rather than only at enterprise.
  • MCP access is included even on Starter.

Cons

  • Only 3 models per lower-tier plan (chosen from 10); regional engines are paid add-ons and all 23 models need Enterprise.
  • Prompt caps of 50/150/350 get used up quickly across markets or product lines.
  • The pricing FAQ contradicts the table (for example Business at 100 vs 150 prompts), so confirm limits before buying.
  • Prices are in euros excluding VAT; the stated 15% yearly discount differs slightly from the listed prices.
  • I found no verifiable independent review data (G2/Capterra) today. Note: the homepage did not show pricing, which was taken from the pricing page.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Finseo cost?

Starter EUR 84/mo, Business EUR 210/mo, Growth EUR 479/mo on monthly billing, ex-VAT. Yearly billing brings them to EUR 70, 175 and 399 per month. Enterprise is custom.

How many AI models can I track?

Three of your choice from ten on Starter, Business and Growth. Enterprise covers all 23, including regional engines.

Is there a free trial?

The pricing page shows a 7-day free trial for Starter; it states none for the other plans.

How many prompts do I get?

50 on Starter, 150 on Business and 350 on Growth per the comparison table, though an FAQ on the same page gives different numbers for some plans, so verify.

Which plan includes API access?

API access is listed from Growth upward; MCP access is on every plan.
