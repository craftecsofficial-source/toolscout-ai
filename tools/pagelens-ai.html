PageLens.ai is a 2026-era AEO startup built around the idea that buyer research has moved from ten blue links to one AI-generated answer. It runs a defined set of buyer prompts against every major model daily and shows brands exactly how ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity describe them, where they rank against named competitors, and which sources those models trust enough to cite. The product is run hands-on by its founders, who take onboarding calls directly, reflecting its early-stage, self-serve-first positioning versus more established, funded competitors like Profound or Scrunch AI.

Where most AEO tools stop at a dashboard, PageLens closes the loop by writing and publishing the actual missing content. Its content engine reads six live source types — Reddit and forum threads, G2 and Capterra listings, competitor pages, news and launches, search-demand data, and existing AI answers — compiles a brief, and ships a finished page to a CNAME'd /feed subdirectory on the customer's own domain rather than a PageLens-hosted page, so the resulting SEO equity and URLs belong to the brand and are portable if they ever leave.

Best for

PageLens.ai fits small to mid-size marketing teams and agencies who want AI-visibility tracking paired with an actual content-production engine, rather than a dashboard they then have to act on themselves — particularly teams comfortable trusting a newer, self-serve startup over a funded incumbent. It's a reasonable entry point for anyone who wants to run a free audit first and scale into paid tracking without a sales call. Larger enterprises that need vendor-verified reviews, security paperwork already in place, or a multi-year track record should treat PageLens as one to watch rather than a safe default today, and may be better served by Profound, Scrunch AI or AthenaHQ in the meantime.

Key features

01

AI visibility audit

A free, roughly 30-second scan of any domain that shows how ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity currently describe the brand and where it ranks against named competitors, with no signup required.

02

Daily multi-engine prompt tracking

Runs a defined set of buyer prompts against every major model daily — ChatGPT on Monitor; adds Google AI Mode and Perplexity on Optimize; adds Claude, Gemini, Grok and Copilot on Growth and Enterprise — and logs the verbatim answers.

03

Share of voice dashboard

Breaks down how often each named competitor is recommended versus the brand across the full prompt set, refreshed over a rolling 30-day window.

04

Sentiment tracking

Extracts the specific adjectives models use about a brand ('reliable,' 'pricey,' 'hard to set up') and buckets them positive, neutral or negative.

05

Source and citation tracking

Traces which specific pages — Reddit threads, G2 listings, competitor blogs, the brand's own site — a model pulled from to build its answer, showing exactly who is winning each citation.

06

Content engine

Reads six live source types (Reddit/forums, G2 & Capterra, competitor pages, news, search-demand data, existing AI answers), compiles a brief, drafts a page, and — after review — publishes it.

07

Publish to your own domain

Content ships to a CNAME'd /feed subdirectory on the customer's own domain rather than a PageLens-hosted page, so the resulting SEO equity and URLs belong to the brand and are portable if they leave.

08

Agent readiness scan

A separate free diagnostic tool that checks whether a site's technical setup (robots.txt, structured data, crawlability) is friendly to AI crawlers and agents.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The content engine is a genuine differentiator: instead of leaving teams with a dashboard and a to-do list, it drafts and ships the actual page to the brand's own domain, closing the loop from insight to citation.
  • Publishing to a CNAME'd /feed subdirectory on the customer's own domain, rather than a PageLens-hosted page, means the resulting SEO equity is portable and belongs to the brand permanently.
  • Entry pricing is transparent and self-serve: $49/mo for daily ChatGPT tracking with no mandatory sales call, unusually accessible for the AEO category.
  • The free, no-signup AI visibility audit is a low-friction way to see real findings about a brand before paying anything.
  • The agency plan lets shops manage unlimited client sites on one workspace with per-client configuration, priced from the same $49/mo floor as a single-brand plan.
  • An explicit 'evidence over dashboards' philosophy runs through the product — every number is built to trace back to a specific cited source rather than an opaque score.

Cons

  • As a brand-new product, PageLens has no visible listing or review history on G2 or Capterra yet, so there's no independent, cross-checkable satisfaction data beyond the testimonials on its own site.
  • Testimonials published on pagelens.ai use first-name-plus-last-initial format with no company names or logos, making them harder to independently verify than a typical G2 review.
  • There's a steep jump from the $199/mo Optimize (tracking-only) tier to the $699/mo Growth tier that adds the content engine, with no mid-priced option in between.
  • Engine coverage is tiered: the entry Monitor plan only tracks ChatGPT, so teams wanting Claude, Gemini or Perplexity coverage from day one need at least the Optimize plan.
  • Content is refreshed and re-measured only monthly on the Growth plan, which may be too slow a cadence for fast-moving competitive categories.
  • The company is young and small, taking demo calls personally through its founders, so long-term platform stability and roadmap depth are unproven relative to funded competitors like Profound or Scrunch AI.

What reviewers say

PageLens.ai is new enough that it has no independent listing on G2 or Capterra to cross-check yet, so the only public sentiment available is the handful of testimonials published on its own marketing site, which read positively — citing gaps found across ChatGPT and Gemini, faster re-citation after content updates, and a useful share-of-voice view — but should be read as vendor-selected rather than independently verified. Prospective buyers should treat early adoption here as a bet on a fast-moving young product rather than a peer-reviewed purchase.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan for PageLens.ai?

There's no ongoing free subscription tier, but PageLens offers a free, ~30-second AI visibility audit for any domain with no signup, plus a free Agent Readiness Scan tool — paid plans start at $49/mo for daily ChatGPT tracking.

How much does PageLens.ai cost?

Monitor is $49/mo (ChatGPT only), Optimize is $199/mo (adds Google AI Mode and Perplexity), Growth is $699/mo (adds Claude, Gemini, Grok and Copilot plus done-for-you content publishing), and Enterprise is custom-priced; annual billing saves about 8%.

What makes PageLens different from a typical AEO tracking tool?

Most AEO tools stop at a dashboard. PageLens's Growth plan also researches, drafts and publishes the actual missing content to a /feed subdirectory on the customer's own domain, then re-measures whether it earned a citation — closing the loop rather than leaving that work to the customer.

Which AI engines does PageLens.ai track?

ChatGPT on the entry Monitor plan; Optimize adds Google AI Mode and Perplexity; Growth and Enterprise add Claude, Gemini, Grok and Microsoft Copilot for full seven-engine coverage.

Does the content PageLens writes stay on my own site?

Yes — content publishes to a CNAME'd /feed subdirectory on the customer's own domain rather than a PageLens-hosted page, so the URLs, traffic and SEO equity belong to the brand and are portable if they ever switch tools.

Is PageLens.ai a good fit for agencies?

Yes, PageLens has a dedicated agency plan starting at $49/mo that supports unlimited client sites on one workspace with per-client prompt, model and cadence configuration plus multi-client reporting.
