Ava is worth a look for B2B sales leaders who want an AI to run most top-of-funnel outbound and are fine buying through a sales call. Skip it if you want transparent self-serve pricing, tight manual control of every message, or a tool for inbound or account management.

Ava finds and prioritizes leads from Artisan's 250M+ contact database and 22+ data sources using signals such as funding rounds and leadership hires. She runs email and social sequences, queues calls in a native dialer, tests message variants, handles replies and books meetings on reps' calendars. Escalation rules, an approval mode for reviewing drafted replies, and do-not-contact lists give you some control; two-way sync works with Salesforce and HubSpot.

Best for

B2B sales and RevOps leaders with a defined ideal customer profile who want to scale outbound without hiring more SDRs, and who will monitor messaging and deliverability. It is not for teams needing a published price, fine-grained manual control, very small outbound volumes, or CRMs other than Salesforce and HubSpot (other integrations unconfirmed).

Key features

01

Automated Prospecting

Ava sources and researches leads matching a company's ideal customer profile, pulling firmographic and contact data automatically.

02

AI-Written Outreach

Generates personalized email copy for each prospect based on research signals rather than templated mail-merge blasts.

03

Multi-Step Follow-Up Sequences

Automatically follows up with non-responders and adapts messaging based on prior engagement.

04

Deliverability Infrastructure

Includes mailbox warmup and sending-domain management aimed at keeping outbound emails out of spam folders.

05

CRM & Tool Integrations

Connects with common CRMs and sales tools so activity and booked meetings sync automatically.

06

Campaign Analytics

Provides reporting on send volume, reply rates, and meetings booked to track outbound performance.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Prospecting data, copywriting, multi-channel sequencing, reply handling and meeting booking sit in one product
  • Approval mode, escalation rules and exclusion lists let you keep humans in the loop
  • Plans are sized by lead volume, and the pricing page claims no hidden line items
  • Native Salesforce and HubSpot two-way sync

Cons

  • No dollar figures are published; you must talk to sales to budget
  • Plan volumes (about 2,500 or 6,000 contacted leads per month) may be too much or too little, and the dialer costs extra
  • Autonomous outreach can sound generic or risk brand and compliance problems without review
  • Reply rates and deliverability results are not independently verified
  • Only Salesforce and HubSpot are named for CRM sync

Frequently asked questions

How much does Ava cost?

Artisan publishes no prices. Plans are Team, Scale and Enterprise, scoped by lead volume, mailboxes and dialer seats. Get a written quote including add-ons.

Can I review messages before they go out?

Artisan describes an approval mode where Ava drafts replies for review, plus escalation rules for handing off to a human. Confirm how it applies to first-touch emails.

Which CRMs does it work with?

Salesforce and HubSpot with two-way sync are named. Ask about any other CRM you use.

Does it make phone calls?

It queues call steps in a native dialer for your reps; the AI dialer is a per-seat add-on. Whether calls are placed autonomously is unconfirmed.

Will it replace my SDRs?

Artisan pitches it as running alongside your team, handing qualified meetings to reps. Treat replacement claims with caution and pilot first.
