Profound is a strong choice for larger marketing teams that want deep, multi-engine tracking of how AI answer engines mention and cite their brand, with agents to act on the findings. Small businesses, freelancers and anyone wanting a clear self-serve price should skip it: the live pricing page shows only a free trial and a custom Enterprise package. The product monitors brand mentions, citations and sentiment, estimates what users ask AI tools (Prompt Volumes), tracks AI crawler and referral traffic to your own domains (Agent Analytics), and offers credit-metered agents, a context manager, projects and spreadsheet-style bulk runs for content work. The homepage names Plaid, MongoDB, Ramp and Zoom as customers and claims a third of the Fortune 100 uses its enterprise features; we did not verify those claims independently.

Best for

Good for enterprise and growth-stage marketing and SEO teams that need many prompts, several engines, exports, API and SSO. Not for solo operators or small teams needing a published monthly price, and not for anyone who only wants a simple dashboard.

Key features

01

Answer Engine Insights

Tracks brand and competitor visibility, citations and sentiment across AI engines; Enterprise lists up to 9 engines including ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, DeepSeek and Google AI Overviews and AI Mode.

02

Prompt Volumes

Research on what people ask AI engines, like keyword research for AI prompts. The trial includes 5 searches.

03

Agents and Context Manager

AI agents that generate or update content; each run consumes credits based on complexity and shows an estimate first.

04

Agent Analytics

Shows AI crawler visits and AI referral traffic; unlimited domains on listed plans.

05

Projects and Profound Sheets

Workspaces for organizing campaigns and running agents across many items at once.

06

ChatGPT Shopping tracking

Brand visibility within ChatGPT Shopping, Enterprise only.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Enterprise tier covers up to 9 engines with unlimited seats and all-time data history, which suits teams reporting AI visibility trends over time.
  • Monitoring and content agents live in one platform, shortening the path from a visibility gap to a content fix.
  • Security items (SSO/SAML, SOC 2) are listed for Enterprise, which procurement teams usually require.

Cons

  • The Starter ($99) and Growth ($399) plans from earlier listings are not on today's pricing page, so we cannot confirm they still exist.
  • The free trial is limited to 7 days of daily tracking on a preset prompt set, which makes a fair evaluation of your own market hard.
  • Credit-metered agents plus overage options mean spend depends on usage.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan for Profound?

There is a free trial (50 prompts tracked daily for 7 days on three engines), not an ongoing free plan.

How much does Profound cost?

Enterprise is custom-quoted. The Agency Growth plan price is not shown, and earlier Starter ($99/mo) and Growth ($399/mo) tiers are no longer on the pricing page; ask sales to confirm.

Which AI engines does it track?

The trial covers ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews; Enterprise lists up to 9 including Perplexity, Claude, Copilot, DeepSeek and Google AI Mode.

Can I export data or use an API?

Per the pricing page, CSV/JSON export and API access are Enterprise features, not part of the trial.
