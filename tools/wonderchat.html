Wonderchat suits SaaS, ecommerce and service businesses that want a chatbot answering strictly from their own site and documents, with a person stepping in when it is unsure. Skip it if you want one fixed low price for a small site, since paid plans start above $30 a month and key features (voice, Teams, Zendesk sync) only arrive on Basic or Scale. You point it at web pages and files, it answers on your site and messaging channels, flags questions it could not answer so you can fix the content, and includes a shared inbox for handoffs.

Best for

Good for support and sales teams that have decent documentation, want grounded answers and handoff, and need helpdesk or CRM links. NOT for hobbyists wanting a free bot at scale (Free is capped at 50 AI replies a month), teams that want pure per-resolution billing, or those needing heavy voice use without testing it first; Chatling or Kommunicate may suit lighter or more channel-led needs.

Key features

01

Grounded answers

The agent answers from your pages and files and cites your data, with a model of your choice.

02

Handoff and inbox

Unsure cases go to a teammate with priority and a drafted reply; includes live takeover and a shared inbox.

03

Review inbox

Flags unanswered questions and suggests knowledge-base articles so gaps get fixed.

04

Channels and integrations

Website, WhatsApp, Slack, Teams, Messenger, Telegram, Discord, email, SMS and voice, plus Zendesk, Freshdesk, HubSpot, Salesforce and more depending on plan.

05

Sales and internal agents

A sales agent for visitors and an internal Slack agent for staff questions.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Answers are grounded in your own content and the review inbox shows which questions failed, so accuracy can be improved over time.
  • A permanent free plan with real features lets you test on your own site before paying.
  • Unlimited team seats on every paid plan, so headcount does not raise the bill.
  • Broad channel and integration list, with helpdesk and CRM syncing on mid tiers.
  • Plan prices are public, with enterprise security items (SSO, SOC 2 report, DPA) clearly placed on the top tier.

Cons

  • Plan cards and the FAQ disagree on pricing display: cards show yearly-billed figures while the FAQ gives $39/$149/$499 for monthly billing, so check at checkout.
  • AI replies are credit-limited and the page warns the agent stops answering customers if credits run out; no overage or top-up price is listed.
  • Free is only 50 AI replies a month, and the cheapest paid plan covers a single agent.
  • Voice, frontier models, Teams, API/MCP and Zendesk sync need Basic ($125/mo) or higher; unlimited ticket sync and routing rules need Scale.
  • The site makes inconsistent claims (40+ languages in one place, 1,000+ in another; 'over 5000 integrations' against a much shorter list), and its resolution-rate and rating figures are vendor-reported.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

Yes: 50 AI replies and up to 20 resolutions a month, 1 agent, 2 seats, fast models only, no card.

What does it cost per month?

Plan cards show Starter $32.50, Basic $125, Scale $417 with yearly billing; the FAQ gives $39, $149 and $499 for monthly billing. Confirm at checkout.

What happens if I run out of credits?

You get an email at 80% use; if credits are not topped up the agent stops replying to customers. Overage prices are not listed.

Which plan has voice and the Zendesk integration?

Voice chat, API/MCP and Zendesk/Freshdesk sync (up to 50 tickets) start on Basic; unlimited tickets need Scale.

How many languages does it support?

The site says 40+ in one place and 1,000+ in another, so test your languages in the free plan.
