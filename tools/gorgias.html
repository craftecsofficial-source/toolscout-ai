Great for Shopify-style ecommerce brands that want agents to handle refunds, cancellations and order lookups without leaving the inbox; skip it if you are not selling through a store platform, or if you need a fixed monthly bill. Its helpdesk is priced by ticket volume rather than seats, and the AI Agent adds a per-resolution charge on top.

Gorgias combines a shared inbox (email, chat, SMS, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook) with native integrations for Shopify, BigCommerce, Magento and WooCommerce. Agents can see customer and order data in the ticket and act on it. The AI Agent answers common ecommerce questions, can recommend products to shoppers, and hands off to humans with context. Voice and SMS can be added on any plan. Figures like 17,000+ brands or 40% of Shopify brands using a helpdesk are Gorgias's own claims, which we did not verify.

Best for

Gorgias is for ecommerce brands, especially on Shopify, that want order actions and AI resolution inside one helpdesk. It is not for B2B or SaaS support teams, non-store businesses, or very cost-sensitive stores that need a single flat, predictable monthly price.

Key features

01

AI Agent

Handles order status, returns, shipping and similar questions using store data, follows your tone and policies, and escalates to a human with context. Charged only when it resolves a conversation.

Shopping assistance

Gorgias says the AI engages shoppers proactively with product recommendations and sales conversations.

03

Unified inbox

Email, chat, SMS, WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook in one place, with routing and assignment.

04

Ecommerce integrations

Native Shopify, BigCommerce, Magento and WooCommerce connections; preferred integrations include Loop Returns, Yotpo, Recharge, Bloomreach and Attentive.

05

Voice and SMS

Add-on channels on any plan, with pricing that rises with call and text volume.

06

Revenue attribution and AI coaching

Reporting that ties support to revenue, plus real-time tips for human agents.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The helpdesk is never priced per agent per Gorgias, so growing a team does not raise seat costs.
  • Agents can view and act on store data inside the ticket, which shortens handling for refunds, cancellations and order lookups.
  • AI Agent is on every plan and charged only when it resolves a conversation, so unused AI costs nothing.
  • Wide ecommerce integration coverage (Shopify, BigCommerce, Magento, WooCommerce plus a large app marketplace).
  • Voice and SMS can live in the same system rather than separate tools.

Cons

  • Three cost layers stack: ticket-tier plan, per-resolution AI fees, and Voice/SMS usage; a sale-day spike can raise all three at once.
  • Plan prices and per-resolution rates were not readable on the live pricing page today; verify them directly before you commit.
  • Overage ticket fees existed in earlier listings; current rates unverified.
  • Ecommerce specialization is a limit: little value outside store-based businesses.
  • Vendor success numbers (automation rates, ROI) are marketing claims, not independent results.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Gorgias cost?

Gorgias says the helpdesk scales from 50 to 5,000 tickets a month and is never priced per agent. Earlier listings showed $10/mo (Starter) up to $900/mo (Advanced), but we could not confirm those figures on the live page today.

Does Gorgias charge per agent?

No, per its pricing page the helpdesk is never priced per agent.

How is the AI Agent billed?

It is on every plan and you pay only when it resolves a conversation. The per-resolution rate was not visible today; earlier listings put it near $0.90-$1.00.

Is there a free plan?

We could not verify a free plan or trial from the pages we read; ask Gorgias or check the demo/signup flow.

Which platforms does it support?

Shopify, BigCommerce, Magento and WooCommerce natively, with Shopify the focus.

How does it compare with Zendesk?

Gorgias is ecommerce-specific and ticket-priced; Zendesk is a general helpdesk priced mostly per seat, better for non-store support operations.
