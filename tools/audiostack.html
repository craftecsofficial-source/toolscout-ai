AudioStack is a strong fit for agencies, adtech platforms and publishers that need to produce large volumes of audio ads or long-form audio through one vendor, and a poor fit for solo creators or small teams who want to sign up and pay by card. It does not publish prices, so you cannot evaluate cost without a sales conversation. The product sits on top of voice, music and sound models from 17 providers and turns a brief or raw text into finished audio. It offers a self-serve console (InstantCreative), variant generation tied to a single VAST tag (DynamicCreative), an embeddable API that returns white-labelled audio (CreativeEngine), and long-form text-to-audio (StoryEngine). The company names customers such as Bloomberg, Omnicom Media Group, Publicis Groupe, Dentsu, Prisa and PocketFM, and claims 2,600+ voices and 1,000+ sonic assets. It is operated by Aflorithmic Labs Ltd.

Best for

Agencies, adtech platforms, broadcasters and publishers producing audio ads, personalized ad variants or serialized audio at scale, and product teams who want to embed white-labelled audio generation. Not for solo creators, podcasters or small teams wanting transparent self-serve pricing, nor for anyone who just needs a single text-to-speech voice for occasional use.

Key features

01

InstantCreative

A self-serve console where non-technical team members type a campaign brief and receive broadcast-ready audio in minutes, no engineering required.

02

DynamicCreative

Generates thousands of localized or personalized variants from a single audio asset, delivered through one VAST tag for programmatic ad campaigns.

03

CreativeEngine

An embeddable API that lets other platforms call AudioStack directly and receive finished, white-labelled audio inside their own product.

04

StoryEngine

Converts raw text into broadcast-grade long-form audio, used for audiobooks and serialized audio fiction at scale.

05

Multi-Provider Voice Orchestration

Aggregates 2,600+ voices and 1,000+ sonic assets across 17 providers including ElevenLabs, OpenAI, Google, and Amazon Polly, so users always have a matching voice available.

06

Custom Voice Cloning & Speech-to-Speech

Supports branded custom voice clones and speech-to-speech conversion for personalized, on-brand audio content.

07

Enterprise Compliance

Holds SOC2, GDPR compliance, and Content Authenticity Initiative (CAI) alignment, plus IAB membership relevant to ad-industry buyers.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Named enterprise customers (Bloomberg, Omnicom, Publicis, Dentsu, Prisa, PocketFM) suggest it is used in real production, not just demos, though these are the vendor's own claims.
  • Vendor-reported case study: 1,500+ hyperlocal ads for a US home improvement retailer in 3 days, and a claim of production up to 90% faster than traditional methods; treat as marketing figures.
  • Orchestrating 17 voice and music providers means you are not locked into a single voice vendor.
  • Offers both a self-serve console and an embeddable API, so marketing and engineering teams can use the same engine.
  • DynamicCreative's single VAST tag approach is aimed squarely at programmatic audio ad delivery, which generic TTS tools do not cover.

Cons

  • No public pricing; you must book a demo to learn cost, which slows evaluation for small teams.
  • Being an aggregator, voice quality and language coverage depend on the underlying provider, and may vary by language.
  • Credit-based billing (as reported by past users) can be hard to translate into a per-asset cost for client billing.
  • We could not confirm on the live site whether a free trial still exists or whether trial output is watermarked, so evaluate this in the demo.
  • SDK support was reported as limited to JavaScript and Python; verify against current docs if you use another language.
