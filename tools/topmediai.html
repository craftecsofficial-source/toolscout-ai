TopMediai suits faceless creators and small teams who want video, music and voiceover from one credit pool and do not need best-in-class output in any single area. Skip it if you need top-tier video realism, studio-grade voice cloning, or a plan whose cost per clip you can predict in advance. It is a web and mobile AI studio that aggregates third-party video models (the site lists Seedance 2.5, Veo 3.1, Kling, Vidu and PixVerse) alongside its own music generator and song covers, text-to-speech (3,200+ voices, 190+ languages claimed), voice cloning, lip sync, video translation and a developer API. All tools draw from one shared credit balance, with plan caps per feature.

Best for

Faceless-channel creators, social marketers and small agencies who want quick video, background music and voiceovers from one subscription and accept mid-tier quality in exchange for breadth. Not for: studios needing the best video or voice quality, teams who need fixed, predictable per-asset pricing, or users who want deep editing (it is a generator, not a full editor).

Key features

01

Multi-Model Video Generation

Text-to-video and image-to-video generation with a choice of underlying models including Sora 2, Veo 3.1, Kling AI, and Seedance 2.5.

02

AI Music Generator & AI Cover

Creates original songs from prompts and generates AI covers using a library of over 10,000 voice models, with duet and pitch controls.

03

Text-to-Speech

Converts scripts into natural voiceovers using a library of 3,200+ voices spanning 190+ languages and accents.

04

AI Voice Cloning

Replicates a person's tone, emotion, and speech patterns from a few uploaded audio samples to build a custom voice.

05

AI Video Translator / Dubbing

Translates and dubs videos into other languages to reach multilingual audiences.

06

AI Lip Sync

Matches mouth movement to dialogue, songs, or translated voiceovers for more natural-looking dubbed content.

07

Singing Photo Maker / Music Video Maker

Turns portraits or character images into singing video moments, or syncs visuals to a generated song.

08

Creation Hub

Centralized workspace that keeps generated videos, voices, music, and projects organized together.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • One dashboard and one credit pool for video, music, speech, voice cloning and dubbing, which can replace several separate subscriptions.
  • Several current video models (Seedance 2.5, Veo 3.1, Kling, Vidu, PixVerse) selectable without separate sign-ups.
  • Wide voice coverage (3,200+ voices, 190+ languages claimed) is useful for localization and dubbing.
  • Web, mobile and API access suit both casual creators and developers.
  • Free credits and a free start make testing low-risk.

Cons

  • Plan prices were not visible on the live pricing page when checked, and earlier tiers (Starter/Creator/Pro) no longer match the 1-Week/1-Month/Lifetime names shown now, so older price lists you find elsewhere may be stale.
  • A 'Lifetime' plan with fixed usage caps needs careful reading of what the caps and term actually are before buying.
  • As an aggregator, quality and credit cost depend on which underlying model you pick, so spend is hard to predict.
  • Per-feature caps (for example only 3 file-based voice clones on weekly and monthly plans) are tight for voice-heavy work.
  • Generalist breadth means specialist tools can beat it in any one category.
  • Promotions (a Halloween discount code was shown) make the headline price a moving target.

Frequently asked questions

What does TopMediai cost?

The live pricing page lists 1-Week, 1-Month and Lifetime plans, but the dollar amounts did not load when checked on 2026-10-27, so we cannot confirm figures. Look at the live page before buying.

Which video models can I use?

The site lists Seedance 2.5, Veo 3.1, Kling, Vidu and PixVerse. Model availability and credit cost per model can change.

Do video, music and voice share one balance?

Yes, the site says all plans give credits usable across video, music and voice tools, with per-feature caps shown in the plan comparison.

Can I clone my voice?

Yes, from uploaded audio samples, but weekly and monthly plans cap file-based clones at 3 per the comparison table.

Is there an API?

The site mentions an API for music, covers, text-to-speech, voice cloning and voice changing, plus an MCP option.
