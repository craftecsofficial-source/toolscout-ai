Salesloft is a good fit for established B2B sales organizations that want one vendor for outbound cadences, call analysis and forecasting, and are happy to buy through a sales process. Solo founders, small teams and anyone who wants to see a price before talking to a rep should skip it. It started as a sequencing tool and now sells itself as an AI revenue system; its site lists Cadence, Rhythm, Conversation Intelligence, Deals, Inspect, Analytics, Signals and AI Agents, plus Clari Forecast. Salesloft and Clari announced a merger agreement on August 7, 2025, with Clari's CEO set to lead the combined company; we could not confirm from the sites how the products have been repackaged since.

Best for

Who it is for: mid-market and enterprise B2B sales teams with dedicated SDRs, AEs and RevOps that want engagement, conversation intelligence and forecasting together. Who it is not for: freelancers, small startups or teams who need public pricing and self-serve signup, or teams that only want cold-email sending at low cost.

Key features

01

Cadence

Multi-step sequences that mix email, calls and other touches for outbound prospecting.

02

Rhythm

A prioritized queue of the next actions for each rep, built from engagement and deal signals.

03

Conversation Intelligence

Records and analyzes sales conversations for coaching and deal context.

04

Deals and Inspect

Deal tracking for account teams and pipeline inspection for leaders.

05

Clari Forecast

Forecasting product from Clari, listed on Salesloft's site under revenue-leader tools.

06

Signals and AI Agents

A layer that collects revenue signals across tools and agents that trigger follow-up actions. We did not verify how mature these are.

07

Services

Consulting, customer education, community and office hours are listed as support options.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • One portfolio spans prospecting, conversation analysis, deal management and forecasting, so fewer separate vendors to integrate
  • Rhythm turns many signals into one daily list, which can reduce rep guesswork
  • Adding Clari Forecast gives leaders a dedicated forecasting product
  • Services such as consulting and education are available for rollout

Cons

  • Pricing is hidden, so you cannot compare cost with tools like Apollo or Outreach without a quote
  • The merger with Clari was announced in 2025 and the sites do not spell out packaging, so expect ambiguity on which products and logins you will get
  • Full value likely needs RevOps time to configure and maintain
  • Overkill for teams without a defined SDR and AE motion
  • We could not verify claims about AI Agents' real-world performance

Frequently asked questions

How much does Salesloft cost?

Salesloft does not publish prices. The pricing page directs you to talk to sales, so we could not verify any per-seat figure.

Is Salesloft owned by Clari now?

The two companies announced a merger agreement on August 7, 2025, with Clari's CEO leading the combined company. Salesloft's site now lists Clari products, but we did not verify the final legal structure.

Does it include forecasting and conversation intelligence?

Both appear on the site (Clari Forecast and Conversation Intelligence). Which plan includes which product is not public.

Is there a free trial?

We found no free plan or self-serve trial on the site; the calls to action are sales demos.

Who should consider a cheaper alternative?

Small teams that mainly need contact data and sequences should look at Apollo.io, which sells self-serve.
