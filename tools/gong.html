Gong is a strong fit for mid-sized and large B2B sales organizations with managers who coach and a forecast process to improve; small teams, or anyone who needs transparent self-serve pricing, should skip it or at least expect a sales conversation first. At its core it records and transcribes customer calls, meetings, and emails, then analyzes them to show what was said, which deals look risky, and how reps behave.

Over time Gong has expanded beyond conversation intelligence. Its site now lists separate products for sales engagement, forecasting, and enablement, plus AI agents that can handle follow-ups, pipeline edits, and forecast corrections. A shared data layer ties these together, so it works best when a team adopts several parts rather than only call recording.

Pricing is not published. Gong's pricing page says licenses are per user with an added platform fee, and that integrations cost nothing extra. Everything else comes through a custom proposal, so we could not verify any price figures, plan names, or contract lengths.

Best for

Gong suits B2B sales, RevOps, and customer-success leaders at mid-market and enterprise companies who run regular deal reviews and coaching and want recorded conversations to drive them. It is NOT for solo sellers or very small teams, buyers who need a listed price or free trial to decide, or teams that only want a cheap call recorder or contact database.

Conversation capture and transcription

Gong Forecast combines deal signals with pipeline data for centralized forecasting and analytics.

04

Sales engagement

Gong Engage helps prioritize and personalize outreach using the captured customer context.

05

Enablement and coaching

Gong Enable builds training and coaching around real customer conversations.

06

AI agents

Agents act on revenue data, for example editing pipeline fields or correcting forecasts.

07

Integrations

Connects to CRMs and the surrounding sales stack; Gong states integrations are free.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Covers the whole revenue workflow (capture, forecast, engage, enable) so fewer separate tools are needed
  • Coaching is based on recorded customer conversations, which gives managers evidence instead of anecdotes
  • Large installed base and an integration/partner ecosystem reduce rollout surprises
  • AI agents can take routine actions like follow-ups and pipeline edits, saving rep admin time

Cons

  • No public pricing at all: expect a quote process, and the per-user plus platform-fee structure means small teams pay proportionally more
  • Value depends on process maturity; without regular coaching and forecast reviews the data goes unused
  • Configuring trackers, scorecards, and dashboards takes ongoing admin effort
  • Call recording needs consent and notification setup that differs by region
  • Buying the wider suite (engage, forecast, enable) likely adds cost; we could not verify bundle pricing

Frequently asked questions

How much does Gong cost?

Gong does not publish prices. Its pricing page says licenses are per user with a platform fee, and you get a custom proposal by submitting a form. Treat any figure you see elsewhere as unverified.

Is there a free plan or trial?

We could not find a free plan or self-serve trial on Gong's pricing page; it points to a custom proposal. Ask sales whether a pilot is available.

Is Gong only call recording?

No. Besides conversation intelligence, Gong lists products for forecasting, sales engagement, and enablement, plus AI agents.

How does Gong compare with Salesloft or Outreach?

Gong started from conversation analysis, while Salesloft and Outreach started from outbound sequencing and execution. The products now overlap, so compare the specific modules you need.

Do I have to worry about call-recording laws?

Yes. Consent rules differ by country and state, so set up recording notices and consent workflows with your legal team before rollout.
