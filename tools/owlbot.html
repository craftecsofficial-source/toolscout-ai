Owlbot is a good choice for solo founders, small businesses and agencies that want a cheap, quick FAQ and lead-capture chatbot trained on their own site or documents. Skip it if you need a bot that takes actions such as refunds or order lookups, a free plan to test first, or message volume above a few thousand a month. You point it at a website or upload PDF, Word and text files, pick one of 12 language models from OpenAI, Anthropic and Mistral, and deploy the bot as a website widget, in WhatsApp, Slack or Discord, or through an API. It cites its sources, supports 95 languages, captures leads, and keeps analytics and conversation history. Paid plans run $19, $39 and $59 per month with hard monthly message caps of 2,000, 5,000 and 10,000, and function calling is only on the top plan. The sign-up page says no credit card is needed to start, and the site's FAQ section showed a rendering glitch where the product name should be, a small sign of rough edges.

Best for

Solo founders, small teams and agencies serving small clients who need an inexpensive document-trained FAQ bot across a website and WhatsApp, Slack or Discord. Not for: teams needing automated actions or workflows, businesses with high conversation volume, or buyers who want a permanent free tier or verified third-party reviews first.

Key features

01

Multi-source knowledge ingestion

Scrapes websites and accepts PDF, Word, and plain-text uploads to build the bot's knowledge base, with a stated 500k-word cap on the Starter plan rising to 2.5M words on paid tiers above it.

02

Multi-channel deployment

The same trained bot can be embedded as a website widget, connected to WhatsApp, added to Slack or Discord, or called directly through a REST API — useful if support happens across more than just your site.

03

Model choice

12 selectable underlying LLMs across OpenAI, Anthropic, and Mistral, switchable per bot, which lets you trade off cost and quality without re-training the knowledge base.

04

Function calling

Enterprise-tier function calling lets the bot invoke external actions rather than only answering from documents, though this is far less developed than DocsBot AI's dedicated AI Actions and MCP layer.

05

Lead capture mid-conversation

The bot can prompt for email, phone number, and context partway through a chat rather than only at the end, aimed at converting support conversations into sales leads.

06

Source-cited answers

Responses reference the source document/page they were drawn from, which helps with trust and makes it easier to spot when the bot is answering from stale content.

07

95-language support

Automatic language detection lets one trained bot serve a multilingual customer base without separate configuration per language.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Very low entry price at $19/month, among the cheapest paid options in the category.
  • Fast setup: scrape a site or upload files and the bot is trained in minutes.
  • Works on WhatsApp, Slack and Discord as well as a website widget, not just one channel.
  • 12 selectable models from OpenAI, Anthropic and Mistral, switchable per bot.
  • Cites sources and captures leads, with white label on Small Business and above.

Cons

  • Hard monthly message caps (2,000 to 10,000) and no stated overage option limit it to low-traffic use.
  • No free tier, so you pay before judging answer quality.
  • Function calling is limited to the $59 plan and needs custom development; there is no built-in actions layer.
  • We could not find G2, Capterra or Trustpilot reviews to verify uptime or accuracy claims.
  • It mainly packages third-party language models, so value comes from price and convenience rather than unique technology.
  • Marketing claims such as a 90% resolution rate are unaudited.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

No free tier is listed. The site says no credit card is required to start and you can cancel anytime; confirm trial terms at sign-up.

Which channels does it support?

Website widget, WhatsApp, Slack, Discord and an API (API access is on Small Business and above).

What happens when I hit the message cap?

Caps are 2,000, 5,000 and 10,000 messages per month by plan. The site does not describe overage pricing, so ask support or move up a tier.

Can the bot take actions like processing refunds?

Only through function calling on the $59 Enterprise plan, which needs your own API work. There is no built-in actions builder.

How big can the knowledge base be?

500k words on Starter and 2.5M words on Small Business and Enterprise.
