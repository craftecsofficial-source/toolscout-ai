Blaze suits small businesses and solo founders who want one tool to plan and publish content, and who might later hand the whole job to a team; skip it if you need deep specialist SEO, ad or design tooling. The software side centers on a content workflow with approval before publishing, plus SEO and AI-answer (AEO) content. The pricing page lists the broader funnel pieces, ads, landing pages, reviews and lead follow-up, under the $999/month managed plan rather than the $79 and $149 self-serve tiers, so check what your tier includes. Self-serve plans run on monthly generation credits: a social post costs 1, an email or blog 3, an AI video 15.

Best for

Small businesses, local service firms and solo founders wanting one marketing system, with an option to outsource. Not for agencies managing many brands (each brand needs its own workspace), heavy daily multi-channel posters on Starter, or teams wanting best-in-class SEO research or ad bidding.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Free 7-day trial needs no credit card for Starter and Growth.
  • The credit table is public, so you can estimate monthly output.
  • Growth gives unlimited users, helpful for small teams.
  • The managed tier replaces a website, ads, lead response and reviews with one stated price.

Cons

  • Starter's 3 accounts and 600 credits: 600 credits is roughly 600 social posts, but only 40 videos or 200 blogs.
  • Top-up credit prices are not published.
  • Limits for the $999 plan are only shared on a booked call.
  • Annual prices after the 20% discount are not listed.
  • One brand per workspace, so multi-brand users pay per brand.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Blaze cost?

Starter $79/month, Growth $149/month, All-In-One managed $999/month. Annual subscriptions get 20% off.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, 7 days; Starter and Growth need no credit card to start.

What counts against my credits?

Static social post 1, email 3, blog post 3, AI video 15. Extra credits can be bought; price not listed.

Can I manage several brands?

Each brand needs its own workspace and Brand Kit. For 5 or more, email support for discounted pricing.

How is it different from Simplified or SmartlyQ?

Blaze's difference is the optional managed tier staffed by Blaze; the others are software-only. Compare feature coverage per plan.
