Enterpret is a strong fit for product and customer-experience teams drowning in feedback from many channels, and a poor fit for small teams with little feedback or a need for a self-serve tool with posted prices. It collects feedback from support, sales calls, reviews and communities, groups it into themes using a taxonomy that adapts to your product, and connects each theme to the customers and revenue involved. An AI agent can then answer questions about the data, watch for emerging issues and hand problems to people or coding tools.

Best for

Product managers, CX leaders and insights teams at mid-market and enterprise companies that receive large volumes of feedback across several channels and want to connect it to accounts and revenue. Not for solo founders or small teams with little feedback, buyers who need transparent self-serve pricing, or teams that only use a single feedback source.

Key features

01

Feedback unification

Connects 30+ sources including support desks, call recording, app stores, review sites and communities into one dataset.

02

Adaptive taxonomy

Classifies feedback into themes that reflect your own product and terminology rather than generic tags.

03

Customer context graph

Links each theme to the accounts and revenue behind it so impact can be weighed.

04

Enterpret Agent

Ask questions with answers traced to conversations, watch for proactive signals, and file issues with customer context.

05

Dashboards and MCP server

Trend dashboards plus an MCP server so AI tools can query the feedback data.

06

Workflow integrations

Sends findings to tools such as Slack, Jira, Linear, Salesforce, HubSpot and Notion.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • One dataset across support, sales calls, reviews and communities removes manual cross-referencing between tools.
  • Linking themes to accounts and revenue helps rank issues by business impact, not just mention count.
  • Answers in the agent are traced back to the underlying conversations, which makes them easier to check.
  • An MCP server lets teams query feedback from AI assistants they already use.
  • Lists SOC 2 Type II and ISO certifications and offers SSO and data residency for enterprise customers.

Cons

  • No published pricing or plan tiers, so you cannot estimate cost before talking to sales.
  • Usefulness scales with how many sources you connect, so expect setup and integration work.
  • Taxonomy and AI classification typically need human review and tuning over time.
  • Overkill for teams with a few hundred feedback items a month; lighter tools may be enough.
  • Many headline capabilities are vendor claims; we did not independently test accuracy.

Frequently asked questions

What does Enterpret do?

It gathers customer feedback from support, sales calls, reviews and communities, classifies it into themes, and links those themes to accounts and revenue.

How much does Enterpret cost?

Pricing is not public. The vendor says it depends on data volume and number of integrations and asks you to submit a form for a custom quote.

Can I try it before buying?

The website offers a demo request and a 'try Enterpret' flow described as taking about five minutes with no commitment; trial limits were not verified.

Which tools does it integrate with?

The site lists sources such as Zendesk, Intercom, Gong and app stores, and outputs to Slack, Jira, Linear, Salesforce, HubSpot and Notion, plus 30+ connectors in total.

Can AI assistants use the data?

Yes, Enterpret offers an MCP server for tools such as Claude, Cursor, Codex and ChatGPT.
