Otterly AI is a sensible choice for marketers who want a dedicated, reasonably priced tracker of brand visibility in AI answers. Skip it if you want a full SEO suite, content writing, or proof that visibility scores translate into traffic. It tracks how often a brand is mentioned and its site cited by engines including ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Copilot (Claude, Gemini and AI Mode as add-ons), benchmarks competitors, and offers prompt research, content audits and GEO recommendations. The site claims a 4.8/5 G2 rating and a 2025 Gartner Cool Vendor listing; these are vendor-reported and were not independently verified today.

Best for

Good for brand and SEO teams, startups and agencies who need AI-search monitoring and can work with a limited prompt count. Not for teams wanting an all-in-one SEO platform, for those who need guaranteed traffic outcomes from tracking, or for people tracking hundreds of prompts on a tight budget, since extra prompts and engines cost more.

Key features

01

Multi-engine tracking

Monitors ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and Microsoft Copilot on all plans, with daily tracking per the listed plan features. Claude, Gemini and Google AI Mode are add-ons.

02

Visibility, citations and sentiment

Shows brand position, which sources get cited, share of AI voice and sentiment, with competitor benchmarks.

03

Prompt research

Helps find the questions and topics that drive AI answers in your category.

04

GEO recommendations and content audit

Suggests ways to improve citations (3 per week on Lite, unlimited on higher plans) and audits URLs for crawlability and citation potential, with a monthly URL-audit quota.

05

API, MCP and Agent Analytics

Programmatic access and tracking of AI-agent traffic from Standard up, plus a Looker/Data Studio connector.

06

ChatGPT Ads Tracking

Listed on the homepage; per-plan availability was unclear on the pricing page.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Single-purpose product, so setup and reports center on AI visibility instead of a sprawling SEO suite.
  • Prompt limits and add-on prices are published, making cost easy to estimate.
  • Unlimited team members on every plan helps agencies and larger teams.
  • API and MCP access allow pulling data into your own reporting from Standard.

Cons

  • Lite's 15 prompts is narrow for anything beyond a small brand or a trial.
  • Covering Claude, Gemini and AI Mode adds cost; on Premium, Claude alone is $439/mo extra.
  • The pricing page showed conflicting price sets and trial lengths (7 vs 14 days), so confirm before buying.
  • Awards and ratings are vendor-reported; this review did not verify them with third-party pages, and the link between visibility scores and traffic is unproven.

Frequently asked questions

Which AI engines does Otterly track?

ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and Microsoft Copilot on all plans; Claude, Gemini and Google AI Mode cost extra.

How much does it cost?

Lite $29/mo, Standard $189/mo, Premium $489/mo, Enterprise custom. The page also listed $25, $160 and $422, likely annual rates, but it was inconsistent.

Is there a free trial?

The site mentions one, but states 7 days in the FAQ and 14 days at sign-up, so check current terms.

Does it help me rank higher in AI answers?

It monitors visibility and gives GEO recommendations. Whether better scores bring traffic is not something the site proves.
