Otterly AI is a small, newer AI search monitoring and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) platform that tracks how a brand and its competitors appear across AI answer engines — by default ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and Microsoft Copilot, with Claude, Google Gemini and Google AI Mode available as paid add-ons. It has been recognized as a Gartner Cool Vendor (2025, AI in Marketing) and won Best AI Search Software Solution at the 2026 European Search Awards.

Otterly's core differentiator versus the AI-visibility features now bundled into large suites like Semrush One or Surfer's AI Tracker is focus: it is built solely around AI-search monitoring and GEO, with transparent per-prompt pricing, API and MCP access, and agent analytics, rather than being one module inside a much broader (and pricier) SEO platform.

Best for

Otterly AI suits marketing teams and agencies that specifically need to monitor and improve brand visibility inside AI answer engines and want a dedicated, API-friendly tool rather than a bolted-on module. It's not the right choice for teams that want one platform covering traditional rank tracking, backlink analysis or content optimization alongside AI visibility; those needs are better served by the bundled AI-visibility features inside larger suites like Semrush or Surfer.

Key features

01

Multi-engine AI tracking

Monitors brand visibility across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and Microsoft Copilot by default, with daily tracking frequency on every paid tier; Claude, Gemini and Google AI Mode are paid add-ons.

02

Brand Visibility Index

A composite score summarizing how often and how prominently a brand appears across tracked AI engines and prompts.

03

Link Citations Analysis

Shows which sources and domains get cited by AI engines in response to tracked prompts.

04

Domain Ranking

Benchmarks a domain's AI visibility directly against named competitors across the same prompt set.

05

GEO Audit

Audits URLs and content for AI-answer optimization readiness, with a monthly quota of GEO URL audits that scales by plan.

06

AI Prompt Research Tool

Helps identify and build the set of prompts worth tracking for a given brand's market.

07

API & MCP access + Agent Analytics

Programmatic access to visibility data (Standard tier and above) plus Agent Analytics for tracking traffic driven by AI agents.

08

Multi-workspace, multi-country support

Unlimited team members on every tier, unlimited workspaces from Standard up, and support for 50+ country/market variants.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Purpose-built specifically for AI-search visibility, with broader eventual AI-engine coverage (up to 6 engines via add-ons) than most bolted-on trackers
  • Transparent, published per-prompt pricing that scales predictably, rather than an opaque credit system
  • Unlimited team members on every tier, and unlimited workspaces from Standard up
  • Strong early reception: 4.8/5 on G2, Gartner Cool Vendor 2025 recognition, and a 2026 European Search Awards win
  • API and MCP access from the Standard tier suits technical teams and agencies
  • A dedicated agency partner tier bundles extra prompts and unlimited client workspaces for GEO service providers

Cons

  • Very small independent review base — around 42 reviews on G2 and essentially no presence on Capterra
  • A detailed Reddit test running Otterly alongside other tools found no consistent correlation between rising AI-visibility scores and actual traffic or conversion gains
  • No Reddit/forum monitoring and no AI-crawler analytics compared to some competitors
  • Full 6-engine AI coverage requires paid add-ons on top of the base plan
  • The jump from Lite ($29/mo, 15 prompts) to Standard ($189/mo, 100 prompts) is steep for teams that outgrow the entry tier
  • As a young company, its feature set and pricing structure have less multi-year track record than incumbents like Semrush or Surfer

What reviewers say

Otterly AI is too new and too small to have a meaningful Capterra footprint, but its early reviews are strongly positive: 4.8/5 on G2, with recurring themes of valuable insights, easy setup and an intuitive interface. The most substantive independent critique comes from Reddit, where a multi-month side-by-side test found the visibility data itself was accurate, but improving AI-visibility scores did not reliably translate into measurable traffic or conversion gains.

Frequently asked questions

Does Otterly AI have a free plan?

No permanent free plan; Otterly offers a free trial for new users, and the cheapest paid plan is Lite at $29/mo ($25/mo billed annually) for 15 search prompts.

How is Otterly AI priced?

Pricing scales by number of tracked search prompts: Lite is $29/mo for 15 prompts, Standard is $189/mo for 100 prompts, Premium is $489/mo for 400 prompts, and Enterprise is custom.

How does Otterly AI compare to Semrush's or Surfer's AI-visibility tracking?

Otterly is a dedicated, standalone AI-visibility tool with deeper per-engine controls, API/MCP access and agency-specific plans, whereas Semrush and Surfer bundle a lighter AI-visibility module inside a much broader SEO platform.

Is Otterly AI worth it?

Early reviewers rate the product highly for ease of use and insight quality, but independent Reddit testing found no guaranteed link between higher visibility scores and actual traffic or conversions — treat it as a monitoring and optimization tool, not a guaranteed growth lever.

Which AI engines does Otterly track?

ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and Microsoft Copilot are included by default on every plan; Claude, Google Gemini and Google AI Mode are available as paid add-ons.

Does Otterly AI have an API?

Yes. API and MCP access are included starting on the Standard plan ($189/mo), along with Agent Analytics for tracking AI-agent-driven traffic.
