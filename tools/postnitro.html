PostNitro is a good fit for creators and small marketing teams who publish carousels on LinkedIn or Instagram and want copy and design done in one pass; it is less suited to agencies managing many client brands or anyone needing a general-purpose design suite. You feed it a topic, URL, article or thread, or chat with its AI assistant, and it writes the slides and places them in one of 100+ templates that you can restyle with saved brand kits. Output exports as PNG, PDF or MP4, and from the $30 Creator+ plan you can schedule posts to LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, Threads, Facebook and Pinterest; X is a content source and export format, not a publishing target. Developer access is available through an API, MCP server, CLI and embeddable editor. Pricing is tiered by downloads, AI slides per carousel, images, brand kits and which AI models you can use.

Best for

Good for solo creators, consultants, founders and small social teams who want fast, branded carousels with scheduling in the same tool. Not for agencies running many client accounts (Team+ caps at 5 workspaces and 30 social accounts), teams who need full freeform design tooling, or anyone who wants to publish natively to X.

Key features

01

AI content + design generator

Converts a topic, URL, article, or Twitter thread into full carousel copy and slide layout in one step using GPT and Claude models.

02

Template library

100+ templates across styles and platforms, fully customizable with the user's own colors, fonts, and images.

03

Brand kits

Save custom fonts, color palettes, logos, and team headshots so every carousel stays visually consistent with minimal per-post setup.

04

Multi-platform export

Export as high-resolution PNGs or a single PDF sized correctly for LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, X, and Threads without manual resizing.

05

Scheduling and publishing

Schedule and publish carousels directly to connected social accounts from within PostNitro rather than exporting and posting manually.

06

Team workspaces

Multiple workspaces and team member seats on higher plans let agencies and content teams collaborate and keep client brands separated.

07

Embed SDK, API, and MCP server

Developers can embed PostNitro's carousel generation into their own products via SDK/API, or connect it to AI agent workflows via an MCP server.

08

Zapier integration

Connects PostNitro to 1,000+ apps via Zapier to automate carousel creation as part of broader content workflows.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • One input produces both copy and finished slides, saving the separate write-then-design steps.
  • Brand kits keep fonts, colors, logos and headshots consistent across posts.
  • Scheduling and publishing to six platforms is built in from Creator+.
  • Unlimited downloads from Creator+ remove usage anxiety for frequent posters.
  • Programmatic options (API, MCP server, CLI, embed SDK) are unusual at this price.

Cons

  • Free plan: watermarked exports and only 5 downloads/month, so real use needs a paid plan.
  • Scheduling needs Creator+ at $30/mo; Starter at $15 is design-only.
  • Advanced AI models (the stronger GPT and Claude tiers) are gated to Creator+ and above.
  • Team+ tops out at 5 workspaces and 30 social accounts, tight for agencies.
  • API use runs on credits (1 per imported slide, 2 per AI slide); Starter needs a $10/mo add-on.
  • The pricing page is slightly inconsistent about a 'Plus' label and Starter API access; confirm at checkout.

Frequently asked questions

Is PostNitro free?

There is a free plan with no credit card: 5 downloads a month, one brand kit, AI carousels up to 5 slides, watermarked exports, and no scheduling or API.

How much does PostNitro cost?

Starter is $15/month, Creator+ $30/month and Team+ $60/month; yearly billing gives two months free. Fees are refundable within seven days of purchase per the pricing page.

Which plan do I need to schedule posts?

Creator+ ($30/month) or above. It allows scheduling to 10 social accounts; Team+ allows 30.

Which platforms can it publish to?

LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, Threads, Facebook and Pinterest. X is supported as a content source and export format only.

How does it compare with AiCarousels?

Both generate carousels with AI; PostNitro adds brand kits, scheduling and developer APIs. Check current limits on each before choosing.
