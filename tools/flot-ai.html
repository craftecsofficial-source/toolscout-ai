Flot.ai is an AI copilot built by a small team and distributed as a browser extension and desktop app (Windows and Mac) rather than a standalone chat website. Its core idea is to meet users where they already work: highlight text in Gmail, Google Docs, Slack, Notion, a code editor, or almost any other application, and a Flot popup appears offering to write, rewrite, summarize, translate, explain, or fix the selected text using GPT-4, Claude, and other underlying models. A global keyboard shortcut also summons the assistant as a floating chat window from anywhere on the desktop, so users don't have to keep a separate browser tab open.

What differentiates Flot from a plain ChatGPT wrapper is its personal knowledge base: notes, meeting summaries, research snippets, and translated passages can be saved and later retrieved through AI-assisted search and chat, effectively giving the assistant long-term memory of a user's own material. Flot supports over 200 languages for drafting and translation and ships with 1,000+ one-click prompt templates covering common writing tasks. The company positions Flot as a consolidation play — replacing a grammar checker, a translator, and a general AI copilot with one $9.99–$39/month subscription. It is a small, community-reviewed product (a handful of verified reviews on G2) rather than a venture-scale brand, and is best understood as a lightweight, cross-application AI layer for individuals rather than an enterprise chatbot platform.

Best for

Flot.ai fits individual writers, students, developers, and marketers who want a single AI helper available inside every app they already use, rather than copy-pasting into a separate chatbot tab. It's a weaker fit for teams needing centralized admin controls, audit logs, or enterprise procurement, and for users who only need one narrow function (e.g., just grammar checking) since Flot's value comes from bundling multiple use cases together.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Consolidates several paid tools into one subscription: a G2 reviewer, a COO at a marketing studio, said Flot replaced roughly five separate paid tools for AI copilot, grammar checking, and translation.
  • True cross-application reach: because it works via a popup triggered from any text field, users report it functioning in web pages, desktop programs, and everywhere else without extra setup per app.
  • Memory function stands out from generic AI extensions: reviewers specifically call out the ability to recall previous content and notes as a differentiator versus a plain chatbot.
  • Multiple underlying AI models in one place: access to OpenAI, Gemini/Claude-class models, plus dedicated grammar and translation tools removes the need to juggle several subscriptions.
  • Affordable entry point: the $9.99/mo Pro tier undercuts many single-purpose AI writing tools while bundling copilot, grammar, and translation functions together.

Cons

  • Reviewers note a real ramp-up period before the tool's structure and shortcuts feel natural, which can frustrate users expecting an instant plug-and-play experience.
  • The interface, while described as visually clean, offers limited customization — one reviewer specifically wanted more control over the look and feel.
  • As a small, independently distributed product it lacks the large review base of major AI assistants, making it harder to gauge reliability at scale from third-party data.
  • Free tier's 100 monthly credits will be consumed quickly by regular users, effectively pushing serious use toward a paid tier.
  • Positioned mainly as an individual productivity tool rather than a team/enterprise platform, so it lacks admin controls or seat-based collaboration features typical of larger AI assistant suites.

Frequently asked questions

What is Flot.ai?

Flot.ai is an AI copilot delivered as a browser extension and desktop app that pops up inside other applications to help write, edit, translate, and remember content.

Which AI models does Flot.ai use?

Flot integrates multiple large language models, including GPT-4 and other providers, depending on the subscription tier.

Is there a free version of Flot.ai?

Yes, Flot offers a free-forever plan with 100 credits per month, 200+ language support, and email support.

Does Flot.ai work outside the browser?

Yes, in addition to a browser extension, Flot has native desktop apps for Windows and Mac so it can be summoned in any application, not just websites.

What is the Flot knowledge base feature?

It's a personal memory store where users save notes, research, and past conversations, which Flot can later search and reference in new chats.

How much does Flot.ai cost?

Paid plans range from $9.99/mo (Pro) to $39/mo (Unlimited), with a free plan available and discounts shown on the pricing page versus list price.
