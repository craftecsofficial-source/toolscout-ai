Flot.ai is aimed at individual writers and knowledge workers who want an assistant that pops up over whatever app they are typing in; it is not a fit for teams needing an enterprise chatbot platform. Our fetch of flot.ai returned only page metadata (title: write, read, memorize with AI), so features and pricing below are carried over from earlier research and could not be re-verified today. As previously described, it runs as a browser extension and desktop app, offers actions such as write, rewrite, translate and summarize on selected text, and keeps a personal knowledge base you can search. Plans were credit-based with a free tier. The site itself is built on Framer and its pages did not render pricing for us, so confirm current plans, model access and credit limits on flot.ai before paying.

Best for

Possibly good for individual writers, students and knowledge workers who want in-place AI editing and translation. Not for teams needing admin controls or compliance guarantees (none verified), and not for anyone who needs confirmed pricing before evaluating.
