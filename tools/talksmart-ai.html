TalkSmart.ai suits small local businesses that lose leads to unanswered phones and can justify a flat $297 a month; it is a poor fit for high call volumes you need to cost out per minute, or for teams wanting a documented integration ecosystem. Its AI Employee answers inbound calls, and the site says it takes over only when nobody picks up the business line. It captures contact details and custom fields, can forward calls to named team members under set conditions, emails a summary and recording after each call and can start follow-up with the new lead. The same product also handles text, Facebook Messenger, Instagram DMs and Google Business chat, and offers email and SMS campaigns, a drag-and-drop email builder and review requests with replies on Google and Facebook. Only one plan is listed, and no usage caps, trial length or third-party integrations are stated.

Best for

Fits local service businesses, agents and small sales teams that miss calls and want automatic lead capture and follow-up. Not for businesses wanting a free or cheap trial, enterprises needing published SLAs, call-minute pricing or listed CRM integrations, or anyone wanting outbound cold-calling, which the site does not describe.

Key features

01

AI voice employee

A conversational AI answers inbound phone calls live, engaging callers in natural dialogue rather than routing them to voicemail.

02

Custom working hours

The business phone rings first during set hours; the AI only takes over if no human answers, preserving a human-first experience when staff are available.

03

Call routing rules

Inbound calls can be forwarded to specific team members based on configured conditions, blending AI triage with human handoff.

04

Lead information capture

The AI collects contact details and custom fields during the call so every conversation becomes a usable lead record.

05

Call summaries and recordings

Each handled call generates an email notification with a recording and summary, keeping the business owner in the loop without listening to every call.

06

Automated post-call follow-up

After hanging up, workflows automatically follow up with new leads via text or email to maximize conversion.

07

Omnichannel lead capture

Combines SMS, Facebook Messenger, Instagram DM, and Google Business Chat into a single conversation stream alongside the AI voice channel.

08

Reputation management

Sends review requests and lets owners reply to Google and Facebook reviews from the same dashboard used for messaging.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Live voice answering of inbound calls rather than a voicemail or menu.
  • Broad channel coverage (SMS, Messenger, Instagram DM, Google Business chat) from one product.
  • Call recordings and summaries delivered by email give an audit trail.
  • Call forwarding to staff under set conditions keeps humans in the loop.

Cons

  • $297/month is a high floor for very small shops and there is no cheaper tier.
  • Usage limits (minutes, contacts, seats) are not published, so overage risk cannot be assessed.
  • The money-back guarantee has no stated time window or conditions.
  • No third-party integrations are named, so CRM or calendar connection could not be verified.
  • Evidence is mostly vendor testimonials; no independent reviews were found.
