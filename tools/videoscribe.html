VideoScribe is a good pick for marketers, teachers and trainers who regularly need hand-drawn whiteboard explainers and want a simple drag-and-drop tool. Skip it if you want AI to generate a finished video from a prompt, need live-action or avatar footage, or want a modern collaborative editor. Made by Sparkol, it lets you place images, text and icons on a canvas and automatically adds the drawing-hand reveal, camera moves and transitions, then export 1080p MP4. AI script, image and voiceover helpers were added on top, but you still build the video scene by scene yourself.

Best for

Marketers, educators, L&D and HR teams and small businesses who make recurring whiteboard explainer or training videos without an animator. Not for: people wanting AI-generated cinematic or avatar video, high-volume short-form social output, live-action editing, or anyone who needs more than a few AI images or voiceovers a month on the cheaper tiers.

Key features

01

Whiteboard animation timeline

A drag-and-drop canvas where placed elements are automatically animated with a drawing-hand reveal, plus one-click styles like pulse and bounce.

02

AI image generation

Generates custom visuals tailored to the video's content directly inside the editor, reducing reliance on stock imagery.

03

AI voiceover generation

Produces natural-sounding narration in more than 45 languages and multiple accents without hiring voice talent.

04

AI script generation

Drafts video scripts from a topic or outline to speed up the writing stage before building the visual timeline.

05

Asset library

Access to over 5 million premium images, illustrations, icons, and GIFs (image count varies by plan tier).

06

Camera controls

Manual control over pan and zoom movements across the canvas to direct viewer attention scene by scene.

07

Audio tools

Built-in voiceover recording plus a library of roughly 200 royalty-free music tracks for background audio.

08

Template library

Ready-made scribe templates for business, education, YouTube, and promotional use cases to start from instead of a blank canvas.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Very low learning curve: you place elements and the drawing animation, camera pans and zooms are generated for you.
  • Core and Max remove watermarks, allow client preview links and, on Max, add reseller rights, custom fonts and brand kit for agency work.
  • All plans include unlimited projects, a music library, Full HD and MP4 export.
  • Max allows unlimited downloads and 20-minute videos, enough for training modules.
  • Long-running product with templates for business, education and training.

Cons

  • The trial and Lite plan cap videos at 5 minutes, and Lite allows just 5 downloads a month.
  • Lite and the trial show watermarks (all videos on trial, premium images on Lite), so only Core and Max suit client-facing publishing.
  • AI allowances are small: for example 20 AI images on Lite and 100 on Max, and no AI voiceover on Lite or the trial per the live limits.
  • The pricing page's annual figures equal 12 times the listed monthly rate, so the advertised 'up to 65%' annual saving did not show in the numbers we saw; monthly-billed prices are unclear.
  • Style is limited to whiteboard-type animation; it is not a general video editor or an AI video generator.
  • Earlier research suggests the interface feels dated and rendering can be slow on long projects; we did not re-verify this today.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

Only a 7-day trial with no credit card. It allows 3 downloads in total, caps videos at 5 minutes and watermarks every video.

What do the plans cost?

The live pricing page shows $150, $255 and $320 per year per license for Lite, Core and Max on annual billing. Monthly-billed prices were not clearly shown, so confirm before choosing monthly.

Which plan removes the watermark?

Core and Max have no watermark. Lite keeps a watermark on premium images, and the trial watermarks everything.

Does it include AI tools?

Yes: AI image, script and voiceover helpers, with monthly limits that rise by plan. AI voiceover is not listed on the trial or Lite limits.

How long can a video be?

5 minutes on trial and Lite, 10 on Core and 20 on Max.
