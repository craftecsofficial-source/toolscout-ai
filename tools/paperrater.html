PaperRater is a free, browser-based writing-evaluation tool that checks submitted essays and papers for grammar and spelling errors, plagiarism, vocabulary usage, passive voice, sentence-length issues, and other style metrics, then returns an automated score and a printable summary report. It has been used for years by students, freelance writers, and content teams as a lightweight second pass before submitting or publishing work, sitting alongside heavier tools like Grammarly. The free tier covers up to 6 pages of text; beyond that, PaperRater historically charged $7.95 for a one-time check up to 15 pages, or $14.95/month for a Premium subscription offering unlimited submissions, deeper plagiarism analysis, and an ad-free experience.

Critically, PaperRater is owned by Learneo (the parent company behind Course Hero, QuillBot, Scribbr, and LitCharts), and Learneo announced on its support site that PaperRater will be officially retired effective August 31, 2026 — just days from the current date. The company says it is consolidating resources into its other Learneo-owned platforms rather than migrating any PaperRater features to a new home; existing accounts remain accessible until the shutdown date, recurring charges will stop before then, and users with active paid subscriptions may be eligible for a prorated refund. Learneo is directing displaced users to its sister products: QuillBot for AI writing/paraphrasing/grammar/citations, Scribbr for professional proofreading and plagiarism checking, Course Hero for study resources, and LitCharts for literature guides.

Best for

Historically a good fit for students, freelance writers, and content teams wanting a fast, mostly-free second check on grammar, plagiarism, and style before submitting or publishing. Given the confirmed August 31, 2026 shutdown, it is not a viable choice for anyone starting fresh — Learneo itself is redirecting users to sister products like QuillBot and Scribbr.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The free plan gives real, usable feedback (grammar, spelling, vocabulary, passive voice, plagiarism) on up to 6 pages without requiring payment, which reviewers repeatedly highlight as good value.
  • Multiple long-tenured reviewers (2+ years of use) on Capterra describe it as a reliable step in their regular writing or editing workflow.
  • Reviewers specifically credit it with catching plagiarism and passive-voice issues other quick checks miss, useful for freelance writers submitting to clients.
  • The workflow is fast and simple — paste or upload a document and get a report back quickly, with no complex setup.
  • It has stayed genuinely free at the entry tier for a long time relative to competitors that have moved to subscription-only models.

Cons

  • Most importantly: the service is shutting down permanently on August 31, 2026, per an official Learneo announcement — any new adoption should account for this immediate end-of-life.
  • No mobile app exists; several Capterra reviewers explicitly wanted one and found the mobile browsing experience clunky.
  • Customer support is weak — reviewers describe automated phone support and no live help, which is frustrating for paid subscribers.
  • One long-time reviewer noted the 'Check' button on the site is frequently crowded out by third-party grammar-tool ads, making the tool harder to actually use.
  • The 'advanced check' option reportedly redirects to Grammarly without clear labeling, which some reviewers found confusing.

What reviewers say

PaperRater holds a solid 4.4/5 on Capterra from a small pool of 11 reviews, with reviewers praising its plagiarism detection, vocabulary/style feedback, and free tier, while criticizing weak customer support and the lack of a mobile app. The service is scheduled to shut down permanently on August 31, 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Is PaperRater shutting down?

Yes. Learneo, PaperRater's parent company, has announced the service will be officially retired on August 31, 2026, with no feature migration to a replacement product.

Will I still be charged after PaperRater shuts down?

No — Learneo says all recurring charges will stop before August 31, 2026, and no billing will occur after that date.

Can I get a refund for an active subscription?

If you have an active paid subscription with time remaining past August 31, 2026, Learneo says you may be eligible for a prorated refund via its support team.

What should I use instead of PaperRater?

Learneo is directing users to its other platforms: QuillBot for AI writing/grammar/paraphrasing, Scribbr for professional proofreading and plagiarism checks, Course Hero for study resources, and LitCharts for literature guides.

Is PaperRater free?

Yes, historically the base plan was free for up to 6 pages, with a $7.95 one-time fee for longer documents or a $14.95/month Premium subscription for unlimited use.

What happens to my PaperRater account data?

Learneo says user accounts and personal data (name, email, billing info) will be deleted after the shutdown, subject to limited retention required by law.
