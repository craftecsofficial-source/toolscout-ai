Clari built its reputation as one of the leading revenue operations platforms by tackling a persistent problem in enterprise sales: forecasts that are built on gut feel and outdated spreadsheets rather than real pipeline data. By connecting directly to the CRM along with email, calendar, and call systems, Clari automatically captures rep activity and surfaces deal risk signals that would otherwise go unnoticed until it's too late in the quarter.

At the center of the platform is its AI-driven forecasting engine, which analyzes historical win rates, deal velocity, and rep-level forecasting accuracy to produce a rolled-up forecast with variance tracking. This is paired with 'Revenue Cadence' workflows that structure how sales leaders run weekly and monthly business reviews, replacing ad hoc spreadsheet reviews with a consistent, data-backed process.

Clari has expanded over time through acquisitions, notably folding in conversation intelligence via Clari Copilot (formerly Wingman), giving it call recording and coaching capabilities alongside its core forecasting and pipeline management products. It's widely used by public and late-stage private companies where forecast predictability is closely tied to investor and board reporting.

Best for

Clari is built for mid-market to enterprise B2B sales organizations, particularly those with multiple layers of sales management who need consistent, board-ready forecasting. It's especially valuable for revenue leaders replacing manual spreadsheet forecasting with an automated, data-driven process. RevOps teams use it to enforce structured pipeline review cadences across large distributed sales teams. It's not designed for small teams or startups without the budget or process maturity to leverage its full feature set.

Key features

01

AI Revenue Forecasting

Rolls up rep, manager, and executive forecasts using AI models trained on historical pipeline and win-rate data, with variance tracking against targets.

02

Deal Inspection & Risk Scoring

Flags at-risk deals based on stalled activity, missing stakeholders, or slipping close dates before they fall out of the pipeline.

03

Automatic Activity Capture

Syncs emails, calendar invites, and calls into the CRM automatically, reducing manual data entry for reps.

04

Revenue Cadence

Structures recurring pipeline review meetings with standardized dashboards so leadership can track deal and rep-level progress consistently.

05

Conversation Intelligence (Clari Copilot)

Records, transcribes, and analyzes sales calls to surface coaching moments and buyer sentiment.

06

Scenario & What-If Planning

Models different pipeline and close-rate scenarios to help leaders stress-test forecast assumptions before committing numbers upward.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Forecasting accuracy is consistently cited as a standout strength, with many enterprise sales leaders replacing spreadsheet-based rollups entirely.
  • The Revenue Cadence framework brings much-needed structure and consistency to weekly and monthly pipeline reviews across large sales organizations.
  • Automatic activity capture meaningfully reduces the administrative burden on reps, improving CRM data quality without extra manual work.
  • Deal risk scoring surfaces problems earlier in the sales cycle than traditional stage-based pipeline reviews typically would.

Cons

  • Pricing is enterprise-grade and quote-only, making it inaccessible for small and mid-sized sales teams with limited budgets.
  • Initial setup and configuration of forecasting models and cadences often requires dedicated RevOps resources and time.
  • Because forecasts depend heavily on CRM data quality, teams with inconsistent CRM usage will see diminished forecasting accuracy.
  • Some users report the sheer breadth of dashboards and configuration options can be overwhelming without proper onboarding.

Frequently asked questions

What is Clari used for?

It's used for AI-driven revenue forecasting, pipeline inspection, and structured deal reviews across enterprise sales organizations.

How much does Clari cost?

Pricing is custom and quote-based, sold as annual per-seat enterprise contracts; there is no published pricing or free tier.

Does Clari integrate with Salesforce?

Yes, Clari integrates deeply with Salesforce and other major CRMs along with email and calendar systems for activity capture.

What is Clari Copilot?

Clari Copilot, formerly Wingman, is Clari's conversation intelligence product that records and analyzes sales calls for coaching insights.

Is Clari suitable for small businesses?

Not typically; its pricing and feature depth are geared toward mid-market and enterprise sales organizations.

Does Clari replace manual forecasting spreadsheets?

Yes, that's one of its core value propositions, replacing spreadsheet rollups with automated, AI-assisted forecasting.
