NewTattoo.ai is a good fit for people who are still choosing a tattoo and want to see candidate designs on their own body before booking an artist. Skip it if you want a finished, tattoo-ready design with no further work, or a studio tool with team features. You describe an idea, pick a style, optionally upload a reference image, and the tool generates original artwork, which you can then overlay on a photo and adjust for size and position. It also has a lettering generator, a gallery of other users' designs, and saved collections.

Best for

Individuals planning a tattoo who want to explore ideas and preview placement, and artists who want fast concept sketches to discuss with clients. It is not for anyone who expects a ready-to-ink design without an artist's input, for studios needing multi-user or team plans, or for people who want to resell the generated artwork, which the site's terms do not allow.

Key features

01

AI tattoo generator

Creates artwork from a written idea, chosen style and placement, in styles such as minimalist, realistic, blackwork, geometric and traditional.

02

3D virtual try-on

Upload a photo of yourself and a design, then adjust size and placement on your skin.

03

Tattoo font generator

Turns words into tattoo lettering and previews it on skin.

04

Reference uploads

Upload an image to guide the AI toward a motif or look.

05

Ideas gallery and collections

Browse designs other users made, save your own, and mark designs private (private designs are listed as a Pro feature).

06

Image-to-stencil converter

Listed as a Pro feature for turning a finished design into a stencil-style image.

07

Commercial use terms

The site says artists may use designs for client work, but reselling or redistributing the artwork itself is not allowed.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Seeing a design on your own photo helps catch placement and size problems before they become permanent.
  • Text prompts, reference uploads and a gallery give several ways in whether you have a clear idea or none.
  • The lettering tool and placement templates cover common tattoo types such as names, quotes and sleeves.
  • All three Pro billing periods unlock the same features, so the annual price is a simple saving if you commit.

Cons

  • The try-on is an image overlay and cannot model skin curvature, healing or fading, so treat it as a rough guide.
  • The site does not state free-tier limits, and the pricing page's 'not included' list overlaps confusingly with the Pro feature list, so what free users lack is unclear.
  • AI-generated art may need redrawing by a professional before it is suitable for tattooing.
  • Only one paid plan is shown; we saw no studio or team pricing.
  • We could not verify image quality or try-on realism independently, or find independent reviews.

Frequently asked questions

What is NewTattoo.ai?

A browser-based tool that generates tattoo designs from text and lets you preview them on a photo of your body.

Is it free?

You can start free, but the site does not list what the free tier includes. Unlimited designs, HD downloads, private designs and the stencil converter are part of the paid Pro Unlimited plan.

How much does Pro cost?

$9.99 monthly, $19.99 quarterly or $47.99 annually, in USD, cancel anytime. The site also offers a money-back guarantee when trying Pro with up to 25 images.

How does the try-on work?

You upload a photo of your body and a design (generated or your own), then resize and move it on the photo.

Can tattoo artists use it for client work?

The site says yes for client work, but reselling or redistributing the artwork itself is not allowed.
