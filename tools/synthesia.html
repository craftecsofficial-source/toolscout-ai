Synthesia is a strong pick for training, onboarding and internal comms teams that need polished presenter-style video in many languages without filming anything. Skip it if you want expressive social content, cinematic footage or a cheap tool for a few videos a year, because paid plans are credit-limited and the enterprise features sit behind a sales call.

You give it a prompt, document, PowerPoint or link and it drafts a script and scenes with an AI avatar presenter. You can then edit, translate or dub the result, add quizzes and branching, share by link or embed, and track views and drop-off. Usage is metered in credits: the pricing page estimates roughly 100 credits per minute of video, with lip-sync doubling the cost.

Best for

For L&D, HR, compliance and enterprise communications teams that publish repeatable training or explainer video and need localization, brand control and LMS delivery. Not for solo creators, social-first marketers or anyone who needs authentic, emotive or cinematic footage, nor for teams wanting SCORM or SSO without an Enterprise contract.

Key features

01

Avatars

Stock avatars with adjustable outfits and backgrounds, an Avatar Builder, and Personal Avatars made from a single photo. Avatar counts rise by plan (9 free, 125+ Starter, 180+ Pro, 240+ Enterprise).

02

Prompt-to-video assistant

Turns a prompt, document, PowerPoint or link into a draft with script, scenes and presenter.

03

Translation and dubbing

One-click translation of finished videos and dubbing of existing footage with voice cloning and optional lip-sync, plus a glossary for brand terms.

04

Interactivity and analytics

Quizzes, clickable calls to action and branching paths, with view, completion and drop-off analytics. Interactive video starts at Pro.

05

Brand and governance

Brand Kits, roles and permissions, approval before publishing and shared workspaces; SAML/SSO and live collaboration are Enterprise.

06

Sessions and API

Roleplay and Survey Sessions with conversational avatars, a video/dubbing API and an MCP server.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • SCORM export, quizzes and branching suit LMS-based training
  • Analytics show completion and drop-off, which matters for training ROI
  • Translation and dubbing make one script reusable across languages
  • Approvals, roles and Brand Kits fit team workflows
  • Lists SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001/27701/42001 and GDPR with EU data residency
  • Drafting from a document or deck cuts first-version time

Cons

  • Credit model: Starter's 1,250 credits is about 12 minutes of video; lip-sync doubles the credit cost
  • Pro's roughly 60 minutes is the practical ceiling before paying for larger credit tiers
  • SCORM export, SSO and brand kits require Enterprise
  • Free plan is watermarked with no MP4 download
  • Avatar delivery reads as presenter-style and corporate rather than natural
  • Add-ons cost extra, such as learner seats at $29 per learner per month for Roleplay

Frequently asked questions

Does Synthesia have a free plan?

Yes. Basic is $0 with 500 credits a month (about 10 minutes), 9 avatars and a watermark; you cannot download MP4s.

How much does Synthesia cost?

Starter is $29/mo billed monthly and Pro is $89/mo billed monthly; yearly billing is discounted (the page says up to 4 months saved). Enterprise is custom.

How many minutes do I actually get?

Credits, not minutes: video costs about 100 credits per minute, so Starter gives roughly 12 minutes and Pro roughly 60. Lip-sync doubles the cost.

Do I need Enterprise for SCORM?

Yes, SCORM export is listed under Enterprise, as are SAML/SSO and brand kits.

Synthesia or HeyGen?

Synthesia leans toward training and governance; HeyGen toward marketing and faster, cheaper entry. Compare your monthly minutes on both.
