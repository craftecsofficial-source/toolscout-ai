RepliQ (vendor: 2MTN AB) is an AI-powered video personalization platform built for cold outreach — sales, marketing, and recruiting teams use it to generate hyper-personalized videos, images, and text at scale from a prospect list. Its signature feature is turning a single photo or short recording into a talking AI avatar that can deliver a scripted message with a dynamic, prospect-specific background (the prospect's own website, LinkedIn profile, Google search results, or one of over a dozen other data sources), so each outreach touch looks individually recorded even when generated for hundreds of leads at once.

RepliQ differentiates itself from generic video-email tools (like Loom or Vidyard, which are built for one-to-one or one-to-few sharing) by being built specifically for one-to-many personalization at outbound scale, with the AI doing the work of tailoring each output to prospect data. It also bundles an AI cold-email writer (using GPT-4 Vision to analyze a lead's website/images and draft icebreakers), dynamic personalized thumbnails, custom sales pages per prospect, and full API/Zapier/Make integration so it can slot into an existing outbound tech stack rather than requiring a rebuild.

Best for

RepliQ is best suited for outbound sales teams, SDR agencies, and recruiters sending cold email or LinkedIn campaigns at meaningful volume who want each touch to look individually made without manually recording per-prospect video. It's a strong fit for teams that already use a cold email/LinkedIn sequencing tool and want to bolt on video/image personalization via API or Zapier. It's a weaker fit for anyone doing low-volume, high-touch one-to-one outreach (where a real Loom recording is just as fast), or teams that need guaranteed, responsive customer support, given the recurring support complaints in reviews.

Key features

01

Talking AI avatar from a photo

Upload a single photo (or record once) and RepliQ generates a talking avatar that delivers a personalized script per prospect, removing the need to re-record video for every lead.

02

Dynamic personalized video backgrounds

Videos can show a live-feeling background pulled from the prospect's own website, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Amazon, Twitter, Indeed, YouTube, Google Search, Google Maps, Crunchbase, GitHub, Spyfu, or any non-login URL.

03

AI cold email writer (GPT-4 Vision)

Analyzes both text and images from a lead's website to generate personalized icebreakers or full emails that reflect the actual context of the lead's business.

04

Custom personalized sales pages

Generates individual landing pages per prospect to increase the relevance of the outreach and give each lead a page that feels built specifically for them.

05

Dynamic image personalization at scale

Creates customized images incorporating prospect-specific data for use in email or LinkedIn outreach, similar to the video personalization pipeline.

06

Comprehensive engagement analytics

Tracks number of videos created, page views, CTA clicks, and percentage watch time per video, plus support for adding Google Analytics or Facebook Pixel to video pages.

07

Full API access and native integrations

API access plus direct integration with Zapier and Make lets teams trigger personalized content generation directly from their existing outbound sequencing tools.

08

Personalized thumbnails and dynamic CTAs

Video thumbnails and calls-to-action are personalized per recipient to lift open and click-through rates beyond what a generic video email achieves.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Genuinely differentiates from a Loom-style "record once, send to one person" tool by automating personalization across an entire prospect list at once.
  • Background source variety (LinkedIn, company websites, search results, social profiles) gives outreach a level of apparent research effort that would be very time-consuming to do manually per lead.
  • G2 reviewers repeatedly cite ease of setup and being usable by beginners without a technical background.
  • Analytics on watch time and CTA clicks give outbound teams real signal on which messaging or personalization angle is converting.
  • API plus Zapier/Make support means it can be wired into existing sequencers (e.g., cold email tools) rather than becoming a standalone silo.
  • Free tier (30 videos) is enough to genuinely test output quality before paying anything.

Cons

  • Customer support is the most consistent complaint in reviews — several users, including one 0.5-star G2 review, describe repeated unanswered support emails, particularly around cancellations and billing disputes.
  • The credit system (1 credit = 1 video/image/3 lines) means cost scales directly with campaign volume, so heavy outbound teams need to budget carefully rather than assuming a flat monthly fee covers everything.
  • New users report a learning curve — RepliQ has a lot of features (avatars, backgrounds, sales pages, analytics) and it takes time to understand the full workflow.
  • AI avatar lip-sync and photo-to-video realism is not always convincing; one reviewer noted the avatar or uploaded photo can look unnatural at times.
  • Deliverability and response-rate gains are inherently dependent on offer quality and existing email reputation/warm-up — RepliQ itself is explicit that it cannot promise meetings, only better engagement mechanics.

What reviewers say

RepliQ's G2 reviews are largely positive on core functionality (ease of use, personalized video generation, time savings) but include a vocal minority of very negative experiences centered on customer support and billing/cancellation handling. AppSumo lifetime-deal buyers rate it higher on average (4.66/5 from 29 reviews) than the G2 sample.

Frequently criticized

Frequently asked questions

How does RepliQ's pricing work?

RepliQ uses a credit system where 1 credit equals 1 personalized video background, 1 image, or 3 personalized lines of text. The free plan includes 30 videos; paid monthly plans start around $39/mo for roughly 200 credits, dropping to about $19/mo if billed annually.

Do I need to be on camera for the videos?

No — RepliQ can generate a talking AI avatar from a single uploaded photo, so you don't need to record yourself for every video.

What backgrounds can I personalize videos with?

RepliQ supports backgrounds sourced from a prospect's website, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Amazon, Twitter, Indeed, YouTube, Product Hunt, Google Search, Google Maps, Crunchbase, GitHub, Spyfu, and most URLs that don't require a login.

Will using RepliQ hurt my email deliverability?

RepliQ states that because each video/message is unique rather than a mass-identical blast, it's designed to improve rather than hurt deliverability and engagement signals — though actual deliverability still depends on domain warm-up, sending infrastructure, and list quality, which are outside RepliQ's control.

Does RepliQ integrate with my existing outreach tools?

Yes — RepliQ offers full API access plus native integrations with Zapier and Make, so it can be triggered from or fed into existing cold email and LinkedIn sequencing tools.

How long does it take to generate personalized videos for a full prospect list?

RepliQ states it can generate around 400 personalized videos in roughly 5 to 20 minutes, since each video's content is uniquely personalized per prospect.
