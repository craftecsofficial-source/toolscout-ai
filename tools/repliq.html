Great for outbound agencies and sales teams that send high volumes of cold email and want each message to carry a personalized video, image or landing page; skip it if you only email a few dozen prospects, need polished brand-safe video, or are wary that heavy automated personalization can look gimmicky. RepliQ builds personalized emails, videos, thumbnails, images and sales pages from a spreadsheet of leads, using the prospect's website or social profile as the video background, and plugs into outbound tools such as Smartlead, Instantly, Lemlist, Clay and HubSpot. Credits are consumed per personalized element, and a free trial offers 30 videos. The exact annual price and per-tier prices could not be confirmed from the pricing page today.

Best for

Good for lead-gen agencies, SDR teams and recruiters running volume outreach. Not for one-to-one relationship selling (a simple recorded video tool fits better), teams uneasy about AI-generated avatars or screenshots in prospecting, or buyers who need transparent annual pricing before talking to anyone.

Key features

01

Talking AI avatar from a photo

Upload a single photo (or record once) and RepliQ generates a talking avatar that delivers a personalized script per prospect, removing the need to re-record video for every lead.

02

Dynamic personalized video backgrounds

Videos can show a live-feeling background pulled from the prospect's own website, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Amazon, Twitter, Indeed, YouTube, Google Search, Google Maps, Crunchbase, GitHub, Spyfu, or any non-login URL.

03

AI cold email writer (GPT-4 Vision)

Analyzes both text and images from a lead's website to generate personalized icebreakers or full emails that reflect the actual context of the lead's business.

04

Custom personalized sales pages

Generates individual landing pages per prospect to increase the relevance of the outreach and give each lead a page that feels built specifically for them.

05

Dynamic image personalization at scale

Creates customized images incorporating prospect-specific data for use in email or LinkedIn outreach, similar to the video personalization pipeline.

06

Comprehensive engagement analytics

Tracks number of videos created, page views, CTA clicks, and percentage watch time per video, plus support for adding Google Analytics or Facebook Pixel to video pages.

07

Full API access and native integrations

API access plus direct integration with Zapier and Make lets teams trigger personalized content generation directly from their existing outbound sequencing tools.

08

Personalized thumbnails and dynamic CTAs

Video thumbnails and calls-to-action are personalized per recipient to lift open and click-through rates beyond what a generic video email achieves.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Built for one-to-many outbound: personalize videos, images, emails and landing pages from one spreadsheet instead of recording each clip.
  • Many background sources and integrations with common outbound stacks (Smartlead, Instantly, Lemlist, Clay, HubSpot) reduce glue work.
  • Free trial with 30 videos and no card lets you test reply impact on a small list.
  • Analytics and API/Zapier/Make support let agencies track and automate.

Cons

  • Pricing is hard to pin down: per-tier monthly prices and the annual price were not shown on the page today.
  • Credits scale with leads, so large campaigns can get expensive.
  • AI avatars and generated screenshots can read as impersonal or deceptive to some recipients, which risks reply rates and deliverability.
  • Review sentiment and support quality could not be verified today; check current reviews yourself.

What reviewers say

RepliQ's G2 reviews are largely positive on core functionality (ease of use, personalized video generation, time savings) but include a vocal minority of very negative experiences centered on customer support and billing/cancellation handling. AppSumo lifetime-deal buyers rate it higher on average (4.66/5 from 29 reviews) than the G2 sample.

Frequently praised

  • Ease of setup and use, even for outreach beginners
  • Personalized video/image generation genuinely improves engagement and reply rates
  • Saves significant time versus manual per-prospect outreach

Frequently criticized

  • Poor or unresponsive customer support, especially around refunds/cancellations
  • Learning curve to understand the full feature set
  • Some limitations in customization options and how pricing scales for advanced features

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

There is a free trial with 30 videos, no credit card required, per the pricing page.

How much does it cost?

The Monthly plan starts at $39/month, with seven credit tiers from 200 to 20,000; the page does not say which price applies to each tier. The annual price is advertised as 50% off but was not shown today, so confirm before buying.

What counts as a credit?

One lead is one credit; the FAQ says a credit covers one personalized element such as a video background, image, AI email, personalized line or sales page.

Does it work with my outreach tool?

The site lists Smartlead, Instantly, Lemlist, Expandi, Clay, HubSpot, GoHighLevel and Salesloft among others, plus an API.

How does it compare with Sendspark or HeyGen?

RepliQ is spreadsheet-driven and bundles emails, images and sales pages; Sendspark is more video-first, and HeyGen is a general avatar video tool.
