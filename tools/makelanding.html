MakeLanding targets the very first step of launching a product: getting a landing page live. Instead of starting from a blank template, users type a short description of their project and the AI produces a complete page draft, including headline copy, supporting text, a logo, and illustrations that match a chosen visual style.

The tool is aimed at solo founders, indie hackers, and small businesses who want to validate an idea quickly, without spending days on design or hiring a freelancer. Templates cover common categories like SaaS products, mobile apps, online courses, communities, and portfolios, and the AI can generate copy in multiple languages.

Once generated, pages can be edited - swapping images, adjusting copy, or changing buttons - before publishing directly from the platform. It is best thought of as a rapid-prototyping and launch tool rather than a full website builder for complex, multi-page sites.

Best for

MakeLanding is best for solo founders and indie hackers who need a landing page live within minutes to test demand for a new idea. It also suits small businesses and course creators who want a simple, professional-looking page without a design budget. Marketers running quick campaigns for a single offer can use it to spin up variations fast. Teams needing a full custom website with complex navigation or multiple page types will likely need a more robust website builder.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Collapses copywriting, logo design, and illustration into a single fast workflow, ideal for pre-launch validation
  • Removes the cost and delay of hiring a designer or copywriter for a simple one-page site
  • Multiple visual styles and templates give some variety across different product categories
  • Good starting point that can be manually refined rather than building from scratch

Cons

  • AI-generated layouts can feel generic and may require manual editing to stand out
  • Not designed for building full multi-page marketing sites or complex funnels
  • Illustration and logo quality/style options are limited compared to dedicated design tools
  • Ongoing hosting and editing depend on staying within the platform's ecosystem

Frequently asked questions

What does MakeLanding actually generate?

It generates a complete landing page including copy, a logo, and illustrations based on a short text description of your product.

Can I edit the AI-generated page?

Yes, users can edit copy, swap images, and adjust buttons after the initial generation.

Do I need design skills to use it?

No, the tool is built specifically so non-designers can produce a polished page without prior design experience.

Can I publish the page directly from MakeLanding?

Yes, pages can be previewed and published directly from the platform.

Does it support languages other than English?

Yes, MakeLanding can generate copy in over 50 languages.

Is MakeLanding good for a full multi-page website?

It is optimized for single landing pages rather than complex, multi-page websites.
