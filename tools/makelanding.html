MakeLanding is a good fit for founders who want many quick landing-page drafts to test ideas cheaply; skip it if you want to try before paying, or need a multi-page site or tight control over layout. You describe your project and it generates the page copy, a logo and illustrations, then lets you edit and publish.

The site lists templates for SaaS, apps, portfolios and courses, 6 art styles and 12 color themes, copy in 50+ languages, and a page editor. Higher plans add analytics, custom domains and support; Premium adds API access.

Best for

Best for indie hackers, solo founders and small businesses wanting a quick single-page launch or several test pages. Not for people who want to trial before paying, or who need a multi-page site, complex navigation or detailed layout control.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Combines copywriting, logo and illustration in one workflow, handy for validating ideas.
  • Page-count tiers suit people who generate many variations for different ideas.
  • Entry price of $9/month is low compared with hiring a freelancer.
  • Monthly billing with cancel-anytime avoids long commitments.

Cons

  • The site states there are no free previews and no refunds (credits to retry are offered instead), a real risk if the first output disappoints.
  • AI layouts can feel generic and may need hand editing.
  • Not designed for multi-page sites or complex funnels.
  • We could not load a dedicated pricing page; hosting details and what happens to published pages after cancelling are unverified.
