B12 is a good pick for solo professionals and small service businesses that want a decent website and client-handling tools in one subscription, but skip it if you need pixel-level design control or a large custom web app. It builds sites, stores and simple apps from a chat with an AI agent, with a visual editor alongside.

Beyond the site, B12 bundles a contact manager, client intake forms, email marketing and automation, and contracts with eSignatures, plus commerce, backends and integrations on the Website plans. Higher do-it-for-you plans (Professional and Advanced) add human help, phone support and, on Advanced, monthly site updates. We could not verify exact yearly pricing or do-it-for-you prices, which the pricing page does not show.

Best for

Best for consultants, coaches, agencies and other service businesses that want a site live quickly with intake forms and email in the same place. It is not for teams that need full design control, complex custom application logic, or published, predictable pricing for managed build services. Heavy AI users may also outgrow the monthly credit allowances.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • A single subscription covers the site plus intake forms, contact management, email marketing and eSignatures, which would otherwise be several separate tools.
  • The $24/month Website plan includes a custom domain (free for the first year) and removes B12 ads.
  • Plans can be switched between Website and Website + Client Engagement, or downgraded to Free, at any time.
  • A human-built path exists for people who do not want to do the work themselves.
  • Chat-based building lets non-technical owners request features like bookings or payments in plain language.

Cons

  • AI credits are capped: 5 per day on Free, 100 per month on Website, 300 on Client Engagement, so iterating a lot with the AI may require upgrading.
  • The jump from $24 to $78/month is steep if you only need one or two of the client tools.
  • Professional and Advanced prices are not published, and those plans cannot be downgraded to lower tiers.
  • Yearly pricing and discounts are not shown on the pricing page, so annual cost cannot be verified.
  • The free custom domain applies only to the first year and is subject to availability.

Frequently asked questions

Is B12 free?

There is a free plan with a b12sites.com subdomain and a small AI credit allowance (5 per day, max 30 per month). A custom domain needs a paid plan.

What does the paid trial cost?

Website and Website + Client Engagement both advertise $1 for the first 7 days, then $24/month and $78/month respectively.

Does B12 include email marketing and invoicing-style tools?

Email marketing, client intake, a contact manager and contracts with eSignatures are on the Website + Client Engagement plan. We could not confirm separate invoicing features on the pricing page.

Can B12 build the site for me?

Yes, Professional and Advanced are do-it-for-you plans, but their prices are not public - contact B12.

Can I switch plans later?

You can move between Website and Website + Client Engagement or down to Free. Professional and Advanced customers cannot downgrade to those lower plans.
