IdeaTwister is a good fit for solo founders and indie hackers choosing between several ideas who want a structured, inexpensive second opinion. Skip it if you want proof of demand: it does not interview customers, and its output is an AI-modeled estimate.

You submit a startup idea or describe an existing business, pay per run, and receive an interactive workspace. For founders it generates alternative directions, scores and ranks them against your original, flags the main risk and, on the deeper tiers, ends with a 90-day plan. For businesses, a separate $349 run ranks possible growth moves. There is no subscription, and failed runs are refunded automatically.

Best for

Built for indie hackers, first-time founders and career-changers who have several candidate ideas and want more structure than a casual chatbot conversation at a fraction of consultant cost. The business track suits small business owners and growth leads with a few plausible next moves to rank. Not for anyone who needs validated demand, investor-grade due diligence or customer interviews, nor for people who want unlimited free iteration or a subscription workspace.

Key features

01

Alternative directions

Beyond judging your idea, deeper tiers explore 10 or 25 alternative directions so you can see what else might work.

02

Scoring and ranking

Options are scored and ranked, with a side-by-side comparison on the higher tiers rather than a single pass/fail.

03

Market reality check

The $9 tier includes a market reality check, a founder decision scorecard, the main failure reason and the first proof you need.

04

Stress-test and yes/no/fix memo

Validate This Idea adds a stress-test and a final yes/no/fix memo; extra validations cost $19 each.

05

90-day plan

Deeper runs end with a 90-day recommendation or roadmap for the leading direction.

06

Business growth track

A separate run for existing businesses ranks growth moves and provides a 90-day execution plan.

07

Interactive workspace

Results are delivered as a browsable workspace; free sample workspaces let you preview the format.

08

Free tools

The site offers an Idea Viability Calculator, an Idea Validation Checklist and other free resources.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Ranked, scored options give a concrete decision frame when you are torn between several directions.
  • Starting at $9 lets you test cheaply and pay for depth only if an idea looks promising.
  • Bundles lower per-run cost noticeably for people testing several ideas, though credits expire after 90 days.
  • The business track means the tool is useful beyond the pre-launch stage.
  • Failed runs are refunded automatically, and results may be used commercially per the pricing page.
  • The 90-day plan turns a verdict into near-term actions.

Cons

  • No free trial of the paid analysis; only static sample workspaces are free.
  • Refunds apply only when a run fails to deliver, not when you dislike the verdict.
  • At $349, the business track is a sizeable speculative spend, and we could not verify its details beyond the homepage listing.
  • Market and demand findings are AI-modeled estimates, not primary research; treat them as hypotheses to test with real prospects.
  • Each tier is a separate purchase, so iterating repeatedly adds up unless you bought a bundle; bundle credits expire in 90 days.
  • Bundle totals on the pricing page do not exactly match its stated per-run prices, so check the checkout total.

Frequently asked questions

How much does IdeaTwister cost?

Founder runs are $9, $29 or $59 depending on depth; prepaid bundles ($32-$85) lower the per-run cost and last 90 days. The business growth run is listed at $349. There is no subscription.

Is there a free trial?

Not for the paid analysis. The site offers free sample workspaces and tools such as an Idea Viability Calculator so you can judge the format first.

Can I get a refund?

Runs that fail before your workspace is delivered are refunded automatically. Completed runs are not refundable.

How is this different from asking ChatGPT?

It is a fixed-price structured run that generates and scores alternative directions and returns a formatted workspace. You get a clear stopping point, but it is still AI output, so check claims yourself.

Which tier should I buy first?

If you only have one rough idea, start with the $9 check. Move to $29 if you want alternatives and a 90-day plan, and $59 if you are unsure which direction to pursue.
