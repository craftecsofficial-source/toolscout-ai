11x is a good fit for B2B revenue teams with real budget and a defined pipeline process that want AI agents to take over prospecting and inbound lead response; small teams and anyone who needs to see a price before talking to sales should skip it. Its site presents Alice, an outbound agent working email, social and dialing, and Julian, an inbound agent on voice, chat, SMS and WhatsApp that qualifies leads and books meetings. It also lists pre-built plays such as speed-to-lead, lead reactivation and website-visitor retargeting, plus data, research, routing and analytics modules. Pricing is not shown on the site as of today, so cost comparisons need a demo.

Best for

Suited to mid-market and enterprise sales or RevOps teams with a clear ideal customer profile and a CRM, who want to add coverage without hiring more SDRs. Not for solo founders, early-stage startups or small teams on tight budgets, nor for buyers who want transparent self-serve pricing or a free trial.

Key features

01

Alice - AI Outbound SDR

Identifies target accounts, researches prospects, and runs personalized multichannel sequences (email, LinkedIn, voice) that adapt based on replies, aiming to book qualified meetings without human sequencing.

02

Julian - AI Phone and Chat Agent

Answers inbound calls, website chat, SMS, and WhatsApp messages in real time, qualifies leads against configured criteria, and routes them to the right rep or books a meeting directly.

03

Managed Mailbox Infrastructure

Every Alice plan includes 11x-managed Gmail mailboxes with domain setup, warmup, inbox rotation, and deliverability monitoring, removing a common technical barrier to cold outbound at scale.

04

Bi-directional CRM Sync

Syncs activity, contact records, and deal data both ways with the customer's CRM so reps see AI-driven actions inside their existing system of record.

05

Multi-language Outreach

Supports outbound and inbound conversations in 105+ languages on higher tiers, aimed at globally distributed sales teams.

06

Signals and Triggers

Surfaces buying signals such as funding rounds, job changes, and website visitor activity to prioritize which accounts Alice reaches out to first.

07

Deep Research and AI Personalization

Pulls public company and contact data to generate personalized messaging angles automatically rather than relying on generic templates.

08

Analytics and Deliverability Dashboards

Tracks pipeline generated, reply rates, mailbox health, and call/chat outcomes so teams can monitor performance without exporting data to a separate BI tool.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • One vendor covers both outbound and inbound, so lead data and routing can live in one system.
  • Supports many channels (voice, chat, email, social, SMS, WhatsApp, human dialer) rather than email alone.
  • Offers ready-made plays for common situations like reactivating old leads and speeding up inbound response.
  • Lists CRM, Slack, G2 and API integrations, which matters for fitting into an existing stack.

Cons

  • No prices are published, so you cannot budget or compare without a sales conversation.
  • Not verified today: contract length, minimums and per-lead or per-seat terms; ask before signing.
  • Outcome claims on the site are vendor-reported; ask for references in your own segment.
  • AI-led cold outreach and calling carry deliverability and compliance risk (consent rules, spam filters) that you still own.

Frequently asked questions

How much does 11x cost?

Pricing is not public and I could not verify any figure today. The site directs buyers to book a demo at 11x.ai/demo. Older third-party reports cited annual-contract pricing in the tens of thousands of dollars, but treat that as unconfirmed.

What is the difference between Alice and Julian?

Alice is the outbound agent that prospects and runs outreach. Julian handles inbound leads and customers over voice, chat, SMS and WhatsApp, qualifying them and booking meetings.

Is there a free plan or trial?

None is listed on the homepage. The only call to action is a demo request.

Will it replace my SDR team?

The vendor frames it as driving revenue at scale, but complex conversations and closing typically still need people. Pilot it against your current results before reducing headcount.
