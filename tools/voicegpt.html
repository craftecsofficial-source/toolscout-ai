VoiceGPT (listed on Google Play as 'Voice AI: Chat Assistant', package gpt.voice.chatgpt) is a third-party Android application built by the small developer studio 'AI Chat & Voice Systems,' run by Jan Horak and based in Sumperk, Czechia. The app functions as a voice-first front end for GPT-based conversational AI: users speak a prompt, VoiceGPT transcribes and sends it, and the reply can be read aloud via text-to-speech, creating a hands-free conversational loop similar to talking to a voice assistant rather than typing into a chat window. It markets itself explicitly as an accessibility tool as much as a convenience app, positioning voice input/output as helpful for users with vision impairments or dyslexia who struggle with text-heavy chatbot interfaces.

The app's differentiating features beyond basic voice chat include a customizable wake-word ('Hey, Chat' by default, or a user-defined phrase) for hotword activation, an 'InstaBubble' floating overlay that lets users summon the assistant from inside any other app, the ability to be set as the device's default assistant via long-press on the home or power button (including Tasker integration for power users), and OCR support to read and process text from photos or documents. It supports 67 languages for both input and output, and includes a 'RunGPT' feature to execute AI-generated code across 70 programming languages directly in-app. Google Play's developer page notes the app is explicitly not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI or Google, despite building its interface around GPT models and ChatGPT-style branding. It has been on Google Play since February 2023 and was last updated July 2026, with the changelog describing a rebuild for 'the newest Android.'

Best for

VoiceGPT suits Android users who want a fast, spoken, hands-free way to interact with GPT-style AI throughout their day — including commuters, multitaskers, and users with vision or reading difficulties who benefit from voice-first and OCR features. It's a weaker fit for users who want a polished, officially OpenAI-affiliated app, or who are frustrated by short listening windows and message caps on the free tier.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • With 500K+ downloads and a changelog update as recent as July 2026 ('fully upgraded for the newest Android'), the app shows sustained, active maintenance rather than being abandoned.
  • The hotword plus InstaBubble combination genuinely delivers hands-free, cross-app access, which reviewers like Neil Murphy on Google Play specifically praised as close to what they wanted from a spoken AI tutor.
  • OCR and voice-first design give it a real accessibility use case for users with vision impairments or dyslexia, a niche most mainstream chat apps don't optimize for.
  • 67-language support and RunGPT's in-app code execution across 70 languages add genuine utility beyond a bare voice wrapper.
  • Support for setting VoiceGPT as the Android default assistant, including Tasker automation hooks, appeals to power users who want deep OS-level integration.

Cons

  • Google Play reviews average 3.8/5 across roughly 3,700+ reviews, with recurring complaints (EnragedN3wb, June 2024) that the wake-word listens but fails to act on the captured input, undermining the core hands-free feature.
  • Multiple reviewers report the microphone cutting off after just a few seconds of silence, interrupting natural pauses while composing a spoken thought (Neil Murphy, 2023).
  • The app is explicitly not affiliated with OpenAI or Google despite its GPT/ChatGPT-centric branding, which can create user confusion about whose AI model is actually being used.
  • The free tier's message cap (cited as 25 messages by reviewers) combined with ads pushes toward a subscription, and users note the sub removes ads but doesn't necessarily lift the message ceiling.
  • As a small independent developer (a single-person studio in Czechia) rather than a larger company, long-term support and rapid bug fixes aren't guaranteed at the same level as bigger competitors.

Frequently asked questions

What is VoiceGPT?

VoiceGPT (listed on Google Play as 'Voice AI: Chat Assistant') is a third-party Android app that adds voice input, spoken output, hotword activation, and OCR to a ChatGPT-style AI assistant.

Is VoiceGPT made by OpenAI?

No. The app's own listing states it is an independent third-party application not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI or Google, despite using GPT-based models.

Is VoiceGPT free?

It's free to download with ads and a limited number of messages (reviewers cite roughly 25); an in-app subscription removes ads and raises limits, though the exact subscription price is not listed publicly on the store page.

What rating does VoiceGPT have?

It holds a 3.8 out of 5 average rating from roughly 3,700+ reviews on Google Play, with 500K+ downloads as of research.

Can I use VoiceGPT hands-free?

Yes — it supports a customizable hotword (default 'Hey, Chat') and a floating 'InstaBubble' widget so you can trigger it from within other apps without unlocking to the main screen.

Does VoiceGPT work in languages other than English?

Yes, it supports voice and text input/output across 67 languages.
