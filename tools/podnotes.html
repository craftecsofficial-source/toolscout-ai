Podnotes is a good fit if you publish a podcast or YouTube show regularly and want one upload to become a transcript, summary, chapters and a batch of social, newsletter and blog drafts. Skip it if you only need an occasional plain transcript, because the monthly minute caps and subscription price are built for repeat use. It takes an audio or video file, a YouTube link or an RSS episode, and returns a speaker-separated transcript with timestamps, then lets you regenerate summaries and posts in different styles and languages (19+ listed). It also offers Magic Chat for asking questions across your uploaded episodes, audiograms for clips, and Notion/webhook hand-offs. Output is AI-written, so expect to edit before publishing.

Best for

Podcasters, YouTubers, ghostwriters and small agencies who publish on a regular schedule and want show notes and social copy drafted for them. Not for people who need a one-off transcript or a cheap pay-as-you-go option, for teams that need publish-ready copy with no editing, or for anyone whose volume is far above the minute caps and would rather pay per hour.

Key features

01

Multi-format transcription

Full transcripts with speaker-segmented distinction and timestamps, generated from uploaded audio/video files, YouTube links, or RSS feed episodes, supporting 19+ languages.

02

AI show notes and summaries

Automatically generated show notes, episode summaries, and chapter timestamps, with adjustable summary length, style, and output language, regenerable on demand.

03

Social and blog content generation

Converts a single episode into tweets, Twitter threads, LinkedIn posts, newsletters for Substack/Beehiiv, and SEO-optimized blog posts using over 100 customizable style presets or custom prompts.

04

Magic Chat

A conversational search tool that lets users chat with a single podcast episode or their entire uploaded library to retrieve information and quotes quickly.

05

Audiogram generation

Creates shareable audiogram video snippets from podcast moments, formatted for Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.

06

Organization and integrations

Folder and tag organization for uploaded podcasts, plus integrations with Notion and webhooks to push generated content into other workflows.

07

Shareable podcast pages

Generates a public page with the podcast's transcript and summary that can be shared directly with an audience.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • One upload yields transcript, summary, chapters and drafts for social, newsletter and blog, so you avoid stitching together separate tools.
  • Input from file, YouTube link or RSS episode removes the download-and-reupload step.
  • Summaries and posts can be regenerated in other styles or languages (19+ listed) without re-uploading.
  • Magic Chat lets you query your whole archive, which helps with finding quotes and repurposing older episodes.
  • Summary and content generation is listed as unlimited on every plan; only transcription minutes are capped.

Cons

  • Transcription minutes are capped per month (350 on the cheapest plan), so weekly long-form shows can run out quickly.
  • Speaker diarization, content calendar and guest research appear only from the $79 Studio plan up, though the cheaper plans' transcripts are described as speaker-separated on the homepage, so check what your tier does in the trial.
  • Plus ($49) and Studio ($79) give the same 700 minutes; the extra $30 buys the add-on tools only.
  • Annual prices are not shown on the pricing page, so the 20% saving cannot be verified before checkout.
  • AI-generated posts and show notes are drafts and generally need human editing for voice and accuracy.

Frequently asked questions

How many languages does Podnotes support?

The site lists 19+ languages for transcription and for generated summaries and content. Check the language picker in the app for your specific language before subscribing.

Is there a free trial?

The homepage says you can sign up free and get 50 minutes of transcription. The pricing page does not describe trial terms, so confirm at sign-up.

Can I use a YouTube link or RSS feed instead of uploading a file?

Yes. The site says you can import from YouTube links and RSS episodes as well as audio and video uploads.

What do I get on the cheaper plans versus Studio?

All plans list unlimited summaries, content and audiograms and unlimited upload size. Studio and above add audio diarization, a one-click content generator, a content calendar and a guest research tool. Plus and Studio both include 700 minutes.

What is Magic Chat?

A chat feature for asking questions about a single episode or your whole uploaded library, useful for finding quotes and topics.
