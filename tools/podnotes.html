Podnotes is a podcast repurposing tool that takes a single audio file, video file, YouTube link, or RSS feed episode and automatically produces a full package of derivative content: a speaker-segmented transcript, a summary, chapter timestamps, and a set of ready-to-publish assets like tweets, Twitter threads, LinkedIn posts, newsletters, and SEO-optimized blog posts. Users can select summary length, style, and output language from a library of 19+ supported languages, and regenerate content in a different style or language without re-uploading. A standout feature is Magic Chat, a conversational interface that lets users ask questions across a single episode or their entire podcast archive, effectively turning uploaded episodes into a searchable knowledge base. The platform also supports audiogram creation for short, shareable video snippets, and integrates with tools like Notion via webhooks for downstream publishing workflows.

Podnotes was built by 1811 Labs, an indie software studio founded by Vatsal Sanghvi that has also shipped several other AI productivity tools including Audionotes, Builder Kit, and Summarify. Podnotes launched with early traction covered by Product Hunt, Ben's Bites, Indie Hackers, and There's An AI For That, and it was previously sold as a lifetime deal on AppSumo, where it holds a strong buyer rating. It has since moved to a standard minutes-based monthly subscription model, positioning itself for podcasters, content creators, ghostwriters, and agencies who need to repurpose long-form audio into multiple content formats without manually writing show notes or social copy for every episode. Its pricing scales from a solo creator plan up through an Agency tier and custom Enterprise plans for networks managing multiple shows.

Best for

Podnotes suits podcasters, content marketers, ghostwriters, and small agencies who need to repurpose episodes into transcripts, show notes, and social/blog content without manual writing. It is a weaker fit for casual or infrequent podcasters who just need occasional transcription, since dedicated transcription tools may be cheaper for that narrower use case.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Podnotes turns one upload into a full multi-format content package (transcript, summary, social posts, blog post) instead of requiring separate tools for each output.
  • Support for 19+ languages on both input and output makes it usable for non-English podcasters and international content teams.
  • Magic Chat's conversational search across an entire podcast archive is a distinctive feature reviewers highlight for information retrieval and content research.
  • AppSumo reviewers rate the product 4.72/5 across 32 verified reviews, citing continuous product updates and strong feature coverage relative to its original lifetime-deal price.
  • Users on AppSumo describe the UI as clean and note that support has continuously improved functionality since launch.
  • The tool covers a wide range of output presets (100+ styles) beyond basic show notes, including custom prompts for specific content needs.

Cons

  • Mid-tier subscription pricing ($49-79/month) is higher than dedicated transcription-only tools, which may be overkill for users who just need a transcript.
  • Minutes-based limits mean high-frequency or long-form podcasters can burn through monthly allotments quickly, especially on the Solo Cast tier.
  • Advanced capabilities like audio diarization, content calendars, and guest research are gated behind the Studio tier and above ($79/month+).
  • The product was previously available as an AppSumo lifetime deal, which is now sold out, meaning new users only have access to the standard recurring subscription.

Frequently asked questions

How many languages does Podnotes support?

Podnotes supports 19+ languages for both transcription inputs and generated outputs like summaries and social content.

Can I generate unlimited content from one podcast episode?

Yes, once an episode is transcribed, users can generate unlimited social posts, blog posts, and other content assets from it at no extra per-generation cost within their plan.

Does Podnotes transcribe YouTube videos directly?

Yes, users can paste a YouTube URL directly instead of uploading a file, and Podnotes will transcribe and process it the same way as an uploaded audio or video file.

Does Podnotes support RSS feeds?

Yes, users can connect an RSS feed and select a specific episode to import and process without manually downloading and uploading the file.

Is there a free plan or trial?

Yes, new users get a free trial with 50 minutes of transcription to test the platform before subscribing to a paid plan.

What is Magic Chat?

Magic Chat is Podnotes' conversational search feature that lets users chat with a single podcast or their entire podcast library to retrieve information and quotes for reference.
