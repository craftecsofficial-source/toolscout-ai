Coveo is aimed at large organizations with fragmented content and real engineering resources, and is overkill for small teams that just need a site search box or an FAQ bot. It is an AI search and relevance platform that indexes content from many enterprise systems and powers search, recommendations, generative answers and AI-agent retrieval for support portals, websites, ecommerce catalogs and internal employee search. It sits on top of your existing help desk, CMS and catalog rather than replacing them.

Best for

Suits mid-market and large enterprises with many content sources, a support, digital experience or commerce platform team, and enough query volume to justify a quoted contract. Not for small businesses, solo founders or anyone wanting self-serve signup and a transparent monthly price; also not a help desk, so it will not replace ticketing.

Key features

01

Unified Relevance Engine

Indexes and ranks content from hundreds of connectors (Salesforce, ServiceNow, SharePoint, product catalogs, and more) using machine learning rather than static keyword rules.

02

Generative Answering

Produces AI-generated, cited answers grounded in a company's own indexed content, reducing hallucination risk versus a standalone LLM.

03

Case Deflection & Agent Assist

Surfaces relevant knowledge articles and suggested responses to customers and support agents to reduce ticket volume and resolution time.

04

Commerce Search & Recommendations

Delivers personalized product search results, autocomplete, and merchandising recommendations tuned to individual shopper behavior.

05

Workplace Search

Provides a single search layer across internal wikis, file shares, and business applications so employees can find information without switching tools.

06

Relevance Analytics & ML Tuning

Continuously learns from click and conversion data to automatically refine ranking models over time, with dashboards to monitor search performance.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Connectors for major enterprise systems reduce custom integration work.
  • One platform spans support, website, commerce and workplace search, which can cut vendor sprawl.
  • Developer tooling (Passage Retrieval API, MCP Server) lets you ground your own AI agents in indexed company content.
  • Behavioral AI uses user activity to improve ranking over time.
  • Commerce offering includes a merchandising hub and conversational product discovery.

Cons

  • No published prices; the pricing page only offers Request Pricing or Talk to sales.
  • Pricing is unit-based (100k queries per unit, catalog items) with separate add-ons for generative AI and Case Assist, so costs scale with traffic.
  • Prices are in USD by default; other currencies only on request.
  • Setup, connector configuration and relevance tuning usually need an implementation project.
  • Too much platform for simple site search or small knowledge bases.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Coveo cost?

Not published. The pricing page asks you to request pricing; it explains that units include 100k queries per month and that generative AI and Case Assist are add-ons.

Is there a free trial?

The site has a Start a free trial link, but the pricing page gives no length or terms, so confirm with Coveo.

Does Coveo replace our help desk or CMS?

No. It layers search and AI answers over existing systems such as Salesforce, ServiceNow, SharePoint and ecommerce catalogs.

Can I use it to ground my own AI agents?

Yes, Coveo offers a Passage Retrieval API and an MCP Server for connecting agents to indexed enterprise content.

Which pricing model applies to ecommerce?

Commerce is sold modularly with units covering queries, recommendations and catalog items; pricing is quote-based.
