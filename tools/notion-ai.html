Notion AI is the artificial-intelligence layer built into Notion, the all-in-one workspace for docs, wikis, projects and databases used by individuals and companies including OpenAI and Figma. Rather than being a standalone AI product, it's a set of AI capabilities — writing assistance, Q&A search, autofill, an autonomous 'Notion Agent' and AI meeting notes — layered directly into the existing Notion workspace a team already uses.

Its core differentiator is that it's contextual to a team's actual content: because Notion AI operates on top of a workspace's existing pages, databases and connected tools (Slack, GitHub, Google Drive and more), it can answer questions or generate content grounded in a company's real internal knowledge, rather than acting as a generic, context-free writing tool.

Best for

Notion AI is best suited for existing or prospective Notion power users — teams that already use or plan to use Notion as their central docs/wiki/project hub and want AI layered directly on top of that content. It's a poor fit for anyone shopping for a standalone AI writing tool independent of a workspace platform, or for teams hoping AI alone will organize a messy, disorganized Notion setup.

Key features

01

Notion Agent

An AI agent that completes multi-step tasks using context pulled from Notion pages, connected apps and the web, going beyond simple text generation to actually execute work.

02

AI writing and editing

Drafts, rewrites, summarizes, translates and adjusts tone of text directly inside any Notion page, without needing to copy content to an external tool.

03

Database autofill and Q&A

Can automatically populate database properties and answer natural-language questions using information already stored across a workspace's pages and databases.

04

AI Meeting Notes

Automatically transcribes and summarizes meetings with follow-up action items, without requiring a separate bot to join the call.

05

Enterprise Search

Searches across connected tools — Slack, GitHub, Jira, Box, OneDrive, Salesforce and Asana (several in beta) — in addition to Notion content.

06

Research mode

Uses deeper multi-step reasoning to produce detailed reports pulling from workspace content, connected tools and current web information.

07

Custom Agents

Lets teams build agents that handle repetitive tasks autonomously; free to try, then billed at $10 per 1,000 monthly Notion credits on top of seat pricing.

08

Core Notion workspace features

Beyond AI, Notion provides docs, wikis, database-driven project management, forms and a public-facing site builder.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • AI is grounded in a team's actual Notion content and connected apps, making answers and drafts more relevant than a generic, context-free chatbot
  • Eliminates tool-switching — writing, summarizing and Q&A happen directly inside the same workspace used for docs and project tracking
  • Notion Agent can complete multi-step tasks rather than just generating text
  • Enterprise Search pulls in Slack, GitHub, Jira and other connected tools, useful for teams whose knowledge is scattered across multiple systems
  • Strong value for teams that were already going to pay for Notion's core workspace product, since AI is bundled rather than a separate purchase
  • AI Meeting Notes requires no separate bot joining the call, unlike many standalone meeting-transcription tools

Cons

  • Full, unlimited Notion AI access requires the Business plan at $20/seat/mo — Free and Plus users only get a short trial, a common source of buyer confusion
  • Output quality for open-ended writing (marketing copy, sales messaging, brand voice) is frequently described as generic, needing human polishing
  • Custom Agents and Workers add usage-based credit costs on top of per-seat pricing, making total cost harder to predict at scale
  • Performance in large, heavily-linked workspaces with many databases is reported as slow and occasionally sluggish
  • AI features are only as good as how well-organized the underlying workspace is
  • Q&A, search and database automation are described as solid but inconsistent compared to the stronger writing/summarization features

What reviewers say

Notion as a workspace platform is very highly rated on both G2 and Capterra (4.7/5 on each), but that rating reflects Notion broadly rather than the AI features specifically, since neither platform separates AI-only feedback. Reddit and dedicated review sites show a more mixed picture of the AI itself: power users with well-organized workspaces report genuine value from writing, summarization and Q&A, while users who came to Notion primarily hoping AI would organize scattered information for them are often disappointed. A recurring theme is that Notion AI's full capability now sits behind the $20/seat/mo Business plan.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to pay extra for Notion AI on top of my Notion plan?

Full, unlimited Notion AI (including Notion Agent) is bundled into the Business plan at $20/seat/mo and Enterprise; Free and Plus plans only include a limited trial of AI features.

Can Notion AI answer questions using data from other tools like Slack?

Yes, via Enterprise Search (currently in beta on Business and Enterprise plans), which searches across connected apps including Slack, GitHub, Jira, Box, OneDrive, Salesforce and Asana in addition to Notion content.

What's the difference between Notion AI and Notion Agent?

Notion AI broadly refers to the writing, summarization and Q&A features available across the product, while Notion Agent specifically completes multi-step, agentic tasks using context from Notion, connected apps and the web.

Is Notion AI good for writing marketing copy?

It's serviceable for drafts and summaries but frequently described by users as generic or safe for brand-voice-sensitive copy, typically needing human editing.

How much do Custom Agents cost?

Custom Agents are free to try, then cost $10 per 1,000 monthly Notion credits, billed on top of a workspace's regular per-seat subscription cost.

Is there a student or education discount for Notion?

Yes — the Plus plan (with a 1-member limit) is free for students and educators who sign up with an eligible school email address.
