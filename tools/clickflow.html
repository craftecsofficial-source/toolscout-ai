ClickFlow suits small marketing teams and agencies that want one tool to plan SEO work, draft articles and track results, and it is easy to try because of the free plan. Solo bloggers on tight budgets and teams wanting mature content-scoring tools should look elsewhere. It connects to your site and Google Search Console, produces a prioritised 30-day list of pages, posts and fixes, writes drafts for them, and tracks rankings and clicks against that plan.

It is made by the growth agency Single Grain. Drafts can go to WordPress or out via a review link, and an MCP server lets you use it from Claude, Cursor or Windsurf. Earlier descriptions of content-decay reports and split-testing experiments could not be re-confirmed from the current homepage, and the dedicated pricing page failed to load, so plan details here come from the homepage only.

Best for

Good for: in-house marketers, SEO managers and small agencies that want a ready plan and drafts, and want a free trial of the workflow. Not for: solo bloggers wanting a sub-$50 tool, sites with no Search Console data, or teams that need published seat and article limits before buying.

Key features

01

Content Decay Reports

Automatically tracks declining organic traffic on existing pages and visualizes the drop-off in graph form so teams know what to fix first.

02

Experiments (Split Testing)

Tests page elements like H1 tags, meta descriptions, or content blocks against a control version and measures the traffic/CTR impact over time.

03

AI Content Editor

Generates SEO-ready drafts with an embedded keyword checklist, People Also Ask questions, and competitor ranking URLs for every article in the roadmap.

04

Topical Map & Strategy Roadmap

Analyzes the site and Google Search Console data to output a prioritized 30-day plan of pages, posts, and technical fixes.

05

Automated Reporting

Tracks rankings and clicks against the original plan and reports what worked, feeding results back into the next cycle.

06

CMS Publishing Integrations

Publishes drafts directly to a connected CMS instead of requiring manual copy-paste into a blog platform.

07

Brand Voice Matching

Learns a company's existing tone and terminology so AI drafts read as though written by the in-house team, not a generic model.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The free plan includes 5 real AI articles, so you can judge quality without a card.
  • It starts from your own Search Console data rather than a blank keyword list.
  • Planning, writing and reporting sit in one loop, reducing tool switching.
  • Drafts can be shared via a review link for approvers before publishing to WordPress.
  • An MCP server allows use from AI coding or chat tools.
  • Backing by a working SEO agency, with an optional managed tier.

Cons

  • Essentials is $159/month with no visible mid-tier between it and custom pricing.
  • Only the free plan's 5-article limit is stated; Essentials post and seat limits are unpublished, so check before committing.
  • The dedicated pricing page returned an error when we checked, so only homepage pricing was confirmed.
  • The free plan hosts your blog on a ClickFlow subdomain, not your own domain.
  • Done For You is a sales call into agency services, not self-serve.
  • Public third-party reviews are scarce; we did not verify any today, so quality claims are unproven.

Frequently asked questions

Is ClickFlow free?

There is a forever-free plan with 5 AI blog articles and a hosted subdomain blog. More requires Essentials.

How much is the paid plan?

Essentials is $159/month per its homepage on 2026-10-23. Done For You is custom.

How many articles does Essentials include?

Not stated on the page we could read. Ask before buying.

Do I need Google Search Console?

It builds its plan from your site and Search Console data, so you will get the most from it with an existing connected site.

How does it compare with Surfer SEO?

Surfer is stronger on content scoring and optimisation of drafts; ClickFlow emphasises a plan-draft-report loop and an agency option.
