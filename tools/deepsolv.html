Deepsolv is a good fit for D2C brands and agencies that spend meaningfully on Meta ads and are short on a systematic way to pick the next creative to test; if your budget is small or sits mostly on Google or TikTok, skip it. It is a creative-intelligence tool for Facebook and Instagram ads: it reads your ad metrics (CTR, CPC, ROAS, fatigue), tracks competitor ads, and mines reviews, comments, DMs and Reddit for customer language, then turns that into a weekly test plan ranked by confidence. A 'Brand Brain' stores what you have already tested and why it worked or failed, and the site also lists drafting of hooks, scripts and static ads plus a Q&A assistant. Pricing is not published, and the case-study numbers on the site come from the company itself.

Best for

Meta-focused growth teams at D2C brands, and agencies managing paid social for several brands, once creative testing volume is high enough that picking what to test by gut feel is costly. NOT for: businesses without an active Meta ad program, teams that need one tool across Google, TikTok and other channels, or buyers who need a public price before talking to sales.

Key features

01

Ranked Weekly Test Plans

Surfaces the highest-confidence creative experiments to run next, ranked by expected impact rather than left as an unprioritized backlog.

02

Performance Analysis

Continuously evaluates live and past ad performance - CTR, CPC, ROAS, conversion rate and audience fatigue - to flag which creatives are winning, plateauing or declining.

03

Competitor Ad Tracking

Monitors competitor Meta ads on an ongoing basis, surfacing their hooks, offers, formats and messaging changes as they happen.

04

Customer Insight Mining

Aggregates reviews, DMs, comments and Reddit discussions into concrete creative angles and messaging ideas grounded in real customer language.

05

Brand Brain

Retains a history of a brand's own past creative tests and outcomes so new recommendations build on what has already been learned rather than repeating dead ends.

06

Test / Iterate / Stop Recommendations

Classifies each active creative as worth testing further, worth iterating on, or worth stopping, turning raw performance data into a clear next action.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Consolidates ad performance, competitor creative and customer-feedback research into one ranked list, reducing manual spreadsheet work
  • Each recommendation comes with reasons and a confidence ranking, which makes it easier to defend a test choice to a team or client
  • Brand Brain keeps a history of past tests, so recommendations can build on your own results rather than generic advice
  • Customer-insight mining (objections, desires, buying triggers from reviews and Reddit) is something most Meta reporting tools do not attempt

Cons

  • Pricing is not published anywhere on the site, so you cannot compare cost against alternatives without a sales conversation
  • Built for Meta only; brands with real budget on Google, TikTok or programmatic need other tools for those channels
  • No independent review rating (G2, Capterra, etc.) was found, so satisfaction is hard to verify; client logos and results such as 4.5x+ ROAS are vendor-reported
  • Output quality depends on how much ad history you have; a brand with little spend or few past tests gives it less to learn from (our inference, not a vendor statement)

Frequently asked questions

What does Deepsolv do?

It analyzes your Meta ad performance, competitor ads and customer feedback, then ranks which creatives to test, iterate on or stop each week.

How much does Deepsolv cost, and is there a free plan?

Pricing is not public. The site only offers a demo booking and lists no free plan or self-serve tier.

Does it cover Google or TikTok ads?

No. It is built for Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads; customer signals come from Instagram, Meta comments and Reddit.

Does it produce finished ad creative?

It focuses on deciding what to make. The site says it can draft hooks, scripts and static ads, but it is not positioned as a full creative production studio.

Is there independent proof it works?

Not that we found. Customer logos and ROAS figures are published by Deepsolv itself, so ask for references during the demo.
