Monterey AI is a reasonable pick for product teams that already have a lot of messy feedback and want AI to sort it, but you should skip it if you need a clear public price or a product with a settled independent future. It reads feedback from calls, email, chat, support desks and community channels, groups it into themes, flags bugs and feature requests, and can route items to the right team. The homepage now carries a banner saying the company has been acquired by Reforge, and the product appears under the name Reforge Insight Analytics, so check what the current offering and contract terms are before committing.

On top of the analysis, it offers a question-and-answer interface for querying your feedback, a feedback widget, a portal and surveys, and connects to Slack, Linear, Jira and Asana. The homepage shows testimonials from named people and vendor-reported efficiency figures; we did not verify those independently.

Best for

Product managers and customer-insight teams at startups and mid-size companies who collect feedback in many places and want AI to cluster and route it. It is not for buyers who need fixed, published pricing, for teams that want a deep product-usage analytics suite, or for anyone who needs certainty about the product's independent roadmap given the Reforge acquisition.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Removes much of the manual reading and tagging of support tickets, call notes and chat logs.
  • Covers many input channels, so patterns that span support and sales calls become visible.
  • Routing of bugs and requests to teams can shorten the path from feedback to a ticket in Jira, Linear or Asana.
  • Seats are unlimited on the listed plan, so wider sharing across the company does not raise the price by headcount.

Cons

  • No prices are published; even the free option has no stated limits, so you cannot budget without talking to the vendor.
  • The Reforge acquisition banner and rebrand mean plans, packaging and roadmap may change.
  • Pricing scales with feedback volume, so costs can grow as you add sources.
  • Theme quality depends on how much and how varied the feedback is that you feed in.
  • Efficiency and savings figures on the site are vendor claims, not independent measurements.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Monterey AI cost?

The pricing page shows one paid plan with custom pricing based on feedback volume and asks you to request a demo. A free start option is offered but its limits are not described. We could not verify the older $39/month Starter price.

Is Monterey AI still an independent product?

Its homepage says the company has been acquired by Reforge and shows the product as Reforge Insight Analytics. Ask the vendor about roadmap and contract terms before signing.

Which tools does it connect to?

The site lists Zendesk, Discord, Slack, Linear, Jira and Asana, plus calls, email and chat as sources. Custom integrations and an API are part of the paid plan.

Does it include a feedback widget?

Yes, the paid plan lists a widget, portal and survey.

How is usage limited?

Seats are unlimited; usage is measured by feedback data consumption. The FAQ questions about how it is calculated and what happens when exceeded have no visible answers, so ask the vendor.
