PhotoPacks.AI is a good fit for job seekers and professionals who want a passable profile photo for about $24 without booking a photographer; skip it if you need guaranteed likeness, precise art direction, or editing after generation. You upload about 10 casual photos, and the service produces 40+ headshots across roughly 20 settings with varied outfits and expressions, which it says arrive in under an hour.

Pricing is a single one-time payment rather than a subscription, and the site advertises a full refund if you request it within 7 days. A separate Teams page targets companies wanting bulk headshots; it mentions bulk savings but shows no prices, so you would need to ask. The site claims over 900,000 headshots created, which we could not independently verify. The workflow is fixed and automated, so you pick from what you get rather than steering the result.

Best for

Good for individuals (job seekers, consultants, remote workers, agents) wanting an affordable, fast LinkedIn or resume photo. Not for anyone needing exact likeness for high-stakes use, custom backgrounds or wardrobe, editing of results, or workplaces where AI-generated profile photos are disallowed or frowned upon.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • At $24 for 40+ images, cost per image is far below a studio session.
  • Delivery in under an hour (per the site) means a usable photo the same day.
  • Needs only casual phone photos, with no styling or location.
  • One-time payment with no subscription to cancel.
  • Advertised 7-day refund window lowers the risk of trying it.

Cons

  • Likeness and quality can vary between batches and depend on the photos you upload.
  • No editing or prompt control; a poor batch means starting over or seeking a refund.
  • Independent review coverage is thin; we did not verify third-party reviews today.
  • Teams pricing is not shown publicly.
  • AI-generated likenesses raise authenticity concerns on some professional platforms.
