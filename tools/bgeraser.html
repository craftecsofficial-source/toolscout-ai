BgEraser is a good pick if you need quick, free background cutouts and don't want an account; skip it if you need an API, fine manual edge control, or a broader photo editor. It removes the background from uploaded JPG, PNG or WebP images automatically and returns a transparent PNG, letting you process up to 20 images per batch and download them together as a ZIP. The site says uploads and results are removed from its servers within two hours. It also offers desktop apps for Windows (Microsoft Store) and Mac (App Store); we could not verify their current pricing today. The site does not mention an API.

Best for

Good for e-commerce sellers, freelancers, students and anyone doing occasional batches of product or portrait cutouts. Not for teams that need an API or automated pipelines, designers who want manual mask refinement, or people who need RAW/TIFF input or full photo retouching.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • No account is needed, so you can go from upload to download in a minute.
  • Batches of 20 with a single ZIP download suit small catalog jobs.
  • Stated 2-hour deletion of uploads and results is a plus for private photos.
  • The web tool shows no stated usage cap or paywall on the homepage.

Cons

  • Single-purpose: no retouching, shadows, resizing or composing.
  • Only JPG, PNG and WebP inputs; RAW and TIFF are not listed.
  • No API is mentioned, so it can't be wired into automated workflows.
  • Desktop app pricing could not be confirmed, and the pricing page was blocked to our fetch.
  • We did not independently test cutout quality on hair or glass; check a few of your own images.
