Beemer is built around a single core promise: describe your business and get a usable pitch deck back in about two minutes. It targets the earliest stage of fundraising or sales prep, where founders need something presentable fast rather than a fully bespoke, agency-designed deck.

The tool offers three main presentation categories - investor pitches, sales decks, and showcase materials - along with a library of more than 35 slide layouts so users can pick a look that fits their brand. Output can be exported to either Google Slides or PowerPoint, letting founders continue editing in the tool they're already comfortable with.

Beemer's free tier covers the core deck generation, while a small paid upgrade adds extras like an executive summary document and a ready-to-send investor pitch email, which are useful for founders who also need supporting materials beyond the slides themselves.

Best for

Beemer works best for early-stage founders who need a fast, low-cost first draft of an investor pitch deck before a fundraising round. It also suits small business owners preparing simple sales or showcase presentations without a big budget for design help. Teams that want to iterate quickly on deck structure before investing in a fully custom design will find the speed useful. Later-stage startups needing a highly polished, narrative-driven deck for a large raise may want to pair it with additional professional design input.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Produces a usable pitch deck draft in about two minutes, saving significant early-stage founder time
  • Low, largely one-time cost makes it accessible for bootstrapped founders compared to hiring a designer
  • Exporting to both Google Slides and PowerPoint means teams aren't locked into a single downstream tool
  • Bundled executive summary and pitch email add-ons address more than just the slides themselves

Cons

  • Slide design range, while decent, is narrower than specialized pitch deck design agencies or premium tools
  • Quality of the final deck depends heavily on how detailed and clear the initial business description is
  • Less suited to founders needing highly bespoke storytelling, animation, or complex data visualization in their deck
  • As a newer, smaller tool it may have less brand recognition than established players like Slidebean or Pitch

Beemer generates a complete pitch deck in roughly two minutes from a short business description.

Yes, decks can be exported to either Google Slides or Microsoft PowerPoint.

Is Beemer free to use?
