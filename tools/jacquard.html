Jacquard is a good fit for large consumer brands running high-volume CRM programs that need on-brand, compliance-checked copy at scale; it is the wrong choice for small teams, startups or anyone wanting self-serve pricing. Formerly Phrasee (rebranded in June 2024), the London company now describes its product as a set of AI agents: a Performance agent that looks for the next engagement win, a Compliance agent that checks rules before sending, a Brand agent that enforces voice during generation, and an orchestration agent that coordinates the other three and aims for one-click approval. It works across email, SMS, push, in-app, paid social and web, learns from a brand's approvals and history, and plugs into ESP and CRM systems such as Salesforce, Adobe, Braze, Iterable, Bloomreach and MessageGears. Performance figures on the site are the company's own claims, and an AI visibility module is listed as coming soon. The real website is jacquard.com; jacquard.ai is not the company's site.

Best for

Good for enterprise retail, travel, financial-services and other consumer brands with large messaging volumes, brand and legal review bottlenecks, and an existing ESP. Not for small businesses, solo marketers or teams without a CRM program of significant scale, and not for anyone who needs to see prices or start a trial without talking to sales.

Key features

01

Generate + Calibrate

Language² architecture generates thousands of message variants per brief while enforcing deterministic, brand-specific stylistic guardrails before any performance scoring happens.

02

Audience-Wide Optimization

Uses experimental design to identify the single best-performing message for an entire audience, aiming to replace traditional A/B testing with statistical certainty.

03

Contextual Messaging

1:1 personalization engine that analyzes product and user-profile data to generate a tailored message per user rather than relying on static audience segments.

04

Omnichannel distribution

Delivers optimized, brand-calibrated content across email, SMS, mobile push, in-app, web push and even ChatGPT-integrated channels from a single strategic brief.

05

Global language calibration

Generates and calibrates native-quality copy across 27+ languages and dialects, applying performance learnings from one market to inform others.

06

Automated governance

Replaces subjective, human-bottlenecked approval with data-led governance, ensuring every variant is compliant, on-brand and performant before submission.

07

Creative Intelligence reporting

Provides explainable performance data on why specific language resonates, plus a quarterly Brand Voice IQ report from Jacquard's in-house linguists.

08

CRM/ESP integrations

Native connections to Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Braze, Iterable, Adobe Campaign and MessageGears for direct campaign deployment.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Brand and compliance checks happen at generation time, which cuts review back-and-forth for regulated or tightly governed brands.
  • Long track record from the Phrasee era in email subject-line optimization, with named enterprise customers.
  • Wide integration list: Salesforce, Adobe, Braze, Iterable, Bloomreach, Optimizely, MoEngage, Cordial, MessageGears and more.
  • Covers many channels from one brief, including email, SMS, push, in-app and paid social.
  • A free brand analysis tool gives a low-commitment look at how it reads your tone.

Cons

  • No public pricing or free trial; every evaluation starts with a sales conversation.
  • Aimed at high-volume programs; the value case is weak for small send volumes.
  • Headline performance numbers are vendor-reported and not independently verified.
  • Brand-specific and per-customer models need your historical data and approvals before they pay off.
  • Reporting and dashboard flexibility were not verified today; treat as a question for the demo.
  • The AI visibility (Brand Insights) module is not yet released.

Frequently asked questions

Is Jacquard the same company as Phrasee?

Yes. Phrasee rebranded to Jacquard in June 2024. The active site is jacquard.com.

How much does Jacquard cost?

Pricing is not published. It is sold through demos and custom enterprise contracts, so expect to talk to sales to get a quote.

Does Jacquard have a free plan or trial?

No free plan is listed. The site offers a free brand analysis tool and demo booking.

Which platforms does Jacquard integrate with?

The site names Salesforce, Adobe, Braze, Iterable, Bloomreach, Optimizely, Epsilon, MoEngage, Insider, Airship, Movable Ink, Cordial, MessageGears and Marigold Liveclicker.

How is it different from Persado?

Both target enterprise marketing language; Jacquard emphasizes agent-based brand and compliance guardrails tied to your ESP, while Persado is known for its emotion-and-language scoring approach. Compare both in a demo.
