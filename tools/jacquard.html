Jacquard is an enterprise AI platform, formerly known as Phrasee, that generates and continuously optimizes marketing language - primarily for email subject lines, SMS, and push notifications - for large consumer brands. The company rebranded from Phrasee to Jacquard in June 2024, naming itself after the Jacquard loom, the early 19th-century programmable weaving machine that is often cited as a precursor to modern computing, a reference meant to evoke automated, patterned craft at scale. Jacquard's platform takes a marketing brief and generates thousands of on-brand language variants (up to 2,500 in 30 seconds, per the company), each calibrated against the client's specific tone, terminology and regional voice rather than generated from a generic model. Customers include Adidas, Costco, Best Buy, Tesco, Nestlé, United Airlines, Sephora, TUI, and GoDaddy, and the platform integrates directly with major CRM/ESP systems including Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Braze, Iterable, Adobe Campaign and MessageGears.

Jacquard differentiates itself from generic AI writing tools through what it calls a "Language²" architecture: deterministic brand-voice guardrails filter content for stylistic compliance before it's ever scored for predicted performance, aiming to avoid the brand-safety risk of raw LLM output. Its Audience-Wide Optimization feature is positioned as a replacement for traditional A/B testing, using experimental design to identify the single highest-performing message for an entire audience with statistical certainty rather than splitting traffic across variants, while Contextual Messaging pushes toward 1:1 personalization using product and user-profile data instead of static segments. The company reports strong, quantified customer outcomes - Currys saw a 42% uplift in opens and 102% increase in revenue after integrating Jacquard for real-time optimization, and P&O Cruises reported consistent quarter-over-quarter performance gains. Notably, the jacquard.ai domain is not the company's active website (it currently sits as a for-sale/parked domain); Jacquard's real corporate site is jacquard.com.

Best for

Jacquard is built for large, established consumer brands (retail, travel, hospitality, financial services) running high-volume CRM programs across email, SMS and push who need AI-generated copy that stays strictly on-brand at scale. It's a poor fit for small businesses or teams wanting self-service pricing, given its enterprise-only, demo-gated sales process and reviewer complaints about cost.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • G2 reviewers rate Jacquard 4.3/5 across 31 reviews; multiple reviewers specifically call out ease of use for email marketing and content creation.
  • A 6-year Wowcher partnership (dating back to the Phrasee era) reports a sustained 37% uplift in transactional email opens and 15% on marketing campaigns.
  • Reviewers in Financial Services and Retail (Enterprise tier, 1,000+ employees) cite fast multi-platform pilots across Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Bluecore, Braze and Attentive simultaneously.
  • Case studies show large, verifiable performance gains: Currys reported 42% uplift in opens, 93% uplift in clicks and 102% revenue increase after adopting real-time optimization.
  • Customer success and onboarding support is repeatedly praised by name in reviews (e.g., one reviewer highlights their CSM "Jenn" going "above and beyond").

Cons

  • Multiple G2 reviewers directly describe Jacquard as "expensive," particularly relative to smaller businesses' budgets.
  • Setup and testing logic are described as somewhat rigid, reducing flexibility for bespoke or insight-led messaging strategies.
  • Reporting and custom-dashboard capabilities are flagged by an Enterprise-tier reviewer as an area that "feels lacking."
  • One reviewer reported bugs and slow performance, including subject lines that didn't populate or match provided messaging.
  • No free trial or self-service tier - engagement starts with a sales demo, adding friction for smaller teams evaluating the tool.

Frequently asked questions

Is Jacquard the same company as Phrasee?

Yes. Phrasee rebranded to Jacquard in June 2024; many G2 reviews still reference the Phrasee name, and current case studies and customers carry over from that era.

What is Jacquard's actual website?

Jacquard's active corporate site is jacquard.com. The domain jacquard.ai, sometimes associated with the brand, is currently listed for sale and is not operated by the company.

How much does Jacquard cost?

Jacquard does not publish pricing; it's sold as a custom enterprise contract via a "Book a Demo" sales process, and G2 reviewers describe it as expensive relative to smaller marketing budgets.

What channels does Jacquard support?

Email, SMS, mobile push, in-app, web push, and channel-specific variants including ChatGPT app integrations, all generated from a single strategic brief.

What makes Jacquard different from using ChatGPT for subject lines?

Jacquard's Language² architecture enforces deterministic brand-voice guardrails before scoring, and its Performance Agent predicts engagement using a causal model built from 1M+ A/B tests, rather than generating unvalidated generic copy.

Which platforms does Jacquard integrate with?

Native integrations include Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Braze, Iterable, Adobe Campaign and MessageGears, among others.
