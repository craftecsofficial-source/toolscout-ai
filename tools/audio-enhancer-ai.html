Audio Enhancer AI is a good fit if you have a noisy voice recording and want it cleaned with minimal effort, but skip it if you need fine control such as EQ, custom noise profiles or multitrack editing. You upload audio or video, choose a mode (clean up speech, noise reducer, fix loudness, or noise and echo removal, or an all-in-one run on paid plans), and download the result. It also targets hum, sibilance and mouth-click reduction. Free use is limited to 3 credits a day at up to 5 minutes each; paid plans raise length and size limits and add formats and storage. Little is published about the company behind it, and we found no verified reviews on major review platforms, so judge quality by testing your own file on the free tier.

Best for

Podcasters, YouTubers, course makers and musicians who occasionally need a fast fix for noisy or echoey speech. Not for professionals who need granular control, studio-grade mastering or enterprise support and vendor transparency, and not for long free batch jobs given the 5-minute free cap.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Upload, process, download is the whole workflow, so non-technical users can get a result in minutes.
  • Runs in the browser, with nothing to install.
  • Several targeted modes (speech cleanup, noise, loudness, echo) let you pick what you need rather than one blunt filter.
  • A daily free allowance lets you judge output on your own audio before paying.
  • Annual pricing is low relative to the monthly rate if you use it regularly.

Cons

  • Free files are limited to 5 minutes and the original format, so podcast-length episodes need a paid plan.
  • The pricing page contradicts itself on yearly-tier limits and on simultaneous files for Weekly, so confirm limits at checkout.
  • A 31% promotional code was shown, and it is unclear whether listed prices already include it.
  • We found no verified ratings on G2, Capterra or Trustpilot, and little public information about the operator.
  • Single-purpose: no EQ, multitrack or mastering tools.

Frequently asked questions

Is Audio Enhancer AI free?

Partly. Everyone gets 3 free credits a day with a 5-minute cap per audio and original-format output only. Longer files, more formats and storage need a paid plan.

How much does it cost?

On 2026-10-31 the pricing page listed Weekly $6.99, Monthly $14.99 and Yearly $40, $69 or $99, plus a 7-day trial that converts to $14.99/month. Lifetime and Creator plans require contacting the company.

Does it accept video files?

Yes, video enhancement is listed as a paid (Premium) feature.

What are the file limits?

Monthly allows 1 GB and 2 hours per file with 3 files at once. Yearly tiers go higher, but the page lists conflicting figures, so verify before buying.

Are there independent reviews?

We found none on G2, Capterra or Trustpilot, so test your own recording on the free tier first.
