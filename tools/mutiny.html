Mutiny is a good fit for B2B sellers who want call notes, follow-ups, proposals and deal rooms generated from their meetings; teams who still want the old website-personalization product or a general A/B testing tool should look elsewhere. The homepage now describes a sales assistant: a notetaker that organizes calls by account, drafts follow-ups in your voice, preps you for meetings, builds pricing proposals and executive decks, and updates the CRM. Usage is metered in credits, so heavy use may push you off the cheaper tiers.

Best for

Suited to account executives and small to mid-size sales teams on HubSpot or Salesforce who spend lots of time on follow-up and proposal work. Not for marketers primarily wanting website personalization (the homepage no longer leads with it), B2C teams, or buyers needing Enterprise features like SSO on a small budget.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Generous free entry: 5 members and daily credits let a team test before paying.
  • Covers the whole post-call chain (notes, follow-up, proposal, deal room, CRM update) in one tool.
  • Business is a flat $50/month with unlimited team members.
  • Asset analytics show how buyers engage with shared materials.

Cons

  • Everything is metered in credits; free is capped at 30 per seat per month and Business at 50 per month, which heavy users may exhaust.
  • Salesforce integration is listed under Enterprise, so Business users on Salesforce should confirm access.
  • Enterprise starts at $40,000/year, a big jump from $50/month.
  • The product has shifted from website personalization to a sales assistant; existing guides and reviews about the old product may not apply.
  • Time-saving and meetings-booked figures on the site are vendor-reported.

Frequently asked questions

Does Mutiny have a free plan?

Yes: $0 for up to 5 team members, with 5 daily credits per seat (up to 30 a month).

How much does Mutiny cost?

Business is $50/month with 50 credits per month. Enterprise starts at $40,000/year. A credit dropdown exists on Business, but its other options were not visible.

Is it still a website personalization tool?

The homepage now presents a sales assistant for notes, follow-ups, proposals and deal rooms. Website personalization is not the headline; confirm with the vendor if you need it.

What do I need Enterprise for?

SSO, custom MSA/DPA terms, per-user credit limits, a dedicated strategist, and the Salesforce integration per the pricing page.
