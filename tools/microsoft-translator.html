Microsoft Translator is a good fit for developers and organisations already on Azure or Microsoft 365 who need an API with a free monthly allowance, and for anyone wanting free consumer translation apps; skip it if you want a polished editor for polishing prose or a published flat price. It offers a free personal app, an Azure Translator API for text and documents, speech translation through Azure's Speech service, Custom Translator for training on your own bilingual data, and container deployment options including disconnected containers.

Best for

Good for developers, IT teams, educators and Microsoft-centric businesses. Not for people who need a human-style document editing workflow, buyers who need transparent price quotes without using the Azure calculator, or anyone expecting guaranteed top quality on every language pair without testing.

Key features

01

Real-Time Text Translation

Translates text between 100+ languages and dialects via a REST API, with language auto-detection, transliteration, and a bilingual dictionary with usage examples.

02

Live Speech Translation

Speech-to-speech and speech-to-text translation for live conversations, presentations, and meetings, powering the Microsoft Translator app's conversation feature and Skype Translator.

03

Custom Translator

Lets organizations train a translation model on their own bilingual content so terminology, tone, and domain-specific language match their brand or industry.

04

Document Translation

Translates entire documents (Word, PowerPoint, PDF, and more) while preserving original formatting and layout, billed separately from standard text translation.

05

Native Microsoft 365 Integration

Built directly into Word, Outlook, Teams, Edge, SharePoint, and Yammer, so users can translate content without leaving the app they're working in.

06

Multi-Device Conversation Mode

Up to 100 participants can join a live translated conversation from their own phones, each seeing captions in their own language — used in classrooms and international meetings.

07

On-Premises & Disconnected Containers

An enterprise edition and disconnected container option let regulated industries and air-gapped networks run translation without sending data to the cloud.

08

Free Cross-Platform Apps

iOS, Android, and Windows apps offer free text, voice, and camera (image) translation, including downloadable offline language packs.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Free consumer apps and an Azure free tier of 2M characters/month make testing cheap.
  • Custom Translator lets you train on your own bilingual data for domain terminology.
  • Disconnected containers exist for air-gapped or regulated environments.
  • Integrates into the Microsoft ecosystem and offers text, document and speech translation under one vendor.

Cons

  • Pricing is spread across several meters (text, document, image, custom training, hosting), so costs are hard to estimate without the Azure calculator.
  • Exact per-million-character rates could not be verified today because the pricing page returned no figures to our fetch.
  • Quality on specific language pairs varies and should be tested on your own content before committing.
  • Branding and product packaging have shifted between Translator, Azure AI Services and Foundry names, which can make docs confusing.

Frequently asked questions

Is Microsoft Translator free?

Microsoft's site advertises free personal apps and a free monthly text translation subscription for the API; Azure's F0 tier allows 2 million characters per month.

Do I need an Azure account?

For the API, yes. The consumer apps do not require paid Azure resources.

How is API usage billed?

Per character via separate meters for text, document, and custom translation, with commitment tiers for volume. Check the Azure pricing calculator for current rates.

Can I train it on my terminology?

Yes, through Custom Translator, with training and hosting billed separately.

Can it run on-premises?

Microsoft lists connected and disconnected containers. The earlier $310,000/year on-premises figure could not be re-verified and has been removed.
