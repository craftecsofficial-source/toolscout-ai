Scrunch is a solid fit for mid-market and enterprise teams ready to pay at least $250 a month for AI visibility tracking plus page audits and crawler data; solo marketers and tight budgets should skip it for a free or cheaper tool. The 7-day trial is the only no-cost way in.

It tracks a set of prompts across major AI engines, shows which sources those engines cite, benchmarks you against competitors by persona, topic and geography, and logs when AI bots crawl your site. Page audits suggest fixes to make your own pages easier for AI to read. Its AXP product creates a lightweight machine-readable version of your site for AI agents; earlier notes described it as limited beta, which we could not re-confirm today.

Sitecore has announced it is acquiring Scrunch (confirmed by multiple news reports; the deal price was not verified). That may mean deeper CMS integration later, but expect some uncertainty about standalone pricing and packaging, so confirm limits before signing.

Best for

Fits mid-market and enterprise brands that want prompt tracking, citation analysis, crawler monitoring and page audits in one platform, especially those on or considering Sitecore. Not for solo marketers, startups or anyone wanting a free plan or a basic mention tracker; also not for teams needing more than 3 to 5 seats and a few hundred prompts without paying for add-ons or Enterprise.

Key features

01

Prompt monitoring

Tracks prompts, topics and entities with trends, rankings and competitor comparisons; Starter allows 350 custom prompts plus 1,000 industry prompts.

02

Citation analysis

Shows which sources AI engines rely on when answering about your category or brand.

03

AI bot crawl feed

A real-time view of AI bot visits, plus detection of crawl failures.

04

Personas and benchmarks

Compare visibility by persona (3 on Starter, 5 on Growth), topic, competitor and geography.

05

Page audits

Scores your pages for AI readability and suggests fixes; 5 per month on Starter, 10 on Growth.

06

AXP

Machine-readable version of your site served to AI agents while human design stays the same.

07

Enterprise controls

SOC 2 Type II, role-based access, SAML/OIDC SSO, multiple brands/sites/regions/languages and a Data API.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Engine coverage is the same on every plan (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode and Overviews, Meta), so you do not pay more to see more engines.
  • Citation analysis and a live AI-bot crawl feed in one tool give a concrete picture of how AI reaches your site.
  • Page audits turn findings into on-site fixes rather than leaving you with only a dashboard.
  • Pricing is public and shows monthly versus annual cost clearly, with a no-card trial.
  • Enterprise tier adds SOC 2 Type II and SSO for security-conscious buyers.

Cons

  • No free plan. The cheapest path is $250/month on annual billing, or $300 month-to-month.
  • Starter is small: 3 seats, 3 personas and 5 page audits a month. Extra seats cost $25/month each, and Starter caps at 5 seats.
  • The annual price requires a yearly commitment; month-to-month costs about 20% more.
  • Sitecore's acquisition may change packaging, pricing or integrations; we could not confirm what happens to standalone plans.
  • We could not verify today whether a dedicated hallucination-detection feature exists; it is not mentioned on the homepage, so ask for a demo if it matters.
  • Earlier copy called the engine list 'Grok coming soon'; the live pricing page lists Meta and no Grok.

      Frequently asked questions

      Is there a free plan?

      No. There is a 7-day free trial of Starter with no card required; paid plans then start at $250/month billed annually.

      How much does Scrunch cost?

      Starter is $250/month annually ($300 monthly), Growth is $417/month annually ($500 monthly), Enterprise is custom. Added seats are $25/month each.

      Which AI engines are covered?

      The pricing page lists ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode and AI Overviews, and Meta on all plans.

      Was Scrunch acquired?

      Sitecore announced it is acquiring Scrunch, per press coverage found today. We did not verify the deal value, and the Scrunch homepage does not mention it.

      What limits should I check before buying?

      Seats, custom prompts, personas and monthly page audits differ by tier, and extra seats cost extra, so size your needs against Starter and Growth first.
