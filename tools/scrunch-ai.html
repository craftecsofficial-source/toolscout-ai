Scrunch AI is a generative/answer engine optimization platform built to help brands monitor, understand, and improve how they appear across AI search and chat surfaces. Rather than positioning itself purely as a rank tracker, Scrunch frames its mission around connecting AI visibility to the broader 'AI customer experience' — auditing whether a brand's own site content is structured in a way AI systems can accurately parse and cite. In June 2026, digital-experience platform vendor Sitecore acquired Scrunch for approximately $225 million, folding its AI-visibility and Agent Experience Platform technology into Sitecore's broader DXP, which materially changes Scrunch's trajectory toward deeper enterprise/CMS integration going forward.

Scrunch's clearest differentiator versus Profound and AthenaHQ is its dedicated hallucination detection — explicitly built to catch when AI models generate false or outdated claims about a brand, not just to measure whether the brand appears at all. Its second differentiator is AXP (Agent Experience Platform), an ambitious but still-beta effort to generate a structured, machine-readable version of a brand's site that AI agents and crawlers can consume directly, aiming to reduce hallucinations and improve citation accuracy at the source rather than after the fact.

Best for

Scrunch AI fits mid-market and enterprise brands — particularly those in regulated, reputation-sensitive, or e-commerce categories — that want AI visibility monitoring combined with hallucination detection and actionable on-site audits, and who are comfortable with a premium, non-free pricing floor. It's also a natural fit for organizations already using or evaluating Sitecore's DXP given the 2026 acquisition. Budget-constrained startups, solo marketers, or teams that just want a basic mention tracker without page-audit or hallucination-detection depth should look at AthenaHQ's free tier or a lighter-weight monitor instead.

Key features

01

Prompt Manager

Centralizes, monitors, and tests the prompts used to evaluate brand visibility across AI engines, with unlimited Key Topic monitoring on every plan.

02

Insights

Surfaces visibility opportunities and content gaps by comparing brand and competitor presence across tracked prompts.

03

Citations Tracking

Shows when and how AI systems reference a brand, helping teams see which sources AI engines are pulling from.

04

Page Audits

Scores and recommends fixes to a brand's own web pages to improve AI readability and citation likelihood; allowance scales from 5 audits/month (Starter) to custom (Enterprise).

05

Hallucination Detection

Flags instances where AI models generate factually incorrect or outdated claims about the brand, a capability not offered as a dedicated feature by most competitors.

06

Agent Traffic Monitoring

Named tracking of AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, etc.) combined with a GA4-connected view of AI referral traffic — widely cited as the platform's most-praised feature.

07

Personas

Lets teams model different buyer personas to see how AI responses vary depending on who is asking, scaling from 3 personas (Starter) to 5 (Growth) to custom (Enterprise).

08

AXP (Agent Experience Platform)

A structured, machine-readable layer of a brand's site built specifically for AI agents/crawlers to consume; in limited beta with select enterprise customers as of 2026, no public launch date yet.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Scrunch is the only tool among these three with a purpose-built hallucination/misinformation detection feature, which matters for regulated or reputation-sensitive brands worried about AI models stating false facts.
  • Its AI-crawler traffic analytics — named bot tracking tied directly into GA4 — gives marketers a concrete, familiar way to see AI-driven traffic alongside their existing web analytics, which reviewers repeatedly call out as best-in-class.
  • Being acquired by Sitecore in June 2026 gives Scrunch significantly more financial and go-to-market runway than most standalone AEO startups, plus a natural path into Sitecore's existing enterprise DXP customer base.
  • The Page Audits feature ties AI visibility directly to concrete, actionable on-site fixes rather than leaving marketers with a dashboard and no next step.
  • Every plan tier, including Starter, includes all core capabilities (Prompt Manager, Insights, Citations, Page Audits, hallucination detection) — features aren't artificially gated the way some competitors gate hallucination or citation tooling to higher tiers.

Cons

  • There is no free tier at all — only a 7-day trial of the Starter plan — and even Starter starts at $250-300/month, meaningfully pricier than some newer entrants like AthenaHQ's free tier.
  • AXP, arguably Scrunch's most distinctive long-term bet, is still in limited beta with select enterprise customers only, so most buyers cannot actually evaluate or use it today despite it being central to Scrunch's differentiation story.
  • Public plan naming and packaging have been inconsistent across Scrunch's own marketing materials over time, so buyers should confirm exact prompt/persona/seat limits directly before signing rather than trusting older third-party writeups.
  • The recent Sitecore acquisition introduces real uncertainty about Scrunch's standalone product roadmap, pricing, and whether it will increasingly be bundled into or gated behind Sitecore's broader DXP rather than sold as an independent tool.
  • Independent review coverage is thin and inconsistently reported: G2 ratings cited across sources range from roughly 50 to 73 reviews at 4.6/5, and Scrunch's Capterra listing exists but currently shows no published reviews.
  • Engine coverage, while solid (6 platforms plus Grok 'coming soon'), is not the widest in the category — Profound and AthenaHQ both advertise coverage of more engines (up to 9) on their top tiers.

What reviewers say

Scrunch AI carries one of the stronger G2 ratings in the young AEO category, with a reported 4.6/5 and a high proportion of five-star reviews. Users most consistently praise ease of use, the GA4-linked AI crawler analytics, and responsive customer support. As with peers in this category, independent review coverage outside G2 is thin, and the Sitecore acquisition is too recent to have generated post-acquisition review sentiment.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan for Scrunch AI?

No. Scrunch offers a 7-day free trial of its Starter plan with no credit card required, but there is no ongoing free tier — paid plans start at $250/month billed annually.

How much does Scrunch AI cost?

Starter is $250/month billed annually (or $300/month month-to-month), Growth is $417/month billed annually (or $500/month month-to-month), and Enterprise is custom-priced.

How is Scrunch AI different from Profound or AthenaHQ?

Scrunch's standout feature is dedicated hallucination/misinformation detection plus AXP, an AI-native site layer for agents, whereas Profound focuses on data depth plus content-generation agents and AthenaHQ focuses on accessible, credit-based pricing with a free entry tier.

Which AI engines does Scrunch AI track?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, and Google AI Overviews, plus Meta AI; Grok support is listed as coming soon per Scrunch's own platform FAQ.

Was Scrunch AI acquired?

Yes. Sitecore, a digital experience platform (DXP) vendor, acquired Scrunch for approximately $225 million, announced June 3, 2026, to combine Scrunch's AI-visibility and Agent Experience Platform technology with Sitecore's DXP.

What is AXP?

AXP (Agent Experience Platform) is Scrunch's effort to generate a structured, machine-readable version of a brand's website specifically for AI agents and crawlers to consume; as of 2026 it is in limited beta with select enterprise customers only.
