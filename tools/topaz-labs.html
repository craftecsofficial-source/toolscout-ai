Topaz Labs is a strong choice for photographers and video editors who repeatedly need to upscale or clean up difficult source material and are happy to pay for ongoing subscriptions; skip it if you only need an occasional upscale, or if your commercial work falls outside the limited licence on non-Pro plans. The company, which says it was established in 2001 and has over a million users, sells desktop apps (Topaz Photo, Gigapixel and Video), cloud-based web and iPhone apps, and Studio bundles that include all of them. It enhances images and video through denoising, sharpening, upscaling, restoration and, for video, frame interpolation, slow motion and stabilization. Prices below were read from its pricing page on 2026-10-20 and include promotions that may end.

Best for

Good for photographers, videographers and post-production teams who need to rescue soft, noisy or low-resolution material, and for creators polishing AI-generated images or video. Not for: occasional users who need one quick upscale (the free web tools or a one-off tool may be enough), buyers who want a one-time purchase (the pricing page lists subscriptions; whether any perpetual licence is still sold was not confirmed), or larger businesses that need commercial rights without buying a Pro plan, since standard plans cover commercial use only for organizations under $1M annual revenue.

Key features

Desktop photo enhancement with models for denoise, sharpen and upscale, plus cloud access to some models.

Image upscaling, described by Topaz as up to 16x pixels, with models such as Standard, High Fidelity and Recover.

Topaz Video

Desktop video enhancement with models including Starlight Precise, Proteus, Iris and Chronos; cloud Starlight models draw on monthly credits.

Topaz for Web and Mobile

Browser and iPhone tools for image and video: unblur, denoise, upscale, face enhancement, background removal, frame interpolation, slow motion and SDR-to-HDR.

Free tools

Unblur, Face Enhancer, Brighten, Sharpen and Video Upscale are offered as free web tools; their limits were not verified.

06

Studio bundles

Studio and Studio Pro include all apps; Pro adds Pro-exclusive models, seat management and a higher cloud export cap.

Enterprise

API access and custom solutions; pricing is not public, contact sales.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Covers photo, video, web and mobile in one vendor, with Studio bundling everything.
  • Desktop plans advertise unlimited local rendering, so costs do not scale with volume.
  • Several free web tools allow testing quality before subscribing.
  • Pro tiers give full commercial use and higher cloud export caps (100 to 256 MP).
  • Dedicated video models (Starlight, Proteus, Chronos) address restoration and slow motion, not just upscaling.

Cons

  • Prices on the pricing page mix list and promotional numbers (and promo codes), so what you pay later may differ from today's discount.
  • Cloud video credits (25 to 600 a month depending on plan) expire at month end and do not roll over; extra credits cost extra, price not listed.
  • Standard plans limit commercial use to organizations under $1M annual revenue.
  • No annual prices were listed for the single desktop apps, so paying monthly is the only shown route.
  • Overlapping plans (Web Pro, Image Web Pro, Studio, Studio Pro) make choosing confusing.
  • Hardware requirements and over-processing behaviour were not verified today; test on your own footage.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Topaz cost?

Listed 2026-10-20: Photo $39/mo, Gigapixel $29/mo, Video $59/mo, Studio $69/mo or $399/yr, Studio Pro $75/mo (12-month billing) or $799/yr, web plans from $12/mo. Many carry limited-time discounts.

Can I buy it once?

The pricing page shows only subscriptions. Whether perpetual licences are still sold could not be confirmed from the page.

Is there a free option?

The site offers free web tools such as Unblur, Face Enhancer and Sharpen. A free trial of the desktop apps could not be verified from the pricing page.

Can I use it commercially?

Standard plans allow personal and commercial use for organizations under $1M annual revenue. Pro plans (Web Pro, Studio Pro) list full commercial use.

Do cloud credits roll over?

No. Monthly cloud video credits expire at the end of each month.
