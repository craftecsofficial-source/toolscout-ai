Clay is a data enrichment and workflow automation platform that has become a favorite among modern GTM and growth teams for building highly customized prospecting pipelines. Rather than relying on a single data provider, Clay runs 'waterfall' enrichment across dozens of third-party sources simultaneously, filling in missing contact and company data with whichever provider returns the best match, and using its AI research agent ('Claygent') to find custom data points that no standard provider offers.

On top of enrichment, Clay provides a no-code, spreadsheet-like workflow builder where users can chain together data lookups, AI processing steps, scoring logic, and outbound actions (like sending personalized emails or LinkedIn messages) into a single automated pipeline. This flexibility has made it popular for building things like account scoring models, intent-based outbound triggers, and highly personalized cold email campaigns at scale.

Clay's pricing is credit-based, since each enrichment lookup or AI action consumes credits, which gives power users flexibility but can make costs unpredictable for teams running high-volume workflows. It has built a strong reputation in the RevOps and growth community, with many public templates and playbooks shared by practitioners.

Best for

Clay is best suited for RevOps, growth, and demand generation teams that want granular control over how prospect data is sourced, enriched, and acted on, without waiting on engineering resources. It fits companies running sophisticated, highly targeted outbound or account-based marketing motions that need custom scoring and personalization logic. It's less appropriate for teams that just need a simple, out-of-the-box contact database and sequencing tool without building custom workflows.

Waterfall Enrichment

02

Claygent AI Research Agent

An AI agent that can browse the web and reason to find custom, non-standard data points not covered by traditional data providers.

03

No-Code Workflow Builder

A spreadsheet-style interface lets users chain enrichment, scoring, and outreach steps into automated pipelines without writing code.

Outbound Integrations

Connects directly to email and LinkedIn outreach tools so enriched, scored leads can flow straight into personalized campaigns.

05

Lead Scoring & Segmentation

Supports building custom scoring models and segments based on enriched firmographic, technographic, and intent data.

06

CRM Sync & API Access

Syncs enriched data back into CRMs and exposes an API for teams that want to embed Clay workflows into other systems.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Waterfall enrichment across many providers significantly improves data fill rates compared to relying on a single source
  • The AI research agent can retrieve highly specific, non-standard data points that traditional enrichment tools can't provide
  • No-code workflow builder empowers RevOps and growth practitioners to build sophisticated automations without engineering support
  • Large, active user community sharing templates and playbooks shortens the learning curve for common use cases

Cons

  • Credit consumption scales with workflow complexity and volume, making monthly costs harder to forecast than flat-fee tools
  • Building advanced multi-step workflows still requires a real learning investment, despite the no-code positioning
  • Enrichment accuracy depends on third-party data providers, so results can be inconsistent across industries or regions
  • Can become a single point of complexity if too many critical GTM workflows are consolidated into one tool without documentation

Frequently asked questions

What makes Clay different from a typical data provider?

Clay doesn't rely on one data source; it waterfalls lookups across 50+ providers and adds an AI research agent to find data points standard providers can't supply.

Do I need to know how to code to use Clay?

No, Clay's core workflow builder is no-code and spreadsheet-like, though building advanced workflows still takes practice.

How is Clay priced?

Clay uses a credit-based model across tiers from free to enterprise, where enrichment lookups and AI actions consume credits.

Can Clay send outbound emails directly?

Clay integrates with email and LinkedIn outreach tools so enriched and scored leads can flow directly into outbound campaigns.

Is Clay only for large companies?

No, Clay is used by teams of all sizes, from solo growth marketers to enterprise RevOps teams, thanks to its tiered, credit-based pricing.

What is 'Claygent'?

Claygent is Clay's AI research agent that can browse the web to find custom, hard-to-source data points for each lead or account.
