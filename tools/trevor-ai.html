Trevor AI is a good fit for individuals who already keep a task list plus a calendar and want a one-click time-blocked day, and a poor fit for teams that need shared projects, or for anyone who wants a native mobile app. It merges tasks and calendar events in one drag-and-drop planner, estimates how long each task will take, and fills open calendar slots with a proposed schedule via 'Plan My Day'.

It syncs with Google Calendar, Outlook/Microsoft 365 calendars, Google Tasks, Microsoft To Do and Todoist (the FAQ also lists Apple Calendar). Extras include Focus Mode (timer, notes and an AI task breakdown), the Ask Trevor chat assistant for adding and rescheduling tasks, a Smart Scheduling Queue and a daily 'Start My Day' email. Trevor Labs Ltd runs it. We re-checked the main site on 2026-10-29; the separate /pricing page could not be fetched, so all pricing here comes from the home page.

Best for

Best for freelancers, founders, remote workers and students who plan their own day. Not for teams that need shared project boards, resource planning or client-facing scheduling links, not for people who want a native iOS/Android app (none is mentioned on the site), and not for anyone who wants every AI feature without paying, since Pro unlocks multiple calendars, Ask Trevor and the scheduling queue.

Key features

01

Plan My Day / Plan My Week

One-click AI scheduling that analyzes open calendar time and unscheduled tasks, then generates a full time-blocked day or week automatically.

02

Predicted Task Duration

Trevor's AI auto-assigns an estimated duration to each task based on patterns, which users can manually override.

03

Smart Scheduling Queue

A prioritization mechanism where users add tasks or whole lists to a queue that Trevor proactively helps schedule, including in bulk.

04

Focus Mode

A distraction-reducing single-task view combining a timer, a notes field, and an AI-generated five-step breakdown of the task at hand.

05

Ask Trevor

A chat-based assistant that can add, schedule, reschedule, and bulk-manage tasks conversationally, with context on the user's calendar and preferences.

06

Multi-calendar and task-list sync

Real-time two-way sync with Google Calendar, Microsoft/Outlook calendars, Google Tasks, Microsoft To Do, and Todoist, keeping scheduled tasks consistent across tools.

07

Recurring Scheduled Tasks

Both scheduled and unscheduled tasks can be set to recur, supporting habit tracking alongside one-off to-dos.

08

Personal AI Model & Coaching

Trevor's model adapts to an individual's planning patterns over time, and a daily 'Start My Day' email delivers progress insights and AI coaching.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Tasks and calendar events live in one draggable view, so you do not juggle a to-do app and a calendar that never talk to each other.
  • One-click Plan My Day / week gives a usable first draft of your schedule, and you can still drag blocks around by hand.
  • Pro at $5-6/mo undercuts most AI scheduling rivals, and there is a 30-day money-back guarantee.
  • Syncs with Google, Microsoft, Todoist, Google Tasks and Microsoft To Do, so you can keep your existing task source.

Cons

  • Web only according to the site, so you rely on a mobile browser rather than a native app with notifications and widgets.
  • The free plan's limits are not spelled out and several features have Pro badges, so you may only learn what is paywalled after signing up.
  • Ask Trevor, multiple calendars and the scheduling queue are Pro features, which matters if you run work and personal calendars.
  • Predicted durations and priorities are estimates and will need correcting, especially early on.
  • Little independent review coverage, so there is less outside evidence about reliability than for bigger tools.

Frequently asked questions

Is Trevor AI free?

Yes, there is a Free plan for personal use with the Task Hub, one synced calendar, unlimited scheduling, Focus Mode and predicted durations. Numeric limits are not stated on the page, so check inside the app.

How much does Pro cost?

The site shows $5/month billed annually or $6/month billed monthly, noting the display may include a 25% sign-up discount. It lists a 30-day money-back guarantee. We could not load the separate pricing page to confirm the post-discount price.

What does Pro add over Free?

Multiple calendar accounts, a personal adaptive AI model, recurring scheduled tasks, the Smart Scheduling Queue, insights, rescheduling suggestions, Ask Trevor, and scheduling blocks.

How does it compare with Motion?

Both auto-schedule tasks into your calendar. Trevor is aimed at individual planning and is cheaper at the listed price; Motion adds project and team features. Check Motion's own page for its current price.

Does it have a mobile app?

The site describes web access on mobile and desktop and does not mention native iOS or Android apps.
