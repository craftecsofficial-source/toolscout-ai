TopicMojo is a content and keyword research platform built to help marketers, bloggers, and small agencies answer a simple question: what should I write about next? Rather than pulling data from a single search engine, TopicMojo's Topic Model aggregates information from more than 50 sources — including social media platforms, forums, news sites, and question-and-answer communities like Quora and Reddit — to surface trending topics, common audience questions, and content gaps in a given niche. Its companion tools, the Question Finder and Search Listener, extend this into ongoing monitoring: Question Finder collects real questions people are asking around a topic, while Search Listener alerts users to newly emerging search queries so they can create content before a trend peaks.

TopicMojo positions itself as a lower-cost alternative to tools like AnswerThePublic and Ubersuggest, and reviewers frequently make that comparison directly, citing similar topic-modeling output at a fraction of the price. The product has also expanded into AI content generation and a 'Humanize' tool for rewriting AI-generated text to reduce AI-detection flags, alongside an SEO Add-on that layers in search volume and PPC difficulty metrics for keyword prioritization. Its pricing has historically included lifetime deals on AppSumo (deals have run from roughly $59 up to a couple hundred dollars depending on the tier and usage caps), alongside a standard SaaS subscription — the vendor's own pricing page lists a Pro plan around $99/month full price, discounted to about $39.50/month when billed annually, plus periodic seasonal discounts.

Best for

TopicMojo is a good fit for solopreneurs, bloggers, and small marketing/agency teams who need an affordable way to generate content ideas and find audience questions across many sources at once. It's a weaker fit for enterprise SEO teams needing deep technical SEO auditing or teams requiring fully localized, non-English topic research out of the box.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • G2 reviewers give TopicMojo a strong 4.6/5 average across 111 reviews, with the intuitive/simple dashboard called out as a key strength by multiple reviewers.
  • Reviewers consistently compare it favorably to AnswerThePublic and Ubersuggest, citing similar or better topic-discovery results at a lower price point.
  • Aggregating data from 50+ sources (social, forums, news, Q&A sites) gives broader content-idea coverage than single-source keyword tools.
  • Several Capterra reviewers highlight it as useful specifically for solopreneurs and small teams needing a steady content idea pipeline without a big budget.
  • The Question Finder feature is repeatedly praised for surfacing real audience questions that translate directly into FAQ content and headings.

Cons

  • Some functionality is gated behind paid add-ons (e.g., an SEO Data Add-on and Search Listener Add-on cited by one reviewer as a combined ~$30/month extra cost).
  • Non-English research can partially default back to English-language results, limiting usefulness for fully localized content strategies.
  • A few reviewers report slow or inconsistent customer support responsiveness when troubleshooting issues.
  • Results can feel repetitive or shallow for more complex/niche topics compared to deeper dedicated research tools.
  • The interface, while functional, has been described by some reviewers as less modern or slightly cluttered compared to newer competitors.

What reviewers say

TopicMojo earns strong marks for ease of use and breadth of topic/question data at an affordable price, with the main criticisms centered on paid add-ons, occasional slow support, and less depth on complex topics.

What is TopicMojo used for?
