TopicMojo suits bloggers, solo marketers and small agencies that want a low-cost way to find what to write about; skip it if you need rigorous keyword data, rank tracking or on-page optimization, or clear published usage limits. It builds a map of topics around a seed term using more than 50 sources, including social platforms, forums and question sites. Question Finder collects questions people ask, Search Listener flags emerging queries, and Social Model shows discussion across 10+ social platforms. An optional SEO add-on brings search volume, CPC and PPC difficulty, and it also offers AI drafting and a Humanize rewriter. Reports can be saved, shared and exported to CSV, with 35 languages supported. The pricing page was unclear when checked, so treat plan details as unconfirmed.

Best for

Good for content ideation by freelancers, bloggers and small agencies on tight budgets. Not for teams needing precise keyword metrics, content scoring against SERP competitors, enterprise support commitments, or transparent usage caps.

Key features

01

Topic Model

Aggregates data from 50+ sources — social, forums, news — to generate a broad set of content topic ideas for a given keyword or niche.

02

Question Finder

Collects real audience questions from platforms like Quora, Google, and Reddit to inform FAQ sections and content angles.

03

Search Listener

Monitors and alerts users to newly emerging search queries so content can be created ahead of a trend.

04

Social Model

Builds a social-media-specific view of what's being discussed around a topic across platforms like Instagram, Twitter/X, and Pinterest.

05

SEO Data Add-on

Adds search volume and PPC difficulty metrics on top of topic and question data for prioritization.

06

AI Content & Humanize Tools

Generates draft content from research and includes a 'Humanize' feature to reduce AI-detection signals in AI-written text.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Multi-source aggregation (social, forums, news, Q&A) gives broader idea coverage than single-engine keyword tools.
  • Question Finder output maps directly onto FAQ sections and headings.
  • Search Listener helps catch rising queries early.
  • Saved lists, workspaces, report sharing and CSV export support small-team workflows.
  • A 7-day money-back guarantee lowers the risk of testing it.

Cons

  • The pricing page does not state plan limits, and the quoted prices are internally inconsistent, so you cannot predict cost accurately.
  • SEO data (volume, PPC difficulty) is sold as an add-on whose price I could not see.
  • Promotional discounts are shown with no end date, making list price unclear.
  • Previously reported weaknesses (slow support, localized results skewing English) could not be re-verified today.
  • Third-party ratings from earlier research (G2 4.6/5) were not re-checked today and are not cited here.
