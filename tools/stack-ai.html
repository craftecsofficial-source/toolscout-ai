StackAI is a good fit for enterprise IT and ops teams that need AI agents with approvals, audit trails and private deployment; solo builders and small teams should look elsewhere, since beyond a small free plan you have to talk to sales. It is a visual canvas where you connect data sources, LLM calls, logic and integrations into agents, and it supports retrieval over your own documents.

The emphasis is governance: human review steps, version control with prompt diffs, dashboards for runs, errors and token usage, and deployment in multi-tenant, VPC or on-premise setups. The site lists SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, GDPR and ISO 27001, and a banner on the homepage says StackAI is joining Asana, so check the current status before committing to a long contract.

Best for

Enterprise IT, risk, legal and operations teams that need governed agents on internal data with private deployment options. It is not for hobbyists, students or small teams wanting a cheap self-serve plan, nor for anyone who just needs a simple chatbot.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Approval steps, versioning with prompt diffs and audit logs are core features, which many lighter agent builders lack.
  • Deployment can be multi-tenant, VPC or on-premise, which matters for data-residency rules.
  • Model-agnostic, so you can choose a different LLM per step.
  • The 500+ listed integrations cover common enterprise systems such as SharePoint and Salesforce.
  • A free plan exists, so you can test a workflow before a sales conversation.

Cons

  • The free plan allows only 500 runs a month, 2 projects and a single seat, so it is a trial rather than a working tier.
  • The pricing page shows no self-serve paid plan; anything beyond free needs a custom quote, which makes budgeting hard.
  • The pricing page leaves unexplained what the asterisk on "runs" means, and does not show which features sit in which plan.
  • Built for enterprise governance; a simple single-purpose chatbot is cheaper elsewhere.
  • The homepage announces StackAI is joining Asana, so product direction and pricing could change.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The pricing page lists Free at $0/month with 500 runs per month, 2 projects and 1 seat, plus Discord community support.

How much does a paid plan cost?

The public pricing page shows only Free and Enterprise; Enterprise is custom-quoted. We could not verify any other public price, so ask sales or see the official pricing page.

Can it be deployed privately?

Per the site, deployment is multi-tenant, VPC or on-premise. On-prem and VPC are listed under Enterprise.

Which models can I use?

The site describes it as LLM agnostic, letting you choose the model per task, and links data processing agreements for OpenAI and Anthropic.

What should I cheaply try instead?

Dify or Pickaxe are the closer ToolScout alternatives if you want lower-commitment options; check each one's current pricing.
