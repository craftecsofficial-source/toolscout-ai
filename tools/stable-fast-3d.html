Stable Fast 3D is a strong pick for developers and technical artists who want quick, rough 3D props from single images and are comfortable running a model themselves; non-technical users who want a web app, and anyone needing hero-quality assets without cleanup, should skip it. Released by Stability AI in 2024 and built on the earlier TripoSR work, it takes one image and produces a UV-unwrapped textured mesh with estimated material properties and reduced baked-in lighting. Stability's page claims generation takes about half a second, though it does not state the hardware behind that figure, and the Hugging Face page says under one second per image. The model has roughly 1B parameters and expects 512x512 input.

Best for

Good for game and VR/AR developers, technical artists and e-commerce teams with ML tooling who need fast prototype assets. Not for non-coders wanting a drag-and-drop product, for studios needing final-quality meshes with clean topology out of the box, or for companies above $1M annual revenue that have not budgeted for an Enterprise License.

Key features

01

Single-image to 3D

One input image yields a complete mesh with UV map and textures, with no multi-angle capture needed.

02

Material and delighting

Estimates albedo and material parameters and tries to strip lighting baked into the source photo so the asset reacts better to new lighting.

03

Optional remeshing

Quad or triangle remeshing can be applied for cleaner topology, per Stability's description.

04

Open, gated weights

Weights are on Hugging Face behind a login and license acceptance, with usage instructions in the GitHub repository, so teams can self-host and fine-tune.

05

Technical report

Method and benchmarks are published as arXiv 2408.00653, useful for judging claims yourself.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Generation speed makes bulk prototyping practical compared with minutes-per-asset reconstruction methods.
  • Free license covers most indie developers and small studios up to the $1M threshold.
  • Self-hosting avoids dependence on a vendor service and allows fine-tuning.
  • Textured, UV-unwrapped output with material estimates reduces manual unwrapping work.
  • Published technical report allows independent evaluation.

Cons

  • No official consumer UI; you need Python, a GPU and the GitHub setup.
  • Weights are gated: you must create a Hugging Face account and accept the license.
  • The revenue threshold applies regardless of revenue source, so growing companies must track it and buy an Enterprise License.
  • Geometry hidden in the source image is inferred, so backs and undersides of complex objects can show artifacts.
  • Meshes are simplified, so close-up or hero assets will likely need retopology and texture touch-up.
  • Hosted API availability and cost for this model could not be confirmed, so budget for self-hosting.

Frequently asked questions

Is Stable Fast 3D free for commercial use?

Yes for organizations and individuals at or under US$1M annual revenue under the Community License. Above that you need an Enterprise License before commercial use, including of outputs.

Do I need to code?

For the open model, yes: weights are on Hugging Face and instructions are on GitHub. Hugging Face lists many community Spaces that use it, which may let you try it without setup, but those are third-party.

How fast is it?

Stability claims 0.5 seconds per asset; Hugging Face says under one second. Hardware conditions are not specified, so benchmark on your own GPU.

Is the output game-ready?

It is textured and UV-unwrapped, which helps, but expect cleanup for anything beyond background props or prototypes.

Is there a hosted API?

Stability promotes a Platform API site-wide, but its Stable Fast 3D page does not say it covers this model, and I could not verify pricing.
