ImgGen AI is a good fit for casual creators and small sellers who want inexpensive, watermark-free images and basic photo fixes under one login; anyone who needs a recurring free allowance, proven top-tier image quality, or enterprise guarantees should look elsewhere. It is a browser-based tool that turns text prompts into images using selectable styles and models, and it also sells a set of editing operations such as upscaling, background removal, photo restoration, colorizing and watermark or text removal, all paid from the same credit balance. The site is operated by Cerebrox Technologies, Inc. Each operation has its own credit cost (for example 3 credits for the default generation model, 1 for background removal, 4 for a 4x upscale, 25 for deblur or denoise), so how far 400 or 1,400 monthly credits go depends on what you do.

Best for

Good for solo creators, social media managers, small e-commerce sellers and developers wanting a low-cost API that covers generation plus editing. Not for people who want a free tier that renews every month (the free grant is one-time), teams that need documented uptime or SLAs without an Enterprise contract, or users who want to cancel and keep their stored credits (they expire on cancellation).

Key features

01

Text-to-image generation

Prompt-based image creation with style options and several models; default generation costs 3 credits per the pricing page, and higher-tier models cost more on paid plans.

02

Credit rollover

On Starter and Pro, unused credits carry over month to month. They expire if you cancel, the account goes inactive, or a renewal payment fails. Switching plans keeps your balance.

03

Editing toolkit

Background removal, background change, upscaling (2x, 4x, 8x), photo restoration, colorizing, shadow generation, watermark and text removal, deblur and denoise, each with a per-use credit cost.

04

API and bulk mode

The site lists image generation and editing APIs plus specialised endpoints, and the pricing page lists API access and bulk mode on Starter and Pro. Exact call quotas could not be verified today.

05

Automatic refunds

The pricing page says failed AI operations are refunded automatically.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Watermark-free output and commercial rights are stated even on the free plan.
  • Rollover on paid plans means a quiet month does not waste credits.
  • Cheap operations like background removal (1 credit) stretch a 400-credit plan a long way.
  • Failed generations are refunded automatically.
  • API access is listed starting at the $9.99 plan.

Cons

  • The free tier is a one-time grant, so it works as a trial rather than ongoing free use.
  • Credits vanish if you cancel, go inactive or a payment fails, so rollover is not a savings account.
  • Heavier operations (deblur and denoise at 25 credits, ImgGen Edit at 20) burn a 400-credit plan quickly.
  • We found no independent review data or quality benchmarks against Midjourney, Ideogram or Flux; the site's usage figures (200K+ creators, 2M+ images) are self-reported and unverified.
  • The pricing page has internal inconsistencies, such as a rollover savings example that does not add up, so check figures before buying.

Frequently asked questions

Is the free plan really free and watermark-free?

Yes per the pricing page: 50 one-time credits, no card, no watermark, commercial rights. It does not renew monthly.

How many images do 50 or 400 credits give me?

The default generation model costs 3 credits, so roughly 16 images on the free grant and about 133 on Starter if you spend only on that model. Other models and edits cost more or less.

What happens to my credits if I cancel?

They expire. Access continues to the end of the billing period, but credits are lost on cancellation, inactivity or a failed renewal.

Can I use the images commercially?

The site states commercial rights on all plans, including Free. Read its terms for details before relying on it for a client project.

Is there an API?

The site advertises generation and editing APIs and lists API access on Starter and Pro. Specific call limits could not be verified today.
