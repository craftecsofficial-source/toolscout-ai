ImgGen AI is a free-to-start, browser-based AI image generator built by CerebroX Technologies, positioned around three specific promises: no watermark on any tier (including free), full commercial usage rights from the first image, and a credit-rollover model on paid plans instead of the reset-every-month structure most competitors use. Users type a prompt, optionally pick from 120+ style presets (realistic, anime, cyberpunk, watercolor, 3D render, and more), and generate high-resolution images in seconds with no sign-up required for the first free images. The company reports 200,000+ creators and 2M+ images generated to date.

Beyond core generation, ImgGen bundles a fairly large suite of adjacent image-editing tools — background removal, upscaling, retouching, restoration, color correction, unblurring, watermark removal, and more — under the same account and credit system, positioning it against both pure generators (like Leonardo or Ideogram) and editing-focused tools (like PhotoRoom, Remini, or Cutout Pro, which it explicitly lists as 'alternatives' on its own marketing pages). Its core differentiator is the rollover credit system: on Starter and Pro plans, unused monthly credits accumulate rather than expiring, which the company markets heavily as a distinguishing feature versus reset-monthly competitors.

Best for

ImgGen AI is best suited to individual creators, social media managers, and small online sellers who want free, watermark-free, commercially-usable images without navigating a complex trial-to-paid funnel, and who value a growing credit balance over a rigid monthly cap. Developers wanting affordable API access to image generation plus editing operations (upscale, background removal) in one API also fit well. Teams needing enterprise-grade reliability guarantees, documented uptime, or verified third-party quality benchmarks should treat the lack of G2/Capterra/Trustpilot review data as a real gap and pilot the tool on real work before committing budget.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Unlike many 'free' AI image tools that add a watermark or restrict commercial use on the free plan, ImgGen gives full commercial rights and zero watermark from the very first image.
  • The rollover-credit design directly addresses a common complaint about SaaS credit systems (use-it-or-lose-it monthly resets), rewarding irregular usage patterns like seasonal or campaign-based work.
  • One subscription covers both generation and a substantial post-processing toolkit (upscaling, restoration, background removal), reducing the need for separate editing subscriptions.
  • No credit card is required to start, which lowers friction for casual or one-off use significantly compared to trial-then-bill models.
  • API access is included starting at the entry paid tier ($9.99/mo), which is unusually accessible pricing for programmatic access versus competitors that gate APIs behind enterprise plans.

Cons

  • The free plan's 50 credits are a one-time allotment, not a recurring monthly grant, so it functions more as a trial than an ongoing free tier for ongoing casual use.
  • No verifiable third-party review data exists on G2, Capterra, or Trustpilot at the time of this research, despite listings existing on both G2 and Capterra — meaning there's no independent signal on support quality, uptime, or output consistency at scale.
  • The testimonials featured on ImgGen's own marketing pages are unattributed/unverifiable (first name + last initial only) rather than linked to verified third-party review profiles.
  • Credits expire entirely if a subscription is cancelled, lapses due to a failed payment, or the account goes inactive — so the rollover benefit is fully lost on cancellation, not preserved.
  • As with most general-purpose AI image generators, quality and prompt-adherence can vary by style and complexity; no independent benchmark comparison against category leaders (Midjourney, Ideogram, Flux) was found.

Frequently asked questions

Is ImgGen AI really free, with no watermark?

Yes — the free plan gives 50 one-time credits with no watermark and full commercial rights; no credit card is required to start.

How many images can I generate for free?

Free users get 50 one-time credits to spend across AI tools (generation, editing, upscaling, etc.), not a recurring monthly free allowance.

What happens to unused credits if I don't use them?

On Starter and Pro plans, unused credits roll over and accumulate month to month as long as your subscription stays active; they're only lost if you cancel, your account goes inactive, or a renewal payment fails.

Can I use ImgGen images commercially?

Yes, all plans — including Free — include full commercial usage rights with no watermark on any generated image.

Does ImgGen AI have an API?

Yes, API access is included starting on the Starter plan ($9.99/mo, 5,000 calls/month), scaling to 10,000 calls/month on Pro.

Is there independent review data (G2, Capterra) for ImgGen AI?

ImgGen AI has listings on both G2 and Capterra, but no verified aggregate rating or review count was available at the time of this research.
