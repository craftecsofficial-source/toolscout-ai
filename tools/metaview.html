Metaview is a good fit for in-house or agency recruiting teams that want AI to take over sourcing, screening and interview note-taking, and especially for teams that are short on recruiter time. It is less suited to very small teams that hire a few times a year, or anyone wanting a flat, fully published price for the whole platform. The company positions itself as an agentic recruiting platform covering the hiring path from sourcing to offer. Agents build candidate shortlists and run outreach (its newer agent is called fillmore), review applications, run screening, and produce interview notes, scorecards and follow-ups. Interview data can be queried for reporting, and admins can set agent rules and review an audit trail. It lists SOC 2 and GDPR/CCPA compliance. Self-serve pricing exists only for the sourcing module; other modules are quoted by sales. Vendor claims such as 40% faster time-to-fill are not independently verified.

Best for

Good for talent acquisition teams and recruiting agencies that spend heavily on sourcing, screening calls and interview notes, and want to consolidate those steps. Not for companies hiring only occasionally, teams that only want a cheap standalone notetaker, or buyers who need transparent full-platform pricing up front.

Key features

01

Sourcing agent

An autonomous agent identifies candidates matching a role's requirements and sends outreach messages, aiming to reduce manual searching on platforms like LinkedIn.

02

Application review

AI screens incoming applications against role criteria and surfaces a ranked shortlist with stated reasoning for each match.

03

Interview notes

The system joins scheduled video calls (Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Webex), records them, and generates structured notes and summaries shortly after the call ends.

04

Recruiting reports

An analytics layer aggregates data across searches and interviews to surface trends such as compensation expectations and candidate signals over time.

05

Fillmore (autonomous scheduling agent)

A newer agent that finds candidates, reaches out, and schedules screening calls with less manual recruiter involvement.

06

Human-in-the-loop controls

Metaview markets 'AI orchestration with human guardrails,' including audit trails intended to keep recruiters and hiring managers able to review and override agent actions.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Sourcing has a real self-serve path, including a free allowance of 100 profiles, so you can test before talking to sales
  • Covers sourcing, screening, notes and reporting in one workspace rather than separate tools
  • Automated notes and scorecards cut manual write-up time; customers report 2 to 6 hours saved per recruiter per week (vendor-published testimonials)
  • Lists SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, CCPA, LGPD and PIPEDA compliance, with admin controls and an audit trail for agent actions

Cons

  • Pricing for application review, notes and reporting is not public, so cost is unknown until a sales call
  • Per-user sourcing pricing adds up: Max at $300 per user per month across a team is significant
  • Autonomous outreach and screening need careful oversight for candidate experience, bias and consent, especially with recorded calls
  • Specific ATS integrations are not listed on the homepage; verify yours on the integrations page before buying
  • Bundling several functions may mean paying for scope you do not need

Frequently asked questions

How much does Metaview cost?

Sourcing is published per user: Free (first 100 profiles), Pro $100/month (200 profiles/month) and Max $300/month (unlimited). The full platform with application review, notes and reporting has no public price; contact sales.

Does Metaview replace my ATS?

It is built to work alongside an ATS, not replace one. The homepage does not name supported systems, so check the integrations page for yours (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and similar) before committing.

Is there a free plan?

Yes for sourcing: the first 100 profiles sourced are free. Other modules are not listed as free.

How are candidate consent and privacy handled for interview notes?

The company lists SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, CCPA, LGPD and PIPEDA, but your team is still responsible for telling candidates about recording and getting consent where the law requires it. Confirm with their security documentation.

Can I buy just one part, like the notetaker?

Sourcing can be bought on its own. The pricing page has tabs for Application Review, Notetaker and All agents, but no prices were visible for them, so ask sales about standalone options.
