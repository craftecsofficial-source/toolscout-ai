Outreach is a strong fit for mid-market and enterprise sales organizations that want outbound sequencing, call coaching and forecasting in a single system. Small teams, founders doing their own selling, or anyone who wants to see a price and start today should skip it: there are no list prices and no advertised free trial.

Originally known as a sequencing tool, Outreach now describes itself as an AI-agent platform for revenue teams. Reps get multi-channel sequences, a built-in dialer, meeting briefs and call transcription, while managers get deal health alerts, coaching cards and forecasting. It sits alongside your CRM rather than replacing it.

Packaging is by capability tier. Essentials is centered on conversation intelligence and coaching, Core on outbound pipeline creation, Plus adds deal coordination, and Pro adds forecasting, scenario planning and territory management. Each package bundles a monthly-style pool of AI credits, with more sold separately.

Best for

Mid-market and enterprise B2B teams with dedicated SDRs, AEs and sales managers, a CRM already in place, and a RevOps owner who can configure it. It is not for solo sellers, small teams or startups wanting transparent monthly pricing; lighter tools like Apollo.io or Instantly.ai are a better match.

Key features

01

Sequences

Multi-channel outbound cadences mixing email, calls and tasks, available from the Core package up.

02

AI agents

Research, Revenue, Personalization and Deal agents that prepare account context, draft messages and update deal records. They consume AI credits.

03

Conversation intelligence

Call transcription, sentiment analysis, meeting insights and live coaching, the core of the Essentials package.

04

Deal and pipeline management

Deal health alerts, account planning and relationship maps, with revenue attribution reporting at Plus.

05

Forecasting

AI-assisted forecasts, scenario planner, rollups, parallel forecasts and territory management, Pro only.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • One vendor for sequencing, dialing, call coaching, deal tracking and forecasting, which can replace several point tools.
  • Tiered packages let a team buy only coaching or only outbound instead of the full suite.
  • Per its pricing page, per-user pricing carries no platform fee and includes support.
  • Higher tiers add API, custom-object and knowledge-storage headroom for large RevOps setups.

Cons

  • No published prices; every package says request pricing, so you cannot budget or compare before a sales call.
  • Cost has two parts, per-seat fees and AI credits. Heavy agent use can burn credits, and extra packs cost more.
  • Forecasting, scenario planning and territory management only come with the top Pro package.
  • No free trial is advertised, so you cannot test it hands-on before a demo and contract.
  • Professional services for deployment are quoted separately, adding to total cost.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Outreach cost?

Outreach does not publish prices. Its four Amplify packages all say request pricing, and cost depends on seat count, AI credits and capabilities. Expect a custom quote.

Is there a free trial or free plan?

None is advertised. The site's main call to action is to request a demo.

What are AI credits?

Each package includes a pool of credits (10,000 to 100,000) consumed by AI features such as agents. More credit packs can be bought at any time.

Which package do I need for forecasting?

Forecasting, scenario planning and territory management are listed under Amplify Pro. Plus includes deal and pipeline management but not the advanced forecasting suite.

How does it compare to Salesloft?

Both are enterprise sales engagement platforms with custom pricing. Salesloft now bundles Clari's forecasting; shortlist both and compare quotes and rep workflows.
