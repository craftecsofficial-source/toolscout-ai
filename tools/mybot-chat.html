MyBot.Chat is a self-serve SaaS platform for building AI-powered chatbots without writing any code. Businesses point the tool at their existing content - a website URL, PDF or Word documents, spreadsheets, plain text, or a simple Q&A list - and MyBot.Chat crawls and processes that content to train a chatbot that understands the business's products, policies and tone. Under the hood, the chatbot is powered by OpenAI's GPT-4o (or the cheaper GPT-4o-mini) or Meta's Llama 3 70B model, letting customers choose a balance of cost and quality. Once trained, the bot can be embedded on any website with a snippet of code, and the company states the whole process - from adding data to a live, embeddable chatbot - takes about five minutes.

MyBot.Chat's main differentiation from broader customer-engagement suites is its narrow, content-ingestion-first focus and aggressive low-end pricing, starting at a genuinely usable free tier and a $19/month Basic plan, well below many chatbot-builder competitors it explicitly compares itself to in its own marketing (Dialogflow, Tidio, Intercom, Chatfuel, HubSpot Live Chat). It emphasizes short-term memory (keeping a single conversation coherent) and long-term memory (retaining details across a user's return visits) to make responses feel more personalized over time. Integrations extend the bot beyond the website to Microsoft Teams, Facebook Messenger and WhatsApp, and top-tier plans add white-labeling for agencies or resellers who want to offer the tool under their own brand. The company appears to be a small, independently run SaaS (contact and roadmap requests go through a single contact form), which keeps pricing low but means it lacks the enterprise features, review-site presence, and support scale of larger competitors.

Best for

MyBot.Chat fits small businesses, solo founders, freelancers and small agencies who want the cheapest possible way to add a ChatGPT-style, content-trained chatbot to a website for support and lead capture. It's a weak fit for larger enterprises or teams needing help-desk features, live-agent escalation, or vendor accountability backed by third-party review platforms.

Custom branding & white-label

Upgrade to customize the chat widget's look, or fully white-label it on the Premium plan for agencies and resellers.

Retraining on demand

Update a chatbot's underlying knowledge anytime by re-adding or editing source content.

Uploaded files are used for training and then deleted; text/website content is stored on U.S.-based servers and is not used to train third-party models.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The free plan is genuinely usable for a small site (100 messages/month, one chatbot, 400K training characters) with no credit card required.
  • Pricing undercuts most competitors it names directly (Intercom, Tidio, Chatfuel, HubSpot Live Chat), starting at $19/month for a live, embeddable bot.
  • Supports both premium (GPT-4o) and cost-efficient (Llama 3 70B) models, letting price-sensitive customers avoid paying for capability they don't need.
  • Ingests a wide range of source formats (website, PDF, Word, Excel, plain text, Q&A) rather than requiring content to be reformatted first.
  • Explicit, plain-language privacy commitments - uploaded files are deleted post-training and customer data isn't used to train OpenAI's or Meta's models.

Cons

  • No presence found on G2, Capterra, Trustpilot or major app-store review platforms, making it hard to independently verify customer satisfaction at scale.
  • Message caps on the Free and Basic tiers (100-2,000/month) will be outgrown quickly by any site with real traffic.
  • No mention of live-agent handoff, ticketing, or help-desk workflows that larger customer-support platforms include.
  • Appears to be a small, independently operated product, which may mean slower feature velocity and less support capacity than larger vendors.

Frequently asked questions

What AI models power MyBot.Chat?

Businesses can choose between OpenAI's GPT-4o, the cheaper GPT-4o-mini, or Meta's Llama 3 70B as the chatbot's underlying model.

How long does it take to set up a chatbot?

MyBot.Chat says a working chatbot can be live in about 5 minutes after adding a website URL, files or text content.

Is there a free plan?

Yes, the Free plan includes 100 messages/month and 1 chatbot with no credit card required, though it auto-deletes after 14 days of inactivity.

Where is my data stored, and is it used to train other AI models?

Data is stored on U.S.-based servers; uploaded files are deleted after training and content is not used to train OpenAI's or Meta's models.

Can I use MyBot.Chat on channels other than my website?

Yes, it integrates with Microsoft Teams, Facebook Messenger and WhatsApp in addition to website embedding.

Can I white-label the chatbot for clients?

Yes, white-labeling is available on the Premium ($199/month) plan, aimed at agencies reselling chatbots under their own brand.
