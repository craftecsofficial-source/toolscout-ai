MyBot.Chat is a decent budget pick for small sites that want a chatbot trained on their own pages and documents, with a usable free tier. Larger teams that need live-agent handoff, ticketing, or a vendor with a visible track record should skip it. You add a website, sitemap, PDFs, Word or Excel files, text or Q&A pairs, it processes them, and you paste an embed snippet on your site. Models offered are GPT-4o, GPT-4o-mini and Llama 3 70B, and the bot can also run on Microsoft Teams, Facebook Messenger and WhatsApp. It has short-term and long-term memory, on-demand retraining, logo and colour branding, and a white-label option on the top plan. The site claims data is encrypted and hosted in U.S. data centres. Pricing is by monthly messages, chatbot count and content size.

Best for

Small businesses, solo founders and small agencies wanting a cheap content-trained website chatbot. Not for enterprises, for support teams needing live-agent escalation or help-desk workflows, or for buyers who need independent third-party reviews and formal compliance documentation, none of which we could verify.

Key features

01

Multi-format content ingestion

Trains the chatbot on website URLs/sitemaps, PDF, Word and Excel files, plain text, or structured Q&A pairs.

02

Model choice

Businesses can select GPT-4o, GPT-4o-mini or Llama 3 70B as the chatbot's underlying language model, trading off cost and quality.

03

Short- and long-term memory

Short-term memory keeps a single chat coherent; long-term memory retains details across a user's repeat visits for more personalized replies.

04

No-code embed

Businesses get a working, embeddable chatbot in about 5 minutes with no coding required.

05

Multi-channel integrations

Deploy the same trained chatbot on a website, Microsoft Teams, Facebook Messenger or WhatsApp.

06

Custom branding & white-label

Upgrade to customize the chat widget's look, or fully white-label it on the Premium plan for agencies and resellers.

07

Retraining on demand

Update a chatbot's underlying knowledge anytime by re-adding or editing source content.

08

Data privacy controls

Uploaded files are used for training and then deleted; text/website content is stored on U.S.-based servers and is not used to train third-party models.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The free plan is genuinely usable for a small site (100 messages/month, one chatbot, 400K training characters) with no credit card required.
  • Pricing undercuts most competitors it names directly (Intercom, Tidio, Chatfuel, HubSpot Live Chat), starting at $19/month for a live, embeddable bot.
  • Supports both premium (GPT-4o) and cost-efficient (Llama 3 70B) models, letting price-sensitive customers avoid paying for capability they don't need.
  • Ingests a wide range of source formats (website, PDF, Word, Excel, plain text, Q&A) rather than requiring content to be reformatted first.
  • Explicit, plain-language privacy commitments - uploaded files are deleted post-training and customer data isn't used to train OpenAI's or Meta's models.

Cons

  • Free plan is for testing: 100 messages a month, and bots are removed after 14 days of inactivity.
  • Message caps (2,000 on Basic) are quickly outgrown by a busy site, and the jump to $99 is steep.
  • Lead capture and GPT-4o selection are locked to Standard and above; white label only on Premium.
  • Per the pricing FAQ, purchased plans run to month end with no partial-month or full refunds.
  • No G2, Capterra or Trustpilot presence found, and no live-agent handoff or ticketing is advertised.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

Yes: $0, 100 messages a month, 1 chatbot, 400,000 characters, 10 links and 1 MB of files. Free bots are deleted after 14 days of inactivity.

What does it cost to remove the branding?

White label is only on the $199/month Premium plan.

Which models can I use?

GPT-4o, GPT-4o-mini and Llama 3 70B. The pricing page lists choice of GPT-4o as a Standard and Premium feature.

Can I get a refund?

The pricing FAQ says plans last until month end and neither partial-month nor full refunds are offered.

What should I use if I want self-hosting?

MyBot.Chat is hosted only. AnythingLLM is an open-source option you run yourself, with more setup effort.
