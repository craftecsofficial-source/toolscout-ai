Semrush is a large, publicly traded (NYSE: SEMR) all-in-one digital marketing platform covering SEO, PPC, content marketing, competitive research, social media and — as of 2026 — AI/LLM visibility tracking under its 'Semrush One' positioning. Founded in 2008, it's one of the largest players in the SEO tooling category alongside Ahrefs and Moz, built on proprietary databases of over 25 billion keywords and 43 trillion+ backlinks.

Semrush's core differentiator is breadth: rather than specializing in one function, it bundles keyword research, technical site audits, backlink analysis, competitor traffic estimation, content tools and PPC research into a single subscription, and has recently extended that same breadth into monitoring brand visibility across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews.

Best for

Semrush fits SEO agencies, in-house marketing teams and competitive-research-heavy businesses that need broad tooling spanning SEO, PPC research, content and now AI-search visibility in one platform. It's a weaker fit for solo bloggers, freelancers, or anyone who only needs one narrow function — a cheaper, single-purpose tool will usually be more cost-effective.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Proprietary databases (25B+ keywords, 43T+ backlinks) give more comprehensive competitive data out of the box than many single-purpose rivals
  • Genuinely all-in-one: replaces separate rank tracking, backlink audit, technical SEO audit and content optimization subscriptions with one login
  • Strong, sustained satisfaction across large review samples on both G2 and Capterra
  • Actively expanding into AI/LLM visibility tracking ahead of many competitors, positioned around 'Semrush One'
  • Extensive free tools (AI Visibility Checker, backlink checker, keyword tool) and Semrush Academy lower the barrier to evaluate the platform
  • Frequent feature releases keep the platform current as search behavior shifts toward AI answer engines

Cons

  • No genuinely free full plan — the free account is capped at roughly 10 requests/day, and the only broader trial is 7 days
  • Pricing is the single most cited complaint across G2, Capterra and Reddit; the entry tier has risen over time
  • Reddit users report Semrush's competitor traffic estimates can be significantly off compared to ground-truth Google Search Console data
  • Steep learning curve for beginners given the sheer breadth of tools bundled into the platform
  • Some users report billing and cancellation friction
  • Extra seats and white-labeled reports are separate paid add-ons, so real per-team cost often runs above the sticker price

What reviewers say

Semrush holds strong, high-volume ratings on both major platforms, with reviewers consistently citing its all-in-one breadth and comprehensive competitor/backlink data as the main draw. Pricing is by far the most consistent complaint across all three sources (G2, Capterra, Reddit), and a secondary but recurring theme is skepticism about the accuracy of its traffic-estimation data compared to first-party analytics.

Frequently asked questions

Does Semrush have a free plan?

Not a full free plan — signing up without a credit card gives a free account limited to about 10 requests/day. Full plans start at $139/mo (or $117.33/mo billed annually), with a 7-day free trial available on paid tiers.

How much does Semrush cost in 2026?

Current tiers are SEO $139/mo, Starter $199/mo, Pro+ $299/mo and Advanced $549/mo on monthly billing, with roughly 17% savings if billed annually. Enterprise is custom-priced.

Is Semrush worth it for a small business or freelancer?

Reviewers are split: the data breadth is valued highly, but pricing is consistently the top complaint from smaller users.

How does Semrush compare to Ahrefs?

Both are broad SEO suites with large proprietary databases; Semrush is generally seen as broader (including PPC, social and AI visibility tools), while Ahrefs is often cited as having a more focused, backlink-centric interface.

What is Semrush's AI visibility tracking?

Introduced under the 'Semrush One' banner, it monitors how often and how favorably a domain is mentioned or cited in AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, with prompt-tracking limits that scale by plan.

Can I add more users or white-labeled reports to my Semrush plan?

Yes, these are separate paid add-ons on top of the base subscription price.
