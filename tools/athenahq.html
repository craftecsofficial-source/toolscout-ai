AthenaHQ is a good first tool for startups and small teams that want to try AI visibility tracking without paying; large teams with predictable-budget requirements should check how fast credits burn before committing. The free Essential plan gives 300 credits, and the next step up is $295 a month.

It tracks where your brand shows up and gets cited in AI answers, compares you with competitors, finds content gaps, and suggests on-page and off-page fixes. A copilot called Ask Athena answers questions from your own data, and there is PR monitoring with sentiment and alerts. Usage is metered in credits, where one credit is one AI response, so more prompts or engines means faster spend.

Some of the more advanced features (Oracle discrepancy detection, the Athena Citation Engine, knowledge base, BI integrations, multi-region tracking) are Enterprise-only and quote-based. Performance figures on its site are vendor-reported and we could not verify them.

Best for

Suits startups, agencies and lean marketing teams who want to start free and pay by usage. Not for large enterprises needing a long-established vendor, for buyers who need a fixed flat fee they can forecast, or for teams needing multi-region or multi-language tracking and BI integrations without negotiating an Enterprise contract.

One credit equals one AI response; Essential includes 300, Starter 3,600 per month, Enterprise is negotiated.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The free Essential plan is a permanent tier with 300 credits and unlimited users, so a solo marketer can try it without a card trial clock.
  • Starter covers 11 models, including Claude, Grok, DeepSeek, Meta AI and Mistral, which is broad for an entry paid plan.
  • The product goes beyond a dashboard, with content recommendations, a content agent and a data-aware copilot.
  • G2 shows 4.9/5 from 39 reviews as of today, though the sample is small.
  • Pricing page is public, with the three tiers clearly listed.

Cons

  • Credits are metered per AI response, so costs grow with prompts and engines tracked and are hard to forecast.
  • API access and extra credits are billed on top of Starter, and their prices are only on request.
  • The page shows 17% off for annual billing but not the annual price, so confirm the real figure with the vendor.
  • Free tier is narrow: 300 credits and 5 engines, and the smaller review base means less independent evidence.
  • Oracle, ACE, the knowledge base, BI integrations and multi-region tracking sit behind quote-based Enterprise.
  • Case-study results on the site are vendor-reported and unverified.

      Frequently asked questions

      Is AthenaHQ free?

      Yes, the Essential plan is free with 300 credits and covers ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini and Copilot.

      How much is the paid plan?

      Starter is $295/month for 3,600 credits and 11 models. Annual billing is listed as 17% off, but the page does not show the resulting price.

      What is a credit?

      One credit is one AI response. Heavier tracking uses credits faster, and extra credits are a paid add-on.

      Is API access included?

      Not on Starter; API access and extra credits are paid add-ons with pricing on request. The pricing page is inconsistent about Enterprise inclusion, so confirm with sales.

      Which features need Enterprise?

      Oracle discrepancy detection, the Athena Citation Engine, knowledge base and claim review, SSO, audit logs, multi-region and BI integrations.
