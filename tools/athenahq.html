AthenaHQ was founded in 2025 by Andrew Yan and Alan Yao, engineers with backgrounds at Google Search, DeepMind, ServiceNow, and early hands-on work with OpenAI's platform. The company is part of Y Combinator and has raised approximately $2.2-2.7M in seed funding. AthenaHQ positions itself around 'action on AI search': rather than stopping at a visibility dashboard, it combines real-time prompt tracking, competitive share-of-voice analysis, sentiment analysis, and hallucination detection with automated content and technical recommendations meant to directly close AI citation gaps.

AthenaHQ's clearest differentiator is accessibility: it is the only platform among Profound, Scrunch AI, and AthenaHQ that offers a real free tier (Essential, with included credits) rather than gating everything behind a paid plan or short trial. Its credit-based model (1 credit = 1 AI response) lets smaller teams start immediately and scale spend with actual usage instead of committing to a fixed monthly seat/prompt bundle, while still offering enterprise-grade depth — including Oracle (a discrepancy/hallucination detection engine) and the newer Athena Citation Engine (ACE) — for larger customers who need it.

Best for

AthenaHQ is best suited for startups, agencies, and lean in-house marketing teams that want to start monitoring AI visibility immediately at zero cost and scale spend gradually via credits as usage grows, particularly teams comfortable with a newer, YC-backed vendor. It's also a reasonable fit for teams that want hallucination/discrepancy detection without Scrunch AI's higher entry price. Large enterprises that need mature, extensively reviewed vendors with long procurement track records, or teams that strongly prefer predictable flat-fee pricing over credit metering, may be better served by Profound or by treating AthenaHQ's Enterprise tier as the more appropriate entry point.

Key features

01

Multi-Model Visibility Monitoring

Tracks brand and competitor presence across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Gemini, Claude, Microsoft Copilot, and Grok, with additional models available on request.

02

Oracle Discrepancy Detection

Flags cases where AI engines generate inaccurate, outdated, or hallucinated claims about a brand, similar in purpose to Scrunch's hallucination detection.

03

Athena Citation Engine (ACE)

A newer, Enterprise-tier feature focused on deeper citation intelligence — identifying and ranking the sources AI engines cite most when discussing a brand or category.

04

Content Optimization Agent

Generates GEO-optimized articles and FAQs and applies self-learning improvement over time based on what drives better AI citation performance.

05

Competitive Benchmarking & Impersonation Detection

Tracks competitor share of voice and flags cases where AI engines misattribute or confuse a brand with competitors.

06

Integrations

Connects AI visibility data to GA4, Shopify, and Webflow on Starter; Enterprise extends this to BI tools including Tableau, Power BI, and Looker.

07

Credit-Based Usage Model

Usage is metered in credits (1 credit = 1 AI response); the free Essential plan includes 300 credits, Starter includes 3,600 credits/month, and Enterprise credit allocation is custom-negotiated.

08

Knowledge Base & Claim Review (Enterprise)

Lets enterprise teams maintain a structured source-of-truth about the brand that AthenaHQ uses to review and validate AI-generated claims against.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • AthenaHQ is the only one of these tools offering an actual ongoing free tier rather than a time-boxed trial, which lowers the barrier to entry dramatically for solo marketers and early-stage startups.
  • Even the entry paid tier (Starter, $295/month) tracks all 9 supported models including less commonly covered engines like Microsoft Copilot and Grok, matching or exceeding the engine breadth of pricier competitors.
  • G2 shows a notably high rating (4.9/5) for AthenaHQ, alongside a Spring 2026 'High Performer' badge and a Slashdot 'Most Loved Software' recognition, suggesting strong early customer satisfaction.
  • The founding team's background (ex-Google Search, DeepMind, and hands-on OpenAI platform experience) and Y Combinator backing lend technical credibility unusual for such a young company.
  • Oracle and the newer Athena Citation Engine give AthenaHQ genuine hallucination-detection and citation-intelligence depth comparable to Scrunch AI's flagship differentiators, not just a rebranded monitoring dashboard.
  • Documented customer case studies cite strong outcomes, though as vendor-reported figures these should be treated as directional rather than independently audited.

Cons

  • The credit-based model (1 credit = 1 AI response) makes monthly cost harder to predict than a flat per-seat or per-prompt plan, especially once teams add API access or extra credits as paid add-ons.
  • AthenaHQ is very new (founded 2025), so its review base is small: G2 shows only 34 reviews as of August 2026, and its Capterra listing currently shows 0 reviews despite being indexed.
  • Pricing has shown some inconsistency across channels — Capterra listed a promotional first-month rate off the standard $295/month, which buyers should clarify directly with AthenaHQ before budgeting.
  • As a young, VC/YC-backed startup with only ~$2.2-2.7M raised, AthenaHQ carries more platform/roadmap risk than better-capitalized competitors like Profound or Scrunch AI (now backed by Sitecore).
  • Some of the most distinctive Enterprise capabilities (ACE, Oracle depth, Knowledge Base, BI integrations) are gated behind custom Enterprise pricing, meaning the free and Starter tiers don't show the platform's full ceiling.
  • Multi-region and multi-language support is limited on Starter, with multi-region support reserved for Enterprise, which limits usefulness for global brands on the lower tiers.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan for AthenaHQ?

Yes. The Essential plan is free and includes $25 of credit (300 credits) to start, covering visibility tracking across ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews, Gemini, and Claude.

How much does AthenaHQ cost?

Essential is free; Starter is $295/month with 3,600 credits/month and coverage of all 9 tracked models; Enterprise is custom-priced. Some third-party listings have shown a promotional first-month rate as low as $95 for new signups — confirm current pricing directly with AthenaHQ.

How is AthenaHQ different from Profound or Scrunch AI?

AthenaHQ's main differentiators are its genuinely free entry tier and credit-based usage pricing, versus Profound's flat-fee enterprise-depth model and Scrunch AI's hallucination-detection-plus-AXP focus (now backed by Sitecore).

Which AI engines does AthenaHQ track?

Up to 9 engines on the Starter plan and above: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Gemini, Claude, Microsoft Copilot, and Grok, with additional models available on request.

What is Oracle in AthenaHQ?

Oracle is AthenaHQ's discrepancy detection engine, which flags cases where AI engines generate inaccurate or hallucinated claims about a brand.

Who founded AthenaHQ and is it a stable company to buy from?

AthenaHQ was founded in 2025 by Andrew Yan and Alan Yao (ex-Google Search/DeepMind) and is backed by Y Combinator with roughly $2.2-2.7M raised; it is a young company, so buyers evaluating it for long-term enterprise contracts should factor in its early stage relative to better-capitalized competitors like Profound.
