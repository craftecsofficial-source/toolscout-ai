Notta suits people who want cheap, multilingual transcription of calls and uploaded recordings, and it is a good fit for solo professionals, students and small teams. Skip it if you need the free tier for real work (each recording is capped at 3 minutes) or want deep sales-coaching analytics. It transcribes live meetings through a bot that joins Zoom, Meet, Teams or Webex, a bot-free device recorder, or uploaded audio and video, in 58 languages per the vendor. On top of the transcript it produces summaries, action items, cross-meeting Q&A and generated documents using an AI-credit allowance, and paid tiers add exports, custom vocabulary, and (Business) CRM and Zapier links.

Best for

Good for freelancers, students, consultants and small teams who record many calls, especially in more than one language. Not for people hoping to run on the free plan (3 minutes per recording), teams that need CRM sync without paying for Business, or buyers who need a quoted price for under 51 seats on Enterprise.

Key features

01

Real-time meeting transcription

Transcribes live speech during meetings and calls as they happen, with support for online meeting bots (Zoom, Meet, Teams, Webex) or bot-free on-device recording.

02

Notta Brain (AI Notes & Summaries)

Generates AI-powered meeting summaries, action items, and lets users ask questions across their entire meeting history, consuming AI credits per use.

03

58+ language transcription & translation

Supports transcription across dozens of languages plus bilingual transcription (two languages at once) and real-time translation during meetings.

04

Document Builder

Converts transcripts and meeting content directly into PDF, Word, Excel files, or slide decks using AI credits.

05

Speaker identification

Automatically distinguishes and labels different speakers within a transcript for easier review and attribution.

06

On-device (privacy mode) transcription

Processes transcription locally on the device so audio content doesn't leave the user's machine, aimed at privacy-sensitive use cases.

07

CRM and workflow integrations

Syncs meeting notes and transcripts to Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho CRM, Zendesk Sell, plus Notion, Slack, Google Drive, and Zapier (Business tier and above).

08

Notta Memo hardware

A companion physical recording device that lets users capture in-person conversations and calls, syncing recordings into the Notta app.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Pro at $97.99/year is inexpensive for 1,800 minutes a month and recordings up to 5 hours.
  • Bot-based and bot-free capture are both offered, which helps when a meeting bot is not welcome.
  • 58-language transcription plus bilingual and translation features (a few free uses monthly, paid add-ons beyond that).
  • Business has unlimited minutes and CRM/Zapier integration at about $16.67 per seat per month on annual billing.

Cons

  • Free plan allows only 3 minutes per recording, so it works as a trial, not a tool.
  • Translation and bilingual transcription are capped at a few free uses per month; more requires paid add-ons ($6-$9/month annual, extra for each).
  • Video recording, CRM and Zapier are Business-only, and Notta Brain AI credits are limited (1,000/month on listed plans) with a separate paid add-on for more.
  • Pricing page does not show monthly-billing prices for Pro or Business, so month-to-month cost could not be verified.

Frequently asked questions

Is Notta free?

Yes, the free plan gives 120 minutes a month, but each recording is limited to 3 minutes, with 50 uploads and 10 AI summaries monthly.

Does Notta need a bot in my meeting?

No. It offers a bot that joins calls on Zoom, Meet, Teams and Webex, and a bot-free option that records from your device.

How many languages does it support?

The vendor states 58 languages for transcription and translation. AI content generation is listed for fewer (English, Japanese, Chinese, Spanish, German and others).

Which plan do I need for CRM sync?

Business or Enterprise. The pricing page lists CRM and Zapier integration starting at Business.

Is it secure enough for regulated work?

Notta lists SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR and HIPAA on its site, but we did not review audit reports or the HIPAA terms, so check with the vendor before handling regulated data.
