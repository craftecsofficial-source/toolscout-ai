Recraft is a good pick for designers, product teams and sellers who need scalable vector graphics, icons and on-brand illustrations rather than one-off pictures. Skip it if you only want occasional casual images (free tools may do) or need to use free-tier output commercially, which its terms do not allow. It generates images and vectors from text prompts, lets you build custom styles from your own reference images, and bundles utilities such as an upscaler, background remover, eraser and mockup generator. You can use it in a browser studio, mobile apps, or via an API.

Best for

Who it is for: designers, brand and marketing teams, and sellers who need vector assets, icon sets or illustrations in a consistent style, and developers who want an image API. Who it is not for: people who only need an occasional free image for commercial use (the Free plan forbids that), or anyone who needs hand-drawn precision without editing afterwards.

Key features

01

Vector generation and vectorizer

Creates editable vector graphics from prompts, and can convert existing images to vectors.

02

Custom styles

Upload your own images to define a style, which the site says works without training a model, to keep generations visually consistent.

03

Image generation

Raster images from text prompts, from photographic looks to illustration; the studio also offers other frontier models alongside Recraft's own.

04

Editing utilities

Photo editor, upscaler, background remover and AI eraser inside the same workspace.

05

Mockup generator

Places generated or uploaded artwork into product and marketing mockups.

06

API

Programmatic generation, editing, background removal, inpainting, outpainting and batch jobs, billed separately in prepaid API Units.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Recraft images commercially?

Only on paid plans, which grant full ownership and commercial rights for images made while subscribed. Free-plan images are public and not licensed for commercial use.

How much does Recraft cost?

There is a Free plan and paid Basic, Pro and Teams plans. We could not verify the current prices from the official page today, so check recraft.ai/pricing.

Do unused credits carry over?

Subscription credits reset each billing cycle and do not roll over. Separately bought top-up credits do not expire.

Does it make real vector files?

Yes, it has vector generation and an image vectorizer that output editable vector graphics.

Is there an API?

Yes. It is billed in prepaid API Units, separate from subscription credits.
