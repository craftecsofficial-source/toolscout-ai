Motion suits busy solo professionals and small teams whose calendar is crowded and who want software to decide when each task gets done. Skip it if you want a simple to-do list, a free tool, or deep project management. It places tasks into your calendar as time blocks by priority and deadline, re-plans when meetings or priorities change, and has added projects, docs, a meeting notetaker and AI chat. Its published figures (time saved, notetaker accuracy) are the vendor's own claims without methodology.

Best for

Freelancers, consultants, founders and small teams with deadline-heavy workloads who accept software-driven scheduling. It is NOT for: people who want full manual control of their day, anyone needing a free plan, teams that need rich project management (dedicated tools go deeper), or users who rely mainly on mobile.

Key features

01

AI Calendar and auto-scheduling

Automatically time-blocks tasks onto a user's calendar based on deadlines and priority, and reshuffles the schedule in real time when meetings or urgent work are added.

02

AI Task Planner

Ranks and sequences tasks by urgency and importance, deciding what should be worked on next rather than leaving prioritization to the user.

03

AI Meeting Assistant

Simplifies meeting scheduling and booking links, finding available time across a user's calendars without manual back-and-forth.

04

AI Chat and Docs Assistant

An in-app AI chat for quick answers and an assistant for drafting and organizing written docs and notes within the platform.

05

AI Meeting Notetaker

Automatically captures meeting notes and summaries.

06

Team Capacity Planning and Gantt charts (Business AI tier)

Adds workload visibility across a team, timeline/Gantt views and time tracking for teams managing multiple people's schedules together.

07

Multi-calendar sync

Connects and reconciles multiple calendars into a single unified schedule to avoid conflicts.

08

Cross-platform apps

Native apps for iOS, Android, Mac and Windows, alongside the web app, though the mobile experience is frequently rated weaker than desktop.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Auto-scheduling genuinely works as advertised for many users, actively rebalancing a chaotic calendar rather than just displaying it
  • Combines calendar automation and project/task management in one product, reducing the need for separate scheduling and PM tools
  • Particularly well-reviewed by users managing high task volume or attention-management challenges, who describe it as reducing decision fatigue
  • Meeting notetaking and AI chat reduce context-switching by keeping notes and quick answers inside the same tool as the schedule
  • Multi-calendar sync is useful for people juggling several work and personal calendars simultaneously
  • Actively rebuilds a schedule automatically when priorities shift, rather than requiring manual re-planning

Cons

  • No permanent free plan — only a 7-day free trial, making it a harder tool to evaluate at length before paying
  • Pricing is a recurring complaint, both for being relatively high per seat and for having increased over time
  • Onboarding and initial setup are frequently described as steep and non-intuitive, with a real learning curve before the AI scheduling 'clicks'
  • The mobile app is consistently rated weaker than desktop, with some features unavailable or harder to use on phones
  • Project management depth is thinner than dedicated tools like ClickUp or Asana
  • The UI is described by some long-time users as cluttered and slow to improve

What reviewers say

Motion's reviews are solid but noticeably more mixed than higher-volume categories like automation or docs tools. Capterra reviews are especially split: several 4-5 star reviews praise the auto-scheduling and 'keeps moving incomplete tasks forward' mechanic as genuinely transformative for busy or easily-overwhelmed users, while a meaningful minority of 2-3 star reviews describe it as expensive, cumbersome and non-intuitive to set up. Reddit and independent review sites echo this split, consistently naming steep onboarding, a cluttered UI, a weak mobile app and rising prices as the most common complaints, even among users who otherwise recommend the tool.

Frequently praised

  • Auto-scheduling automatically reprioritizes and reschedules incomplete tasks
  • Combines calendar automation and project management in a single tool
  • Particularly valuable for users managing high task volume or attention/focus challenges
  • Multi-calendar sync consolidates work and personal schedules into one view

Frequently criticized

  • No permanent free plan, only a 7-day trial
  • Pricing seen as steep and has increased over time
  • Steep onboarding curve and a UI many long-time users call cluttered
  • Mobile app is notably weaker than the desktop/web experience

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

No permanent free plan was listed; the pricing page offers a free trial but did not state its length (earlier data said 7 days).

What are AI credits?

Per the pricing page, Pro AI includes 7,500 credits per seat per month and Business AI 15,000, with extra credits sold at 25 and 19 cents per 100 respectively.

Pro AI or Business AI?

Business AI adds team capacity planning, dashboards, Gantt charts, time tracking and permissions. Individuals usually only need Pro AI.

Does it work with Google and Outlook calendars?

The site lists Gmail and Outlook integrations and says it merges work and personal calendars.
