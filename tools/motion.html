Motion is an AI-powered task, calendar and project management platform that automatically builds and continuously re-optimizes a user's daily schedule based on task deadlines, priorities and existing meetings. Rather than being a passive to-do list or calendar app, its core mechanic is active: Motion's AI engine assigns time blocks to tasks, and if something is missed or a new priority appears, it automatically reshuffles the rest of the schedule to compensate.

Its core differentiator versus general project-management tools like Asana or ClickUp is that Motion actively schedules work for you rather than just tracking it — you give it tasks, deadlines and calendars, and it decides when you'll actually do each thing. Versus simpler calendar-automation tools like Reclaim, Motion adds a fuller project-management layer (projects, dashboards, Gantt-style timelines on the Business tier) on top of the scheduling engine.

Best for

Motion is best suited for solo professionals and small teams with genuinely calendar-heavy, deadline-driven workloads — freelancers, consultants, attorneys and founders juggling many meetings and shifting priorities who want their schedule actively managed rather than manually maintained. It's a weaker fit for people whose main need is note-taking or knowledge management, teams needing deep, dedicated project-management features (better served by ClickUp, Asana or Monday), or anyone unwilling to pay a per-seat AI-tool price without a permanent free tier.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Auto-scheduling genuinely works as advertised for many users, actively rebalancing a chaotic calendar rather than just displaying it
  • Combines calendar automation and project/task management in one product, reducing the need for separate scheduling and PM tools
  • Particularly well-reviewed by users managing high task volume or attention-management challenges, who describe it as reducing decision fatigue
  • Meeting notetaking and AI chat reduce context-switching by keeping notes and quick answers inside the same tool as the schedule
  • Multi-calendar sync is useful for people juggling several work and personal calendars simultaneously
  • Actively rebuilds a schedule automatically when priorities shift, rather than requiring manual re-planning

Cons

  • No permanent free plan — only a 7-day free trial, making it a harder tool to evaluate at length before paying
  • Pricing is a recurring complaint, both for being relatively high per seat and for having increased over time
  • Onboarding and initial setup are frequently described as steep and non-intuitive, with a real learning curve before the AI scheduling 'clicks'
  • The mobile app is consistently rated weaker than desktop, with some features unavailable or harder to use on phones
  • Project management depth is thinner than dedicated tools like ClickUp or Asana
  • The UI is described by some long-time users as cluttered and slow to improve

What reviewers say

Motion's reviews are solid but noticeably more mixed than higher-volume categories like automation or docs tools. Capterra reviews are especially split: several 4-5 star reviews praise the auto-scheduling and 'keeps moving incomplete tasks forward' mechanic as genuinely transformative for busy or easily-overwhelmed users, while a meaningful minority of 2-3 star reviews describe it as expensive, cumbersome and non-intuitive to set up. Reddit and independent review sites echo this split, consistently naming steep onboarding, a cluttered UI, a weak mobile app and rising prices as the most common complaints, even among users who otherwise recommend the tool.

Frequently asked questions

Does Motion have a free plan?

No, Motion does not offer a permanent free plan — it offers a 7-day free trial on both the Pro AI and Business AI tiers before billing begins.

What's the difference between Pro AI and Business AI?

Pro AI ($19/seat/mo annual) covers core AI scheduling, tasks, calendar and docs for individuals and small teams, while Business AI ($29/seat/mo annual) adds team capacity planning, Gantt/timeline views, time tracking and permissions.

How does Motion's auto-scheduling actually work?

You give Motion your tasks, deadlines and priorities alongside your connected calendars, and its AI engine assigns time blocks automatically, continuously re-shuffling the plan in real time whenever new meetings or urgent tasks appear.

Is Motion good for teams or just individuals?

Both — Pro AI is aimed at individuals and small teams, while Business AI adds team-specific features like capacity planning, shared dashboards and central billing for larger organizations.

What are Motion's AI credits used for?

Credits are consumed by AI-powered features like the task planner, writer/editor and chat; Pro AI includes 7,500 credits/seat/month and Business AI includes 15,000/seat/month.

Is Motion worth the price compared to a regular calendar app?

For people with genuinely heavy, shifting workloads and many meetings, reviewers generally say yes — the active rescheduling saves real time; for lighter scheduling needs, it's harder to justify against free calendar tools.
