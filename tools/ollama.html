Ollama is a great choice for developers and tinkerers who want to run open-source models on their own machine with no service fees and private prompts. Skip it if you need frontier-quality models with no hardware limits and no tuning, or want a polished chat product out of the box. It handles downloading, running and serving open models behind a simple CLI and local API on macOS, Windows and Linux, and plenty of third-party tools plug into that API. For models too large for your machine it also offers a cloud option with monthly plans that include usage credits, and pay-as-you-go token pricing beyond that.

Best for

Who it is for: developers building scripts, agents or apps on open models, teams that need prompts to stay on their own hardware, and hobbyists with a decent GPU or Apple Silicon Mac. Who it is not for: people with weak hardware who want large models at fixed cost (cloud use is metered), non-technical users wanting a ready-made chat app, or teams needing a managed enterprise inference SLA from the free tier.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Local inference has no service fees and no usage cap beyond your hardware
  • Setup takes minutes and works the same on macOS, Windows and Linux
  • Prompts stay on your device in local mode, and the site says prompts are never stored or trained on
  • Many third-party UIs, editors and agent frameworks already integrate with its API
  • Cloud option lets you reach bigger models without buying a GPU, with transparent per-token rates (for example gpt-oss:20b at $0.07 input / $0.30 output per million tokens)

Cons

  • Which models are practical depends on your RAM and GPU; large models can be slow or impossible locally
  • Cloud usage is metered: included credits do not roll over, and extra credits are bought separately
  • Concurrency is capped (Free 1, Pro 3, Max 10); extra requests queue or are rejected when the queue is full
  • Team is listed at $500/mo as early access, a big jump from individual plans
  • Mostly CLI and API; you will likely add a separate chat front end
  • Peak-hour cloud rates are higher (weekdays 12:00-18:00 UTC)

Frequently asked questions

Is Ollama free?

Running models on your own hardware is free and unlimited. The Free cloud tier gives starter credits for starter models; larger cloud models need a paid plan or purchased credits.

What does Ollama Pro cost and include?

$20/mo or $200/yr, with $60 in monthly usage credits and 3 concurrent requests. Unused credits do not roll over.

What hardware do I need?

It runs on macOS, Windows and Linux. Small models run on modest machines, but bigger models need substantial RAM or a capable GPU; I did not verify exact requirements.

Is my data private?

Local mode keeps prompts on your machine, and the site says prompts are never stored or trained on.

How do I use it with other tools?

Through its local REST API, which many editors, chat UIs and agent frameworks support.
