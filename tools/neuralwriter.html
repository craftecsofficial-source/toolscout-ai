NeuralWriter is a free, browser-based AI paraphrasing tool that rewrites pasted text into different wording while aiming to preserve the original meaning. The core web tool offers a 'Light' mode (faster, more conservative rewriting) and a 'Hard' mode (deeper rewriting, higher uniqueness score) for up to 10,000 characters at a time, with no registration or payment required at any point. Around the core paraphraser, the site bundles a family of adjacent free utilities — a translator, a text summarizer, a word counter, a headline generator, an AI content detector, and AI image generation/upscaling tools — positioning NeuralWriter as a lightweight, all-in-one free writing-utility hub rather than a single-feature product. A companion Chrome extension ('Rewrite AI — Paraphrase & Rewrite Text') lets users select text on any webpage and rewrite it in place via a Ctrl+Alt+P shortcut, using either a local offline engine for English or the same cloud AI backend as the website.

The company operates out of Barcelona, Spain, and its extension listing identifies the developer as 'AI Teknolodgies.' Technically, the extension authenticates to NeuralWriter's backend via a Chrome MV3 offscreen document that loads a token page hosted at research.neuralwriter.com, refreshing the session token roughly every 50 minutes — a detail disclosed in the extension's own Chrome Web Store listing. NeuralWriter differentiates itself from paid paraphrasers like QuillBot primarily on price (fully free vs. subscription-gated advanced modes) and frictionlessness (no account required), while offering a narrower feature set — it has no dedicated grammar checker, no citation tooling, and its plagiarism/AI-detection claims are unverified by any third party. The site's homepage claims '200,000+ users' and a 4.1/5 rating for its extension, but the live Chrome Web Store listing for that same extension shows a much smaller footprint: roughly 2,000 users and just 4 ratings averaging 4.2/5, a discrepancy worth noting when evaluating the tool's claimed scale.

Best for

Best fit for students, bloggers, and non-native English speakers who want a fast, no-cost way to rephrase a paragraph or beat basic uniqueness checks without signing up for anything. A weak fit for professional writers or teams needing grammar checking, citation support, brand voice control, or any tool with a verifiable independent review track record.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Zero cost and zero signup friction — most competing paraphrasers gate their better rewrite quality or word counts behind a paid tier or account creation; NeuralWriter's core rewriting is free outright.
  • The Chrome extension's in-page rewriting (select text anywhere, hit a hotkey) is a genuinely convenient workflow compared to copy-pasting into a separate tab.
  • Bundling adjacent tools (translate, summarize, word count, headline generation) means casual users can do several small writing tasks from one free hub instead of juggling multiple sites.
  • The 'diff' style comparison and stated uniqueness scoring give users a visible signal of how much a Hard-mode rewrite actually changed versus the original.
  • Support for 30+ languages on the web tool is broader than many free competitors that are English-only.

Cons

  • The tool's own Chrome Web Store listing shows only 4 ratings and about 2,000 users, far below the '200,000+ users, 4.1/5' figure advertised on NeuralWriter's own homepage — that scale claim could not be independently verified.
  • No dedicated grammar or spell-checking layer, and no citation/plagiarism-report tooling, unlike more full-featured competitors such as Grammarly or QuillBot.
  • Because rewriting relies on a cloud backend, pasted text is transmitted to NeuralWriter's servers, and the extension's own privacy disclosure states it handles personally identifiable information — worth noting for anyone pasting sensitive text.
  • There is no visible company transparency (no team page, funding info, or customer list), and no listing on G2, Capterra, or Trustpilot to independently corroborate quality or support responsiveness.
  • Free, ad/traffic-supported paraphrasing tools like this typically monetize through affiliate links or ads elsewhere on the site rather than a clear business model, which is worth factoring into trust assessments.

What reviewers say

Frequently asked questions

Is NeuralWriter actually free?

Yes — the paraphrasing tool, Chrome extension, and bundled utilities (translator, summarizer, word counter, headline generator) are all usable without payment or account creation.

Will NeuralWriter's rewrites be flagged as AI-generated?

NeuralWriter claims its rewrites are humanized and less likely to be flagged, but this claim is not independently verified by any third-party AI-detection benchmark.

Does paraphrasing with NeuralWriter count as plagiarism?

The company claims its Hard mode changes entire phrases rather than just swapping synonyms, which it argues makes output less likely to be flagged as plagiarized — but this is a vendor claim, not an independent finding.

What's the difference between Light and Hard mode?

Light mode is faster with minor changes and lower resulting uniqueness; Hard mode does a deeper rewrite of full sentences for higher uniqueness.

Does NeuralWriter work in languages other than English?

Yes, the web tool supports 30+ languages, and the Chrome extension supports 11, including Spanish, French, German, Chinese, and Japanese.

Is NeuralWriter the same company as NeuronWriter (the SEO tool)?

No — NeuralWriter (neuralwriter.com) is a free paraphrasing/rewriting utility site; it is a distinct product from NeuronWriter, an unrelated SEO content-optimization platform, and the two are sometimes confused due to the similar name.
