Business Pitch suits first-time founders who want a quick, structured first draft of an investor or stakeholder pitch from a one-line idea; skip it if you need a polished, fully branded deck or know you need detailed slide editing and file export. It turns a short description into a value proposition, business summary, market breakdown and supporting visuals, framed around standard pitch conventions.

The public pages say little about how you edit the result or what formats you can export, so treat it as a drafting aid and test the free tier before paying.

Best for

First-time founders, solo entrepreneurs and early-stage teams who want a credible first-draft pitch fast without design or pitch-writing experience. It is not for teams that need pixel-perfect brand control, agency-level design, or confirmed PPTX export and collaboration features, nor for a high-stakes fundraise without human review of the content.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Replaces researching pitch structure and laying out slides with a single short prompt, saving setup time for a first draft.
  • Covers sections investors expect (value proposition, summary, market) without prior knowledge of deck conventions.
  • The free weekly allowance is enough to test whether the tone and structure fit your idea.
  • Paid tiers are clearly listed, and the Founder tier adds weekly live pitch reviews, a human-feedback element few generators offer.
  • Low barrier: no templates or design tooling to learn.

Cons

  • Three free pitches per week is tight if you want to iterate on wording or angles.
  • The plan page does not say whether prices are monthly or yearly, so true cost is unclear until checkout.
  • No sample deck or testimonial on the marketing page, so quality is judged only after sign-up.
  • How much you can edit slides, reorder content or change visuals afterwards is not described publicly.
  • Export formats (PDF, PPTX, etc.) are not mentioned, which matters if you must share or edit outside the platform.
  • Ads appear on lower tiers (the paid plans are advertised as ad-free).

Frequently asked questions

What does Business Pitch generate?

A structured pitch from a short idea description, including a value proposition, business summary, market breakdown and visuals.

Is it free?

There is a free plan with 3 pitch creations per week. Paid plans are Medium at $6.99 (35 creations) and Founder at $69.99 (450 creations).

Is $6.99 / $69.99 monthly or yearly?

Not stated on the plan page, which only says users can choose a monthly or yearly plan. Confirm at checkout.

Can I export to PowerPoint or PDF?

I could not verify export formats from the public pages; test it on the free plan first.

Can it replace a pitch consultant?

It gives a fast first draft, but for a serious fundraise you will want human review; the Founder plan includes weekly live pitch reviews.
