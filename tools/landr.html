LANDR is a good fit for solo artists who want a quick, affordable way to master and release tracks without hiring anyone. Skip it if you want hands-on control over EQ and compression, or if you only need cheap distribution, since you would pay for a bundle you do not use.

You upload a mix and its AI engine returns a mastered version in minutes; the same engine also runs as a DAW plugin and in a mobile app. Around that sit distribution to 150+ streaming and social platforms with unlimited releases, a 3M+ sample library, 70+ plugins, remote collaboration sessions, a directory for hiring music professionals and 200+ courses. Its Fair Trade AI program lets artists opt in to AI training use in exchange for payment (currently a $5 advance per eligible track, as a limited-time offer).

Best for

Solo and independent musicians who want fast, affordable mastering and release distribution in one place. Not for engineers or labels wanting fine manual mastering control, artists who only need distribution, or anyone who needs guaranteed fast support on tight release schedules.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Mastering, distribution, samples and plugins sit under one login and subscription, which can replace a separate distributor plus mastering service.
  • Mastering is quick and needs no audio-engineering knowledge: upload, choose a style, download.
  • The same engine is available as a DAW plugin and mobile app, so you can master inside a session.
  • Distribution is unlimited in releases, covering 150+ platforms.
  • Fair Trade AI is opt-in and pays contributing artists, unlike platforms that train without consent.

Cons

  • AI mastering offers limited manual control compared with a human engineer or a dedicated mastering plugin; tracks needing surgical fixes should be mixed better first.
  • The bundle is poor value if you only need distribution; dedicated distributors may be cheaper for that single job.
  • The pricing page we could read showed a single bundled price and no tier breakdown, so confirm what your plan's file formats and limits are before paying.
  • Past user reports mention slow support near release deadlines and difficulty editing metadata after release; verify current behavior before a time-critical launch.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free LANDR plan?

LANDR offers a free account for people not ready to subscribe, but the exact free limits were not verifiable. Check the sign-up page.

How much does LANDR cost?

The Studio bundle is advertised from $8.25/month. Other tiers and pay-per-track prices were not verifiable, so see landr.com/pricing.

Is distribution included with mastering?

Per the Studio bundle description, yes: unlimited AI mastering and distribution are included together.

Can I master inside my DAW?

Yes, LANDR offers a mastering plugin for DAWs as well as a web app and a mobile app.

Should I use LANDR or a human mastering engineer?

Use LANDR for fast, low-cost masters of reasonably good mixes; hire an engineer if you need detailed manual control or a release-critical result.
