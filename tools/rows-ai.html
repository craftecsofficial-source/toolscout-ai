Rows is a good fit for small teams that want recurring business reports pulled from several tools and described in plain English, and a poor fit for anyone who needs deep Excel-style modeling, heavy statistics, or certainty about the product's long-term future. The site now carries a banner saying Rows joined Superhuman, without saying what that means for the product, so buyers should treat the roadmap as unconfirmed. In practice Rows works as a spreadsheet with an AI analyst: you describe what you want, it imports data from over 50 sources or extracts it from PDFs, merges it, builds reports and charts, and refreshes them on a schedule. Reports and dashboards can be shared or embedded, and several people can edit at once.

Best for

Good for small business owners, marketers and finance or ops people who build the same reports every week from analytics, database or bank data. Not for analysts who need advanced statistics, large-scale modeling or Excel macros, and not for anyone who needs a guaranteed multi-year product roadmap given the Superhuman change.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Describing a report in plain words removes the formula barrier for non-technical staff.
  • Connectors to 50+ sources mean fewer manual CSV exports.
  • Scheduled refresh suits weekly or monthly reporting routines.
  • Plus tier is inexpensive per seat, and annual billing lowers it to $6.

Cons

  • The banner says Rows joined Superhuman but gives no detail; pricing, features or availability could change.
  • Pro charges a $79 monthly base ($59 annual) before seats, which is steep for tiny teams.
  • Plan-level usage limits (AI credits, rows, refresh frequency) were not visible on the pages checked.
  • It is not a drop-in replacement for Excel or Google Sheets for advanced modeling.

Frequently asked questions

Is Rows still operating after joining Superhuman?

On 2026-10-29 the site was live with working pricing and a banner saying Rows joined Superhuman. It did not describe future plans, so check with support@rows.com before committing long-term.

How much does Rows cost?

Free; Plus $8/user/month ($6 annual); Pro $79/month plus $8/user ($59 plus $6/user annual); Enterprise by contacting sales. Prices exclude tax.

Do I need to know formulas or SQL?

No, the product is built around plain-language requests, though you can still work in a spreadsheet grid.

Can I get data from my tools automatically?

Yes, it lists 50+ sources including databases, analytics tools and bank accounts, with scheduled refresh. Check that your specific source is on the list before buying.
