LogicMonitor is a good fit for IT operations teams running mixed on-prem and cloud infrastructure who want broad monitoring and AI alert reduction in one product; small single-cloud teams or those mainly needing code-level application tracing should look at lighter or more APM-focused tools. It is a SaaS observability platform covering infrastructure, cloud, logs, internet and digital experience monitoring, with an AI layer called Edwin AI for event intelligence and automated remediation. Pricing is based on Hybrid Units across Essentials, Advanced and Signature + Edwin AI packages.

Best for

LogicMonitor is for IT operations, NOC and infrastructure teams at mid-size and large organizations with hybrid or multi-cloud environments. It is not for small teams with a simple single-cloud setup, nor for developers who mainly want deep code-level tracing, nor for buyers wanting fixed all-in pricing without a quote.

Key features

01

Infrastructure observability

Monitoring for networks, servers, VMs, databases, storage and configuration.

02

Cloud observability

Containers plus AWS, Google Cloud, Azure and Oracle Cloud, with SaaS monitoring and cost optimization available.

03

Edwin AI

AI layer with Event Intelligence (alert-noise reduction), AI Automation for remediation playbooks, AI agents and an ITOps context graph.

04

Log management

Centralizes logs and correlates them with metrics.

05

Internet and digital experience monitoring

CDN, DNS and BGP monitoring plus real user, synthetic, endpoint and website monitoring.

06

Integrations

The site cites more than 3,000 integrations.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Covers infrastructure, cloud, logs and internet monitoring in one platform, reducing tool sprawl.
  • Edwin AI targets alert noise, with vendor-reported 88% noise reduction (unverified by us).
  • Starting prices are published per Hybrid Unit, which is rarer than pure sales-only pricing.
  • Free trial lets you test before a contract.
  • Large integration library cuts onboarding work.

Cons

  • Listed prices are starting figures; the final price needs a quote and depends on volume and term.
  • A Hybrid Unit mixes device, IaaS, PaaS, access point and OpenTelemetry volume, so estimating cost takes work.
  • Add-ons such as AI agents, synthetics, RUM and cost optimization have no listed price and digital experience is not in any package by default.
  • Essentials is capped at 999 Hybrid Units, pushing larger estates to higher tiers.
  • The site gives two different trial lengths (15 and 14 days).

Frequently asked questions

How is LogicMonitor priced?

Per Hybrid Unit per month. Starting list prices are $16 (Essentials), $27 (Advanced) and $53 (Signature + Edwin AI); final pricing needs a quote.

What is a Hybrid Unit?

Per the pricing page: one on-prem collector-monitored device, one cloud IaaS resource, seven cloud PaaS resources, five wireless access points, or 100 GB of OpenTelemetry metrics.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. The pricing page says 15 days on any plan; another banner says 14 days of full platform access.

Is Edwin AI included?

Edwin AI Event Intelligence is included in Signature. Edwin AI agents and automation are listed as add-ons without prices.

Does it replace APM tools?

It lists APM among its capabilities, but confirm depth of tracing in a trial if code-level APM is your main need.
