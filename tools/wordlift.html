WordLift is an Italian company (WordLift srl, based in Rome) that started as a WordPress plugin for automated schema and structured data, and has since grown into a full AI-powered SEO platform built around Knowledge Graphs, a durable, brand-owned semantic layer connecting a business's entities, products, and concepts. It works by analyzing a site's content, detecting key entities (people, places, products, events), linking them into a personalized knowledge graph, and automatically generating schema.org markup so that both traditional search engines and newer AI systems can understand, retrieve, and cite the content more accurately.

By 2026, WordLift explicitly frames itself as infrastructure for the transition into "AI Mode" search, helping businesses move from classic search and content workflows to a paradigm where AI-driven discovery and automation play a bigger role. Its product line is organized into four solutions: Visibility (knowledge graph and structured data), Product Performance (AI content generation and journey optimization for e-commerce), Agent (WordLift Agent and Customer Agent for brand-customer interactions), and Intelligence (business intelligence and integrations). WordLift emphasizes a human-led methodology and client data ownership rather than black-box automation, with deep vertical expertise in e-commerce, news/media, travel, and legal. On G2 it holds a 4.7/5 rating from 41 reviews spanning 2019 to 2026, with support quality scoring an especially high 9.4/10, while cost is the most consistently repeated criticism.

Best for

WordLift is best suited to content-heavy publishers, e-commerce catalogs, and enterprise or agency SEO teams with the budget and technical appetite to build out a genuine knowledge graph and want to improve both classic search rankings and visibility in AI-generated answers. It's a weaker fit for solo bloggers, very small businesses, or anyone wanting a quick, low-cost schema plugin, since current published pricing starts at $879/month and the platform rewards sustained investment in entity and content strategy rather than a plug-and-play setup.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • G2 reviewers repeatedly credit WordLift with measurable ranking and traffic gains — one small-business reviewer reported organic traffic tripling within months, another reported ranked keywords doubling over six months.
  • Support quality scores especially high on G2 (9.4/10), with several reviews specifically calling out a responsive, knowledgeable team across WordLift's India, Spain, and Italy offices.
  • As one of the earliest dedicated knowledge-graph and entity SEO tools, with reviews dating back to 2019-2020, WordLift has a long track record and deep specialization in semantic SEO versus general-purpose SEO suites that added structured data as a side feature.
  • The WordPress plugin integration is described by multiple reviewers as easy to install with real-time entity/tag suggestions while writing, lowering the day-to-day effort of maintaining structured data.
  • Enterprise customers, including EssilorLuxottica per one G2 review, cite a high level of customization and dedicated support for large-scale content and e-commerce catalogs.
  • The 2026 product roadmap adds AI citation monitoring and "AI Mode" readiness, positioning it ahead of competitors still focused solely on classic Google rankings.

Cons

  • Cost is the single most repeated complaint on G2 — multiple reviewers explicitly call it expensive for smaller companies, with the current published Business+ tier starting at $879-$1,100/month.
  • There's a real learning curve: several reviewers note that building a knowledge graph properly requires understanding both content strategy and semantic/entity concepts, which isn't a set-and-forget setup.
  • WordLift's plugin works best natively with WordPress; a few reviewers note that using it on non-WordPress platforms is less intuitive, and one lower-rated (2-star) review described ongoing bugs and slow support responsiveness, though WordLift's team responded publicly to address it.
  • The current published pricing shows only two tiers (Business+ and Enterprise custom), both aimed at businesses with meaningful budgets and URL volume, with no visible low-cost entry plan for hobbyists or very small sites.
  • Smart Credits for AI features (Q&A generation, product descriptions, agent interactions) expire monthly or yearly depending on billing cycle, so unused capacity doesn't roll over.

Frequently asked questions

What is a WordLift knowledge graph?

It's a structured, brand-owned network connecting your content's key entities (people, products, places, concepts) so search engines and AI systems can understand and retrieve your content more accurately.

Does WordLift only work with WordPress?

No, though its plugin integration is most mature on WordPress; WordLift also supports Drupal, Shopify, Magento, and other platforms via its cloud and API-based tools.

How much does WordLift cost?

The current published plan is Business+ at $879/month billed yearly ($1,100/month billed monthly), including 2,500 URLs and 5 knowledge graphs; Enterprise pricing is custom for larger, more complex deployments.

What are Smart Credits?

Usage-based tokens included with each plan that are spent on AI features like Q&A generation, product description writing, internal link suggestions, and WordLift Agent interactions; extra credits can be purchased.

Does WordLift help with visibility in ChatGPT and other AI assistants, not just Google?

Yes, its 2026 product line includes AI Search and SEO research tools and AI citation/visibility tracking aimed specifically at how brands appear in AI-generated answers, not just traditional search results.

Is there a free trial?

WordLift's current pricing page requires requesting a discovery session rather than offering self-serve signup or a listed free trial, so prospective customers go through a sales conversation first.
