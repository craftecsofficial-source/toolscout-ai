WordLift is a strong fit for content-heavy publishers, large e-commerce catalogs and enterprise SEO teams that want structured data and entities handled as a long-term programme; solo bloggers and small businesses should skip it on price alone. It analyses your pages, links the people, products and concepts it finds into a knowledge graph you own, and generates schema markup from that graph so search engines and AI assistants can read your content more reliably.

The company, which began as a WordPress plugin, now groups its offer into four areas: visibility (graph and markup), product performance (content generation and page optimisation for e-commerce), agents (a WordLift Agent and a Customer Agent) and intelligence (analytics and training). Its marketing makes large traffic claims and cites case studies; these are the vendor's own figures and we have not independently confirmed them. Pricing is sales-led with one published tier.

Best for

Good for: enterprise and agency SEO teams, news and media publishers, and e-commerce sites with large catalogs that want durable, machine-readable brand data and can budget for a sustained project. Not for: solo bloggers, small businesses, or anyone wanting a cheap one-click schema plugin or a self-serve trial.

Key features

01

Dynamic Knowledge Graph

Builds and maintains a brand-specific knowledge graph connecting entities, products, and concepts across a website's content.

02

Automated Schema Markup

Generates and manages structured data (FAQ, Product, Article, and more) at scale, reducing manual coding of schema.org markup.

03

WordLift Agent

An AI agent for content generation and Q&A that operates grounded in the site's knowledge graph rather than generic prompting.

04

AI Search & Visibility Tracking

Monitors AI citation and mention performance across AI Mode search and chatbot answers, not just traditional Google rankings.

05

Content Optimization & Audits

Identifies content gaps and recommends improvements tied to the entities a page should be covering.

06

Internal Linking Automation

Suggests and helps insert contextual internal links based on entity relationships within the knowledge graph.

07

CMS Integrations

A native WordPress plugin plus support for Drupal, Shopify, Magento, and other platforms via cloud and API-based tools.

08

Smart Credits System

Usage-based credits included with each plan, spent on AI features like Q&A generation, product descriptions, and Agent interactions, with extra credits purchasable.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Focused on entity and structured-data SEO, so the knowledge graph and markup are the core product rather than an add-on.
  • The graph is described as brand-owned data, which matters if you want to keep it outside any one vendor's black box.
  • Business+ includes a dedicated project manager and strategy sessions, useful because the setup is not trivial.
  • Free AI visibility audit and structured data audit tools let you gauge your gaps before talking to sales.
  • Includes AI-search work (visibility research, citation focus), not only classic Google rankings.
  • A free developer Resolve API tier (1,000 requests/month) allows technical teams to experiment without a contract.

Cons

  • Cheapest published paid plan is $879/month on an annual commitment, or $1,100 month-to-month; there is no low-cost entry tier.
  • No free trial is listed for Business+; you start with a discovery session.
  • Business+ caps at 2,500 URLs and 5 knowledge graphs; larger sites must move to custom Enterprise pricing.
  • Smart Credits expire at the end of the month (monthly plans) or subscription year (yearly plans), and buying more in the dashboard was listed as coming soon, otherwise via an account manager.
  • Getting value requires content and entity strategy work; it is not a set-and-forget plugin.
  • Website had visible staging leftovers (placeholder notification text, local-domain privacy links) when checked, a minor polish concern.

Frequently asked questions

How much does WordLift cost?

Business+ is $879/month billed yearly or $1,100/month billed monthly, per its pricing page on 2026-10-23. Enterprise is quoted on request.

Is there a free trial or free plan?

No trial is listed for the paid plans. There are free audit tools and a free-tier developer Resolve API (1,000 requests/month) in public beta.

What are Smart Credits?

Usage credits for AI tasks. One credit covers one of: 25 Q&A pairs, 20 short product descriptions, 60 internal link suggestions or 10 Agent interactions. They expire at period end.

What are the usage limits on Business+?

2,500 URLs and 5 knowledge graphs. Beyond that you need Enterprise.

How does it compare with Semrush or Surfer?

Those are general SEO and content tools. WordLift is narrower and deeper on knowledge graphs and schema, and much pricier than Surfer's entry plans.
