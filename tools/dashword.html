Dashword is an AI-powered SEO content optimization platform built for content marketing teams who need to plan, write, and maintain search-optimized content at scale. Its core workflow starts with a content brief: Dashword analyzes top-performing pages for a target keyword and uses AI to generate a brief that defines the tone, structure, and specific topics a piece of content should cover, similar in spirit to competitors like Clearscope, Surfer SEO, and MarketMuse. Writers then draft inside Dashword's real-time content editor, which surfaces relevant term suggestions, a live content score, and readability feedback as they type — turning what is typically a post-hoc SEO audit into an in-the-moment feedback loop.

What sets Dashword apart in 2026 is its developer-facing positioning: the company has made its API and a native MCP (Model Context Protocol) server available on every plan, including free accounts, explicitly marketing to teams that want to connect Claude, Cursor, or custom AI agents directly to Dashword's brief-building and scoring functions — agents can create a report, read the brief, and score a draft programmatically, with scoring itself unlimited and free (only full reports consume paid credits). Beyond initial optimization, Dashword also offers content monitoring: once a piece is published, Dashword tracks its keyword performance with automated weekly reports and flags underperforming pages that need refreshing, closing the loop between writing and long-term content maintenance. Pricing is straightforward and public (a rarity in this category), starting at $99/month for small teams and scaling to $349+/month for larger content operations needing SSO and bulk report creation.

Best for

Dashword suits SEO-focused content teams and agencies that want AI-assisted briefs, real-time draft scoring, and ongoing content monitoring, especially teams already building AI-agent workflows who value its free API/MCP access. It's a weaker fit for buyers who need extensive verified third-party review data before purchasing, or very large enterprises needing a mid-tier plan between 30 and 100 reports/month.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Dashword includes full API and MCP server access on every plan, including free accounts, with unlimited free content scoring — a notably more open approach than competitors that reserve API access for top-tier plans.
  • Pricing is fully public and transparent on the website, with a free first report and no credit card required to start evaluating the tool.
  • Content monitoring proactively tracks published pages weekly and flags underperformers, extending value beyond a one-time pre-publish optimization check.
  • The real-time editor with live scoring lets writers correct course during drafting rather than after submitting a finished piece for review.
  • MCP support specifically targets teams already using AI coding/agent tools like Claude and Cursor, letting them build briefs and score drafts programmatically as part of automated content pipelines.

Cons

  • No independently verifiable third-party review platform data (G2 shows the product as not yet reviewed; a Capterra listing was not confirmed) was found at the time of this research, so real-world user sentiment can't be quoted from those sources.
  • The Startup plan's 30-report monthly cap can be restrictive for larger content teams or agencies managing many clients simultaneously.
  • There's a large price jump between the Startup ($99/mo) and Business (from $349/mo) tiers, without an evident mid-tier option for growing teams.
  • As a smaller/newer entrant compared to Surfer SEO or Clearscope, it has less publicly available comparative case-study data on ranking outcomes.

Frequently asked questions

What does Dashword do?

Dashword is an SEO content optimization tool that generates content briefs, scores drafts in real time as you write, and monitors published content performance to flag pages that need updating.

How much does Dashword cost?

The Startup plan is $99/month for 30 content reports and 5 seats; the Business plan starts at $349/month for 100 reports, 10 seats, and SSO, with a 20% discount for annual billing.

Does Dashword have a free plan or trial?

Yes, new users get a free first content report with no credit card required, and content scoring itself is free and unlimited on every plan.

Does Dashword offer an API or MCP server?

Yes, both the API and a native MCP server (for Claude, Cursor, and custom agents) are included on every plan, including free accounts, with unlimited free scoring.

What is content monitoring in Dashword?

It's an automated weekly reporting feature that tracks published content's keyword performance and flags underperforming pages that may need to be refreshed or re-optimized.

Is Dashword good for agencies?

Yes, the Business plan adds bulk report creation, 10 user seats, and SSO, which are aimed at agencies and larger teams managing content across many pages or clients.
