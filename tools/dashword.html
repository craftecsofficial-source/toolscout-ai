Dashword is a good fit for small SEO content teams and agencies that want briefs and draft scoring at a clear flat price, especially if they plan to wire it into AI agents. Skip it if you need a free ongoing plan, a mid-sized tier, or a broad all-in-one SEO suite. It researches the pages ranking for a keyphrase and compiles a brief with competitor outlines, keywords and FAQs, then scores your draft in an editor until it is ready to publish. After publishing, it tracks traffic and keyword rankings with weekly reports and can import pages with a crawler. An API, an MCP server (for Claude, Codex, Cursor, OpenCode) and a Google Docs add-on are available.

Best for

Good for SEO content teams, freelancers and agencies producing roughly 30 to 100 optimized pieces a month, and for teams building agent workflows around content research. Not for people who want a permanent free plan (you get one free report), teams needing something between 30 and 100 reports, or anyone who wants backlink, site audit or paid-ads tools in the same product.

Key features

01

Content briefs

Researches pages ranking for a keyphrase and compiles competitor outlines, keywords and FAQs into a brief.

02

Draft scoring editor

Scores your draft as you write until it is ready to publish. Scoring is free and unlimited on every plan; only reports use credits.

03

AI Writer

Drafts content with AI; Startup has a 100k-word limit and Business lists no stated limit.

04

Content monitoring

Tracks traffic and rankings after publishing, sends weekly keyword reports and imports pages through a crawler.

05

Bulk report creation

Business plan only; create many reports at once.

06

API and MCP server

Available on every account with a verified email, letting agents research a keyphrase and score drafts.

07

Google Docs add-on

Use the optimization workflow inside Google Docs.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • API and MCP server access is included on every plan, and scoring costs nothing, so you can automate scoring without a top-tier plan.
  • Pricing is public, there is no minimum contract and you can cancel anytime.
  • It tracks published pages with weekly keyword reports, so it helps with refreshes and not only first drafts.
  • Per-report credits are easy to budget: 30 on Startup, 100 on Business.

Cons

  • There is no ongoing free plan, just one free report, so you cannot test at scale before paying.
  • Startup is capped at 30 reports a month, which agencies will outgrow quickly.
  • Business starts at $349, over three times Startup, with nothing in between.
  • After cancelling you keep your data but cannot generate new keyword reports.
  • Annual billing is described as 20% off but exact annual prices are not shown on the pricing page.
  • We found no verified third-party review ratings, so real-world quality compared with larger rivals is unproven.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Dashword cost?

Startup is $99/month for 30 reports and 5 seats. Business starts at $349/month for 100 reports, 10 seats, bulk creation and SSO. Annual billing is 20% off.

Is there a free plan?

Not a permanent one. New accounts get one free report with no credit card. Draft scoring is free and unlimited on all plans.

Do I need a top plan for API or MCP access?

No. API access is open to any account with a verified email and the MCP server is on every plan.

What happens if I cancel?

No minimum contract. You keep access to your data but cannot generate new keyword reports.

How does it compare with Surfer SEO?

Both build briefs and score drafts. Dashword is simpler with two plans and per-report credits; Surfer is a wider suite. Compare current plans before choosing.
