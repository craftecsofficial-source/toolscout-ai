MakeShorts.ai is a cheap, simple option for podcasters and YouTubers who want shorts cut automatically from long talking-head YouTube videos; skip it if your source is not on YouTube, if you need a timeline editor, or if you need heavy branding control. You paste a YouTube link and it picks engaging moments, reframes them to vertical 9:16 with face-tracking, adds captions and writes descriptions and hashtags. Plans are priced by credits/upload minutes of source video rather than by number of shorts. Details were checked on the homepage on 2026-10-29; the separate pricing page could not be fetched, so limits beyond those shown on the homepage (storage days, upload sizes) are carried over from earlier research and unverified.

Best for

Good for podcasters, interviewers and educators repurposing YouTube videos. Not for people with raw files that are not on YouTube, for editors wanting manual control, or for B-roll-heavy or music-driven footage.

Key features

01

AI Clipping

Automatically identifies the most engaging, self-contained segments of a long video and turns them into standalone short clips.

02

Autofocus Cropping

Uses face-detection to convert landscape footage into vertical 9:16 video while keeping the speaker centered in frame.

03

AI Subtitling

Fast, accurate AI transcription burns styled captions directly onto each generated short for sound-off viewing.

04

AI Social Captions & Hashtags

Auto-generates titles, descriptions, and relevant hashtags for each clip to speed up publishing across platforms.

05

YouTube Link Import

Users paste a YouTube URL rather than uploading a file; MakeShorts pulls and processes the video directly.

06

Multi-language Support

Transcription and captioning cover 30+ languages and regional dialects, including English, Spanish, Portuguese, Hindi, and more.

07

Flexible Source Video Limits

Plans scale from 15-minute source videos on Free up to 4-hour videos on Pro, with matching upload size caps.

08

Tiered Clip Retention

Generated clips are stored for a plan-dependent window ranging from 3 days (Free) to 60 days (Pro) before expiring.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Paste a YouTube link and get captioned vertical clips with little manual work.
  • A Free plan (30 credits) lets you judge clip quality first, though exports are watermarked.
  • Face-detection autofocus keeps the speaker framed when converting landscape to 9:16.
  • Paid annual prices are low compared with the listed prices of $12, $39 and $79.
  • Captioning across 30+ languages was claimed in earlier research (not re-verified today).

Cons

  • YouTube links only; the homepage says direct uploads are not currently supported.
  • No timeline editor or brand kit based on available information, so you take the AI's cuts.
  • Quoted paid prices are annual-billing discounts against much higher list prices; check what renews after year one.
  • Free videos are capped at 15 minutes and carry a watermark.
  • The pricing page could not be fetched; credit-to-minute conversion and retention windows are unverified.

Frequently asked questions

How much does MakeShorts.ai cost?

Homepage today: Free $0; Lite $6/mo, Plus $9.75/mo, Pro $19.75/mo, billed annually at $72, $117 and $237 per year. Monthly-billing prices were not verified.

Is there a free plan and a watermark?

Yes: 30 credits, videos up to 15 minutes, watermarked output. Paid plans remove the watermark.

Can I upload my own video files?

Not currently. The homepage says only YouTube links are supported.

How does it compare with Wisecut?

MakeShorts is link-based and focused on picking viral moments from YouTube videos; Wisecut also handles uploads and offers silence cutting and editing, so choose it if your footage is not on YouTube.
