BooleanMaths is a good fit for established Shopify D2C brands that spend real money on Meta and Google ads and want better tracking and attribution; skip it if you are not on Shopify or your order volume is too low for a $99+ monthly tool to pay back. Its pricing and trial terms also differ between its own site and the Shopify listing, so verify before you commit.

It sends server-side conversion events to Meta and Google, models attribution across channels, rebuilds journeys lost to third-party checkouts and COD, and shows a live P&L and creative-fatigue analysis. An MCP endpoint lets AI assistants such as Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini answer questions from that data.

Best for

Shopify D2C brands with meaningful monthly Meta and Google ad spend and enough orders that better attribution moves real money. Not for non-Shopify stores, very low-volume or pre-revenue shops, SaaS teams wanting product analytics, or brands already covered by a broader marketing suite.

Key features

01

Multi-touch attribution

Spreads credit across channels and touchpoints instead of giving it all to the last click.

02

Server-side CAPI

Sends conversion events from its servers to Meta and Google, reducing losses from pixel blocking.

03

Journey recovery

Reconnects sessions lost to third-party checkouts, COD and RTO flows, and cross-browser journeys.

04

BooleanVision

AI analysis of ad creative hooks and fatigue.

05

Live P&L

Margin, COGS, shipping, RTO and ad spend rolled into profitability views.

06

Post-purchase surveys and audiences

Self-reported attribution data and audience segments usable for Meta, Google and WhatsApp.

07

MCP integration

Lets Claude, Gemini and OpenAI tools query your data conversationally.

08

Marketing mix modeling

Spend-based modeling on Business tier and above.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • It targets Shopify-specific gaps (COD, RTO, third-party checkouts) that generic analytics tools handle poorly.
  • Server-side CAPI is a tracking investment that can feed better data back to Meta and Google independent of any dashboard.
  • MCP access means you can ask Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini about channel performance rather than learning another BI tool.
  • Prices are public, tiers follow order volume, and every plan has a free trial plus no setup fees.

Cons

  • It is Shopify-only; WooCommerce, BigCommerce or headless stores cannot use it.
  • Its own pricing page says 14-day trial and custom Enterprise; the Shopify listing says 7-day trial and $799/mo Enterprise. Get current terms in writing.
  • Claims such as +82% tracking visibility vs GA4 and +5.6% vs Triple Whale come from the vendor's own tests. Validate during your trial.
  • Ten Shopify reviews, all 5-star, is too thin to judge support or performance at scale.
  • Order-based tiers push growing stores into higher plans, so model cost against your growth. A promotional BFCM discount was shown on the site, so prices may be temporarily lower.

Frequently asked questions

How much does BooleanMaths cost?

Per its pricing page: Startup $99/mo, Pro $199/mo, Business $399/mo, Enterprise custom, scaled by monthly orders (3K, 7.5K, 18K, 36K+). Yearly billing saves about 25%. The Shopify listing shows Enterprise at $799/mo.

How long is the free trial?

Its pricing page says 14 days on all plans, while its Shopify App Store listing says 7 days. Confirm which applies when you install.

Does it work outside Shopify?

It is built for Shopify brands. We found no support for other platforms.

Is it worth it for a small store?

Probably only if ad spend is large enough that better attribution and CAPI signal would save real money. At $99+/mo it is hard to justify for low order volume.

Are the accuracy claims independent?

No. The uplift figures are the vendor's own case studies and tests. Compare against your own data during the trial.
