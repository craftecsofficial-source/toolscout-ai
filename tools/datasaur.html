Datasaur is a fit for regulated enterprises (healthcare, legal, finance, public sector) that want custom AI running on their own infrastructure and have a budget of at least $50K a year. Individuals, startups and small teams wanting cheap self-serve labeling or hosted AI should skip it. The company now describes itself as an AI lab building private AI on open-weights models, with products like Datasaur Chat, Health+, Legal+, Finance+ and Sovereign, while Data Studio, its older labeling product, is linked as a separate offering.

Best for

Compliance-heavy organizations wanting on-premises or air-gapped AI plus extraction, redaction and chat tools. Not for small teams, hobbyists or buyers who only want a low-cost annotation tool, and not for anyone who needs transparent self-serve pricing.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Everything is designed to run inside your own environment, with the vendor saying every byte stays on-premise.
  • Security badges listed: SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, GDPR.
  • Sovereign option covers bare-metal, air-gapped deployments.
  • Vertical products for health, legal and finance reduce build-from-scratch work.
  • Published starting prices are rare for custom enterprise AI work.

Cons

  • Entry price of $50K/yr rules out most small and mid-size teams.
  • Pricing page lists no usage limits, seats or deliverables per plan.
  • The labeling product's current plans and status are unclear from the site.
  • Security claims come from the vendor's site; we did not see audit reports.
  • We found no independent review source verified today.

Frequently asked questions

What does Datasaur do now?

It builds private, customized AI for regulated enterprises on open-weights models running on the customer's own infrastructure, and still lists Data Studio for data labeling.

How much does it cost?

Enterprise engagements start at $50K/year, $85K/year or $150K/year depending on scope, with Strategic Partnership custom. Data Studio pricing could not be verified.

Is it secure for healthcare or legal data?

The site shows SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA and GDPR badges and says data stays on the customer's servers. Request the audit reports to confirm.

Is Data Studio still available?

The homepage links to it as a separate product, but we couldn't load its page. Confirm with sales.

Can it run air-gapped?

Datasaur Sovereign is described as supporting bare-metal, air-gapped, zero-egress deployment.
