Presentations.AI is a strong fit for sales, marketing and customer-success teams that rebuild the same deck repeatedly and want figures kept current; skip it if you just need an occasional one-off deck, where its higher tiers add little. Its standout is Refresh, which updates a master deck from sources like Google Sheets, Salesforce or HubSpot, but the pricing page lists it only on the $100/month Gold plan. The base product turns a prompt, notes, document or URL into a branded deck exported as editable .pptx, with Brand Sync pulling logo, fonts and colors from a website. An API, webhooks and Zapier/Make support generation at scale. I could not verify the SOC 2, GDPR, investor or user-count claims beyond the company's own site.

Best for

Good for teams producing recurring decks (QBRs, proposals, board updates), agencies managing brands, and companies wanting to embed deck generation via API. Not for casual users needing a single deck, for those who need Refresh on a small budget, or for buyers who need independently verified compliance evidence before trusting the claims.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Refresh addresses stale decks, a real recurring pain that one-shot generators ignore.
  • API, webhooks and Zapier/Make make bulk or embedded generation practical.
  • Brand Sync gets on-brand output with minimal setup.
  • Free tier allows trying generation with 200 credits.
  • Pricing is published openly for the self-serve tiers.

Cons

  • Refresh costs $100/month on annual billing, a big jump from Pro at $20.
  • Starter is capped at 20 slides and Pro/Gold appear annual-only.
  • SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, 12 million customers and rival comparisons are company claims I could not independently verify.
  • The pricing page is inconsistent on free-tier export: the marketing page implies upgrade needed, while the table shows PowerPoint export on every plan.
  • Connecting CRMs and warehouses adds setup and admin work.

Frequently asked questions

What does it cost?

Starter is free with 200 credits; Pro is $20/month billed annually; Gold is $100/month billed annually. Enterprise pricing is not public.

Which plan has Refresh?

Per the pricing page, only Gold includes Presentation Refresh.

Do I get real PowerPoint files?

The site says decks export as editable .pptx; the pricing table lists PowerPoint export on all plans, though marketing wording on the free tier is unclear.

Is it secure for enterprise use?

The company states SOC 2 Type II and GDPR compliance. I could not verify certificates, so ask for documentation.

Can I use it through an API?

Yes, REST API, webhooks and Zapier/Make are advertised; API and white-label terms are contact-sales.
