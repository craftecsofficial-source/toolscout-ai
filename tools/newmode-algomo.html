NewMode, branded Algomo on its own site, suits B2B marketing teams that run paid campaigns and want their website to react to who is visiting; it is a poor fit for small sites, consumer businesses or anyone who wants a published price per feature. The platform tries to identify which company a visitor belongs to, changes the site's content for that account, and runs an AI agent that talks to the visitor and books meetings. It extends the same targeting outward by generating LinkedIn ad copy, images and video for target accounts and building personalized landing pages for outbound email sequences. The site says plans begin at $200/month with no per-seat charges, but the pricing page shows only two tiers, Growth and Enterprise, without individual prices, billing terms or usage limits, and pushes you to a sales call. Value depends heavily on traffic volume and how accurate the visitor identification is, neither of which we could verify from public material.

Best for

Good for B2B SaaS and services marketing teams already spending on LinkedIn or other paid channels who want inbound qualification tied to campaign context. Not for consumer sites, ecommerce checkouts, solo founders with little traffic, or buyers who need a transparent price list and independent proof before a demo.

Key features

01

Website deanonymization

Aims to match anonymous visits to accounts and the campaign that brought them. Accuracy and data sources are not documented on the pages we read.

02

Website personalization

Adapts headline, content, chat behavior and calls to action across the site for each identified account.

03

AI inbound agent

An AI SDR-style chat agent that uses visitor and campaign context, qualifies leads and books meetings.

04

LinkedIn ads

Generates ad copy, images and video for specific target accounts, listed on the Growth tier as 1:1 personalized ads, and syncs to LinkedIn.

05

Outbound prospecting pages

Builds personalized landing pages for prospects, designed to work with existing email sequencing tools.

06

Enterprise extras

Enterprise adds unlimited campaigns, custom integrations, a dedicated success manager, priority support and advanced analytics.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Covers several normally separate jobs (visitor identification, site personalization, chat, ad creative, outbound pages) in one place.
  • No per-seat pricing means adding colleagues should not raise the bill, according to the site.
  • The AI agent is given visitor and campaign context, so conversations are not generic FAQ answers.
  • A stated starting price of $200/month gives a rough budget floor.

Cons

  • The $200 figure is a starting point only; tier prices, billing terms and limits such as visitors or conversations are not published.
  • Identification-based features depend on traffic volume and data quality, which we could not verify.
  • Mostly useful for B2B with named target accounts; low-traffic sites will see little benefit.
  • The Algomo and NewMode naming is unexplained on the site, and no acquisition or rebrand notice was found.
  • No independent G2, Capterra or Trustpilot data was verified, so performance claims are the vendor's own.
