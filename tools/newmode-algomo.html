NewMode, operating under the product brand Algomo (newmode.ai), is a B2B go-to-market platform built around a single premise: most websites show every visitor the exact same generic page, wasting the targeting precision that companies pay for in ads, email, and SEO. Algomo's platform de-anonymizes website traffic — matching anonymous visits to specific accounts and the campaign or UTM context that brought them there — and then personalizes the entire website in real time for that visitor: headline, body content, chat behavior, and calls to action all adapt based on who's browsing and how they arrived.

Sitting on top of that identification and personalization layer is an AI Inbound Agent, functioning like an AI SDR: it knows who's on the site and which campaign drove the visit, has a relevant conversation grounded in that context rather than a generic FAQ script, and books meetings directly for the sales team. Algomo extends the same account-level personalization outward too — generating hyper-personalized LinkedIn ad copy, images, and video for target accounts with one-click sync to LinkedIn, and building personalized landing pages for outbound email sequences. The company's core pitch is that these pieces feed each other in a continuous loop (ads inform the website, the website informs the AI agent, the agent informs outreach) rather than operating as disconnected point solutions, positioning itself directly against buying five separate tools for deanonymization, personalization, chat, ad creative, and outbound.

Best for

NewMode (Algomo) suits B2B SaaS and enterprise marketing teams running meaningful paid-ad spend (particularly LinkedIn) who want their website, ad creative, and inbound qualification working from the same account-level data instead of operating as disconnected tools. It's a strong fit for teams practicing account-based marketing who are frustrated that their website shows the same generic page to every visitor regardless of source. It's a weak fit for B2C, ecommerce, or low-ACV transactional businesses where account-level deanonymization and personalization don't apply, and for teams that want transparent self-serve pricing without a sales call.

Key features

Website Deanonymization

Identifies anonymous website visitors and matches them to known accounts using UTM and campaign context, converting anonymous traffic into named-account intelligence.

02

Real-Time Website Personalization

Adapts headline, body content, chat behavior, and CTAs across the entire website — not just a single landing page — based on the identified visitor's account, role, and traffic source.

03

AI Inbound Agent

Acts as an AI SDR that knows visitor identity and campaign origin, engages in contextually relevant conversation rather than generic chat, qualifies leads, and books meetings directly for the sales team.

04

AI-Generated LinkedIn Ads

Generates personalized ad copy, images, and video for specific target accounts, with one-click publishing directly to LinkedIn.

05

Outbound Prospecting Pages

Builds hyper-personalized landing pages for individual prospects, designed to integrate with existing email sequencing tools for outbound campaigns.

06

Closed-Loop Channel Intelligence

Feeds data between channels continuously — ad context informs website personalization, website behavior informs the AI agent's conversation, and agent conversations inform further outreach — creating one connected system instead of siloed tools.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Consolidates five typically separate categories — visitor deanonymization, website personalization, AI chat/SDR, ad creative generation, and outbound landing pages — into one connected platform
  • The 'closed-loop' design (ads inform site, site informs agent, agent informs outreach) is architecturally distinct from point tools that don't share context with each other
  • Transparent flat pricing starting at $200/month with no per-seat fees is simpler to budget than usage- or seat-based competitors
  • One-click LinkedIn ad publishing with AI-generated creative reduces the manual work of building account-based ad campaigns
  • Published case studies referencing recognizable companies (HubSpot, Brex, Notion, as example use cases) suggest real enterprise-caliber usage, though these appear to be illustrative examples rather than confirmed customers

Cons

  • No self-serve pricing breakdown beyond the $200/month starting point — both tiers require booking a sales call rather than transparent per-feature pricing
  • No independent listings or ratings found on G2, Capterra, or Trustpilot to verify performance claims like the marketed '40% pipeline cost reduction'
  • Purpose-built for B2B account-based marketing with identifiable company traffic; not applicable to consumer, ecommerce, or low-intent transactional websites
  • Visitor deanonymization inherently depends on accurate company-level identification, which varies by traffic source, geography, and privacy regulation (GDPR compliance is addressed on the site but adds complexity for EU-facing teams)
  • As a platform this deeply integrated across ads, website, and CRM, switching away from it later likely requires more rework than swapping out a single point tool

Frequently asked questions

What does NewMode/Algomo actually do?

It identifies anonymous website visitors down to the account and campaign level, personalizes the entire website in real time for each visitor, deploys an AI agent to qualify and book meetings with contextual awareness of who's visiting, and extends the same personalization to LinkedIn ads and outbound landing pages.

How much does it cost?

Plans start at $200/month for the Growth tier, with a custom-quoted Enterprise tier for unlimited campaigns and dedicated support. There's no per-seat pricing, but exact costs require booking a call with sales.

Is this the same company as 'Algomo' the chatbot tool?

Yes — the product is branded 'Algomo' on its own marketing site (newmode.ai), while the underlying company/domain is registered as NewMode/newmode.ai; the two names are used interchangeably in its own materials and press.

Does it work with any website platform?

The company states it doesn't require developers to get started and is designed to work across common website platforms via a script-based integration, though platform-specific compatibility should be confirmed during a demo.

Is visitor deanonymization GDPR compliant?

The company addresses this directly in its FAQ, indicating GDPR compliance is a designed-for requirement, though specifics (data retention, opt-out mechanics) should be confirmed directly for regulated industries or EU-heavy traffic.

Can I try it before committing to a paid plan?

The site offers a 'build a live assistant for my site' self-serve entry point and demo bookings, though full platform access appears to be gated behind a sales conversation rather than a self-serve free trial.
