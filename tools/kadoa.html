Kadoa is a good choice for finance and data teams that want maintained web data feeds without running scraper infrastructure; teams wanting a cheap, DIY scraping library or published per-credit prices should look elsewhere. You describe the data you need and the Kadoa agent builds, runs and verifies the pipeline. The product is split into Monitors (alerts on filings, prices and site changes), Pipelines (maintained scrapers) and Datasets (custom data built from public web sources). The company says it has 30,000+ production pipelines with a 99%+ run success rate; this is its own claim, not independently verified.

Best for

Kadoa is for investment firms, analysts and data teams that need reliable, continuously updated data from public websites and are happy to pay for a managed service. It is not for hobbyists doing one-off scrapes, developers who want to self-host and pay nothing, or buyers who need published per-unit pricing before any conversation.

Key features

01

Pipelines

Automated scrapers that the platform builds and keeps working as target websites change.

02

Monitors

Alerts on filings, prices and changes to websites.

03

Datasets

Custom datasets built from public web data; the site advertises a free sample before you pay.

04

Verification

Runs check completeness, plausibility and schemas plus custom rules; unresolved issues escalate to human reviewers.

05

Delivery

Sends data to spreadsheets, Slack, warehouses (Snowflake, Databricks, BigQuery), S3, SFTP, webhooks, API or MCP.

06

Security and compliance

SOC 2 Type II, SSO/SAML, SCIM, audit logs, robots.txt checks and on-prem or private cloud options, per the site.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Scraper maintenance is handled by the platform, saving engineering time on broken selectors.
  • Each value links back to its source, which helps audit and trust.
  • Built-in run checks and human escalation add a safety net beyond pure automation.
  • Wide delivery options, including data warehouses and MCP, fit analyst and engineering workflows.
  • Free trial and free dataset sample let you test on your own use case.

Cons

  • No prices are shown for any plan, so you cannot budget without trying it or talking to sales.
  • The current positioning is finance-first; teams with unrelated scraping needs should confirm fit.
  • Reliability claims (99%+ success) are the vendor's own figures.
  • Sites with aggressive anti-bot protection or restrictive terms remain a risk for any scraping tool.
  • Enterprise features such as SSO, SCIM and all integrations sit in the sales-only tier.

Frequently asked questions

Does Kadoa have a free option?

The Starter plan includes a free trial and every plan has a no-commitment evaluation period. Dataset samples are also offered free. Ongoing free-tier limits are not stated.

How much does it cost?

The pricing page lists a consumption-based Starter plan and a sales-quoted Enterprise plan without numbers. We could not verify any dollar figures.

What happens when a website changes?

The platform is designed to build and maintain scrapers as sites change, and escalates unresolved issues to human reviewers.

Where can the data be delivered?

Excel, Google Sheets, Slack, Snowflake, Databricks, BigQuery, S3, SFTP, webhooks, API and MCP, per the site.

Is scraping with Kadoa legal?

It depends on the target site's terms and applicable law. Kadoa says it runs automated robots.txt checks, but you remain responsible for your use case.
