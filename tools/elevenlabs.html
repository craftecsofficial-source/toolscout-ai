ElevenLabs is an AI audio research and product company, founded in 2022 by Piotr Dabkowski and Mati Staniszewski, that builds text-to-speech, speech-to-text, voice cloning, dubbing and conversational voice-agent technology. It has grown from a text-to-speech novelty into one of the most widely used AI voice platforms, powering audiobook narration, dubbing, podcast production and increasingly enterprise voice agents for call centers and customer support.

Its core differentiator is breadth on a single platform: text-to-speech, voice cloning, dubbing, sound effects, music generation and now full conversational voice agents (ElevenAgents) with low-latency voice-to-voice interaction, rather than being a single-purpose narration tool like Murf or WellSaid Labs.

Best for

ElevenLabs is best suited for developers and technical teams building voice into products via API, content creators and studios producing audiobooks, dubbing or narration who want best-in-class voice realism, and companies building voice agents for support or sales. Teams that need only occasional, simple narration on a tight, predictable budget may find Murf AI or WellSaid Labs' flatter, minutes-based pricing easier to plan around.

Key features

01

Text to Speech

Converts written text into speech across 30+ languages using multiple model tiers so users can trade off latency, cost and expressiveness.

02

Instant & Professional Voice Cloning

Clone a voice from a short sample (Instant, Starter plan and up) or a larger, higher-fidelity dataset (Professional, Creator plan and up).

03

Dubbing Studio

Automatically translates and re-voices video or audio into other languages while preserving timing and the original speaker's vocal character.

04

Conversational AI / Voice Agents (ElevenAgents)

Build low-latency, voice-to-voice AI agents for customer support, phone calls and other real-time use cases.

05

Studio & Projects

A long-form editor for producing audiobooks, courses and multi-speaker productions with per-paragraph editing and pronunciation control.

06

Sound Effects & Music Generation

Generate short sound effects or full music tracks from text prompts for video, game and podcast production.

07

Voice Isolator & Voice Changer

Clean background noise out of existing recordings, or convert one voice's performance into a different AI voice while preserving delivery.

08

Developer API

A REST/websocket API with per-character credit billing, used to embed TTS, STT and agents directly into third-party products.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Widely regarded by reviewers and independent testers as having some of the most natural, emotionally expressive AI voices of any mainstream TTS platform
  • Single platform spans TTS, cloning, dubbing, sound effects, music and voice agents, reducing the need for multiple vendors
  • Developer-friendly API that reviewers report getting working in under 15 minutes
  • Broad language coverage (30+ languages) with dubbing that preserves timing and speaker tone
  • Free tier is genuinely usable for testing (10,000 credits/month) without a credit card
  • Startup Grants program offers free credits for eligible early-stage companies building voice agents

Cons

  • The credit-based pricing system is confusing to many users — reviewers say it's hard to predict costs or see per-feature spend before generating audio
  • Commercial use requires at minimum the paid Starter tier; the Free plan cannot be used commercially
  • Costs escalate quickly for heavy users — the Business plan runs $990/month before any enterprise negotiation
  • Professional Voice Cloning is gated behind the Creator plan and up, putting a marquee feature behind additional spend
  • Some reviewers report burning through credits quickly on longer-form or multi-take projects without clear per-generation cost visibility
  • Getting the exact intended tone or emotional inflection sometimes takes trial and error, particularly for longer narration

What reviewers say

ElevenLabs is consistently rated highly on both G2 and Capterra, with reviewers repeatedly citing voice realism, ease of setup and responsive support. The most consistent criticism across G2, Capterra and Reddit is pricing — the credit system makes it hard to predict costs, and per-feature spend isn't clearly surfaced, which frustrates users who burn through credits without warning. Reddit threads note it's considered the gold standard for high-end content creators and developers, but a real constraint for anyone on a tight budget.

Frequently asked questions

Does ElevenLabs have a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan gives 10,000 credits per month with no credit card required, but it does not include commercial usage rights — those start on the $6/mo Starter plan.

How much does ElevenLabs cost per month?

Paid plans run from $6/mo (Starter) up to $990/mo (Business), with a custom-priced Enterprise tier above that. Annual billing saves roughly 17% on every paid tier.

What's the difference between Instant and Professional voice cloning?

Instant Voice Cloning creates a synthetic voice from a short sample and is available from the Starter plan; Professional Voice Cloning uses a larger training dataset for a more accurate clone and requires the Creator plan or above.

Can I use ElevenLabs for commercial projects?

Only on paid plans. The Free plan is for evaluation only; a Commercial License is included starting with the $6/mo Starter plan.

Is ElevenLabs worth it compared to Murf or WellSaid Labs?

If voice realism, API access and multi-feature breadth (cloning, dubbing, agents) matter most, ElevenLabs is generally rated the strongest option. If predictable per-minute pricing and a simpler no-code studio matter more, Murf or WellSaid Labs may be easier to budget for.

What is ElevenAgents?

ElevenAgents is ElevenLabs' conversational AI product for building low-latency, voice-to-voice agents — for example, phone-based customer support bots — on top of the same voice models used for TTS.
