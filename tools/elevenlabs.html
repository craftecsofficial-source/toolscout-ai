ElevenLabs is a strong pick for creators, developers and companies who care most about natural-sounding voices and want cloning, dubbing or voice agents from one vendor. Skip it if you need occasional narration on a fixed, easy-to-predict budget, because everything is metered in credits and the cost per feature is not obvious up front.

It covers text to speech, speech to text, voice cloning, dubbing, sound effects, music and conversational agents, with a developer API. Plans are monthly credit allowances (roughly 1,000 credits per minute of speech on the plans listed). Commercial use starts on Starter, Instant cloning on Starter, and Professional cloning on Creator.

Best for

Good for: audiobook and video creators, podcasters, developers embedding voice via API, and teams building voice agents who value realism. Not for: anyone who needs commercial rights on the free plan, people on a tight budget with long-form daily output (credits go fast), or users who want flat per-minute pricing and a simple no-code studio, who may prefer Murf or WellSaid.

Key features

01

Text to Speech

Turns text into speech in 30+ languages with several model tiers that trade latency, cost and expressiveness.

02

Instant and Professional Voice Cloning

Instant cloning from a short sample is included from Starter; Professional cloning from a larger dataset starts on Creator.

03

Dubbing Studio

Translates and re-voices video or audio into other languages. Included from Starter.

04

ElevenAgents

Builds low-latency, voice-to-voice conversational agents for support and phone use.

05

Studio projects

Long-form editor for audiobooks and multi-speaker work: 3 projects on Free, 20 on Starter.

06

Sound effects, music and voice design

Generate effects, music and new voices from prompts. Music commercial use is listed from Starter.

07

Speech to Text

Transcription included across plans, including Free.

08

Developer API

REST and websocket API billed per character; the API pricing page lists $0.04 to $0.08 per 1K characters depending on model.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Voice realism and emotional range are widely praised in reviews and independent tests
  • One vendor covers TTS, STT, cloning, dubbing, music and voice agents
  • Free plan is real enough to evaluate the voices before paying
  • Commercial license and Instant cloning start at only $6/mo
  • Rising plans lift concurrency (2 on Free up to 25 on Business), which matters for API and agent use
  • Enterprise tier offers HIPAA BAAs, SSO and SLAs

Cons

  • Credits are consumed per generation and per feature, so projected cost is hard to estimate; re-takes burn credits
  • Starter and Creator headline prices are introductory first-month offers; the standard rates are $6 and $22
  • Free plan cannot be used commercially
  • Professional Voice Cloning needs Creator or above, and only 3 clones on Scale
  • Large jumps between tiers: $99, $299, $990 per month
  • Getting the exact tone sometimes needs multiple regenerations

Frequently asked questions

Does ElevenLabs have a free plan?

Yes: 10,000 credits a month (about 10 minutes of speech) with no card, but no commercial license. Commercial use starts on Starter.

How much does ElevenLabs cost?

Starter $6/mo (first month $1), Creator $22/mo (first month $11), Pro $99, Scale $299, Business $990, plus custom Enterprise. Prices exclude taxes. Check the official page for annual rates, which I could not read.

Which plan do I need for voice cloning?

Instant Voice Cloning is on Starter and up. Professional Voice Cloning requires Creator or above.

Can I use the output commercially?

Only on paid plans; Starter and above include a commercial license.

How does it compare with Murf or WellSaid Labs?

ElevenLabs is usually rated stronger for realism, API and breadth; Murf and WellSaid are simpler studios that can be easier to budget. Try the free plan against your own script.
