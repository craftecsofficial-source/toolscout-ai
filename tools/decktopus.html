Decktopus is a good pick for solo professionals who want a quick first-draft deck plus a way to practise delivering it; skip it if you need pixel-level design control, a free tool you can use indefinitely, or heavily data-driven decks. You give it a topic, notes or a file, and it writes and lays out the whole presentation. You then refine it by typing instructions or editing directly.

Beyond generation, it adds three things. Loop is a rehearsal feature with four AI coaches named Profiler, Architect, Red Team and Mirror. Shared decks live as links that update when you change the deck, show viewer activity, and can require an email address before viewing. Beautify restyles an existing deck without touching its content. It also makes LinkedIn and Instagram carousels, applies a Brand Kit, and exports to PDF, PPT and PNG.

Cost works on AI credits: a slide costs about 5 credits, so Pro's 750 monthly credits is roughly 150 slides. The homepage says new users get free credits for a 3-slide presentation, and credits can also be topped up without a subscription. Always check the credit cost of regenerations before committing to a plan.

Best for

Good for freelancers, consultants, coaches, educators and founders who repeatedly build proposal, pitch or training decks and want to rehearse them and see who opens them. Not for people who need a permanently free tool, designers who want precise layout control, teams that need auto-updating data-driven reporting decks, or anyone who must work heavily inside Google Slides or native PowerPoint add-ins.

Key features

01

Prompt-to-deck generation

Start from a topic, notes or an uploaded file and get a full deck with copy and layout. Edit by typing what you want changed.

02

Beautify

Restyles an existing presentation while keeping your content.

03

Loop rehearsal coach

Four AI coaches (Profiler, Architect, Red Team, Mirror) give feedback on your delivery and practise audience pushback with you.

04

Trackable sharing

Share links update when the deck changes, show viewer activity, and can collect an email before viewing.

05

Carousels and Brand Kit

Make LinkedIn/Instagram carousels and apply your colors, fonts and logo across decks.

06

Exports and assistants

Download as PDF, PPT or PNG. A ChatGPT app and a remote MCP server let AI assistants build decks.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • You get a complete first draft (outline, wording and layout), which removes the blank-page problem for proposals and summaries.
  • Loop gives structured rehearsal feedback, which most slide generators do not offer at all.
  • Share links stay in sync with the deck and show who viewed it; the optional email gate makes decks usable for lead capture.
  • PPT and PDF export means you can hand the deck off to a normal PowerPoint workflow.
  • Annual Pro at $9.99/mo is inexpensive, and students get 50% off with a code.

Cons

  • There is no lasting free plan. The free credits only cover about a 3-slide deck, so you cannot properly evaluate it without paying.
  • Credits drain per slide generated (about 5 each), so lots of regenerating and editing on Pro's 750 credits will run out before month-end.
  • The $9.99 rate requires annual billing; month-to-month is $24.99.
  • Business pricing is not published; you must go through sales or the pricing FAQ to learn it.
  • Layout control is limited next to Canva or PowerPoint, and AI-written content needs fact-checking before important talks.

Frequently asked questions

Does Decktopus have a free plan?

Not a standing one. New users get a small amount of free credits, enough for roughly a 3-slide presentation; after that you need a Pro subscription or a credit top-up.

How much does Decktopus Pro cost?

$9.99/mo billed annually ($119.88/year), $19.99/mo billed every 3 months, or $24.99/mo monthly. Pro includes 750 AI credits per month, about 150 slides.

What happens when I run out of AI credits?

Generation and AI edits stop until credits renew or you top up. The homepage says credits can be topped up without a subscription; check the pricing page for current top-up rates.

Can I get my deck into PowerPoint?

Yes, Decktopus exports to PPT, PDF and PNG, though complex layouts may need tidying after export.

What is Loop?

A rehearsal feature with four AI coaches (Profiler, Architect, Red Team, Mirror) that give feedback on your delivery and practise audience questions. It is included with Pro.
