Slidebean is a strong, inexpensive choice for founders building a first investor pitch deck; skip it if you need a general-purpose presentation tool or want to import and edit a deck you already made, since uploading existing decks is not supported. It is built around pitch decks and the fundraising workflow rather than all-purpose slides.

You write content and the design engine lays out the slides. The AI Pitch Deck Builder has a beta generator that drafts a deck from your company website or a short prompt, and paid plans include unlimited AI deck reviews, 100+ templates, view tracking on shared decks, a cap table template and a financial modeling bootcamp.

Separately, Slidebean runs an agency side (pitch deck design, presentation design, financial modeling and fundraising services) for which no prices are listed publicly. The Accelerate tier is a software-plus-advice bundle with a CEO strategy call, Investor Finder and CRM, and two 30-minute expert sessions a month.

Best for

Early-stage founders building a first fundraising deck who want a fast design engine, investor-focused extras, and optional coaching. Not for: teams needing everyday sales or internal presentations, people who want to import and polish existing PowerPoint files, or anyone wanting deep manual design control.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Free plan has no time limit, so you can draft a full deck before paying
  • Starter at $7/month billed yearly undercuts most dedicated deck tools
  • Pitch-specific extras (cap table template, financial modeling bootcamp, view tracking) go beyond slide layout
  • Accelerate gives real human advice and an Investor Finder for founders who want it

Cons

  • You cannot upload an existing deck or document in another format to edit it, so migrating is manual
  • On the free plan, sharing, downloading and branding are locked
  • Starter's price is quoted for yearly billing; a monthly price was not shown
  • Accelerate costs $99/month and has a limited amount of expert time per month
  • Pricing page gave inconsistent annual discounts (up to 20% in the header, 35% on toggles)
  • Agency services are unpriced publicly, so budgeting needs a sales conversation

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The free plan needs no credit card and is not time-limited, but sharing, downloading and brand customization require a paid plan.

How much does it cost?

Starter is $7/month billed yearly. Accelerate is $99/month billed monthly. Check the pricing page for current monthly-versus-annual figures.

Can I import my existing deck?

No. The pricing page states that uploading existing decks or documents in other formats for editing is not supported.

What extra do I get with Accelerate?

A CEO strategy call, Investor Finder and CRM, a financial forecast tool, and each month a 30-minute deck writing session and a 30-minute financial modeling session.

Does Slidebean design decks for me?

Yes, through its agency services, but prices are not published; you need to contact the team.
