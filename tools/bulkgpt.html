BulkGPT, built by ISOTOPE LLC, is a no-code AI workflow automation platform aimed at SEO teams, marketers, sales/growth operators, and researchers who need to process large volumes of data through AI rather than working prompt-by-prompt in ChatGPT. Instead of manually copy-pasting rows into a chat window, users import a CSV, JSON file, or Google Sheet, then visually chain together steps — Google or Bing search, web scraping, and one or more LLM generation calls — into a repeatable workflow that runs across thousands of rows simultaneously. The platform reports processing over 100 million records and executing more than 1 million workflows for its user base, positioning itself around use cases like SEO content and metadata generation, lead enrichment, competitor analysis, and bulk research summarization.

BulkGPT differentiates itself from a plain "bulk ChatGPT" tool by combining data acquisition (search + scraping) with generation in a single pipeline, so a workflow can search for a product, scrape the resulting page, and generate SEO copy or a lead-enrichment summary in one automated run, exporting results to CSV, JSON, HTML, Markdown, or plain text. Usage is metered through a "Bulk Token" (BT) system, where different workflow steps (e.g., a GPT call, a web scrape, a LinkedIn scrape) consume different token amounts, and plans bundle a monthly BT allowance with overage pricing beyond that. The company maintains an active Discord community for support and continues to expand its library of free SEO utility tools (sitemap finders, XML-to-CSV converters) alongside the core workflow product.

Best for

BulkGPT is best suited for SEO agencies, in-house marketing teams, growth/sales operators, and researchers who need to run AI generation, search enrichment, or web scraping across thousands of rows of data at once. It is a poor fit for users who only need occasional single-prompt AI assistance, since the value comes specifically from high-volume, repeatable batch workflows.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • G2 reviewers rate BulkGPT 4.2 out of 5 across 24 reviews, with multiple 5-star reviews praising its ability to replace manual 'ExcelGPT' workflows for bulk prompt processing.
  • The free tier (200 tasks, no credit card) gives users a real way to test multi-step workflows before committing to a paid plan.
  • Reviewers highlight time savings from running thousands of prompts, scrapes, and searches in parallel instead of one at a time — one reviewer cited processing capability described as 'petabytes of data... in less than a few minutes.'
  • Combining web scraping, search, and generation in one workflow removes the need to stitch together separate scraping and AI tools manually.
  • Multiple G2 reviewers specifically praise its use for SEO tasks — generating meta descriptions, alt tags, and blog content in bulk with minimal setup.

Cons

  • Several G2 reviewers note a learning curve, describing the platform as requiring more upfront configuration than a single ChatGPT prompt, with one reviewer wanting a 'more descriptive' demo.
  • A few reviewers mention limited customization and customer support responsiveness, especially at lower-tier plans.
  • One reviewer described output as sometimes 'clunky' and weaker on nuanced or complex queries compared to using ChatGPT directly.
  • Pricing can become a concern at higher usage volumes, since overage is billed per Bulk Token beyond each plan's monthly allowance.

What reviewers say

BulkGPT holds a 4.2 out of 5 average on G2 from 24 reviews. Reviewers consistently praise the time saved on bulk/batch AI tasks and SEO content generation, while common criticisms cite a learning curve, occasional UI clunkiness, and limited customer support at scale.

Frequently asked questions

Is BulkGPT free to start?

Yes. BulkGPT offers 200 free tasks with no credit card required, letting users test workflows before upgrading to a paid plan.

Do I need to know how to code to use BulkGPT?

No, BulkGPT is built as a no-code visual workflow tool for chaining search, scraping, and AI generation steps.

What is a Bulk Token (BT)?

BT is BulkGPT's usage-metering unit; different workflow steps (like a GPT call, a web search, or a LinkedIn scrape) consume different amounts of BT per task.

What are BulkGPT's paid plans?

BulkGPT offers an Essential plan at $15/month (1,000 BT) and a Premium plan at $85/month (10,000 BT), each with overage pricing per additional BT.

Can BulkGPT scrape websites and enrich data with AI in one workflow?

Yes, BulkGPT is designed to combine website scraping, search results, and AI generation into a single automated workflow.
