This is a sensible pick for PowerPoint users who like SlideModel's business-style templates and want an AI-drafted starting point; skip it if you need Google Slides, Canva or PDF export today, or if you want a polished fully automated result. You enter a topic or upload a document, review an AI-suggested outline, choose a template and style, and get a draft with text, images and visuals.

The result is edited in the web tool and finished in PowerPoint. The FAQ says output is currently .pptx only, and that Google Slides, Canva and PDF are still in development, even though the page shows a Google Slides compatibility badge. Document upload is mentioned but the FAQ invites you to contact the company to see it, so check it works before relying on it.

I could not verify the free-tier limits or paid pricing: the AI page and the pricing-related page I fetched showed no amounts.

Best for

Good for consultants, analysts and corporate teams who work in PowerPoint and want a template-backed draft. Not for people who need Google Slides, Canva or PDF export right now, or who want a fully automated, design-from-scratch AI deck tool.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The template library gives drafts a more designed look than generic AI themes.
  • Native .pptx output avoids lock-in to a web editor.
  • Outline review step lets you steer structure before slides are made.
  • Free sign-up lets you try it before paying.
  • Shareable links help with quick feedback.

Cons

  • Only .pptx export is available at present.
  • Google Slides compatibility is shown on a badge but the FAQ says support is still in development.
  • Document upload availability is unclear.
  • Free limits and paid prices were not stated on pages I could access.
  • Likely better for structured business decks than expressive creative ones.
