Dzine (formerly known as Stylar AI) is an AI image and design platform whose defining feature is model flexibility: rather than building or committing to a single proprietary image generator, Dzine sits on top of a rotating roster of the market's leading image and video models — including Nano Banana Pro, Midjourney, Seedream, Flux, Wan, Kling, Sora, Veo, and others — and lets users switch between them inside a single account and credit balance. On top of raw generation, Dzine layers a controllable editing toolkit: predefined style presets, layering and positioning tools, sketch-to-image, generative fill, one-click artifact fixes, auto-selection for layer masking, and a conversational chat-based editor that lets users refine outputs in natural language rather than re-prompting from scratch. The platform also extends into video with text-to-video, image-to-video, lip sync, multi-character lip sync, face swap, virtual try-on, and consistent-character generation for maintaining a subject's appearance across multiple outputs.

Dzine uses a unified credit system that spans both image and video generation, with plans ranging from a free tier (100 signup credits) through Beginner, Creator, Master, and Master Pro, the latter two offering "unlimited" access to most image models under a fair-use policy plus growing monthly video generation allowances. Commercial usage rights and private (non-public) generation are reserved for paid tiers; free-tier output is restricted to personal use. As an image/video aggregator rather than a single-model lab, Dzine's competitive position depends heavily on staying current with new model releases — the pricing page already reflects newer entrants like Nano Banana Pro 2k/4k, Sora 2, and Seedream 4.5/5.0 — which is both its core value proposition and an ongoing operational challenge as the underlying model landscape shifts quickly.

Best for

Dzine suits designers, marketers, and content creators who want the flexibility to compare and switch between multiple leading AI image and video models without subscribing to each separately, and who value hands-on editing control (layering, inpainting, character consistency) over pure one-shot generation. It's a weaker fit for users who want simple, predictable flat-rate pricing or who have had negative billing experiences reported by some Trustpilot reviewers and want to avoid that risk.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The core differentiator — access to a rotating lineup of top-tier image and video models under one subscription — means users aren't locked into a single engine's style or limitations, per independent reviews.
  • Editing controls go beyond simple text-to-image, with layering, sketching, generative fill, and auto-selection giving more granular creative control than many single-model competitors.
  • Master and Master Pro tiers offer effectively unlimited image generation across most supported models, which can be cost-competitive against paying for several individual model subscriptions.
  • Reviews note that support is responsive to both technical issues and feature requests, with Trustpilot commentary describing the tool as having "evolved from a good toolset to an amazing tool."

Cons

  • Trustpilot reviews include specific complaints about being charged after cancellation and credits being removed unexpectedly, which is a meaningful billing-trust concern for a subscription product.
  • The "unlimited" generation claim on paid tiers is bounded by a fair-use policy, and some users report the platform slowing down once that threshold is approached.
  • A single unified credit pool spanning many different models and video generation makes it harder to predict monthly costs than a flat per-image or per-video pricing model.
  • Free and Beginner tier users are limited in commercial usage rights and access to the top-tier models, pushing serious commercial users toward the $59.99+/month tiers.

What reviewers say

Dzine sits in mixed-but-leaning-positive territory on Trustpilot: users praise its multi-model flexibility and support responsiveness, while a notable minority report billing and cancellation issues.

Frequently asked questions

What is Dzine used for?

Dzine is an all-in-one AI image and video platform for generating, editing, and stylizing visuals using multiple top AI models like Nano Banana Pro, Midjourney, and Seedream in one workspace.

How much does Dzine cost?

Dzine has a free tier with 100 signup credits, then paid plans from Beginner ($8.99/mo) up to Master Pro ($149.99/mo), with up to 22% savings on annual billing.

Does Dzine offer unlimited image generation?

Master and Master Pro plans offer unlimited generation across most supported image models, subject to a fair-use policy that can throttle heavy usage.

Can I use Dzine-generated images commercially?

Commercial use is available on paid plans (Beginner and above); free-tier users are restricted to personal, non-commercial projects.

What models does Dzine support?

Dzine integrates models including Nano Banana Pro, Midjourney, Seedream, Flux, Wan, Kling, Sora, Veo, Qwen, and Z-image, with new models added as they're released.

Does Dzine generate video as well as images?

Yes, Dzine supports text-to-video, image-to-video, lip sync, face swap, and video enhancement/upscaling up to 8K on higher-tier plans.
