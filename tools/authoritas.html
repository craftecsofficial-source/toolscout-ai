Authoritas is a strong pick for agencies and in-house SEO teams that care mostly about accurate, granular rank tracking and want AI-search visibility reporting alongside it; it is a poor pick for beginners or solo site owners who want a simple, polished all-in-one tool. The UK-based company (formerly Analytics SEO, which absorbed Linkdex in 2018) sells two main products on separate price ladders: an SEO platform (rank tracking, crawler and audits, backlinks via Majestic data, competitor visibility) and an AI Search platform that runs prompts against AI models and measures how often your brand is mentioned or cited. Both have free tiers and credit-based paid plans, and the enterprise tier adds API access and BigQuery/Looker Studio export. Pricing is shown in pounds on the site, so US buyers should check the currency toggle before budgeting.

Best for

Good for SEO agencies and mid-to-large in-house teams that track many keywords across countries and devices, and for brands starting to measure visibility in AI answers. Not for solo bloggers or tiny sites that want a simple low-cost tool, for teams that need the biggest backlink index on the market, or for anyone who wants a modern, minimal interface.

Key features

01

Global Rank Tracking

Tracks keyword positions across Google, Bing, and Yahoo by country, region, city, and device, using real-browser checks for accuracy.

02

AI Search Platform (AEO/GEO)

Monitors brand visibility and citations inside AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, and DeepSeek.

03

Site Crawler & Technical Audits

Crawls sites to surface technical SEO issues, Core Web Vitals, and page experience problems across large domains.

04

Backlink Analysis

Pulls backlink data (via Majestic) to evaluate link profiles for a site and its competitors.

05

Competitor & Market Share Reporting

Visibility Explorer and market share tools show how a brand's organic presence compares to competitors over time.

06

Custom Reporting & BigQuery Export

API access and native Google BigQuery integration let teams build custom dashboards in Looker Studio.

07

Content & Keyword Research Tools

Keyword research, question/FAQ discovery, and content opportunity tools support content planning alongside tracking.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Rank tracking is the core strength: bulk keyword upload, tagging, and country, region and device-level checks.
  • Covers both classic SEO and AI-answer visibility, with the AI Search product priced separately so you only pay for what you use.
  • A real free tier on both products (1,000 keyword credits; 50 AI prompt credits) lets you test before paying.
  • Credit-based pricing without per-seat charges on lower tiers suits agencies with many stakeholders.
  • Enterprise plans include API and BigQuery/Looker Studio export for custom reporting.

Cons

  • Pricing is a credit system across two separate products; budgeting SEO plus AI Search means reading two price tables, and AI queries on flagship models burn 2 credits each.
  • Listed prices are in GBP; the free plan expires if you do not log in for 30 days.
  • Backlink data comes from Majestic and is generally seen as thinner than Ahrefs.
  • Interface is widely described as dense and dated compared with newer SEO suites.
  • Free AI Search tier is only 50 credits, enough for a quick test rather than ongoing monitoring.
  • Not independently re-verified today: review-site ratings, so none are quoted here.

Frequently asked questions

Does Authoritas have a free plan?

Yes. The SEO platform's free plan gives 2 websites, 1,000 keyword rank credits a month and 1 user, and requires a login every 30 days. The AI Search platform has a separate free tier of 50 prompt credits.

How much does Authoritas cost?

SEO plans listed today are Essential £99, Expert £459 and Business £679 per month; AI Search plans run from £89 to £379 per month, with Enterprise tiers from £1,129. Annual billing gives one month free on SEO plans. Prices are shown in GBP by default.

Can Authoritas track visibility in ChatGPT and other AI engines?

Yes, through its AI Search platform, which runs prompts against models such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude and reports mentions and citations. Each query uses 1-5 credits depending on the model.

How does Authoritas compare with Semrush?

Authoritas leans toward precise rank tracking and agency reporting with usage-based credits; Semrush is broader on keyword research, content and backlink tools. Pick Authoritas if tracking accuracy and AI-search measurement matter most.
