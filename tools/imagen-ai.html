Imagen is best for professional photographers who shoot many images per job and want edits that match their own look; hobbyists with few photos, and anyone wanting to generate images from text, should skip it. It is not an image generator, so the previous category label was misleading: it is a photo post-production service. It studies your past edited work to build a profile, applies it to new shoots, can cull and retouch, and connects to Adobe Lightroom. The company claims large time savings; we could not verify the figure.

Best for

Good for volume shooters such as wedding, event, school-sports and real estate photographers, and studios needing API automation. Not for casual users, anyone who prefers local-only processing, people wanting text-to-image generation, or those who want to hand-edit every frame.

Key features

01

Personal AI profiles

Trained on your edits and updated as you work; ready-made profiles from other photographers are sold in the Profile Shop.

02

AI culling

Picks the frames worth delivering; an add-on on PAYG, included in Limitless.

03

Retouching and masking

Straighten, crop, subject and background masks, denoise, skin smoothing; some tools such as object and hair removal are free in beta.

04

Integrations

Adobe Creative Cloud/Lightroom, with Pic-Time and HoneyBook named, plus an API.

05

Video

Adaptive color grading with LUTs; an assistant editor is listed as coming soon.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Edits reflect your own style rather than a generic preset.
  • Culling plus editing saves round-trips between apps.
  • Unlimited plan removes per-photo anxiety.
  • Unused $7 minimum rolls over.

Cons

  • The old $0.05/photo headline is not on today's pricing page; costs depend on which tools you run.
  • Annual plans and credit packages are no longer offered to new self-serve customers, per the pricing page.
  • Free trial amount is inconsistent across the site (1,000, 250 and 1,500 mentioned).
  • Requires uploading photos to the cloud.

Frequently asked questions

Does Imagen generate images?

No. It edits and culls photos you have taken.

How much is the free trial?

The pricing page says 1,000 free edits with no card; other pages mention different numbers, so confirm at signup.

Is culling included?

Only in Limitless. On pay-as-you-go it is an add-on from $29/mo.

Does it work with Lightroom?

Yes, the site lists Adobe Lightroom integration.
