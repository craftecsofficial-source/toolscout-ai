Inventive AI is a strong fit for teams that answer RFPs and security questionnaires every month and have decent reference content already written; teams that see only a handful per year, or want a free trial, should skip it. With pricing starting around $10K a year, it needs enough volume to pay back.

It reads your connected knowledge sources, extracts questions from incoming documents, drafts cited answers, and flags conflicting or outdated content. It also adds project management (assignments, tracking, reporting), bid qualification, answers for procurement portals like Coupa, Ariba and Jaggaer, and an MCP connector for AI assistants. It is a response tool for inbound buyer paperwork, not a prospecting tool.

Best for

Proposal, sales enablement and security/compliance teams at mid-market to enterprise software and services companies that respond to buyer RFPs, RFIs, security questionnaires or DDQs every month. Not for teams with only a few responses per year, buyers who need a self-serve free trial, construction or tender bidding, or outbound prospecting.

Key features

01

AI response drafting

Generates draft answers to RFP, RFI, security questionnaire and DDQ questions from your connected content, citing sources for a human to review.

02

Knowledge Hub

Central library that syncs past responses and company content from SharePoint, Google Drive, Notion and Confluence.

03

Content governance

Flags conflicting or outdated information in the knowledge base so old answers are not reused.

04

Bid qualification and compliance

Go/no-go assessment before you start, and a compliance agent that checks after submission.

05

Procurement portal answers

Supports answering inside Coupa, Ariba and Jaggaer portals.

06

Project management and reporting

Assign sections, track progress and report on win rates and team output.

07

MCP connector

Lets Claude, Cursor or ChatGPT work with the platform's data.

08

Security controls

The vendor claims SOC 2 compliance, role-based access, encryption and GDPR/CCPA adherence; details are on its security page, which we did not review.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Live sync with SharePoint, Google Drive, Notion and Confluence means you can start from content you already have.
  • The governance layer targets a real problem in proposal work: old or contradictory answers quietly getting reused.
  • Unlimited users and rollover of unused RFPs make cost depend on volume rather than headcount.
  • Handling RFPs and security questionnaires together avoids running two separate answer libraries.

Cons

  • Plans start around $10K/year, which is hard to justify for a team answering only a few RFPs a year.
  • The fixed platform fee is not stated, so the real first-year cost is unknown until you talk to sales.
  • No free trial is mentioned; evaluation means a demo or a product tour.
  • Headline numbers (50% higher win rate, 90% faster, 95% accuracy, 'zero hallucinations') are vendor-stated and the site itself is inconsistent on accuracy. Test on your own past RFPs.
  • An 'AI Revenue Execution Platform' is listed as coming soon with a waitlist; do not buy on that promise.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Inventive AI cost?

Its pricing page says from $830/month billed annually (plans start at $10K/year), usage-based per RFP or security questionnaire, with unlimited users. A fixed platform fee applies but its amount is not published. Enterprise pricing is custom.

Is there a free trial?

None is mentioned. The site offers a demo, a product tour and a 15-minute pricing call.

Which tools does it connect to?

The site names SharePoint, Google Drive, Notion, Confluence, Slack and Salesforce, and mentions Zendesk in its FAQ. It also offers an MCP connector for Claude, Cursor and ChatGPT.

Does it handle security questionnaires as well as RFPs?

Yes. It covers RFPs, RFIs, security questionnaires and DDQs from the same knowledge hub.

Can I trust the accuracy claims?

Treat them as vendor claims. The site cites 95% accuracy in one place and 'over 90%' in the FAQ. Pilot it on past RFPs and have a person review every answer.
