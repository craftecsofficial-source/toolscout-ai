Coachvox AI is a UK-based SaaS product that lets coaches, consultants, and other expert-led businesses build a conversational AI trained specifically on their own material — articles, books, course content, session transcripts, and frameworks — so it can answer questions the way the real person would. Rather than a generic chatbot giving generic answers, Coachvox positions its product as capturing a coach's actual thinking, tone, and methodology, then deploying that as a 24/7 assistant that can be embedded on a website, shared via a custom link, or placed inside an online community. Training happens through drag-and-drop content upload or, notably, through a direct connection to Claude, where a coach's conversations inside Claude become both training data and a live signal of what their audience wants to know.

The company frames its product around three business outcomes for coaches: building an email list (every AI conversation can capture a lead), supporting existing clients between paid sessions with on-brand answers, and warming up prospects before a sales call so the coach starts from a more informed, further-along conversation. Coachvox is an official business partner of the International Coaching Federation and has been used and referenced by known creators such as Ali Abdaal, positioning it within the creator-economy and coaching-industry niche rather than as a general-purpose customer support bot. Unlike broad chatbot builders, Coachvox intentionally keeps scope narrow — no video, no social scheduling — and instead goes deep on lead capture, monetization (coaches can charge for access to their AI), and Zapier-based CRM integration, aiming to be the AI extension of a single expert's brand rather than a multi-purpose support tool.

Best for

Coachvox is a strong fit for established coaches, consultants, and expert-led creators who already have a library of content and want to turn website visitors into leads and support clients between paid sessions using an AI in their own voice. It's a weaker fit for brand-new coaches without existing content, for businesses needing a general customer-support chatbot unrelated to a personal brand, or for anyone who also needs video/social content tools bundled into the same platform.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Credibility signal from the ICF partnership: Coachvox is listed as an official business partner of the International Coaching Federation, which matters for buyers evaluating trust in the coaching industry specifically.
  • Notable creator case studies: the site features recognizable names like Ali Abdaal and Carrie Green as customers, suggesting real adoption among established online coaches and course creators, not just hypothetical use cases.
  • Speed to launch: the company claims an AI can be trained and made live in under an hour via drag-and-drop upload or the Claude connection, lowering the barrier to trying the product versus a multi-week chatbot build.
  • Built-in monetization: unlike many coaching chatbots that are purely a support tool, Coachvox lets coaches directly charge for access to their AI, turning the assistant itself into a revenue line rather than just a cost center.
  • Content privacy commitment: Coachvox states uploaded content is used only to train the individual coach's AI and is never used to train other/wider models or shared between customers.

Cons

  • All-in pricing starts at $83/month even on the discounted annual plan, which is a meaningful recurring cost for solo coaches early in building an audience, especially compared to free/low-cost general chatbot builders.
  • Scope is deliberately narrow — Coachvox explicitly does not offer video creation, content repurposing, or social media scheduling, so coaches need separate tools for those workflows.
  • Quality of output depends heavily on how much existing content a coach has; someone without a backlog of articles, transcripts, or course material will get a much thinner AI persona.
  • No independent third-party review aggregation (G2 listing exists but shows no meaningful review volume as of research), making it harder to benchmark satisfaction against alternatives using outside data.
  • Being a single, all-inclusive plan means there's no lower-cost tier for coaches who only want basic lead capture without the full content-generation and community features.

Frequently asked questions

What is Coachvox AI?

Coachvox is a platform that trains an AI on a coach's own content, methodology, and voice so it can talk to their audience, capture leads, and support clients 24/7.

How much does Coachvox cost?

Coachvox uses a single all-inclusive plan priced at $83/month when billed annually, with monthly billing available at a higher rate; a 14-day free trial is offered.

How is Coachvox different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT gives generic answers to everyone, while a Coachvox AI is trained only on one coach's content and methodology and is built specifically to capture leads and support that coach's business.

Can I charge people to access my AI coach?

Yes, Coachvox supports charging for access, adding coupons, and offering trial periods, and some coaches bundle AI access into existing paid packages.

What content can I use to train my AI?

Articles, books, course material, session transcripts, frameworks, and podcasts can all be uploaded, or a coach can connect Claude to train through conversation.

Does Coachvox integrate with other tools?

Yes, Coachvox connects to CRMs and email tools via Zapier, can be embedded anywhere that accepts HTML, and offers a direct Claude integration for training.
