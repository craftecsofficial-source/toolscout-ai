Coachvox AI is a good fit for established coaches and consultants who already have articles, transcripts or frameworks to train on and want lead capture and between-session support. Solo beginners with little content, or anyone who needs a general customer-support bot, should skip it. It builds a chatbot from your material that answers in your voice, which you can embed on a site, share by link or charge for. Training works by uploading files, adding web or text content, or connecting Claude. Conversations can capture lead details and send them to a CRM or email tool through Zapier, and a dashboard shows chat stats and audience questions. It also drafts LinkedIn, blog and short social posts. There is a single all-inclusive plan, so the choice is billing period, not feature tier. The site describes ICF partnership and creator case studies; we did not independently verify those.

Best for

Good for coaches, consultants and expert founders with a library of their own content. Not for beginners with nothing to train on, not for businesses wanting a multi-purpose support or sales bot, and not for anyone who needs video, repurposing or social scheduling.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Narrow focus on one expert's brand means lead capture, paid access and Zapier come in the base plan.
  • Unlimited chats and leads, so growth does not raise the bill.
  • Several ways to train, including a Claude connection, lower the setup effort.
  • 14-day trial and cancel-anytime terms limit commitment.

Cons

  • Roughly $83/month is a lot for early-stage coaches, and there is no cheaper tier.
  • The monthly price is not stated on the page we could fetch, so budget uncertainty remains.
  • A card is required for the trial.
  • Quality depends on how much good source content you have.
  • No video, repurposing or social scheduling tools.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Coachvox cost?

One plan at $83/month billed annually. A monthly option exists but its price was not visible, so check the site.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, 14 days. A card is required and you can cancel any time.

What can I train it on?

Uploaded .docx, .pdf and .txt files, web or text content, and a Claude connection.

Can I connect it to my CRM?

Yes, through Zapier, which sends leads to CRM or email tools.

Can I charge people to use my AI?

Yes. The site lists paid access with coupons and trial periods.
