Edimakor is a good fit for solo creators and small teams who want a conventional timeline editor and AI generation in one install, and who like the idea of a one-time license. Skip it if you want a browser-first collaborative editor, or if you plan to generate a lot of AI video or avatar footage, because every AI action burns a limited credit balance. It is HitPaw's desktop editor for Windows and Mac (plus a web app) with multi-track editing, keyframes, masking, motion tracking and screen recording. On top sit AI tools for text/image-to-video, talking avatars and photos, voice cloning and text-to-speech, auto subtitles, video translation, background and watermark removal, and a clip maker that cuts long footage into shorts. Generation is routed to outside models rather than one in-house engine, and all of it is paid for from the same AI credit pool.

Best for

Solo creators, YouTubers, course makers and small marketing teams who want to cut a normal edit and add AI clips, avatars and voiceovers in one desktop app, especially if they prefer paying once. Not for: teams that need a browser-based collaborative editor, anyone producing high volumes of AI video who needs predictable per-clip costs, or mobile-only creators.

Key features

01

Text-to-video and image-to-video generation

Generates video clips from text prompts or still images by routing requests to third-party models such as Veo 3, Sora 2, Kling, Vidu, and Pixverse, selectable inside the editor.

02

AI avatars and talking photos

Turns a photo or script into a talking or singing avatar with lip-sync, usable for presenter-style or explainer video content.

03

Voice cloning and text-to-speech

Clones a voice or generates speech from text using a library of AI voices across 35+ languages, useful for narration and dubbing.

04

Auto subtitles and speech-to-text

Automatically transcribes spoken audio into timed subtitles and can also translate/dub video into other languages using AI voices.

05

AI clip maker

Analyzes longer footage (e.g., podcasts or webinars) and automatically cuts it into shorter, social-ready clips.

06

Traditional multi-track editing

Unlimited timeline tracks, keyframe animation, video masking, motion tracking, cropping/rotating, and audio editing for standard manual editing work.

07

Large built-in asset library

Includes thousands of effects, filters, transitions, text animations, stickers, and tens of thousands of stock clips, photos, and customizable templates.

08

Screen recording and background/watermark removal

Built-in screen recorder and live-streaming support, plus AI tools for removing video backgrounds and watermarks.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • A real multi-track editor (keyframes, masking, motion tracking, screen recording) sits next to the AI tools, so it is not just an AI wrapper.
  • A perpetual license option ($69.95 listed) is rare among AI video tools, which are mostly subscription-only.
  • Several third-party video models are selectable from one app, saving separate sign-ups.
  • Covers the whole workflow: generate, edit, subtitle, translate, and cut long videos into short clips.
  • Large built-in library of effects, transitions, templates and stock media per the vendor.

Cons

  • AI use draws down a fixed credit pool (roughly 20 credits per second of avatar video, per earlier research), so heavy users will run out and need credit packs.
  • The free version exports with a watermark, so it is a trial, not a usable free tier for publishing.
  • Desktop install on Windows or Mac; there is no mobile app yet and the web version is the lighter option.
  • Output quality and credit cost depend on which third-party model you pick, so results are uneven.
  • Standard licenses are for one PC; multiple machines need a business tier.
  • Prices and credit allotments change often (the site shows discounted-from prices), so confirm at checkout.

Frequently asked questions

Is HitPaw Edimakor free?

You can use the non-AI editing tools free, but exports carry a watermark and new users get only 30 AI credits. Removing the watermark and doing meaningful AI work needs a paid plan or credit purchase.

Which AI models does it use?

The vendor lists third-party models including Google Veo 3, Kling, Hailuo, Vidu, Pixverse and Nano Banana, selectable inside the app. Availability changes, so check the current list.

Is there a one-time purchase instead of a subscription?

Yes, a perpetual license was listed at $69.95 for one PC with a lifetime allotment of 10,000 credits. This price could not be re-verified today, so confirm it at checkout.

How do AI credits work?

Each AI feature consumes credits at its own rate, drawn from your plan's balance; extra credits can be bought separately. Avatar and video generation use far more than subtitles.

Does it run on phones or in the browser?

Desktop apps exist for Windows and Mac, and there is a web version. A mobile app is described as still in development.
