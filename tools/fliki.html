Fliki is a text-to-video and text-to-speech platform built by Nine Thirty Five, positioned around a simple premise: give it an idea, script, blog post, or slide deck, and it assembles a fully narrated video using AI voiceover paired with stock footage, stock images, or AI-generated visuals. The company reports over 100 million videos created and 12 million users worldwide as of 2026, and has expanded well beyond basic text-to-speech into a broader creative suite that includes AI avatars, voice cloning, AI image and video clip generation, and automated translation into 80+ languages. Its core differentiator against heavier editing-first tools like Vyond or CapCut is workflow simplicity: rather than assembling scenes manually, users largely describe what they want (an idea, a pasted script, a blog URL) and Fliki auto-builds a scene-by-scene draft that can then be lightly edited.

Fliki's pricing model is built around annual credits rather than flat per-video limits, with a genuinely usable free-forever tier (not just a trial) that includes 300 stock and premium voices and basic video creation up to 1-minute exports at 720p with a watermark. Paid tiers scale up resolution (1080p), export length (15 minutes on Standard, 40 minutes on Premium), voice library size (1,000+ to 2,000+ voices including ultra-realistic options), and unlock features like voice cloning, AI avatars, commercial usage rights and API access. The platform integrates recent-generation third-party AI video and image models (Veo 3.1, Kling 3 Pro, Seedance 2, Nano Banana 2, GPT Image 2) directly into its scene builder, letting users generate AI video clips or images inline rather than relying solely on stock footage — a notable evolution from its original text-to-speech roots.

Best for

Fliki is a strong fit for solo content creators, bloggers, educators and small marketing teams who want to turn existing written content (scripts, blog posts, slide decks) into narrated video quickly, without video-editing skills. It's a weaker fit for teams needing precise manual scene control over every frame, or for agencies needing many simultaneous cloned voices and API-scale automation, where the credit caps and per-tier feature gating become limiting.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Fliki's free plan is a genuine ongoing tier (not a 7-day trial) with 300 voices and core idea-to-video workflows, letting new users properly evaluate output quality before paying anything.
  • The voice library is one of the largest in the category (2,000+ voices, 80+ languages on Premium), which matters for creators making multilingual or accent-specific content.
  • The idea-to-video and blog-to-video workflows genuinely cut production time for creators who don't want to build scenes manually, since Fliki auto-drafts scene structure from source text.
  • Integrated third-party AI models (Veo 3.1, Kling 3 Pro, Nano Banana 2) mean users can generate original AI video clips and images inline without leaving the platform or paying for a separate tool.
  • Both G2 and Capterra reviewers converge around a consistent ~4.7/5 rating across hundreds of reviews, suggesting reliable satisfaction rather than a small, cherry-picked sample.

Cons

  • The free plan is capped at roughly 1-minute exports in 720p with a visible watermark, which limits it to testing rather than any real publishing use.
  • Export length and resolution scale by tier — 15 minutes on Standard, 40 on Premium — so longer-form content requires the top plan.
  • Advanced production features (API access, multiple voice cloning, AI video clip generation, team collaboration) are gated to the Premium tier, pushing serious creators toward the $44-88/month plan quickly.
  • As a largely automated, template-driven tool, fine-grained manual scene control is more limited than in a full editor like Vyond or CapCut, which can mean more re-generation to get an exact result.
  • Voice cloning is capped even on paid plans (1 clone on Standard, 3 on Premium), which may not suit agencies managing many client voices.

Frequently asked questions

Is Fliki really free to use?

Yes, Fliki offers a free-forever plan with no credit card required, though it's limited to short exports (about 1 minute) at 720p with a watermark.

How do Fliki's credits work?

Credits are consumed based on video duration and features used (e.g., AI avatar or AI video clip generation increases the rate); paid plans allocate credits annually rather than monthly.

Can Fliki clone my voice?

Yes, voice cloning is available starting on the Standard plan (1 clone) and expands to 3 clones on Premium.

What's the maximum video length Fliki supports?

Up to 15 minutes on the Standard plan and up to 40 minutes on the Premium plan; the free plan is limited to roughly 1 minute.

Does Fliki support languages other than English?

Yes, it supports 300+ voices on the free plan up to 2,000+ on Premium, spanning 80+ languages, plus auto-translation and dubbing of existing videos.

Does Fliki offer an API?

API access is included on the Premium and Enterprise plans, not on Free or Standard.
