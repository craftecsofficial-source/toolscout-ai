Fliki is a good pick for solo creators, marketers and educators who want to turn a script, blog post or slide deck into a narrated video quickly, and it has a usable free plan to test that. Skip it if you need cinematic AI footage, deep timeline editing or a fixed, easily verified price, because the paid plan amounts were not displayed on the live page when I checked. It builds a scene-by-scene draft from your text using AI voices (up to 2,000+ across 80+ languages), stock media or AI-generated visuals, plus avatars, voice cloning, translation and YouTube publishing on paid plans.

Best for

Good for YouTubers, course creators, bloggers repurposing posts, and small marketing teams making narrated explainers at volume. Not for filmmakers wanting fine-grained editing or premium generative video, nor for enterprises needing strict security reviews (use the Enterprise tier and ask sales).

Key features

01

Idea/script/blog/PPT to video

Multiple entry points let users generate a video draft from a one-line idea, a full script, a blog post URL, or an uploaded PowerPoint presentation.

02

2,000+ AI voices across 80+ languages

A large voice library including hundreds of ultra-realistic and multilingual expressive voices on higher tiers, used to narrate generated videos automatically.

03

AI avatars and voice cloning

Paid plans unlock AI presenter avatars and the ability to clone a user's own voice (or a custom voice) for narration, with Premium supporting multiple cloned voices.

04

Stock and AI-generated visuals

Videos can be built from Fliki's stock image/video/music library, or generated on the fly using integrated AI image and video models like Nano Banana 2 and Kling 3 Pro.

05

Auto-translation and dubbing

Existing videos can be translated and re-narrated into 80+ languages, useful for repurposing a single script into multiple language versions.

06

AI thumbnail and social carousel generation

Beyond video, Fliki can generate thumbnails, social carousels and presentation decks from the same idea-to-content pipeline.

07

YouTube publishing and bulk create

Standard and Premium tiers support direct YouTube publishing and bulk video creation, useful for channels producing content at volume.

08

Brand kits and custom fonts

Paid plans support saved brand kits (colors, logos) and custom fonts to keep generated videos visually consistent with a creator's or company's identity.

Frequently asked questions

Is Fliki free?

Yes, there is a free-forever plan with 3 credits/month, 720p, a 5-minute cap and a watermark.

How much do paid plans cost?

The pricing page says yearly billing is 25% off, but the dollar amounts were not displayed when I checked, so confirm on fliki.ai/pricing.

Can I clone my voice?

Yes, from Standard (1 clone); Premium allows 3.

Do I get commercial rights?

Listed on Standard and Premium; not on the Free plan.

Can I use the API?

API access is listed on Premium and Enterprise.
