Rewording.io is a good fit if you have existing English text and want to reword it quickly while controlling how much it changes. Skip it if you need to write from scratch, need a published price list before committing, or work mainly in languages other than English. It takes text you paste, drag in or upload from images and documents, detects the language, and rewrites it at a strength you set between 45% and 100%, in a tone you choose. Each scan handles up to 10,000 characters. A sidebar adds an AI detector, resizer, plagiarism tool, cowriter and a grammar check labelled beta, and signed-in users get automatic history. The site says text is processed securely and not stored or shared; that is the vendor's own claim and we have not verified it. The site is somewhat inconsistent about plagiarism checking and language support, so test it on your own text first.

Best for

Students, bloggers, marketers and editors who need to rephrase existing English text. It is not for people who want a tool to draft new content, anyone who needs confirmed pricing and word limits up front, or users who need dependable non-English output. Do not treat its plagiarism or AI-detection tools as proof for academic or publishing decisions.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The strength slider gives real control, from light edits to heavy restructuring.
  • Free to start, so you can judge output quality on your own text before paying.
  • Taking input from images and documents saves retyping.
  • Several adjacent tools in one place cut down on tab switching.

Cons

  • No prices or billing terms for premium on the pages we could check, so cost is unknown until you sign up.
  • The free word allowance is not stated, so you may hit the limit unexpectedly.
  • Language support is unclear: one place lists English, French, Spanish and Portuguese while the FAQ says English is primary and others are planned.
  • The FAQ says it is not a plagiarism checker while the sidebar has a Plagiarism tool, which makes that feature hard to trust.
  • The grammar check is labelled beta.

Frequently asked questions

Is Rewording.io free?

Yes, there is a free plan with a limited word allowance. The exact allowance is not stated on the site.

How much does premium cost?

We could not find prices on the pages checked, so this is unverified. The site says premium adds higher word limits and priority support.

How much text can I rewrite at once?

Up to 10,000 characters per scan.

Does it work in languages other than English?

Unclear. The site lists several languages in one place, but its FAQ says English is primary and others are planned.

How does it compare with Wordtune or NeuralWriter?

Its strength slider and bundled side tools are the draw; Wordtune is more of an in-flow rewriting assistant, and NeuralWriter is another free paraphraser.
