Krisp is an AI-powered voice and meeting productivity app founded in 2017 by Davit Baghdasaryan and Artavazd Minasyan (originally under the name 2Hz) in Armenia, now headquartered in Berkeley, California. Its core technology uses deep learning models trained to recognize and suppress more than 20,000 distinct background noise types — traffic, keyboard clicks, barking dogs, other people talking — in real time with under 10 milliseconds of latency, processing audio locally on the user's device rather than routing it through the cloud. Krisp launched publicly in 2019 as the first software-only AI noise cancellation tool that could plug into any communication app, and saw major adoption during the 2020 remote-work shift; its noise cancellation has since been deployed on more than 200 million devices and processes over 80 billion minutes of voice traffic per month.

Since 2022-2023 Krisp has expanded from a pure noise-cancellation utility into a broader voice AI and meeting assistant platform, adding real-time transcription, automatic meeting summaries, and action-item extraction that sit on top of its original audio-cleanup engine. Because it operates as a system-level audio layer rather than a plugin tied to one video platform, Krisp works across Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Slack huddles, and any other app that uses computer audio, which differentiates it from meeting-note tools that only integrate with a handful of calendar-linked platforms.

Best for

Krisp is a strong fit for remote and hybrid workers, sales/support teams on constant calls, and podcasters or streamers who need clean audio without a hardware setup, especially those already invested in multiple different conferencing apps. It's a weaker fit for teams that just want a single deeply-integrated meeting-notes tool tied to a calendar and CRM, where dedicated transcription platforms may offer more workflow depth.

Key features

01

AI noise cancellation

Removes background noise from both the microphone and speaker output in real time, trained on tens of thousands of distinct noise types, processed entirely on-device.

02

Background voice cancellation

Isolates the primary speaker's voice even when other people are talking nearby, useful in shared offices, cafes or open workspaces.

03

Universal app compatibility

Runs as a system-level audio filter, so it works inside virtually any conferencing, recording or calling app without needing per-platform integrations.

04

AI meeting transcription

Generates real-time or post-call transcripts of conversations, searchable and exportable for later reference.

05

AI meeting summaries and action items

Automatically produces a structured recap of each meeting, including key discussion points and follow-up tasks, reducing manual note-taking.

06

Meeting recording and history

Records audio/video of calls (Pro and above) and stores a searchable meeting history for later review.

07

HIPAA and GDPR compliance

Paid tiers include compliance controls that make Krisp usable in regulated industries like healthcare and legal services.

08

Team management and SSO

Business and Enterprise tiers add centralized license management, CRM integrations, and single sign-on for larger organizations.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The noise cancellation engine is genuinely platform-agnostic — it sits below the OS audio layer, so it works identically in Zoom, Teams, Discord or a plain phone call app, unlike bot-based meeting assistants tied to calendar integrations.
  • On-device processing is a real privacy differentiator for regulated industries (Krisp offers HIPAA/GDPR-compliant tiers) since raw audio doesn't need to leave the user's machine to be cleaned.
  • G2 reviewers consistently cite noticeable, immediate improvement in call clarity, particularly for users working from home, co-working spaces, or noisy commutes.
  • AI meeting summaries and action items reduce the manual overhead of note-taking, and integrate directly with the same app already cleaning the audio.
  • Krisp's technology scale (200M+ devices, 80B+ minutes/month) suggests a mature, well-tested noise model rather than an early-stage product.

Cons

  • Krisp removed its unlimited free noise-cancellation tier in favor of a capped 60-minutes-per-day free plan, so heavy users of calls now hit a paywall faster than they used to.
  • Capterra reviewers describe per-seat pricing as expensive for individual users, especially once currency conversion and monthly (vs. annual) billing are factored in.
  • Meeting-assistant features (transcription, summaries) are less differentiated than Krisp's noise-cancellation core, competing directly against dedicated tools like Otter.ai and Fireflies that have deeper calendar/CRM integrations.
  • Some advanced management features (SSO, CRM integration) are gated behind the Business tier, which adds cost for teams that only want core noise cancellation.
  • As with most on-device AI audio tools, effectiveness can vary with extreme or unusual noise types, occasionally requiring a settings adjustment.

Frequently asked questions

Does Krisp work with any video calling app?

Yes, Krisp operates as a system-level audio filter, so it works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Discord, and any other app that uses your computer's microphone and speakers.

Is my audio sent to Krisp's servers?

No, Krisp's noise cancellation processes audio locally on your device rather than sending it to the cloud, which is a key part of its privacy and compliance positioning.

Does Krisp have a free plan?

Yes, Krisp offers a free plan with 60 minutes of noise cancellation per day and limited AI meeting summaries; unlimited use requires a Pro or Business subscription.

Can Krisp transcribe and summarize meetings?

Yes, Pro and above plans include AI meeting transcription plus automatically generated summaries and action items.

Is Krisp HIPAA compliant?

Krisp's Pro and higher tiers include HIPAA and GDPR compliance features, making it usable for healthcare and other regulated industries.

How much does Krisp cost for a team?

The Business plan starts at $10/mo per seat billed annually ($15/mo billed monthly); Enterprise pricing is custom for larger organizations needing SSO and API access.
