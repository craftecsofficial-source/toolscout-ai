Krisp is a strong pick for people on many calls who want clean audio and meeting notes without a bot joining, and it is cheap enough per seat to try. Skip it if you want a permanent free plan, or if you need deep calendar and CRM workflow built around a dedicated notetaker. It sits on your computer's audio, so the noise, echo and background-voice removal works in whatever call app you use, and the same engine feeds transcription, recordings, summaries and action items. The company says its English transcription runs on-device, with server-based transcription for 15 other languages. Krisp also sells Call Center AI and developer SDK products, which have separate pricing and are largely sales-led.

Best for

Good for remote and hybrid workers, sales and support reps and anyone who takes calls in noisy spaces and wants notes without a bot. Not for people who want a free-forever tool, teams needing a CRM-deep conversation-intelligence platform, or buyers who need HIPAA or SSO without going through sales.

Key features

01

Noise, echo and voice cancellation

Removes background noise, echo and cross-talk on both your mic and what you hear, inside any call app.

02

Bot-free AI note taker

Transcribes and records meetings from your device without a bot joining; an optional bot is available.

03

AI notes and action items

Produces summaries and follow-ups and can send them to tools like Salesforce, HubSpot and Slack.

04

Ask Krisp

A chat feature for asking questions of your meeting transcripts.

05

Accent conversion

Real-time accent conversion for calls; Core allows 1 hr/day, Advanced allows more.

06

Mobile and in-person notes

The mobile app records and transcribes in-person conversations, including offline, but does not yet do noise cancellation per the site.

07

Security and compliance

The site lists SOC 2, GDPR and HIPAA; HIPAA and SSO/SCIM are on the Enterprise plan, with a BAA for 100+ seats.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Works in any app that uses your mic and speakers, with no per-platform setup and no visible bot in the call.
  • Noise cancellation is unlimited on paid plans, and members are unlimited on every plan.
  • Notes can flow into HubSpot, Slack, Notion or Zapier even on the cheapest paid tier.
  • The cheapest paid tier is low-priced per seat compared with many notetaker subscriptions.
  • Krisp states English transcription runs on-device, which can help privacy-sensitive users (a company claim I did not independently verify).

Cons

  • The pricing page now shows a 7-day trial rather than an ongoing free plan, so light users have no permanent free option.
  • The low $8 and $15 rates are the annual-toggle prices; monthly billing is $16 and $30, double.
  • Storage is capped (10 GB Core, 60 GB Advanced); more costs extra.
  • The mobile app does not include noise cancellation yet, per the site.
  • HIPAA, SSO/SCIM and on-device private transcription are Enterprise-only and priced by sales.
  • Call-center and developer products have no public prices apart from CC Core at $15/agent/mo.

Frequently asked questions

Does Krisp have a free plan?

The pricing page now shows a 7-day free trial with no credit card, not an ongoing free plan. Older plans with a daily free allowance are not listed.

How much does Krisp cost?

Core is $8/user/mo and Advanced $15/user/mo at the annual-toggle rate; monthly billing is $16 and $30. Enterprise is custom.

Does a bot join my meetings?

No, the main note taker is bot-free and works from your device. An optional bot that joins meetings is also offered.

Which call apps does it work with?

Krisp lists Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, and it works as a system-level audio layer for other apps that use your mic and speakers.

How does it compare with Otter.ai?

Otter.ai is centered on meeting transcription and summaries, while Krisp adds noise and voice cancellation to calls plus a bot-free approach; check both pricing pages for your team size.
