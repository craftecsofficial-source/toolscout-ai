Adori AI is a good fit for marketers who already have written or audio content and want quick narrated videos from it; skip it if you need long videos, heavy AI video generation on a small budget, or a deep timeline editor. It takes a blog URL, PDF, script or podcast, splits it into scenes, adds stock or AI images, a synthetic voiceover and synced captions, and lets you tweak the result in a scene-based editor before publishing to YouTube. It has also added an AI talking-avatar generator (50+ avatars or a custom one) for ads and UGC-style clips, and it resells access to third-party models such as Veo, Runway, Kling and ElevenLabs rather than running its own. Pricing was checked on the live pricing page on 2026-10-29.

Best for

Good for content marketers, bloggers and podcasters repurposing existing material. Not for filmmakers or agencies needing long-form, high-volume or precise timeline editing, nor for anyone who mainly wants lots of AI-generated video clips on a low tier (Free and Basic include none). Not ideal if you need verified third-party reviews before buying.

Key features

Blog/PDF/Audio-to-Video Automation

Automatically converts a pasted blog URL, uploaded PDF, or audio/podcast file into a structured video with scenes, images, and pacing.

Provides access to over 100 million copyright-free, trending, and contextually relevant photos, videos, and music tracks.

Live Video Editor

A real-time, scene-based editor with side-by-side preview lets users instantly switch scenes and adjust animations.

06

AI Talking Avatar Generator

Turns a script or product brief into a talking-avatar video using 50+ AI avatars or a custom-built avatar, output in landscape or portrait HD.

07

SEO & Publishing Tools

Adds AI-suggested SEO metadata and supports direct publishing to YouTube to extend organic reach.

08

Multi-Source Input

Accepts text, PDF, blog, podcast, and audio as source material, not just written blog posts.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Turns a blog URL, PDF or podcast into a first-draft video with scenes, voiceover and captions, which removes most of the manual editing for repurposing work.
  • Several third-party models (Veo, Runway, Kling, ElevenLabs, Flux and others per the site) sit behind one login, so you do not need separate subscriptions for each.
  • The talking-avatar generator (50+ avatars, custom avatar from a photo) adds an ad/UGC use case on top of blog-to-video.
  • The Free plan needs no credit card, so the core workflow can be tested before paying.
  • Scene-based editor with live preview lets non-editors adjust scenes and captions quickly.

Cons

  • AI video generation (Veo3, Runway, Grok, Haliuo) is not included on Free or Basic; you need Premium at $45/mo for 10 generations.
  • Script limits of 1, 3 and 5 minutes mean it targets short videos; long-form explainers are out of scope.
  • Storage is small (100MB, 500MB, 1GB) and 1080p is Premium-only, which may bite teams producing many videos.
  • Video export counts and some earlier figures could not be confirmed on the current pricing page, so check before relying on them.
  • Quality of the output depends on the underlying third-party models; no independent rating on G2, Capterra or Trustpilot was verified.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Adori AI cost?

Per the pricing page on 2026-10-29: Free $0, Basic $15/mo, Premium $45/mo, Enterprise via sales. The site says yearly billing saves 20%, but exact annual prices are not shown, so the figures of about $12 and $36 are our own arithmetic and unverified.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. It includes 1 project, 20 AI images, 100MB storage, 720p video and a 1-minute script limit, with no AI video generations. The site says no credit card is required.

Can I generate AI video clips on Basic?

No. Free and Basic list 0 AI video generations; Premium lists 10.

How does it compare with Fliki or HeyGen?

Adori is strongest at converting existing blog, PDF and podcast content into videos. Fliki is a similar script-to-narrated-video tool; HeyGen is the stronger pick if talking avatars and localization are your main need.
