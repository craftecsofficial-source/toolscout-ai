AIPRM is great for people who use ChatGPT or Claude every day for repeat tasks like SEO, marketing copy or support replies and want a library of ready-made prompts. If you rarely use AI chat, or already keep your own prompt notes, you can skip it. It is a browser extension (Chrome and Edge) that adds a prompt library and prompt manager inside the chat page. You can use community-approved prompts, save private ones, and share lists with a team. It is a prompt manager, not a content generator.

Best for

Good for marketers, SEO professionals and small teams that reuse the same prompts daily in ChatGPT or Claude. Not for occasional AI users, people who prefer writing their own prompts, or anyone who needs a refundable or discounted plan.

Key features

01

Public prompt library

Prompts that the community has used, reviewed and approved. The site gives differing counts, 4,000+ and 5,400+.

02

Private prompts

Create, organize and reuse your own prompts. The number of private templates and list items scales by plan.

03

Team sharing

Team plans let admins assign licenses and share selected prompts and lists with a group.

04

Custom GPT prompts

Browse and manage prompts for individual Custom GPTs.

05

ChatGPT and Claude versions

Both are listed as free to start; Edge and Chrome are supported.

06

Plan-gated extras

Hiding prompt templates needs Plus or above; customizing tone and writing styles and AIPRM Verified Prompts need Pro or above; prompt forking is called an Elite feature.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • You can try it free before deciding on a paid plan.
  • The shared library gives quick starting points for common marketing and SEO tasks.
  • Private prompt storage keeps your own best prompts in one place.
  • Works with both ChatGPT and Claude.
  • Team sharing helps a group keep prompts consistent.

Cons

  • Paid prices could not be confirmed from the live pages, so check the pricing page before relying on any figure.
  • Terms: all sales are non-refundable, no coupons or custom prices, and annual price indexation may raise costs.
  • Limits such as private templates, list sizes and upcoming prompts depend on the plan and can be confusing.
  • Prompts are only as good as their authors; community prompts vary in quality.
  • It only adds prompts; it does not improve the AI model's output itself.

Frequently asked questions

Is AIPRM free?

Yes, a free version is available and the site says it can be used for an unlimited time before buying a premium plan.

How much do paid plans cost?

We could not verify current prices: the pricing pages checked on 2026-10-19 did not show a full table. Check aiprm.com/pricing directly.

Does AIPRM work with Claude?

Yes, the site lists AIPRM for Claude alongside AIPRM for ChatGPT.

Can I get a refund?

The billing terms say all sales are non-refundable and no pro-rata refunds are given on upgrades or downgrades.

Does AIPRM generate content itself?

No. It supplies and organizes prompts that you run in ChatGPT or Claude.
