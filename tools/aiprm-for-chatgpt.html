AIPRM for ChatGPT is a browser extension, built by AIPRM, Corp., that layers a curated prompt marketplace and prompt-management toolkit on top of ChatGPT and Claude. Rather than typing prompts from scratch, users browse a library of over 4,000 prompt templates organized by category — SEO, marketing, sales, copywriting, productivity, and customer support — and insert them with one click directly inside the chat interface they're already using. Prompts fall into two tiers: Community Prompts, submitted and upvoted by the AIPRM user base, and AIPRM Verified Prompts, which the company's team has tested, actively maintains, and guarantees to work as described. Free users get access to team-shared prompt lists, custom profiles that auto-insert company information into outputs, advanced prompt search, and an omnibox shortcut for triggering prompts directly from the browser's address bar.

AIPRM differentiates itself by being one of the earliest and largest prompt marketplaces built specifically for the ChatGPT ecosystem, growing to reportedly over 1,000,000 users within its first year by riding the wave of ChatGPT's 2022-2023 breakout adoption. Paid Premium tiers unlock Custom Tones & Writing Styles for on-brand output, Live Crawling to pull real webpage content into a prompt, Prompt Forking to privately customize public prompts, and Power Continue for one-click content expansion when ChatGPT's output gets cut off. The company, based in Wilmington, Delaware, has since expanded beyond ChatGPT to support Claude and added a Business/Team pricing track for organizations that want centralized prompt libraries and shared brand tone settings across multiple employees, positioning AIPRM less as a standalone writing tool and more as an operating layer for teams that already rely heavily on conversational AI.

Best for

AIPRM suits individuals, marketers, and support/sales teams who spend significant time inside ChatGPT or Claude and want a large, organized library of tested prompts plus lightweight brand-tone controls, without switching to a separate AI writing app. It's a weaker fit for users who need a standalone document editor or long-form drafting environment, since AIPRM is an add-on layer for existing chat interfaces rather than a full writing platform on its own.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The Chrome Web Store listing shows AIPRM for ChatGPT used by over 1,000,000 people, with a publisher record marked as having no history of policy violations.
  • Reviewers describe the tool as a fast way to increase productivity, calling it "very powerful and easy to use" for boosting ChatGPT's practical output for business tasks.
  • The free tier is genuinely functional on its own, giving teams access to thousands of prompts, team sharing, and custom profiles without payment.
  • AIPRM for Claude, a companion extension, holds a notably higher 4.8/5 rating on the Chrome Web Store, reflecting strong satisfaction among users of that specific extension.
  • The prompt-forking and verified-prompt system gives users a way to filter for tested, maintained prompts rather than relying purely on unvetted community submissions.

Cons

  • The Chrome Web Store aggregate rating sits at 3.9/5 from 3,400+ ratings, notably lower than the 4.8/5 for the Claude version, suggesting more mixed sentiment on the ChatGPT extension specifically.
  • AIPRM's own FAQ explicitly states there are no refunds, discounts, or coupons under any circumstances, which is stricter than most SaaS competitors.
  • Reviewers note the extension can be buggy at times and the company itself recommends disabling other ChatGPT extensions for reliable operation.
  • Solo and Team plan pricing scales steeply (up to $999/mo Solo, $2,990/mo Team), which can be expensive relative to simpler prompt-library alternatives.
  • Because pricing tiers stack in non-additive ways for certain limits, understanding exactly what a given plan unlocks can require reading detailed FAQ explanations.

Frequently asked questions

Is AIPRM for ChatGPT free?

Yes, AIPRM offers a free version with access to Community and Verified prompts, team sharing, custom profiles, and advanced search; paid Solo and Team tiers unlock additional customization and higher limits.

Does AIPRM work with Claude as well as ChatGPT?

Yes, AIPRM offers a dedicated extension for Claude in addition to its original ChatGPT extension, both available on the Chrome Web Store.

How many prompts are in the AIPRM library?

AIPRM's library includes over 4,000 prompt templates spanning Community-submitted prompts and AIPRM-Verified prompts that the company actively tests and maintains.

Can I get a refund if I don't like AIPRM?

No, AIPRM's terms explicitly state that all sales are non-refundable; users can cancel a subscription at any time to stop future charges instead.

What is the difference between Community and Verified prompts?

Community Prompts are submitted and voted on by users without formal review, while AIPRM Verified Prompts are tested, maintained, and guaranteed to work by the AIPRM team.

Can teams share prompts in AIPRM?

Yes, both free and paid plans support Teams functionality, letting businesses share curated prompt lists across everyone using the extension.
