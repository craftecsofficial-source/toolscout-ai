Reggie was researched as an AI customer-service assistant reportedly built for the plant hire and skip hire industry — trade businesses that take a high volume of phone and web enquiries for equipment and waste-skip bookings, availability, and pricing. In that framing, an AI agent named 'Reggie' would answer routine calls and chats, quote hire rates, check equipment or skip-size availability, and pass bookings through to depot staff, freeing human teams from repetitive scheduling calls. This is a plausible and underserved niche: skip and plant hire firms are typically small-to-mid-sized trade businesses with heavy phone-order volume and little in-house software, making them a reasonable target for a narrow, vertical AI agent.

However, this could not be confirmed during research. As of the research date, reggie.ai resolves directly to a GoDaddy 'domain for sale' listing (priced at $74,999 to buy outright or $2,500/month to lease), with no product, pricing page, or company information behind it. Web searches for 'Reggie' AI combined with plant hire, skip hire, or UK trade customer service did not surface an active company matching this description — only unrelated results such as a Jersey Financial Services Commission regulatory chatbot also named 'Reggie' and an unrelated sales-enablement platform called Regie.ai. No G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, or App Store listing could be found either. This entry should be treated as unverified pending a working URL or independent confirmation, since publishing unconfirmed pricing or features would risk misleading directory users.

  • The underlying concept — a vertical AI receptionist for plant and skip hire trade businesses — targets a real gap, since these businesses handle high call volumes with minimal existing software.
  • A narrowly scoped, industry-specific AI agent can plausibly outperform generic chatbots on domain vocabulary (skip sizes, plant categories, hire terms) if it exists and works as described.
  • Positioning as a phone-first tool (versus a web widget) matches how most plant/skip hire customers actually contact these businesses today.

Cons

  • The primary domain, reggie.ai, currently resolves to a GoDaddy resale listing rather than an active product, which is a strong signal the company is inactive, pre-launch, or has moved domains.
  • No independent evidence — press coverage, review site listings, social presence, or app store entry — could be found to confirm this product currently operates in the plant/skip hire space.
