MarketMuse suits content teams, agencies and publishers with big content libraries who want to decide what to write or update using site-specific topic data, and who accept a sales-led purchase. Skip it if you are a solo blogger or small site that wants self-serve, published pricing or a quick writing assistant. It audits the pages you already have, scores topic authority and difficulty against competitors, and produces briefs and strategy documents plus an Optimize editor that scores drafts. It does not write the article for you. Its public pricing page shows plan limits but no dollar amounts, and third-party reporting says Siteimprove acquired the company; the product site remains live and was still advertising a demo when checked.

Best for

Who it is for: mid-size and enterprise content teams, SEO agencies and publishers who need to audit and prioritize hundreds or thousands of pages. Who it is not for: solo creators or small businesses, anyone who needs published self-serve pricing, and anyone expecting an AI tool that drafts finished articles.

Key features

01

Content Inventory

An automatically maintained, page-by-page map of everything published on a site, scored against personalized metrics so teams can see what's underperforming or missing without a manual audit.

02

Personalized Difficulty & Topic Authority

Proprietary metrics that estimate how hard it would be for a specific site (not a generic domain) to rank for a topic, based on its existing content footprint and historical performance.

03

Competitive Advantage scoring

Compares a site's Personalized Difficulty against the topic's general difficulty to flag topics where the site has an edge over competitors.

04

Content Briefs

Auto-generated writing briefs across nine formats (article, listicle, how-to, comparison, FAQ collection, etc.) built from topic models of top-ranking competitive content.

05

Content Strategy Documents

Higher-level planning documents that roll up inventory and topic data into prioritized recommendations for content teams and stakeholders.

06

Optimize editor

A real-time content scoring workspace that compares a draft's topical coverage against a target score derived from competitive pages.

07

SERP X-Ray & Heatmap

Visualizes the structure and topical emphasis of top-ranking pages for a given query to reveal why they're ranking.

08

Site Heatmap & Connect

Visual, site-wide view of topical strength and weakness across a domain's content, integrated with CMS/analytics connections.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Personalized Difficulty is based on your own site's content footprint, not a generic domain score
  • Content inventory helps triage a large library without a manual audit
  • Higher tiers offer all nine brief types and strategy documents, shortening the research-to-writer handoff
  • Permanent free tier gives access to SERP X-Ray and Topic Navigator to test the approach

Cons

  • No dollar prices are published for any paid tier; budgeting requires a sales conversation
  • Each paid tier is capped on briefs, tracked topics and users (for example 5 briefs/month on Optimize)
  • Only one site inventory on every plan listed
  • Optimize and Research include only the Article brief type
  • Concepts like topic authority take time to learn; expect a ramp-up for non-SEO staff
  • I could not verify current customer ratings today, so none are cited

Frequently asked questions

How much does MarketMuse cost?

The pricing page shows no dollar amounts. The Free plan is $0 (10 queries/month); Optimize, Research and Strategy go through a demo request for a quote.

Does MarketMuse write articles for me?

No. It produces research, briefs and strategy documents and scores drafts in its Optimize editor; the writing is done by you or another tool.

Who owns MarketMuse?

Third-party coverage (e.g. Search Engine Journal) reports Siteimprove acquired it. The MarketMuse homepage itself does not mention a parent company.

What do I get on the Free plan?

One user and 10 queries per month with Topic Navigator, Keywords, SERP X-Ray and Heatmaps. No site inventory, tracked topics, briefs or strategy documents.

Is there a free trial of paid plans?

The pricing page has a 'Start my free trial' link, but I could not verify what a trial includes; ask sales.
