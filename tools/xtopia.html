XTOPIA is a reasonable pick for a small or mid-sized business in Malaysia or Southeast Asia that wants one AI agent covering WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram and phone calls, and that is happy to run a demo before buying. Skip it if you need transparent, published pricing, independent reviews to lean on, or a mature helpdesk with ticketing built in. XTOPIA is an agent platform from XIMNET, a Malaysian digital agency. You point an agent at your website and uploaded documents, and it answers customer questions from that content. Chat agents connect to WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger and Instagram, and a website agent goes live by pasting a code snippet. There are also voice agents and ready-made templates for restaurants, healthcare and wellness, hospitality, real estate, and HR and finance. Sign-up gives free credits, the Starter plan begins at RM 500, and extra credits, projects and users are bought as add-ons. Business and Enterprise customers get custom plans and integrations. The Microsoft Teams integration was listed as planned for May 2026; confirm its status before relying on it.

Best for

Owners and operations or customer-experience leads at small to mid-sized businesses, especially in hospitality, healthcare, real estate, education or retail in Southeast Asia, who want a WhatsApp and voice assistant without building one. Not for: teams wanting a full helpdesk with ticketing and reporting, buyers who need fixed published prices, or regulated teams that need audited performance data before signing.

Key features

01

Multi-channel messaging

One agent configuration handles WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Instagram DMs, and embeddable web chat.

02

Voice agent

Inbound and outbound phone call handling with CRM sync, used for booking confirmations, reminders, and lead calls.

03

Grounded RAG responses

Answers are retrieved from a business's own uploaded documents and structured data rather than generated from general model knowledge alone.

04

Pre-built role agents

Ready-made templates for HR support, hospitality concierge, and tutoring use cases that teams can customize.

05

Guardrails and escalation

PII masking, toxicity filtering, and human hand-off points for actions that shouldn't be fully automated.

06

Third-party integrations

Connectors for Google Workspace, Twilio, Shopify, Microsoft Teams, Zendesk, and Stripe, described as requiring minimal setup.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Covers WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram and voice from one platform, so a small team does not need a separate tool per channel.
  • Agents answer from your own website and uploaded documents, which should keep replies closer to your real policies than a generic bot.
  • Website install is a pasted snippet, so a non-technical owner can get a first agent live quickly.
  • Industry templates (restaurants, healthcare, hospitality, real estate, HR) give a starting point instead of a blank page.
  • Free sign-up credits let you test with your own content before committing to the RM 500 starter.

Cons

  • There is no pricing page. Only the RM 500 starting figure is public, and the billing period and what it includes are not clearly stated, so budgeting needs a sales conversation.
  • We could not find independent review coverage on G2, Capterra or Trustpilot, so claims about answer quality and support are unverified.
  • Answer quality depends on how clean your source content is; expect to spend time curating documents.
  • Presence and case studies are concentrated in Malaysia and Southeast Asia, so language coverage and local support elsewhere are unproven.
  • Some integrations are marked as planned (for example Microsoft Teams), so check what is actually shipped.

Frequently asked questions

Is XTOPIA free?

New sign-ups get free credits (amount not published). Ongoing use needs a paid plan starting at RM 500; the billing period is not stated on the site, so confirm it when you sign up.

Which channels does it support?

WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Instagram, a website widget and voice calls. Microsoft Teams was listed as planned for May 2026, so verify it before relying on it.

Do I need to code to set it up?

Not for the basics. You train the agent on your website and documents and paste a code snippet to add it to your site. Custom integrations are offered on Business and Enterprise plans.

Are there independent reviews?

We found none on the major review sites. Ask for a demo with your own content and request customer references.

Can it be customised for my industry?

The site lists agents for restaurants, healthcare and wellness, hospitality, real estate, and HR and finance, which you can adapt to your own content.
