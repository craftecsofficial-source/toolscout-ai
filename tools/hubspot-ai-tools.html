HubSpot's Breeze AI is a good fit for teams already on HubSpot that want agents working on their own CRM data. Teams not using HubSpot should skip it, since Breeze is not sold as a standalone product and the value comes from the data and Hubs behind it. It is a collection of AI agents and assistants spread across HubSpot's marketing, sales, service and data products, including a Campaign Agent, Content Agent, Nurture Agent, Prospecting Agent, Customer Agent, Data Agent and Revenue Agent, a hub for managing them, and a builder for custom agents. HubSpot says many of these run on HubSpot Credits, bought in packs or pay-as-you-go.

Best for

Good for small and mid-size businesses and sales or marketing teams already committed to HubSpot. Not for teams on another CRM, for anyone who wants a standalone AI writing or SEO tool, or for budget-sensitive buyers who need a fixed monthly cost, because Credits and onboarding fees add variable spend.

Key features

01

Agent Hub

A central place to see, activate and build agents across the platform. Included in Starter, Professional and Enterprise editions.

02

Marketing agents

Campaign, Content and Nurture agents for planning, creating and sending campaigns. On the Marketing Hub pricing page the Content Agent is listed as Beta on Professional and Enterprise; availability of the Nurture Agent per tier could not be confirmed.

03

Sales agents

A Prospecting Agent that watches buying signals and reaches out, and Smart Deal Progression that suggests next steps after calls.

04

Customer Agent

Resolves common customer inquiries across channels. Per-tier availability could not be confirmed.

05

Data and Revenue agents

The Data Agent answers questions about contacts and companies; the Revenue Agent automates invoice follow-up.

06

Agent Builder

Build custom agents on one canvas with your prompts, knowledge and CRM data. Custom agents consume Credits.

07

AEO (Beta) and SEO recommendations

Professional includes AEO (answer engine optimization) with 25 prompts and 2,500 answers per month, Enterprise 50 prompts and 5,000 answers. Standalone AEO is $50/mo ($45/mo annual) for 25 prompts across 3 answer engines.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Agents draw on your actual contacts, deals and marketing history, which generic AI writers cannot do.
  • Covers marketing, sales, service and data tasks in one system rather than several tools.
  • Real free tier and free standalone tools for trying things out.
  • Starter pricing is low per seat (from $7 on the current page) for small teams.

Cons

  • Credit-based usage makes monthly cost uncertain, and per-action credit costs were not published on the pages checked.
  • Professional and Enterprise require large one-time onboarding fees ($3,000 and $7,000) on top of the monthly price.
  • Several of the AI features, such as Content Agent and AEO, are labeled Beta.
  • Prices shown are for Marketing Hub; Sales, Service and other Hubs have their own pricing not verified here.
  • Which agents are available on which tier was not clear from the public pages.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Breeze AI without HubSpot?

No. It is built into HubSpot's Hubs rather than sold standalone, though HubSpot offers some free standalone AI tools like AEO Grader.

What are HubSpot Credits?

A usage currency that many AI features and custom agents spend. They cost $0.01 each, and paid Marketing Hub editions include a monthly allotment that resets without rollover.

Is there a free option?

Yes. Marketing Hub Free has $0 pricing for up to 2 users with basic features. Which Breeze features are free was not specified.

What does Marketing Hub Professional really cost?

From $800/mo (list $890) plus a one-time $3,000 onboarding fee, per the pricing page checked today.
