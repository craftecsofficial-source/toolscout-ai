HARPA AI is a Chrome browser extension, built by HARPA AI Technologies (based in Finland), that embeds AI models directly into the browsing experience. Triggered with a hotkey (Alt+A) or by typing "//" on any page, it opens a sidebar that can answer questions about the page you're on, summarize long YouTube videos and PDFs, draft emails and social posts, and run any of 100+ pre-built automation commands. Rather than positioning itself as just another chatbot wrapper, HARPA frames itself as an "AI automation agent": it can scrape structured data from pages into CSV or JSON, monitor pages or prices for changes and alert the user, fill out forms, and trigger external workflows in Zapier, Make.com, or n8n.

HARPA differentiates itself by bundling access to multiple leading models — GPT-5.x, Claude Sonnet, Gemini Pro, Grok, DeepSeek, Perplexity and Llama — through either its own Cloud GPT connection or free web-session connections to ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, all under one subscription. It markets this as replacing over $140/month in separate AI subscriptions for a $12–19/month plan. The extension has built a large user base (roughly 300,000–500,000 users cited across its own materials) and holds a strong 4.7 out of 5 rating from about 3,200 reviewers on the Chrome Web Store, having also been recognized among Google's favorite Chrome extensions of 2025. Its privacy design reads pages only on explicit user action and does not store server-side logs, positioning it for users who want AI automation without their browsing data flowing to a third party's servers.

Best for

HARPA AI is best suited to power users, marketers, researchers and small teams who live in the browser and want to automate repetitive web tasks — scraping, monitoring, summarizing, drafting — without paying for multiple separate AI subscriptions. It's a weaker fit for users who just want a simple standalone chatbot, or for teams needing enterprise-grade support and refund guarantees, since HARPA's support is primarily self-serve (Discord, email) and purchases are final.

HARPA Grid API

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Bundles seven-plus premium AI subscriptions (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Llama, Perplexity, DeepSeek) into one $12–19/month plan, which HARPA's own pricing page frames as replacing over $140/month of separate subscriptions.
  • Goes well beyond chat: page monitors, scrapers, and webhook triggers to Zapier/Make/n8n make it a genuine browser automation agent, not just an AI sidebar.
  • Highly rated on the Chrome Web Store — 4.7 out of 5 across roughly 3,200 ratings — and named one of Google's favorite Chrome extensions of 2025.
  • Privacy-forward design: reads pages only on explicit user action, does not store server-side logs, and cloud model calls aren't used for training.
  • The "for Life" lifetime plan lets users bring their own API keys and use web session connections indefinitely for a single one-time payment, appealing to cost-conscious long-term users.

Cons

  • The sheer volume of features (commands, monitors, modes, model connections) creates a real learning curve; several reviewers describe the interface as feeling dated or confusing at first.
  • HARPA offers no refunds on any purchase — its stated policy is to rely on the free DEMO tier for evaluation instead, which is less forgiving than competitors with 14–30 day refund windows.
  • Billing in "Megatokens" is more transparent than an opaque credit system but still requires users to estimate consumption per task type, since page-aware commands consume more tokens than plain chat messages.
  • Independent review volume on major B2B platforms like G2 is very thin (essentially a single review), so buyers have limited peer validation beyond the Chrome Web Store.

Frequently asked questions

Is HARPA AI free?

Yes, HARPA offers a free DEMO plan with up to 10 web-session chat messages per day and 100 total AI command runs; paid plans remove those daily limits and add Cloud AI model access.

What AI models does HARPA connect to?

HARPA connects to GPT-5.x, Claude Sonnet, Gemini Pro, DeepSeek, Perplexity, Grok and Llama, either through its own Cloud GPT connection or through free web-session connections to ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini.

What is a Megatoken?

A Megatoken is 1 million input tokens (roughly 750,000 words) consumed on HARPA's Cloud AI connection; output tokens cost about 3x input tokens, and premium models consume more tokens per request than efficient models.

Does HARPA AI offer refunds?

No, HARPA does not offer refunds; instead it provides a free demo period on every paid feature so users can evaluate before purchasing.

Can HARPA automate tasks beyond chatting?

Yes, HARPA can scrape and export web data, monitor pages or prices for changes, fill forms, and trigger Zapier, Make.com or n8n webhooks in addition to answering page-aware questions.

What's the difference between the "for Pros" and "for Life" plans?

"for Pros" is a $19/month subscription with 225 Megatokens per year, scheduled commands and advanced automation; "for Life" is a single $240 payment that gives lifetime access to the app itself (bring your own API keys or use free web-session connections), with Cloud AI tokens purchasable separately at a discount.
