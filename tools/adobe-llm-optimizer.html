Adobe Brand Visibility (the product formerly called Adobe LLM Optimizer) suits large enterprises that already run web content on Adobe Experience Manager or a major CDN and want fixes shipped, not just reported. Small teams, agencies and anyone on a non-Adobe stack should skip it: there is no public price and the purchase runs through a sales demo.

Adobe's page describes tracking of mentions, citations, sentiment and share of voice across ten LLM families, with benchmarking against up to five competitors. It layers Semrush search data on top to find gaps, can apply technical fixes at the CDN edge (Fastly, Akamai or Cloudflare) or hand recommendations to Experience Manager Sites, and then measures outcomes against pre-launch baselines in Adobe Analytics and Customer Journey Analytics.

Best for

Fits enterprise marketing, SEO and web teams on Adobe Experience Cloud who need AI-visibility work tied to analytics and revenue reporting. It is NOT for small businesses, freelancers or agencies wanting a cheap monthly tracker, nor for teams on non-Adobe stacks that only need monitoring; self-serve trackers cost far less.

Key features

01

Auto-identify, auto-suggest, auto-optimize

A three-stage workflow that first detects visibility gaps and crawler issues, then proposes fixes, then implements approved changes automatically.

02

CDN-edge deployment

Serves AI-optimized versions of pages directly to AI crawlers at the CDN layer, bypassing the normal CMS publish cycle, with one-click rollback.

03

Cross-engine share-of-voice tracking

Monitors brand presence and citation share across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, and Microsoft Copilot using a database of nearly 300 million AI search prompts.

04

Agentic crawler analytics

Reads CDN logs to show exactly when and how AI crawlers and agents access a site's content, distinct from simply reporting on rankings.

05

Content gap and topic cluster mapping

Identifies where a brand's SEO authority should be earning AI citations but isn't, and prioritizes content briefs by projected topic-cluster impact.

06

Off-site authority generation

Surfaces opportunities on Wikipedia, YouTube, Reddit, and earned media to build the third-party citation authority that AI engines pull from.

07

Business Impact / revenue attribution

Connects AI-driven visibility and traffic changes to Adobe Analytics and Customer Journey Analytics so teams can prove GEO work moved real business outcomes.

08

Cross-team workspace

Unifies SEO, PR, social, and affiliate teams around shared AI-visibility priorities, progress tracking, and automated issue routing.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Closes the loop: it can unblock crawlers, add structured data and fix 404s at the CDN edge, with rollback, rather than leaving that to a ticket queue.
  • Impact is measured natively in Adobe Analytics and Customer Journey Analytics against pre-launch baselines, which helps justify spend internally.
  • Semrush data (Adobe's page cites 2.18B observed AI responses) gives market context beyond your own tracked prompts.
  • Supports 25+ languages with state and city breakdowns, useful for multi-market brands.

Cons

  • No price is published; budget is only learned after a sales conversation. Figures seen elsewhere (annual minimums, six-figure estimates) are unofficial and unverified.
  • Value drops sharply if you are not on Adobe Experience Manager or Adobe Analytics, since the integrated fix-and-measure loop is the main selling point.
  • Competitor benchmarking is capped at five competitors per the product page.
  • Product is mid-rebrand, so documentation, screenshots and third-party articles may still use the old LLM Optimizer name.
  • I found no verifiable independent review data (G2/Capterra) today, so peer validation is thin.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Adobe Brand Visibility (LLM Optimizer) cost?

Adobe does not publish pricing; the product page only offers 'Get started' and 'Request a demo'. Quotes come through sales, so treat any third-party figure as unofficial.

Is Adobe LLM Optimizer the same as Adobe Brand Visibility?

Yes. Adobe's page now reads 'Adobe Brand Visibility (Formerly Adobe LLM Optimizer)'.

Do I need Adobe Experience Manager?

Not strictly. Fixes can go to a CDN edge (Fastly, Akamai, Cloudflare) or to Experience Manager Sites, but the revenue measurement relies on Adobe Analytics or Customer Journey Analytics.

Which AI engines does it cover?

Adobe lists ten LLM families, including ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Claude and Copilot. Confirm the full list in a demo.

Is it suitable for a small team?

Probably not. It is sales-led and aimed at enterprises; self-serve trackers are a better match for small budgets.
