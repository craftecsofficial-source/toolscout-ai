Reimagine Home AI is a good fit for homeowners, agents and stagers who want fast visual ideas or staged listing photos; skip it if you need measured, buildable plans or a licensed designer's judgment. It takes a photo of a room or exterior and a text description, then generates a redesign that keeps the existing layout, which you can refine in steps.

Beyond full redesigns it offers decluttering, object removal, furniture and paint changes, landscaping, sky replacement, upscaling, and a batch mode for up to 50 listing photos. On higher tiers the designs can include real retailer products you can buy from the retailer's site. We could not verify per-plan dollar prices, only the credit allowances.

Best for

Homeowners and renters planning a refresh, and real estate agents, stagers and landscape designers who need quick visuals. Not for architects or contractors needing accurate drawings or structural feasibility, and not for anyone who wants unlimited free experimentation, since only 5 free designs are offered.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • No design or 3D skills needed; one photo and a description is enough.
  • Edits can be incremental on Pro, so you do not restart after every tweak.
  • One tool covers interiors, exteriors, staging and photo cleanup.
  • Batch mode of up to 50 photos suits listing workflows.
  • Plain credit rules: a design costs 1 credit, with real products 2, and chatting about ideas is free.

Cons

  • Credits run down with every regeneration, so iterating a lot gets costly.
  • The entry plan withholds conversational editing, reference photos, real products and high-resolution quality.
  • Generated rooms are not dimensionally accurate; verify sizes and structure with a pro.
  • Per-plan dollar prices were not shown on the page we read; only a $14-$99/mo range in metadata, so confirm before buying.
  • Results vary run to run and may need several attempts.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes, 5 free designs with no credit card, using the same AI as Pro according to the site.

How do credits work?

A design or visualization costs 1 credit, one with real products costs 2, and conversation or idea exploration costs 0.

What do the plans include?

Essential gives 30 credits a month, Pro 200, Advanced 400 and Agency 900. Pro adds conversational editing, reference photos and batch processing.

Can it do exteriors and landscaping?

Yes, including lawns, hardscaping, pools, sky replacement and day-to-dusk conversion.

Can I rely on it for renovation measurements?

No. Treat output as inspiration and confirm dimensions and structure with a professional.
