DiffusionBee is a free macOS application, created by developer Divam Gupta, that packages Stable Diffusion into a simple, installable desktop app so users don't need Python, command-line tools, or a cloud API key to generate AI art. Launched in 2022, it was covered by TechCrunch, Fast Company, Digital Trends, and AppleInsider as one of the first tools to make local Stable Diffusion approachable for everyday Mac users. Everything runs on-device: prompts, trained models, and generated images never leave the user's computer, which distinguishes it from cloud-based generators like Midjourney or DALL-E that require an internet connection and typically charge per generation or subscription.

The app has grown from a basic text-to-image tool into a fuller creative suite, adding image-to-image transformation, generative fill/inpainting, an AI upscaler, image variation generation, custom model training on personal datasets, structured/control-guided image generation, an illusion generator, and even basic AI video and animation tools. Its core pitch is speed and simplicity paired with total ownership: because generation happens locally, there are no per-image costs and no data sent to a third party. Performance depends on hardware, generating an image in roughly 30 seconds on an 8GB M1 MacBook Air and faster on higher-spec Apple Silicon machines; it also runs on Intel Macs, though considerably slower without a dedicated GPU. DiffusionBee requires macOS 13.1 or later and is distributed as a free download with no account, credit card, or subscription required, funded instead through community goodwill, its GitHub open-source presence, and an active Discord community.

Best for

DiffusionBee is ideal for Mac-owning hobbyists, artists, and privacy-conscious users who want free, unlimited local AI art generation without technical setup or ongoing costs. It's a weak fit for Windows/Linux users, teams needing cloud collaboration, or power users who want the broader model and extension ecosystem available in open frameworks like ComfyUI.

Local Text-to-Image Generation

03

Generative Fill / Inpainting

Lets users select a region of an image to add, remove, or replace objects while keeping the rest of the image intact.

04

Custom Model Training

Trains personalized image generation models on a user's own photo dataset, entirely offline, to reproduce specific people, objects, or styles.

05

AI Upscaler

Increases image resolution and sharpness using AI-based upscaling rather than simple interpolation.

06

Control Images / Structured Generation

Generates images that follow a specific structure or pose defined by a reference control image.

07

Image Variants

Produces multiple stylistic variations of a single generated or uploaded image.

08

Illusion & Video Tools

Includes an illusion generator for hidden-pattern art and basic AI animation/video generation tools.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Because generation runs on-device, there is no recurring subscription or per-image fee, which press coverage from TechCrunch and Fast Company highlighted as a key differentiator at launch.
  • Full data privacy: prompts, training images, and outputs never leave the local machine, appealing to users generating sensitive or personal content.
  • Custom model training lets users fine-tune on their own photos for personalized styles or subjects without needing cloud GPU rental or coding knowledge.
  • The one-click macOS installer removes the technical barrier that command-line Stable Diffusion setups (like Automatic1111) present to non-developers.
  • Active open-source GitHub repository and Discord community provide ongoing updates, troubleshooting help, and a responsive developer.

Cons

  • Available only on macOS, leaving Windows and Linux users without an official native equivalent.
  • Generation speed is tied directly to Apple Silicon performance; users on base-spec or Intel Macs report significantly slower render times.
  • Being a solo-maintained free app, it lacks the extensive plugin/extension ecosystem, custom checkpoint marketplace, and community model sharing found in tools like ComfyUI or Automatic1111.
  • No official cloud sync or mobile companion app, so work is tied to a single machine.

Frequently asked questions

Is DiffusionBee really free?

Yes, DiffusionBee is completely free to download and use with no subscription, credits, or account required.

Does DiffusionBee require an internet connection?

No, once installed, all image generation runs 100% locally on your Mac; nothing is sent to the cloud.

Does DiffusionBee work on Windows?

No, DiffusionBee is currently macOS-only, requiring macOS 13.1 or higher, with Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) recommended for best performance.

How fast does it generate images?

On an 8GB M1 MacBook Air, DiffusionBee generates an image in roughly 30 seconds; higher-spec Apple Silicon Macs are significantly faster.

Can I use the generated images commercially?

Yes, generated images can be used freely as long as usage complies with the CreativeML Open RAIL-M license that governs the underlying Stable Diffusion model.

Can I train custom models in DiffusionBee?

Yes, DiffusionBee lets users train custom image generation models on their own image datasets, entirely locally, with just a few clicks.
