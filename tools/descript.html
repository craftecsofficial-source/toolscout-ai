Descript is an all-in-one audio and video editing platform, best known for pioneering text-based editing — where cutting a word in the transcript cuts the corresponding audio or video. The company says over 6 million creators and teams use it for podcasts, YouTube videos, screen recordings and marketing video production.

Its core differentiator versus traditional timeline editors (Premiere Pro, Final Cut) and single-purpose AI voice tools (ElevenLabs, Murf) is editing video and audio like a word-processor document: delete text in the transcript and the matching clip disappears. Descript layers AI features on top of that text-first workflow — Studio Sound, Remove Filler Words, Eye Contact, AI Speech voice cloning, and an AI co-editor called Underlord — positioning it as a creator-friendly middle ground between professional NLEs and pure voice-generation apps.

Best for

Descript fits podcasters, YouTubers, course creators and marketing teams who want fast, transcript-driven editing plus built-in AI cleanup tools without hiring a dedicated video editor. Teams that need frame-accurate color grading, complex multicam work, or pixel-perfect export control for broadcast or film delivery should look at a traditional NLE like Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve instead.

Key features

01

Text-based editing

Edit video or audio by editing the transcript; deleting a word removes the matching clip from the timeline.

02

Underlord AI co-editor

A prompt-driven AI assistant that can execute edits, generate clips, write show notes and handle other repetitive editing tasks on request.

03

Studio Sound

One-click audio cleanup that removes background noise and enhances speech to near-studio quality without external gear.

04

Eye Contact & Green Screen

AI subtly adjusts gaze to simulate eye contact with the camera, and removes video backgrounds without a physical green screen.

05

Remove Filler Words & Regenerate Speech

Automatically strips filler words and repeated words, and can regenerate or patch small sections of speech using AI voice matching.

06

AI Speech (voice cloning & stock voices)

Create a custom AI clone of your own voice or choose from 25+ (up to 60+ on higher tiers) stock voices for text-to-speech narration.

07

Rooms

Remote multitrack recording for podcasts and video calls with up to 10 participants, automatic cloud backup and auto-import into the editor.

08

Translation & dubbing

Translate captions into up to 61 languages and dub audio/video into up to 30 languages, with translation proofreading on Business and Enterprise.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Text-based editing genuinely speeds up rough cuts for talking-head and podcast content — the platform's signature time-saver
  • Underlord AI co-editor plus 20+ built-in AI tools consolidate several point solutions into one app
  • Free plan is a real, usable starting point for text-based editing and basic AI tools
  • Strong Capterra scores for ease of use, especially on shorter, simpler projects like podcasts and training videos
  • Rooms makes high-quality remote multitrack recording straightforward for distributed teams
  • Broad translation and dubbing language support on higher tiers

Cons

  • Users consistently report the app can be slow, laggy or prone to crashing, especially on longer or higher-resolution projects
  • AI credits are consumed quickly by frequent use of Underlord and other AI tools, and running out mid-project is a common frustration
  • Customer support response times draw complaints, with some users reporting multi-day waits for bug replies
  • Export compression and limited control over export settings can produce a noticeable quality drop for professional delivery
  • Not a full replacement for professional NLEs for complex transitions, b-roll and effects work
  • Media-hour and storage limits on lower tiers fill up quickly for video-heavy workflows

What reviewers say

Descript rates well on both G2 and Capterra, with users praising the text-based editing workflow and how much time it saves on straightforward projects. The most consistent complaint pattern, echoed across G2, Trustpilot and Reddit, is reliability — slowness, lag and crashes on longer or heavier video projects — plus frustration that AI credits get used up faster than expected and that support can be slow to resolve blocking bugs.

Frequently asked questions

Is Descript free to use?

Yes, there's a genuine Free plan with 60 minutes of media per month, 100 one-time AI credits, and 720p watermarked export — no credit card is needed to start.

How much does Descript cost?

Paid plans start at $24/mo (Hobbyist, or $16/mo billed annually) and go up to $65/mo (Business, or $50/mo billed annually) for a 5-seat team; Enterprise pricing is custom.

What are Descript's 'AI credits' and 'media hours'?

Media hours track how much audio/video you upload or record in Descript each billing period, while AI credits track usage of AI features like Underlord, Studio Sound, Green Screen and AI-generated media.

Is Descript good enough to replace Premiere Pro or Final Cut?

For talking-head YouTube videos, podcast repurposing and simple clips, many users find it sufficient; for full productions with complex transitions and effects, reviewers generally recommend sticking with a traditional NLE.

Does Descript offer voice cloning like ElevenLabs?

Yes, through its AI Speech feature you can create a custom clone of your own voice, though it's positioned as a supporting feature for patching and regenerating dialogue rather than Descript's primary use case.

Why do some reviews mention Descript being slow?

A recurring complaint across G2, Capterra and Reddit is that the app can lag or crash on longer or higher-resolution projects; this is one of the most cited downsides alongside AI credit limits.
