Looka is an AI-powered logo and branding tool that walks users through a short questionnaire about their business name, industry, and style preferences, then generates dozens of logo concepts to choose from and refine. Once a logo is selected, users can adjust colors, fonts, icons, and layout in a live editor before purchasing final files.

Beyond the logo maker, Looka offers a Brand Kit subscription that extends the generated identity across business cards, letterheads, social media graphics, email signatures, and a basic website builder, aiming to give small businesses a cohesive look without hiring a design agency.

Looka has positioned itself as one of the more established players in the AI logo space, with a large library of icons and templates and a straightforward one-time-purchase model alongside optional recurring plans for ongoing brand asset needs.

Best for

Looka is best suited to early-stage founders, freelancers, and small local businesses who need a clean, professional logo fast and affordably. It also appeals to non-designers who want a consistent brand look across business cards and social posts without learning design software. Agencies or brands wanting a highly original, custom mark should consider a human designer instead.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Delivers a usable, professional logo in under 10 minutes for far less than a freelance designer would charge
  • The style-preference quiz does a reasonably good job narrowing results to relevant concepts
  • Brand Kit extends value beyond the logo into everyday marketing materials
  • No design experience needed thanks to the guided, template-driven editor

Cons

  • Buying vector files and full brand kits together can approach the cost of some freelance logo packages
  • Because it draws from template libraries, similar icon-and-font combinations can appear across different users' logos
  • Customization is bounded by Looka's editor; it can't produce truly original illustration work
  • The Brand Kit subscription is a recurring cost on top of the one-time logo purchase

What reviewers say

Users on G2 and Trustpilot generally praise Looka for speed, affordability, and ease of use for non-designers, while some note the recurring Brand Kit costs and occasional overlap in generated designs.

Frequently praised

  • Customer support response times can be slow for billing issues

Frequently asked questions

Do I own the rights to my Looka logo?

Yes, once purchased, Looka grants a broad license to use the logo commercially, though exclusivity isn't guaranteed since similar elements may be used elsewhere.

Can I edit my logo after purchase?

Yes, purchased logos remain editable in Looka's dashboard, and users can regenerate variations.

Does Looka offer refunds?

Looka has a limited refund policy; it's best to review current terms before purchasing since final digital files are typically non-refundable once downloaded.

Is there a free version?

Users can generate and preview logos for free, but downloading usable files requires a paid package.

What file formats do I get?

Depending on the tier, files range from basic PNG/JPG up to vector SVG, EPS, and PDF formats suitable for print.

Can Looka build a full website?

It includes a basic website builder aimed at simple branded sites, not a full-featured CMS or e-commerce platform.
