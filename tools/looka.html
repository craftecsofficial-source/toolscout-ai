Looka is a good pick if you are a non-designer who needs a serviceable logo and basic brand assets this week, and want to see options before paying. Skip it if you need a distinctive, original mark or a trademark-grade identity, since its logos are assembled from a shared pool of icons and fonts.

You answer a short quiz on name, industry, style and colors, and Looka generates logo options you can tweak in an editor. Nothing is charged until you download. The Basic package gives a single low-res PNG; Premium adds high-res PNG, SVG, EPS and PDF files, transparent backgrounds and color variations.

A separate annual Brand Kit subscription applies your logo to 300+ templates such as business cards, invoices, email signatures and social profiles, and keeps unlimited logo changes available. A Web tier adds an AI-generated website. Looka was previously called Logojoy.

Best for

Early-stage founders, freelancers and small local businesses that need a clean logo and everyday stationery quickly and cheaply. Not for: agencies or brands wanting an original illustrated mark, anyone who needs a trademark-ready unique identity, or teams needing a full design system.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • You can generate, edit and preview logos for free, so you only pay once you like one
  • Premium includes vector formats (SVG, EPS, PDF), which printers and sign makers usually require
  • Brand Kit covers many routine needs (invoices, email signatures, business cards, social profiles) from one logo
  • The guided quiz means no design skill is needed

Cons

  • The Basic package is a single low-res PNG, so most buyers end up needing Premium
  • Brand Kit is billed annually; the cost keeps recurring if you want ongoing template access
  • Shared icon and font libraries mean similar-looking logos can appear for other businesses; exclusivity and trademarkability are not guaranteed
  • Dollar prices were not visible on the official pricing page in our check, and no refund policy was listed there, so read the terms before paying
  • The AI website is a convenience add-on, not a replacement for a full site builder

Frequently asked questions

Is Looka free?

Creating and previewing logos is free. You pay when you want to download files, via a one-time logo package, or an annual Brand Kit subscription.

What is the difference between the logo packages?

Per the official pricing page, Basic gives one low-res PNG. Premium adds high-res PNG, EPS, SVG and PDF files, transparent and color variations, and unlimited post-purchase changes.

What does Brand Kit add?

An annual subscription with 300+ templates (social profiles and posts, business cards, email signatures, letterheads, invoices, brand guidelines) and unlimited logo changes. The Web tier adds a website.

Can I get a unique, trademarkable logo?

Looka logos are assembled from shared icons and fonts, so uniqueness is not guaranteed. If trademarking matters, check with a lawyer or hire a designer.
