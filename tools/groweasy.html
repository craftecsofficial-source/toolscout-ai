GrowEasy is worth a look for small and mid-size Indian businesses that want lead ads, WhatsApp follow-up and a CRM from a single vendor, and a poor fit for companies outside India or those that already run a mature CRM and want only one piece. It combines ad management, a CRM, WhatsApp Business API campaigns, calling and AI voice agents; the pricing page lists four monthly plans from ₹999 to ₹79,999. On top of the subscription there is a managed-marketing fee of 5-20% of ad spend, per-minute voice AI charges and a telephony add-on, so the real monthly bill depends heavily on how you use it.

Best for

Small and mid-size businesses in India, particularly real estate, clinics, dealerships and local services, that run lead-generation ads and follow up on WhatsApp and phone, and prefer one vendor. NOT for: businesses outside India, teams that want a flat, fully predictable bill, or companies with an established CRM that only need one capability.

Key features

01

Lead-generation ad management

Runs lead ads and tracks performance; the two lower tiers use platform-managed ad accounts, the third and fourth tiers allow your own or platform-managed accounts.

02

WhatsApp Business API

Campaigns and automated follow-ups on every plan; WhatsApp lead qualification starts on the second tier.

03

CRM

Lead management with AI lead summaries and automatic updates. The pricing page lists no CRM record limits.

04

Telephony

Inbuilt calling from the CRM with recordings, transcripts and AI summaries; priced as an add-on on all plans (TalkEasy is the related calling app).

05

AI voice agents

Calls and qualifies leads; available from the second tier at ₹8, ₹7 or ₹6 per minute depending on plan.

06

Conversion API

Included on all plans, to feed lead-quality signals back to ad platforms.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Covers lead ads, WhatsApp, CRM and calling in one product, avoiding several vendor contracts.
  • The agency fee drops from 20% to 5% as you move up plans, which rewards larger ad budgets.
  • Unlimited users on every plan avoids per-seat charges.
  • Rupee pricing and a stated setup time under 7 days with free migration and training (per the vendor) suit Indian SMBs.

Cons

  • Cost is multi-part: subscription, 5-20% agency fee on ad spend, per-minute voice AI, and a telephony add-on, so budget carefully.
  • Plan 1 is limited: ₹30,000 ad-spend cap, platform-managed ad accounts only, no Voice AI or WhatsApp lead qualification.
  • The price jump between plans is steep (₹999 to ₹9,999 to ₹39,999 to ₹79,999).
  • The pricing page shows no CRM record limits or plan names, so confirm both before buying.
  • No G2 or Capterra rating was found; adoption claims such as 2,000+ businesses are the company's own.

Frequently asked questions

How much does GrowEasy cost?

Four monthly plans: ₹999, ₹9,999, ₹39,999 and ₹79,999, plus an agency fee of 20%, 15%, 10% or 5% of ad spend.

Is there a free plan?

None is listed; the ₹999 plan is the cheapest tier.

What does Voice AI cost?

It is not available on the ₹999 plan; from the ₹9,999 plan it is billed per minute at ₹8, ₹7 and ₹6 on the higher tiers.

Do I need my own ad accounts?

The two lower plans use platform-managed ad accounts; the third and fourth plans allow self or platform-managed.

Is telephony included?

No, telephony is listed as an add-on on every plan. The related TalkEasy app is the calling product.
