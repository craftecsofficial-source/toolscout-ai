GrowEasy positions itself as an AI-powered lead generation and automation platform for the Indian SMB market, reporting over 2,000 businesses on the platform. Rather than selling ad management, WhatsApp marketing, calling and a CRM as separate products, GrowEasy bundles them into tiered plans so a business can run its whole lead-to-conversion funnel from one dashboard - a lead comes in through an ad, is logged in GrowEasy's CRM, followed up over WhatsApp Business API, and, on higher tiers, called and summarized by an AI voice agent.

The product's calling layer runs under its own brand, TalkEasy (talk.groweasy.ai), which can be used as part of a GrowEasy subscription or evaluated as a standalone virtual-number and AI-calling product. GrowEasy's pricing is structured around the Indian market specifically, with plans quoted in rupees and a managed-marketing fee that scales down as a business's ad spend and plan tier increase.

Best for

GrowEasy is built for small and mid-size businesses in India - particularly real estate, clinics, dealerships and local-service businesses - that want lead generation, WhatsApp follow-up, AI calling and a CRM without hiring a martech team or integrating several separate SaaS tools. It's a weaker fit for businesses outside India (pricing and WhatsApp API positioning are India-specific) or teams that already have a mature CRM and just need one point solution.

Key features

01

Lead Generation Ad Management

Runs and manages lead-generation ad campaigns, on GrowEasy's own ad accounts (Starter) or a business's connected accounts (Professional and up).

02

WhatsApp Business API Marketing

Sends WhatsApp campaigns and automated follow-up messages through the official WhatsApp Business API, included on every plan.

03

Built-in CRM

Tracks and manages lead records in one place, scaling from 1,000 records on Starter up to 500,000 on Enterprise.

04

AI Voice Agents

Automates lead follow-up calls and qualification with AI agents, available from the Professional plan upward.

05

Telephony / TalkEasy Add-on

Adds a virtual business number, call handling and call analytics through GrowEasy's TalkEasy product, available as an add-on across plans.

06

AI UGC Video Ads

Generates UGC-style ad video content (billed per video) for use in ad campaigns, available on Professional and Enterprise plans.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Consolidates the full lead-to-conversion loop - ads, CRM, WhatsApp, calling - into one platform and one bill, which is attractive for SMBs that don't want to manage several vendor relationships
  • Managed ad-spend fee scales down from 20% at Starter to 5% at Enterprise, so the platform gets proportionally cheaper as a business grows its ad budget
  • Unlimited users on every tier removes a common SaaS friction point (per-seat pricing) for growing sales teams
  • Rupee-denominated pricing and India-specific positioning (real estate, clinics, dealerships) make it easier to budget against than dollar-priced global tools

Cons

  • The Starter plan is fairly limited - no AI agents, no UGC videos, and a 1,000-record CRM cap - so many businesses will need Professional to get the AI features that differentiate GrowEasy
  • The ad-spend-based managed marketing fee (5-20%) is a variable cost on top of the flat subscription and needs separate budgeting versus flat-fee competitors
  • GrowEasy shows no filed reviews yet on SourceForge and no independent G2/Capterra rating volume, so third-party validation is limited
  • Customization is only available as a paid add-on, and only from the Enterprise tier

Frequently asked questions

What does GrowEasy actually do?

It's an all-in-one platform for lead-generation ads, WhatsApp Business API marketing, AI voice calling and a CRM, aimed at Indian SMBs.

How much does GrowEasy cost?

Plans run from ₹999/month (Starter) to ₹99,999/month (Enterprise), plus a managed-marketing fee of 5-20% of ad spend depending on tier.

Is there a free plan?

No free plan is listed; the Starter plan is the entry-level paid tier.

What is TalkEasy and how does it relate to GrowEasy?

TalkEasy (talk.groweasy.ai) is GrowEasy's dedicated AI calling and virtual-number product, offered as part of GrowEasy or usable on its own.

Do I need my own ad accounts?

On the Starter plan, campaigns run through GrowEasy's own ad accounts; from Professional upward, you can connect your own ad accounts.

How many businesses use GrowEasy?

The company reports over 2,000 businesses across India use the platform.
