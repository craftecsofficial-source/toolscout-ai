VoiceDub is a good pick for hobbyists and social creators who want quick AI song covers, duets and cloned-voice clips in the browser, and its free vocal remover and vocoder are useful on their own. Skip it if you need licensed, professional-grade voices for commercial work or dependable output quality per credit, since each generation uses a credit and some users report poor value. You upload a song, it separates the vocals, converts them to one of 10,000+ user-created voices and remixes over the original backing. It also offers duets with 2-4 voices, voice cloning from a roughly 30-second sample, text-to-speech, and a free vocal remover and vocoder. Many voices are user-created and some imitate real artists, so check the rights and platform rules before you publish anything.

Best for

Music hobbyists, meme and social-video creators and Discord communities who want fast AI covers, duets and voice clips without a DAW. Not for businesses needing licensed voices or professional dubbing and localization, for artists who must clear rights on a voice before releasing, or for anyone who wants guaranteed quality per generation, since credits are spent on every attempt.

Key features

01

AI Voice Covers

Upload a song, choose a voice, and VoiceDub renders a polished AI cover with the original vocals replaced, directly in the browser.

02

AI Duets

Cast 2 to 4 different AI voices on a single track, letting creators mix who 'sings' which part of the song.

03

10,000+ Voice Library

A large, user-created catalog of AI voices spanning celebrities, characters and original creator voices.

04

v2 Instant Voice Cloning

Creates a reusable cloned voice from user-submitted audio samples, usable across covers, duets and text-to-speech.

05

Text-to-Speech

Converts written scripts into spoken audio using the same voice library, useful for narration, tags or quick concept testing.

06

Free Vocal Remover

Splits any uploaded track into isolated vocal and instrumental stems at no cost, independent of subscription tier.

07

Free Vocoder

Pushes a song through a video so an on-screen character appears to lip-sync/perform the track, offered free forever.

08

Custom AI Model Training

On the PRO plan, users can work with support to train a fully custom AI voice model beyond the standard cloning flow.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The vocal remover and vocoder are listed as free with no plan required, so there is real utility before paying.
  • The 10,000+ voice library, built from user-created voices, offers far more variety than a single-model tool.
  • Duets with 2-4 voices and a mix editor to reassign sections are a distinctive feature for covers.
  • A $2.99 weekly Basic plan lets you try paid features without a monthly commitment.
  • Plan limits are stated clearly in dubs and cloned voices per week or month.

Cons

  • Basic is capped at 5 dubs and 1 cloned voice per week, which is quickly limiting for regular use.
  • The pricing page shows two prices for Influencer and PRO (a discounted intro price and a higher list price), and the intro price appears to apply to the first month only, so the ongoing cost is easy to misread.
  • Annual prices are not displayed even though a ~50% discount is advertised.
  • Voices are user-uploaded and often imitate real artists, which creates copyright and platform-policy risk for anything you publish.
  • I did not verify third-party review scores today, so treat satisfaction claims on the vendor's own site cautiously.

Frequently asked questions

Is VoiceDub free?

The vocal remover and vocoder are listed as free. Voice covers, duets and cloning need a paid plan, starting at $2.99 billed weekly.

What will Influencer and PRO actually cost after the intro price?

The pricing page shows $4.99 (Influencer) and $49.99 (PRO) as intro prices and $24.99 and $99.99 in its comparison table. The intro prices appear to be first-month offers; confirm the renewal price at checkout.

How do I clone my own voice?

Create a voice profile from a short sample (about 30 seconds per the site). Cloned-voice allowances are 1 per week on Basic, 20 per month on Influencer and 200 per month on PRO.

What is an AI Duet?

A cover that casts 2-4 AI voices on one song, with a mix editor to assign which voice sings which section.

Can I use covers commercially?

This was not verified. Many voices imitate real artists, so check VoiceDub's terms and the rights to the song and voice before releasing anything.
