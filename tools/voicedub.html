VoiceDub is an AI voice studio focused on music and voice-content creators rather than enterprise or professional dubbing workflows. Its core product lets users upload a song, pick from over 10,000 user-created AI voices, and render an AI cover with the vocals replaced — similar in spirit to tools like Kits.AI or Voicify. Beyond single-voice covers, VoiceDub supports 'AI Duets' that cast two to four voices across one track, instant voice cloning (v2) from a short sample so creators can reuse their own or custom voices across covers and text-to-speech, and a text-to-speech mode for narration or short-form content. Two tools — a vocal remover/stem isolator and a vocoder that syncs a face to a track in video — are offered free forever regardless of subscription status, which the company uses as a top-of-funnel acquisition tool alongside a Discord community and a 30% affiliate program.

Paid access is tiered by usage volume rather than by feature set: Basic ($2.99 billed weekly) caps users at 5 dubs and 1 cloned voice per week; Influencer (around $24.99/month, with introductory discounts) raises that to 100 dubs and 20 cloned voices per month with faster processing and premium support; and PRO ($99.99/month) unlocks up to 1,000 dubs and 200 cloned voices per month, priority access to all voice models, and custom AI voice training via support. One-time credit top-up packs ($9.99 for 25 credits) are available once a user moves beyond Basic. Annual billing is offered at roughly 50% off the monthly equivalent. VoiceDub has real, mixed public feedback: it carries a 4.3/5 TrustScore on Trustpilot from 90 reviews, with users praising ease of use and voice quality, but a notable minority (around 27% of reviews are 1-star) complaining about value for money and inconsistent generation quality per credit spent.

Best for

VoiceDub suits individual music creators, YouTubers and Discord-community hobbyists who want to make AI covers, duets or cloned-voice clips without professional DAW skills. It is a weaker fit for businesses needing dubbing for professional video localization, enterprise support, or guaranteed output quality per generation, given the credit-per-attempt model and mixed reviews on value.

Creates a reusable cloned voice from user-submitted audio samples, usable across covers, duets and text-to-speech.

Text-to-Speech

Converts written scripts into spoken audio using the same voice library, useful for narration, tags or quick concept testing.

06

Free Vocal Remover

Splits any uploaded track into isolated vocal and instrumental stems at no cost, independent of subscription tier.

07

Free Vocoder

Pushes a song through a video so an on-screen character appears to lip-sync/perform the track, offered free forever.

08

Custom AI Model Training

On the PRO plan, users can work with support to train a fully custom AI voice model beyond the standard cloning flow.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The vocal remover and vocoder are genuinely free forever, not just a limited trial, giving non-paying users real utility and a reason to keep coming back.
  • A 10,000+ voice library, fed by user-generated content, gives far more variety than most single-voice-clone competitors.
  • Trustpilot reviewers (4.3/5 across 90 reviews) specifically call out high-quality results and an easy-to-use interface as strengths.
  • The tiered, volume-based pricing model (5/week to 1,000/month) lets both casual hobbyists and heavy creators find a plan that roughly matches their output.
  • A 30% affiliate program and active Discord community suggest an engaged creator base actively promoting and using the product.

Cons

  • A meaningful share of Trustpilot reviews (roughly 27% are 1-star) complain about getting little value for money, with each generation — good or mediocre — consuming a credit with no free retries.
  • The Basic tier's 5-dubs-per-week cap is restrictive for anyone experimenting seriously, effectively pushing new users toward a paid upgrade quickly.
  • Pricing display is inconsistent across the site (intro discounted prices vs. standard listed prices), making it harder to know the real ongoing cost before checkout.
  • G2 reports insufficient reviews to generate a buying-insight score, so enterprise or B2B buyers have limited third-party validation beyond Trustpilot.

Frequently asked questions

Is VoiceDub free to use?

The vocal remover and vocoder tools are free forever with no payment details required. Voice covers, duets and cloning require a paid plan starting at $2.99/week.

How many voices does VoiceDub offer?

VoiceDub gives access to over 10,000 user-created AI voices across its cover, duet and text-to-speech tools.

Can I clone my own voice on VoiceDub?

Yes, the v2 instant voice cloning feature creates a reusable voice from a short sample, available from the Influencer plan upward.

What is an AI Duet on VoiceDub?

AI Duets let you cast 2 to 4 AI voices on a single song, with every voice singing the full track so you can mix who performs which part.

What do real users say about VoiceDub?

VoiceDub holds a 4.3/5 rating on Trustpilot from 90 reviews. Praise centers on voice quality and ease of use, while complaints focus on value received per credit spent.

Can I cancel my VoiceDub subscription anytime?

Yes, VoiceDub plans can be upgraded, downgraded or cancelled at any time, and credits are applied automatically after checkout.
