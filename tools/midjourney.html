Midjourney is an independent research lab and AI image-generation service, best known for producing some of the most stylistically distinctive and painterly output of any text-to-image tool on the market. It is not backed by a major tech company or VC-scale funding the way OpenAI or Adobe are; it operates as a small, self-funded team and has historically run entirely through Discord, with a separate web-based app added later for account management, image galleries and generation without Discord.

Its core differentiator is aesthetic quality and artistic coherence rather than prompt-following precision or productivity integration. Where DALL-E's successor models and Adobe Firefly emphasize accurate prompt adherence and commercial-safety guarantees, Midjourney is generally regarded by users and reviewers as producing the most visually striking, gallery-ready images by default, at the cost of a less conventional interface and no free tier.

Best for

Midjourney is best suited for illustrators, concept artists, marketers and hobbyists who prioritize striking visual quality over precise prompt control or workflow integration, and who are comfortable paying before trying the product. It's a poor fit for teams that need tight brand consistency across many images, users who want a conventional SaaS interface without Discord, or anyone who wants to test the tool for free before committing — Adobe Firefly is a better starting point for that kind of evaluation.

Key features

01

Discord and web-based generation

Images are generated by typing /imagine prompts in Discord or through the standalone web app at midjourney.com, both syncing to the same account and image library.

02

Version 8.2 model

Midjourney's latest model generation, offering improved prompt coherence, texture detail and more consistent human anatomy compared to earlier versions.

03

Relax, Fast and Turbo generation modes

Standard and higher plans can toggle between slower queue-based 'relax' generations (unlimited on Standard+) and faster paid-GPU-time generations, trading speed for cost.

04

Image-to-image and remix

Users can upload a reference image or use an existing generation as a base, then apply new prompts to remix composition, style or subject matter.

05

Stealth mode (Pro and Mega plans)

Hides a user's generations from Midjourney's public community feed, useful for commercial or unreleased creative work.

06

Video generation

Midjourney supports generating short AI video clips from images or prompts, with HD output available on Pro and Mega tiers.

07

Community gallery and prompt exploration

A public feed of other users' generations and prompts that can be browsed and remixed, useful for prompt inspiration.

08

Commercial usage rights

Paid subscribers own the images they generate and can use them commercially, subject to Midjourney's terms.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Produces some of the most visually polished, artistic default output of any mainstream image generator, particularly for concept art, illustration and stylized marketing visuals
  • Version 8.2 has meaningfully improved anatomical accuracy and prompt coherence over earlier versions
  • Active, very large community (600,000+ member Discord/subreddit) means extensive prompt examples and troubleshooting help are freely available
  • Stealth mode and commercial usage rights make it usable for professional work without publicizing in-progress projects
  • Video generation is considered strong value relative to dedicated AI video tools at a similar price point
  • Frequent model updates mean quality has improved steadily release over release

Cons

  • No free trial or free tier — the only way to evaluate the tool is to pay for a month of Basic
  • Refunds are only available if lifetime GPU usage is under 20 minutes, a policy users frequently describe as unfair given no trial exists
  • Character and subject consistency across multiple images remains difficult, a common complaint for anyone building a consistent visual series or brand character
  • The core workflow still runs through Discord for many users, which feels cluttered compared to a dedicated app interface
  • Some users report generated images being removed or made inaccessible without clear notice
  • Users with over $1M in annual company revenue are contractually required to be on the Pro or Mega plan, adding cost for larger organizations

What reviewers say

There is a stark split between review platforms. G2, whose audience evaluates output quality and feature capability, rates Midjourney highly (4.4/5) and praises its image quality and creative range. Trustpilot, which captures the full subscriber experience including billing and support, is reported far more negatively, driven almost entirely by refund and cancellation complaints rather than output quality. Reddit's r/midjourney community (680,000+ members) is generally positive about output quality and treats recurring 'is it still worth it' threads as a referendum on price and workflow rather than art quality, which most users don't dispute.

Frequently praised

  • Exceptional default image aesthetics for concept art and illustration
  • Version 8.2 improved coherence and anatomy noticeably
  • Large, active community with abundant free prompt knowledge

Frequently criticized

  • No free trial combined with a restrictive refund policy (under 20 minutes lifetime GPU usage)
  • Character/subject consistency across a series of images is weak
  • Discord-first workflow feels cluttered to many users
  • Reports of generated images being deleted without notification

Frequently asked questions

Does Midjourney have a free plan?

No. Midjourney removed its free trial some time ago; the cheapest way to use it is the $10/mo Basic plan (or about $8/mo billed annually).

Do I need Discord to use Midjourney?

No, Midjourney now has a standalone web app at midjourney.com for generating and managing images, though the Discord bot is still available and widely used.

Can I use Midjourney images commercially?

Yes, paid subscribers own the images they generate and can use them commercially, subject to Midjourney's terms of service.

What's the difference between Basic and Standard plans?

Basic gives about 3.3 hours of fast GPU time per month with no unlimited slower generation mode, while Standard adds unlimited 'relaxed' (queued, slower) generations on top of 15 hours of fast time.

Can I get a refund if I don't like Midjourney?

Only if your lifetime GPU usage is under 20 minutes — a policy many users find restrictive since there's no free trial to test the tool first.

How does Midjourney compare to Adobe Firefly for accuracy?

Reviewers consistently rate Midjourney higher for artistic quality and lower for literal prompt-following compared to Adobe Firefly, which prioritizes prompt accuracy and commercial-safety guarantees.
