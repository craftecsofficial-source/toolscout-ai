Midjourney is a strong pick for artists, designers and marketers who want stylish, painterly images and will pay for them, but skip it if you want a free trial, precise control over layouts or text, or a tool that lives inside your office suite. It turns text prompts (and reference images) into images and short videos, through its web app or the Discord bot.

Its official site describes it as a small, self-funded research lab, and the subscription is the only way in: the plans page lists four paid tiers and no free trial. The plans differ mainly in fast GPU hours, whether relax mode and stealth mode are included, and how many jobs run at once.

Best for

Good for illustrators, concept artists, art directors and content creators who prize visual style and are comfortable iterating on prompts. Not for people who want to test for free first, teams needing exact brand-consistent layouts or accurate on-image text, or anyone who just needs a few occasional images (cheaper or free generators fit better).

Key features

01

Web app and Discord

Generate and manage images in the Midjourney web app or via the Discord bot; both use the same account.

02

V8.x models

The model docs reference V8.1 as the highest version we could verify, with options such as higher-resolution output via --hd. Check the docs for the current default.

03

Fast and Relax modes

Fast uses your monthly GPU hours; Relax is a slower queue with unlimited images on Standard, Pro and Mega (SD video too on Pro and Mega). Basic has no Relax mode.

04

Stealth mode

Keeps generations out of the public gallery. Only Pro and Mega include it.

05

Video generation

Short video clips from images; SD on every plan, HD on Standard and above.

06

Concurrent jobs

Basic runs 3 fast image jobs and 1 video job at once; Standard 3 and 3; Pro and Mega run up to 12 fast image jobs.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Distinctive, polished default look that many users prefer for concept art, illustration and mood boards
  • Standard at $30 adds unlimited relaxed images, which suits heavy casual use
  • Pro and Mega include stealth mode for unreleased work
  • Video is included in every plan rather than sold separately
  • Public gallery makes it easy to learn prompt styles

Cons

  • The plans page mentions no free trial, so evaluation means paying for at least a month
  • Basic offers only about 3.3 fast hours and no Relax mode, so it runs out quickly
  • Extra fast GPU time costs $4/hour on every plan
  • Stealth mode is locked to the $60 and $120 tiers, so Basic and Standard images can appear in the public gallery
  • Prompt-following and text rendering are less literal than tools built for that; consistent characters across a series take effort
  • Plans auto-renew; check the current refund and company-revenue terms in the official terms before subscribing

Frequently asked questions

Does Midjourney have a free plan?

The official plans page does not mention a free trial or free tier. The cheapest option is Basic at $10/month, or $8/month billed annually.

What is the difference between Basic and Standard?

Standard ($30) gives 15 fast GPU hours, unlimited Relax images and HD video. Basic ($10) has about 3.3 fast hours, no Relax mode and SD video only.

Do I need Discord?

No. There is a web app, though the Discord bot is still available.

What if I run out of fast hours?

Extra GPU time is $4/hour on every plan, or you can use Relax mode on Standard and above.

Midjourney or Adobe Firefly?

Midjourney is usually chosen for style; Firefly for Adobe integration and commercial-safety positioning. Try Firefly first if you want a free way to compare.
