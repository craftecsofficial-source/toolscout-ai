Hedra is a good choice for creators and marketers who want to turn a still photo and a voice clip into a talking character video, and who like having many video models in one place. Skip it if you need predictable per-video costs or a long-form editor, because credits are spent per second and vary by model. It centers on its Character models for lip-synced talking characters and also offers access to 28+ video, image and audio models (such as Veo, Kling and Seedance), plus a developer API and an Enterprise track.

Best for

Suits social creators, ad makers and developers who want fast talking-avatar clips and model variety. Not for teams that need fixed budgets, full-length video editing, or buyers wary of credit-based billing; test on the free plan first.

Key features

01

Character-3 photo-to-video

Animates a single uploaded photo into a talking character video with automatic lip sync driven by an audio clip or text-to-speech.

02

Multi-model access

One platform provides access to 28+ AI models spanning video, image, and audio generation, including third-party models like Veo, Kling, Seedance, and Wan alongside Hedra's own Character models.

03

Audio and text-driven generation

Supports generating character performances either from an uploaded audio clip (voice-driven) or from a text prompt/script.

04

Commercial rights & watermark-free exports

All paid subscription tiers include commercial usage rights and remove the watermark present on free-tier generations.

05

Developer API

Exposes the full model catalog programmatically for developers building video generation into their own products.

06

Enterprise deployment options

Custom credit volumes, dedicated Slack support, forward-deployed engineers, private deployments, and single sign-on for large organizations.

07

Per-second credit metering

Credits are deducted based on actual generated video length (e.g., a 10-second clip is billed for 10 seconds, not rounded up to a minute).

08

Credit packs (rollover top-ups)

Users who exhaust monthly plan credits can buy additional credit packs, which, unlike subscription credits, don't expire between billing cycles.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hedra free?

Yes, the live plans page lists a free plan with 300 credits per month and slower generation.

How much does it cost?

Lite $10/mo, Creator $30/mo, Professional $75/mo monthly; $8, $24 and $60 per month on yearly billing.

Do credits roll over?

The plans page marks paid-plan credits as rolling over monthly, but earlier notes said they reset; verify the current policy in your account.

Can I use videos commercially?

Paid plans list commercial use; the free plan does not.

How are credits spent?

Per second of generated video, with the rate depending on the model.
