JingleMaker is a free web tool built by ElevenLabs to showcase its Eleven Music API: a user pastes a website URL (or X/Twitter profile), the AI analyzes the site's text, images, and overall structure to infer the brand's personality, picks one of six preset musical styles, and generates a complete short jingle in roughly 10-30 seconds. The tool was released as a marketing showcase for ElevenLabs' generative music technology rather than as a standalone paid product, and it requires no signup to try — only an email address to download the finished WAV file.

Note on domain: the tool most commonly referenced by the name "JingleMaker" and covered in reviews is hosted at jinglemaker.ai, built and operated by ElevenLabs (Aflorithmic's/ElevenLabs' broader AI audio ecosystem). The domain jinglemaker.com, sometimes assumed to host this product, is a parked domain listed for sale via HugeDomains and does not host any active tool — so listings referencing that domain should be corrected to jinglemaker.ai. Because it's a lightweight demo rather than a full SaaS platform, it has no pricing tiers, dashboard, or account system of its own; users who want more control over lyrics, instrumentation, or track length are pointed to ElevenLabs' paid ElevenMusic product.

Best for

JingleMaker suits small businesses, marketers, podcasters, and social content creators who want a fast, free, branded jingle for ads, intros, or reels without hiring a composer. It's a poor fit for anyone needing precise creative control over lyrics, instrumentation, or track length, or for teams that want a persistent project dashboard and history — those users should go directly to ElevenLabs' paid ElevenMusic product instead.

02

Six Musical Styles

Choose from Radio Ad, Madison Avenue, Dramatic, Midwest Farmer, US Politician, and Funky Rap to set the jingle's tone.

03

Brand-Aware Content Analysis

The AI reads text, images, and site structure to infer a brand's personality and translate it into musical characteristics.

04

Fast Generation

Produces a complete jingle in approximately 10-30 seconds, versus days or weeks for traditional jingle production.

05

Shareable Player & Download

Generated jingles play instantly in-browser and can be shared via link or downloaded as WAV after entering an email.

06

Commercial Use Rights

Jingles generated through the tool can be used for business, advertising, and other commercial purposes.

07

ElevenMusic Hand-off

Users wanting finer control over tempo, instruments, or vocals can continue editing in ElevenLabs' full ElevenMusic tool.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • It's genuinely free with no paywall, subscription, or watermark — a rarity among AI jingle tools, most of which gate downloads behind a paid plan.
  • Generation speed (under a minute) makes it useful for quick creative exploration or a first-draft jingle concept before committing to a paid composer or platform.
  • Because it's powered by ElevenLabs' Eleven Music model, the audio quality is notably higher-fidelity than many free jingle generators.
  • The URL-based workflow removes the need to write a prompt — non-musical users just supply a website and get a relevant result, which lowers the barrier to entry.
  • Commercial use rights are included by default, so small businesses can use output in ads or social content without a separate licensing step.

Cons

  • The six preset styles are the ceiling of customization — there's no way to edit lyrics, swap instruments, or adjust tempo without leaving the tool entirely for ElevenMusic.
  • Sites with sparse or generic content tend to produce generic-sounding jingles, since the AI's analysis has little brand signal to work with.
  • As a marketing demo rather than a maintained product, there's no dashboard, project history, or account system, so past generations aren't saved for reuse.
  • The tool's real domain (jinglemaker.ai) is easy to confuse with the unrelated, parked jinglemaker.com domain, which can mislead people searching for it.
  • Because it's a promotional tool for ElevenLabs, its long-term availability and feature set are not guaranteed the way a subscription product's roadmap would be.

Frequently asked questions

Is JingleMaker really free?

Yes, generating and downloading a jingle is free; you only need to provide an email address to receive the WAV file.

What is jinglemaker.ai, and how is it different from jinglemaker.com?

jinglemaker.ai is the real, active tool built by ElevenLabs. jinglemaker.com is an unrelated parked domain listed for sale and does not host any jingle-making product.

Can I customize the lyrics or instruments in my jingle?

Not within JingleMaker itself — it offers six preset musical styles. For deeper editing (lyrics, instruments, tempo), you need to continue in ElevenLabs' ElevenMusic tool.

How long does it take to generate a jingle?

Typically 10-30 seconds after you submit a website or X profile URL and select a style.

Can I use the jingle commercially?

Yes, jingles generated through the tool come with commercial use rights for ads, marketing, and business content.

Who built JingleMaker?

It was built by ElevenLabs as a showcase of its Eleven Music generative audio API.
