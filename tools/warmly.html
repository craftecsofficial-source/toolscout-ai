Warmly is a strong fit for B2B teams with steady website traffic and the budget to act on it, but a poor fit for small teams, early startups or sites with little traffic. Its published pricing now starts at $10,000 a year, so it is no longer the cheap entry point it was once described as. It watches who visits your site, identifies the companies and sometimes individual people behind anonymous sessions, and enriches them with firmographic data. From there it can alert reps in Slack, engage visitors with an AI chat agent, and trigger follow-up outreach. Identification is probabilistic, so match rates depend heavily on your traffic mix, and you pay in credits tied to volume.

Best for

Good for B2B SaaS and mid-market revenue teams with meaningful inbound traffic, a sales team ready to follow up within minutes, and budget of five figures a year. Not for solo founders, early-stage teams, sites with only a few hundred visits a month, mainly consumer or offline businesses, or buyers who want a free or low-cost monthly plan.

Key features

01

Visitor De-anonymization

Identifies companies and, where possible, individual visitors browsing a website using IP and behavioral matching combined with data enrichment.

02

AI Chat

On-site AI chatbot that can engage identified visitors in real time and qualify or route them to sales.

03

Warm Email Sequencing

Automatically triggers personalized outbound email sequences to identified visitors based on their on-site behavior.

04

Real-Time Slack Alerts

Notifies sales reps instantly in Slack when a target account or high-intent visitor is browsing the site.

05

Intent Scoring

Scores and prioritizes accounts based on visit frequency, pages viewed, and other engagement signals.

06

CRM & Engagement Integrations

Connects with common CRMs and sales engagement tools to push enriched visitor data directly into existing workflows.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Combines identification, alerts, chat and outreach in one product, so you do not stitch several tools together
  • Acts on your own site visitors, who are already interested, which usually beats cold lists for reply rates
  • Slack alerts let reps respond while a visitor is still on the site
  • Quarterly billing gives a lower-commitment way to try it than a full annual contract (confirm terms)

Cons

  • Published pricing starts at $10,000/year and rises steeply with chat and autopilot tiers, which prices out small teams
  • Identification accuracy varies; individual-level matches are less reliable than company-level ones and depend on your traffic
  • Credit-based volume means a traffic spike can use your allowance quickly (exact caps not public)
  • Some prices (video agent) are not listed, and quarterly pricing does not reconcile cleanly with annual, so expect negotiation
  • Value depends on having enough traffic and reps fast enough to act on the signals

Frequently asked questions

How much does Warmly cost?

The pricing page currently lists AI Web-Deanonymization from $10,000/year, Inbound Chat from $20,000/year and AI Inbound Autopilot from $30,000/year, each with from 10K credits per month. Add-ons start at $10,000/year. Confirm exact terms with sales.

Is there still a free plan?

We could not confirm one. The pricing page does not describe a free tier, though the login link hints at a free sign-up. Check directly before planning around it.

How does Warmly identify anonymous visitors?

It combines IP matching and behavioral signals with third-party data enrichment. Company-level identification is more reliable than individual-level, and match rates vary by traffic.

Do I need a lot of website traffic for it to be worthwhile?

Yes. The value comes from identifying enough real prospects to act on. With low traffic, the annual cost is hard to justify versus a contact database or enrichment tool.

What happens after a visitor is identified?

Depending on tier, you get Slack alerts and CRM sync, an AI chat agent can engage the visitor, and automated follow-up can be triggered. Privacy and consent rules (GDPR, CCPA) still apply to how you use the data.
