Murf suits marketing, training and e-learning teams that want polished voiceover for slides, courses and videos from a no-code browser editor. If you need a developer-first voice platform, or cheap custom voice cloning on a small plan, check alternatives first.

You paste or write a script, pick one of 200+ voices, adjust pitch, speed, emphasis and pronunciation, and sync the audio to slides or video. Murf also offers a voice changer, AI dubbing, text-to-speech APIs (Falcon 2 for real-time agents, Gen 2 for voiceovers) and conversational voice agents. Murf's pricing page could not be loaded today (it requires JavaScript), so these figures are the last ones listed on toolscout and were not re-verified.

Best for

Good for L&D teams, course creators, marketers and presenters who want quick, professional narration in a browser studio. Not for developers wanting deep API-first control, for small teams needing affordable custom voice cloning, or for anyone who needs published pricing verified before budgeting: confirm on murf.ai/pricing.

Key features

01

Murf Studio

Browser-based editor for building voiceover projects and syncing audio to slides or video.

02

200+ voices

Large voice library with controls for pitch, speed, intonation and a custom pronunciation library; homepage lists 35+ languages though other Murf pages say 20+.

03

Say It My Way

Lets you record your own delivery to guide how the AI voice reads a line.

04

Voice cloning and voice changer

Clone a voice or convert a recording into an AI voice. Plan availability for cloning was not verifiable today; it was last listed as Enterprise-only.

05

AI dubbing

Translates and re-voices video and audio into other languages while trying to keep the original voice and tone.

06

Integrations

Canva, Google Slides, PowerPoint, Adobe Captivate, a TTS Reader, Voices for Windows and a public API.

07

APIs and voice agents

Falcon 2 real-time API (listed at $0.01 per minute on the homepage) and Gen 2 API, plus multilingual conversational agents.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Approachable no-code studio for people without audio production experience
  • Slide and course-tool integrations fit narrated presentation workflows
  • Wide voice library with fine control over delivery and pronunciation
  • Offers APIs and dubbing alongside the studio, so one vendor covers several voice needs
  • Lists SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR and HIPAA compliance (self-reported)

Cons

  • Live pricing could not be loaded today, so plan limits above may be out of date
  • Voice generation was last listed in yearly hour allotments, which can run out for high-volume teams
  • Voice cloning was last listed as Enterprise-only, a gap for small teams
  • Free plan was last listed as 10 one-time minutes with no commercial rights
  • Murf's language counts are inconsistent across its own pages; test your target language and accent before committing

Frequently asked questions
