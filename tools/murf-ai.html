Murf AI is a text-to-speech and AI voiceover platform aimed at marketers, e-learning developers and content teams who need studio-quality voiceovers without hiring voice talent. It offers 200+ AI voices across 20+ languages and accents inside a browser-based 'Murf Studio' editor, and lists customers including Salesforce, PepsiCo, Deloitte and Accenture.

Compared to ElevenLabs' developer/API-first, research-driven positioning, Murf leans into being a polished, no-code studio for non-technical users — timeline-based editing plus PowerPoint, Google Slides and Canva plug-ins — with per-minute 'voice generation time' pricing that's easier for marketing and L&D teams to budget against than ElevenLabs' character-credit system.

Best for

Murf AI is best suited for marketing teams, e-learning and course creators, and presenters who want a no-code, browser-based studio to add professional-sounding narration to videos, slides and ads at a predictable per-minute price. Teams that need true custom voice cloning without an Enterprise contract, or deep API and developer control, are better served by ElevenLabs.

Key features

01

Murf Studio

A browser-based, slide/timeline editor for building voiceover projects, syncing audio to video or slides, and managing multiple projects at once.

02

200+ AI voices, 20+ languages

A large voice library with adjustable pitch, speed, emphasis and pauses ('Say It My Way' style controls) for natural-sounding delivery.

03

Voice Cloning

Custom voice clones trained from a user's own voice, available only on the Enterprise tier as an add-on.

04

AI Dubbing (Murf Dub)

Translates and re-voices video content into other languages for localized versions of the same project.

05

Voice Changer

Converts an existing spoken recording into one of Murf's AI voices while preserving the original timing and delivery.

06

Integrations

Native plug-ins for PowerPoint (Business+), Google Slides (Business+) and Canva (all paid tiers), plus a developer API.

07

Audio-to-Text / Transcription

Converts recorded audio into editable text, available from the Business tier and up.

08

Pronunciation & style controls

Fine-grained control over emphasis and variability (Business tier and up) for more natural, less robotic-sounding narration.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Large, well-reviewed voice library (200+ voices, 20+ languages) with G2 Momentum Leader recognition for multiple consecutive years
  • No-code, timeline-based Studio is approachable for marketers and L&D teams without production experience
  • Real free plan (no credit card) lets users test the interface and voice catalog before paying
  • Native PowerPoint, Google Slides and Canva integrations streamline voiceover-for-presentation workflows
  • Annual billing is meaningfully cheaper than monthly for budget-conscious teams
  • Consistently strong ease-of-use and support scores in reviews

Cons

  • Voice cloning is locked behind the Enterprise plan — Creator and Business users cannot clone a custom voice at all
  • Reddit users report inconsistent pronunciation in non-English voices, with Spanish regional accents cited repeatedly
  • Enterprise voice-cloning setup has been reported as expensive by users on Reddit
  • The free plan is very limited (10 minutes, one-time, no commercial rights)
  • No native integration with major video editors like Adobe Premiere
  • Editor seats are capped at 1 on both Creator and Business plans — teams need Enterprise to add more than one active editor

What reviewers say

Murf AI is one of the more consistently well-reviewed AI voice tools on G2, with specific praise for ease of use and support quality; its Capterra presence is far thinner and less statistically reliable than G2's. Reddit sentiment is generally positive on voice quality, especially newer voice generations, but repeatedly flags pronunciation inconsistencies in non-English voices, a costly and Enterprise-only path to custom voice cloning, and a free plan many consider too limited to be useful for real projects.

Frequently asked questions

Does Murf AI have a free plan?

Yes — Murf's Free plan requires no credit card and includes 10 projects and 10 minutes of one-time voice generation, but it does not include commercial usage rights.

How much does Murf AI cost per month?

Paid plans start at $19/mo (Creator, billed annually; $29/mo billed monthly) and go up to $66/mo (Business, billed annually; $99/mo billed monthly), with custom Enterprise pricing above that.

Can I clone my own voice with Murf AI?

Only on the Enterprise plan, as a paid add-on. Voice cloning is not available on the Creator or Business tiers, which is a notable gap compared to ElevenLabs.

Is Murf AI worth $29/month?

For marketers, e-learning creators or presenters who need frequent professional voiceovers with an easy no-code editor and PowerPoint/Canva integration, most reviewers consider it worth it.

How does Murf AI compare to ElevenLabs?

Murf focuses on a no-code studio experience with per-minute pricing and integrations for slides and presentations, while ElevenLabs is more developer/API-first with broader features like voice agents and self-serve voice cloning at lower tiers.

Does Murf AI support commercial use?

Yes, but only on paid plans — the Free plan explicitly excludes commercial rights, which are included starting on the $19/mo Creator plan.
