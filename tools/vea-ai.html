VEA AI is a reasonable fit for owners of small client-based service businesses who want to consolidate CRM, bookkeeping-style tracking and task management into one system; skip it if you sell products, rely on a specific accounting package you do not want to replace, or need to try before paying. It is paid-only and the site mentions no trial, so you commit before you can judge how well the AI agents actually work.

The product bundles customer records, transactions, tasks, checklists, team scheduling and live dashboards, with AI agents that assign work and raise alerts about revenue and renewals. A company knowledge base and, on the top plan, an AI mentor and executive-assistant features sit on top. The vendor says it replaces separate accounting and CRM tools rather than integrating with them, and names no third-party integrations on its homepage.

Best for

Best for owners of small service businesses with clients, projects and a small team who want one system for customers, finances and tasks. Not for solo founders with little to track, product or e-commerce businesses, teams who want to keep an existing accounting or CRM package, or buyers who need a free trial before paying.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Accounting, client management and project tracking are built in, so a small firm may be able to drop a separate CRM and task tool.
  • Prices are public per seat with no sales calls, so you can work out cost for your team size before contacting anyone.
  • Cancellation is stated as no-penalty with access kept until the period ends and data export available, which lowers lock-in worries.
  • You can put admin staff on Business Class and keep higher tiers for the people who need them.

Cons

  • No free trial or demo booking is mentioned, so you pay before testing the AI agents on your own data.
  • Cost climbs fast: five users on First Class is $250/month, and Owners Club is $597 per user, which only suits a very owner-centric setup.
  • The Owners Club page promises to double revenue in 12 months, guaranteed, but lists no terms or refund process. Treat it as marketing, not a contract.
  • No integrations are named. If your bookkeeper uses QuickBooks or Xero, we could not confirm VEA works alongside it; the vendor's pitch is to replace such tools.
  • We could not independently verify claims such as fast setup or the quality of AI task assignment; no third-party reviews were checked.

Frequently asked questions

Does VEA AI have a free plan or trial?

The vea.ai homepage lists no free plan and mentions no free trial or demo. It does say you can cancel anytime with no penalties.

How much does VEA AI cost?

Per user per month, monthly/annual billing: Business Class $30/$25, First Class $50/$40, Private Jet $150/$120, Owners Club $597/$497. The site says there are no setup fees.

Does VEA AI integrate with QuickBooks or my existing CRM?

The site names no integrations. It positions VEA as replacing separate accounting and CRM tools, so ask the vendor directly if you need to keep an existing one.

What is the difference between the plans?

Business Class has the core assistants. First Class adds a knowledge base, AI resume analysis, checklist builder and team visibility. Private Jet adds the most advanced AI models. Owners Club adds the mentor, executive assistant, daily brief and a private owner's knowledge base.

Can I get my data out if I leave?

The site states you can export your data and keep access until your billing period ends after cancelling.
