AdVoice is a text-to-speech platform purpose-built for advertisers who need voiceovers in Indian languages. Rather than positioning itself as a general-purpose TTS tool, it narrows in on a specific, underserved job: producing short, punchy, broadcast-ready ad voiceovers in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Punjabi, Odia and English. Users type or paste a script, pick a language and one of 40+ voices spanning tones like professional, warm, energetic and storytelling, and the system renders a 24kHz MP3 in under 10 seconds. Translation is bundled into the generation step, so a single English script can be adapted into any of the supported languages without a separate translation pass. The output comes with full commercial usage rights, making it usable directly in Meta Ads, Google Ads, YouTube pre-rolls and Instagram Reels without additional licensing steps.

What differentiates AdVoice from large multilingual TTS platforms like ElevenLabs or Google's text-to-speech offerings is depth over breadth: it optimizes specifically for the accents, cadence and colloquial phrasing that Indian advertisers need, and it prices per-generation (from roughly Rs 3-5 per voiceover) rather than via monthly subscription tiers, which suits the bursty, campaign-driven workflow of ad production. The site also publishes vertical-specific landing pages (for example, for automobile dealerships) showing it is marketing directly to performance-marketing and local-business buyers rather than enterprise media teams. As a newer, narrowly-scoped product, AdVoice does not yet have a public track record on major review platforms, so claims about voice quality and language coverage are best verified with a free trial generation before committing to volume use.

Best for

AdVoice is a strong fit for Indian performance marketers, D2C brands, ad agencies and local businesses (like auto dealerships) that need fast, cheap, native-language voiceovers for short-form video ads and social content. It is a weaker fit for teams needing long-form narration, audiobook production, voice cloning, or non-Indian language coverage, where broader platforms will serve better.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Rock-bottom per-unit pricing (from roughly Rs 5) makes it accessible for small businesses and freelancers who only need a handful of voiceovers per month, unlike subscription-only competitors.
  • The 11-language Indian focus, including regional languages like Odia and Punjabi that are often missing from global TTS catalogs, is a genuine differentiator for the Indian ad market.
  • Bundling translation with voice generation removes a full step from the ad-localization workflow, saving agencies time when adapting one master script into multiple languages.
  • Sub-10-second generation speed supports rapid A/B testing of ad scripts and voice styles within a single campaign sprint.
  • No expiry on purchased credits means budget can be allocated once and drawn down over time without renewal pressure.

Cons

  • As a narrow, ad-focused tool, it lacks the broader feature set (voice cloning, dubbing, long-form narration) found in general-purpose voice AI platforms.
  • No published reviews on G2, Capterra, or Trustpilot yet, so prospective buyers have limited independent validation of voice quality and reliability at scale.
  • Per-generation pricing, while cheap per unit, can add up for high-volume content teams compared to flat monthly plans on competing platforms.
  • Limited public documentation on API access, enterprise features, or team collaboration tools compared to more established voice AI vendors.

Frequently asked questions

What languages does AdVoice support?

AdVoice supports 11 Indian languages: Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Punjabi, Odia, plus English.

How much does AdVoice cost?

AdVoice uses pay-as-you-go pricing with voiceovers starting from around Rs 5 each; purchased credits do not expire and there is no mandatory subscription.

Do I need to translate my script separately?

No. Translation is included in the generation flow, so you can submit a script in one language and generate a voiceover in another supported language directly.

Can I use AdVoice audio in paid ads?

Yes, all generated voiceovers come with full commercial usage rights for paid advertising, YouTube, Instagram Reels and product demo videos.

How fast is generation?

AdVoice generates broadcast-ready audio in under 10 seconds per voiceover, which supports rapid iteration across multiple scripts and languages.

What audio format and quality does AdVoice deliver?

AdVoice outputs high-quality MP3 files at a 24kHz sample rate, suitable for direct use on YouTube, Meta Ads and Google Ads.
