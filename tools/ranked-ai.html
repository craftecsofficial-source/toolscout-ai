Ranked AI is a hybrid SEO/PPC offering that combines a software platform with a fully managed service team, positioned as an alternative to both traditional SEO agencies (expensive, opaque) and self-serve SEO software (powerful but requires in-house expertise to act on). Every plan bundles the same software suite — unlimited keyword and prompt tracking, SEO audits, daily backlink tracking, GA/GSC/Google Business Profile integrations, local heatmaps, competitor analysis, and AI visibility tracking across engines like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews — with a team of senior SEO and PPC professionals who actually execute the work: writing and publishing blog content, doing on-page optimization, acquiring backlinks, and managing Google Business Profile listings. The company describes its process as running on 'agentic workflows' where AI agents flag opportunities (e.g., thin content, broken schema, ranking-stalled pages) but a human professional reviews and ships every deliverable, communicating with clients through whatever channel they prefer — Slack, WhatsApp, Microsoft Teams, live chat, email, or phone calls.

The product's positioning explicitly spans both 'traditional' search engines and the newer AI search surfaces, tracking not just keyword rankings but how often a brand appears in AI-generated answers. Ranked AI also offers a White Label plan letting agencies resell the entire software-plus-service stack under their own branding, with the client dashboard, reports, and portal carrying the agency's logo instead of Ranked AI's. The company states it powers search presence for 5,000+ businesses ranging from local trades (e.g., HVAC, plumbing) to national brands, and backs its service claims with a real, verifiable Trustpilot presence — 4.6 out of 5 stars from 224+ reviews at time of research, with many long-tenured customers (some citing 2-5+ years of continuous use) praising responsiveness and measurable ranking improvements. Pricing is flat and transparent per website, billed monthly with no long-term contracts, which differentiates it from the opaque custom-quote pricing common among traditional SEO agencies.

Best for

Best fit for local and multi-location businesses, and small-to-mid agencies (via White Label), that want SEO and PPC handled end-to-end by a professional team with transparent flat pricing and real software behind it. Less of a fit for businesses that want a pure self-serve SEO tool to run their own strategy in-house, or teams managing many domains who need per-site costs to stay low.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Ranked AI pairs its tracking/reporting software with a real execution team, so clients get finished work (published blog posts, fixed pages, acquired backlinks) rather than just a dashboard telling them what to fix themselves.
  • Pricing is unusually transparent for managed SEO: flat, published per-website rates from $99-$349/month with no long-term contract, versus the typical opaque custom-quote model of SEO agencies.
  • The Trustpilot record is both real and substantial — 4.6/5 from 224+ reviews — with a notable number of reviewers citing multi-year retention (2, 3, even nearly 4-5 years), which is a meaningful trust signal for a managed service.
  • Tracking explicitly spans both classic search engines and AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews), which keeps the offering relevant as search behavior shifts toward AI-mediated answers.
  • The White Label option lets agencies offer a fully-staffed SEO/PPC service under their own brand without building an internal team, effectively letting smaller agencies punch above their weight.

Cons

  • Pricing is charged per website, so businesses or agencies managing multiple domains (outside the White Label agency plan) will see costs multiply per site rather than a bundled discount.
  • As a managed service rather than pure software, results and communication quality can vary somewhat by which account team a client is assigned, introducing a human-consistency variable that pure SaaS tools don't have.
  • Content velocity tops out at 4 blog articles/week even on the Enterprise plan, which could be limiting for programs that need a much higher publishing cadence.
  • Because the software is bundled with the service and not sold standalone, businesses that only want the tracking/audit tools without the managed execution don't have a lighter, software-only pricing tier.

What reviewers say

Ranked AI's own reviews page cites a verified 4.6 out of 5 rating from 224+ Trustpilot reviews, with a large share of reviewers highlighting multi-year retention, responsive human support, and measurable ranking improvements across both local and agency-managed sites.

Frequently praised

  • Many long-tenured customers (cited from several months up to nearly 5 years) report consistent ranking improvements and high responsiveness from the account team
  • Reviewers frequently single out customer support quality — fast responses via chat/Slack and proactive communication rather than reactive ticket handling
  • Agencies using the white-label option report the platform and dashboard make them 'look good' in front of their own clients

Frequently criticized

  • Review sample skews toward Trustpilot testimonials collected/promoted by the company itself rather than an independent, non-vendor-curated source
  • No independent G2 or Capterra listing with rating/review data was found to cross-verify the Trustpilot figures
  • Reviews are largely about the managed-service relationship (responsiveness, results, retention) rather than detailed critique of the standalone software/dashboard experience

Frequently asked questions

Is Ranked AI a software tool or an agency?

Both — it bundles a full SEO/PPC software suite (tracking, audits, reporting) with a team of human professionals who implement the work, rather than being purely self-serve or purely a traditional agency.

How much does Ranked AI cost?

Plans run $99/month (Standard), $198/month (Plus), and $349/month (Enterprise), each billed monthly per website with no long-term contract. A custom-priced White Label plan is available for agencies.

Does Ranked AI track AI search visibility, not just Google rankings?

Yes — its software tracks brand and keyword visibility across both traditional search engines and AI answer engines like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews.

Can agencies resell Ranked AI under their own brand?

Yes, the White Label plan lets agencies offer the full software-and-service stack to their clients with the agency's own branding on the dashboard, reports, and client portal.

How do I communicate with the Ranked AI team?

Clients can reach their account team via Slack, WhatsApp, Microsoft Teams, live chat, email, or scheduled calls, rather than being limited to a ticketing system.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, Ranked AI offers a free trial before requiring payment, giving prospective customers a look at both the software and the service before committing.
