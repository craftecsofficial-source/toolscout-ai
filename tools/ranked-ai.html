Ranked AI is a good fit for small and local businesses that want SEO handled for them at a modest, predictable monthly fee; it is a poor fit for teams that want hands-on control of their own strategy or need high-volume content. It is a managed service with software attached, not a self-serve tool. Each plan covers one website. A human team writes and publishes blog posts, optimizes pages and builds backlinks, while the bundled platform handles daily rank tracking (desktop, mobile, Maps and AI Mode), site audits, local heatmaps and monitoring of how often your brand appears in answers from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and others. The three tiers differ mainly in volume: 25, 50 or 100 focus keywords, 1, 2 or 4 articles a week, and roughly 4, 8 or 16 backlinks a month, with a dedicated account executive on the top tier. Agencies can resell it under their own branding. Plans are month-to-month with a 7-day trial.

Best for

Good for local service businesses, small multi-location operators and agencies that want to outsource SEO execution at flat per-site pricing. Not for in-house SEO teams wanting a self-serve research suite, publishers needing daily content output, or anyone who wants fine-grained control over every page and link.

Key features

01

Unlimited keyword and prompt tracking

No caps on the number of keywords or AI prompts tracked, covering both traditional search rankings and how a brand shows up in AI-generated answers.

02

Weekly managed blog content

The team writes, optimizes, and publishes 1-4 blog articles per week depending on plan tier, hosted on an included blog CMS.

03

On-page and technical optimization

Includes detailed SEO audits and fixes — from thin content to broken schema markup — executed by the account team, not just flagged.

04

Backlink acquisition and tracking

High-quality backlink building scaled by plan tier (1x/2x/4x volume across Standard/Plus/Enterprise), with daily tracking of link status.

05

AI visibility tracking

Monitors brand presence and prompt performance across AI answer engines like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews alongside classic SERP rankings.

06

Local heatmaps and GBP management

Visualizes local ranking performance geographically and includes Google Business Profile management for local/multi-location businesses.

07

White label for agencies

Agencies can resell the full software and service stack under their own branding, with a client-facing portal, dashboard, and reports carrying the agency's logo.

08

Multi-channel human support

Direct access to the account team via Slack, WhatsApp, Microsoft Teams, live chat, email, or scheduled calls rather than a ticket-only support model.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Finished work is part of the price: published articles, fixed pages and acquired links, not just a dashboard of to-dos.
  • Pricing is flat and public per website, with no contract, a 7-day trial and cancellation at period end.
  • Tracking is uncapped on every plan, including AI prompts across several answer engines.
  • White-label dashboards and reports let agencies resell it under their own brand.
  • Integrations with Search Console, Analytics and Business Profile, plus API/MCP access, suit teams that want data in their own tools.

Cons

  • Cost scales per site: ten sites means ten subscriptions.
  • The $99 tier is thin (25 focus keywords, one article a week, about 4 links a month); competitive niches likely need a higher tier.
  • You rely on the assigned team's quality and speed; it is less predictable than doing the work yourself.
  • Case studies on the site are the vendor's own claims, and I could not verify them independently.
  • Yearly prices and PPC plan prices were not visible on the pricing page; the page was also inconsistent about which plan includes what.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Ranked AI cost?

As of 2026-10-30 the pricing page lists Standard at $99/mo, Plus at $198/mo and Enterprise at $349/mo, each per website and billed monthly. Yearly billing is shown at 20% off without listed prices.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, the pricing page lists a 7-day free trial with no contract; plans are month-to-month.

What actually differs between the tiers?

Volume: 25/50/100 focus keywords, 1/2/4 blog articles per week and roughly 4/8/16 backlinks per month. Enterprise adds a dedicated account executive.

Can agencies resell it?

Yes. A white-label option provides branded dashboards and reports; contact the vendor for agency pricing.

Is it software or an agency?

Both: you get the tracking and audit software plus a human team that does the implementation.
