Frase is a good fit for small-to-mid content and SEO teams that publish regularly and want audit, drafting, publishing and AI-search tracking in a single subscription. Skip it if you write a handful of posts a year, or if you need deep standalone keyword and backlink research, where a full SEO suite goes further.

It scores your existing pages, builds a prioritized list of fixes and new topics (including decaying pages and questions visitors ask), then drafts content from what currently ranks plus a brand profile. Drafts go to a review queue, and publishing is automatic only for content types where you switch autonomy on. It pushes to WordPress, Webflow, Sanity or Wix, or to its own hosted CMS. A visibility module tracks whether your brand shows up in AI answers, and Content Guard watches published pages for traffic or ranking drops and drafts a repair for approval. An MCP server and CLI let you drive it from other AI tools. Frase lists SOC 2 Type II and GDPR compliance on its site; I did not independently verify those.

Best for

Frase suits in-house marketers, SEO teams and small agencies that publish steadily and want audit, drafting, publishing and AI-visibility tracking in one place. It is not for casual bloggers or low-volume publishers, who will not use the recurring audit and monitoring loop. It is also not for teams that need a full SEO suite with deep keyword databases and backlink analysis, or agencies needing white-label reporting without an enterprise contract.

Key features

01

Site Audit

Scores each page for search and AI-answer readiness and ranks fixes so you know what to do first.

02

Opportunity Report

Merges search data, competitor gaps, decaying pages and visitor questions into one prioritized to-do list.

03

AI Drafting with Brand Profile

Writes content against top-ranking pages using a brand voice learned from your site or set in the Brand Hub; supports 70+ languages.

04

Review Queue and Autonomy

Nothing goes live until approved, unless you enable autonomous publishing for specific content types.

05

AI Visibility Tracking

Monitors mentions in AI answers; ChatGPT and Google AI on Starter, Perplexity added on Professional, Claude and Gemini added on Scale.

06

Content Guard

Watches published pages for ranking or traffic decline and drafts a fix for approval (10, 75 or 300 pages by plan).

07

Frase Answers

A site widget that captures the questions visitors ask, feeding topic ideas back into the pipeline.

08

Publishing, FraseCMS, MCP and CLI

Publishes to WordPress, Webflow, Sanity or Wix, or to FraseCMS (free up to 100,000 page views/month with a Frase badge; paid hosting add-ons). MCP server and CLI work on every plan.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Audit, drafting, publishing, decay monitoring and AI-citation tracking sit in one subscription, so you are not stitching together three tools.
  • Nothing publishes without approval by default, which keeps AI drafts from going live unreviewed; autonomy is opt-in per content type.
  • Pricing is public for Starter through Scale, with a 7-day no-card trial, and overage rates are listed per unit.
  • Content Guard turns a manual chore (spotting and fixing decaying pages) into a draft-and-approve workflow.
  • MCP server and CLI on every plan, including Starter, help if you already work from Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor.

Cons

  • Starter allows only 10 articles and 50 audit pages a month and has no overage option, so a busy month means waiting or upgrading.
  • Overage is off by default and costs add up: at $5 per extra article on Professional, 10 extra articles is $50 on top of $103.
  • AI Visibility coverage is staged: Claude and Gemini tracking requires Scale, and prompt volume is metered, so heavy monitoring costs extra.
  • Monthly billing is about 25 percent higher than the yearly rate on every paid plan, so the headline prices assume a yearly commitment.
  • Agencies managing many clients hit the Scale cap of 10 domains, and white-label reporting is quote-only Enterprise.
  • AI-drafted content still needs a human edit for accuracy and voice before it earns trust.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan or trial?

There is no free plan, but each paid plan has a 7-day trial with no credit card. The trial uses Professional-level features capped at 5 articles, 50 audit pages, 25 AI prompts and 3 seats.

What happens if I hit my article limit?

On Starter, usage stops until the cycle resets or you upgrade. On Professional and Scale you can enable optional overage, off by default, at $5/$4 per article, $0.50/$0.40 per audit page and $0.25/$0.20 per AI prompt.

Do I save by paying yearly?

Yes. Yearly billing is $39, $103 and $239 per month versus $49, $129 and $299 month to month, for Starter, Professional and Scale.

Does it publish on its own?

Not by default. Drafts wait in a review queue; autonomous publishing only happens for content types where you enable it. It publishes to WordPress, Webflow, Sanity, Wix or FraseCMS.

How does Frase compare with Surfer or MarketMuse?

All three help optimize content against what ranks. Frase leans toward running the whole loop, including audit, publishing, decay monitoring and AI-citation tracking; check each vendor's current pricing and limits before choosing.
