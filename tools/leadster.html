Leadster is a good fit for small marketing teams that want a chatbot whose only job is to capture and qualify leads on a website, and it is a poor fit for anyone who needs customer-support automation or a published, predictable price list. It starts conversations with visitors based on the page or behavior, asks qualifying questions through flows you build without code, and passes the answers to your CRM. It also reports conversions to Google Ads and Meta Ads so you can see which paid traffic produced chatbot leads. Day to day you get a flow builder, a dashboard with dropout tracking and A/B tests, and add-ons for in-chat meeting booking and a ChatGPT integration. The commercial side is murky: the homepage shows a Free plan limited to 30 leads and a Starter plan at $37, but the pricing page we fetched showed no amounts, only a visitor-volume slider, and says a proposal follows the 14-day trial. Earlier listings quoted $47 to $1,000 per month, which we could not confirm today.

Best for

Good for small businesses, agencies and marketing teams that run paid traffic to a website and want conversational lead capture feeding a CRM. Not for teams that need a support or FAQ bot, helpdesk handoff, voice agents or heavy analytics. Also not for buyers who need a fixed public price before talking to anyone, since higher tiers are quoted by traffic.

Key features

01

No-code flow builder

Build branching qualification conversations without code. The pricing page lists unlimited chat flows.

02

Smart approaches

Starts a chat proactively depending on the visitor's behavior or page rather than waiting for them to open the widget.

03

Lead qualification and CRM integration

Collects answers in-chat and sends the lead to a connected CRM. Which CRMs are supported was not detailed on the pages we could read.

04

Ads integrations

Google Ads and Meta Ads integrations let chatbot conversions be attributed to paid campaigns.

05

Dashboard, dropout tracking and A/B tests

Shows flow performance, where visitors abandon a conversation, and lets you compare flow variants.

06

Paid add-ons

Meeting scheduling via chat starts at $9/month and the ChatGPT integration at $35/month.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Focused on lead generation, so flows, scoring and ad reporting are all aimed at that single job.
  • The Free plan and a no-card 14-day trial let you test before paying, though the free plan stops at 30 leads a month.
  • Ad platform integrations help tie chatbot leads to paid campaigns, which general support bots rarely do.
  • The entry price shown on the homepage ($37) is low for a lead-gen chatbot.

Cons

  • Beyond Starter you have to go through a post-trial proposal based on traffic, so budgeting up front is hard.
  • The pricing page showed a blank price and listed 'Unlimited Leads' alongside the 30-lead free cap, so what each plan includes is unclear.
  • Meeting scheduling and ChatGPT integration cost extra on top of the plan.
  • We found no independent review data we could verify today, so claims such as the '132 leads per month' average are the vendor's own and not a guarantee.
  • The list of supported CRMs was not confirmed on the live pages.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

Yes, a Free plan capped at 30 leads per month, plus a 14-day free trial that does not require a credit card.

How much does Leadster cost?

The homepage shows Starter at $37 without stating the billing period. The pricing page showed no figures, only a traffic slider, and Leadster says it presents a proposal after the trial. Older listings of $47 to $1,000 per month could not be verified.

Will I be charged automatically after the trial?

According to the site, no. They review your website traffic and then present a proposal.

Does it do meetings and ChatGPT?

Both are paid add-ons: meeting scheduling from $9/month and the ChatGPT integration from $35/month.

Is it a support chatbot?

No. It is designed for lead capture and qualification, so pick a support-oriented tool if you need helpdesk automation.
