Leadster is a conversational marketing platform, originally built in Brazil and now marketed internationally, that positions itself specifically as a lead-generation chatbot rather than a general customer-support or FAQ bot. Instead of waiting for visitors to initiate a chat, Leadster proactively approaches them with behavior- and page-based conversation flows, gathers qualifying information, and either books a meeting, generates a quote, or hands the lead directly to a sales rep — all without requiring human intervention during the initial qualification stage. Flows are built through a no-code interface, which the company positions as fast enough to convert an existing lead-capture form into a conversational experience in a single afternoon.

What differentiates Leadster from broader chatbot platforms is its tight focus on the marketing funnel: captured leads sync into CRMs (native integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce, and RD Station, plus 2,000+ apps via Zapier and webhooks), and conversion events are pushed back to Google Analytics, Google Ads, and Meta Ads so marketing teams can optimize paid campaigns based on actual chatbot-driven conversions rather than just form fills. A results dashboard tracks chatbot performance by channel. Leadster has been sold via lifetime-deal platforms like AppSumo in the past alongside its standard subscription plans, which range roughly from $47 to $1,000 per month depending on traffic and features, with a free tier and a 14-day trial on paid plans.

Best for

Leadster fits small and mid-sized marketing teams that want a chatbot dedicated specifically to converting website traffic into qualified, CRM-ready leads — particularly teams already running paid Google Ads or Meta Ads campaigns who want conversion data flowing back into those platforms. It's a weaker fit for teams that need robust, dependable customer support responsiveness from the vendor (reviews flag this as a real risk) or teams that need deep reporting/analytics out of the box, since reviewers specifically call that out as a current weak point.

Displays real-time results and conversion metrics for chatbot flows broken down by marketing channel and campaign.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Purpose-built specifically for marketing lead generation, with conversation flows designed around qualification, meeting-booking, and quoting rather than general support
  • Direct conversion-event integration with Google Ads and Meta Ads lets marketing teams optimize paid campaigns using actual chatbot conversion data
  • No-code flow builder is genuinely fast to set up — reviewers on G2 specifically praised converting existing forms to chat-style flows in a single afternoon
  • Native CRM integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce, and RD Station reduce manual lead-import work
  • A free tier and 14-day paid trial let teams test real conversion performance before committing to a paid plan

Cons

  • G2 reviews are genuinely mixed — 3.5/5 average across only 4 reviews, including one 1-star review describing completely unresponsive customer support after an AppSumo lifetime-deal purchase
  • Multiple reviewers specifically flag limited reporting tools and difficulty extracting report data compared to more mature marketing platforms
  • Pricing scales up steeply — from $47/month at entry to as much as $1,000/month — which can be a large jump for a growing small business
  • Small review sample size (4 on G2) means there isn't yet a statistically robust picture of typical customer experience
  • One reviewer specifically noted 'limited integration tools' and higher implementation cost relative to closest competitors

What reviewers say

Leadster holds a mixed 3.5/5 rating on G2 from a small sample of 4 reviews as of mid-2026. Reviewers who had a good experience praised the fast, no-code setup and effective lead generation, while at least one reviewer reported serious, unresolved customer support issues after purchasing via a lifetime deal.

Frequently praised

  • Simple to implement — reviewers describe converting existing forms into chat-like conversational leads in an afternoon
  • Effective at lead generation and natural language-based qualification without requiring technical setup
  • Easy integration with CRM and automation tools like Pluga speeds up deployment

Frequently criticized

  • At least one reviewer reported completely unresponsive customer support, including bounced emails to the listed contact address
  • Reporting and data-extraction tools are described as limited, making it hard to pull detailed performance data
  • Higher implementation cost and more limited integration options relative to closest competitors, per one reviewer

Frequently asked questions

Is Leadster free to try?

Yes — there's a free tier with limited functionality, and paid plans include a 14-day free trial.

How much does Leadster cost?

Paid subscriptions range from roughly $47/month to $1,000/month depending on plan level and traffic/feature needs.

What CRMs does Leadster integrate with?

It has native integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce, and RD Station, plus access to 2,000+ additional apps via Zapier and webhooks.

Does Leadster work with my ad campaigns?

Yes — it sends chatbot conversion events directly to Google Analytics, Google Ads, and Meta Ads so you can measure and optimize campaign performance based on real chatbot conversions.

Do I need to code to build a chatbot flow?

No — Leadster's flow builder is designed to be no-code, letting marketing teams create branching qualification conversations without developer help.

Is Leadster's customer support reliable?

Reviews are mixed on this point — most reviewers report a smooth experience, but at least one reported serious difficulty reaching support after a lifetime-deal purchase, so it's worth testing the trial and support responsiveness directly before committing.
