LanguageTool is a great pick if you write in more than one language, or in German, French, Spanish, Dutch or Portuguese, and want an affordable checker that works in your browser, email and office apps. Skip it if you mainly want deep AI rewriting and tone coaching in English only, or if you need long documents checked on the free plan. It checks grammar, spelling, punctuation and style in over 30 languages and dialects, including US, UK, Canadian, Australian, New Zealand and South African English.

It is available as a web editor, desktop apps, browser add-ons, email add-ons and office add-ins, with a paraphrasing tool and a business offering and API for companies. The free tier catches basics; Premium surfaces the full set of checks.

Best for

Good for multilingual writers, non-native English speakers, students and teams working in several languages. Not for users who want a heavyweight generative AI writing assistant, or anyone who needs to proof very long texts for free given the 2,000-character field limit on Basic.

Key features

01

Multilingual checking

Grammar, spelling, punctuation and style in over 30 languages and dialects; Premium adds over 20,000 extra checks for English, German, French, Spanish, Dutch, Polish and Portuguese.

02

Paraphrasing tool

Rewrites sentences to be more formal, simpler or more concise. Basic gets 3 rephrasings a day; Premium is unlimited.

03

Integrations

Browser add-ons, Gmail, Outlook (beta), Apple Mail and Thunderbird add-ons, and Google Docs, Word, Pages and LibreOffice add-ins.

04

Desktop apps

Apps for macOS and Windows for checking text outside the browser.

05

Personal style guide and dictionary

Premium lets you save custom words and style preferences, with text statistics.

06

Teams and API

Teams add user invites, a team style guide and dictionary; a Proofreading API is offered for developers.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Covers 30+ languages and dialects, so it can be your single checker if you switch languages.
  • Works in browsers, email clients, Word, Google Docs, Pages and LibreOffice, plus desktop apps.
  • Premium lifts the per-field limit from 2,000 to 150,000 characters, enough for long documents.
  • A 14-day money-back guarantee lowers the risk of trying Premium.
  • Student discounts and team accounts are available.

Cons

  • The free plan's 2,000-character cap per field and 3 rephrasings per day are tight for long or heavy use.
  • Price amounts did not load on the live pricing page, so we could not verify current Premium or Teams costs; check at checkout.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically, so note the billing period you choose.
  • Paraphrasing is a built-in extra, not a full generative writing assistant.

Frequently asked questions

How many languages does LanguageTool support?

Over 30 languages and dialects, mainly English, Spanish, German, French, Dutch and Portuguese. English has six variants.

Is LanguageTool free?

Yes. The Basic plan is free to register and covers basic grammar, punctuation, style and spelling checks, limited to 2,000 characters per text field and 3 AI rephrasings per day.

How much does Premium cost?

We could not verify it: the pricing page showed an error instead of prices when checked. It is offered monthly, quarterly, annually or every 2 years, prices include VAT, and a 20% limited-time discount was advertised.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, there is a 14-day money-back guarantee, and you can cancel anytime; the subscription ends at the close of the billing period.

LanguageTool or Grammarly?

LanguageTool is stronger on non-English languages; Grammarly focuses on English with deeper tone and clarity features.
