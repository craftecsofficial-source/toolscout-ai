AI Phone, built by Second Phone Number Inc., is a consumer mobile app that solves a specific everyday problem: talking on the phone with someone who doesn't share your language. Rather than a text-translation tool, AI Phone performs live, two-way speech-to-speech translation during real phone calls — each person speaks their own language and hears the other person's words translated in near real time. The same translation engine extends to video and voice calls inside WhatsApp, WeChat, and Telegram, displaying bilingual live subtitles on both participants' screens, and the company markets a simple invite-link flow so the other party doesn't need to install anything themselves.

Beyond phone and app-based calling, AI Phone also offers standalone voice translation for in-person bilingual conversations and a camera mode that translates photographed text — restaurant menus, street signs, documents — instantly. It supports over 150 languages and accents and specifically markets accuracy across accents, dialects, slang, and mixed-language speech, differentiating itself from simpler consumer translator apps. The company positions three core audiences directly on its marketing site: immigrants adapting to daily life in a new country, travelers navigating bookings and directions abroad, and small business owners or professionals coordinating internationally. AI Phone has been featured on Product Hunt and covered by outlets including Yahoo Finance and Business Insider for expanding its live call-translation language coverage.

Best for

AI Phone is well suited to immigrants and expatriates handling daily calls (banks, landlords, appointments) in a new country, international travelers booking hotels or navigating logistics abroad, and small business owners or professionals who occasionally need to call international clients, suppliers, or partners without hiring a human interpreter. It's less suited to users who need zero-latency, professional-grade simultaneous interpretation for high-stakes business negotiations or legal/medical settings, where the reported translation delay and consumer-grade accuracy may not meet the bar required.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Strong, verifiable App Store rating — 4.8/5 with over 1 million users, notably higher and more broadly validated than most single-purpose translation apps
  • True two-way, real-time speech translation on live phone calls is a harder technical problem than text translation, and is the app's clear core differentiator
  • Cross-platform reach into WhatsApp, WeChat, and Telegram calling covers the messaging apps most commonly used for international communication
  • The invite-link mechanism means the other party never needs to install anything, lowering friction for one-off calls (e.g., calling a business or government office abroad)
  • Positive, specific user testimonials describe real use cases — client calls, international relationships, family calls, business negotiations — beyond generic marketing claims

Cons

  • Multiple user reviews report a noticeable delay (around 5 seconds) between speaking and hearing the translation, which can make fast back-and-forth conversation feel stilted
  • Credit-based billing means no plan offers truly unlimited translation minutes; some reviewers explicitly suggest the app would be better priced per-minute instead
  • As a consumer mobile app, there's no G2 or Capterra listing to independently benchmark it against other translation tools the way B2B software typically is
  • Audio translation on some call types is described in reviews as one-way rather than fully simultaneous in every mode, which can create confusion about which mode to use for a given call type
  • Heavy reliance on cloud processing means call quality and translation accuracy depend on the user's mobile data/Wi-Fi connection during the call

Frequently asked questions

Does the other person on the call need to install AI Phone too?

No for regular phone calls and invite-link calls — the app is designed so the other party doesn't need to download anything; for in-app messaging translation (WhatsApp, WeChat, Telegram) they simply see translated subtitles on their end.

How many languages does AI Phone support?

It supports over 150 languages and accents for live call, voice, conversation, and camera-based translation.

Is there a delay during translated calls?

Yes — some users report a delay of a few seconds between speech and translation, which is a commonly cited trade-off for real-time AI speech translation.

Can I use AI Phone for video calls, not just voice?

Yes — it supports two-way video and voice call translation inside WhatsApp and similar apps, with bilingual live subtitles shown to both participants.

Is AI Phone free?

It's free to start and download, with paid in-app subscription or credit-based plans required for extended usage beyond the free allowance.

Can it translate photos, like menus or signs?

Yes — its camera mode can translate a photographed or saved image (menus, signs, documents) instantly.
