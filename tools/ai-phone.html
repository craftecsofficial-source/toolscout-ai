AI Phone is a good pick for everyday cross-language calls, such as calling a landlord, hotel or supplier, when you have no interpreter; skip it for legal, medical or negotiation calls where errors and lag are costly. The app translates both sides of a live phone call so each person speaks their own language, and the other person can use a regular phone or an invite link. It also handles video calls in WhatsApp, WeChat, Telegram and other apps with subtitles, plus in-person, camera and text translation, and provides call summaries and transcripts. Pricing is not on the website, so check the subscription price in the App Store or Google Play before committing.

Best for

Good for travelers, immigrants handling bank or appointment calls, and small businesses with occasional foreign-language calls. Not for high-stakes interpreting (legal, medical, contracts) where a human professional is needed, for anyone who wants published pricing up front, or for people with poor mobile connections, since the translation depends on network processing.

Key features

01

Live phone call translation

Each person speaks their own language and hears the other's translated; the other party can use a normal phone, including a landline.

02

Video call translation

Adds bilingual subtitles to video calls in WhatsApp, WeChat, Telegram, LINE, Messenger, FaceTime and Viber, started from a shared invite link.

03

Second number

Incoming calls to an AI Phone second number are translated as well.

04

Call tools

Type-to-Speak for names, addresses and numbers, highlighting of key information, AI call summaries and full transcripts.

05

Other translation modes

Voice, face-to-face conversation and camera translation for signs and menus, across 150+ claimed languages.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • It handles real phone calls with live two-way speech translation, which is harder than text translation and its main selling point.
  • The other party needs no app, which lowers friction for one-off calls to businesses.
  • Wide messaging-app coverage means you can use it on the platform people already use abroad.
  • Transcripts and summaries give you a record of what was said.

Cons

  • No public price list: the pricing page returned 404 and the site only says to see the app.
  • The free trial is tied to following the company on social media, with no stated length or terms.
  • Machine translation of live speech can lag or mistranslate names, slang and numbers, which is why Type-to-Speak exists; verify important details.
  • Marketing claims such as "millions" of users and a rating could not be confirmed from the site text, so treat them with caution.

Frequently asked questions

Does the other person need to install AI Phone?

No. The site says phone calls work with their regular phone, including landlines, and video calls start from a shared invite link.

How much does AI Phone cost?

We could not verify it. The app is free to download, translation needs a subscription, and prices are shown only in the app; the site's pricing page returned 404 on 2026-10-31.

Which apps does video call translation work with?

The site lists WhatsApp, WeChat, Telegram, LINE, Messenger, FaceTime and Viber, with bilingual subtitles on both screens.

Can I get a transcript of a call?

Yes, the site lists AI call summaries and full transcripts.

Should I use it for legal or medical calls?

We would not rely on it alone for those. Use a qualified human interpreter where mistakes carry serious consequences.
