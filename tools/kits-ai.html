Kits.AI is a strong pick for independent singers, cover artists and producers who want licensed, browser-based AI voice conversion and vocal cleanup in one place. Skip it if you only need text-to-speech narration, or if you need to export a lot of audio on a small budget, since download minutes are capped below the $60 tier. The platform lets you convert a vocal recording into another singing voice, clone your own voice, blend or design voices, and use tools such as a vocal remover, stem splitter, de-harmony, mastering and an instrument library. It also offers a library of artist-licensed voices with revenue sharing, and a desktop app plus API are linked from the site. Outputs from your own voices are described as royalty-free; licensed artist voices carry extra approval rules.

Best for

Good for independent musicians, cover artists, YouTubers and producers who want quick vocal ideas, voice conversion and stem work without a DAW plugin. Not for anyone who mainly needs spoken-word text-to-speech, who needs full-song mixing or mastering control, or who wants to export lots of audio on a low budget, because downloads are capped on Starter and Producer.

Key features

01

Instant Voice Cloning

Generates a usable voice clone from roughly 30 seconds of dry vocal audio with no model training required, useful for quick experimentation.

02

Professional Voice Cloning

A higher-fidelity, trained cloning method available on paid plans that produces more accurate, studio-quality vocal models for repeated use.

03

Voice Designer

Lets users build an entirely new synthetic voice by adjusting sliders for breath, power, warmth, and randomness rather than cloning an existing voice.

04

Voice Blender

Merges the characteristics of multiple trained voice models to create a new, unique vocal timbre.

05

Vocal Remover & Stem Splitter

Isolates vocals from a mixed track and further splits instrumentals into stems (drums, bass, other), handling four stems total.

06

Vocal De-Harmony

Separates a lead vocal from background harmonies within a single vocal stem for cleaner remixing or cover production.

07

AI Mastering

A built-in mastering tool for polishing finished tracks without needing separate mastering software.

08

Licensed Voice Library

A catalog of artist-approved voice models where commercial use requires artist sign-off, with royalties shared back to the voice owner.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Singing-focused voice conversion plus stem splitting, vocal removal and mastering in one browser workspace
  • Artist-licensed voice library with revenue sharing is a more defensible rights story than unlicensed cloning
  • Voice Designer and Blender let you make original voices instead of imitating someone
  • Cloning is available from the $10 Starter tier per the current pricing page
  • No DAW plugin required, so setup is minimal

Cons

  • Free tier gives no voice slots and no downloads, so it only lets you hear results
  • Starter limits conversion to 60 minutes and downloads to 15 minutes a month, which heavy users will exhaust
  • High-fidelity model quality starts at $30 Producer; Starter is medium quality
  • Jump from $10 to $30 to $60 is steep, and unlimited usage is subject to fair use
  • Licensed artist voices need artist approval for commercial release
  • Not a substitute for a DAW when you need full mixing

Frequently asked questions

Is Kits.AI free?

There is a free plan with 15 conversion minutes, 0 voice slots and 0 download minutes, and no cloning. Paid plans start at $10/month.

Which plans include voice cloning?

The pricing page currently lists instant and professional cloning on Starter, Producer and Professional, and not on Free. Check the page before buying, as plan details change.

How many downloads do I get?

Starter 15 minutes/month, Producer 60 minutes/month, Professional unlimited, Free none.

Can I use the results commercially?

The site describes outputs as royalty-free, but voices from the artist-licensed library require the artist's approval before commercial release. Read the terms for your use.

Are there annual discounts?

The site advertises up to 47% off annual billing; I could not see annual prices, so check in the app.
