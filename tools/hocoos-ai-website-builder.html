Hocoos is a good pick for a small business or freelancer who wants a decent site live today with minimal effort; skip it if you need a distinctive custom design, complex integrations or fine control over code. It builds a site from a short description of your business, writing the copy and choosing the design and images for you.

After generation you edit everything in a click-and-edit editor. The platform bundles hosting, SSL, SEO settings, mobile optimization, and add-ons such as online payments, booking, blogs and email marketing. It sits closer to Wix or Squarespace in scope than to a developer tool, but starts from an AI draft instead of a blank template.

Best for

Best for local service providers, freelancers and early-stage solopreneurs who want a working site quickly and have no designer or copywriter. Not for agencies, larger companies, or anyone needing pixel-perfect custom design, heavy integrations or custom code.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Removes the blank-page problem: you get layout, copy and images to edit rather than create.
  • One clear paid plan at $15/month makes budgeting simple, and the annual plan bundles a domain and mailbox.
  • Hosting, SSL, SEO controls and mobile layout are included, so there is little to assemble yourself.
  • Includes selling and booking features for simple service or product businesses.

Cons

  • AI-assembled sites tend toward a common look, so strong brand differentiation takes manual work.
  • The editor is aimed at non-technical users; developers wanting custom code or animations will feel boxed in.
  • Extras cost more: the professional mailbox is $2.5/month and a domain is $15/year outside the annual plan.
  • The pricing page lists a trial rather than a permanent free plan, so you must pay to keep a site live. We could not verify feature limits per plan or the size of its app ecosystem.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

The pricing page lists a 14-day free trial, not a permanent free tier. After that you need Premium at $15/month or $150/year.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Connecting a domain you already own is free. A new domain costs $15/year, and the first year is free on the yearly plan.

Can I get a refund?

Premium plans come with a 14-day money-back guarantee, per the pricing page.

Can I edit the AI-generated site?

Yes. Text, images and layout can be changed in the click-and-edit editor.

Can I sell products?

The site lists e-commerce and online payments among its features. We did not verify product or order limits.
