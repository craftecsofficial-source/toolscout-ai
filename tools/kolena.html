Kolena is built for operations teams in real estate, insurance, banking and finance who drown in leases, loss runs, loan files and similar documents. Skip it if you want a general-purpose AI assistant, work outside those industries, or need transparent self-serve pricing. It is an agent platform that pulls files from Google Drive, SharePoint or email, applies your extraction and analysis rules, fills an output template and exports the result, processing many documents in parallel. Outputs include citations back to source documents, and a library of prebuilt agents covers tasks like lease abstraction, CAM reconciliation, rent roll and loss run analysis. The company states SOC 2, HIPAA and PCI compliance and says customer data is not used for training.

Best for

Good for ops, underwriting, claims and due-diligence teams in real estate, insurance, banking and financial services handling hundreds or thousands of similar documents. Not for individuals or small teams wanting self-serve tools, or companies outside these industries.

Key features

01

AI Agents Automation

Runs entire document workflows end to end — ingest, extract, apply logic, generate output, export — across thousands of documents in parallel.

02

Agent Library

A catalog of hundreds of prebuilt, expert-verified AI agents for use cases like lease abstraction, CAM reconciliation, rent roll analysis, and loss run analysis, ready to launch without AI expertise.

03

Process Documentation & Retrieval

Every workflow becomes living documentation; updating a policy realigns the relevant agents automatically, and staff can query rules and past results in plain language.

04

Auto-Ingest

Automatically pulls source documents from Google Drive, SharePoint, or email inboxes to kick off a workflow without manual uploading.

05

QA & Model Benchmarking

Kolena's team continuously benchmarks new LLMs against existing workflows, letting customers upgrade to the best-performing, most cost-efficient model in one click.

06

Audit Trail & Citations

Every AI output includes reasoning and citations back to source documents, supporting audit and compliance review.

07

Enterprise Security

SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA/PCI-aligned controls, encryption in transit and at rest, RBAC, and zero data retention options for customer data.

08

Industry-Specific Solutions

Purpose-built workflows for real estate, financial services, insurance, and banking, covering tasks like underwriting, compliance testing, and claims processing.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Automates the whole pipeline from ingestion to export rather than answering questions about one uploaded file.
  • Outputs are designed to include reasoning and citations back to source, which helps compliance reviewers sign off.
  • Prebuilt agents for common finance, insurance and real estate documents shorten time to first workflow.
  • Auto-ingest from Google Drive, SharePoint or email reduces manual uploading.
  • Lets you switch to a better-benchmarked model without re-testing from scratch, per the vendor.

Cons

  • No published pricing, so you cannot budget before talking to sales; the pricing page did not load when checked.
  • The trial's limits and terms are not published.
  • Narrow vertical focus: not a fit for general productivity, marketing or engineering workflows.
  • Case-study results (such as labor reductions at a bank customer) and compliance claims are the vendor's own and were not independently verified.
  • Each new use case likely needs configuration of logic and templates before full automation.
