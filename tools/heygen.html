HeyGen is an AI video generation platform focused on AI avatar videos, voice cloning and video translation/dubbing for creators, marketers and businesses. It markets itself as serving over 100,000 businesses and was named G2's #1 Fastest Growing Product of 2025, reflecting rapid adoption particularly among marketing teams, sales outreach and content creators.

HeyGen's core differentiator versus Synthesia is speed of iteration and creator/marketer orientation rather than enterprise L&D focus: it emphasizes fast processing, expressive 'Avatar IV' generation with more natural motion and gesture, unlimited photo avatars on paid tiers, and integrations (Zapier, HubSpot, n8n, Make) aimed at marketing and sales workflows rather than compliance training.

Best for

HeyGen is best suited for marketers, sales teams, solo content creators and agencies that need fast, expressive AI avatar video and multi-language dubbing without a heavy enterprise procurement process, especially those already using tools like HubSpot or Zapier. Teams with strict compliance/L&D requirements may find Synthesia's enterprise-first feature set a better match, and anyone billing-sensitive should read HeyGen's credit and cancellation terms carefully.

Business-tier draft commenting, task tagging, team templates and role-based content access controls.

Native integrations and API

Direct integrations with n8n, Make, HubSpot and Zapier on the Business plan, plus a documented API.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Fast processing speed relative to competitors, particularly at Pro/Business tiers
  • Avatar IV engine produces noticeably more natural gestures and expressions than earlier-generation avatar tools
  • Unlimited photo avatars and unlimited voice cloning on Creator ($29/mo) and above offer strong value relative to Synthesia at similar price points
  • Broad language/dialect coverage (175+) for dubbing and translation
  • Native marketing-stack integrations (HubSpot, Zapier, n8n, Make) make it easier to fit into existing workflows
  • Named G2's #1 Fastest Growing Product of 2025

Cons

  • Billing structure is a recurring complaint — HeyGen's two-layer billing (subscription tier plus credit consumption) has led to unexpected charges according to multiple reports
  • Refund requests within the stated window are not always honored according to some user reports
  • Render times have reportedly increased for some users beyond the marketed 'fast' processing
  • Credit-based system means heavy users on Avatar IV or Video Agent burn through monthly allotments quickly
  • Free plan is very limited (3 videos/month, 1 minute each), making it hard to meaningfully evaluate the platform
  • Higher Pro/Business tiers get expensive quickly for teams needing more than a handful of long-form videos per month

Frequently asked questions

Does HeyGen have a free plan?

Yes, the Free plan allows 3 videos per month up to 1 minute each, with no credit card required.

How much does HeyGen cost?

Creator starts at $29/mo billed monthly ($24/mo billed yearly) with 600 credits. Pro is $49/mo for 1,000 credits. Business is $149/mo plus $20/seat/month.

What are HeyGen credits and how fast do they run out?

Credits are HeyGen's usage currency: standard avatar videos cost 3 credits/minute, while Avatar IV/Video Agent cost 20 credits/minute. Heavy use of the premium avatar engines can exhaust a monthly allotment quickly.

Is HeyGen better than Synthesia?

HeyGen is generally faster to iterate with and has more generous avatar/voice-cloning limits at its $29/mo tier, making it popular with marketers and creators. Synthesia leans more enterprise-focused with stronger SCORM/LMS and compliance-training features.

Do HeyGen credits roll over?

Monthly subscribers' unused credits roll over for one additional month; annual subscribers' credits accumulate until the annual renewal date.

Are there real billing complaints to be aware of before subscribing?

Yes — user reports on Trustpilot and Reddit describe unexpected charges tied to the credit-plus-subscription billing structure, and some refund requests reportedly weren't honored. It's worth reviewing HeyGen's cancellation policy carefully before committing.
