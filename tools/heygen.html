HeyGen is a good fit for marketers, sales teams and creators who want avatar video and quick multilingual versions of existing footage. Skip it if you need predictable per-video costs on heavy usage or strict enterprise training governance, since the credit system can drain fast and the Business tier is where LMS features begin.

You can generate video from a script, prompt, slides, image or audio, or let Video Agent assemble one from a prompt with B-roll and a presenter. Its translation tool dubs and lip-syncs videos into 175+ languages, and custom avatars need on-camera identity verification. The AI Studio editor handles delivery, captions and brand kits, and an API is available.

Best for

For marketing, sales, social and agency teams who want fast avatar and localized video, and for small teams that want voice cloning and photo avatars at a modest price. Not for teams needing a compliance-first training platform with deep governance on a small plan, or for anyone who needs fixed, predictable monthly costs at high volume.

Key features

01

Multiple input modes

Text, image, audio and PPT/PDF-to-video, with each slide becoming a scene with narration.

02

Video Agent

Generates a video from a prompt with motion graphics, B-roll and presenter; elements stay editable.

03

Avatars

Avatar V is the flagship model; 500+ stock avatars, custom avatars from your face and voice, unlimited photo avatars on Creator and up.

04

Translation and lip-sync

Translates footage into 175+ languages and dialects with voice cloning; Pro adds editable translation scripts.

05

AI Studio

Text-based editor with delivery and gesture controls, comments, multi-user editing, brand kit and auto captions.

06

Business features

SSO, centralized billing, SCORM export, LMS integrations and 5 custom avatars.

07

Safety controls

Identity verification for custom avatars and content moderation; lists SOC 2 Type II and GDPR.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Translation with lip-sync covers 175+ languages
  • Photo avatars and voice cloning are unlimited from the $29 plan
  • Multiple input modes (slides, audio, image, prompt) shorten the start
  • Credits roll over one month for monthly subscribers and accumulate for annual
  • Identity verification reduces misuse of custom avatars
  • API available for automation

Cons

  • Credits vary by model, length and complexity, so cost per minute is hard to forecast
  • Free plan limits you to three 1-minute videos a month
  • Yearly prices for Pro and Business are not listed on the pricing page
  • Business starts at $149 plus $20 per extra seat, a big jump from Pro
  • SCORM and LMS integrations only on Business
  • Pro scales by buying more credits, up to $4,300/mo, which gets expensive

Frequently asked questions

Does HeyGen have a free plan?

Yes: 3 videos a month up to 1 minute each, no credit card, with Avatar IV access, one custom avatar and one voice clone.

How much does HeyGen cost?

Creator $29/mo ($24/mo yearly), Pro from $49/mo, Business $149/mo plus $20 per seat, Enterprise custom.

How do credits work?

Each plan includes monthly credits (600 Creator, 1,000 Pro, 1,500 Business). Use varies by AI model, length and complexity. Monthly unused credits roll over one month; annual credits accumulate until renewal.

Do I need Business for SCORM?

Yes, SCORM export and LMS integrations are listed on Business and up.

HeyGen vs Synthesia?

HeyGen is cheaper to start and marketing-oriented; Synthesia is stronger for governed training. Estimate your minutes on each before choosing.
