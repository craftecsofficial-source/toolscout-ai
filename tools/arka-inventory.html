Arka Inventory is a strong option for companies that already run on Salesforce and want inventory, purchasing and light manufacturing without a separate ERP; it makes little sense if you are not on Salesforce. It handles stock visibility, order commitments, alerts, multiple warehouses, purchase orders, bills of materials and production, with lot/serial tracking and landed cost on the Enterprise tier. The live pricing page lists Basic at $199/month and Advance at $499/month, each paid yearly with extra users billed on top, and Enterprise on request.

Best for

Growing businesses already on Salesforce that need real inventory, purchasing, kitting or multi-warehouse control inside their CRM. NOT for: companies not on Salesforce, very small shops that do not need multiple locations or manufacturing, or buyers who need lot/serial tracking and landed cost but do not want to negotiate Enterprise pricing.

Key features

01

Inventory visibility and alerts

Tracks stock through the sales cycle inside Salesforce and sends alerts for low stock and other issues.

02

Commit inventory

Reserves stock against specific orders to avoid overselling.

03

Warehouses, POs and barcoding

Basic includes multiple warehouses, purchase orders and transfers, barcoding and packing slips.

04

Bill of materials and production

Advance adds bills of material, production and kitting.

05

Lot/serial and landed cost

Enterprise adds sales orders, lot and serial tracking, landed cost and bin tracking.

06

Forecasting and reporting

Forecasting to help automate purchase orders, plus custom reports and dashboards.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Native Salesforce build means inventory data lives alongside sales and service records rather than needing a separate integration.
  • Entry pricing is public, and the base plan already includes multiple warehouses, purchase orders and barcoding.
  • A clear upgrade path adds BOM/production, then lot tracking and landed cost.
  • Dedicated onboarding and support are listed on the Enterprise tier.

Cons

  • Requires Salesforce; it is not a standalone inventory system.
  • Lot/serial tracking, landed cost and sales orders are Enterprise only and Enterprise pricing is not published.
  • Extra users cost $42-$52 per month each on top of the base fee, so larger teams will pay more.
  • The vendor mentions a trial, but the length and what it includes are not stated on the pricing page.
  • Third-party reviewers (e.g., on Capterra) have mentioned unclear BOM setup, limited PDF formatting and tedious import/export; we could not confirm those listings refer to the Salesforce-native product, so treat as indicative.

Frequently asked questions

Does Arka Inventory require Salesforce?

Yes, the vendor describes it as built natively on Salesforce.

How much does it cost?

Basic is $199/month (1 user) and Advance is $499/month (2 users), both paid yearly; extra users are $42 and $52 per month. Enterprise is custom.

Is there a free trial?

A 'risk-free trial' is mentioned but its length and terms are not stated; ask during the demo.

Which plan has lot and serial tracking?

Enterprise only, along with landed cost, bin tracking and sales orders.

Does it support manufacturing?

Bills of material, production and kitting are included from the Advance plan.
