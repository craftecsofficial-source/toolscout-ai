SheetSavvy AI is a browser-based add-on, built by AI Hunt Inc., that embeds AI assistance directly inside Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets (as well as Google Docs and Slides, and Microsoft Word and PowerPoint). Rather than requiring users to copy data into a separate chatbot, it installs as a sidebar/add-on through the Google Workspace Marketplace or Microsoft AppSource and lets users type plain-English prompts that get converted into spreadsheet formulas, automation scripts, or data transformations. The core pitch is removing the need to memorize or look up complex formula syntax: a user can describe what they want (e.g., "categorize this list of expenses" or "extract emails from this column") and SheetSavvy AI generates the formula or script and can explain how it works.

Beyond formula generation, SheetSavvy AI markets itself as a broader productivity add-on, bundling AI writing assistance (summarizing text, fixing grammar, drafting emails) and slide/presentation generation into the same install, powered by models including GPT-3.5, GPT-4, and DALL-E depending on plan tier. It operates on a freemium model: a free tier offers a capped number of monthly AI formula and content requests on GPT-3.5, while paid tiers unlock GPT-4 access and higher usage limits. As a smaller, newer entrant in the spreadsheet-AI space (alongside tools like Numerous.ai and SheetAI), it has modest but growing adoption — its Google Workspace Marketplace listing shows installs in the thousands and a small number of user reviews, and it has limited coverage on major B2B review platforms like G2, which currently shows too few reviews to display an aggregate rating.

Best for

SheetSavvy AI suits individual spreadsheet users, analysts, small business owners, and students who want fast, plain-English formula generation and light data cleanup without learning Excel/Sheets formula syntax in depth. It's a weaker fit for larger teams or enterprises that need robust vendor due-diligence support (extensive third-party reviews, dedicated account management) or full-scale spreadsheet workflow automation beyond formulas and scripts.

Key features

01

AI Formula Generator

Converts a plain-English description of a desired calculation into a working Excel or Google Sheets formula, removing the need to manually look up syntax.

02

Formula Explanation

Given an existing formula, SheetSavvy AI produces a plain-English explanation of what it does, useful for auditing inherited spreadsheets.

03

Script Generator

Generates automation scripts (e.g., Apps Script/VBA-style logic) for repetitive spreadsheet tasks beyond what a single formula can handle.

04

Data categorization & extraction

Uses AI to classify rows of data into categories or pull specific information (like names, emails, or dates) out of unstructured text columns.

05

Data cleaning automation

Automates common cleanup tasks such as standardizing formats and removing inconsistencies across a dataset.

06

Cross-app AI assistant

The same add-on extends into Google Docs/Slides and Microsoft Word/PowerPoint for text summarization, grammar fixes, and slide generation.

07

Real-time data import

Connects to external data sources to pull live data directly into a spreadsheet without manual copy-pasting.

08

GPT-3.5 / GPT-4 model tiers

Free users get GPT-3.5-powered requests with monthly caps; paid tiers unlock GPT-4 for more accurate and complex formula/script generation.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Installing directly inside Excel and Google Sheets (via Google Workspace Marketplace / Microsoft AppSource) means users never have to leave their spreadsheet to get AI help, unlike standalone chatbot-based workflows.
  • Covers both ends of the formula workflow — generating new formulas from plain English and explaining/auditing existing ones — which is useful for inherited or legacy spreadsheets.
  • A genuinely free tier (not just a time-limited trial) lets users validate the tool with real formulas before committing to a paid plan.
  • Bundling similar AI assistance into Word, Docs, PowerPoint, and Slides gives users one add-on across their whole Office/Workspace suite rather than separate tools per app.
  • Reviews on its Google Workspace Marketplace listing (mixed, roughly a dozen-plus reviews) skew positive on speed and formula accuracy, with several users specifically calling out time saved looking up formulas.

Cons

  • SheetSavvy AI has minimal presence on major B2B software review platforms — its G2 listing currently shows too few reviews to generate a rating, making it hard for buyers to validate quality through third-party sources at scale.
  • Pricing tiers reported across sources are inconsistent (some list Pro at $4.99-9.99/mo, others show a Personal/Pro/Premium/Ultimate structure up to $59.99/mo), suggesting pricing has changed over time or varies by promotion — buyers should verify current pricing directly on the site before purchasing.
  • As a smaller company (AI Hunt Inc.), it lacks the brand recognition, dedicated support infrastructure, and update cadence of larger players like Numerous.ai or established spreadsheet-AI tools.
  • Feature scope is narrower than full spreadsheet-automation platforms — it focuses on formulas, scripts, and light content generation rather than end-to-end workflow automation or BI reporting.
  • The free tier's monthly request caps are low enough that regular users will likely need to upgrade quickly for sustained use.

Frequently asked questions

Is SheetSavvy AI free to use?

Yes, SheetSavvy AI offers a free plan with limited monthly AI requests powered by GPT-3.5; paid Pro, Premium, and Ultimate tiers add GPT-4 access and higher usage limits.

Does SheetSavvy AI work in both Excel and Google Sheets?

Yes, it installs as an add-on available through the Google Workspace Marketplace for Google Sheets/Docs/Slides and through Microsoft AppSource for Excel/Word/PowerPoint.

What can SheetSavvy AI's formula generator do?

It converts plain-English descriptions of a calculation into a working spreadsheet formula, and can also explain what an existing formula does in plain language.

Can SheetSavvy AI clean or categorize data automatically?

Yes, it can categorize rows of data, extract specific information like names or emails from text, and perform basic data-cleaning transformations using AI.

Who makes SheetSavvy AI?

SheetSavvy AI is developed by AI Hunt Inc.

How does SheetSavvy AI compare to tools like Numerous.ai or SheetAI?

All three add AI formula generation to spreadsheets; SheetSavvy AI differentiates by bundling similar AI assistance across Word, Docs, PowerPoint, and Slides in the same install, whereas some competitors focus more narrowly on the spreadsheet itself.
