Chatling is a no-code AI customer service platform that lets businesses train AI agents on their website content, help center articles, uploaded documents, and other business knowledge, then deploy those agents to answer customer questions, generate leads, and handle repetitive support tickets without writing code. It supports over 80 languages and lets a single AI agent operate across a company's website widget, WhatsApp, and Instagram, with conversations flowing into a unified live chat inbox that human agents can join when the bot cannot resolve a query. Unlike many chatbot builders locked to a single LLM provider, Chatling exposes a large catalog of 30+ interchangeable AI models — including multiple GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, and Mistral variants — each consuming a different number of "AI credits" per response, giving teams direct control over the cost/quality tradeoff.

Beyond answering questions, Chatling agents can execute "Actions" in real time during a conversation — such as sending an email or triggering a workflow — and the knowledge base can be set to auto-sync on a schedule (weekly on Starter, daily on Growth) so answers stay current without manual re-uploads. The platform also includes a visual, block-based flow builder for scripted conversation paths, an AI Copilot that helps human agents draft replies and summarize chats, and analytics covering conversation volume, peak chat times, and top countries. Pricing is structured around monthly AI credit allowances rather than flat seat costs, with add-ons available for extra credits, knowledge base storage, AI agents, and seats, making it straightforward to scale usage incrementally rather than jumping tiers.

Best for

Chatling suits SMBs, agencies, and ecommerce or SaaS teams that want to launch an AI-powered support and lead-gen widget on their website, WhatsApp, and Instagram quickly, without engineering resources, and that value having many AI model choices. It is a weaker fit for enterprises needing polished human-agent escalation workflows, accessibility compliance, or guaranteed 24/7 support response times.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • G2 reviewers repeatedly single out ease of use and an intuitive interface as Chatling's strongest attribute, with an Ease of Use score of 9.5/10 and 17+ reviews specifically citing setup simplicity.
  • The model catalog (30+ options spanning every major AI lab) is unusually broad for a chatbot builder at this price point, letting teams balance answer quality against AI-credit consumption per response.
  • The free plan supports unlimited non-AI chats and includes a full 500,000-character knowledge base, making it realistic to trial the product on a live low-traffic site before paying anything.
  • Auto-sync keeps the knowledge base current without manual re-training, which matters for businesses with frequently changing docs, pricing, or policies.
  • AI Actions let the agent do more than answer questions — e.g., triggering emails or workflow steps directly inside a conversation — closing some of the gap with more expensive 'agentic' platforms.

Cons

  • G2's category-specific scoring shows Chatling's weakest area is 'Route To Human' at 6.7/10, well below its other scores, indicating the human-handoff experience is less polished than the AI-answering experience.
  • There is no 24/7 live support channel; users have reported relying on email for support issues, which can be slow to resolve during time-sensitive incidents.
  • The platform currently lacks accessibility support for blind users relying on screen readers, a real gap for businesses with accessibility compliance requirements.
  • Reviewers note the absence of flow import/export and block duplication features, which slows down building similar bots or migrating configurations between agents.
  • AI credit consumption varies significantly by model (as little as 0.5 credits to as much as 25 credits per message), so costs can be harder to predict than flat per-conversation pricing.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan for Chatling?

Yes, the Free plan includes 200 AI credits/month, 1 AI agent, 1 seat, and a 500,000-character knowledge base, with unlimited chats as long as AI credits aren't exhausted.

What AI models can I use?

Chatling supports 34+ models across OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, DeepSeek, and Mistral, each consuming a different number of AI credits per response.

Which channels does Chatling support?

The core widget works on any website, with WhatsApp and Instagram available as add-on channels starting on paid plans; Messenger, Telegram, and API access are also supported at higher tiers.

How do AI credits work?

Each AI-generated response consumes a number of credits based on the model used, ranging from about 0.5 credits (lightweight models) to 25 credits (top-tier models like Claude Opus or GPT-5.6 Sol) per message.

Does Chatling remove 'Powered by Chatling' branding?

Branding removal is included on the Scale plan ($295/mo) or available as a $35/mo add-on on lower plans.

Does Chatling train on my data?

No. Chatling states it does not use customer data or uploaded documents to train its underlying AI models, per its Terms of Service.
