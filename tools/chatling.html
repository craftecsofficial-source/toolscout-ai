Chatling suits small teams that want an AI support agent live in an afternoon, without code, and across messaging apps as well as a website widget. It is less suited to large support organizations needing deep routing and ticketing, or to anyone who needs many separate bots on a low budget. You feed it your site, files and help center, and it answers from that content, hands off to a human with an AI summary when needed, and captures leads. A shared inbox covers web, WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram and Messenger. Usage is metered by monthly AI credits rather than chat counts.

Best for

Good for solo founders, small businesses, agencies and ecommerce or SaaS teams wanting fast setup and lead capture. Not for large contact centers needing advanced queue routing and ticketing, teams that need dozens of bots on a small budget, or buyers who require 24/7 vendor support (not verified here).

Key features

01

Auto-syncing knowledge base

Train from your website, documents and help center. Sync frequency depends on plan: weekly on Starter, daily on Growth. Character limits run from 500K (Free) to 100M (Scale).

02

Multi-channel inbox

Website, WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram and Messenger conversations managed in one place.

03

Human handoff and live chat

Staff can take over a chat; the AI provides a conversation summary. Round-robin routing is on Growth and above.

04

Actions and workflows

Per-agent actions and workflows, with caps rising by plan (Free 2 actions/1 workflow; Scale 12 actions and unlimited workflows).

05

Lead capture and CSAT

Qualify prospects, including after hours, and run post-chat satisfaction surveys (survey on Growth and up).

06

Model choice

Several AI models selectable; advanced models start on Starter. Exact model count not re-verified.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Setup is no-code and quick, with a free tier to test accuracy on your own content before paying.
  • Pricing is transparent, with granular add-ons instead of forced tier jumps.
  • Messaging coverage (WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram, Messenger) is wider than many website-only builders.
  • Handoff includes an AI summary so the human doesn't re-read the whole chat.

Cons

  • Credits are the real limit; a busy site can burn through the monthly allotment and need paid top-ups.
  • Agent counts are small on lower tiers, which hurts agencies running many client bots.
  • Free and Starter lack some features: branding stays, workflows and actions are capped.
  • Escalation and routing are basic compared with full helpdesk platforms; I could not verify third-party review scores today.

Frequently asked questions

Are chats really unlimited?

The pricing page lists chats as unlimited on every plan, but AI replies consume monthly AI credits, which are capped per plan.

Can I remove the Chatling branding?

Yes, on Scale, or on other plans via a $35/mo add-on.

Does it work on WhatsApp and Instagram?

The site lists WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram and Messenger alongside web. Free is web-only per the plan details; check which paid tier enables each channel.

What happens if I run out of credits?

You can add credits at $10/mo per 1,000 or upgrade; behavior at zero credits was not verified.
