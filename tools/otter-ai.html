Otter is a good fit for people who sit in many video calls and want automatic notes and a searchable archive; it is a poor fit if you need unlimited long meetings on a small budget or transcripts you can trust without review. It records meetings through a joining bot or a bot-free desktop recorder, transcribes live with speaker recognition, then produces summaries, decisions and action items. You can also ask its chat assistant questions across past meetings and connected apps. Higher tiers add CRM sync, admin analytics and, on Enterprise, SSO, an API and webhooks. Its accuracy in noisy or crosstalk-heavy calls was not something I could verify from the vendor's site.

Best for

Good for consultants, salespeople, recruiters, students and managers who attend many video calls and want searchable notes and action items. Not for people who need long recorded sessions on a free or cheap plan, anyone who requires verified near-perfect transcripts of noisy or technical audio without checking them, or teams that want audio editing rather than notes (Descript is closer there).

Key features

01

Live transcription

Real-time transcript with speaker recognition, available on every plan including free.

02

Flexible recording

Records via a meeting bot or bot-free from Mac, Windows, mobile or browser; works with Zoom, Teams and Google Meet.

03

Summaries and action items

After each meeting it drafts a summary with decisions and next steps; paid plans add more advanced templates.

04

Otter AI Chat

Ask questions about one meeting or across meetings and connected apps. Monthly query limits apply: 20 Basic, 50 Pro, 200 Business.

05

Pre-call briefings and Live-Assist

The site advertises briefings before meetings and real-time prompts during calls; how well these work was not verified.

06

CRM and integrations

Salesforce, HubSpot and Zapier on Pro and up; Slack, Notion, Jira, Asana and others are listed on the homepage.

07

MCP server and API

An MCP server lets AI assistants such as Claude read meeting knowledge; the API and webhooks are Enterprise-only.

08

Specialized agents

Sales, education, media, recruiting and SDR agents are advertised for specific workflows.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The free plan includes live transcription, speaker identification and the big three meeting platforms, enough to evaluate it properly.
  • Bot-free desktop recording is an option, useful if guests dislike a bot joining.
  • Pro adds unlimited storage and history at a modest annual price.
  • Chat across meetings makes older conversations easy to query rather than re-read.
  • Integrations list is broad, and the MCP server lets you reach notes from other AI assistants.

Cons

  • Free meetings are capped at 30 minutes, and imports at 3 for life, so long calls get cut off.
  • Pro's 90-minute meeting cap and 1,200-minute pool can be tight for back-to-back meeting days.
  • AI Chat is metered: 20, 50 and 200 queries per month on Basic, Pro and Business.
  • The seat minimums for Pro and Business were not clearly labelled on the pricing page, so confirm before buying for a team.
  • Headline discounts are tied to conditions (India-issued card, first-time subscribers), so real prices differ by buyer.
  • I did not verify accuracy, supported languages or the HIPAA details beyond what the vendor's pages state.

Frequently asked questions

Does Otter have a free plan?

Yes. Basic is free with 300 transcription minutes per month, a 30-minute cap per meeting, 3 lifetime file imports and 20 AI Chat queries a month.

How much do Pro and Business cost?

Pro is $8.33/month billed annually or $16.99 monthly. Business is $19.99/user/month annually or $30 monthly. Some lower promo prices apply only to India-issued cards or first-time subscribers.

What are the key limits on Pro?

1,200 minutes per month, 90 minutes per meeting, 10 imports per month, 2 concurrent meetings and 50 AI Chat queries per month.

Do I need a bot in my meeting?

No. Otter offers bots that join calls, and also bot-free recording from Mac, Windows, mobile or browser.

How does it compare with Krisp or Descript?

Otter is centred on transcripts, summaries and chat across meetings. Krisp is another meeting assistant with a noise-cancellation focus, and Descript is for editing audio and video through the transcript.
