Otter.ai is an AI-powered transcription and meeting-notes platform founded in 2016 (originally as AISense) by Sam Liang and Yun Fu, two AI engineers based in Mountain View, California. The core product, OtterPilot, automatically joins scheduled Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet calls, produces a live transcript as people speak, and generates a post-meeting summary with an outline, key topics, and action items. Otter also supports manual recording via its iOS/Android apps and Chrome extension, and can transcribe uploaded audio or video files. The company reports usage by more than 35 million people across over a billion meetings, positioning it among the most widely adopted AI notetakers on the market.

What differentiates Otter from simpler transcription tools is its shift toward a broader "conversational knowledge" layer: Otter AI Chat lets users ask questions across one meeting or their entire meeting history, and the platform now offers use-case-specific agents for sales (CRM sync, follow-up drafting), education, media, recruiting, and SDR outreach. Speaker identification, custom vocabulary, automated slide capture during screen shares, and searchable/exportable transcripts round out the core experience. Otter integrates with Slack, Salesforce, HubSpot, Zapier, and as of 2026 exposes an MCP server and API/webhooks for enterprise workflows, reflecting its push from a consumer note-taking app toward an enterprise meeting-intelligence platform.

Best for

Otter.ai is best suited for individuals, sales teams, recruiters, journalists, and students who take frequent meetings or interviews and want automatic transcription plus searchable summaries without paying for a premium tier immediately. It's a weaker fit for teams needing near-perfect accuracy in noisy, multi-accent, or highly technical conversations, or for larger organizations that have already outgrown its per-minute pricing model and need unlimited enterprise-grade capture with minimal manual cleanup.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The free Basic plan (300 minutes/month) is genuinely usable on its own, which is a common reason reviewers cite for choosing Otter over competitors that gate transcription entirely behind a paywall.
  • For clean, single-speaker or well-separated audio, transcription accuracy is strong enough that Capterra reviewers describe it as their primary substitute for manual note-taking.
  • Native auto-join for Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet means most users never have to remember to start a recording.
  • Automated summaries and action items save meaningful review time, particularly praised by project managers and consultants in verified reviews.
  • Search across past transcripts and the newer Otter AI Chat make it easy to find what was said in a specific meeting weeks later.
  • Mobile apps support in-person recording, which several reviewers highlighted as valuable for interviews and in-office conversations.

Cons

  • Multiple Capterra and G2 reviewers report that accuracy degrades noticeably with overlapping speakers, accents, or background noise, sometimes requiring manual correction of entire passages.
  • Speaker identification is inconsistent in group settings; users report Otter mislabeling or merging speakers, especially on first use before custom vocabulary is trained.
  • The Basic and Pro minute caps (300 and 1,200 minutes/month respectively) are frequently cited as too low for people in back-to-back meetings all day.
  • Some reviewers found the auto-join behavior intrusive, joining calendar events that weren't actual meetings until manually disabled per-event.
  • Compared to newer competitors like Fireflies or Fathom, some users feel Otter's action-item detection and deeper context understanding lag behind, prompting churn to alternatives.

Frequently asked questions

Does Otter.ai have a free plan?

Yes. The Basic plan is free forever and includes 300 transcription minutes per month, live transcription, speaker identification, and 3 lifetime file imports.

Which meeting platforms does Otter integrate with?

Otter auto-joins and transcribes Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet calls, and also supports manual recording via its mobile apps and Chrome extension.

How accurate is Otter's transcription?

Otter performs well on clear, single-speaker audio but accuracy noticeably decreases with background noise, heavy accents, or multiple people talking over each other, per user reviews.

Can Otter transcribe in languages other than English?

Yes, Otter supports transcription in English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, and Chinese.

What's the difference between Pro and Business plans?

Business removes the meeting-length cap (up to 4 hours vs. 90 minutes on Pro), allows unlimited in-app recording minutes, supports 3 concurrent meetings, and adds admin analytics and prioritized support; it requires a minimum of 5 billed seats.

Does Otter offer an API for developers?

Yes, Otter API and webhook access, along with an MCP server for connecting Otter data to other AI assistants, are available on the Enterprise plan.
