MIDI Agent is a good buy for DAW-based producers who want AI to sketch editable melodies, chords, drums and basslines, and who like owning a plugin instead of renting one. Skip it if you want finished audio songs, a free trial, or one flat price that covers unlimited AI use. It installs as a VST3, AU or AAX plugin (or standalone app) and sends your text prompt to an AI model you connect, then drops the result into your session as normal MIDI notes. Because the output is MIDI, you can change any note, timing or velocity and play it through your own instruments. It does not ship a single in-house model: you can use a ChatGPT account, your own API keys (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, xAI, DeepSeek, OpenRouter), or local models via Ollama or LM Studio. It can also extend or vary existing MIDI and transcribe audio to MIDI.

Best for

Good for producers, beatmakers and composers who work in a DAW and want fast, editable ideas, and who are happy to manage their own AI access (or pay for Pro). Not for people who want complete rendered songs with vocals (look at audio generators instead), anyone who needs to try before buying since there is no trial, or buyers who assume the lifetime license includes unlimited AI usage.

Key features

01

Text-to-MIDI Generation

Converts natural-language prompts describing melody, chords, bass, or drums into an editable MIDI pattern inserted directly into the DAW session.

02

Extend & Vary Existing MIDI

Accepts an existing MIDI file and asks the AI to continue, vary, or reimagine it in a different style, rather than only generating from scratch.

03

Audio-to-MIDI Transcription

Drops in an audio file and returns a MIDI transcription of it, letting producers iterate on existing recorded material with AI assistance.

04

Flexible AI Access

Supports connecting a personal ChatGPT account, bringing your own API keys (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, xAI, DeepSeek, OpenRouter), or running fully offline via local LLMs (Ollama, LM Studio) — availability and limits depend on the chosen path.

05

Cross-DAW Plugin Formats

Ships as VST3, AU, and AAX plugin formats plus a standalone application, covering all major DAWs on both Windows and macOS.

06

MIDI Agent Pro (optional)

A $20/month add-on providing managed AI access without needing personal API keys, plus premium providers including Google Lyria for music generation, ElevenLabs for audio/sound effects, and PozaLabs' specialized MIDI models, with 500 AI generations per month.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • One-time license is rare among AI music tools, which are mostly subscriptions
  • Editable MIDI output keeps you in a normal DAW workflow instead of a locked audio file
  • Choice of AI backend lets you trade cost against privacy, including fully offline local models
  • Wide format coverage (VST3, AU, AAX, standalone) across ten-plus DAWs
  • Vendor states generated MIDI is yours to use commercially with no royalties (per its own site; not independently verified)

Cons

  • No free trial, so quality is untested until you pay
  • Ongoing AI cost is easy to overlook: ChatGPT plan, API fees, or $20/month Pro
  • The $49 price is presented as a limited-time discount; I could not verify when it ends
  • No independent review-site presence found, so evidence of quality is mostly vendor testimonials
  • Cloud providers need an internet connection; only local models work offline
  • Output quality varies with the AI model you pick, and naming specific artists as style references is discouraged by the vendor

Frequently asked questions

Is MIDI Agent a subscription or one-time purchase?

The plugin is a $49 one-time lifetime license (shown as discounted from $99). Pro is an optional $20/month add-on with 500 generations per month.

Do I need my own API keys?

No. You can use a ChatGPT account, your own provider keys, local models via Ollama or LM Studio, or subscribe to Pro for managed access.

Which DAWs are supported?

Ableton Live, FL Studio, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper, Bitwig and Reason (VST3), Logic Pro and GarageBand (AU on macOS), Pro Tools (AAX), plus a standalone app.

Does it work offline?

Only with local models through Ollama or LM Studio. Cloud providers need internet.

Can I try it first?

Not currently. The vendor says there is no free trial because of infrastructure and usage costs.
