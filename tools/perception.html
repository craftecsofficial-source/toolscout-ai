Perception suits brand designers and marketers who want to justify color choices with mood data, and it is unnecessary for anyone who just wants a pretty random palette. It turns a word or mood into palettes and can analyze an existing palette against related moods.

Beyond generation there is a palette dictionary organized by mood, color extraction from images and export to Adobe tools. The free Lite plan is restrictive; the Studio plan adds larger palettes and full mood analysis, and Enterprise targets bigger teams.

Best for

Brand designers, agencies, marketers and small product teams who want mood-based color direction and an audit of current brand colors. Not for people who need layout, imagery or full identity design, for teams wanting a free tool with unlimited output, or for buyers who need public enterprise or API pricing.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Mood-based generation gives a concrete rationale to present to clients.
  • The analyzer lets you test whether current brand colors convey the intended feeling.
  • Image color extraction and Adobe export fit into common workflows.
  • Lite is free and needs no payment to try.
  • Studio pricing is a flat per-member rate, easy to budget.

Cons

  • Lite caps palettes at 3 colors and 3 results at a time, which is restrictive for real projects.
  • Color-emotion associations vary by culture and audience; treat scores as input, not proof.
  • Covers only color, so you need other tools for the rest of an identity.
  • Enterprise and API pricing require contacting sales; SSO is only listed as coming soon.
  • We did not verify the research behind the scoring or test output quality.

Frequently asked questions

Is Perception free?

Yes, Perception Lite is free with limits: 3 palettes at a time, up to 3 colors each, and 10 favorites.

How much is Studio?

$14 per month per member on monthly billing; annual billing is advertised as saving 21% but the exact price was not stated.

Can it analyze my existing brand colors?

Yes, it analyzes a palette against related moods; Lite shows the top 3 moods and Studio shows all.

Can I export to design software?

The plans list export to Adobe tools; no other integrations were confirmed.

Does it have an API?

The pricing page lists API services that require contacting sales.
