CrystalSound is a good fit if you take frequent calls from noisy spaces and want noise removal that works in any conferencing app; skip it if you mainly want AI meeting notes or transcripts, because those features were marked unavailable or coming soon when we checked on 2026-10-31. It installs on Windows, macOS or Linux as a virtual microphone, so Zoom, Meet, Teams, Discord and others treat it as an ordinary mic. It suppresses background noise, other voices and echo in both directions, offers a My Voice Only mode, and can record the screen plus both sides of the audio. Pricing is only partly public: the paid Premium price is not shown on the pricing page.

Best for

Good for remote workers, sales and support staff, and streamers or podcasters who take calls in noisy homes or open offices and want one tool across every call app. Not for anyone who mainly needs meeting transcripts, summaries or analytics (those are unavailable or coming soon), for buyers who need a published Premium price before trying a product, or for people who cannot install a system-level audio driver on their machine.

Key features

01

Two-way noise cancellation

Removes background noise and background voices from your microphone and from the audio you hear, and helps prevent echo.

02

My Voice Only

Captures only your voice, useful in shared or noisy rooms.

03

Virtual microphone

Appears as a selectable mic, so it works with Slack, Discord, Skype, Meet, Teams and other apps without a dedicated plugin.

04

Screen and audio recording

Records the screen with two-way call audio. Cloud call recording is listed on Premium as a Preview.

05

Meeting minutes and insights

Marketed as generated minutes, keywords and insights; on the pricing page, smart minutes and keyword/sentiment analysis were marked Unavailable for Premium.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Because it is a virtual microphone, one install covers every call app you use, with no per-vendor setup.
  • It processes both directions of a call, so it can quiet a noisy colleague as well as your own room.
  • The 7-day Premium trial, a free fallback tier and a 30-day refund lower the cost of testing it in your environment.
  • My Voice Only mode is helpful in open offices or households where others talk nearby.

Cons

  • Premium's price is blank on the pricing page and no monthly price is shown, so cost comparison needs a trial or sales contact.
  • Smart minutes and keyword/sentiment analysis show as unavailable, cloud recording is a Preview, and transcription sits in the not-yet-released Elite tier, so the meeting-intelligence pitch is ahead of what ships.
  • The free Starter tier is not described in detail, so what you keep after the trial is unclear.
  • A system-level audio driver means more install and permission friction than a browser tool, and the Elite discount wording is ambiguous.

Frequently asked questions

What does CrystalSound actually do?

It is a desktop app that acts as a virtual microphone and removes background noise, background voices and echo from both sides of a call, with screen and audio recording on top.

Which apps does it work with?

Any app that lets you choose a microphone. The site shows Slack, Discord, Skype, Google Meet and Teams, and it is available for Windows, macOS and Linux.

How much does Premium cost?

We could not verify it: the Premium price was blank on the pricing page on 2026-10-31. It is billed per user, with a monthly or yearly toggle and a 7-day free trial, so check the live page or start the trial.

Is there a refund?

The pricing page describes a 30-day money-back guarantee for paid subscriptions. After 30 days you keep access until the end of the billing period but get no refund for the remainder.

Does it do meeting transcription?

Not yet. Transcription is listed under the Elite tier at $228/user/year, marked coming soon.
