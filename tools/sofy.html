Sofy is a good fit for QA teams that test mobile apps on real devices or run SAP and Dynamics 365 workflows and want to stop maintaining scripted tests; skip it if your team is happy with Selenium/Playwright code, needs a cheap unit-test tool, or wants a single clear price. It is a no-code testing platform where you describe tests in plain language and AI agents author, run and self-heal them as the UI changes, with failure analysis and CI/CD hooks (GitHub Actions, GitLab, Jenkins, Azure DevOps).

Sofy started as real-device mobile testing and now sells two pricing structures side by side: cheaper task-based AI-agent plans ($49 and $249/mo) and annual-contract device-minute plans ($749/mo and up). The site claims thousands of real devices in one place and 'hundreds' in another, so check the actual device list before buying. Efficiency numbers on the site are vendor claims we could not independently verify.

Best for

Good for QA and engineering teams with mobile or ERP (SAP, Dynamics 365) testing needs who want natural-language, self-healing tests. Not for solo developers who only need unit tests, teams that want full control through code-based frameworks, or buyers who need one transparent all-in price.

Key features

01

Natural-language test authoring

Testers write functional tests in plain English rather than code; Sofy's AI translates the description into an executable, maintainable test.

02

Self-healing test automation

When an application's UI changes, Sofy automatically detects the change and adapts the existing test path instead of failing outright, reducing manual test maintenance.

03

Real device cloud

Tests run on physical iOS and Android devices (not just emulators) in Sofy's device lab, with parallel session limits scaling by plan tier.

04

AI failure analysis agent

Performs root-cause analysis and pattern recognition on test failures, aiming to auto-debug issues instead of leaving engineers to sift through raw logs and screenshots.

05

CI/CD integration

Plugs into GitHub Actions, GitLab, Jenkins, and Azure DevOps so testing agents run automatically as part of the deployment workflow.

06

Enterprise ERP test agents

Dedicated pre-built testing agents for SAP (Concur, HCM, CRM) and Microsoft Dynamics 365 (Finance, Sales, Service, Business Central, Customer Insights), validating domain-specific workflows like payroll, lead-to-cash, and expense approval.

07

Performance and DSAT security testing

Built-in load/stress testing with response-time and scalability analysis, plus AI-driven dynamic security (DSAT) testing including vulnerability scanning and compliance checks.

08

JIRA and Slack integration

Connects testing workflows and results directly into existing team tools like JIRA (for bug/issue tracking) and Slack (for notifications), fitting into current processes rather than replacing them.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Tests are written in natural language, so non-coders can contribute and nobody maintains Appium scripts by hand.
  • Self-healing is aimed at the biggest cost in UI automation: tests breaking after every UI change.
  • Real physical iOS/Android devices are available in the same platform, avoiding a separate device farm.
  • Dedicated SAP and Dynamics 365 agents cover enterprise workflows generic tools mostly ignore.
  • The task-based tier starts at $49/mo with unlimited users and a free trial, a low entry point.

Cons

  • Two price lists (task-based vs device-minute) with no explanation on the pricing page of how they relate; ask sales before budgeting.
  • Device plans need annual contracts and overage is billed per contract terms, so costs can exceed the base fee.
  • Starter caps at 100 tests/month and Pro at 500; busy CI pipelines may hit limits quickly.
  • AI-authored tests are less transparent than code you wrote; teams wanting full control may find it limiting.
  • Marketing claims (70-80% maintenance reduction, device counts) are inconsistent or unverified.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Sofy cost?

Task-based plans: Starter $49/mo (up to 100 tests) and Pro $249/mo (up to 500). Real-device plans: $749/mo (3,500 minutes) and $1,999/mo (8,000 minutes) on annual contracts. Enterprise tiers are contact sales. Prices checked on sofy.ai/pricing on 2026-10-29.

Is there a free plan?

No permanent free plan was listed. The site offers free trials, and Starter is shown as not needing a credit card. Trial length and limits were not stated, so we could not verify them.

What is the difference between the $49 and $749 Starter plans?

The $49 plan is task-based AI-agent testing billed monthly. The $749 plan is real-device time (3,500 minutes, 3 parallel sessions) on an annual contract. The site does not say whether they can be combined.

How does Sofy compare with Selenium or Appium?

Sofy removes scripting and adds self-healing and a device lab, at a subscription cost. Selenium and Appium are free and fully controllable, but you maintain the scripts and infrastructure.
