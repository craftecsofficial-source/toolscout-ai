MagicSlides is a good fast first-draft maker for students, teachers and creators who want to turn a prompt, document, video link or audio into slides, but it is no longer free to use: the pricing page lists only paid app plans. Skip it if you need polished custom design or want a free tier to test thoroughly.

You supply a topic, text, PDF, DOCX, Excel data, YouTube link or audio, and it generates a deck in 136+ languages. It exports to PPTX, PDF and Google Slides (PNG, JPG and HTML on Premium) and has a Google Slides add-on, Figma plugin, Chrome extension, mobile apps and other integrations. We did not test output quality.

Best for

Good for students, educators, content creators and busy professionals who need a quick structured draft from a video, article or document. Not for people who want a free plan, since none is listed for the app, nor for those needing brand-perfect, animation-heavy or investor-grade decks without manual polish.

Key features

01

Prompt-to-deck generation

Enter a topic or short prompt and MagicSlides generates a structured multi-slide presentation.

02

YouTube-to-slides

Paste a YouTube video link and the AI converts the video's content into a slide deck summary.

03

Document import

Upload PDFs, Word docs, or paste text/blog content to convert existing material into slides.

04

Automatic visuals

AI selects relevant images and icons to accompany slide content automatically.

05

Multi-format export

Export finished decks as PowerPoint (PPTX) files or directly into Google Slides.

06

Theme and template library

Choose from multiple visual themes to change the look of the generated deck.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Many input types: prompt, PDF, DOCX, Excel, YouTube link and audio
  • Exports to PowerPoint, PDF and Google Slides, plus a Google Slides add-on and other integrations
  • All paid tiers are listed as watermark-free
  • Optional done-for-you design service for important decks
  • 136+ languages supported per the site

Cons

  • No free app plan on the pricing page, so you cannot test without paying
  • Essential limits you to 10 slides per deck and 10 decks a month
  • PNG, JPG and HTML export and the 50+ slide limit need Premium
  • Output prioritizes speed over design; polished decks need manual refinement
  • Listed prices appear as discounts from higher struck-through prices, so confirm the price at checkout

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

The pricing page lists only paid app plans; a free starter plan is mentioned only for the API.

Can it make slides from a YouTube video?

Yes, YouTube links are a supported input, along with documents, text and audio.

What export formats exist?

PPTX, PDF and Google Slides on paid plans; PNG, JPG and HTML on Premium.

How much does it cost?

Essential $8, Pro $16 and Premium $29 per month, cheaper billed annually ($80, $149, $279 per year).

Does it work in Google Slides?

Yes, there is a Google Slides add-on, plus a Figma plugin and Chrome extension.
