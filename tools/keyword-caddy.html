Keyword Caddy is a sensible, low-cost pick for a local service business owner or small marketer who wants keywords, AI-written content and rank tracking in one place. Agencies with many clients, large sites or anyone needing independently reviewed, enterprise-grade data should skip it. It is an AI-assisted local SEO platform that analyses your domain and competitors, groups keywords by buying intent (including near-me searches), drafts content, and tracks rankings with alerts. There are two routes: a self-serve SaaS Platform at $29/month shown as billed annually, and a Done-For-You service where its team runs the SEO with an account manager and monthly strategy calls. Customer case studies on the site are the company's own claims.

Best for

For local service businesses and in-house marketers who want an affordable all-in-one tool. Not for agencies managing many client sites, enterprise teams, or buyers who need third-party reviews first.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Consolidates keywords, content, tracking and backlinks at a price well below typical SEO suites.
  • Targets local buying-intent searches, which is where small service firms get calls.
  • Unlimited AI-written words on the self-serve plan.
  • 3-day trial needs no credit card.
  • A managed Done-For-You option exists if you outgrow DIY.

Cons

  • Only one self-serve tier, capped at 3 domains, so multi-client agencies will outgrow it.
  • The 3-day trial is short for judging SEO tools whose results take weeks.
  • Case-study numbers are self-reported, and I found no independent review listings to confirm them.
  • The page shows only the annual-billed price; the exact monthly-billing price was not clear.
  • Data depth versus Semrush or Ahrefs is unproven.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Keyword Caddy cost?

The SaaS Platform is $29/month, displayed as billed annually. The pricing page also has a monthly toggle, so confirm the monthly price at checkout. Done-For-You is custom-quoted.

Is there a free plan or trial?

No free plan, but there is a 3-day free trial of the full platform with no credit card required.

What are the main limits?

Up to 3 domains, articles up to 3,000 words, 1,200 SERP-tracked keywords and 5,000 monthly searches.

Does it do more than content writing?

Yes: keyword research, rank tracking, competitor monitoring, backlink tools and a local SEO audit sit alongside the AI Writer.

How does it compare with Koala AI or Writesonic?

Those lean toward AI article generation. Keyword Caddy is narrower in audience but adds local rank tracking and competitor tools.
