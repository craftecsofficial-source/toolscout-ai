GoZen Content AI is a sensible budget choice for small businesses and solo marketers who want blog drafts, social captions, email copy and a few AI images from one low-cost tool. Skip it if you need large monthly volumes, polished brand-voice control or a mature enterprise workflow, where bigger writing platforms are stronger. It is part of the GoZen marketing suite and works from templates: you pick a format, enter a topic or keywords, and it drafts the text. A Blog Wizard walks through titles, meta descriptions, outlines, intros and conclusions. Plans are priced by monthly word allowance, with a free tier to try it.

Best for

Good for freelancers, small business owners, bloggers and small agencies on a tight budget who produce SEO blogs, social posts and email copy. Not for high-volume content teams (the biggest self-serve plan is 60,000 words a month), for anyone who needs strict brand-voice training and approval workflows, or for people who want a tool with a long public track record of independent testing.

Key features

01

Blog Wizard

A guided flow that builds a blog post step by step: topic research, titles, meta descriptions, intro, outline and conclusion. Starter and above include unlimited Blog Wizards, and the Free plan lists it too.

02

Content templates

Over 75 templates cover social captions, ads, cold emails, subject lines, video scripts and landing page copy. The exact count shown depends on your plan.

03

Keyword ideas and SEO

A keyword idea generator and SEO-oriented content generator help you aim drafts at search terms. Treat it as a starting point, not a replacement for a dedicated SEO tool.

04

AI image generation

Create images from text prompts, counted as 'AI Arts' (20 on Free, 100 on Starter, 200 on Essential, 300 on Pro per month). Image generation and editing are listed under Essential and above, so check what your plan really allows.

05

Writing helpers

Grammar checking, proofreading, paraphrasing, a summarizer and a translation tool for multilingual output.

06

Integrations and team tools

Chrome extension and Google Docs integration. Essential and above add collaboration and user management; Pro and Premium add API access.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • One account covers both writing and images, so a small team avoids two subscriptions.
  • The free plan lets you judge draft quality before paying anything.
  • Entry-level paid pricing ($19/mo) is low compared with many writing platforms.
  • The Blog Wizard gives beginners a clear structure for long posts.
  • Higher tiers add team seats, workspaces and API access without jumping to a sales call until Premium.

Cons

  • Monthly word caps are small for teams publishing daily; 10,000 words is only a handful of long articles.
  • The pricing page mixes 'words' and 'credits' without explaining the conversion, and image availability on the free plan is not stated consistently.
  • Like any template-driven generator, drafts need human editing for accuracy, voice and originality.
  • Chat Gozen is listed as coming soon, so conversational features are limited today.
  • We did not verify output quality or independent review scores today, so test the free plan yourself.

Frequently asked questions

Is GoZen Content AI free?

There is a free plan at $0 with 2,000 words a month, 1 seat and an allowance of 20 AI Arts. Paid plans run from $19 to $99 a month, plus custom Premium pricing.

How much does GoZen Content AI cost?

Starter is $19/mo, Essential $49/mo and Pro $99/mo on monthly billing, with Premium on custom pricing. The site advertises 20% off for yearly billing.

What are the word limits?

Free 2,000, Starter 10,000, Essential 30,000, Pro 60,000 words per month; Premium is listed as unlimited.

Does it generate images as well as text?

Yes, it has AI image generation counted as AI Arts. The page lists image generation and editing as an Essential-and-above feature, so confirm before relying on the free plan for images.

How does it compare with Jasper or Writesonic?

GoZen is cheaper to start and simpler, but its monthly word caps are lower and it offers less in the way of advanced brand-voice and enterprise features. It suits light to moderate content needs.
