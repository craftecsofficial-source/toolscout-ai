Framer AI is a strong choice for founders, freelancers and small teams who want a good-looking marketing site or portfolio fast and are happy to refine the result in a visual editor. Skip it if you need complex e-commerce or backend logic, or want predictable costs with no credit limits. It is the AI layer of the Framer website builder: you prompt agents to create pages, sections, copy and visuals, then keep editing by chat or on the canvas, and publish on Framer hosting.

Best for

Who it is for: founders, freelancers, agencies and small businesses building marketing sites, landing pages or portfolios who want AI to do the first draft. Who it is not for: teams needing complex e-commerce or custom backends, and anyone who needs a fixed price with no usage credits or add-ons.

Key features

01

Prompt-to-site building

Agents create editable pages, sections, copy and visuals; you can refine in chat or switch to the canvas.

02

Editing existing sites

Change pages, layouts, CMS content or SEO settings on a live project without regenerating the whole site.

03

Custom code components

Describe an interaction and an agent builds a code component you can review and adjust.

04

Pre-publish review

Agents can flag contrast issues, typos, missing alt text, SEO gaps and inconsistent styles.

05

Analytics suggestions

Agents can review traffic and conversion gaps and suggest next steps.

06

Model choice and external agents

Pick among several AI models, and connect Claude Code, Cursor or Codex to Framer.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The result is an ordinary Framer project, so you can keep editing visually with animations and layouts
  • Hosting, CMS, SEO and password protection are in one product
  • Pre-publish checks catch typos, alt text and contrast problems
  • Lets you pick models and connect external coding agents

Cons

  • A paid plan is required for a custom domain; Free stays on a Framer domain
  • AI runs on credits, and the pricing page does not explain how many credits a typical site build uses
  • Add-ons (extra pages, CMS, bandwidth, editors at $20 each, locales at $20 each) can raise the bill past the headline price
  • Agents suggest changes but you make the final calls; responsive tweaks may need follow-up
  • No deep e-commerce or backend features on the pages I checked

Frequently asked questions

Is Framer AI free?

You can build with the Free plan, which includes 500 AI credits to try and a Framer subdomain. A custom domain requires Basic ($10/mo) or higher.

How much do the paid plans cost?

Basic is $10/mo and Pro is $30/mo per the pricing page, which lists monthly rates; yearly prices were not shown. Extra editors are $20 each and content editors $10.

How do AI credits work?

Plans include monthly credit tiers (1,000 to 15,000 on Basic, 3,000 to 100,000 on Pro). How many credits a full site uses was not stated, so test on Free first.

Can I edit what the AI builds?

Yes. It is a normal Framer project you can edit on the canvas, including CMS content and layout.

Is it good for online stores?

I could not verify built-in store features on the pages checked. Plan on external tools if you need serious e-commerce.
