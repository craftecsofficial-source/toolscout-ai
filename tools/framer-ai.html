Framer AI is the AI website-generation entry point built into Framer, a popular no-code web design and publishing platform. Instead of starting from a blank canvas or a rigid template, users describe their business, product, or project in a short text prompt, and Framer AI generates a complete, multi-section website with layout, copy placeholders, and visual styling already in place.

What sets Framer AI apart from many other AI website generators is that the output lands directly inside Framer's full visual editor, a mature no-code design tool known for production-quality animations, responsive layouts, and design flexibility. This means the AI-generated starting point is not a dead-end template but a fully editable foundation that can be refined down to pixel-level detail, including custom interactions and animations.

Because it is part of the broader Framer platform, sites built with Framer AI also inherit Framer's built-in CMS for blog and dynamic content, one-click publishing and hosting, custom domain support, and baseline SEO handling, making it a practical path from idea to a live, professional website without needing separate design and development tools.

Best for

Framer AI is best suited for founders, freelancers, agencies, and small businesses who want a professional-looking marketing site, portfolio, or landing page quickly, without hiring a dedicated designer or developer. It also appeals to designers who want an AI-generated starting point they can then customize deeply using Framer's animation and layout tools. It is less suited to teams needing complex custom backend logic or large-scale e-commerce beyond what Framer's ecosystem natively supports.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Produces a genuinely usable starting site rather than a generic, hard-to-customize template
  • Full integration with Framer's editor means the AI output is a real foundation, not a throwaway draft
  • Combines website generation with professional-grade animation and interaction tools in one platform
  • Publishing, hosting, and CMS are built in, avoiding the need to stitch together separate services

Cons

  • Generated copy and imagery are usually placeholders that still need real content and brand-specific editing
  • Custom domains and higher usage require a paid plan, so the free tier alone is not enough for a live business site
  • Users needing complex e-commerce or backend logic beyond Framer's native features may need external integrations
  • The depth of Framer's editor, while powerful, adds a learning curve beyond simply accepting the AI's first output

Frequently asked questions

How does Framer AI generate a website?

You describe your business or project in a text prompt, and Framer AI generates a complete multi-section website layout that opens in Framer's visual editor.

Can I edit the AI-generated site?

Yes, the generated site is fully editable using Framer's standard no-code design canvas, including layout, styling, and content.

Is Framer AI free to use?

You can build and preview a site for free, but publishing on a custom domain and higher usage limits require a paid plan.

Does Framer AI support blogs or dynamic content?

Yes, sites can use Framer's built-in CMS to manage blog posts or other dynamic collections.

Can I add animations to an AI-generated site?

Yes, since the output lives inside Framer's editor, you have full access to its animation and interaction tools.

Is Framer AI good for e-commerce?

It covers basic website and content needs well, but complex e-commerce functionality may require additional integrations.
