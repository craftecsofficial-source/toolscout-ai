AutoPPT is a reasonable pick for students and light business users who want a quick first-draft deck from a topic or a file, but the free plan only allows a single deck generation, so anyone producing decks regularly must pay. Skip it if you need strict brand governance or heavily custom design.

You type a topic or upload a file (PDF, Word, text, Excel, PPT or image), and it generates a slide deck. You can swap templates, edit text, fonts, colors and tables in an online editor, export to PowerPoint and share a link. The site also bundles related utilities such as AI mind maps and PDF summarization, and claims support for 100+ languages. We could not verify output quality; treat drafts as needing review.

Best for

Best for students, teachers and professionals who need occasional quick decks from a prompt or an existing document. Not for teams that need brand-compliance controls or shared workspaces, nor for anyone who wants to test thoroughly on the free plan, which allows only one generation. Not ideal if you need pixel-level design control.

Key features

01

Prompt-based deck generation

A short text description is expanded by AI into a full slide-by-slide outline and matching visual design.

02

PDF and Word conversion

Existing PDF or Word documents can be uploaded and automatically restructured into a PowerPoint deck.

03

Template library

A range of pre-designed PowerPoint templates is available to apply to AI-generated or converted content.

04

AI mind mapping

Users can generate mind maps to organize ideas visually, complementing the standard linear slide workflow.

05

Slide-level editing

Generated decks remain editable, allowing manual adjustment of text, images, and layout after the AI pass.

06

Multiple export options

Finished presentations can be downloaded in standard PowerPoint format for use in Microsoft Office.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Accepts both a typed topic and uploaded files in several formats (PDF, Word, Excel, PPT, images)
  • Online editor for fonts, colors and tables, plus PowerPoint export and share links
  • Yearly Pro gives 480 generations versus 20 on monthly Pro, so annual billing is far better value
  • Extra utilities like mind maps and PDF tools come in the same account

Cons

  • Free plan allows just one AI deck generation, so it is a demo rather than a usable tier
  • Pro monthly caps at 20 generations and omits chart generation
  • Pro is required to use all templates and upload PDFs, per the site
  • AI drafts need fact-checking and editing; we did not test output quality
  • No team, brand-governance or collaboration features were listed

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

Yes, but it is very limited: one AI PPT generation, one template replacement and one upload/summarization.

How much does Pro cost?

$19.99/month billed monthly, or $12.49/month billed yearly, with higher limits on the yearly plan.

Can it turn a PDF or Word file into slides?

Yes, uploads include PDF, Word, text, Excel, PPT and images, though the site says Pro is needed for PDF upload.

Can I export to PowerPoint?

Yes, decks can be downloaded as PowerPoint files and shared by link.

Is the deck editable after generation?

Yes, an online editor covers fonts, colors, tables and templates.
