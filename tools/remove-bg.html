Remove.bg, developed by Kaleido AI (now part of Canva), pioneered accessible AI background removal for the mainstream market. Users simply upload a photo, and the underlying deep learning model automatically segments the foreground subject from the background in a matter of seconds, delivering a transparent PNG cutout with no manual tracing required.

The tool has grown well beyond a simple web uploader: it now includes desktop apps for Windows and Mac, mobile apps for iOS and Android, plugins for Adobe Photoshop and other design tools, a Chrome extension, and a robust REST API that lets developers embed background removal directly into their own products and workflows. Bulk processing lets e-commerce teams clean up entire product catalogs at once.

Because of its accuracy and ease of integration, Remove.bg has become something of an industry default for background removal, widely used across e-commerce, photography, marketing, and design use cases, and frequently referenced as the benchmark competitors are measured against.

Best for

Remove.bg is ideal for e-commerce businesses needing clean, consistent product photos at scale, as well as photographers and marketers who need quick cutouts without opening a full photo editor. Developers benefit from its API to automate background removal inside their own apps and platforms. Casual users needing an occasional free background removal can also use the tool, though they'll hit resolution limits quickly. It suits anyone who values speed and accuracy over granular manual control.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Delivers highly accurate cutouts for common subjects like people and products with essentially no manual effort
  • Massive integration footprint, including a public API, makes it easy to embed into existing tools and pipelines
  • Bulk upload support saves significant time for e-commerce teams processing large product catalogs
  • Cross-platform availability (web, desktop, mobile, browser extension) fits into almost any workflow
  • Backed by Canva, giving it continued investment and reliability as a long-term tool

Cons

  • Free tier only outputs low-resolution images, pushing most serious users toward paid credits
  • Complex subjects with fine detail such as hair strands, fur, or glass/transparency can produce imperfect edges
  • Cost can scale quickly for high-volume users compared to a flat-fee alternative
  • Occasional misdetections on unusual or cluttered scenes still require manual touch-up
  • API and bulk features add complexity that casual users may not need

Frequently asked questions

Is Remove.bg free to use?

There is a free tier with limited credits and low-resolution downloads; full-resolution results require a paid plan or credits.

How accurate is the background removal?

It performs very well on common subjects like people, products, and animals, though fine details like hair or transparent objects can sometimes need manual correction.

Does Remove.bg have an API?

Yes, it offers a REST API for developers to integrate automatic background removal into their own applications.

Can I remove backgrounds from multiple images at once?

Yes, bulk processing is supported for handling batches of images, such as product catalogs.

Who owns Remove.bg?

Remove.bg was built by Kaleido AI and is now part of Canva.

Are there desktop or mobile apps?

Yes, Remove.bg offers desktop apps, mobile apps, and a browser extension in addition to the web tool.
