Tabscanner is an OCR (optical character recognition) API purpose-built for reading receipts and invoices and converting them into structured, usable data. Rather than returning raw text, it parses documents into fields like merchant name, transaction date, line items, subtotal, tax, and total - ready to drop into an expense report, accounting system, or loyalty program.

The product is aimed squarely at developers: integration happens through a REST API, with responses delivered as JSON and support for batch or asynchronous processing when volume is high. It's commonly used behind the scenes in expense management tools, receipt-scanning consumer apps, and fintech products that need to digitize paper or photographed receipts at scale.

Tabscanner differentiates on speed, accuracy claims, and a straightforward, volume-based pricing model, with an optional human-in-the-loop review layer available for customers who need near-perfect accuracy on messy or low-quality scans.

Best for

Tabscanner is best suited to software teams building expense management, accounting, fintech, or loyalty products who need to convert photographed or scanned receipts and invoices into structured data automatically. It fits companies that want to avoid building their own OCR pipeline from scratch and are comfortable integrating a third-party API. It is less useful for non-technical users looking for an out-of-the-box receipt-scanning app, since Tabscanner is infrastructure, not an end-user tool.

Supports processing large volumes of receipts asynchronously, suited to high-throughput expense or loyalty platforms.

Multi-Language & Currency Support

Handles receipts from multiple countries and currencies, useful for global expense management products.

Fraud & Duplicate Detection

Flags duplicate or potentially fraudulent receipt submissions to help protect expense and reimbursement workflows.

Optional Human-in-the-Loop Review

An add-on service routes low-confidence scans to human reviewers for higher accuracy guarantees.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Purpose-built for receipts and invoices, so extraction is more accurate out-of-the-box than generic OCR for this use case
  • Simple, transparent pricing tiers with a usable free tier for evaluation before committing
  • Designed for developers, with clear API documentation that speeds up integration
  • Handles scale via batch/async processing, useful for apps with spiky or high receipt volume

Cons

  • Not a standalone consumer product - there's no end-user app, so it only fits teams with engineering capacity to integrate an API
  • Costs scale with usage, so unpredictable volume spikes can raise monthly bills quickly outside an enterprise contract
  • Narrow scope (receipts/invoices) means it's not a general-purpose document extraction solution
  • Accuracy on very low-quality or handwritten receipts may still require the paid human-review add-on

Frequently asked questions

Does Tabscanner offer a free trial?

Yes, there is a free plan that includes 200 receipt scans per month, useful for testing integration before upgrading.

What kind of documents can Tabscanner process?

It's optimized for retail receipts and invoices, extracting fields like merchant, date, line items, and totals.

Is Tabscanner a consumer app?

No, it's a developer-facing API meant to be integrated into other software products rather than used directly by end users.

How is pricing structured?

Pricing is based on the number of scans processed per month, with a free tier, a mid-tier Business plan, and custom Enterprise pricing for high volume.

Can Tabscanner guarantee 100% accuracy?

No OCR system guarantees perfect accuracy; Tabscanner offers an optional human-in-the-loop review add-on for customers needing higher accuracy on difficult scans.

Does it support multiple languages and currencies?

Yes, it is built to handle receipts from different countries, languages, and currencies.
