Tabscanner is great for developers who need line-item data out of retail receipts through a simple API, and it has a usable free tier for testing. Skip it if you want a ready-made end-user app, or need to parse many document types such as contracts or forms. It reads a receipt image and returns structured JSON with merchant, date, line items, tax and totals. Pricing is per scan (1 credit = 1 receipt), with optional human-in-the-loop review for higher accuracy, and the company states it holds ISO 27001 and GDPR compliance.

Best for

Good for: engineering teams building expense, loyalty, market-research or accounting software that must capture receipt line items. Not for: non-technical users wanting an app, teams processing general documents beyond receipts and invoices, or anyone needing guaranteed 99.99% accuracy without paying for human review.

Key features

01

Structured Receipt & Invoice Parsing

Extracts vendor, date, line items, totals, and tax fields from receipt and invoice images rather than returning unstructured text.

02

REST API Integration

A straightforward API with SDKs and documentation lets developers upload images and receive structured JSON results.

03

Batch & Async Processing

Supports processing large volumes of receipts asynchronously, suited to high-throughput expense or loyalty platforms.

04

Multi-Language & Currency Support

Handles receipts from multiple countries and currencies, useful for global expense management products.

05

Fraud & Duplicate Detection

Flags duplicate or potentially fraudulent receipt submissions to help protect expense and reimbursement workflows.

06

Optional Human-in-the-Loop Review

An add-on service routes low-confidence scans to human reviewers for higher accuracy guarantees.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Returns line items, not just totals, which is what expense and loyalty products usually need.
  • Published pricing from free to $360/month makes costs predictable before you commit.
  • Human-in-the-loop review is available when automated accuracy is not enough.
  • Code samples in several languages and a live demo uploader shorten the evaluation.

Cons

  • The site advertises 99.99% accuracy, but the company's own FAQ lists 99+% as standard; the higher figure depends on the human-review add-on.
  • Its competitor comparison chart is labelled illustrative, so speed and accuracy claims are not independently verified.
  • Per-scan costs ($0.06 to $0.08 beyond included credits) add up at high volume until you negotiate Enterprise.
  • It is focused on receipts, so other document types need a different tool.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Starter gives 200 credits a month at $0, with no credit card required. One credit equals one receipt scan.

What does a scan cost beyond the included credits?

$0.08 per extra credit on the $24 Per Credit plan and $0.06 on the $360 Business plan. Enterprise is custom.

How accurate is it?

The company's FAQ gives 99+% as the standard rate, with 99.99% available through optional human-in-the-loop review. These are vendor claims, not independently verified.

Can it handle receipts that follow unusual formats?

The Pro Service plan ($500/month) adds custom training on up to 10 new receipt formats per month.

Do I need to be a developer?

Yes, it is an API. The site provides code samples for Python, Node.js and C#.
