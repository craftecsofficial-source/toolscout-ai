R2 Copilot is a good fit for teams in confidentiality-sensitive work who want AI inside Outlook, Word, Excel or Gmail with admin oversight; individuals who just want a cheap chatbot, or anyone who needs independently audited security proof, should look elsewhere. It acts as a managed layer between your staff and several AI models (GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Llama, DeepSeek and self-hosted open-weight models). It ships as add-ins for Microsoft 365 apps and Teams, Google Workspace apps, a Chrome extension and a Telegram bot. The vendor says prompts are encrypted before they leave the device and routed through its own system, and it adds role-based access, policy rules, PII/PHI handling and audit logs. Those privacy and compliance statements come from the vendor's site and could not be verified independently. Pricing is per seat at the bottom (Pro) and flat-rate per group above that.

Best for

Good for legal, healthcare, finance and compliance-minded teams, and small businesses wanting one governed AI across Office and Google apps. Not for students or hobbyists wanting a free assistant, solo users who only need one model (a direct ChatGPT or Claude plan is simpler and cheaper), or buyers who require third-party audit evidence, since compliance claims could not be verified here.

Key features

01

Multi-model access

One interface exposes GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Llama, DeepSeek and, on higher tiers, endpoints you host yourself.

02

Office and Google add-ins

Add-ins for Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote and Teams, plus Gmail, Docs and Sheets, so drafting and analysis happen inside the documents.

03

Encrypted prompt routing

The vendor says prompts are encrypted before leaving the device and split across routes (its SDNP system). This is an unverified vendor claim.

04

Governance and audit

Role-based access, policy rules, PII/PHI handling and logging; Business adds SAML/SCIM SSO, DLP rules and audit-log export.

05

Enterprise deployment

Enterprise lists custom cloud deployment (GovCloud/VPC), bring-your-own-keys, a BAA and custom SLAs.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Covers Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace, so mixed-suite organisations avoid two tools.
  • Multiple model families under one subscription, avoiding separate vendor accounts.
  • Governance features (roles, policies, logs, SSO on Business) are aimed at IT and compliance sign-off.
  • Team tier at $249 flat for up to 10 seats works out to roughly $25 per seat, slightly under the $29 Pro seat price.

Cons

  • No free plan or trial was listed on the pricing page we saw, so you pay before you can fully test.
  • Encryption, no-training and compliance claims are the vendor's own; ask for audit reports before relying on them.
  • Pro is one seat only; teams over 10 need Business at $999/month, a steep step.
  • Enterprise pricing starts at $2,559/month and details need a sales conversation.
  • It resells access to models you could buy directly, so you are paying mainly for the privacy and governance layer.
