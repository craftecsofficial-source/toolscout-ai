Base44 is a good pick for non-developers who want a hosted, working app quickly, and for founders validating an idea cheaply. Skip it if you need tight control over architecture, strict compliance review or very custom logic, and watch the credit limits if you iterate a lot. You describe what you want in chat and the platform generates the screens, backend logic, database and sign-in, then hosts the result. You keep refining by conversation, and paid plans allow in-app code edits and custom domains. It has also added Superagents for automating workflows, payments through Wix Payments, and analytics and SEO tools. Note it is not really a data-analysis product despite its listing category; it is an app builder.

Best for

Good for founders, product managers and non-developers building internal tools, MVPs and simple customer-facing apps. Not for teams that need full control of the codebase and infrastructure, strict compliance or security audits, or heavy custom engineering. Also not a fit if you want a data-analysis or BI tool, despite its category listing.

Key features

01

Chat-based building

Describe an app or website and the AI generates its structure, design and logic; further chat messages adjust it.

02

Bundled backend

Database, file storage and user authentication are included, so no separate services need wiring up.

03

Hosting and domains

Apps go live on the platform; every paid plan lets you connect a custom domain and includes a free domain for the first year.

04

Integrations and payments

One-click links to third-party tools, plus payment handling through Wix Payments.

05

Superagents

AI agents that connect to your tools and automate workflows, per the official site.

06

Analytics and promotion

App analytics, an SEO/GEO dashboard and social content tools for promoting what you build.

07

Design controls

AI design options with previews, global color and font settings, and a shared canvas for feedback.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The free plan includes authentication, database and analytics, so you can test a real app before paying.
  • Frontend, backend, database, auth and hosting arrive together, which saves assembling separate services.
  • Even the $16 Starter plan allows unlimited apps, code edits and a custom domain.
  • Higher tiers add model selection, GitHub integration and premium support when you outgrow the basics.
  • Payments, analytics and SEO tooling are built in, useful for small commercial products.

Cons

  • Everything is metered by credits: 25 messages a month on Free is only enough for a small experiment.
  • Paid prices on the page are annual-billing rates only; monthly rates are not shown, so budget for a yearly commitment.
  • GitHub integration is not on Starter or Free, which limits exporting or collaborating on code at the low end.
  • AI-generated backends still need human review for security and correctness before handling sensitive data.
  • Complex custom logic may take many credit-consuming chat rounds, and enterprise pricing is only available via sales.

Frequently asked questions

Is Base44 free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0, needs no credit card, and gives 25 message credits and 100 integration credits per month with up to 5 apps.

How much do paid plans cost?

Billed annually: Starter $16/mo, Builder $40/mo, Pro $80/mo, Elite $160/mo. Monthly-billing prices are not listed on the pricing page. Enterprise is custom.

What limits should I watch?

Credits. Message credits per month range from 25 (Free) to 1,200 (Elite), and integration credits from 100 to 50,000. Heavy iteration uses them up.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes, on every paid plan, which also includes a free domain for the first year.

Is it owned by Wix?

The site footer shows Wix.com Ltd. copyright and it links to Wix Payments, but the homepage does not state the ownership; it was widely reported as acquired by Wix in 2025.
