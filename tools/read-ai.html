Read AI is a strong pick for people in many video calls who also want one searchable place for meetings, email and chat; it is less compelling if you only need cheap transcription of a few recordings or need more than about 20 languages. A bot joins Google Meet, Zoom and Microsoft Teams calls to produce transcripts, summaries, action items and live meeting metrics, and the Ask Read assistant answers questions with citations across meetings, email and messages. It also offers apps, a Chrome extension, an API, webhooks, an MCP server and an assistant called Ada.

Best for

Good for professionals and teams with heavy meeting loads on Zoom, Meet or Teams who want searchable history and coaching. Not for people who need many languages (the vendor says 20+), free usage beyond 5 meetings a month, or video playback without paying for Enterprise. HIPAA and SSO require Enterprise+ with at least 5 licenses.

Key features

01

Automated meeting recording and transcription

Read's assistant joins scheduled calls on Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams, records the session, and produces a full transcript without manual setup.

02

AI-generated meeting reports

Each meeting produces a structured report with a summary, key topics, and a list of action items, reducing the need to write minutes by hand.

03

Real-time in-call analytics

During the meeting, Read surfaces live metrics on talk time, sentiment, and engagement so hosts can adjust pacing or participation on the spot.

04

Speaker Coach

A coaching feature that gives participants feedback on their speaking patterns, aimed at improving meeting communication over time.

05

Playback and video highlights

Users can replay key moments of a meeting with synced audio and video, including auto-generated highlight clips of notable topics or reactions (Enterprise tier and above).

06

Ask Read cross-source search

A search assistant that answers questions across a user's meetings, emails, and messages, citing the exact source where the information appeared.

07

Ada digital twin

A personal AI agent that learns from a user's behavior over time and can handle task management and email-based workflow automation on their behalf.

08

Broad integration ecosystem

Native connections to Slack, Gmail, Outlook, Google Calendar, Salesforce, HubSpot, Notion, Asana, Jira, Confluence, Linear, Zapier, plus an API, webhooks, and an MCP server for AI tools like Claude and ChatGPT.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Free plan has no card requirement and includes Meeting Coach and Ask Read.
  • Works across Zoom, Meet and Teams and links to Slack, Gmail, Outlook, Salesforce, HubSpot, Notion and Jira.
  • Ask Read gives cited answers across meetings, email and messages instead of just per-meeting notes.
  • Annual volume discounts of 10-20% are listed for 100+ licenses.

Cons

  • Free plan allows 5 meetings per month and 1-hour meetings, which is thin for daily meeting schedules.
  • Playback and video highlights need Enterprise; HIPAA, SSO and SCIM need Enterprise+ with a 5-license minimum.
  • Monthly billing costs roughly a third more than annual, and the pricing page says refunds are not offered and only credit cards are accepted.
  • The vendor lists 20+ languages, fewer than dedicated multilingual transcription tools.

Frequently asked questions

Is Read AI free?

Yes. The free plan covers 5 meetings per month of up to 1 hour each, with no credit card required.

Which meeting platforms work?

Google Meet, Zoom and Microsoft Teams, plus iOS, Android, Windows and macOS apps and a Chrome extension.

What does paid cost?

Annual billing is $15 (Pro), $22.50 (Enterprise) and $29.75 (Enterprise+) per user per month; monthly billing is $19.75, $29.75 and $39.75. Enterprise+ needs 5 or more licenses.

Can I get HIPAA compliance?

The pricing page lists HIPAA compliance only on Enterprise+.

Does it do more than meetings?

Yes. It connects email, calendar and CRM context, offers Ask Read search, and has an assistant called Ada. We did not test how well these work.
