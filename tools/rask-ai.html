Rask AI is a video and audio localization platform built by Brask Inc. that positions itself as an end-to-end dubbing pipeline: upload a video, and the platform transcribes it, translates the script into a target language, clones the original speaker's voice, generates a new voice track, and optionally re-syncs the speaker's lips to match the translated audio. The company reports over 2,000,000 users and counts SEMrush, Mengniu and Publicis among its customers, positioning the product for both individual content creators localizing YouTube or course content and larger organizations translating training, marketing or product videos at scale. Rask differentiates itself from generic dubbing tools by preserving the original speaker's vocal identity and emotional tone across languages, rather than swapping in a generic AI voice, and by supporting automatic multi-speaker detection for videos with more than one person talking.

The product is organized around a per-minute credit system rather than flat generation limits: every minute of translated or dubbed output consumes one "minute" of the plan's monthly (or yearly, on annual plans) allowance, with lip-sync consuming additional minutes depending on whether standard or enhanced lip-sync is used. This usage-based structure scales from a Creator tier aimed at individuals translating a handful of videos, through a Creator Pro tier built for repeat publishing with lip-sync included, up to a Business tier for teams managing a growing video library with reviewer workflows and terminology consistency tools, and a custom Enterprise tier with SLAs, dedicated integrations and managed QA. Rask holds SOC 2 Type II and GDPR compliance, which, combined with enterprise controls like SSO/SAML and a translation dictionary for consistent terminology, makes it a plausible fit for corporate learning-and-development and compliance-sensitive localization workflows, not just creator-scale dubbing.

Best for

Rask AI is a strong fit for course creators, YouTubers, and marketing or L&D teams that need to take existing video content and localize it into many languages quickly while preserving the original speaker's voice. It's a weaker fit for anyone who only needs an occasional one-off translation and wants a permanently free tool, or for teams on a tight budget who will regularly need lip-sync, since that feature multiplies the effective per-minute cost.

Key features

01

AI video translation and dubbing

Translates spoken content in a video into any of 130+ target languages and generates a new dubbed audio track synced to the original video.

02

Emotion-preserving voice cloning

Clones the original speaker's voice into 32 supported languages so the dubbed track sounds like the same person rather than a generic AI voice, preserving tone and emotional inflection.

03

Lip-sync (standard and enhanced)

Adjusts the on-screen speaker's mouth movements to match the translated audio; enhanced lip-sync offers higher fidelity at a higher per-minute credit cost.

04

Multi-speaker detection and translation

Automatically identifies multiple speakers in a single video and translates/dubs each speaker's dialogue with a distinct cloned voice.

05

Built-in editor for scripts and timestamps

Lets users edit the translated transcript, adjust timestamps, relabel speakers, and fine-tune voice settings before final export.

06

Translation dictionary and prompting

Custom glossary and tone/formality prompting ensure specific terms and brand language are translated consistently across every video.

07

Auto subtitles and SRT support

Generates and embeds captions automatically, or exports/imports standard SRT subtitle files for further editing elsewhere.

08

Reviewer and approval workflows

Business and Enterprise tiers add reviewer roles so translated content can be routed for approval before publishing, useful for brand and compliance teams.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Voice cloning across 32 languages preserves the original speaker's vocal identity and emotional tone rather than substituting a generic AI narrator, which noticeably improves how natural the dubbed output feels.
  • The built-in transcript, timestamp and voice editor gives users granular control to fix mistranslations or awkward phrasing before export, instead of relying entirely on automated output.
  • Multi-speaker detection handles interviews, panels and multi-host content automatically, which many single-speaker dubbing tools cannot do well.
  • SOC 2 Type II certification, GDPR compliance and enterprise SSO/SAML support make it credible for corporate L&D and compliance-sensitive localization projects, not just creator content.
  • Annual billing offers steep discounts (up to 45-48% off list monthly pricing) plus minute rollover, rewarding teams that commit to regular localization work.

Cons

  • There is no ongoing free tier — only a single 3-minute trial — so evaluating real-world output quality on a full video requires paying for a plan first.
  • Lip-sync is metered separately from translation and can multiply minute consumption (enhanced lip-sync uses 3 minutes of credit per minute of video), making costs less predictable than they first appear.
  • The entry Creator plan's 25 minutes/month is thin for anyone localizing more than one short video regularly, pushing many real users toward Creator Pro or higher within a month or two.
  • Monthly-plan minutes do not roll over and are forfeited at the end of each billing cycle, unlike annual plans where unused minutes accumulate.
  • Refunds are restricted to technical failures and only within narrow usage and time thresholds (under 25% of plan minutes used, within 14 days), limiting recourse for quality dissatisfaction.

Frequently asked questions

How many languages does Rask AI support?

Rask AI translates from nearly any source language into 135+ target languages, with voice cloning specifically available for 32 of those languages.

Is there a free plan?

There's no ongoing free tier, only a one-time 3-minute free trial that requires no credit card to try the core translation and voice cloning features.

How does the minute-based pricing work?

One minute of credit equals one minute of translated output; lip-sync uses additional minutes on top of translation (1 extra minute for standard lip-sync, 3 extra minutes for enhanced lip-sync per minute of video).

Can I clone my own voice for dubbing?

Yes, Rask clones the original speaker's voice and emotional tone into the target language rather than using a generic AI voice, across 32 supported languages.

Do unused minutes roll over?

On annual plans, unused minutes roll over and accumulate for as long as the subscription is active; on monthly plans, unused minutes are forfeited at the end of each billing cycle.

Is Rask AI suitable for enterprise/compliance use?

Yes, Rask is SOC 2 Type II and GDPR compliant and offers SSO/SAML, translation dictionaries, and managed QA workflows on its Business and Enterprise tiers.
