Rask AI is a good fit for creators and teams who need to turn existing videos into many languages quickly, with the original speaker's voice kept. Skip it if you only need a one-off translation for free or want to use lip-sync heavily on a small budget, because that eats minutes fast. Rask transcribes your video, translates it, clones voices and generates a dubbed track, with optional lip-sync, an editor for corrections and detection of multiple speakers. It translates into 135+ languages and clones voices in 32+. The site lists SOC 2 Type II and GDPR compliance.

Best for

Who it is for: YouTubers, course creators and marketing or training teams localizing finished video into several languages. Who it is NOT for: people wanting a permanent free tool, creators who need generated footage rather than translation (see Kling or Luma), or low-budget users who need frequent lip-sync.

Key features

01

Translation and dubbing

Translates spoken content into 135+ languages and produces a dubbed audio track.

02

Voice cloning

Clones the original speaker's voice in 32+ languages.

03

Lip-sync

Standard lip-sync uses 1 minute per minute of video; enhanced (beta) uses 3 minutes per minute.

04

Multi-speaker detection

Identifies and translates multiple speakers in one video.

05

Captions and SRT

Auto-generated captions plus SRT import and export.

06

Translation dictionary and prompting

Controls terminology and tone for consistent output.

07

API and enterprise controls

API access, plus SSO/SAML, teamspaces, voice presets and human-in-the-loop review on enterprise options.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Keeps the speaker's own voice across 32+ languages instead of a generic narrator
  • Editor lets you correct transcript and timing before export
  • Handles videos with several speakers
  • SOC 2 Type II, GDPR and C2PA membership are listed on the site
  • Clear published prices with 30-33% yearly savings
  • API access for automating large jobs

Cons

  • Only a one-time 3-minute trial, so judging quality on a full video means paying
  • Enhanced lip-sync costs 3 minutes per video minute, so real cost can triple
  • Creator's 25 minutes per month is thin for regular publishers
  • Pricing page is inconsistent in places (FAQ tiers and banner discount differ from the cards)
  • Machine translation of technical terms still needs a human check

Frequently asked questions

How many languages does Rask support?

Translation covers 135+ languages and voice cloning covers 32+.

Is there a free plan?

No ongoing free plan. There is a one-time 3-minute trial with no credit card.

How does lip-sync affect my minutes?

Standard lip-sync counts 1 minute per video minute; enhanced (beta) counts 3 minutes.

Is it suitable for enterprise use?

The site lists SOC 2 Type II and GDPR compliance, and SSO/SAML and human-in-the-loop review on enterprise options.
