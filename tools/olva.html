OLVA is aimed at sales, support and customer-facing people who want live help during a call, and it is weaker for anyone who just wants a hands-off recorder that joins every meeting. Skip it if you need a cloud bot that records meetings you are not attending, or if you want a long-proven vendor with lots of independent reviews. It runs as a desktop app (Windows and macOS) that captures your device's audio instead of joining as a visible participant, and transcribes in real time. While the call is going, it spots questions, answers them from notes and documents you uploaded, offers follow-up suggestions and a coach, and can translate. Afterward it produces summaries, action items, searchable history and even a slide deck. Mobile apps capture the phone microphone for in-person talks, not other apps' audio.

Best for

Individual sales reps, customer success and support staff, interviewees and multilingual-call workers who want in-the-moment prompts and answers. Not for teams that need a bot to record meetings unattended, organizations banning unapproved audio-capture apps, or anyone planning to record people without telling them.

Key features

01

Live Transcription

Real-time speech-to-text covering 87 languages with automatic multilingual detection, so mixed-language meetings get transcribed without manual setup.

02

Question Detection & Instant Answers

OLVA listens for questions as they're asked and answers using connected documents and prior meeting context, surfacing the response on-screen during the call.

03

Live AI Coach

Provides in-the-moment prompts on framing, tone, or missed talking points while the conversation is still happening.

04

Live Translation

Translates the transcript automatically or on demand, aimed at multilingual sales and customer conversations.

05

Bot-Free Capture

Captures audio locally from the device rather than joining as a visible meeting participant, working across Zoom, Meet, Teams, Webex, Slack Huddles, and in-person talks.

06

Post-Meeting Intelligence

After the call, generates summaries, action items, a searchable meeting archive, and can produce a slide-deck recap of the conversation.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • No bot appears in the participant list, avoiding host-permission and etiquette friction.
  • Helps during the call (answers, coaching, translation) instead of only after it.
  • Starts free, and the mid-tier Plus plan at $14.99/mo is far cheaper than enterprise call-intelligence tools.
  • Covers Zoom, Meet, Teams, Webex, Slack Huddles and in-person conversations from one app.
  • Reusable context from your notes and documents grounds the answers it gives.

Cons

  • Free is 2 hours total, not per month, so it is a trial; Starter is only 2 hours monthly.
  • Prices rose versus earlier listings (Plus now $14.99, Pro $24.99), and annual prices are not shown on the pages checked.
  • Local capture means it cannot join or record meetings where the app is not running.
  • Slack, Drive, Notion integrations need Pro or higher, and incognito mode is Pro/Ultra only.
  • Few independent reviews, so accuracy and reliability are largely unverified.
  • Bot-free does not remove recording-consent duties; tell participants where the law requires it.
  • Mobile apps do not capture other apps' system audio, only the phone mic.

Frequently asked questions

How much does OLVA cost?

Free (2 hours total), Starter $2.99/mo, Plus $14.99/mo, Pro $24.99/mo and Ultra $99.99/mo, per the homepage table. Annual prices were not visible; Enterprise is custom.

Is the free plan enough?

Only for trying it out: it gives 2 hours total, not per month. Starter also has only 2 hours per month.

Which plan removes the meeting-time limit?

Pro ($24.99/mo) and Ultra ($99.99/mo) offer unlimited meeting time; Ultra also removes AI usage limits.

Do I need to tell participants I am using it?

Being bot-free does not remove consent requirements; follow your local recording laws and company policy.

How does it differ from Otter or Fathom?

Those typically join as a bot and focus on notes after the call. OLVA captures locally and focuses on live answers and coaching during the call.
