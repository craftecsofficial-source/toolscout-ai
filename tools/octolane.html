Octolane is a strong pick for B2B sales teams tired of manual CRM updates who are willing to review AI-proposed actions, and a poor pick for solo founders who want a cheap or free CRM, or for teams that need a mature, heavily customizable CRM today. It assigns an AI agent to every account that watches emails and meetings, proposes the next step (stage change, follow-up, enrichment), and waits for a human to approve, edit or reject it.

Octolane AI, Inc. sells this as 'Revenue Superintelligence', with OctoRevenue as its first product, layered on your existing CRM and tools. The live pricing page (checked 2026-10-29) now lists Business at $79/seat/month billed yearly, plus custom Team and Enterprise tiers; the earlier $1,000/month 'Best Friend' plan no longer appears there. The company's claims about agent share of revenue work are self-reported and not verified.

Best for

Best for B2B sales and revenue teams with real deal flow who want the CRM to log activity and draft follow-ups for them. Not for solo founders or tiny teams who just need a free contact list, not for teams who do not want AI proposing actions on customer accounts, and not for buyers who need fully published pricing for the Team or Enterprise tiers.

Key features

01

Dedicated Agent per Account

Every account in the CRM gets an always-on AI agent that monitors activity, proposes next actions, and waits for human approval before acting, so coverage isn't limited by rep bandwidth.

02

Self-Updating Pipeline

Reads emails, meeting notes, and other signals to update deal stages and CRM fields automatically instead of requiring reps to manually log activity.

03

AI-Drafted Follow-ups

Generates follow-up emails based on deal context and prior conversations, which reps can approve, edit, or reject before sending.

04

Predictive Lead Scoring

Analyzes account and contact behavior to prioritize which deals and leads a rep should work on first each day.

05

Company and Contact Enrichment

Enriches CRM records with firmographic and contact data automatically, reducing manual research before outreach.

06

Natural Language Reporting

Lets users query pipeline data and generate forecasts using plain-language questions instead of building manual dashboards.

07

Learning from Human Corrections

Every time a rep approves, edits, or rejects a proposed action, that decision becomes a signal the agent uses to improve future recommendations for that specific team.

08

Enterprise Security and Compliance

Built with SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR, and CCPA compliance, with zero shared-model training and an audit log on every automated action.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The agent-per-account design tries to fix CRM data decay by proposing updates from real email and meeting activity rather than waiting for reps to type.
  • Every proposed action needs human approval, and the company says corrections train a model only for your team.
  • Enterprise tier lists SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR and CCPA, which suits security-conscious buyers.
  • The Business plan has unlimited seats and a published price, much lower than the older $1,000/month entry point.
  • Works alongside existing CRM and tools instead of forcing a rip-and-replace, per the company.

Cons

  • Business limits are tight for active outbound: 500 emails/month, 10 workflows, one email/calendar account and 500 lifetime company identifications.
  • Usage is metered by AI credits ($60/mo included), so heavy use could cost more; overage terms could not be verified.
  • Team and Enterprise are demo-only, so you cannot budget without a sales call.
  • Reporting, forecasting and end-to-end agents are in the Team tier, not Business.
  • Many claims (share of revenue work handled, pipeline managed) are self-reported and not independently verified.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Octolane cost?

Business is $79 per seat per month billed yearly. Team and Enterprise are custom — contact sales. The older $1,000/month 'Best Friend' plan was not on the live pricing page when we checked.

Is there a free plan or trial?

No free plan was listed. The Business plan shows a 7-day trial.

What are the main limits on Business?

500 emails per month, 10 automation workflows, one email/calendar account, 6 months of activity history, 500 lifetime companies identified and $60/month of AI credits.

Does it replace HubSpot or Salesforce?

Octolane says it layers on top of your existing CRM and tools. Check which CRM integrations apply to your plan; overlap between tiers could not be fully verified.

How is it different from 11x?

11x is an AI SDR for outbound prospecting; Octolane positions itself as a CRM layer spanning the account lifecycle.
