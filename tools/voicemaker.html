Voicemaker is a good fit if you want inexpensive, no-frills narration across many languages and are happy picking from stock voices; skip it if you need voice cloning on a regular plan, studio-grade emotional range, or a free tier you can properly test with. It converts text to speech with a large catalogue of voices (the homepage claims 2,000+, other pages say 1,000+), and adds speech-to-speech conversion, speech-to-text with SRT export, an audio workspace with effects, and a pay-as-you-go API.

Everything runs on shared credits. Standard voices cost 1 credit per character, mid-tier ProPlus and Pro V2 voices 2, and the most expressive models 4, so a $5 plan that looks like four hours of audio shrinks quickly if you favour the premium voices. Cloning is reserved for Enterprise (with a $2/year clone slot add-on on eligible plans), which is the main reason ElevenLabs-style users may look elsewhere. Page figures are not fully consistent: the pricing page says 140 languages on paid plans while the homepage says about 130 for TTS, and the FAQ and plan table disagree on hours of audio per credit.

Best for

Creators, YouTubers, e-learning builders and small teams who need affordable multilingual voiceovers from stock voices, and developers who prefer usage-based API billing. Not for anyone who needs custom voice cloning on a self-serve plan, the most natural emotional delivery available, or a generous free tier for testing (250 characters per conversion, personal use only).

Key features

01

Large multilingual voice library

Homepage claims 2,000+ AI voices; TTS language coverage is cited at about 130 (homepage) to 140 (pricing page). Voice model tiers consume credits at 1x, 2x or 4x per character.

02

VoxFX voice effects

A library of 100+ creative voice effects and transformation techniques for stylizing generated speech, included free for unlimited conversions on paid plans.

03

Speech-to-Speech conversion

Voice conversion and redubbing tools that let users transform one recorded voice into another, billed at 100 credits per second of audio.

04

Speech-to-Text transcription

Transcription support for 90+ languages with SRT subtitle export, billed at 10 credits per second of audio.

05

SSML and pronunciation controls

A pronunciation editor and full SSML support let users fine-tune pacing, emphasis, and word-level pronunciation in generated audio.

06

VoxStudio all-in-one workspace

An integrated workspace for mixing multiple voices, background music, and audio files into a single production, available from the Starter plan up.

07

Developer API

A standalone pay-as-you-go REST API platform for TTS, STT, and STS with full documentation, code samples, and commercial-use rights for production workloads.

08

Team and audiobook plans

Teams ($30) and Business ($50) add shared credit management, member administration and 3-month rollover; the pricing page lists no credit allowance for them. A separate $25/year Audiobook & Podcast plan gives 1,000,000 credits and 100,000 characters per conversion.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Low entry cost: $5/month gets 200,000 credits with commercial use, and the $25/year Audiobook & Podcast plan is cheap for long-form narration (100,000 characters per conversion).
  • One credit balance spans text-to-speech, speech-to-speech and speech-to-text, so you do not need separate subscriptions for related audio jobs.
  • Broad voice and language catalogue with SSML and a pronunciation editor for fixing awkward words.
  • Developer API is billed per use rather than flat subscription, which suits variable production workloads.
  • Top-ups at $20 per 1M credits let heavy months be covered without jumping a plan tier.

Cons

  • Free plan allows only 250 characters per conversion and personal use, so it is hard to judge the paid voices before paying.
  • Credit multipliers matter: Expressive and High-Res models cost 4 credits per character and Pro V2/ProPlus Turbo 2, so the advertised 'hours of audio' assume cheap voices. CJK characters cost 2 credits each.
  • Voice cloning is not on Starter, Creator or Pro; only Enterprise includes it.
  • Credits do not roll over on Starter or Creator, and Pro rolls over for just one cycle on monthly billing.
  • Teams and Business list $30 and $50 with no stated credit allowance or billing period, so compare carefully before buying.
  • Voice quality varies by tier and language; the cheaper default voices can sound synthetic (unverified here; judge from samples).

Frequently asked questions

How does the credit system work?

Credits are spent when you click Convert, not on download. Standard voices use 1 credit per character, Pro V2 and ProPlus Turbo 2, Expressive and High-Res 4. CJK characters cost 2 credits. Speech-to-speech is 100 credits per second, speech-to-text 10 per second.

Is there a free plan, and can I use it commercially?

Yes, but it is limited to 250 characters per conversion, a limited voice library and personal use only. Commercial use starts on Starter.

Can I clone my voice?

Per the pricing page, voice cloning is included only on Enterprise, and Starter, Creator and Pro are not eligible. A $2/year voice clone slot add-on requires an eligible plan.

Do unused credits roll over?

Starter and Creator: no. Pro: one billing cycle, monthly plan only. Teams and Business: three months.

How is the API priced?

Pay-as-you-go and usage-based with commercial use included. Per-unit rates are not shown on the public pricing page; check the developer dashboard.
