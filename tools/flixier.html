Flixier is a good pick for marketers, course creators and small teams who want quick, caption-heavy video edits in a browser without needing a fast computer. It is a weaker fit for editors who need deep colour grading, offline work or the full power of desktop tools like Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve, and the best AI features are locked behind the $39/seat Creator plan. It is a multi-track timeline editor where editing and exporting happen on Flixier's servers instead of your device. On top of that sit AI captions and translation (130+ languages per the site), edit-by-transcript, a new assistant called Eva that can cut silences and filler words and find clips in long recordings, and AI video generation inside the timeline using third-party models. It also includes screen and webcam recording, a brand kit and team review comments.

Best for

Who it is for: social and marketing teams, course creators, agencies and anyone turning recordings into captioned clips on a modest computer or across several devices. Who it is not for: people who need offline editing, heavy compositing or advanced colour work, anyone on a poor internet connection, or users who want AI generation on a budget, since those tools need Creator or above.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Cloud rendering means export speed does not depend on your own hardware, and projects open from any browser
  • Captions, translation, transcript editing, recording and AI generation sit in one tool, so there is less exporting between apps
  • A free plan lets you test the editor with no payment, though exports carry a watermark
  • Eva can do a first-pass cut (silences, filler words, captions) which saves time on talking-head and course footage
  • Team comments and a brand kit suit agencies and small marketing teams

Cons

  • Fully browser-based, so large uploads and editing suffer on slow or unstable connections
  • Free plan is tight: 10 export minutes a month, 720p, watermark, 2 GB storage and 3-day project history
  • Starter caps exports at 60 minutes per seat a month and 1080p; 4K and unlimited exports need Creator
  • Eva and AI generation need Creator or higher, and AI generation uses credits; credit allotments were not verified on the pricing page, so check before relying on it
  • Yearly prices are not shown on the pricing page, so true annual cost has to be confirmed at checkout
  • Generation quality depends on the third-party models used, not on Flixier

Frequently asked questions

Is Flixier free?

There is a free plan with 10 minutes of export per month, 720p, 2 GB storage and a Flixier watermark. Paid plans start at $19/seat/month.

Which plan do I need for AI features?

The site says Eva and AI video generation require Creator ($39/seat/month) or higher. Check the live page for current credit allowances.

How much does yearly billing save?

The pricing page advertises 'Save 52%' for yearly billing but does not list annual prices, so confirm the figure at checkout.

Will it run on an old computer?

Largely yes, since rendering happens in the cloud, but you still need a reliable internet connection for uploading and editing.

Is it a replacement for Premiere Pro?

For quick marketing, course and social edits, often yes. For advanced colour, effects or offline work, no.
