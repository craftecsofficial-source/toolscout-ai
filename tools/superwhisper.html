superwhisper is great for people who would rather talk than type across many apps, particularly on Apple Silicon Macs where offline models run well. Skip it if you want a single flat free tool for transcribing recordings or an Intel Mac offline setup. The core loop is hold a hotkey, speak, release, and the cleaned-up text lands in whatever app has focus. Modes control tone, custom vocabulary handles names and jargon, and a Mac meeting feature produces notes.

Best for

Good for heavy dictators: writers, founders, developers and anyone replying to lots of email and chat. Not for Intel Mac users wanting offline use, people who need free file or video transcription (that is Pro), or buyers who need a verified fixed price today, since Pro price could not be confirmed.

Key features

01

Dictate anywhere

Push-to-talk or hotkey capture, with the edited transcript pasted into the active app.

02

Modes

Predefined Voice, Message, Email, Formal, Casual, Legal and Chat modes, plus custom modes with your own prompts and model choice.

03

Offline and cloud models

Works without Wi-Fi using local models; cloud models are also available. Offline performs best on Apple Silicon.

04

Languages and translation

100+ languages; Pro translates any language to English.

05

Meetings and files

Meetings recording and notes on Mac (listed on Free); audio/video file transcription on Pro.

06

Custom vocabulary

Save names, abbreviations and technical terms.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Hotkey dictation works across apps, which beats per-app dictation for mixed workflows.
  • Local transcription means audio need not leave your machine.
  • Modes and custom prompts reduce post-dictation editing.
  • Free tier plus 3,000-word Pro trial lets you test before paying.

Cons

  • Offline quality and speed depend on Apple Silicon; Intel Macs are steered to cloud models.
  • Cloud AI modes send text to third-party models, which matters for confidential material.
  • Pro price could not be verified from live pages today; confirm in the app or site before buying.
  • Windows, iOS and Android apps were not tested here, so feature parity with Mac is unverified.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work offline?

macOS, Windows, iOS and Android per the site today.
