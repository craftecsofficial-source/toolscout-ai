Apex Vision AI suits students who want fast, step-by-step help on questions sitting in their LMS and who will use it to learn rather than to copy answers. Anyone whose course bans AI assistance, or who wants a general-purpose chatbot, should skip it. It is a Chrome extension with iOS and Android apps that reads the question open on a Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle or Top Hat page, including images and answer options, and returns a worked explanation. A guided mode gives escalating hints instead of the answer, and the same subscription covers flashcards, study guides, practice exams, a math solver and an essay helper. Usage is metered in requests: a free allowance of 30 that refills every 12 hours, then 500, 1,000 or 2,000 per month on paid plans. Accuracy and satisfaction figures on the site are the vendor's own and could not be checked independently.

Best for

Good for undergraduates who study inside an LMS and want explanations, flashcards and practice tests. Not for students whose institution prohibits AI help on graded work, not for anyone who wants an open-ended general chatbot or unlimited usage, and not for professionals outside education.

Key features

01

On-page question reading

Reads the question already open in a browser-based LMS such as Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle or Top Hat, including diagrams and options, and explains it in place.

02

Image solver

Photograph a worksheet or textbook problem and get a worked solution.

03

Guided study and voice tutor

Offers progressively stronger hints, and lets you ask questions aloud and hear answers.

04

Study material generators

Builds flashcards, study guides and practice exams from your notes or course material.

05

Math solver and essay helper

Dedicated tools for equations with worked steps and for drafting or improving essays.

06

AI Agents

Listed on the pricing page only under the Mastermind plan; the vendor describes them as agents for completing homework and making notes.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Works inside the LMS page, so there is no copy-paste step between the question and the assistant.
  • The free tier needs no card and refills every 12 hours, enough for light use.
  • Entry price of $7.99/mo is low, and one-time packs that never expire help with irregular use.
  • Guided mode offers hints before answers, which supports learning if the student chooses it.

Cons

  • Requests are capped per month and reset each billing period, so heavy users will hit limits or buy packs.
  • Plans are split by subject: the cheapest covers history, English and intro sciences, while STEM and AI Agents need the $19.99 tier.
  • The 1-day trials on the two lower plans convert automatically, so forgetting to cancel costs money.
  • Using it on graded quizzes can violate academic-integrity policies; that risk is the student's.
  • The 93%+ accuracy and review figures are vendor claims; we could not verify them.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

Yes. You get 30 requests, refilled every 12 hours, with no credit card required.

How much do paid plans cost?

$7.99/month for 500 requests, $14.99/month for 1,000 and $19.99/month for 2,000. One-time request packs run $3.99 to $19.99.

Which LMS platforms does it work with?

The vendor lists Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle and Top Hat and says it works in browser-based LMS pages generally.

Do I need the top plan for STEM?

The pricing page positions Mastermind ($19.99) for STEM subjects and includes AI Agents only there; the cheaper tiers target other subjects.

Is it safe to use on graded work?

That depends on your school's policy. Many courses prohibit AI help on assessments, so check before using it on anything graded.
