Apex Vision AI is an AI-powered study assistant available as a Chrome extension and as iOS and Android apps, built specifically for students working inside a learning management system (LMS) such as Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle, or Top Hat. Rather than requiring students to copy a question into a separate chat window, the tool reads the question already open on the page - including diagrams, graphs, and multiple-choice answer options - and returns a worked, step-by-step explanation, typically within a couple of seconds. It offers six ways to access help: an AI Image Solver for photographed problems, an extension pop-up, on-screen instant-assistance buttons embedded in the LMS, a side panel, a browser toolbar, and one-click study-guide generation. The company markets the product directly against ChatGPT and Chegg, positioning itself as purpose-built for coursework rather than a general assistant or an answer-sharing database.

What sets Apex Vision AI apart is its LMS-aware context and its "Guided Study" mode, which detects where a student is stuck and offers progressively stronger hints rather than the final answer outright, then generates similar practice questions to confirm understanding. A Voice Tutor feature lets students ask questions aloud and receive a spoken explanation tied to whatever page they're viewing. The platform also automatically routes different question types (math, essay, science) to the model best suited to answer them. Beyond homework help, it bundles an AI flashcard maker, study guide generator, practice exam generator, math solver, and essay helper into the same subscription. It has built a strong reputation among students, holding a 4.9-star rating from roughly 1,900 public reviews on the Chrome Web Store and a 4.9-star iOS App Store rating with over 10,000 downloads.

Best for

Best for college and university students who regularly work inside an LMS like Canvas or Blackboard and want fast, step-by-step explanations alongside auto-generated study materials. It's a weaker fit for younger students, instructors, or anyone looking for a general-purpose chatbot rather than a coursework-specific tool, and its answer-focused framing means some instructors may restrict its use.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Holds a 4.9/5 rating across roughly 1,900 public ratings on the Chrome Web Store, and a matching 4.9/5 on the Apple App Store.
  • Works directly inside the LMS a student is already using (Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle, Top Hat) rather than requiring them to copy questions into a separate chatbot.
  • Guided Study mode teaches with progressive hints rather than just handing over answers, which student reviews specifically call out as more helpful than raw answer tools.
  • Multi-modal input (text, screenshot, photo, voice) covers more real-world homework scenarios than a text-only chatbot.
  • Bundles flashcards, study guides, practice exams, a math solver and an essay helper into one subscription instead of requiring separate tools.

Cons

  • The core value proposition - fast answers to homework and quiz questions - sits close to academic-integrity gray areas depending on how instructors and students use it.
  • Free tier is capped at 30 requests per day, which heavy users will exhaust quickly.
  • The strongest accuracy (93%+) and fastest responses are reserved for the top $19.99/month tier, so casual users get a weaker experience.
  • Primarily built for the U.S. college LMS market; support for non-English coursework or non-standard LMS platforms is not emphasized in the marketing.

What reviewers say

Apex Vision AI does not appear on G2 or Capterra, but is strongly rated on its primary distribution channels - the Chrome Web Store and Apple App Store - where students consistently praise accuracy and ease of use.

Frequently praised

  • Explanations are described as clear, accurate and consistently helpful across subjects
  • Works smoothly without needing to switch tabs between the LMS and a separate AI tool
  • Several reviewers say it improved their grades on quizzes and assignments

Frequently criticized

  • A few users note the daily free-tier request limit feels restrictive
  • Some reviewers compare it to other "snap and solve" tools and say accuracy varies by subject complexity

Frequently asked questions

Which LMS platforms does Apex Vision AI work with?

It works inside Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle, Top Hat and virtually any browser-based LMS, reading the question already on the page rather than requiring a copy-paste.

Is Apex Vision AI free?

Yes, there's a free plan with 30 requests per day and no credit card required; paid plans start at $7.99/month for higher volume and accuracy.

Does it just give answers, or does it explain them?

Every response includes step-by-step reasoning, and its Guided Study mode is designed to give hints and check understanding rather than only stating the final answer.

Is it available on mobile?

Yes, native iOS and Android apps are available alongside the Chrome extension, with a camera scanner for photographing problems.

Does Apex Vision AI collect my school login or course data?

No, it does not request school passwords; it operates via an on-page workflow and states it does not access LMS credentials.

How accurate is it?

The company advertises 93%+ accuracy on its top-tier Mastermind plan, with accuracy varying by question complexity and subscription tier.
