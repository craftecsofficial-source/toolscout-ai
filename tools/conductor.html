Conductor is a strong choice for mid-size and enterprise marketing teams that want AI-search tracking, content help and site monitoring in one place, and it is one of the few in this class with a try-for-free entry tier. Skip it if you want a plain price tag, a lightweight prompt tracker, or a solo-consultant budget. It tracks brand visibility across several AI assistants and classic search, links that to traffic, assists with content drafting, and monitors site health including how AI bots crawl. Tiers are usage-based, with published allowances for keywords, pages and drafts but no prices.

Best for

Good for in-house SEO and content teams at growing or large companies. Not for freelancers or small agencies needing cheap self-serve pricing, or teams wanting only a narrow prompt-tracking tool. Essentials limits (3 sites, 5 competitors) will not fit multi-brand organizations.

Key features

01

Conductor Intelligence

Tracks brand visibility in ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Claude and traditional search and connects it to traffic and revenue.

02

Conductor Creator

AI tools to generate and optimize content for search and LLMs; plans include annual Writing Assistant draft quotas.

03

Conductor Monitoring

Always-on website health monitoring, including AI bot crawl behavior, with real-time alerts.

04

AgentStack

LLM apps, developer tools and turnkey agents for AEO workflows, plus APIs and an MCP server.

05

Tiered usage allowances

Keywords, competitors, pages analyzed and monitored scale from Essentials to Enterprise.

Contact sales

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Published allowances let you size a tier before talking to sales.
  • Essentials can be tried before commitment.
  • Tracks Claude and Copilot in addition to ChatGPT and Gemini.
  • Technical monitoring of AI-bot crawling is rare among AEO tools.

Cons

  • No prices at any tier, so budgeting needs a sales call.
  • Usage-based billing may produce uneven costs.
  • Essentials limits are modest for large sites.
  • How long Essentials stays free versus a paid trial is not clear from the page.
