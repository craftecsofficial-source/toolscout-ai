Conductor has been an enterprise SEO platform for over a decade, historically known as Conductor Searchlight, serving brands like Microsoft, Verizon, American Express, and Adidas with keyword tracking, content optimization, and technical SEO monitoring. As AI answer engines began reshaping search, Conductor extended its existing 'Unified Data Engine' into AEO, launching Conductor Intelligence for AI visibility tracking and Conductor AgentStack for optimizing how content is surfaced to LLMs and AI agents. This gives Conductor's AEO features a foundation in over a decade of proprietary SEO data rather than being a from-scratch AI-monitoring startup.

Its core differentiator versus Adobe LLM Optimizer and BrightEdge is breadth combined with a single unified platform: Conductor positions itself as an all-in-one suite (Creator for content generation, Intelligence for AEO/SEO reporting, Monitoring for technical site health, AgentStack for agentic optimization) so customers avoid a 'stack tax' of paying for multiple disconnected tools. Pricing is also usage-based rather than flat-seat licensing, scaling with pages analyzed, keywords tracked, and AI search credits consumed.

Best for

Conductor fits large, cross-functional enterprise organizations, especially those with dedicated SEO and content teams, that want one platform to handle content creation, technical site monitoring, and AI-search visibility together rather than buying separate tools for each. Smaller teams, solo marketers, or budget-constrained organizations should look at lighter-weight, self-serve AEO or AI-visibility tools instead, since Conductor's custom pricing, annual contracts, and platform depth are built for enterprise scale and come with a real learning curve.

Conductor Intelligence (AEO + SEO reporting)

An AI writing assistant that generates and scores content drafts, with content profiles and internal link recommendations to support topical authority.

Conductor Monitoring

Continuous 24/7 technical monitoring across up to hundreds of thousands of pages and multiple tracked websites to catch issues before they hurt visibility.

04

Conductor AgentStack

A layer purpose-built for optimizing how content is structured and surfaced to LLMs, apps, and AI agents, extending beyond passive answer-engine tracking into agentic search readiness.

05

Citation and share-of-voice mapping

Shows where a brand is cited (or missing) across AI answer engines relative to competitors.

06

Content Score and Content Profiles

Scores draft and published content against topical and competitive benchmarks to guide what to create or revise next.

07

Usage-based tiered plans

Essentials, Growth, and Enterprise tiers scale limits on writing drafts, AI search credits, tracked keywords/competitors, and monitored pages rather than charging per seat.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • A genuinely unified platform: content creation, AEO/SEO reporting, technical monitoring, and agent-facing optimization all live in one system rather than requiring separate point tools.
  • Built on a mature, decade-plus data engine from its Searchlight SEO roots, giving its AI-visibility benchmarking more historical depth than newer entrants.
  • Customer support is consistently one of the most praised aspects across G2 reviews, with responsive teams that participate actively in customer meetings.
  • Usage-based pricing tiers scale with actual consumption rather than flat per-seat licensing, which can align cost more closely to value at scale.
  • Trusted by a long roster of major global brands (Microsoft, Verizon, American Express, Adidas, PayPal), suggesting proven reliability at enterprise scale.
  • AgentStack gives it a forward-looking angle on optimizing for AI agents and app-embedded search, not just chatbot answer boxes.

Cons

  • Pricing is entirely custom and not published; third-party estimates and user reports put annual costs anywhere from roughly $60,000 to $180,000+ depending on scale, a steep commitment for smaller marketing teams.
  • Multiple reviewers describe a steep learning curve, with a UI and navigation that can feel complex or dated relative to newer, more streamlined AI-visibility-only tools.
  • Some users report aggressive or persistent sales outreach during and after the evaluation process.
  • A handful of reviews mention performance issues, including slow load times and data not loading reliably within the platform.
  • As with most enterprise SEO incumbents, AEO/AI-visibility tracking was added on top of an existing SEO product rather than built ground-up for AI answer engines, so depth in pure AEO can lag dedicated AEO-only startups on newer AI-specific signals.
  • Long, sales-assisted procurement cycle with annual contracts makes it a poor fit for teams that want to try a tool quickly and scale usage month to month.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Conductor cost?

Conductor does not publish pricing; it sells three usage-based tiers (Essentials, Growth, Enterprise) that scale with pages analyzed, tracked keywords, AI search credits, and monitored pages. Third-party estimates and user reports suggest typical enterprise spend in the range of roughly $60,000 to $180,000+ per year, but you'll need a custom quote.

How is Conductor different from dedicated AEO-only tools?

Conductor bundles AEO/AI-visibility tracking together with traditional SEO reporting, AI content generation, and technical site monitoring in one unified platform built on over a decade of proprietary SEO data, rather than being a single-purpose AI-visibility tracker.

Which AI engines does Conductor track?

Conductor tracks visibility and citations across major AI answer engines including ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, and Google AI Overviews/AI Mode, alongside traditional organic search.

Is Conductor a good fit for smaller teams?

Generally no. Conductor is priced and built for enterprise-scale, cross-functional teams with custom annual contracts; smaller or budget-constrained teams typically find it too expensive and feature-dense relative to lighter self-serve AEO tools.

What is Conductor AgentStack?

AgentStack is Conductor's layer purpose-built for optimizing how content is structured and surfaced not just to AI answer engines but to LLM-powered apps and autonomous agents, extending Conductor's AEO focus beyond chatbot answer tracking.

Does Conductor offer a free trial?

Conductor offers a free trial and a free AI visibility analysis tool on its site, though full platform access for the Growth and Enterprise tiers requires talking to sales for a custom quote.
