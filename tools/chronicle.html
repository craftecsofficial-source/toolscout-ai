Chronicle is an agentic AI platform focused on one problem: helping large content brands find and grow the right audiences on social media before, during, and after a launch. Rather than treating social marketing as a series of disconnected posting and ad-buying tasks, Chronicle frames it as a continuous loop of discovery, simulation, acquisition, and measurement, with AI agents handling the research and optimization work that would otherwise require a dedicated growth team. The platform pulls together creator and audience mapping, content concept testing, and campaign optimization into a single system meant to support franchises as they expand from a single show, game, or artist into a broader cultural presence.

The company is LA-based and operates under 360 Media Holdings, having closed an oversubscribed seed round in mid-2025, which points to a young but well-capitalized product still building out its customer base. Chronicle's differentiator is its focus on IP and franchise-scale audience growth rather than generic social media management: its features are explicitly built around validating new content or franchise bets against real audience demand, simulating how a brand's content might land before committing production budget, and then measuring results all the way through to commercial outcomes. This positions it less as a scheduling or analytics tool and more as an audience strategy layer sitting above the usual marketing stack.

Best for

Chronicle is best suited to marketing, growth, and franchise-strategy teams inside media studios, animation and game companies, podcast networks, music labels, sports organizations, and enterprise brands that are scaling an IP or talent property across multiple platforms and territories. These teams typically have complex, high-stakes launches where guessing at audience fit is expensive, and they need a system that can simulate and test before committing budget, then optimize and measure spend afterward. It's a poor fit for solo creators, small businesses, or lean marketing teams looking for an affordable, self-serve social media tool, since Chronicle is priced and positioned as an enterprise platform accessed only through a sales conversation.

Key features

01

Audience discovery

Maps creators and audience segments globally to surface where a brand's most relevant fans and lookalike audiences already exist online.

02

Competitor and market analysis

Benchmarks a brand's content and audience footprint against comparable franchises to spot gaps and opportunities.

03

Content and concept testing

Lets teams test story formats, characters, or content concepts with real audiences before committing to full production.

04

Franchise simulation

Runs brand-specific modeling to forecast how a piece of content or IP might perform offline, ahead of a public launch.

05

Creative optimization

A/B tests titles, thumbnails, and posting schedules to improve how content performs once it's live.

06

Targeted audience acquisition

Runs campaigns aimed at converting discovered audience segments into engaged followers and customers.

07

Full-funnel measurement

Tracks results from initial engagement through to downstream revenue, connecting social activity to business outcomes.

08

Cross-platform, cross-genre coverage

Designed to operate across many social platforms and content categories under one unified system, rather than one channel at a time.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Unifies research, testing, campaign execution, and measurement in one platform instead of stitching together separate research, ad, and analytics tools.
  • The franchise-simulation and content-testing features let brands validate audience demand before spending on production or a full launch.
  • Coverage spans many platforms and content genres, which suits brands managing IP across film, games, music, sports, and other formats simultaneously.
  • Full-funnel measurement connects social growth activity to commercial results, which is useful for justifying marketing spend to leadership.
  • Recent, well-funded seed round suggests ongoing investment in the product and support for enterprise customers.
  • Positioned specifically for large-scale IP and franchise growth, a niche that generic social media tools don't address directly.

Cons

  • Pricing is entirely custom and hidden behind a sales demo, making it hard to budget for or compare against other tools without a call.
  • No free trial or low-commitment tier means smaller teams can't evaluate the platform hands-on before buying in.
  • The product's messaging and feature set are clearly built for large franchises and studios, so independent marketers and small businesses are unlikely to be a fit.
  • As a relatively new company built on a 2025 seed round, its long-term track record and platform maturity are still unproven.
  • Heavy reliance on AI agents for audience simulation and forecasting means outputs still need human judgment before major production or budget decisions.
  • Being enterprise and demo-gated, onboarding and time-to-value likely involve a longer sales and implementation cycle than typical marketing tools.

Frequently asked questions

What does Chronicle do?

Chronicle is an AI-powered audience platform that helps media brands and franchises discover, test, and acquire social media audiences, then measure results across the full funnel.

Who is Chronicle designed for?

It's built for media studios, game and animation companies, music and podcast networks, sports organizations, and enterprise brands scaling an IP or talent property, rather than solo creators or small businesses.

How much does Chronicle cost?

Chronicle does not publish pricing; it uses a custom, enterprise pricing model and requires prospective customers to book a demo to get a quote.

Does Chronicle offer a free trial?

No free trial or free plan is available; access is arranged through a sales demo.

What makes Chronicle different from typical social media management tools?

Chronicle focuses on franchise-scale audience strategy, including simulating and testing content before launch and tracking full-funnel revenue impact, rather than just scheduling posts or reporting basic engagement metrics.
