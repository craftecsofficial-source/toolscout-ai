Lavender is a good fit for outbound sales teams that want feedback on their cold email and are happy to work inside Chrome. It is a poor fit if you need a general writing assistant, coaching for LinkedIn or calls, or are a solo rep who only sends a handful of emails (the free plan stops at 5 analyses a month). It centres on Email Coach, a Chrome extension that studies your past emails and scores new drafts, plus Ora, a newer AI sales agent that writes outbound emails for you. The site also offers CRM enrichment, a sales email course, a newsletter and a team leaderboard. The vendor says it is SOC2 certified and GDPR compliant. Paid Email Coach plans start at $27 a month on annual billing, while Ora is priced per agent at $500 a month, which puts it in a different budget bracket.

Best for

Who it is for: SDRs, account executives and sales managers who send high volumes of outbound email and want data-driven feedback in Chrome. Who it is not for: people who want a general writing assistant, multi-channel coaching beyond email, or a solo user who just wants a free email checker (the free plan is capped at 5 analyses a month).

Pros and cons

Pros

  • You can install Email Coach for free, so you can judge it before any commitment.
  • It learns from your own sent emails, which is more relevant than generic writing advice.
  • Managers get a team view and a leaderboard, which turns scores into coaching.
  • The vendor claims SOC2 and GDPR compliance, which helps with security reviews (we have not audited this).
  • Having both a coach and a drafting agent covers review and generation in one vendor.

Cons

  • The free Basic plan only covers 5 email analyses and 5 personalizations a month, so regular senders will need Starter ($27/mo annually, $29 monthly) or above.
  • Ora is priced separately at $500 per agent per month once launched, so the AI SDR side is a much bigger commitment than the coaching extension.
  • The focus is email; we found no evidence of LinkedIn or cold-call coaching.
  • The emphasis has moved to Ora, so the coaching product may get less attention than before.
  • Third-party review claims about bugs, support and repetitive suggestions are unverified and not repeated here.

Frequently asked questions

Is Lavender free?

Yes. The Basic plan is free forever and includes 5 email analyses and 5 personalizations a month. Paid plans come with a 7-day free trial and no card is needed.

How much does Lavender cost?

Email Coach Starter is $27/mo billed annually ($29 monthly), Individual Pro $45/mo ($49 monthly) and Team $89 per seat/mo ($99 monthly). Ora costs $500 per agent per month once launched (verified 2026-11-11).

What is Ora?

Ora is Lavender's AI sales agent that drafts outbound emails. It is separate from the Email Coach extension.

Does Lavender only work for email?

The pages we read describe email coaching only. We did not find LinkedIn or call-script coaching.

How does Lavender compare with MailMaestro or Grammarly?

Lavender is built for sales outreach and learns from your sent emails. MailMaestro is a general email assistant for Gmail and Outlook, and Grammarly is a broad grammar and tone checker.
