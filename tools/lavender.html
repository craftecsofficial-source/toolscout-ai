Lavender is a good fit for outbound sales teams that want feedback on their cold email and are happy to work inside Chrome. It is a poor fit if you need a general writing assistant, coaching for LinkedIn or calls, or a price you can check before you sign up. It centres on Email Coach, a Chrome extension that studies your past emails and scores new drafts, plus Ora, a newer AI sales agent that writes outbound emails for you. The site also offers CRM enrichment, a sales email course, a newsletter and a team leaderboard. The vendor says it is SOC2 certified and GDPR compliant. Current prices were not visible on the site when checked, so earlier numbers we listed have been removed.

Best for

Who it is for: SDRs, account executives and sales managers who send high volumes of outbound email and want data-driven feedback in Chrome. Who it is not for: people who want a general writing assistant, multi-channel coaching beyond email, or anyone who needs published pricing up front.

Key features

01

Email Coach

A Chrome extension that analyses your historical emails to show what has worked for you and your team, then helps with new drafts.

02

Ora AI sales agent

A newer product that drafts outbound emails automatically; the vendor pitches it as less robotic than typical AI cold email.

03

CRM enrichment

Listed on the site as a way to add prospect data; details were not available on the pages we could read.

04

Team leaderboard

A gamified #EmailWizard leaderboard that gives managers a view of rep performance.

Learning resources

A Sales Email Certification course and a weekly newsletter, The Lavender Letter.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • You can install Email Coach for free, so you can judge it before any commitment.
  • It learns from your own sent emails, which is more relevant than generic writing advice.
  • Managers get a team view and a leaderboard, which turns scores into coaching.
  • The vendor claims SOC2 and GDPR compliance, which helps with security reviews (we have not audited this).
  • Having both a coach and a drafting agent covers review and generation in one vendor.

Cons

  • Prices were not visible on the main site and the pricing page failed to load, so cost planning is not possible from public information we could verify.
  • Older figures such as $29, $49 and $69 plans and a 5 emails per month free tier could not be confirmed and have been removed.
  • The focus is email; we found no evidence of LinkedIn or cold-call coaching.
  • The emphasis has moved to Ora, so the coaching product may get less attention than before.
  • Third-party review claims about bugs, support and repetitive suggestions are unverified and not repeated here.

Frequently asked questions

Is Lavender free?

The official site says you can install Email Coach for free. It does not show what the free version includes, so limits could not be verified.

How much does Lavender cost?

We could not verify current prices; the site lists none and the pricing page did not load. Confirm on lavender.ai or with sales.

What is Ora?

Ora is Lavender's AI sales agent that drafts outbound emails. It is separate from the Email Coach extension.

Does Lavender only work for email?

The pages we read describe email coaching only. We did not find LinkedIn or call-script coaching.

How does Lavender compare with MailMaestro or Grammarly?

Lavender is built for sales outreach and learns from your sent emails. MailMaestro is a general email assistant for Gmail and Outlook, and Grammarly is a broad grammar and tone checker.
