Qualifier.ai suits small and mid-sized B2B sales teams, especially in Europe and the Nordics, that want an AI assistant to run outbound prospecting for them and are happy to pay a monthly fee rather than do the setup themselves. It is a weaker fit for teams wanting a free trial, self-serve experimentation, high-volume cold email at rock-bottom cost, or a fully customizable workflow builder. Its assistant, Qai, finds decision-makers from your criteria or a plain-language description, verifies emails, and runs sequences across email, phone and LinkedIn. It sends in the recipient's time zone, categorizes replies, surfaces interested leads, and syncs with CRMs such as Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive and Dynamics 365. The vendor says it has reached over 5 million prospects, a figure we could not verify independently.

Best for

Good for B2B teams with a clear target profile who want outbound handled with minimal setup, and for companies that value EU data handling and local support. Not for solo operators who need a free tier, teams that want to test before talking to sales, agencies sending very large cold email volumes, or buyers needing fully public tiered pricing.

Key features

01

AI Prospect Discovery

Qai searches professional networks, web sources, and public business data to find decision-makers matching criteria like job title, industry, and geography, then verifies each email address through multiple providers before outreach begins.

02

AI Research & Personalization

Before composing a message, the platform researches each prospect's role, company background, and recent activity so outreach is individualized rather than templated.

03

Multi-Channel Sequencing

Outreach combines email, phone, and LinkedIn in a single automated workflow, with sends scheduled to respect each recipient's timezone and business hours.

04

Team Action Prompts

Phone calls and LinkedIn connection steps surface as prompts for human reps, complete with verified contact info and context, and the platform uses points, streaks, and leaderboards to encourage teams to complete them.

05

Automated Reply Triage

Incoming responses are automatically classified as interested, out-of-office, referral, rejection, or maybe-later, and sequences pause automatically once a prospect engages.

06

Deliverability Protection

Daily sending limits, bounce-rate monitoring, pre-send spam scoring, and timezone-based pacing are built in to protect sender reputation; the company reports a 98% delivery rate.

07

CRM & Inbox Integrations

Native connections to Gmail, Outlook, Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Upsales, and Lime Go keep prospect and activity data synced with existing sales systems.

08

Analytics Dashboard

A reporting dashboard tracks response rates, interested rates, meetings booked, and sequence-level performance, with team-wide visibility and data export.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Single product covers lead discovery, email verification, multi-channel sequences and reply triage
  • Starting price of about €339/month with no setup fee is modest for a managed outbound tool
  • Integrates with Gmail, Outlook, SMTP/IMAP and several CRMs including Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Dynamics 365, Upsales and Lime Go
  • Deliverability features (pacing, validation, bounce detection) and strategy sessions are part of the offering

Cons

  • No free trial is listed; evaluation goes through a demo and sales conversation
  • Final price depends on prospect volume and users, and exact tiers are not published
  • Cold outreach at scale carries compliance and reputation risk that the tool cannot remove
  • Vendor-reported results (prospects reached, deals closed, delivery rate) are not independently verified
  • Smaller ecosystem and fewer third-party reviews than the biggest outbound platforms

Frequently asked questions

How much does Qualifier.ai cost?

From €339/month billed annually, with monthly billing at a slightly higher rate and no setup fee. Price depends on your agreed monthly prospect volume and user count, so get a quote.

Is there a free trial?

None is mentioned on the pricing page; the calls to action are Book a demo and Talk to Qai. Confirm with sales whether a pilot is possible.

Which tools does it integrate with?

Gmail, Outlook, SMTP/IMAP, Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Dynamics 365, Upsales and Lime Go.

Can I change my volume later?

Per the pricing page, you can raise volume at any time and lower it at renewal.

Is cold outreach through it GDPR-compliant?

The tool is positioned for European use, but compliance depends on how you target and message people. Check lawful-basis rules in your target countries and ask the vendor for their data processing terms.
