Plus AI is a strong pick if your team lives in Google Slides or PowerPoint and wants AI drafting, rewriting and document-to-deck conversion there. Skip it if you want a free tool, a standalone web deck builder, or heavy usage on a small budget, since there is no free plan and credits are metered. It works as an add-on: describe a deck or upload a document, and it generates slides you then edit in the normal editor; you can also insert single slides or rewrite and remix existing ones.

Best for

Good for professionals and teams already working in Google Slides or PowerPoint, and developers wanting an API or MCP route to decks. Not for people who want a free plan, students on a tight budget, users who prefer a standalone presentation app, or heavy daily generators who would burn through Basic's 1,500 monthly credits.

Pro and above include the Plus AI MCP Server and Presentation API.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Editing happens in Google Slides or PowerPoint, so output stays fully editable and shareable with colleagues who have no Plus AI account.
  • Rewrite/remix and document uploads help with existing material, not only blank-page generation.
  • API and MCP server let developers or AI assistants generate decks programmatically.
  • Annual pricing is moderate at the entry tiers ($10-$20).

Cons

  • Plans have no free tier; the 7-day trial requires a credit card and is for new customers only.
  • Credits are consumed at about 40-170 per task, so Basic allows only roughly 9-37 tasks a month.
  • Branding and shared presets require Team at $30/user/month.
  • Quality of generated content was not independently tested here and no third-party reviews were verified.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

No. All plans begin with a 7-day trial including 1,000 credits; it needs a credit card and is for new customers only.

How many presentations do credits cover?

Tasks typically use 40-170 credits depending on complexity, so Basic (1,500/month) covers roughly 9-37 tasks.

Does it work with both Google Slides and PowerPoint?

Yes, both are listed, including on Basic.

Can I use it via API?

Yes, the Presentation API and MCP server are included from Pro upward.
