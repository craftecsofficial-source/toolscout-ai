Plus AI differentiates itself from many AI presentation tools by living directly inside Google Slides and PowerPoint as a native add-on, rather than requiring users to build a deck in a separate web app and then export or paste it in. This matters in practice because most teams already have established workflows, sharing permissions, and version history built around Google Slides or PowerPoint files — Plus AI works with that existing infrastructure instead of asking users to migrate to a new platform.

Beyond generating decks from a prompt or outline, Plus AI's redesign feature is a notable differentiator: users can drop in an already-written but visually rough deck and have the AI reformat it using cleaner, more professional layouts inspired by management-consulting style frameworks (SWOT analysis, BCG growth-share matrix, roadmaps, and similar). This appeals to founders and business teams who have the content right but want it to look more polished quickly.

Plus AI has built a broad user base spanning students, startup teams, and business professionals, and is one of the more established and widely reviewed AI presentation add-ons in its category, reflecting steady growth since its launch as generative AI tools for presentations became mainstream.

Best for

Plus AI is best suited for people who already live inside Google Slides or PowerPoint and want AI assistance without switching tools — this includes startup founders building pitch decks, consultants and analysts producing client-ready slides, students working on class presentations, and business teams standardizing on brand-consistent templates. Its redesign feature makes it especially valuable for anyone who has already-written content in a rough deck and just needs it to look more professional. Teams needing heavier enterprise governance (brand enforcement across large orgs, slide libraries) may want to compare it against more enterprise-focused tools like Prezent.ai.

Key features

01

Prompt-to-Deck Generation

Users provide a topic, outline, or rough notes, and Plus AI generates a structured multi-slide deck with titles, bullet content, and suggested visuals.

02

Slide Redesign

Existing slides or decks can be fed into Plus AI to be reformatted into cleaner, more visually consistent layouts without changing the underlying content.

03

Native Google Slides & PowerPoint Integration

Runs as an add-on inside both platforms, so generation and editing happen in the same file users already collaborate in.

04

Consultant-Style Frameworks

Includes templates modeled on frameworks commonly used in strategy and consulting work, such as SWOT, BCG matrix, and roadmap/timeline slides.

05

Brand Kits & Custom Templates

Teams can upload brand colors, fonts, and logos so generated and redesigned slides stay consistent with company style guides.

06

AI Image & Icon Generation

Generates supporting images and icons to match slide content, reducing the need to source stock visuals separately.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Because it's an add-on rather than a standalone app, teams keep their existing file structure, sharing permissions, and version history intact while gaining AI features.
  • The redesign capability is genuinely useful beyond first-draft generation — many users already have content and just need it to look more professional quickly.
  • Supporting both Google Slides and PowerPoint (rather than picking one ecosystem) makes it accessible to a wider range of organizations.
  • Consultant-style frameworks give less experienced presenters access to structures typically associated with professional strategy decks.
  • Being one of the more established tools in this space, it has a longer track record and more user feedback than many newer entrants.

Cons

  • AI-generated first drafts, as with most tools in this category, often still need manual polish to feel truly on-brand or precisely worded.
  • The most valuable team features — brand kits, shared templates, admin controls — sit behind paid tiers, limiting the free plan's usefulness for teams.
  • Output quality is sensitive to how much detail is given in the initial prompt or outline; vague prompts produce generic decks.
  • As an add-on, functionality is somewhat bounded by what Google Slides' and PowerPoint's extension APIs allow, compared to a fully custom-built web app.
  • Users deeply invested in one ecosystem (e.g., only Keynote) aren't directly supported.

Frequently asked questions

Does Plus AI work with both Google Slides and PowerPoint?

Yes, it's available as a native add-on for both platforms.

Can it redesign a deck I've already built?

Yes, the redesign feature takes existing slides and reformats them into cleaner, more professional layouts.

Do I need to leave Google Slides or PowerPoint to use it?

No, Plus AI operates as an add-on within those tools, so generation and editing happen in the same file.

Is there a free plan?

Yes, there is a free tier with a limited number of AI generations; paid plans expand usage and unlock brand kits and team features.

What kinds of templates does it offer?

In addition to standard slide layouts, it includes consultant-style frameworks like SWOT analysis, BCG matrix, and roadmap/timeline templates.

Can teams enforce brand consistency?

Yes, paid team plans support custom brand kits with company colors, fonts, and logos applied to generated and redesigned slides.
