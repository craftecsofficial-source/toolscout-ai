Tabnine is one of the earliest AI code completion tools on the market, having pivoted over the years from a pure autocomplete plugin into a full AI coding platform spanning code completions, in-IDE chat, a terminal-native CLI, and autonomous coding agents. Its central differentiator has always been control and privacy: Tabnine can be deployed as a SaaS product, inside a customer's VPC, fully on-premises, or completely air-gapped, and it commits to zero code retention with no training on customer code. This makes it a common choice for regulated industries, government contractors, and large enterprises that cannot send proprietary code to third-party cloud APIs. The platform supports multiple underlying LLMs (from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Meta, Mistral, and others), letting organizations choose or bring their own model rather than being locked into a single provider.

Tabnine's product is now split into two tiers: the Code Assistant Platform ($39/user/month annually), which covers completions, chat, and enterprise security/deployment options, and the Agentic Platform ($59/user/month annually), which adds autonomous coding agents, the Tabnine CLI, and the Tabnine Context Engine — a system that gives agents organization-level understanding of a company's architecture, standards, and connected tools (Git, Jira, Confluence) via the Model Context Protocol. In 2026, Tabnine was acquired by Tricentis, the agentic quality engineering company, positioning Tabnine's coding platform alongside Tricentis' testing tools. On G2, Tabnine holds a 4.1 out of 5 rating from 61 reviews as of August 2026, with reviewers praising IDE integration and coding-productivity gains but noting it lags rivals like GitHub Copilot on ease of setup and overall usability, and can be resource-intensive on larger codebases.

Best for

Tabnine is best suited to security- and compliance-driven engineering organizations — banks, government contractors, healthcare, and other regulated enterprises — that need an AI coding assistant they can deploy entirely within their own infrastructure with guaranteed zero code retention. It's a weaker fit for individual developers or small teams wanting a free or low-cost assistant, since Tabnine no longer offers a free tier and pricing starts at a premium.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Offers the broadest deployment flexibility of any major AI coding assistant — SaaS, VPC, on-premises, or fully air-gapped — which is a decisive factor for regulated and security-sensitive organizations.
  • Commits to zero code retention and no training on customer code, addressing IP concerns that block adoption of cloud-only assistants at many enterprises.
  • Model-agnostic: supports leading LLMs from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Meta, and Mistral, avoiding vendor lock-in to a single AI provider.
  • The newer Context Engine and Agentic Platform extend Tabnine beyond simple autocomplete into full agentic workflows grounded in organizational standards.
  • G2 reviewers consistently credit Tabnine with meaningfully speeding up coding tasks and integrating well across a wide range of IDEs.

Cons

  • Tabnine discontinued its free tier; pricing now starts at $39/user/month billed annually, positioning it as a premium rather than budget option.
  • Self-hosted or air-gapped deployments require the customer to provision and pay for their own GPU infrastructure, adding potentially $500-2,000+/month on top of per-seat fees.
  • On G2, Tabnine trails category leader GitHub Copilot on usability (8.1 vs 9.2) and ease of setup (6.9 vs 9.3), suggesting a steeper onboarding curve.
  • Some users report the assistant can be resource-intensive and affect performance on larger projects.
  • Full pricing details for enterprise deployments require contacting sales rather than being available self-serve.

Frequently asked questions

What does Tabnine cost?

Tabnine offers two tiers billed annually: Code Assistant Platform at $39/user/month and Agentic Platform at $59/user/month. Both require contacting sales for a formal quote.

Does Tabnine have a free plan?

No, Tabnine discontinued its free tier; a 90-day free trial is available for evaluation instead.

Can Tabnine run fully on-premises or air-gapped?

Yes, Tabnine supports SaaS, VPC, fully on-premises, and completely air-gapped deployment options, giving organizations full control over where code is processed.

Does Tabnine train on my code?

No, Tabnine states it has zero code retention and does not train its models on customer code.

Which LLMs does Tabnine support?

Tabnine supports multiple leading LLMs, including models from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Meta, and Mistral, rather than locking customers into a single model.

Who owns Tabnine now?

Tabnine was acquired by Tricentis, the agentic quality engineering company, in 2026.
