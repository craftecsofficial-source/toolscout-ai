Fontjoy is a great pick if you want a fast, no-friction way to find a heading and body font that look good together. Skip it if you need licensed or custom brand fonts, saved shortlists, or help with a whole type system.

The homepage explains the workflow plainly: click Generate to create a new pairing, lock the fonts you want to keep, and use Edit to choose one manually. You can replace the sample text with your own, such as a company name, to judge the pairing in context.

Our check was limited to the homepage text. It did not mention a contrast slider, the font library used, accounts, pricing or saving, so claims about those in older write-ups (Google Fonts source, a similarity slider, deep learning model) could not be confirmed today.

Best for

Fontjoy suits web designers, developers, and marketers who need a quick, decent pairing for a site, deck or simple brand. It is not for brands that need licensed or custom fonts, or type designers wanting detailed control. Use it early as a brainstorming aid rather than a final decision.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Removes much of the trial and error of choosing fonts for people without design training
  • Locking a font lets you build a pairing around one you already like
  • Editable preview text shows how the pairing looks with your real copy
  • The homepage shows no sign-up or payment barrier

Cons

  • Does only pairing; spacing, scale and layout are out of scope
  • The homepage does not mention saving or exporting pairings, so you may need to note them down yourself
  • We could not verify which font library it draws from, so confirm licensing before using a font commercially
  • Pairings are suggestions and can feel generic; treat them as a starting point

Frequently asked questions

Is Fontjoy free?

The site shows no pricing or account requirement, so it appears free, but we could not find an explicit pricing statement.

Can I keep one font and change the others?

Yes, the Lock feature keeps fonts you want while Generate changes the rest.

Can I choose a font myself?

Yes, the Edit option lets you select a font manually.

Can I use my own text?

Yes, the sample text is editable, for example to preview your company name.

Can I save pairings?

The homepage does not mention saving, so assume you need to record pairings yourself.
