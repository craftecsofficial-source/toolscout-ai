StudyMonkey is an AI-powered study assistant built by ITW Creative Works and aimed squarely at students, from homework help through essay writing and exam preparation. The product functions as a chat-based tutor: students ask questions in math, science, language, history, computer science, and dozens of other subjects, and StudyMonkey responds with step-by-step explanations rather than just a final answer, aiming to build understanding rather than simply supply completed homework. Beyond direct Q&A, the tool can break large topics into manageable study chunks and turn lengthy chapters, notes, or articles into concise summaries, positioning itself as a study companion for exam prep as much as a homework-answer engine.

StudyMonkey uses a freemium model that is unusually generous for the category: a Basic plan is free forever and includes 100 questions per month across 5 subjects with a standard AI model, letting students test the product with no credit card required before committing to a paid tier. Paid plans scale from a $7/month Plus tier (500 questions, unlimited subjects) through a $13/month Pro tier (3,000 questions, essay writer, advanced AI model) up to a $24/month Max tier with unlimited questions and priority support, plus a custom Enterprise tier for schools and larger organizations. The company states it serves 10,000+ students worldwide across 100+ supported subjects, and backs paid upgrades with a 14-day free trial and a 7-day money-back guarantee. As a smaller, founder-led education tool rather than a venture-scale platform, its trust signals lean on on-site testimonials and a self-reported 4.9 student rating rather than a large presence on third-party review sites like G2 or Capterra.

Best for

StudyMonkey fits middle school, high school, and college students who want a low-cost, always-available AI tutor for homework help, exam prep, and (on paid tiers) essay writing across a wide range of subjects. It's a weaker fit for students who need only occasional help and won't outgrow the free tier's 100 monthly questions, or for institutions wanting a heavily benchmarked, third-party-reviewed platform for large-scale deployment.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Genuinely free tier: unlike many AI study tools that gate almost everything behind a paywall, StudyMonkey's Basic plan offers 100 real questions per month across 5 subjects at no cost and with no credit card required.
  • Low price of entry: the $7/month Plus plan undercuts many competing homework-help apps and tutoring subscriptions while already removing the subject-count limit.
  • Risk-reduced upgrades: a 14-day free trial on premium plans combined with a 7-day money-back guarantee gives students and parents room to evaluate fit before fully committing.
  • Purpose-built pedagogy: the product explicitly emphasizes step-by-step explanations and topic breakdowns rather than just delivering final answers, aligning with how students say they want to actually learn.
  • Broad subject and format coverage: 100+ subjects and support for varied question formats mean one subscription can realistically replace several single-subject tutoring apps for most students.

Cons

  • The free plan's 100-question monthly cap is easy to exhaust for students juggling multiple classes, effectively nudging serious users toward a paid tier within the first few weeks of consistent use.
  • Essay writing support, arguably one of the most requested student use cases, is locked behind the $13/month Pro tier and above rather than included at the entry paid price point.
  • No listing was found on G2, Capterra, or Trustpilot with an independently verifiable rating, so the company's self-reported '4.9 rating from students' and '10K+ students' figures can't be cross-checked against a neutral third-party source.
  • The standard AI model on the Basic and Plus tiers is explicitly less capable than the 'advanced AI model' reserved for Pro and Max, meaning budget-tier answers may be noticeably weaker on harder material.
  • As a smaller, single-company product (ITW Creative Works) rather than a major education platform, long-term roadmap and support depth are harder to predict compared to larger, better-funded ed-tech competitors.

Frequently asked questions

Is StudyMonkey free?

Yes, the Basic plan is free forever and includes 100 questions per month across 5 subjects with no credit card required; paid plans unlock more questions, subjects, and features.

How much do StudyMonkey's paid plans cost?

Paid plans range from $7/month (Plus, 500 questions) to $13/month (Pro, 3,000 questions plus essay writer) to $24/month (Max, unlimited questions and priority support), with annual billing options that include a discount.

Does StudyMonkey help with essay writing?

Yes, but the essay writer feature is only included starting with the Pro plan ($13/month) and above.

What subjects does StudyMonkey cover?

StudyMonkey supports 100+ subjects, including math, science, language, history, and computer science, with the free plan limited to 5 subjects.

Is there a free trial for paid plans?

Yes, StudyMonkey offers a 14-day free trial on premium plans and a 7-day money-back guarantee, so users can cancel during the trial for a full refund.

How does StudyMonkey explain answers?

Rather than only giving final answers, StudyMonkey is designed to provide step-by-step explanations to help students understand the reasoning behind each solution.
