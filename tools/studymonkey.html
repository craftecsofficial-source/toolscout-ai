StudyMonkey is great for school and college students who want a cheap, step-by-step AI tutor, and a poor choice for anyone needing verified answers or work to submit unedited. It is a chat-based homework helper covering 100+ subjects from 1st grade through masters level, handling multiple choice, short answer and essay questions, and it keeps a history of past questions. Replies are meant to explain the reasoning rather than just give a result, and the site advertises roughly 10-second responses. Plans are metered by monthly question count: Basic is free with 100 questions and 5 subjects, Plus ($7/month) gives 500, Pro ($13/month) gives 3,000 plus an essay writer and an advanced model, and Max ($24/month) is unlimited. Annual billing figures on the pricing page are $90, $160 and $290, which are actually a little higher per month than the monthly prices, despite an advertised annual discount.

Best for

Fits students from school to college who want affordable, always-on homework explanations and, on Pro and above, essay drafting help. Not for people who need fact-checked or citation-backed research, anyone whose school bans AI-written work, or users wanting a general-purpose assistant; a general chatbot may serve those better.

Key features

01

Step-by-step explanations

Answers walk through the reasoning process rather than just giving a final result, aimed at helping students actually understand the material.

02

AI essay writer

Available from the Pro tier up, helps students draft, structure, and refine essays alongside general homework help.

03

Topic breakdown tool

Splits large or complex topics into smaller, manageable study chunks to make studying less overwhelming.

04

Chapter and note summarization

Condenses lengthy chapters, class notes, or articles into concise summaries for faster review.

05

Broad subject coverage

Supports 100+ subjects spanning math, science, language, history, and computer science, among others.

06

24/7 availability

Accessible at any time, letting students get help during late-night study sessions rather than waiting for tutoring hours.

07

Tiered AI models

Standard AI model on free and entry paid tiers, with an advanced AI model unlocked on Pro and Max plans for more complex questions.

08

Account dashboard and chat history

A persistent dashboard and chat interface let students revisit prior questions and continue ongoing study threads.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • A real free tier with 100 questions per month and no card needed.
  • Entry paid plan is $7/month, cheap next to human tutoring.
  • Wide subject and grade coverage, from 1st grade to masters.
  • Past questions and answers can be reviewed later.

Cons

  • 100 free questions across only 5 subjects run out fast for multi-class students.
  • Essay writer and the advanced model require Pro ($13/month) or above.
  • Annual billing ($90, $160, $290) works out slightly more per month than monthly prices, so there is no real annual saving despite 'up to 20% off' wording.
  • Trial terms are unclear: the FAQ mentions a 14-day trial but the plan table only states a 7-day money-back guarantee.
  • Like any AI tutor, answers can be wrong; no accuracy guarantee is published.

Frequently asked questions

Is StudyMonkey free?

Yes. Basic is free with 100 questions per month and 5 subjects, no credit card required.

How much are the paid plans?

Plus $7/month, Pro $13/month, Max $24/month. Annual billing is quoted at $90, $160 and $290 per year.

Is there a refund or trial?

Paid plans list a 7-day money-back guarantee. The FAQ also mentions a 14-day trial, but the terms were inconsistent, so confirm at checkout.

Do I need Pro for essay writing?

Yes, the essay writer appears on Pro and Max, not Basic or Plus.
