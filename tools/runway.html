Runway suits filmmakers, ad agencies and content teams who want to edit and direct AI video rather than just roll the dice on prompts. Skip it if you need long videos on a tight budget or want predictable, flat-rate costs, because everything runs on credits that vanish faster on premium models.

Its Creative suite covers video, image and audio generation, built around the Gen-4.5 model, with tools like Aleph and Act-Two for transforming footage and transferring performances. Workflows, Agent, Characters and a Model Router help chain steps together. Separate products cover a developer API (Runway Dev), advertising (Runway Ads) and robotics world models.

Best for

Great for: video creators, agencies, filmmakers and product teams who want editing control, plus developers using the API. Not for: people wanting unlimited generation at a fixed price, hobbyists who generate a lot of long clips, or anyone needing a talking-avatar presenter tool, where specialised products fit better.

Key features

01

Gen-4.5 video generation

Runway's own video model, available with text and image inputs inside the Creative suite.

02

Aleph and Act-Two

Aleph 2.0 and Act-Two are listed as research models for transforming existing footage and transferring performance.

03

Workflows, Agent and Model Router

Chain steps into repeatable pipelines and let an agent or router choose models.

04

Audio and voice

Music, voice and sound-effect generation on paid plans; Pro adds one custom voice clone, Max up to three.

05

4K upscaling and studio outputs

4K upscaling on Standard, unlimited on Pro and Max; Max adds HDR, ProRes and image-sequence exports.

06

Runway Dev and MCP

API access to Runway and other labs' models; MCP on Pro and Max.

07

Brand Kits

One on Pro, up to three on Max.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Editing and transformation tools extend past simple prompt-to-clip generation
  • Multiple models draw on one credit pool
  • Free credits never expire, so testing is low pressure
  • Extra purchased credits never expire (1,000 minimum purchase)
  • API, MCP and Workflows make it automatable

Cons

  • Credit cost per clip varies by model and length, making monthly budgets hard to forecast
  • Monthly credits on Standard and Pro reset without rollover; Max rolls over just one month
  • Free plan is a one-time grant, not a recurring allowance
  • Annual billing is needed for the lowest listed price; monthly costs 25% more
  • Standard caps you at 3 projects and 20GB

Frequently asked questions

Is Runway free?

Yes, with 125 one-time credits that do not expire, but nothing refreshes monthly.

How much is it per month?

Standard $15, Pro $35, Max $95 billed monthly; $12, $28 and $76 per month when billed annually.

Do credits roll over?

Not on Standard or Pro. Max rolls unused credits over for one month. Separately purchased credits never expire.

How does it compare with Kling or Luma?

Runway leans on editing tools and workflow depth; Kling and Luma are more focused generators, so compare per-clip credit cost for your use.
