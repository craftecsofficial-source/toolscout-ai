Generative video and editing suite for creators

Free tier available; paid plans from ~$12/mo

Runway's Gen model line produces cinematic video clips from text or images, alongside a full suite of AI-assisted editing tools for creative teams.

Best for

Filmmakers and creative teams who want cinematic control over AI-generated video.

Key features

  • ✓Gen-model text-to-video and image-to-video generation
  • ✓Motion Brush and Director Mode for precise camera control
  • ✓Act-Two performance capture for character animation
  • ✓Full editing suite plus API access

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Most creative control of any generative video platform
  • Preferred for indie film, advertising and enterprise production
  • Professional export formats and color grading tools

Cons

  • Steeper learning curve than avatar-based tools
  • Higher-end features require paid plans
