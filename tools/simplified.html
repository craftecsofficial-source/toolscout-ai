Simplified began as a Canva-style graphic design and video tool aimed at small businesses and creators, then progressively layered in AI writing, video generation, and social media scheduling to become a broader marketing platform. Made by TLDR Technologies, it now positions itself as an "agentic marketing OS": users work inside a shared workspace with a brand kit, and can either use its individual AI tools directly (image generator, video editor, AI writer, post scheduler) or brief its AI agent, Riley, in plain language — something like "launch our spring promo on Instagram and LinkedIn" — and receive a finished, on-brand batch of assets to review and approve before anything publishes. Simplified reports over 15 million users worldwide and native publishing integrations across 12+ social platforms.

By 2026 the platform expanded further into an execution layer for external AI agents, adding an MCP server, CLI, and API so tools like Claude or Codex can trigger Simplified workflows programmatically rather than only working through the web app. Simplified explicitly contrasts itself with copilot tools like Jasper and ChatGPT, which it says draft text and stop there, versus its own agents which produce finished designs, copy, and assembled campaign assets ready for approval. It also positions its pricing well below full marketing suites — its Starter plan starts at $79/month compared to roughly $800/month for a comparable HubSpot Marketing Hub tier, per its own published comparison. On G2, Simplified holds a 4.6/5 rating from close to 5,000 reviews, with ease of use and time savings the most frequently cited strengths.

Best for

Simplified suits small businesses, solo marketers, freelancers, and lean in-house teams who want a single AI-assisted platform to draft copy, generate creative, and schedule social posts without paying for five separate tools. It's a weaker fit for large enterprises needing deep, specialized functionality in any one area (like enterprise-grade marketing automation or a professional video editing suite), or for teams running very high campaign volume, since the per-campaign credit pricing can get expensive at scale.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Genuinely bundles AI writing, graphic design, video editing/generation, and social scheduling in one workspace — G2 reviewers specifically cite "less tool switching" as a top benefit versus juggling separate design, copy, and scheduling apps.
  • The Riley agent and campaign-credit model produce a full set of finished assets (copy + creative + schedule) from a single brief, a step beyond copilot tools like Jasper or ChatGPT that only draft text.
  • Broad publishing reach across 12+ social platforms plus native apps (iOS, Android, Chrome extension, WordPress plugin, Shopify app) keeps most day-to-day marketing work inside one ecosystem.
  • G2 rates it 4.6/5 across roughly 5,000 reviews, with high mention counts for ease of use (1,456 mentions) and time-saving capabilities (330 mentions), a large and largely positive review base for the category.
  • Pricing undercuts full marketing suites significantly — Starter begins at $79/month versus roughly $800/month for a comparable HubSpot Marketing Hub tier, per Simplified's own published comparison.
  • MCP server, CLI, and API access let technical teams trigger Simplified workflows from Claude, Codex, or custom agents instead of only working through the web app.

Cons

  • G2 reviewers frequently cite limited free credits (437 mentions) and pricing concerns (301 mentions) — the shared AI-credit system spent across every tool can run out faster than expected for active users.
  • AI-generated copy is described as occasionally generic (346 mentions on G2) and typically needs editing before it's publish-ready.
  • The campaign-credit pricing model ($79/month for 5 campaigns, $10 per additional campaign) is less predictable than flat seat-based pricing, which can complicate budgeting for agencies running many concurrent client campaigns.
  • Plan downgrades only take effect at the next billing period, and unused campaign credits don't roll over month to month.
  • With such a broad feature set spanning design, video, writing, scheduling, and agents, the platform can feel like it's doing a lot of things reasonably well rather than any single job exceptionally, compared to specialized tools that go deeper on one function.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as one "campaign" in Simplified's pricing?

One campaign is one brief-to-approval cycle producing a single output type: a social batch of 6 posts, an email sequence of 3-5 emails, or a paid ad creative set of 3 A/B variants for Meta or Google.

Do unused campaigns roll over to the next month?

No, campaign credits refresh each billing period and unused ones expire; additional campaigns can be purchased for $10 each on any plan.

Is there a free plan?

Yes, the free plan includes 1 user seat, up to 3 connected social accounts, 500 MB storage, and a one-time AI word-credit allowance (about 5,000 words) to try the platform.

How is Simplified different from Jasper or ChatGPT?

Jasper and ChatGPT are copilots that draft text and stop there; Simplified's agents take a brief and produce the finished design, copy, and assembled assets for review before anything ships.

Can I use Simplified without briefing the AI agent (Riley)?

Yes, every plan includes the underlying design, video, writing, and scheduling tools on their own; the agent layer is additive, not a replacement.

Does Simplified integrate with external AI tools like Claude?

Yes, via an MCP server, CLI, and API that let Claude, Codex, or custom agent clients trigger Simplified workflows programmatically.
