Simplified is a sensible pick for small businesses, solo marketers and small agencies that want routine social, email and ad content drafted, designed and scheduled in one place. Skip it if you need deep specialist tools (a professional video editor, enterprise marketing automation) or very high campaign volume, where per-campaign pricing adds up. It grew out of a design and video tool and now adds AI writing, social scheduling and an agent called Riley that turns a plain-language brief into finished campaign assets for you to approve. The company is TLDR Technologies, Inc.

Best for

Who it is for: lean marketing teams, freelancers and agencies running regular social, email and ad creative. Who it is not for: enterprises needing depth in one area, teams running many concurrent client campaigns on a tight budget, and buyers who need fully public pricing for every tier (Pro and Business prices were not shown).

Key features

01

Riley agent

Plans, creates and queues campaign content from a brief; drafts wait for approval by default and auto-publish is opt-in.

02

Writing, image and video tools

AI writing, image generation, photo editing, video generation and editing, subtitles and short clips.

03

Social suite

Scheduling, analytics, unified inbox, link-in-bio and client approvals across 10+ channels.

04

Brand memory

Stores brand, product and knowledge context that the agent and tools reuse.

05

Workflows

Repeated jobs can run as reusable, scheduled workflows.

06

Developer access

MCP server, CLI and API let external agents such as Claude or Codex run Simplified tasks.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • One workspace for copy, design, video and scheduling instead of several subscriptions
  • Human approval is the default, so the agent does not publish unreviewed work
  • Tools work standalone, so you can skip the agent
  • Cancel anytime with no fees; upgrades apply immediately
  • MCP, CLI and API access suit teams wiring it into other agents

Cons

  • Campaign-based pricing: each campaign yields one output type (6 posts, a 3-5 email sequence, or 3 ad variants), so volume gets costly at $10 per extra campaign
  • Campaigns reset each billing period and do not roll over
  • Downgrades take effect only at the next billing period
  • Pro and Business prices are not published, and annual pricing was not listed
  • Our fetch of the pricing page found no free plan while the homepage says free to start; confirm before signing up
  • We did not independently test output quality, so judge generic-copy risk with the trial

Frequently asked questions

How much does Simplified cost?

Starter is $79 per month for 5 campaigns. Pro and Business are named but their prices were not shown. Extra campaigns are $10 each.

What counts as a campaign?

One brief-to-approval cycle producing one output type: a 6-post social batch, a 3-5 email sequence, or 3 ad variants.

Is there a free plan?

The homepage says free to start with no card, but the pricing page lists a trial and no free plan. Could not verify current limits.

Do unused campaigns roll over?

No. They reset each billing period.

Can I use it without the Riley agent?

Yes. The design, video, writing and scheduling tools work without the agent.
