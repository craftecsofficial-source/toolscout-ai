Plotagon is a fun, low-barrier choice for students, teachers and hobby storytellers who want a typed script turned into a 3D animated scene; skip it if you need realistic AI-generated footage or polished marketing video. You pick a location, add stylized actors, write dialogue and stage directions, and the app handles movement, lip-sync, camera and voices. The site currently offers three apps: Studio Mobile (newest, more than two actors per scene, no ads), Story Mobile (older, with ads) and Studio Desktop for Windows. The website lists no prices, and commercial use is covered by a separate per-user license.

Best for

Good for classroom projects, fan fiction, simple narrative shorts and anyone who can write but cannot animate. Not for marketers or professionals wanting modern photorealistic output, anyone wanting to stream or broadcast results commercially without checking the license terms, or people who need transparent upfront pricing.

Key features

01

Script-to-animation

You type dialogue and directions and the app animates the actors, lip-sync and camera for you.

02

Actors

Customizable stylized actors; Studio Mobile adds independent emotes and more than two actors in a scene.

03

Locations

The site cites over 200 virtual locations to set scenes in.

04

Voices

Multilingual voices are built in, and the earlier app versions also let you record your own voice.

05

Camera, audio and transitions

Tools to frame shots, add sound and move between scenes, plus storyboarding.

06

Three apps

Studio Mobile (newest), Story Mobile (older, with ads) and Studio Desktop (older Windows app).

Pros and cons

Pros

  • No drawing, rigging or keyframing is needed; the whole workflow is script plus pickers.
  • Studio Mobile adds more than two actors per scene and independent emotes over the older app.
  • No ads in Studio Mobile, while the older Story app carries ads, per the site.
  • Over 200 locations and multilingual voices give plenty of setting variety for a script-based tool.
  • The product is still actively promoted and updated (a desktop build 1.11.0 and 2026 copyright appear on the site).

Cons

  • No prices appear on the official site, so total cost can only be learned inside the app stores.
  • The commercial license is separate, limited to one named user for 12 months, and restricts broadcast and streaming without agreement.
  • Plotagon is listed as co-author of every movie under the license terms, which some creators will dislike.
  • Three overlapping apps (Studio Mobile, Story Mobile, Studio Desktop) with different features can confuse new users.
  • We did not verify current store ratings or export reliability today, so older reports of crashes on long videos are unconfirmed.

Frequently asked questions

Is Plotagon free?

The website does not list prices. Earlier listings showed a free download with in-app purchases, but we could not verify current amounts, so check the App Store or Google Play.

Which app should I use?

The site presents Studio Mobile as the newest, with more than two actors per scene and no ads. Story Mobile and Studio Desktop (Windows) are the older apps.

Can I use my videos commercially?

Only with the separate Commercial License: one named user, 12 months, renewable. It bars broadcasting or streaming via cable, satellite, subscription, pay-per-download or OTT unless agreed.

Who owns the movies I make?

Under the license terms Plotagon is listed as co-author of all movies, while your own scripts, recorded voices and materials remain yours.

How is it different from AI video generators?

Plotagon animates pre-built 3D actors from your script rather than generating new footage, which gives predictable results but a simpler, stylized look.
