Topic (usetopic.com, built by Chia Labs Inc., based in San Francisco) is an AI-powered content optimization platform aimed squarely at content marketers, SEO specialists, and agencies who need to produce SEO-ready content briefs at scale. The core workflow is simple: a user enters a target keyword, and Topic's Content Briefs tool analyzes real-time search data from top-ranking pages to generate an outline of subtopics, questions, and semantically related terms the content should cover. Writers then draft against that brief, and Topic's Content Grader scores the draft in real time, flagging missing topics so editors can push content past a 'good enough' bar before publishing.

Topic differentiates itself from heavier competitors like Clearscope, MarketMuse, and Surfer SEO by emphasizing simplicity: reviewers repeatedly note that its interface surfaces 'just the right amount of information' rather than overwhelming users with data. It also offers 'Handoff Mode,' letting agencies share graded briefs directly with freelance writers or clients via a link, and its case studies claim results like clicks from organic search increasing by 540/month after content optimization — equivalent to roughly $15,000/year in AdWords-equivalent value. Customers cited on its site include Quizlet, Plaid, Magoosh, and Qualaroo. Pricing runs from a $99/month Starter plan up through custom Enterprise plans with unlimited reports and white-label/API options, and Topic markets a low-cost entry offer ($7 for the first 3 content briefs) to let prospects trial the product before committing to a subscription.

Best for

Topic is a strong fit for content marketing teams, SEO specialists, and agencies producing SEO content at volume who want a fast, simple way to build briefs and grade drafts without a steep learning curve. It's a weaker fit for teams needing deep enterprise workflow features, extensive third-party integrations beyond Google Docs, or highly technical SEO auditing beyond content optimization.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Both G2 (5.0/5 across 37 reviews) and Capterra (5.0/5 across 20 reviews) show unusually consistent, near-perfect ratings with 100% positive sentiment on Capterra.
  • Reviewers on Capterra report cutting content brief creation time from 'a half hour to an hour' down to under 15 minutes, with one agency scaling from 5-6 briefs/week to 50-60.
  • Multiple reviewers who switched from MarketMuse or Clearscope cite better pricing and an easier learning curve as reasons for switching to Topic.
  • The Content Grader is repeatedly called out by name as the single most-loved feature, helping enforce a consistent quality bar across freelance writers.
  • Case-study evidence (540 additional organic clicks/month on one optimized piece) gives a concrete, quantified ROI story that's rarer among competitors.
  • Simple, low-friction UI is highlighted again and again as a reason teams chose Topic over more feature-dense competitors.

Cons

  • There is a real learning curve for new users; one reviewer specifically warned not to skip Topic's onboarding videos.
  • The UI has been described by at least one long-time user as 'occasionally buggy,' though usually fixed by refreshing.
  • GPT-based suggestions can go 'off the rails' for niche, low-volume keywords per one reviewer's account of the GPT-3 integration's early limitations.
  • No free trial is listed on Capterra's pricing page — plans start at $99/month directly, though the marketing site offers a low-cost 3-brief trial.
