Topic is a decent pick for small content teams and agencies that want fast, low-friction SEO briefs and a draft checker; skip it if you need a modern, high-volume content platform or reassurance about an actively developed roadmap. You enter a focus keyword and it studies the top 30 Google results to propose headings, questions and related terms. Writers then draft against an outline, and the Content Grader scores the text and points out topics still missing. Add-ons include Google Docs and WordPress plugins and a keyword research tool on every plan. Plans are capped by brief count, with unused briefs rolling over for one month. The company's site announces it has joined the CafeMedia family, and the pages I could read look partly outdated, so check what is current before committing.

Best for

Good for freelancers, agencies and in-house marketers producing a modest number of SEO articles a month. Not for large publishers needing unlimited reports at low cost, teams wanting a deep analytics or full content-planning suite, or buyers who want a free trial.

Key features

01

Content Briefs

Generates a structured outline of subtopics, headers, and key terms to cover based on real-time analysis of top-ranking search results for a keyword.

02

Content Grader

Scores drafts against the brief in real time so writers and editors can see exactly what's missing before publishing.

03

NLP Topic Analysis

Surfaces semantically related topics from top-performing organic content to help build topical authority rather than just keyword-stuff.

04

Interactive Outline Builder

Lets editors build and refine outlines that can be handed directly to writers.

05

Handoff Mode

Share graded briefs via a link with freelance writers or clients without giving full platform access.

06

Keyword Research Tool

Included on all paid plans to support keyword discovery alongside brief creation.

07

Google Docs Integration

Connects briefs and grading workflow to Google Docs for easier writer collaboration.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Straightforward workflow: keyword, brief, outline, graded draft, with little setup.
  • Cost per brief drops from $10 on monthly Starter to $4.80 on annual Premium.
  • Unlimited sharing: writers and collaborators do not count against user seats.
  • Google Docs and WordPress plugins keep grading inside the writing tools people already use.
  • Geo-targeting covers English, French, German, Portuguese and Italian.

Cons

  • Brief caps are strict; 50 briefs a month tops out at the $299 tier before you need an Enterprise quote.
  • No free trial; the $7 trial covers only 3 briefs.
  • The pricing page still mentions a 2021 sale and GPT-3, suggesting it is not frequently updated.
  • The site says it joined CafeMedia, which may change pricing, focus or support; I could not find details.
  • Priority chat support is limited to weekday business hours Pacific time.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Topic cost?

Starter is $99/mo for 10 briefs, Plus $199/mo for 25, Premium $299/mo for 50; annual billing is about 20% cheaper. Enterprise is by quote. The pricing page looked dated, so confirm at checkout.

Is there a free trial?

No free plan. The entry offer is 3 briefs for $7 over 7 days.

What happens to unused briefs?

They roll over for one month on all listed plans.

Who owns Topic now?

The site announces it has joined the CafeMedia family; I did not find further details.

How does it compare to Clearscope or MarketMuse?

It is simpler and cheaper to start, but narrower in scope and capped by brief count.
