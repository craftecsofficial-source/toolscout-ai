Vocalo is a good fit for adult non-native English speakers who want cheap, on-demand speaking practice and feedback, and a poor fit if you need other languages today, unlimited practice, or the accountability of a human tutor. It is an AI speaking buddy: you talk with it by voice, then get feedback on grammar, vocabulary, filler words and fluency. A curriculum adapts to your level, exercises come from your own conversation history, and live translation, streaks and badges are included. The site claims 23,600 users and 255,600 minutes spoken, and advertises 35+ languages, but multi-language learning is still marked coming soon on Premium.

Best for

Adult non-native English speakers who want frequent low-stakes speaking practice with instant feedback and cannot easily afford a human tutor. Not for learners of other languages right now, people needing unlimited daily practice, or those who need a human teacher for exams or accountability.

Key features

01

Live AI Conversations

Users speak naturally with the AI, which mimics real continuous conversation to build speaking confidence and reduce fear of speaking.

02

Actionable Real-Time Feedback

The AI listens and responds live, then provides granular feedback on grammar, vocabulary, filler words, and fluency after each session.

03

Personalized Curriculum

A curriculum is generated from the user's current level and goals and automatically updates as their skill improves.

04

Live Translation

Conversations are translated in real time (and available after the session ends) in over 100 languages to aid comprehension.

05

Dynamic Exercises

Exercises are generated based on a user's conversation history, current level, and stated learning goals.

06

Gamified Progress Tracking

Points, badges, streaks, and detailed progress insights keep learners motivated and show improvement over time.

07

Conversation History

Full history of past spoken conversations and feedback is stored and accessible for review.

08

Language coverage

The site lists about 33-35 languages, but multi-language learning is marked coming soon on Premium, so treat English as the supported focus.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Voice-based practice targets the speaking-confidence gap that text and flashcard apps skip.
  • Feedback covers grammar, vocabulary, filler words and fluency after each session.
  • Curriculum and exercises adapt to your level and conversation history.
  • Far cheaper than regular human tutoring and available any time.
  • Live translation helps beginners follow along.

Cons

  • Free plan is one 15-minute session, a thin trial.
  • Sessions are capped at 30 or 60 of 15 minutes per month, so practice is not unlimited.
  • Non-English learning is not yet fully available despite the 35+ language marketing.
  • Only self-selected testimonials on its own site; no independent rating verified.
  • Yearly and lifetime prices are not stated, so check at checkout.

Frequently asked questions

Is Vocalo free?

There is a free tier with one 15-minute session, basic feedback and live translation, no card required.

How much does it cost?

Standard is $15/month (30 sessions) and Premium $25/month (60 sessions), both 15 minutes each. Yearly saves 20% and a lifetime option is mentioned, but those prices are not published. Checked 2026-10-31.

Can I learn languages other than English?

The site lists 35+ languages, but multi-language learning is marked coming soon on Premium, so English is the safe assumption.

What feedback do I get?

After each session you get feedback on grammar, vocabulary, filler words and fluency.

Does it replace a human tutor?

It suits daily conversation practice; it does not offer a human's accountability or exam-specific coaching.
