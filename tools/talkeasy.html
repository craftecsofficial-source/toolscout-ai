TalkEasy is a good pick for small sales teams who want a separate business number with AI call summaries at a low flat price, and a weak pick for high-volume calling teams or anyone needing CRM integration on the cheap plan. It is a virtual-number and dialer app from the GrowEasy team that records calls, summarises them with AI, offers an AI receptionist and voice agents, automates missed-call follow-up and shows team call analytics. The Pro plan is ₹999 + GST per month with 3 hours of talktime a day; CRM customization and integration is listed as Enterprise only. The site does not publish independent reviews.

Best for

Small businesses and sales teams, such as real estate, clinics and dealerships, that want a dedicated business number separate from personal phones, with AI summaries and team call tracking. NOT for: high-volume call centres that would exceed 3 hours/day of talktime, teams that need custom CRM integration without negotiating an Enterprise quote, or businesses wanting a published, independent track record before buying. Note it is made by the GrowEasy team.

Key features

01

Virtual Business Number

Provisions a professional business phone number without any hardware, claimed to be ready to use in under 2 minutes.

02

AI Call Handling

Automatically answers and routes calls around the clock, so incoming calls aren't missed when the team is unavailable.

03

AI Call Summaries

Generates a concise summary automatically after every conversation, saving reps from manual post-call note-taking.

04

Call Recording & Analytics Dashboard

Records calls automatically and surfaces team performance metrics - call volume, duration and outcomes - in a dashboard.

05

Missed-Call Automation

Triggers automatic callbacks and follow-up actions when a call is missed, so leads aren't lost to an unanswered phone.

06

Team Management & CRM Sync

Supports unlimited team members with centralized call visibility, and auto-updates CRM records from call activity.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • No SIM cards or hardware: a virtual number plus app on Android and iOS keeps work calls apart from personal ones.
  • AI call summaries and recording reduce time reps spend on manual notes.
  • Missed-call automation targets a common leak for small sales teams, unanswered inbound calls.
  • Unlimited users on the Pro plan keeps cost flat as the team grows.

Cons

  • Talktime is capped at 3 hours a day on Pro; heavier users pay ₹49 + GST per hour or must move to an unpriced Enterprise plan.
  • CRM customization and integration is listed as Enterprise only, so the CRM auto-update advantage depends on a quote.
  • Enterprise pricing is not published.
  • The pricing page's comparison tables mention a 'free plan' that is never described; do not assume one exists.
  • No independent review rating was found. Figures like 'trusted by 2500+ businesses' are the company's own claims.

Frequently asked questions

How much does TalkEasy cost?

Pro is ₹999 + GST per month with 3 hours of daily talktime, extra at ₹49 + GST per hour. Enterprise is custom-priced.

Is there a free plan?

None is described on the pricing page, although its comparison tables mention a 'free plan'. Ask before assuming one exists.

What happens if my team talks more than 3 hours a day?

On Pro, extra talktime is billed at ₹49 + GST per hour; Enterprise lists unlimited talktime at a custom price.

Does it integrate with my CRM?

The site lists Zoho, HubSpot, Salesforce and Freshsales, but CRM customization and integration is shown as an Enterprise feature, not Pro.

Can I use it without GrowEasy?

It is sold as its own app; the GrowEasy relationship is a commercial one, so check bundle terms with the vendor.
