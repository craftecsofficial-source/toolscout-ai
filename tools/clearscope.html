Clearscope is a good fit for content teams and agencies that publish SEO content regularly and want a clear, writer-friendly way to check each piece against what already ranks. Skip it if you are a solo blogger or on a tight budget, since the cheapest plan is $129 a month and there is no light tier.

The product is split into modules: Discover finds topics worth writing, Write and Optimize help draft and grade content against top-ranking pages, Protect monitors published pages so traffic loss gets caught, Localize supports location-focused content, Linking suggests internal links, and Expand tracks how your brand appears in AI chatbot answers. The homepage cites visibility tracking in Google and ChatGPT. Pricing is based on tracked queries, pages, topic explorations and drafts, with unlimited users and projects. I could not verify today which integrations (such as Google Docs or WordPress) are currently offered, because the pages I checked do not list any.

Best for

Clearscope suits in-house content teams, SEO agencies and publishers who produce SEO content weekly and want consistent optimization across many writers. It is not for solo bloggers, hobby sites or anyone who needs a cheap entry plan, nor for teams looking for an all-in-one SEO suite with rank tracking and backlink analysis.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Unlimited users and projects mean a growing writer or freelancer pool does not change the bill; only usage limits do.
  • The three-tier structure is simple to read and prices are public, with a 14-day trial and no contracts per the pricing page.
  • One product covers topic research, writing, ongoing monitoring (Protect) and AI-search tracking, which reduces tool sprawl for content teams.
  • Add-ons for pages and drafts let you scale a single limit without jumping to the next tier.
  • Business includes a dedicated account manager, useful for agencies and larger teams.

Cons

  • The entry price of $129/month has no lower tier, so freelancers and very small sites pay for capacity they may not use.
  • Both paid plans cap drafts at 20 per month; extra drafts cost $50 per block on Essentials, which adds up for high-volume teams.
  • Essentials covers only 50 pages and 50 tracked queries, which a mid-sized site can outgrow quickly, pushing extra-page add-ons or the $399 tier.
  • Business is a $270 jump but adds only 30 more topic explorations and no extra drafts; the value is in pages, queries and the account manager.
  • The public pages do not list integrations, rank tracking, backlink or keyword-database features, so you should verify those during the trial and expect to pair it with a broader SEO suite.
  • Enterprise limits and prices are not public.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Clearscope cost?

Essentials is $129/month and Business is $399/month, with Enterprise on custom pricing. Both public plans include unlimited users and projects.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, a 14-day free trial is offered. The pricing page says there are no contracts; it does not describe what happens when the trial ends.

What are the main limits?

Essentials: 50 tracked queries, 50 pages, 20 topic explorations and 20 drafts a month. Business: 300 queries, 300 pages, 50 topic explorations and 20 drafts a month.

Can I buy more pages or drafts?

Yes. Extra pages start at $25/month on Essentials and $15/month on Business; extra drafts start at $50 on Essentials and $20 on Business. The page does not state a billing period for the draft add-on.

How does it compare with Surfer or MarketMuse?

All three help optimize content against what ranks. Clearscope's pricing is tiered by queries, pages and drafts with unlimited users, so compare limits against your own volume before choosing.
