Clearscope is a content-first discoverability platform for SEO and AI search, built around pairing keyword and topic research with real-time content grading. It scans the top-ranking pages for a target keyword, extracts the most relevant related terms and questions, and gives a draft a letter grade (F to A+) based on how comprehensively it covers those terms, alongside readability and length guidance. Writers work either directly in Clearscope's own editor or through its Google Docs and WordPress integrations, so grading happens inside their existing writing tools rather than requiring a separate app.

By 2026 the product expanded beyond classic SEO into AI-search visibility: its Expand and Discover modules track specific prompts across ChatGPT and Gemini to show when and how a brand gets mentioned, and surface the background web searches ("query fan-out") that AI platforms run to construct their answers, so content teams can target the sources AI systems actually cite. Clearscope counts Adobe, Shopify, IBM, Intuit, Condé Nast, Webflow, and HubSpot among its customer logos, and holds among the strongest review scores of any tool in the SEO category, with a 4.9/5 rating on both G2 (92 reviews) and Capterra (60 reviews).

Best for

Clearscope is a strong fit for in-house content marketing teams, SEO agencies, and content-heavy publishers producing enough SEO content monthly to justify $129-$399/month, especially those already writing in Google Docs or WordPress. It's a weaker fit for solo bloggers or very occasional writers on a tight budget, since there's no low-cost entry tier and the per-plan draft and prompt limits are built around teams producing content regularly rather than one-off use.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Clearscope's 4.9/5 rating on both G2 (92 reviews) and Capterra is among the highest of any tool in the SEO and content category, with an unusually large share of 5-star reviews going back to 2019.
  • The Google Docs integration is repeatedly singled out by reviewers as the standout feature, letting writers see live grading and keyword coverage without switching tools or breaking their normal drafting workflow.
  • The A-F letter grade system is simple enough that reviewers report onboarding new or freelance writers with a five-minute walkthrough, no deep SEO training required.
  • Multiple reviewers report concrete traffic outcomes, including one citing a 187% increase in blog pageviews and another a 50% increase in organic traffic within about two months of adopting it.
  • Every plan includes unlimited users and projects, prompt and brand-visibility tracking across ChatGPT and Gemini, and priority support with no upsell, which is unusual generosity for a SaaS tool at this price point.
  • Customers named on its site (Adobe, Shopify, IBM, Intuit, Condé Nast, Webflow, HubSpot) indicate it holds up at enterprise content scale, not just for small blogs.

Cons

  • Cost is the most consistent complaint across years of reviews — multiple reviewers explicitly call it expensive for freelancers or small businesses, even though most still find it worth the price.
  • Draft and report credits are capped per plan (20 monthly drafts on both Essentials and Business), and additional drafts cost extra ($50 for 10 more on Essentials, $20 on Business), which can constrain high-volume content operations.
  • A minority of reviewers say Clearscope's keyword and term suggestions are less exhaustive or precise for ranking than specialized alternatives like MarketMuse for certain competitive terms.
  • The platform's core strength is content grading and optimization rather than full keyword research (rank tracking, backlink analysis, etc.), so many teams still pair it with a broader SEO suite like Semrush or Ahrefs.
  • Some reviewers note the UI's terminology (queries, terms, themes) takes a little time to fully understand, even though the interface itself is generally described as intuitive.

Frequently asked questions

How does Clearscope's content grading work?

It analyzes the top-ranking pages for a target keyword, extracts the most relevant related terms and questions, and assigns a draft a letter grade (F to A+) based on how comprehensively it covers those terms alongside readability and length.

Does Clearscope work inside Google Docs?

Yes, its Google Docs integration shows live content grading and keyword suggestions as you write, without needing to copy content into a separate app.

Does Clearscope help with AI search visibility, not just Google rankings?

Yes, its prompt tracking monitors specific prompts across ChatGPT and Gemini to show when and how a brand is mentioned, and its query fan-out feature reveals the background searches AI platforms run to build answers.

What's the difference between the Essentials and Business plans?

Essentials includes 50 tracked prompts, 50 pages, and 20 monthly drafts for $129/month; Business raises those to 300 tracked prompts and 300 pages for $399/month and adds a dedicated account manager.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, Clearscope offers a 14-day free trial with no contract required to start.

Can I get more drafts or pages than my plan includes?

Yes, additional pages and drafts can be purchased as add-ons (for example, $25/month per 100 extra pages, or $50 per 10 extra drafts on Essentials), with lower per-unit costs on the Business plan.
