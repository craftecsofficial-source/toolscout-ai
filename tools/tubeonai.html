TubeOnAI is an AI content-summarization platform founded in 2023 that positions itself as an all-in-one summarizer for YouTube videos, podcasts, articles, web pages, PDFs, and Google Drive files. The core product transcribes and condenses long-form audio and video into short text summaries or natural-sounding audio playback, and the company claims over 30,000 users rely on it to save hours of weekly content consumption. It ships as a web app, Chrome extension, and native iOS and Android apps, and supports summarization in 22 languages.

Beyond straight summarization, TubeOnAI's differentiator is its content repurposing layer: a library of 150+ custom prompts lets users turn a single summarized video or podcast into a blog post, Twitter/X thread, email newsletter, or podcast script, and a 'Collections' feature organizes summaries into reusable libraries. Auto channel subscriptions generate summaries automatically whenever a followed YouTube channel or podcast publishes new content, with instant or scheduled notifications. The product targets a broad mix of use cases -- students, professors, researchers, content creators, marketers, and business owners staying on top of industry news -- rather than a single vertical.

Best for

TubeOnAI fits students, researchers, professors, and content creators who regularly need to extract key points from long videos, podcasts, or documents and want to repurpose that material into written content. It is a weaker fit for teams needing enterprise-grade collaboration, admin controls, or guaranteed unlimited usage on a budget plan, since the credit system and repurposing features require moving up to the Pro or Enterprise tiers.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The free Starter plan includes 400 credits and 3 channel subscriptions with no credit card required, making it easy to test the core summarization quality before paying.
  • Support for content up to 4 hours long covers use cases like lengthy podcasts, conference talks, and lectures that shorter summarizer tools often can't handle.
  • The repurposing layer (150+ prompts for blog posts, threads, newsletters, and scripts) extends the product beyond a one-off summarizer into an ongoing content workflow tool for creators and marketers.
  • Availability across web, Chrome extension, iOS, and Android means summaries and audio playback are accessible wherever a user is already consuming content.
  • 22-language support and text-to-audio playback broaden accessibility for non-English speakers and users who prefer listening over reading.
  • All paid plans include a 7-day free trial, reducing the risk of committing to a subscription before testing higher-tier features like content repurposing.

Cons

  • The credit-based system means heavy users summarizing many long videos or podcasts daily can burn through monthly allowances quickly, especially on the Essential tier.
  • Core differentiators like content repurposing, text-to-audio, and Collections are gated behind the Pro and Enterprise plans, so the free and Essential tiers are limited to basic summarization.
  • TubeOnAI's G2 presence is still small at 14 verified reviews, which limits the amount of independent, at-scale user feedback available compared to more established productivity tools.
  • Subscriptions are explicitly non-refundable per the company's stated policy; canceling only stops future billing rather than issuing a prorated refund.
  • As a smaller, narrowly-focused company (founded 2023), it lacks the broader integration ecosystem of larger productivity suites.

Frequently asked questions

How much does TubeOnAI cost?

TubeOnAI has a free Starter plan with 400 credits, then paid plans at $9/month (Essential), $19/month (Pro), and $29/month (Enterprise), each with a cheaper annual billing option and a 7-day free trial.

What types of content can TubeOnAI summarize?

It summarizes YouTube videos, podcasts, web articles, Google Drive files, and PDFs, including content up to 4 hours long.

Does TubeOnAI support languages other than English?

Yes, it supports summarization and translation in 22 languages, with content repurposing available in 30+ languages on paid plans.

Can TubeOnAI turn a video into a blog post or social content?

Yes, its content repurposing feature includes 150+ prompt templates for blog posts, Twitter threads, email newsletters, and podcast scripts, available on the Pro and Enterprise plans.

Is there a refund if I cancel my subscription?

No. TubeOnAI states subscriptions are prepaid with no refunds; canceling simply stops future billing at the next renewal date.

What platforms is TubeOnAI available on?

It's available as a web app, a Chrome extension, and native iOS and Android mobile apps.
