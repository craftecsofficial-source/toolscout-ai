TubeOnAI suits students, researchers and creators who want quick summaries of YouTube videos, podcasts, articles and documents and are happy to work within a credit budget. It is less suited to people who need verbatim transcripts, deep analysis or an unlimited free tool. It turns a link or file into a text or audio summary, lets you ask follow-up questions, and can repurpose a summary into blog posts, social posts, newsletters or scripts using 150+ templates.

It runs on the web, as a Chrome extension and in iOS and Android apps, and can auto-summarize new uploads from channels you subscribe to. The site now claims 150k+ users (the earlier copy said 30,000+); we could not verify either number. The site also advertises a summer 70% discount whose link mentions Black Friday, so it may be outdated; confirm at checkout. Checked 2026-10-29.

Best for

Best for students, researchers, creators and busy professionals who consume lots of video and podcast content. Not for anyone who needs an unlimited free summarizer (free is 400 credits), not for people who need exact transcripts or citations with timestamps (not verified here), and not for casual users who only summarize a video or two a month and would do fine on the free plan.

Key features

01

YouTube and Podcast Summarizer

Generates accurate text and audio summaries of YouTube videos and podcasts, including long-form content running up to 4 hours.

02

Article and Document Summarizer

Summarizes publicly available web articles, PDFs, and Google Drive files in addition to video and audio sources.

03

Content Repurposing with Custom Prompts

Offers 150+ prompt templates to turn a summary into a blog post, Twitter thread, email newsletter, or podcast script, plus support for fully custom prompts.

04

Text-to-Audio Summaries

Converts written summaries into natural audio playback with background play support, so users can listen to summaries on the go.

05

Auto Channel Subscriptions

Automatically generates summaries for new uploads from subscribed YouTube channels or podcasts, with instant or scheduled notifications.

06

Ask Follow-up Questions

Lets users chat with a piece of summarized content to dig deeper into specific points instead of re-watching or re-reading the source.

07

Collections

Organizes summaries into customized libraries for easier retrieval and bulk repurposing later.

08

Multi-platform Access

Available as a web app, Chrome extension, and native iOS and Android apps, with support for 22 languages.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The free Starter plan lets you test summary quality with 400 credits and 3 channel subscriptions before paying.
  • One tool covers YouTube, podcasts, web articles, PDFs and Google Drive files, plus follow-up chat on the result.
  • The 150+ repurposing templates turn a summary into blog, thread, newsletter or script drafts, useful for creators.
  • Channel subscriptions auto-summarize new uploads, saving the manual step of pasting links.
  • Web, Chrome extension, iOS and Android coverage.

Cons

  • Everything is metered by credits; the page does not say here how many credits a given video costs, so heavy users should test first.
  • Content repurposing, text-to-audio and Collections are not in Free or Essential.
  • Enterprise limits are described inconsistently between monthly and annual billing.
  • Pricing promotions on the home page look possibly stale, so the price you pay may differ.
  • Satisfaction and user-count claims are self-reported; no verified third-party rating was checked.

Frequently asked questions

Is TubeOnAI free?

Yes. Starter is free with 400 credits, 3 channel subscriptions and low priority support.

How much do paid plans cost?

Essential $9/mo ($80/yr), Pro $19/mo ($170/yr), Enterprise $29/mo ($260/yr), per the pricing page on 2026-10-29.

Which plan do I need for repurposing and audio?

Pro or Enterprise. Essential covers summaries and 10,000 credits per month but not repurposing, audio or Collections.

What are the limits?

Credits: 400 on Free, 10,000/mo on Essential, 20,000/mo on Pro. Enterprise is listed as unlimited, though the wording differs between monthly and annual pages.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes: iOS and Android apps plus a Chrome extension are advertised.
