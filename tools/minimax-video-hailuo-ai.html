MiniMax Video, marketed to consumers under the Hailuo AI brand (hailuoai.video), is the video-generation product line from MiniMax, a Shanghai-based general AI company founded in 2022 with the mission of 'building AGI with everyone.' The platform turns text prompts or a single reference image into short video clips — typically 5 to 15 seconds — complete with native, synchronized stereo audio, a feature that distinguishes it from many competitors that generate silent video requiring a separate audio pass. Its flagship models include the Hailuo 2.3 family (Standard, Pro, and Fast variants) and the newer MiniMax H3, an open-weight omni-modal model announced in 2026 that unifies understanding, generation, and precise editing across formats, targeting use cases in film, advertising, e-commerce, and social content at 2K resolution.

MiniMax positions Hailuo AI as a value alternative to higher-priced frontier video models like Google's Veo 3 and Kling AI, emphasizing per-clip cost efficiency — its developer API charges as little as $0.19 per clip for fast, lower-resolution output, undercutting many rivals on raw compute cost. The web app runs on a credit system with five access tiers (Free, Standard, Pro, Master, Max), where credit cost per video scales sharply with resolution and model quality: a baseline 512p/6-second clip costs 12 credits, while 1080p or 'cinematic' renders can cost 50-80 credits. MiniMax also operates a broader AI product suite — including the M-series LLMs, MiniMax Design, MiniMax Code, MiniMax Audio, and the Talkie companion app — serving what the company describes as 200+ million users globally, with Hailuo Video as its dedicated visual generation front end for both casual creators and API-driven studio pipelines.

Best for

Best fit: budget-conscious creators, marketers, and developer teams who want fast, inexpensive AI video clips with built-in audio and are comfortable working within a credit-based system, especially via the pay-per-clip API for automated pipelines. Weak fit: teams needing guaranteed high-resolution output at predictable low cost (1080p pricing scales steeply), or buyers who weigh deep G2/Capterra review history heavily, since MiniMax's video product has limited independent B2B review coverage so far.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Per-clip API pricing as low as $0.19-$0.56 per video undercuts many rival frontier video APIs on raw cost, per third-party pricing breakdowns comparing it to Kling 3.0 and Veo 3.
  • Native synchronized audio generation means many clips need no separate sound-design pass, a differentiator versus silent-by-default competitors.
  • Entry-level Standard plan at $7.99/month removes watermarks and grants commercial rights, a low bar to paid, publishable output.
  • MiniMax H3 unifies generation, understanding, and precise editing in one open-weight model, giving more workflow flexibility (e.g., targeted edits) than single-purpose generators.
  • Backed by a well-funded, high-scale AI company (MiniMax, reportedly serving 200M+ users across its product suite), suggesting infrastructure stability and continued model investment.

Cons

  • Real HD cost is steep relative to marketing headlines: 1080p/cinematic renders use a 50-80 credit multiplier versus 12 credits for baseline 512p output, roughly a 3-6x jump per clip.
  • Aggregated third-party review commentary describes a pattern of complaints around unexpected auto-renewal charges, credits deducted on failed generations, and slow support response — worth validating during a trial before committing to an annual plan.
  • Monthly membership credits expire at the end of each billing cycle and don't roll over, unlike separately purchased top-up credits (valid up to 2 years), which can waste unused allocation.
  • Free trial is tightly limited — 200 one-time credits expire in just 3 days, a narrow window to evaluate output quality before choosing a paid tier.
  • Independent B2B review coverage is minimal at time of research compared to more established competitors like Runway or Synthesia, making it harder to validate reliability claims with volume review data.

Frequently asked questions

Is MiniMax Video the same product as Hailuo AI?

Yes — Hailuo AI (hailuoai.video) is MiniMax's consumer-facing video generation app, built on its Hailuo/H3 model family.

How much does Hailuo AI cost?

Web plans range from $7.99 to $199.99 per month based on monthly credit allotment; the developer API charges pay-as-you-go, from about $0.19 per clip.

Does it generate audio along with the video?

Yes, Hailuo AI produces native synchronized stereo audio alongside the generated video, unlike many competitors that output silent clips.

What does the free plan include?

A one-time 200 credit grant that expires after 3 days, capped at 720p resolution with mandatory watermarks and slow single-thread processing.

How long are the generated video clips?

Typically 5 to 15 seconds depending on the model and settings selected.

Why does 1080p or cinematic output cost so much more?

Higher-resolution and cinematic renders apply a credit multiplier of 50-80 credits per clip versus 12 credits for a baseline 512p/6-second video.
