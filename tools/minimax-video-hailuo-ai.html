Hailuo AI is a good fit for creators making short social clips from prompts, photos, or templates, and it is a weaker choice if you need verified, predictable pricing or tightly controlled long-form output. It is MiniMax's consumer web app for generating video and images, and the homepage currently features the MiniMax H3 model alongside an agent, reference inputs, and many effect templates.

Best for

Good for social media creators, marketers making short ads, and hobbyists who like template-led generation. Not for people who need confirmed pricing before they start, teams that need long-form or fully controlled edit pipelines, or anyone averse to credit-based billing.

Key features

01

Create Video and Create Image

Main tools for generating video and images from prompts or uploaded photos.

02

MiniMax H3

The homepage marks MiniMax H3 as live, describing native multimodal generation and precise editing.

03

Omni Reference

A reference-input feature that lets you supply guidance material to the generator.

04

Hailuo Agent

An assistant feature listed on the homepage; we did not test how it works.

05

Templates

Photo-to-motion, transformation, dance, K-Pop music video, ad, pet, and cinematic style templates are shown on the homepage.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Large template library makes it easy to get a usable clip without careful prompting.
  • One app covers video, images, reference inputs, and an assets library.
  • Backed by an established AI company with active model releases (H3 is featured as live).

Cons

  • We could not read plan prices on the official page, and third-party figures contradict each other, so budget carefully.
  • Credit cost per clip depends on model and mode, making total cost hard to forecast.
  • Features such as native audio, clip length, and resolution were not confirmed on the official site today.
  • Third-party coverage mentions billing and renewal complaints; we did not verify them, so confirm cancellation terms yourself.
