LLMrefs suits marketers and agencies who want a cheap, self-serve way to see where a brand appears in AI answers, with the price visible up front. Teams that need enterprise support, bespoke reporting or content production should skip it, since it only measures.

The tool tracks 500 prompts across the major AI engines, auto-suggests prompts from conversational search patterns, records which sources get cited, and benchmarks share of voice against named competitors. Pricing is a single $79/month plan flagged as a limited-time rate, so the long-term price is unknown.

Best for

Good for SEO and marketing teams, consultants and agencies (unlimited projects and seats) wanting a low-cost first AEO tracker. It is NOT for enterprises needing account management or SSO-style controls, brands tracking thousands of prompts, or teams wanting the tool to write and publish content.

Key features

01

Multi-engine prompt tracking

Monitors up to 500 prompts per month across 11+ AI search and chat engines in one plan, rather than charging separately per engine as some competitors do.

02

Auto-generated prompt discovery

Generates candidate prompts based on patterns from real user conversations with chatbots, reducing the manual work of building a keyword/prompt list from scratch.

03

Citation and source tracking

Identifies which web sources AI engines are actually citing when they answer prompts related to a brand's category, useful for spotting which pages or domains AI models trust.

04

Competitor share-of-voice benchmarking

Compares a brand's presence and position in AI answers against named competitors to quantify relative visibility over time.

05

Geo and language targeting

Runs tracking across 50+ countries and 20+ languages, which matters for brands whose AI visibility varies by region or where AI answers are localized.

06

Weekly automated reporting

Delivers scheduled visibility reports rather than requiring users to manually pull dashboards, with monthly AI prompt volume estimates included.

07

CSV export and API access

Lets teams pull raw tracking data out of the platform for use in their own BI tools or client reporting decks.

08

Supplementary AEO utilities

Bundles smaller tools referenced on the pricing page — an AI crawlability checker, a Reddit threads finder, and an llms.txt generator — alongside the core tracking product.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Published price and a no-card trial let you test cost before talking to anyone.
  • One plan covers every supported engine, so broadening coverage does not add fees.
  • Unlimited seats, projects and domains makes it economical for agencies.
  • Auto-generated prompts lower the setup effort for teams new to AEO.

Cons

  • The $79 rate is labeled 'limited time only' with no end date or later price, so budgeting beyond the trial is uncertain.
  • The pricing page says extra capacity can be added, but gives no price; the FAQ mentions a '50 keywords' threshold that conflicts with the 500-prompt limit.
  • Geo/language coverage is stated inconsistently across the site (50+ countries vs 20+).
  • Tracking only: fixes, content and outreach are left to you.
  • I found no verifiable independent review data (G2/Capterra) today.

Frequently asked questions

How much does LLMrefs cost?

One paid plan, All in One, at $79/month, labeled limited time only. It includes 500 prompts and all supported engines.

Is there a free trial or free plan?

A 7-day free trial with no credit card is offered, and the pricing page also mentions a free account without detailing its limits.

Which AI engines are tracked?

ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Grok, Copilot and Meta AI, with DeepSeek and ChatGPT Search also mentioned on the site.

What if I need more than 500 prompts?

The site says you can upgrade for more, but no add-on price is published; ask support before relying on it.

Does it create or publish content?

No. It tracks and reports; content and technical fixes need other tools.
