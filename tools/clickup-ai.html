Great for teams that already live in ClickUp and want AI that can read their tasks, docs and chats; skip it if you don't use ClickUp, since Brain is not a standalone assistant. It is also a poor fit for small teams where only one or two people would use AI, as the add-on is priced per user.

Brain is ClickUp's built-in assistant. It pulls from your workspace, connected apps and optionally the web to answer questions, draft and summarise, build decks or dashboards, and analyse CSV files. You can pick the underlying model (Claude, GPT, Gemini), save preferences it remembers, and share AI chats with teammates. Simple chat is unlimited on the add-on, while heavier features such as Super Agents, AI automations and AI fields spend monthly Super Credits.

Best for

Teams already running projects in ClickUp that want AI with direct access to their tasks, docs and chats, and that will have many people using it. Not for individuals or small teams that just want a general chatbot, not for anyone not using ClickUp as their work hub, and not for buyers who need one fixed, predictable AI price.

Key features

01

Model choice

Switch between models such as Claude, GPT and Gemini; each one can see your workspace context.

02

Memory and skills

Remembers your role, preferences and communication style, which you can view, edit or delete, and runs custom workflows on command. Memories can be imported from ChatGPT or Claude.

03

Shared AI

Shared chats, topic channels, AI tasks and projects so several teammates work from the same persistent context.

04

Deep Search

Searches the workspace, connected apps and the web for harder questions.

05

Notetaker and presence

Joins calls, takes notes and surfaces related context. The full Notetaker sits in the Everything AI tier.

06

Connected apps and MCP

Links Google Drive, GitHub, Salesforce and other tools, plus MCP servers.

07

Generated deliverables

Produces slide decks, dashboards, landing pages and prototypes, and analyses CSV data with charts and exports.

08

Desktop, mobile and voice

Apps for macOS, Windows, iOS and Android, a Chrome extension, and voice input.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Lets you choose among Claude, GPT and Gemini inside one subscription, so you avoid paying for each separately.
  • AI sits on top of tasks, docs, chat and dashboards you already use, so answers reference real work.
  • Everyday Brain chat is unlimited on the add-on and does not burn credits.
  • Memory can be inspected, edited, deleted and imported from ChatGPT or Claude.
  • ClickUp says providers retain no customer data and it lists SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR and HIPAA compliance (company claims, not independently verified here).
  • A free plan lets you try Brain before paying.

Cons

  • You need a ClickUp workspace; the AI is of little use if your work lives elsewhere.
  • Credit-metered features (Super Agents, AI automations, AI fields) can run out of the monthly allowance, with extra credits at $10 per 10,000.
  • The pricing page showed two layouts with different plan prices (e.g. Business $12 vs $17 annual), so confirm the real cost before buying.
  • The page does not state whether the $9 and $28 AI prices are annual or monthly; the older listing said $18 monthly for Brain.
  • Per-user AI pricing multiplies across a big team even if few people use it (check with ClickUp whether it can be limited to some seats).
  • Not verified here: performance on large workspaces or user ratings of the AI itself.

Frequently asked questions

Is ClickUp Brain free?

There is a free plan to try Brain, with a small AI credit allowance. Ongoing use is sold as an add-on from $9 per user per month.

How much does it cost?

Brain AI is $9 per user per month with 1,500 Super Credits; Everything AI is $28 per user per month with 5,000 credits and Notetaker, AI Fields, Automations and Super Agents. This is on top of the ClickUp plan itself. The pricing page did not say whether the AI prices are annual or monthly.

What are Super Credits?

Credits spent by advanced features such as Super Agents, AI automations and AI fields. Brain conversations themselves are unlimited. Extra credits are listed at $10 per 10,000.

How is it different from ChatGPT or Claude?

Brain reads your ClickUp tasks, docs and chats and connected apps directly and lets you pick the model, so you do not paste context. A standalone chatbot is better if your work is not in ClickUp.

Is my data used to train AI models?

ClickUp says customer data is not used to train third-party models and providers retain none of it. That is the company's statement.
