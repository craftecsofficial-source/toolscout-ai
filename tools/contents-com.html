Contents.com is a good fit for mid-size and large marketing teams that care about brand consistency, approvals and audit trails around AI-written content. Solo creators and small businesses should probably skip it, since there are no public prices and the product is now pitched squarely at enterprises. It was previously known for self-serve plans; today the site presents a platform where you can run content operations yourself or have the vendor run them. Core pieces are an AI Writer (articles, product copy, social posts, emails), AI Art for images, an HTML editor for building pages and emails through chat, and Brand Voice settings. Governance tools cover approval chains, brand guardrails and audit trails. AI Chat, AI Translation and Audio-Text are shown as coming soon, and some usage statistics on the home page look like unfilled placeholders, so treat the roadmap items with caution.

Best for

Suits enterprise and agency content teams that need governed, brand-consistent AI output at scale. It is not for freelancers, solo bloggers or small businesses wanting a cheap monthly plan with visible prices.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Approval chains, permissions and audit trails are built in, which matters for regulated or multi-brand teams.
  • Brand Voice and knowledge-base organisation help keep many writers on message.
  • Text, images and HTML page/email building live in one workspace.
  • A managed-service option helps teams without in-house prompt or workflow skills.
  • Batch generation and reusable workflows suit high-volume product or catalogue copy.

Cons

  • No prices are published, so budgeting or comparing against self-serve tools requires a sales call.
  • AI Chat and AI Translation are still listed as coming soon, so they should not factor into a purchase today.
  • The site is inconsistent about language support (it cites both 6 and 25 content languages), so test your language before committing.
  • Home page stats look like placeholders, which suggests the marketing site is still being finished.
  • Small teams and freelancers will find the enterprise tooling heavier than they need.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Contents.com cost?

No prices are published. The Plans page shows a single Enterprise plan with a Contact Sales button, so you need to request a quote.

Is there a free plan?

A free plan is not stated. The site offers a Start Free Trial button, but its length and limits are not published.

Did Contents.com used to have Starter and Premium plans?

Older listings quoted Free, Starter and Premium tiers. They no longer appear on the current Plans page, so we have removed those prices.

Which languages does it support?

The interface is offered in seven languages including English, Italian and Arabic. The site refers to both 6 and 25 content languages, so verify your target language in a trial.

How does it compare with Jasper or Writesonic?

Contents.com leans harder on approvals, audit trails and a managed-service option. Jasper and Writesonic are more commonly bought self-serve by smaller teams.
