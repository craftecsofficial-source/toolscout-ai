Contents.com (known in its home market of Italy under the earlier name Zulla) is an AI-powered content creation platform aimed at marketers, agencies, and enterprises that need to produce written and visual content across multiple languages. The platform bundles three core tools — an AI Writer for long-form and SEO content, an AI Chat assistant for brainstorming and editing, and AI Art for generating accompanying images — inside a single dashboard, so users can move from a content brief to a finished blog post, ad, or social caption without switching software. It supports content generation in more than 25 languages, with six languages (English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, and Brazilian Portuguese) using natively trained datasets rather than purely machine-translated models, which the company positions as a quality differentiator for non-English markets.

Contents.com differentiates itself from US-centric AI writing tools by leaning into its European roots and multilingual-first design, and by blending pure AI generation with an optional human copywriting credit on higher tiers, letting teams mix machine-drafted content with professional editing when quality bars are higher. The company offers a free tier capped at a handful of daily creations so prospective customers can test output quality before subscribing, then scales up through Starter and Premium plans toward custom Enterprise pricing for larger organizations with volume or compliance needs. Capterra reviews, concentrated heavily among Italian users, describe the platform as intuitive even for people with no prior AI experience, and reviewers frequently mention the Contents.com team responding directly to feedback in review comment threads, suggesting an actively maintained product with a hands-on support culture.

Best for

Contents.com suits marketing teams and agencies, especially in Europe, that need SEO content, social copy, and images in multiple languages from one platform, and value access to an optional human-copywriting layer. It's a weaker fit for teams needing a pure English-only, US-centric workflow or advanced video content generation, since those aren't core strengths of the platform.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Capterra reviewers repeatedly describe the platform as easy to pick up even without prior experience using AI tools, with several noting they were productive within 30 minutes.
  • The AI Chat and industry trend features are called out specifically as useful for brainstorming, per a 2024 Capterra review from a consultant in industrial automation.
  • Reviewers who compared Contents.com to plain ChatGPT say the built-in topic and heading suggestions gave it an edge over generic chat interfaces.
  • Multiple Italian-language reviews praise the responsiveness of customer support, including direct replies from the Contents.com team addressing specific feature requests.
  • Users highlight the combination of writing, chat, and image tools in one subscription as saving them from paying for multiple separate AI apps.

Cons

  • One reviewer flagged that generated articles and reviews can go off-topic, requiring manual correction of inconsistencies before publishing.
  • A recurring complaint is a limited word count for content regeneration on lower tiers, though the company has said improvements are in progress.
  • The platform lacks text-to-video generation, according to at least one 2024 review, which some users wanted as part of the same suite.
  • Non-English output occasionally contains minor language errors, for example a reviewer noted mistakes specifically in Ukrainian-language text.

Frequently asked questions

What languages does Contents.com support?

Contents.com generates content in 25+ languages, with six languages — English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, and Brazilian Portuguese — built on natively trained datasets rather than machine translation.

Does Contents.com have a free plan?

Yes, a free tier allows up to 8 content creations per day, letting users test the platform before subscribing to Starter or Premium.

Can Contents.com generate images as well as text?

Yes, the AI Art module generates images inside the same workspace used for AI Writer and AI Chat, so text and visuals can be produced together.

Does Contents.com include human copywriters?

The Premium plan includes a monthly credit toward professional human copywriting services for content that needs an editorial pass beyond pure AI output.

Who was Zulla and how does it relate to Contents.com?

Zulla was the original Italian brand name for the platform before it was rebranded internationally as Contents.com; longtime Italian reviews still reference the Zulla name.

Is Contents.com good for SEO content?

Yes, the AI Writer is built around generating SEO blog articles and keyword-driven content, making it a core use case for the platform alongside social and ad copy.
