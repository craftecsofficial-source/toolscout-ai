Originality.ai launched in 2023, founded by Jon Gillham, as one of the first dedicated AI-content detectors built specifically for the publishing and content-marketing industry rather than the academic-integrity market served by tools like Turnitin. It positions itself around a "Publish with Integrity" message aimed at website owners, content marketers, and agencies who need to verify that outsourced writers or freelancers aren't submitting undisclosed AI-generated copy. The tool combines an AI detector tuned for outputs from ChatGPT, GPT-4o, Gemini, Claude, and Llama-family models with a plagiarism checker, readability scoring, a grammar and spelling checker, and a Fact Checker that flags claims that may be hallucinated or unverified.

Credits work on a pay-per-100-words model, with 1 credit covering a 100-word AI-only check and 2 credits covering a combined AI-plus-plagiarism scan of the same length. The product supports full-site scans, URL-based scanning, file uploads for Word and PDF documents, and a Chrome extension that can run checks directly inside Google Docs, including a replay feature that reconstructs how a document was written over time. Originality.ai has been referenced in coverage by outlets including the New York Times, Reuters, and the Guardian in stories about AI-generated content, and lists SEO platforms such as Surfer among its integration partners.

Best for

Originality.ai is best suited to content agencies, digital publishers, and site owners who regularly work with freelance or outsourced writers and need a systematic way to verify originality and AI usage before publishing. It's a weaker fit for individual writers or small blogs with low content volume, since the credit-based pricing is optimized for teams scanning content regularly rather than occasional one-off checks.

Key features

01

AI Content Detector

Scores text for the likelihood it was generated by ChatGPT, GPT-4o, Gemini, Claude, Llama, and other popular AI models, including detection of paraphrased AI content.

02

Plagiarism Checker

Cross-references submitted text against web content to flag copied or unoriginal passages, positioned by the company as more accurate than tools like Copyscape.

03

Fact Checker

An automated aid that flags claims in a document that may be inaccurate or hallucinated, intended to reduce the risk of publishing factually wrong AI-assisted content.

04

Readability Checker

Scores content against readability tests calibrated from a large dataset of ranking articles, helping publishers tune content for both readers and search engines.

05

Grammar & Spelling Checker

A built-in grammar and spelling pass that can be run alongside AI and plagiarism scans in the same workflow.

06

Full Site Scan

Scans an entire website or specific pages in bulk by URL, letting agencies audit large existing content libraries for AI or plagiarism issues at once.

07

Chrome Extension for Google Docs

Runs AI and plagiarism scans directly inside Google Docs and can replay the writing process of a document to help spot suspicious editing patterns.

08

Team Management & Tagging

Assigns team roles and lets users tag and organize scans systematically, useful for agencies managing many writers and clients.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Reviewers and SEO practitioners such as Glenn Gabe of GSQI.com describe Originality.ai as one of the most accurate AI detectors they've tested, including its ability to catch paraphrased AI content that simpler detectors miss.
  • The full-site and URL-based scanning options let content teams and agencies retroactively audit entire existing websites for AI-generated or plagiarized content, not just newly submitted drafts.
  • Bundling AI detection, plagiarism, readability, grammar, and fact-checking into one credit system reduces the need to subscribe to multiple separate point-tools for editorial QA.
  • The Chrome extension's document replay feature gives editors visibility into how a piece was actually written, which is useful evidence when confronting writers about undisclosed AI use.
  • API access on Enterprise lets agencies and publishers wire AI/plagiarism checks directly into their own CMS or freelancer-management workflows for scale.

Cons

  • The credit-based model means costs scale directly with content volume, and heavy publishers running both AI and plagiarism checks on every piece can burn through credits quickly.
  • There is no ongoing free tier for regular use — new users only get a limited number of free signup credits before needing to subscribe.
  • Like all AI detectors, Originality.ai can produce false positives on human-written text that happens to be highly structured or formulaic, which reviewers note requires human judgment rather than blind trust in the score.
  • The jump from the Pro tier's 2,000 monthly credits to Enterprise's $179/mo price point is steep for mid-sized teams that outgrow Pro but don't need full agency-scale volume.

Frequently asked questions

How does Originality.ai's credit system work?

One credit covers a 100-word AI-only scan, while a combined AI-plus-plagiarism scan of the same 100 words uses 2 credits; unused subscription credits expire at the end of each month.

Can Originality.ai check an entire website at once?

Yes, both the Pro and Enterprise plans support full-site scans and scanning individual pages by URL, not just single documents.

Does Originality.ai offer a free trial?

New users get 50 free signup credits with no credit card required, which can be used to test the AI checker and plagiarism checker before subscribing.

What AI models can Originality.ai detect?

The detector is tuned to identify content generated by ChatGPT, GPT-4o, Gemini Pro, Claude 3.5, Llama 3.1, and other popular AI writing and paraphrasing tools.

Is there an API for Originality.ai?

Yes, API access is included on the Enterprise plan, letting agencies and publishers integrate AI and plagiarism checks directly into their own tools and workflows.

How long are scan reports stored?

Pro plan scan history is retained for 30 days, while Enterprise plan scan history is retained for 365 days.
