Originality.ai is a strong fit for publishers, agencies and editors who need to screen lots of submitted content for AI writing and copied text. Skip it if you want a verdict you can treat as proof, for example to penalize a student or writer on the score alone, since AI detectors can misjudge text. It reads your text and gives a likelihood that it was AI-written, highlights suspect sentences, and can also run a plagiarism check, fact check, grammar check and readability score. You can scan pasted text, uploaded files, URLs or whole sites, and use it through browser extensions, Google Docs or an API.

Best for

Good for content teams, agencies, publishers and educators who review many documents and want an auditable scan history. Not for people who need a definitive ruling on authorship (the score is an estimate), for occasional users who only need one or two free checks, or for anyone uncomfortable with credit-based pricing. Casual users should note the free tool may use submitted text for training.

Key features

01

AI detection

Estimates how likely text is to come from AI models such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Grok, with sentence-level highlighting. An AI Allowance setting lets you set how much AI assistance you accept.

02

Deep Scan

Explains why text was flagged and suggests edits.

03

Plagiarism and quality checks

A plagiarism checker plus fact and AI hallucination checking, grammar, readability, a content quality score and a guideline checker.

04

Bulk, URL and site scans

Pro and Enterprise can scan many documents, individual URLs and full sites, with file upload, team management and scan tagging.

05

Reports and history

PDF reports and scan history; Enterprise adds shareable reports and 365-day history.

06

Integrations

Chrome and Firefox extensions (including a writing replay), Google Docs, a Moodle plugin and an API/MCP option.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Pairs AI detection with plagiarism, fact and readability checks, so one scan workflow covers several editorial tasks.
  • Sentence highlighting and PDF reports make it easy to discuss flagged passages with a writer.
  • Whole-site and URL scanning is useful for auditing existing content libraries.
  • Pro is cheap relative to many team tools, and credits can be topped up if you run out.
  • Offers an AI-training opt-out and TLS 1.2+ encryption.

Cons

  • Detection is probabilistic; paraphrased or heavily edited text can slip through and human writing can be flagged, so do not treat scores as proof.
  • Credits cost 1 per 100 words, and a 100-word minimum per scan applies; subscription credits expire each month.
  • The free plan is detection-only, and text entered into the free tool may be used for training.
  • API access, shareable reports and long history sit behind the $179/mo Enterprise plan.
  • The pricing page comparison table appears to show a stray '$179' against the free plan, so check the current page before buying.

Frequently asked questions

Is Originality.ai free?

There is a free Basic plan: AI detection only, 60 credits renewing daily (3 scans a day) and up to 2,000 words per scan. Plagiarism checking and site scans need a paid plan.

How much does it cost?

Pro is $14.95/mo ($12.95/mo on yearly billing) and Enterprise $179/mo ($136.58/mo yearly).

How do credits work?

One credit equals 100 words. Pro includes 2,000 credits a month and Enterprise 15,000; subscription credits expire monthly, while purchased top-up credits last two years.

Can I rely on the AI score as proof?

No. It gives a probability, and any AI detector can produce false positives or miss edited AI text. Use it as one signal alongside editorial judgment.

What are the alternatives?

Grammarly and Sapling include AI detection features, but Originality.ai is more focused on publisher workflows like bulk and site scanning.
