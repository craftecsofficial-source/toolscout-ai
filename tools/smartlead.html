Smartlead is a good choice for lead-gen agencies and outbound teams that run many domains and mailboxes and want a sender-rotation tool priced by send volume. Skip it if you only send a few hundred emails a month, need deep native CRM sync, or want a gentle, simple interface.

It connects as many mailboxes as you like, warms them up automatically, and spreads each campaign's sends across them. Replies from every account land in one inbox. Plans are capped by monthly email sends, verified-email credits and contacts rather than by mailbox count.

The headline price omits extras: whitelabeling is $29/month per client workspace, and buying domains and mailboxes through its SmartSenders marketplace, inbox-placement testing (SmartDelivery, from $49/month) and calling (SmartDialer) are billed separately.

Best for

Lead-gen agencies, SDR teams and founders sending thousands of cold emails a month from several domains. It is not for low-volume senders, teams wanting a full CRM or sales suite, or anyone who needs a simple guided setup; Apollo.io bundles data and sequencing instead.

Key features

01

Unlimited mailboxes, warmup and rotation

Connect any number of sending accounts; Smartlead warms them and rotates campaign sends across them.

02

Unified inbox

All replies from all mailboxes in one place with lead categorization.

03

Email verification

Verified-email credits are included per plan (2,000 on Base up to 170,000 on Prime) and more can be bought.

04

Multichannel and calling

Sequences can add LinkedIn steps via integrations; SmartDialer voice calling starts at $9 in monthly credits.

05

SmartSenders and SmartServers

Buy domains and mailboxes (about $4-9 per mailbox per month) or dedicated servers ($39 each per month) inside the platform.

06

Agency workspaces

Separate client workspaces; whitelabeling costs $29/month per client workspace.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Unlimited mailboxes on all plans, so adding sender accounts does not raise the subscription.
  • Month-to-month billing with no lock-in and a free trial without a card.
  • Rotation, warmup and a unified inbox are core, not paid upgrades.
  • Agency workspaces and whitelabeling exist, with transparent per-client pricing.
  • Domains, mailboxes, verification and placement testing can be bought inside the same platform.

Cons

  • Send caps bite fast: Base allows 6,000 sends a month, roughly 200 a day.
  • Real cost is higher than the headline: whitelabel is $29 per client, mailboxes about $4-9 each per month, SmartDelivery from $49.
  • The pricing page was inconsistent in places during our check (a stray $59 figure), so confirm the final price at checkout.
  • Native CRM integrations are limited, so many teams rely on Zapier or API.
  • Many settings make setup confusing for first-time cold emailers; good deliverability still depends on your domains and copy.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Smartlead cost?

Monthly: Base $39, Pro $94, Unlimited Smart $174, Unlimited Prime $379. Annual billing brings them to $32, $78, $144 and $315 per month. Add-ons cost extra.

Is there a free plan or trial?

The pricing page says every plan has a free trial with no credit card; trial length is not stated. There is no permanent free plan.

Is the number of mailboxes limited?

No, the site says all plans include unlimited email accounts. The limits are monthly sends, verified emails and contacts.

Does it cost extra for agencies?

Yes. Whitelabeling is $29/month per client workspace; Prime includes 3 client workspaces and Pro and Smart offer them as add-ons.

How does it compare to Instantly?

Both are cold email tools with unlimited mailboxes. Compare send limits, lead database needs and agency features on each pricing page before choosing.
