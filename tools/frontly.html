Frontly is a good pick for small teams and solo builders who want a quick AI-generated portal or internal tool at a low price. Skip it if you need complex business logic, heavy data volumes, or confirmed connectors to a specific database, because those could not be verified. It is a no-code app builder where you describe the software you want, get an AI-generated first version, and then edit it visually. Its template list covers client login portals, events management, expense and habit trackers, and real-estate listing sites. Plans differ mainly by monthly operations, AI credits, support level, branded apps and API access.

Best for

Good for small teams, freelancers and agencies with a few simple portals or trackers to ship quickly. Not for teams with complex workflows, large data volumes, strict compliance needs, or those who must have a specific database connector confirmed up front.

Key features

01

AI app generation

Describe the app you want and Frontly drafts it; AI credits per month are 250 (Starter), 1,000 (Basic) and 5,000 (Professional).

02

Visual no-code editing

Adjust the generated app by hand in an editor without writing code.

03

Templates

Starting points include a client login portal, events management platform, expense tracker, habit tracker and real estate listings site.

04

Unlimited apps and users

No caps on the number of apps or users on any plan; limits come from operations and AI credits instead.

05

Custom branded apps

Basic includes 1 and Professional includes 3 custom branded apps.

06

API access

Available on the Professional plan.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Apps and users are unlimited on every plan, so cost tracks usage rather than headcount.
  • The free tier lets you build and test before paying anything.
  • AI generation removes the blank-canvas problem; you edit a draft instead of starting empty.
  • Pricing steps are clear and low ($30 and $100 per month) compared with enterprise internal-tool platforms.

Cons

  • Operation quotas (1,000 free, 10,000 on Basic) can run out on busy portals, and what counts as an operation was not verified.
  • Free apps carry Frontly branding, and white-label branding is limited to 1 or 3 apps on paid plans, which is tight for agencies.
  • The homepage does not state which databases or APIs it connects to, so check this before committing.
  • AI credits are a second metered resource to watch alongside operations.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Starter is free with 1,000 operations and 250 AI credits per month, and it shows Frontly branding.

How much do paid plans cost?

Basic is $30/month and Professional is $100/month, per the homepage on 2026-11-03. Enterprise pricing is custom.

Is there a limit on apps or users?

The homepage says apps and users are unlimited on all plans. Limits apply to monthly operations, AI credits and branded apps.

Which data sources can it connect to?

Not confirmed. The public homepage did not list connectors, so ask the vendor or test the free plan.

Can I use my own branding?

Yes, but only on paid plans: 1 custom branded app on Basic and 3 on Professional.
