Frontly is built around a specific and common pain point: teams have data living in spreadsheets, APIs, and databases, but no easy way to turn that data into a usable, permissioned web app for employees or clients. Rather than positioning itself as a general website builder, Frontly focuses on internal tools and portals — think admin dashboards, client-facing status portals, approval workflows, and data entry apps — built by connecting directly to existing data sources instead of requiring a from-scratch backend.

The platform's AI layer is aimed at reducing the setup time for these apps: instead of manually placing every table, form, and chart, users can describe what they want in a prompt and get a starting layout that's already wired toward their connected data, which they can then refine using Frontly's visual editor. Built-in support for user authentication and role-based permissions means the resulting apps can be handed to specific employees or external clients with appropriate access controls, rather than being limited to internal-only prototypes.

Frontly's positioning sits between spreadsheet-to-app tools and heavier internal-tool platforms — it's lighter-weight to get started with than enterprise low-code suites, but more structured around data-bound apps than general-purpose no-code website or app builders. This makes it a natural fit for agencies building client portals and ops teams that need a quick internal dashboard without engineering time.

Best for

Frontly suits operations teams, startups, and agencies that already have their data in spreadsheets, databases, or APIs and need a fast way to expose it as an internal dashboard, approval tool, or client portal. It's particularly useful for agencies delivering white-labeled client portals without engineering overhead. Teams needing deeply custom application logic, heavy transaction processing, or enterprise-scale data volumes may need a more code-centric or established low-code platform instead.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Turns existing operational data (sheets, APIs, databases) into a usable app far faster than building a custom backend and frontend from scratch.
  • AI-assisted generation reduces the blank-canvas problem common to no-code builders, giving users a working starting point immediately.
  • Built-in authentication and permissions make it viable for client-facing portals, not just internal prototypes.
  • White-labeling and custom domains let agencies deliver Frontly-built portals under a client's own branding.

Cons

  • Highly bespoke business logic or complex multi-step workflows can be harder to model than in a full-code application.
  • App quality is tied to the structure and cleanliness of the connected data source — messy spreadsheets or inconsistent APIs can require extra cleanup work.
  • As a smaller, newer platform, it has fewer third-party integrations, templates, and community resources than established no-code incumbents.
  • Teams with very large-scale or high-concurrency data needs may eventually outgrow a spreadsheet/API-first architecture.

Frequently asked questions

What data sources can Frontly connect to?

Frontly connects to REST APIs, Google Sheets, Airtable, and SQL databases as the backend for the apps you build.

Does Frontly require any coding?

No, Frontly is a no-code, drag-and-drop platform, though its AI features can also generate an initial layout from a text prompt.

Can I build a client-facing portal, not just an internal tool?

Yes, Frontly supports white-labeled, custom-domain client portals in addition to internal-only tools.

Does Frontly support user permissions?

Yes, built-in authentication and role-based permissions let you control who can access which parts of an app.

Is there a free plan?

Yes, Frontly offers a free tier for building and testing basic apps, with paid plans for more apps, custom domains, and higher usage.

Is Frontly a good fit for complex, highly custom applications?

It's better suited to data-bound dashboards and portals; apps needing very custom business logic may require a more code-flexible platform.
