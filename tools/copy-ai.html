Copy.ai is an AI content and workflow-automation platform originally built for marketing copywriting, now repositioned as a go-to-market (GTM) AI platform for sales and marketing teams. It was acquired by Fullcast, Inc. in October 2025, and the product has since shifted from a simple "generate copy fast" tool into a broader automation layer that connects to CRMs and GTM systems to run multi-step AI workflows.

Its core differentiator versus adjacent tools like Jasper or Writesonic is the Workflow Builder: instead of one-off text generation, Copy.ai chains research, content generation, and tool integrations (CRM enrichment, prospecting, account intelligence) into repeatable automations, positioning it closer to an AI operations layer for revenue teams than a pure writing assistant.

Best for

Copy.ai is best suited to sales and marketing operations teams at mid-size to large companies who want to automate multi-step GTM processes (prospecting research, CRM enrichment, personalized outreach at scale) and are prepared to pay $1,000/mo or more once they outgrow the small Chat tier. Solo freelancers, small business owners or bloggers who just want a low-cost tool for generating ad copy or emails should look elsewhere, since the pricing structure no longer supports a mid-tier, self-serve growth path the way it used to.

Key features

01

Workflows & Workflow Builder

A visual builder for chaining multiple AI steps (research, generation, formatting, tool calls) into a single repeatable automation, rather than one-shot prompting.

02

Chat

A ChatGPT-style interface with access to multiple underlying models (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini), used for ad hoc writing and brainstorming outside of structured workflows.

03

Infobase

A brand knowledge repository that stores product, brand and company data which can be pulled into generations via tags, so outputs stay grounded in real company facts.

04

Brand Voice

Lets teams define and apply a consistent tone and style across all generated content so output matches brand guidelines without manual editing.

05

Tables

A structured-data layer for organizing leads, accounts or content assets that workflows can read from and write to, functioning as a lightweight data backbone.

06

Copy Agents / Actions

Pre-built and customizable AI agent actions (e.g. web research, CRM lookups, content generation) that serve as the building blocks of workflows.

07

GTM system integrations

Paid plans include connections to 20+ sales and marketing tools plus API access, letting workflows push and pull data from a company's existing stack.

08

Multi-model access

Self-serve plans include access to OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini models from within Chat, avoiding the need for separate subscriptions to each model provider.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Ease of use is consistently one of its highest-rated attributes on both G2 and Capterra, with reviewers citing fast onboarding and simple template/workflow setup
  • Multi-model access (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini) is bundled into the Chat plan, so users are not paying for separate model subscriptions
  • The Workflow Builder genuinely differentiates it from single-shot copywriting tools by chaining research, generation and CRM actions together
  • The free plan is permanent (not a time-limited trial) and usable enough for testing before committing
  • Capterra reviewers report strong sentiment overall, especially around speed of output for ads, emails and short-form marketing copy
  • Fullcast's acquisition brings deeper GTM/RevOps integration ambitions, which is attractive to sales and marketing ops teams already investing in workflow automation

Cons

  • The October 2025 acquisition by Fullcast and pivot to a "GTM AI platform" has drawn sharply negative sentiment on Trustpilot, with complaints centered on pricing changes and shifting product direction
  • Pricing now has a steep gap: the self-serve Chat plan tops out at $29/mo (5 seats), and the next tier up, Growth, jumps to $1,000/mo — there is no mid-market option
  • The free plan's 2,000-word monthly cap is restrictive and most users hit the wall within a few sessions
  • Users report intermittent reliability issues and downtime, which can disrupt workflows mid-run
  • Raw output is generally described as needing human editing; several users note content can feel generic or repetitive after extended use
  • The rebrand toward GTM/RevOps workflows has confused some longtime users who originally signed up for simple copywriting

What reviewers say

Sentiment is polarized depending on when and how a reviewer used the product. Pre-acquisition G2 and Capterra reviews are strongly positive, praising ease of use and speed for short-form marketing copy, with Capterra showing roughly 91% positive sentiment. Post-acquisition sentiment, especially on Trustpilot, is considerably worse, driven by the pricing restructure and the pivot away from simple copywriting toward a GTM automation platform. Reddit discussion echoes this split: users generally find it good for speed and volume but note the free plan is thin, downtime is a recurring frustration, and output quality plateaus over extended use.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Copy.ai cost in 2026?

Copy.ai has a permanent free plan (2,000 words/month), a Chat plan at $29/mo ($24/mo billed annually) for small teams, and then business tiers starting at $1,000/mo (Growth) up to custom Enterprise pricing. There is no longer a mid-priced (~$49/mo) individual plan.

Is Copy.ai still a copywriting tool?

It still supports copywriting through Chat and templates, but since its October 2025 acquisition by Fullcast, the company has repositioned around GTM workflow automation for sales and marketing teams rather than standalone copywriting.

Does Copy.ai have a free plan?

Yes. The free plan includes 2,000 generated words per month, access to Chat and Infobase, and 200 one-time workflow credits for new users — it does not expire, but the word cap is restrictive for regular use.

Is Copy.ai worth it in 2026?

It depends on use case: for a solo user or small team wanting occasional AI writing help, the $29/mo Chat plan is reasonable value. For workflow automation across a GTM stack, it can be worthwhile at $1,000/mo+, but that price point puts it out of reach for most individuals and small businesses.

How does Copy.ai compare to Jasper or Writesonic?

Copy.ai differentiates on its Workflow Builder for multi-step GTM automation, whereas Jasper focuses more on brand-consistent long-form content and Writesonic has pivoted toward AI search visibility (GEO) tracking. Copy.ai's self-serve pricing is now cheaper at the entry tier but far more expensive at the team/automation tier.

Can I cancel or downgrade my Copy.ai plan?

Yes, self-serve customers can upgrade or downgrade at any time; upgrades are prorated for the current month, and downgrades apply as a credit on the next bill.
