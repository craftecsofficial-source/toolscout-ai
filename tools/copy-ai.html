Copy.ai is a good fit for sales and marketing operations teams with budget for automation; solo writers and small businesses looking for cheap copy should look elsewhere. The product has moved from a copywriting generator to a go-to-market platform, and the only inexpensive entry point is the Chat plan.

The core idea is Workflows: you chain AI steps such as research, drafting and CRM lookups into repeatable runs, using Actions, Agents and Tables as building blocks. Chat handles one-off prompts across several model providers, while Infobase and Brand Voice supply company facts and tone. The site lists integrations with tools such as Salesforce, HubSpot and Outreach.

Best for

For: revenue, sales-development and marketing-ops teams who want AI automations that read and write to their CRM and other GTM systems. Not for: freelancers, bloggers or small shops that only need ad copy or emails, since pricing jumps from $29/mo straight to $1,000/mo.

Integrations

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Workflows chain research, generation and CRM actions, which single-shot writers cannot do
  • Chat plan at $24-29/mo includes five seats and several model providers
  • Infobase and Brand Voice reduce off-brand or invented details
  • Lists SOC 2, GDPR and SSO on the site for security-minded buyers

Cons

  • No mid-priced tier: the jump from Chat ($29/mo) to Growth ($1,000/mo) is steep
  • Growth, Expansion and Scale are annual-only commitments of $12,000 to $36,000
  • Workflow credits are capped per month, so heavy automation can push you up a tier
  • The marketing is now GTM-focused; plain copywriting is an afterthought
  • A free plan was not shown on the pricing page fetched today, so check the signup flow

Frequently asked questions

How much does Copy.ai cost?

Chat is $29/mo or $24/mo billed annually. Growth is $1,000/mo, Expansion $2,000/mo and Scale $3,000/mo, all billed annually. Enterprise is custom.

Does Copy.ai have a free plan?

Older listings mention one, but the pricing page checked on 2026-10-16 showed no free plan. Confirm at signup.

Is it still a copywriting tool?

Chat can write copy, but the product is now positioned as a go-to-market automation platform.

What is the main limit on paid plans?

Monthly workflow credits (20K on Growth, 45K Expansion, 75K Scale) and seat counts.

Copy.ai or Jasper?

Copy.ai suits workflow automation for sales teams; Jasper is closer to a marketing content workspace with brand voice.
